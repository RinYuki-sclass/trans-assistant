4. Turning Point
Cheon Kang-hyun, the President of the Korean Hunter Association and former President of South Korea, was a man consumed by ambition. However, even with all the wealth and honor befitting his ambition, he was not satisfied. This alone was not enough.

Born an ordinary man, unable to awaken, the era demanded great hunters. Excellent and powerful hunters were the very power of the nation. The Association President desperately wanted to seize that power.

So, he ultimately sought to satisfy his remaining desires by putting his son forward. The man who would become South Korea's top S-class Hunter and a world leader. That was the goal Cheon Kang-hyun's son had to endure.

The problem, however, was the existence of one man.

"[Next up in the news. It has been reported that Alpha Guild's gate clearance rate has surpassed 98 percent, setting a new industry record….]"

*Click.* The Association President turned off the TV. He clicked his tongue. Everywhere he went, it was only news about *him*. The man who had effortlessly taken the place that should have belonged to his son.

"…Won Hwi-do."

The Association President muttered his name as if spitting out a curse. A man whose very existence was contemptible. Won Hwi-do was the first S-class Hunter in South Korea, appearing like a comet after the emergence of dungeon gates.

At first, everyone expressed concern at the appearance of an S-class Hunter whose identity wasn't even properly known, a mere fourteen-year-old. But soon, his outstanding achievements became known not only domestically but also internationally, and it didn't take long for him to gain the reputation of being the youngest S-class Hunter in the world.

At the time, the Association's system had not yet been established, so it lacked the proper conditions to control Won Hwi-do's existence. Although other hunters appeared across the country and gathered at the Association, Won Hwi-do always deployed to dungeons alone.

He refused to obey anyone's orders, and no one could rein him in. The guilds established by the Association at the time tried to scout him numerous times, but even that was useless.

The Association's high-ranking officials wracked their brains. Even if a talent like Won Hwi-do merely joined a guild, the nation's power would rise by a level, so his operating outside the organization's control was a huge source of instability. Therefore, the Association proposed an alternative.

That Won Hwi-do establish a guild himself under his own authority, but at least operate under the Association's regulations.

Normally, several conditions were required for guild establishment. Capital, personnel, establishment permit review, and financial requirements. None of these existed for Won Hwi-do.

However, the Association applied an exception due to his rescue strategy capabilities and contributions to the nation. It was unusual, but an unavoidable choice.

As a result, Won Hwi-do succeeded in establishing a guild with just a single member. And in less than a year, it began to make a name for itself as the number one guild in the country.

Alpha Guild's influence grew day by day, and even when new S-class Hunters Kang Tae-shin and Ha Seong-il appeared later, the position of number one in the country did not shift. Won Hwi-do remained consistently strong, and continued to grow without stopping.

The Association President still felt nothing but lamentation when he thought of that time. As a mere member of the National Assembly then, he could not stop Won Hwi-do's progress. Although he later rose to the presidency and greatly expanded the Association's authority over private guild hunters, it was only to that extent. No matter how much he interfered or imposed sanctions, there was no way to break Won Hwi-do's momentum.

That's why it was even more infuriating. His adopted son, Cheon Sang-hyun, who merely hovered at second place, felt utterly useless.

Suddenly, a knock sounded at the door. The Association President commanded coldly.
“Come in.”

As the door opened, a man appeared. A man with ash-gray eyes and a composed face, bearing no resemblance to the Association President who had been twitching his eyebrows while watching TV moments ago.
“You called for me.”
Cheon Sang-hyun said in a flat voice. Just then, a glass ashtray flew past his face. The ashtray shattered against the wall with a loud crash. Cheon Sang-hyun stood still, his expression unchanged.

“You useless bastard.”
The Association President hurled an insult at his son. Even after being cursed at to his face, Cheon Sang-hyun showed not the slightest reaction.
“What did you call me for?”

“What did I call you for? This kind of report landed on my desk, and you’re so nonchalant!”
He snapped, this time throwing a stack of documents. The papers scattered at Cheon Sang-hyun’s feet, and their contents slowly came into view.
There were clear photos of Cheon Sang-hyun and Won Hwi-do leaving a restaurant, along with a report detailing the time and place of their meeting.

Cheon Sang-hyun remained consistently calm. His composure only fueled the President’s rage. The Association President’s neck veins bulged.
“It seems you’ve been meeting that bastard often lately.”
“I don’t meet him often.”
“Silence!”

The Association President fumed, glaring at his son. Not blinking once since he entered, it felt as if he were facing a wall.
“I told you to surpass that Won Hwi-do, but instead of keeping your distance, you’re practically waiting on him hand and foot? Are you out of your mind?!”
The report didn’t just contain photos and meeting locations. It meticulously documented Cheon Sang-hyun’s activities, such as Delta Guild’s corporate card usage and his schedule, all entirely tailored to Won Hwi-do.

Cheon Sang-hyun didn’t answer. His deep-set gaze quietly observed the documents scattered on the floor. Soon, he bent down and began picking up the reports, one by one, particularly those with clear photos of Won Hwi-do. The Association President clicked his tongue, glaring at his son.
“I knew something felt off… I thought that bastard had become quite docile lately, but you haven’t been giving him useless information, have you? Otherwise, there’s no reason for that unruly punk to change! Right? Isn’t that right?!”

“That’s not it.”
“What?”
“Guild Leader Won’s change isn’t because of me.”

Cheon Sang-hyun slowly stood up after gathering enough of the reports to his satisfaction. He gazed at his adoptive father with calm eyes.
“It’s his will.”

“Ha… his will, you say?”
“Yes.”

The Association President raised an eyebrow. Today, his son’s attitude was completely different from usual. Cheon Sang-hyun was always detached and calm. He was impeccably flawless to an unsettling degree, yet he had never contradicted his father’s opinions. But today, for some reason, he sensed a strange defiance from him. It was a rather unpleasant feeling.

“…Right. So that’s how it is.”
The Association President muttered softly, looking out the window. Beyond the darkened window, his adopted son’s upright figure was reflected. It wasn’t just Won Hwi-do who had changed.

Change. He hated change. If an anomaly occurred, it had to happen under his control. But Cheon Sang-hyun and Won Hwi-do were moving in ways he hadn’t foreseen. It was unacceptable.

“Don’t associate with that fellow again.”
He didn’t know what had gotten into his son to make him go around everywhere with a guy like Won Hwi-do. In fact, he didn’t care.

For a very long time, he had never once wanted to know what his son was thinking or what he wanted to do. For an adopted son, with whom he shared no blood and who was brought in merely for a purpose, such things were a luxury. He was simply anxious that his adopted son might be tainted by Won Hwi-do’s shadow, and thus become an obstacle to his own ambitions.

“If I receive another report like this, I won’t let it slide.”
But no answer came. The Association President glared, turning around. His adopted son still stood there, expressionless. It was hard to read his expression to know what he was thinking, but an uneasy silence continued. The Association President slammed the table.

“Aren’t you going to answer?”
A heavy silence descended. After a long silence, Cheon Sang-hyun spoke with an unconcerned expression.
“Understood.”

“…You really understood, didn’t you?” The Association President asked, his voice full of doubt. His son had always been obedient. But today, his compliant attitude was strangely irritating.

“Yes.” Cheon Sang-hyun replied calmly. No emotion was discernible in his clear, ash-gray eyes. This was the Cheon Sang-hyun the Association President knew. Still, something didn’t sit right with him, but it seemed there was nothing more he could pry out.

“Alright. You may go.” “Yes.” Cheon Sang-hyun bowed politely, then turned and left without a sound. The Association President didn’t take his eyes off Cheon Sang-hyun until he closed the door and disappeared. As soon as his presence was completely gone, the Association President immediately called his secretary.

[Yes, Mr. President.] “Assign more people to my son. I want a thorough report on what he’s doing, who he’s meeting, from text records to call logs.” [Understood.]

After the brief conversation, the Association President looked down at the few documents still scattered on the floor. Strangely, there was no report containing photos of Won Hwi-do. That wasn’t an issue.

The problem was his son. There was definitely something he didn’t know. It was the intuition of the Association President, who had risen to his position through all sorts of schemes and stratagems. His intuition was rarely wrong. So, he had to set things straight now.

His son had to remain a loyal dog, no more, no less. If he tried to step outside the fence his father had built, then it wouldn’t be overlooked as it was now.

The Association President took out his phone from his pocket. Soon, he made a call somewhere. Before long, a voice could be heard from the other end of the phone.

[Hello, Mr. President! What can I do for you?] The moment he heard the Vice President’s voice, thick with flattery, the Association President’s lips curled into a silent smile. Never had the voice of this hyena-like man, who treated him like a puppet, been so welcome.

“Come see me for a moment.”

Won Hwi-do’s red eyes glanced at his phone with dissatisfaction. But the phone was silent. No one had contacted him. There was no need to pay it much mind. He wasn’t the type to dwell on such things.

Or so he thought.

“Min Ji-hyun.”
Won Hwi-do found himself calling Min Ji-hyun.
[Yes, Guild Leader.]
*No contact from Delta Guild?* He barely managed to swallow the words that had risen to the tip of his tongue. Won Hwi-do clicked his tongue and gave an insincere answer.
“…It’s nothing.”
[Understood. If you need anything, please call me anytime.]

After ending the call, Won Hwi-do decided to focus on his work. There weren’t many dungeons today, so he was able to return quickly. However, strangely, the documents just weren’t registering today. In fact, it wasn’t just today. Ever since his last meeting with Cheon Sang-hyun, he’d felt a persistent tightness in his chest and a general lack of clarity, which was extremely unpleasant.

At that moment, his phone suddenly vibrated. Won Hwi-do immediately looked at the screen.
[Kang Tae-shin: Won Hwi-do, how have you been? I’ve got a lot to do here. But I’ve had some free time lately, so…]
Won Hwi-do didn’t read further and flipped his phone over. Normally, he would have replied with at least a sentence to a message that came after so long, but he wasn’t in the mood right now. Perhaps it was because this wasn’t the person he was waiting to hear from.

It was strange to begin with. Won Hwi-do was absolutely not the type to enjoy exchanging messages or such activities with anyone. Let alone waiting for a specific person’s contact – that was even more unheard of.

“……”
Won Hwi-do took his eyes off the documents. Before he knew it, his hand had picked up his phone. Naturally, no messages had arrived from anyone. Soon, Won Hwi-do’s gaze found the last message he had exchanged with Cheon Sang-hyun.

[Don’t come find me.]
That firm notification was a text message Won Hwi-do had sent himself. It was already a week ago, the evening he had impulsively kissed Cheon Sang-hyun. It was an act he’d committed in a fit of pique, unlike himself.

He didn’t know why, but after that incident, he hadn’t felt like facing Cheon Sang-hyun. Even now, whenever he recalled that moment, his face strangely flushed, and a sensation like his chest was being squeezed lingered. It was an uncomfortable and unpleasant sensation.

However, this feeling only intensified as Cheon Sang-hyun’s absence continued.

Something was strange. Was Cheon Sang-hyun really so simple-minded as to cut off contact just because he’d told him not to come?

The Cheon Sang-hyun Won Hwi-do knew was a man with a persistent side, contrary to his appearance. All the unpleasant memories related to him that had accumulated until now were precisely because of that.

He was annoying. Even when he was right in front of him, he was an endlessly bothersome presence, but now that he wasn't around, that feeling intensified tenfold.

Eventually, his patience reached its limit. Won Hwi-do picked up his phone. He called Cheon Sang-hyun directly. The ringing tone continued for a long time. Won Hwi-do waited. He waited, and waited again. What came back was a cold, mechanical voice.
[The call cannot be connected. After the beep, you will be connected to voicemail….]
“……”

Something was strange. He hadn't imagined that Cheon Sang-hyun wouldn't answer the phone. Just a short while ago, Cheon Sang-hyun had picked up immediately the moment he called. He'd naturally assumed it would be the same this time. Perhaps because his expectation was wrong, his mood twisted even further.

Won Hwi-do called again. Again, and again, and once more. But each time, the connection failed. With every dropped call, the veins throbbing at Won Hwi-do's temples grew more prominent. His questions also deepened.

Why? How could this be? Did he, of all people, have something important to do? Well, if he was clearing a dungeon, it wouldn't be strange for him not to answer the phone. Conceding this, Won Hwi-do waited for an hour.

And so, an hour later.
[The call cannot be connected. After the beep….]
*CRASH!*
A fist, veins stark white, slammed down on the table. The table, crafted from an SS-class magic stone, *cracked* and split. Won Hwi-do finally rose. Abandoning his work, he left his office and ran into Jo Min-chan, who happened to be passing by in the hallway. She widened her eyes at the sight of Won Hwi-do moving so quickly.

“Guildmaster, do you have an urgent errand? And also, there was a loud noise just a moment ago…”
“I’m going out for a bit.”
“Huh?”

Won Hwi-do didn't answer further and immediately teleported. The full view of Delta Guild's main building came into sight. Won Hwi-do strode into the guild lobby. As he crossed the lobby, the curious gazes of Delta Guild members followed him.

It was a common occurrence, so he paid no mind. Won Hwi-do headed straight for the lobby desk. The timid desk clerk he usually saw turned pale upon spotting Won Hwi-do.

“…Ah, good afternoon, Guildmaster Won.”
“When will Guildmaster Cheon return?”
“Well… he’s very busy today.”
“What?”

This wasn't the answer he'd expected. He'd anticipated something like, "You'll have to wait a few minutes," or an offer to lead him to the reception room. When Won Hwi-do glared at him coldly, the employee swallowed dryly and averted his gaze.

"I-I'm sorry, but today..."
"I'll wait until he's available."
Won Hwi-do cut off the employee and headed straight for the reception room. The employee stared blankly at Won Hwi-do's rapidly retreating back before scrambling to follow him.

"Guild Leader Won! I'm truly sorry, but it would be best if you didn't wait."
"Why not?"
At the employee's somewhat significant words, Won Hwi-do immediately turned around. The employee instantly lowered his gaze.

"W-well, that is..."
"Speak quickly."
At the cold urging, the employee's shoulders hunched even further. He fixed his eyes on the floor and opened his mouth with difficulty.

"Our Guild Leader... he said that if Guild Leader Won came, we shouldn't make you wait, but just send you away..."
"...What?"
Won Hwi-do unconsciously frowned. He replayed the employee's words in his mind. He hadn't misheard, and there was no way the employee would be bold enough to lie.

So Cheon Sang-hyun's instruction to send him away was probably true, yet somehow, he couldn't believe it. A dull throb in his head made him feel as if he'd been struck.
Won Hwi-do stood motionless, thinking. Cheon Sang-hyun refusing him of his own volition was something he had never considered. For reasons unknown, he seemed to have held a baseless belief that Cheon Sang-hyun would never reject him, no matter what. That, too, was quite a shock.

"Um... Guild Leader Won?"
As he stood still, the employee cautiously spoke to him. Won Hwi-do immediately moved, not towards the lobby entrance, but towards the elevator leading to the offices. And before anyone could stop him, he arrived in front of Cheon Sang-hyun's office.

"Cheon Sang-hyun."
*Bang!* He burst through the violently opened door. His head was hot. As if he were angry.
In reality, there was no reason for him to be angry. Cheon Sang-hyun was a nuisance, and if he was going to avoid him first, it should have been a welcome development. But it wasn't welcome at all.

Not now. If Cheon Sang-hyun wanted to avoid him or didn't want to see him, it had to be when *he* wanted it.

Won Hwi-do scoured the office with icy eyes. Cheon Sang-hyun's presence was nowhere to be felt. It seemed certain he was busy. He had, in fact, confirmed the information that Cheon Sang-hyun had taken on a Class 3 dungeon before coming here.

Even knowing that, the intense emotions boiling within his chest wouldn't subside.

Since when had he been so busy? Won Hwi-do muttered inwardly as he walked toward Cheon Sang-hyun's desk. His eyes darted around busily, as if searching for some special mark or message left for him.

At that moment, Jung Yoon-seop rushed into the office.

"Guild Leader Won! You can't just barge in like this!"

"I'll wait until Cheon Sang-hyun arrives."

When Won Hwi-do uttered this coldly, Jung Yoon-seop's face quickly froze. She seemed to hesitate, her lips parting slightly, before answering cautiously.

"...I'm afraid that will be difficult. The Guild Leader said he doesn't want Guild Leader Won to waste his time."

"Waste my time?"

Won Hwi-do rolled Jung Yoon-seop's words around in his mouth, as if to himself. Ha, a hollow laugh escaped him involuntarily. Who was it that had so persistently made him waste his time until now, only to say this? Jung Yoon-seop nervously continued her explanation.

"So, rather than waiting here, it would be better to wait for future contact..."

"I understand."

There was no need to hear more. Won Hwi-do turned, creating a cold gust of wind. For now, he had no choice but to leave. He didn't want to stay here any longer.

"I will escort you to the entrance."

"No need."

Won Hwi-do snapped coldly and teleported to the lobby. Naturally, Cheon Sang-hyun's presence was still nowhere to be felt. Instead, only Won Hwi-do's chilling aura permeated the surroundings.

Passing Delta Guild members scattered, fleeing from Won Hwi-do as if he were parting the Red Sea. Their murmurs as they fled drifted into his ears.

"What brings Guild Leader Won here again?"

"He looks incredibly angry, or am I mistaken...?"

"Did he really fight with our Guild Leader?"
"Watch your mouth...!"
Won Hwi-do stopped abruptly, without realizing it. Normally, it would have been a conversation he'd just ignore. He could usually disregard whatever people gossiped about him. But something bothered him.
Fight? With Cheon Sang-hyun?

Now that he thought about it, with his current feelings, it wouldn't be strange to fight him right now. He wanted to violently cut down the man who had made him so utterly displeased.
Yes. That would be better. He should go to the dungeon Cheon Sang-hyun had deployed to right now and settle things with that man directly. With a new goal, Won Hwi-do set off.

But then.
"Oh..."
Suddenly, a familiar voice stopped him in his tracks. The moment he reflexively turned his head, he met a pair of eyes. Black eyes, shimmering with an unknown curiosity. Moon Yeo-kyung stood there, dressed in a Delta Guild training uniform.

Won Hwi-do found himself staring at him for a long time. Along with that, he heard other guild members whispering.
"It's Moon Yeo-kyung."
"Ah, the one Guild Leader brought in personally..."
"Does he know Guild Leader Won?"

Won Hwi-do quietly looked down at Moon Yeo-kyung. Then, Moon Yeo-kyung, who had been momentarily dazed, quickly bowed.
"Ah, hello!"

Won Hwi-do didn't answer. He was a presence he hadn't thought of at all. At the same time, a memory surfaced in his mind.
*Please don't meet him.*
The voice of a man who had pleaded with such earnestness. That voice echoed in his mind. Along with it, an unexpected emotion began to rear its head. A childish defiance that the usual him would never have harbored.

Won Hwi-do reached out a hand. A slender forearm, for a man, was caught in his grasp. Moon Yeo-kyung visibly panicked, struggling slightly.
"...! Wh-why are you doing this..."

“Follow me.”
“What?”
Moon Yeo-kyung’s eyes widened. Won Hwi-do turned his head without answering. As the Delta Guild members watched, the two disappeared.

Jo Min-chan and Min Ji-hyun were currently experiencing unprecedented confusion. To them, Won Hwi-do was an absolute being. No matter how many exceptional SS-class and SSS-class hunters there were in other countries, for Jo Min-chan and Min Ji-hyun, Won Hwi-do was the best hunter.

When they learned that such a Won Hwi-do was secretly involved in an intimate relationship with Cheon Sang-hyun, they felt their world crumble. Cheon Sang-hyun, of all people. While he was indeed the country’s second-best hunter, the truth that such a stiff, stone-faced man was their Guild Leader’s partner was something they didn’t want to believe. Moreover, wasn’t Cheon Sang-hyun closely connected to the Association, which was desperate to take down Won Hwi-do? At first, they even suspected that Cheon Sang-hyun might have approached Won Hwi-do with ulterior motives.

Nevertheless, they tried their best to understand Won Hwi-do. Given Won Hwi-do’s personality, it was highly unlikely he would meet someone trying to use him, and they even hoped he might use Cheon Sang-hyun to gain control of the Association. But now, they couldn’t make sense of this scene.

“Looks like you’re adapting well to guild life.”
“…Yes.”
In response to Won Hwi-do’s question, the slender man sitting opposite him cautiously nodded. The two were sitting opposite each other at a table in the middle of the Alpha Guild lounge. Jo Min-chan and Min Ji-hyun, who had just returned from a dungeon run, secretly observed the scene. They quickly exchanged glances.

“Hunter Min Ji-hyun, who is that man?”
“I don’t know either. Seeing as he’s wearing a Delta Guild training uniform, it looks like he was brought from there…”
“…Delta Guild?”
Jo Min-chan muttered in an unusual tone. The Delta Guild was Guild Leader Cheon Sang-hyun’s guild. Could this just be a coincidence? Min Ji-hyun seemed to be thinking the same thing.

“Why the Delta Guild, of all places?”
“I don’t know. What’s clear is that it can’t be a coincidence.”
“I think so too.”
The two fell into deep thought. They tried to make all sorts of conjectures about what connection there could possibly be between Cheon Sang-hyun, Won Hwi-do, and that mysterious man. Then, at one moment, Min Ji-hyun’s face froze in a serious expression.

Could it be that man was his target from the start, not Cheon Sang-hyun? Now that she thought about it, unlike the stiff, wooden Cheon Sang-hyun, that man was quite cute and pretty, and suited Won Hwi-do quite well. But if that were the case, what was all that about him sticking with Cheon Sang-hyun and exchanging flowers earlier?

Amidst a flurry of countless questions, Jo Min-chan’s pager rang. She immediately picked up the pager.

“Yes, this is Jo Min-chan from Alpha Guild.”

[Hello, Hunter Jo Min-chan. This is Jung Yoon-seop from Delta Guild.]

“…? What’s the sudden matter from Delta Guild?”

“What? Delta Guild?” At Jo Min-chan’s voice, Min Ji-hyun pricked up her ears. Meanwhile, Jung Yoon-seop asked in a somewhat tired voice.

[Is Trainee Moon Yeo-kyung there, by any chance?]

“Cheon Sang-hyun seems to have taken good care of him. Seeing that your complexion has improved quite a bit.”

“…Does it look that way?” Moon Yeo-kyung raised his head from his tea. The carefully approaching gaze seemed to subtly scrutinize his face. It was an unpleasant sensation.

A natural silence fell. There was nothing to say between them.

Surprisingly, it was Moon Yeo-kyung who spoke first.

“…Actually, since coming to Delta Guild, I haven’t encountered Guild Leader Cheon Sang-hyun.”

“Is that so?”

“Yes.” Moon Yeo-kyung nodded meekly. Won Hwi-do wasn’t curious why he was saying such a thing. From the start, Cheon Sang-hyun would never intentionally want to encounter Moon Yeo-kyung.

“He dislikes me, you see.”

Ah.

“…You knew.”
Of course he would know. Other people’s emotions, especially negative ones, tend to be felt particularly clearly. And the stronger the emotion, the more so.

“Of course I would know.”
Suddenly, a sigh-like voice was heard. His eyes met Moon Yeo-kyung’s. The intense gaze coming towards him made it impossible to guess what he was thinking.

“He…”
Moon Yeo-kyung’s lips parted. He seemed about to say something, but for some reason, he closed his mouth. Suddenly, his gaze fell to the table.

“…He’ll dislike it if I’m with you.”
The words had subtly changed. He wondered what Moon Yeo-kyung had originally intended to say, but decided not to ask.

“I know.”
“So that’s why you brought me here.”
“Yes.”
Won Hwi-do answered calmly. Cheon Sang-hyun intensely disliked him meeting Moon Yeo-kyung. Knowing this, he had deliberately brought Moon Yeo-kyung along just to provoke him. Even he found it difficult to understand what kind of emotion this was.

“…I see.”
Moon Yeo-kyung smiled faintly. A precarious smile, as if it might vanish at any moment. It was a face he had seen somewhere before. Where could it have been?

Suddenly, a memory surfaced.
–Because the Guild Leader pities me.
It was that same face then. As if resigned to something, or perhaps yearning for it. Was it possible for two completely unrelated emotions to intersect? In truth, Won Hwi-do lacked the ability to distinguish such feelings. He had no interest in them. After all, they were irrelevant to him. Knowing them would be useless.

“What about him… What do you like about Guild Leader Cheon Sang-hyun?”
Perhaps that was why. At Moon Yeo-kyung’s sudden question, his mind instantly went blank.

“…What?”

A dazed voice escaped his lips. It was an unexpected question, as sudden as its timing. It was nonsense to begin with.

“What strange things you’re saying. When did I ever say I liked that guy?” Uttering the words made him even more displeased. Liking Cheon Sang-hyun? He’d have to be crazy. But Moon Yeo-kyung, on the contrary, seemed surprised.

“Huh? But if not…” “Guild Leader!” At that moment, Jo Min-chan’s voice suddenly pierced his eardrums. He turned his head to see Jo Min-chan and Min Ji-hyun rushing towards them. Won Hwi-do instinctively knew. It was time to send Moon Yeo-kyung away.

But he wasn’t satisfied. What had Moon Yeo-kyung been trying to say? He was curious about the rest of the sentence he hadn’t heard. He wanted to ask what grounds she had for spouting such nonsense about him liking someone like Cheon Sang-hyun.

But there was no time for that. “Guild Leader, is that Moon Yeo-kyung, the trainee?” Jo Min-chan asked, looking at Moon Yeo-kyung. Won Hwi-do slowly nodded. “Yes. I’ll send him back soon.” “Well… Delta Guild’s side…” “I’ll take responsibility.”

Won Hwi-do stood up without hesitation. Moon Yeo-kyung also awkwardly stood up with him. A gaze full of regret somehow reached him. His brow furrowed involuntarily. The unresolved question remained as an unpleasant feeling.

Won Hwi-do decided to ignore that feeling. Enough. It was probably just idle talk anyway, not even worth hearing. “You can go now.” Won Hwi-do turned away coldly. News of his contact with Moon Yeo-kyung would have reached Cheon Sang-hyun’s ears. There was no need to keep Moon Yeo-kyung here any longer.

But why did he feel so uneasy?
–Don’t meet him.
–What part of Guild Leader Cheon Sang-hyun do you like?

Two completely unrelated voices simultaneously echoed in his mind. Along with them, a sense of unease swelled even larger. He felt as if he had committed something he shouldn’t have. His chest felt tight, and strangely, he felt that Cheon Sang-hyun shouldn’t know about this situation. Why did he want to escape this place as quickly as possible?

“Um…” Just then, a hesitant voice stopped him. It was Moon Yeo-kyung’s voice. Won Hwi-do didn’t turn around.

“…Will I be able to see you again?” It was strange. They had only exchanged a few words and met eyes a few times. Why did Moon Yeo-kyung harbor goodwill for him based on just that? But that wasn’t important now. Won Hwi-do replied curtly. “No.”

That wouldn’t happen. Because, “Guild Leader Won.” Suddenly, a familiar voice pierced his eardrums. At the same time, a figure caught his eye. Cheon Sang-hyun was standing tall in the middle of the lounge, watching Won Hwi-do, though he didn’t know how long he’d been there. Everyone’s gaze converged on him.

“Guild Leader Cheon?” “What brings you here so suddenly…” But he didn’t reply. His cool, ash-gray eyes were fixed solely on Won Hwi-do, unmoving. Won Hwi-do was flustered. To his own surprise, Cheon Sang-hyun’s gaze was incredibly cold and sharp.

Was it really something to react to like that? While it was true he had contacted Moon Yeo-kyung to draw out Cheon Sang-hyun, he hadn’t expected such a reaction. He hadn’t expected him to come in person, openly radiating such killing intent.

Soon, that gaze shifted to someone’s face. He felt Moon Yeo-kyung, who was standing behind him, take a step back. At the same time, an eerie aura was felt around his neck. Won Hwi-do instinctively deployed a shield.

*CRACK!* A shadow extending from the ground instantly pierced the shield. It was an attack aimed precisely at Moon Yeo-kyung. Moon Yeo-kyung’s face turned pale. Jo Min-chan and Min Ji-hyun were aghast.
