“Follow me.”
“What?”
Moon Yeo-kyung’s eyes widened. Won Hwi-do turned his head without answering. As the Delta Guild members watched, the two disappeared.

Jo Min-chan and Min Ji-hyun were currently experiencing unprecedented confusion. To them, Won Hwi-do was an absolute being. No matter how many exceptional SS-class and SSS-class hunters there were in other countries, for Jo Min-chan and Min Ji-hyun, Won Hwi-do was the best hunter.

When they learned that such a Won Hwi-do was secretly involved in an intimate relationship with Cheon Sang-hyun, they felt their world crumble. Cheon Sang-hyun, of all people. While he was indeed the country’s second-best hunter, the truth that such a stiff, stone-faced man was their Guild Leader’s partner was something they didn’t want to believe. Moreover, wasn’t Cheon Sang-hyun closely connected to the Association, which was desperate to take down Won Hwi-do? At first, they even suspected that Cheon Sang-hyun might have approached Won Hwi-do with ulterior motives.

Nevertheless, they tried their best to understand Won Hwi-do. Given Won Hwi-do’s personality, it was highly unlikely he would meet someone trying to use him, and they even hoped he might use Cheon Sang-hyun to gain control of the Association. But now, they couldn’t make sense of this scene.

“Looks like you’re adapting well to guild life.”
“…Yes.”
In response to Won Hwi-do’s question, the slender man sitting opposite him cautiously nodded. The two were sitting opposite each other at a table in the middle of the Alpha Guild lounge. Jo Min-chan and Min Ji-hyun, who had just returned from a dungeon run, secretly observed the scene. They quickly exchanged glances.

“Hunter Min Ji-hyun, who is that man?”
“I don’t know either. Seeing as he’s wearing a Delta Guild training uniform, it looks like he was brought from there…”
“…Delta Guild?”
Jo Min-chan muttered in an unusual tone. The Delta Guild was Guild Leader Cheon Sang-hyun’s guild. Could this just be a coincidence? Min Ji-hyun seemed to be thinking the same thing.

“Why the Delta Guild, of all places?”
“I don’t know. What’s clear is that it can’t be a coincidence.”
“I think so too.”
The two fell into deep thought. They tried to make all sorts of conjectures about what connection there could possibly be between Cheon Sang-hyun, Won Hwi-do, and that mysterious man. Then, at one moment, Min Ji-hyun’s face froze in a serious expression.
