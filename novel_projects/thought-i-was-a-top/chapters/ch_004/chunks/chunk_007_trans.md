This wasn't the answer he'd expected. He'd anticipated something like, "You'll have to wait a few minutes," or an offer to lead him to the reception room. When Won Hwi-do glared at him coldly, the employee swallowed dryly and averted his gaze.

"I-I'm sorry, but today..."
"I'll wait until he's available."
Won Hwi-do cut off the employee and headed straight for the reception room. The employee stared blankly at Won Hwi-do's rapidly retreating back before scrambling to follow him.

"Guild Leader Won! I'm truly sorry, but it would be best if you didn't wait."
"Why not?"
At the employee's somewhat significant words, Won Hwi-do immediately turned around. The employee instantly lowered his gaze.

"W-well, that is..."
"Speak quickly."
At the cold urging, the employee's shoulders hunched even further. He fixed his eyes on the floor and opened his mouth with difficulty.

"Our Guild Leader... he said that if Guild Leader Won came, we shouldn't make you wait, but just send you away..."
"...What?"
Won Hwi-do unconsciously frowned. He replayed the employee's words in his mind. He hadn't misheard, and there was no way the employee would be bold enough to lie.

So Cheon Sang-hyun's instruction to send him away was probably true, yet somehow, he couldn't believe it. A dull throb in his head made him feel as if he'd been struck.
Won Hwi-do stood motionless, thinking. Cheon Sang-hyun refusing him of his own volition was something he had never considered. For reasons unknown, he seemed to have held a baseless belief that Cheon Sang-hyun would never reject him, no matter what. That, too, was quite a shock.

"Um... Guild Leader Won?"
As he stood still, the employee cautiously spoke to him. Won Hwi-do immediately moved, not towards the lobby entrance, but towards the elevator leading to the offices. And before anyone could stop him, he arrived in front of Cheon Sang-hyun's office.

"Cheon Sang-hyun."
*Bang!* He burst through the violently opened door. His head was hot. As if he were angry.
In reality, there was no reason for him to be angry. Cheon Sang-hyun was a nuisance, and if he was going to avoid him first, it should have been a welcome development. But it wasn't welcome at all.
