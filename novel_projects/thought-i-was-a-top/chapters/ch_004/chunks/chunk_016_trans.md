However, the imaginable purposes for this posture were limited. Won Hwi-do narrowed his eyes and warned in a low voice.
“I told you to move.”
“Why did you meet with Hunter Moon Yeo-kyung?”

Won Hwi-do paused, the strength instantly draining from the hand that had been gripping Cheon Sang-hyun’s shoulder as if to crush it. So that was the reason, after all. Won Hwi-do glared coldly at the fierce gaze directed his way.

Seeing his somberly sunken eyes, Won Hwi-do’s heart twinged. He felt like a criminal. Yet, at the same time, he also felt a surge of anger.

“What business is it of yours whom I meet?”
“Excuse me?”
“Ridiculous.”
Won Hwi-do shoved Cheon Sang-hyun’s shoulder hard. He had only hit him a little forcefully, but there was a crunch, the sound of a bone dislocating. Cheon Sang-hyun, however, paid no mind to the dislocation. His face was painted with an infinitely deep shock.

“…How could you say such a thing?”
Uncharacteristically deep emotion seeped from his trembling voice. Won Hwi-do rose without even looking at him. A subtle displeasure emanated from his hands as he straightened his disheveled clothes.

He had thought seeing Cheon Sang-hyun in pain would make him feel better, but it didn’t at all. Instead, his chest felt stuffy and constricted, only making him uncomfortable. He began to regret following him here.

At that moment, he felt a hand firmly grasp his arm. Suddenly, his vision flipped again. It was a terrifyingly brute force. Veins bulged on Won Hwi-do’s temples.

“You bastard…!”
“Didn’t you say you would only allow yourself to me?!”
“Don’t talk nonsense!”

He had never said such a thing. He might have uttered something similar while kissing him, but Cheon Sang-hyun had no right to shout now. Won Hwi-do grabbed Cheon Sang-hyun by the collar and slammed him onto the bed.

In an instant, their positions were reversed. Even so, Cheon Sang-hyun stubbornly refused to let go of Won Hwi-do’s arm. Instead, he glared at Won Hwi-do with an even more tenacious gaze.

“So you lied to me.”
“Think what you want.”
