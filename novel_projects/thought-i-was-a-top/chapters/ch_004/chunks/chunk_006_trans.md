He was annoying. Even when he was right in front of him, he was an endlessly bothersome presence, but now that he wasn't around, that feeling intensified tenfold.

Eventually, his patience reached its limit. Won Hwi-do picked up his phone. He called Cheon Sang-hyun directly. The ringing tone continued for a long time. Won Hwi-do waited. He waited, and waited again. What came back was a cold, mechanical voice.
[The call cannot be connected. After the beep, you will be connected to voicemail….]
“……”

Something was strange. He hadn't imagined that Cheon Sang-hyun wouldn't answer the phone. Just a short while ago, Cheon Sang-hyun had picked up immediately the moment he called. He'd naturally assumed it would be the same this time. Perhaps because his expectation was wrong, his mood twisted even further.

Won Hwi-do called again. Again, and again, and once more. But each time, the connection failed. With every dropped call, the veins throbbing at Won Hwi-do's temples grew more prominent. His questions also deepened.

Why? How could this be? Did he, of all people, have something important to do? Well, if he was clearing a dungeon, it wouldn't be strange for him not to answer the phone. Conceding this, Won Hwi-do waited for an hour.

And so, an hour later.
[The call cannot be connected. After the beep….]
*CRASH!*
A fist, veins stark white, slammed down on the table. The table, crafted from an SS-class magic stone, *cracked* and split. Won Hwi-do finally rose. Abandoning his work, he left his office and ran into Jo Min-chan, who happened to be passing by in the hallway. She widened her eyes at the sight of Won Hwi-do moving so quickly.

“Guildmaster, do you have an urgent errand? And also, there was a loud noise just a moment ago…”
“I’m going out for a bit.”
“Huh?”

Won Hwi-do didn't answer further and immediately teleported. The full view of Delta Guild's main building came into sight. Won Hwi-do strode into the guild lobby. As he crossed the lobby, the curious gazes of Delta Guild members followed him.

It was a common occurrence, so he paid no mind. Won Hwi-do headed straight for the lobby desk. The timid desk clerk he usually saw turned pale upon spotting Won Hwi-do.

“…Ah, good afternoon, Guildmaster Won.”
“When will Guildmaster Cheon return?”
“Well… he’s very busy today.”
“What?”
