“Guild Leader Won…!”
Along with a voice laced with pain, his cheek was suddenly seized. Rough breaths pierced through his lips. Won Hwi-do’s eyes shot open at the sudden surge of hot warmth invading his mouth. It didn’t feel bad at all. Rather, this warmth, touching him after so long, felt quite welcome. But that was precisely why he was angry.

“Get lost!”
Won Hwi-do slammed his fist into Cheon Sang-hyun’s jaw. With the sound of bone cracking, Cheon Sang-hyun’s head snapped to the side. He was furious. He was furious at Cheon Sang-hyun for finally showing up and demanding attention as if it were his due, but most of all, he was furious at himself for feeling a subtle satisfaction rather than disgust at the other man’s manipulation.

Won Hwi-do raised his fist again. Hitting him once wasn’t enough. He felt like he wouldn’t be satisfied until Cheon Sang-hyun was beaten to a pulp. Thwack. His knuckles, stark white, slammed into Cheon Sang-hyun’s cheek. The barrage of punches didn’t stop. Cheon Sang-hyun’s neat features became stained with bruises.

Even though he could have easily dodged, he didn’t. As if even this violence was simply welcome. As if he was simply delighted to have Won Hwi-do’s attention all to himself right now. His servile and persistent attitude fueled Won Hwi-do’s seething rage.

“Look away.”
He didn’t like that gaze. Those ash-gray eyes, which had disappeared so nonchalantly, now approached him as if it were only natural.

“I said, look away!”
Thwack! Cheon Sang-hyun’s jaw snapped back limply. At the same time, blood splattered onto the sheets. At that moment, Won Hwi-do stopped his hand. He gasped for breath and looked down, his eyes falling on the blood-stained face. Even then, those two eyes, without a hint of intimidation, stared straight at Won Hwi-do. For some reason, his stomach churned.

“…Damn it.”
He muttered through gritted teeth and stood up. He wanted to leave this place as quickly as possible. But just as he was about to teleport.

“Has your anger subsided now?”
The low, calm voice seemed to hold his ankle, preventing him from leaving. Won Hwi-do tried to ignore that voice.

“I don’t understand why you’re angry.”
But for some reason, he couldn’t just ignore it. Perhaps he didn’t want to. Won Hwi-do glared at Cheon Sang-hyun with eyes that gleamed white.

“You don’t understand?”
A chilling voice escaped his lips. In the meantime, Cheon Sang-hyun, having completed his self-healing, slowly rose. Won Hwi-do didn’t take his eyes off him for a second.
