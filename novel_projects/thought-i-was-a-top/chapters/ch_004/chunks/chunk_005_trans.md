“Min Ji-hyun.”
Won Hwi-do found himself calling Min Ji-hyun.
[Yes, Guild Leader.]
*No contact from Delta Guild?* He barely managed to swallow the words that had risen to the tip of his tongue. Won Hwi-do clicked his tongue and gave an insincere answer.
“…It’s nothing.”
[Understood. If you need anything, please call me anytime.]

After ending the call, Won Hwi-do decided to focus on his work. There weren’t many dungeons today, so he was able to return quickly. However, strangely, the documents just weren’t registering today. In fact, it wasn’t just today. Ever since his last meeting with Cheon Sang-hyun, he’d felt a persistent tightness in his chest and a general lack of clarity, which was extremely unpleasant.

At that moment, his phone suddenly vibrated. Won Hwi-do immediately looked at the screen.
[Kang Tae-shin: Won Hwi-do, how have you been? I’ve got a lot to do here. But I’ve had some free time lately, so…]
Won Hwi-do didn’t read further and flipped his phone over. Normally, he would have replied with at least a sentence to a message that came after so long, but he wasn’t in the mood right now. Perhaps it was because this wasn’t the person he was waiting to hear from.

It was strange to begin with. Won Hwi-do was absolutely not the type to enjoy exchanging messages or such activities with anyone. Let alone waiting for a specific person’s contact – that was even more unheard of.

“……”
Won Hwi-do took his eyes off the documents. Before he knew it, his hand had picked up his phone. Naturally, no messages had arrived from anyone. Soon, Won Hwi-do’s gaze found the last message he had exchanged with Cheon Sang-hyun.

[Don’t come find me.]
That firm notification was a text message Won Hwi-do had sent himself. It was already a week ago, the evening he had impulsively kissed Cheon Sang-hyun. It was an act he’d committed in a fit of pique, unlike himself.

He didn’t know why, but after that incident, he hadn’t felt like facing Cheon Sang-hyun. Even now, whenever he recalled that moment, his face strangely flushed, and a sensation like his chest was being squeezed lingered. It was an uncomfortable and unpleasant sensation.

However, this feeling only intensified as Cheon Sang-hyun’s absence continued.

Something was strange. Was Cheon Sang-hyun really so simple-minded as to cut off contact just because he’d told him not to come?

The Cheon Sang-hyun Won Hwi-do knew was a man with a persistent side, contrary to his appearance. All the unpleasant memories related to him that had accumulated until now were precisely because of that.
