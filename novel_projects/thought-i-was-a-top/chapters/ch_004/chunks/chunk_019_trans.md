"And yet, you still came to me."
Silence. He closed his mouth, and Won Hwi-do also stared at Cheon Sang-hyun without a word. A fierce heat emanated from his calm, sunken gray eyes. Soon, a low voice spoke.
"...Did you contact Hunter Moon Yeo-kyung to call me out?"
"Yes."
"Why?"

A large hand approached his cheek. He briefly considered swatting it away with his fist but decided to let it be. A careful touch slowly caressed his jaw. It wasn't as unpleasant a sensation as he'd expected. He didn't know when the anger that had been boiling in his chest just moments ago had subsided.
"I don't know."
He truly didn't know. Was it simply because Cheon Sang-hyun had been so vexing? Perhaps that was all there was to it. But if that were the case, there was still something bothering him. Something he was curious about but felt he shouldn't know, something endlessly frustrating and unsettling.

A straight thumb brushed his lips. Won Hwi-do turned his head, avoiding the touch.
"...To use the Association President as a mere excuse, how pathetic."
"Even if he is incompetent, he is still the Association President."
Still?
It was a rather significant remark. As he raised his eyes, Cheon Sang-hyun reached out a hand. A firm arm wrapped around his waist and pulled him closer. The broad warmth against his body was bothersome, yet it didn't feel bad at all. Soon, a low voice came close to his ear.
"News of our contact will soon reach his ears."
"Your pathetic efforts have become useless."
"Since I've met you, that's not necessarily the case."
"Now, move aside."
This warmth was slowly starting to feel stifling. As Won Hwi-do tried to pull away, Cheon Sang-hyun urgently hugged his waist tighter.
"Please don't go."
"...Weren't there disadvantages to being with you?"
