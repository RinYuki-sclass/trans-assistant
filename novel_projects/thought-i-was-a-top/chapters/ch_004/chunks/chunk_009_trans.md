"Did he really fight with our Guild Leader?"
"Watch your mouth...!"
Won Hwi-do stopped abruptly, without realizing it. Normally, it would have been a conversation he'd just ignore. He could usually disregard whatever people gossiped about him. But something bothered him.
Fight? With Cheon Sang-hyun?

Now that he thought about it, with his current feelings, it wouldn't be strange to fight him right now. He wanted to violently cut down the man who had made him so utterly displeased.
Yes. That would be better. He should go to the dungeon Cheon Sang-hyun had deployed to right now and settle things with that man directly. With a new goal, Won Hwi-do set off.

But then.
"Oh..."
Suddenly, a familiar voice stopped him in his tracks. The moment he reflexively turned his head, he met a pair of eyes. Black eyes, shimmering with an unknown curiosity. Moon Yeo-kyung stood there, dressed in a Delta Guild training uniform.

Won Hwi-do found himself staring at him for a long time. Along with that, he heard other guild members whispering.
"It's Moon Yeo-kyung."
"Ah, the one Guild Leader brought in personally..."
"Does he know Guild Leader Won?"

Won Hwi-do quietly looked down at Moon Yeo-kyung. Then, Moon Yeo-kyung, who had been momentarily dazed, quickly bowed.
"Ah, hello!"

Won Hwi-do didn't answer. He was a presence he hadn't thought of at all. At the same time, a memory surfaced in his mind.
*Please don't meet him.*
The voice of a man who had pleaded with such earnestness. That voice echoed in his mind. Along with it, an unexpected emotion began to rear its head. A childish defiance that the usual him would never have harbored.

Won Hwi-do reached out a hand. A slender forearm, for a man, was caught in his grasp. Moon Yeo-kyung visibly panicked, struggling slightly.
"...! Wh-why are you doing this..."
