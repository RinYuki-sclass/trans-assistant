"You, who vanished without a word, are hardly in a position to say that."
"...Pardon?"
Cheon Sang-hyun's blood-stained face clouded with doubt. A moment of silence fell. It was Cheon Sang-hyun who broke the stillness that had seemed as if it would last forever.
"Were you looking for me?"

In that instant, Won Hwi-do realized something was wrong. Cheon Sang-hyun, having risen from the bed, slowly walked closer.
"Was your reaching out to me... to meet me?"
A faint hint of expectation could be felt in his low voice. Won Hwi-do clicked his tongue. He had said something unnecessary. His earlier remark carried the nuance of someone who had been waiting for Cheon Sang-hyun's contact for an entire week. He couldn't possibly have been like that.
*Could he really not have been?*
Suddenly, a strange voice echoed from deep within his mind. Won Hwi-do held his breath. What was that voice just now?

"...I didn't think you'd expect me to come, since Guild Leader Won told me to stay away."
Cheon Sang-hyun said quietly. Hearing that answer, a surge of anger welled up inside him.
"So, you're quite good at obeying my words, I see."
"I apologize."
"Shut up."
That wasn't what he wanted to hear. Won Hwi-do wanted to know the reason. Someone like Cheon Sang-hyun wouldn't just cut ties based on a mere word from him. Especially not for an entire week.

"What have you been doing all this time?"
He turned his head, and Cheon Sang-hyun's face, which had drawn so close in the interim, was right before him. His deeply sunken eyes meticulously scrutinized Won Hwi-do's face. He slowly opened his mouth.
"I'm being monitored by the Association President."
"And?"
"If I had been with you, it would have caused you trouble."
