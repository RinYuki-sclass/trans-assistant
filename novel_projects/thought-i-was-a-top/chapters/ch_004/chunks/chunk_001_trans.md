4. Turning Point
Cheon Kang-hyun, the President of the Korean Hunter Association and former President of South Korea, was a man consumed by ambition. However, even with all the wealth and honor befitting his ambition, he was not satisfied. This alone was not enough.

Born an ordinary man, unable to awaken, the era demanded great hunters. Excellent and powerful hunters were the very power of the nation. The Association President desperately wanted to seize that power.

So, he ultimately sought to satisfy his remaining desires by putting his son forward. The man who would become South Korea's top S-class Hunter and a world leader. That was the goal Cheon Kang-hyun's son had to endure.

The problem, however, was the existence of one man.

"[Next up in the news. It has been reported that Alpha Guild's gate clearance rate has surpassed 98 percent, setting a new industry record….]"

*Click.* The Association President turned off the TV. He clicked his tongue. Everywhere he went, it was only news about *him*. The man who had effortlessly taken the place that should have belonged to his son.

"…Won Hwi-do."

The Association President muttered his name as if spitting out a curse. A man whose very existence was contemptible. Won Hwi-do was the first S-class Hunter in South Korea, appearing like a comet after the emergence of dungeon gates.

At first, everyone expressed concern at the appearance of an S-class Hunter whose identity wasn't even properly known, a mere fourteen-year-old. But soon, his outstanding achievements became known not only domestically but also internationally, and it didn't take long for him to gain the reputation of being the youngest S-class Hunter in the world.

At the time, the Association's system had not yet been established, so it lacked the proper conditions to control Won Hwi-do's existence. Although other hunters appeared across the country and gathered at the Association, Won Hwi-do always deployed to dungeons alone.

He refused to obey anyone's orders, and no one could rein him in. The guilds established by the Association at the time tried to scout him numerous times, but even that was useless.

The Association's high-ranking officials wracked their brains. Even if a talent like Won Hwi-do merely joined a guild, the nation's power would rise by a level, so his operating outside the organization's control was a huge source of instability. Therefore, the Association proposed an alternative.

That Won Hwi-do establish a guild himself under his own authority, but at least operate under the Association's regulations.

Normally, several conditions were required for guild establishment. Capital, personnel, establishment permit review, and financial requirements. None of these existed for Won Hwi-do.

However, the Association applied an exception due to his rescue strategy capabilities and contributions to the nation. It was unusual, but an unavoidable choice.

As a result, Won Hwi-do succeeded in establishing a guild with just a single member. And in less than a year, it began to make a name for itself as the number one guild in the country.

Alpha Guild's influence grew day by day, and even when new S-class Hunters Kang Tae-shin and Ha Seong-il appeared later, the position of number one in the country did not shift. Won Hwi-do remained consistently strong, and continued to grow without stopping.

The Association President still felt nothing but lamentation when he thought of that time. As a mere member of the National Assembly then, he could not stop Won Hwi-do's progress. Although he later rose to the presidency and greatly expanded the Association's authority over private guild hunters, it was only to that extent. No matter how much he interfered or imposed sanctions, there was no way to break Won Hwi-do's momentum.

That's why it was even more infuriating. His adopted son, Cheon Sang-hyun, who merely hovered at second place, felt utterly useless.
