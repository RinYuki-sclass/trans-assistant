A dazed voice escaped his lips. It was an unexpected question, as sudden as its timing. It was nonsense to begin with.

“What strange things you’re saying. When did I ever say I liked that guy?” Uttering the words made him even more displeased. Liking Cheon Sang-hyun? He’d have to be crazy. But Moon Yeo-kyung, on the contrary, seemed surprised.

“Huh? But if not…” “Guild Leader!” At that moment, Jo Min-chan’s voice suddenly pierced his eardrums. He turned his head to see Jo Min-chan and Min Ji-hyun rushing towards them. Won Hwi-do instinctively knew. It was time to send Moon Yeo-kyung away.

But he wasn’t satisfied. What had Moon Yeo-kyung been trying to say? He was curious about the rest of the sentence he hadn’t heard. He wanted to ask what grounds she had for spouting such nonsense about him liking someone like Cheon Sang-hyun.

But there was no time for that. “Guild Leader, is that Moon Yeo-kyung, the trainee?” Jo Min-chan asked, looking at Moon Yeo-kyung. Won Hwi-do slowly nodded. “Yes. I’ll send him back soon.” “Well… Delta Guild’s side…” “I’ll take responsibility.”

Won Hwi-do stood up without hesitation. Moon Yeo-kyung also awkwardly stood up with him. A gaze full of regret somehow reached him. His brow furrowed involuntarily. The unresolved question remained as an unpleasant feeling.

Won Hwi-do decided to ignore that feeling. Enough. It was probably just idle talk anyway, not even worth hearing. “You can go now.” Won Hwi-do turned away coldly. News of his contact with Moon Yeo-kyung would have reached Cheon Sang-hyun’s ears. There was no need to keep Moon Yeo-kyung here any longer.

But why did he feel so uneasy?
