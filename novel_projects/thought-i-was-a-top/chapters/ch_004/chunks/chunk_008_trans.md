Not now. If Cheon Sang-hyun wanted to avoid him or didn't want to see him, it had to be when *he* wanted it.

Won Hwi-do scoured the office with icy eyes. Cheon Sang-hyun's presence was nowhere to be felt. It seemed certain he was busy. He had, in fact, confirmed the information that Cheon Sang-hyun had taken on a Class 3 dungeon before coming here.

Even knowing that, the intense emotions boiling within his chest wouldn't subside.

Since when had he been so busy? Won Hwi-do muttered inwardly as he walked toward Cheon Sang-hyun's desk. His eyes darted around busily, as if searching for some special mark or message left for him.

At that moment, Jung Yoon-seop rushed into the office.

"Guild Leader Won! You can't just barge in like this!"

"I'll wait until Cheon Sang-hyun arrives."

When Won Hwi-do uttered this coldly, Jung Yoon-seop's face quickly froze. She seemed to hesitate, her lips parting slightly, before answering cautiously.

"...I'm afraid that will be difficult. The Guild Leader said he doesn't want Guild Leader Won to waste his time."

"Waste my time?"

Won Hwi-do rolled Jung Yoon-seop's words around in his mouth, as if to himself. Ha, a hollow laugh escaped him involuntarily. Who was it that had so persistently made him waste his time until now, only to say this? Jung Yoon-seop nervously continued her explanation.

"So, rather than waiting here, it would be better to wait for future contact..."

"I understand."

There was no need to hear more. Won Hwi-do turned, creating a cold gust of wind. For now, he had no choice but to leave. He didn't want to stay here any longer.

"I will escort you to the entrance."

"No need."

Won Hwi-do snapped coldly and teleported to the lobby. Naturally, Cheon Sang-hyun's presence was still nowhere to be felt. Instead, only Won Hwi-do's chilling aura permeated the surroundings.

Passing Delta Guild members scattered, fleeing from Won Hwi-do as if he were parting the Red Sea. Their murmurs as they fled drifted into his ears.

"What brings Guild Leader Won here again?"

"He looks incredibly angry, or am I mistaken...?"
