Two completely unrelated voices simultaneously echoed in his mind. Along with them, a sense of unease swelled even larger. He felt as if he had committed something he shouldn’t have. His chest felt tight, and strangely, he felt that Cheon Sang-hyun shouldn’t know about this situation. Why did he want to escape this place as quickly as possible?

“Um…” Just then, a hesitant voice stopped him. It was Moon Yeo-kyung’s voice. Won Hwi-do didn’t turn around.

“…Will I be able to see you again?” It was strange. They had only exchanged a few words and met eyes a few times. Why did Moon Yeo-kyung harbor goodwill for him based on just that? But that wasn’t important now. Won Hwi-do replied curtly. “No.”

That wouldn’t happen. Because, “Guild Leader Won.” Suddenly, a familiar voice pierced his eardrums. At the same time, a figure caught his eye. Cheon Sang-hyun was standing tall in the middle of the lounge, watching Won Hwi-do, though he didn’t know how long he’d been there. Everyone’s gaze converged on him.

“Guild Leader Cheon?” “What brings you here so suddenly…” But he didn’t reply. His cool, ash-gray eyes were fixed solely on Won Hwi-do, unmoving. Won Hwi-do was flustered. To his own surprise, Cheon Sang-hyun’s gaze was incredibly cold and sharp.

Was it really something to react to like that? While it was true he had contacted Moon Yeo-kyung to draw out Cheon Sang-hyun, he hadn’t expected such a reaction. He hadn’t expected him to come in person, openly radiating such killing intent.

Soon, that gaze shifted to someone’s face. He felt Moon Yeo-kyung, who was standing behind him, take a step back. At the same time, an eerie aura was felt around his neck. Won Hwi-do instinctively deployed a shield.

*CRACK!* A shadow extending from the ground instantly pierced the shield. It was an attack aimed precisely at Moon Yeo-kyung. Moon Yeo-kyung’s face turned pale. Jo Min-chan and Min Ji-hyun were aghast.

“Guild Leader Cheon!” “What are you doing?!” The two couldn’t believe that Cheon Sang-hyun’s attack was directed at Moon Yeo-kyung, a fellow guild member. Therefore, they immediately drew their swords. The situation was growing increasingly serious.

Won Hwi-do clicked his tongue. This was his mistake. He had to resolve this situation before a bigger fight broke out. He immediately teleported right in front of Cheon Sang-hyun.
