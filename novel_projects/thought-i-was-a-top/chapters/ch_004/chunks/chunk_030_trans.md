“I’m glad you sought me out today.”
“What a delusion.”
Won Hwi-do snapped coldly. Cheon Sang-hyun quietly smiled and embraced Won Hwi-do. They were already pressed tightly together, leaving no room to draw closer.

Cheon Sang-hyun endlessly caressed Won Hwi-do, who filled his arms. Though the touch was gentle, Won Hwi-do felt displeased, as if he were being treated like a woman. Cheon Sang-hyun’s subtle smile also bothered him.

“Get off me.”
“I wish you had even the slightest feelings for me.”
Cheon Sang-hyun suddenly said in a quiet voice. Won Hwi-do didn’t understand his words for a moment. Then a few seconds passed.

“…What?”
The moment he belatedly understood, his chest felt strangely uncomfortable. It felt stifling and unpleasant, as if something was caught in his throat. He didn’t know why. Not wanting to be with him anymore, Won Hwi-do tried to get up. But Cheon Sang-hyun immediately held him fast.

“Where are you going?”
“Let go.”
“You can’t.”
“I said, let go!”

But he couldn’t resist any further. This was because the flesh that had been between his legs suddenly swelled up.
“……!”

Cheon Sang-hyun immediately lifted Won Hwi-do’s body. As they embraced face to face, their union deepened even further. Won Hwi-do gritted his teeth and glared at him.

“You beast.”
“Do you hate it?”

Won Hwi-do tried to answer immediately. To say he hated it, that it was utterly unpleasant, and to get lost. But for some reason, the words wouldn’t come out. Why had he said something like that just now, despite clinging to him so shamelessly?
—I wish you had even the slightest feelings for me.
