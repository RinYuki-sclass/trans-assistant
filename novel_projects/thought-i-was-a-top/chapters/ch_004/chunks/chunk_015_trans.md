“Cheon Sang-hyun.”
Only then did Cheon Sang-hyun’s gaze return. His murky, sunken eyes seemed devoid of reason.

“Guild Leader, it’s dangerous!”
“Step back!”
Jo Min-chan and Min Ji-hyun cried out urgently as they rushed forward. But there was no time to pay them any mind. Won Hwi-do abruptly grabbed Cheon Sang-hyun by the collar.
“Come with me.”

He didn’t answer. Instead, a large hand covered the one gripping his collar. The chilling warmth felt exceptionally vivid. Soon, his vision swayed dizzily.
But something was off.

“...!”
The teleportation was blocked. Cheon Sang-hyun had rejected Won Hwi-do’s teleportation.
His magic arbitrarily pulled Won Hwi-do’s body. Won Hwi-do watched Cheon Sang-hyun’s actions, his brow furrowed. It seemed he had another desired destination. Though he didn’t like it, he decided to comply.

The two figures, swirling in subspace, vanished somewhere. In an instant, his spinning, swirling vision returned to normal. At the same time, a soft impact hit his back.

Won Hwi-do immediately got to his feet with agile movements. It was dark. Where could this be? As the question arose, a strange sense of déjà vu washed over him.
A space surrounded by darkness on all sides. Dense magic palpable everywhere. Stifling, murky air that felt like it would choke him.
It was a familiar space. However, it wasn’t a memory Won Hwi-do currently possessed.
This was a dungeon created by Cheon Sang-hyun.

“Guild Leader Won.”
As realization dawned, a low voice pierced his eardrums. His vision flipped along with it. His chest was unexpectedly shoved. He lifted his head at the breath that had suddenly drawn close to his nose. Immediately, their gazes locked. Eyes like black, caked ash stared intently into Won Hwi-do’s. His brow furrowed instantly.

“Move.”
Won Hwi-do said coldly, gripping Cheon Sang-hyun’s shoulder. But Cheon Sang-hyun didn’t budge. In terms of raw strength, Won Hwi-do was clearly superior. Cheon Sang-hyun must have known that fact as well. Yet, his persistence like this clearly had a purpose.
