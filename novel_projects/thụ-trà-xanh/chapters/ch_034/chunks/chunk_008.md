It seemed the World's Child of Destiny's physical condition influenced the world's energy fluctuations. He might be able to exploit this to probe the Main Star.
But this method could easily backfire and get him killed. He decided to wait until he had successfully resolved the matters with Lin Zizhuo and Gu Lang according to plan before taking action.
With the investigation this far along, the truth about the Lin family's case would likely surface soon.
All they needed now was proof that the deadly drug wasn't from W.N, but from another source, and that the medicine they had produced according to the original formula was completely harmless. That would be enough.
Thinking this, Shen Feizhi contacted the Director.
The call connected, and the chaotic background noise on the other end pierced his ears.
"What's going on?" Shen Feizhi frowned.
"Your estrus period is over?" The Director's voice sounded delighted. "Great! Hurry over here now that you're better! All hell's breaking loose!"
"What's the situation?" Shen Feizhi sat up.
"Don't even ask! Our institute allocated most of its experimental facilities and funding to the W.N Project," the Director lamented, his hair thinning noticeably from recent stress. "The other projects didn't get their funding approved and are in an uproar!"
When Shen Feizhi arrived at W.N the next day, a fierce battle was raging in the first-floor conference room.
On the left stood the teams from the Energy Quantum Project and the Biotechnology Project, while on the right was Pharmacology, the team that included the W.N Group.
Project members were pointing fingers and hurling insults at each other, their usual refined and elite demeanor completely abandoned, reduced to the level of street brawlers.
The researchers were all teetering on the brink of mental exhaustion. Those who had managed to get into W.N were particularly fanatical about their work, so it was hardly surprising that they would clash over research funding and project progress.
Shen Feizhi quietly skirted around the chaos and approached the Director, Ritter, and the Biotechnology Team Leader, who were all wearing grim expressions.
