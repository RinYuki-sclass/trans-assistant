Maybe it's just my imagination, he thought, but the effects of this estrus period seemed far more intense than usual.

Something was wrong, terribly wrong. His physical condition was deteriorating rapidly… Was it because his body was rejecting this world?
Shen Feizhi stumbled to his feet and rushed to the pile of medicine Gu Suizhou had given him, but he didn't reach for the medicine.
After a flurry of rustling sounds, he unearthed a small box from among the syringes and vials.
It was a box of chocolates Gu Suizhou had bought for him.
His trembling fingers fumbled with the box several times before finally managing to open it. He tore through the flamboyant heart-shaped wrapping Gu Suizhou had meticulously folded.
Only when the cloyingly sweet taste spread across his tongue did Shen Feizhi exhale heavily.
