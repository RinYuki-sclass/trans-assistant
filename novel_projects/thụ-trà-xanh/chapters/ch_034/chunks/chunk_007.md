"Alright, let's call it a day," Shen Feizhi said, pressing his temples. "I'm feeling a bit unwell. Go back and rest. We'll talk later."
"Ah, okay," Gu Suizhou snapped out of his thoughts. "Oh, right, Brother Shen, your report card! This time, we got first and second place in the whole grade. I edged you out in foreign languages, so you're second."
He pulled out two small booklets—his graduation certificate and Shen Feizhi's.
Shen Feizhi showed no surprise at his own results, but he looked at Gu Suizhou's certificate in astonishment. "Aren't you a sophomore?"
"Mm, I applied for early graduation," Gu Suizhou said with a smile. "It got approved just yesterday."

Shen Feizhi raised an eyebrow. "What about university?"
"Guaranteed admission," Gu Suizhou's eyes lit up, practically begging for praise. "A top-tier Federation school. The principal said I can choose whichever one I want."
"So the exams you've been holed up preparing for recently were for this?"
Shen Feizhi looked into his eyes and relented, reaching out to pat his head. "Not bad."
Those two words made Gu Suizhou happier than winning eight million in prize money.
"Then I'll head out now!" he said, his eyes crinkling into crescents. "Good night, Brother Shen~"
Shen Feizhi grunted lazily in response. As soon as Gu Suizhou left, the relaxed expression on his face vanished. He lay back on the bed, replaced by a thick layer of frustration.
He coughed softly twice, wiping his lips with the back of his hand.
The back of his hand brushed across his thin, rosy lips, leaving two crimson streaks. The color drained from his lips instantly.
He didn't have any lipstick, so he smeared a bit of blood from the wound on his wrist onto his lips to avoid looking too disheveled.
This estrus period had been particularly brutal, and his body still hadn't recovered.
If that were all, it wouldn't have been so bad. What truly shocked him was the message Ritter had sent last night: the energy field above the clouds had been fluctuating wildly since Tuesday, and after monitoring the data, he found it only stabilized this morning.
This timing…
coincided perfectly with his estrus period.
