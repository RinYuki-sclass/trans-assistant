Gu Suizhou nodded, then suddenly turned to him. "Brother Shen, why are you helping me get revenge?"
Shen Feizhi remained silent.
These past few days, he had vaguely run through the scenario in his mind.
He had been summoned to this world because the protagonist was about to fail at a critical juncture.
Given the original host's personality and behavior, it was highly likely that upon meeting Gu Suizhou, he would have been instantly captivated by the man's appearance, thereby earning Gu Suizhou's disdain and making it impossible for him to access W.N's core operations.
W.N's main storyline had only emerged because of his arrival. Therefore, the key to this world's survival—with an eighty percent probability—lay in helping Lin Zizhuo and Gu Lang clear their names and successfully overturn their wrongful convictions.
When Shen Feizhi remained silent, Gu Suizhou didn't press him further, his eyes fixed on the other man's every move.
Brother Shen's skin seems even paler, almost bloodless. He must not have been eating properly during his estrus period. I'll have Xu Zhong hire a good chef to help him recover later, even though Brother Shen's own cooking is unmatched…
Lost in his rambling thoughts, he suddenly noticed the bandages wrapped around Shen Feizhi's wrist.
Hidden beneath long sleeves, they hadn't been visible until Shen Feizhi raised his arm to flip through blueprints.

Gu Suizhou gritted his teeth, but his only option was to reluctantly release his grip.
Shen Feizhi was clearly hiding something from him, but Gu Suizhou didn't care. All he needed to know was that Shen Feizhi was on his side, and he would help him achieve his goals.
Shen Feizhi couldn't afford to neglect his health.
Gu Suizhou's expression darkened as he recalled the mercenaries who had been appearing around Shen Feizhi with increasing frequency lately.
Someone was clearly plotting against them, sending wave after wave of enemies, like endlessly sprouting weeds that were endlessly irritating.
The mastermind behind these attacks must be someone powerful. With Gu Suizhou's current abilities, he couldn't even begin to track them down.
