The Director pinched the bridge of his nose. "At this point, the only rational ones left here are probably the four of us."
Just as the Biotechnology Team Leader was about to lament, someone from the other side yelled:
"R&D, R&D? What have you actually researched? You've been working on that anti-hair loss gene modification surgery for two years—and what's the result? Your team leader is still as bald as a billiard ball!"
Everyone: "……"
The Biotech Team Leader erupted, instantly joining the fray. "Bald-headed motherfucker! This isn't just luck, it's wisdom ! Wisdom! And what has your Pharmacology Team accomplished? We couldn't develop a gene-altering hair transplant, so you invent anti-baldness potion?!"
Director: "Alright, alright, let's calm down. Now it's just the three of us being reasonable."
Ritter's face was etched with melancholy. "Truth be told, Director, Little Shen Angel, while our Energy Group is small, we've recently made some significant breakthroughs. We desperately need funding. You can ask Little Shen himself!"
Shen Feizhi, who never lied, nodded. "I thought W.N wasn't exactly broke. Why are they suddenly fighting over money?"
"It's all because of that investor!" The Director's voice simmered with resentment. "Two days ago, he suddenly sent a message withdrawing his funding. The nerve! W.N is his parents' legacy, for crying out loud! How could he be so heartless?!"
Shen Feizhi and Ritter exchanged a glance.
"How much influence does Lin Sui really have?" Shen Feizhi asked. "Where did he even get this kind of money?"
