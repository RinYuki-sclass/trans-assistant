Energy channel collapses weren't unheard of, but the original owner of a body completely vanishing? That was a first in recorded history.
The why could be figured out later. Right now, the priority was preventing anyone from discovering that the original owner had been "radically altered."
He highly doubted the original Shen Feizhi possessed a face as devastatingly handsome as his own.
"Second Young Master."
The person who entered moved like a cat, making almost no sound.
"You don't seem to be well."
No shit, Shen Feizhi thought. You try standing in a -20°C room with no heating, wearing only a thin shirt. See how lively you feel then.
Without raising his head, Shen Feizhi glanced sideways, revealing one ink-black eye. "Uncle Liao, did you need me for something?"
He had identified the man the moment he spoke.
Butler Liao Chen—the only person in the Shen family who treated the original owner with any decency.

The butler seemed completely oblivious to Shen Feizhi's deliberate attempt to hide his face. His gaze was fixed worriedly on the young man's thin clothes. Words of concern hovered on his lips, but he swallowed them back to address the urgent matter first.
"Second Young Master, Elder Shen and the Madam are waiting for you downstairs."
Beings from High-Tier Worlds possessed comprehensive knowledge of the past and future of Lower-Tier Small Worlds. They compiled this information into novels from the perspective of the Children of Destiny, referring to them simply as "Books."
"Book 6/9" chronicled the story of a Child of Destiny who shared his name and surname. After presenting as an Alpha, this Shen Feizhi slept his way through a lineup of high-quality Omegas and Betas, transforming from a universally despised illegitimate son into a triumphant winner in life.
Shen Feizhi recalled the Child of Destiny's family background quite clearly.
The original owner's biological father, Meng Lang, had ruined his health through excessive debauchery and died young. The true power in the Shen household now rested with Shen Father's first wife and Elder Master Shen.
And now both of them were here?
Shen Feizhi tapped his thigh with his fingertips, feigning understanding while inwardly confused. He revealed half his face again. "I'll come down later."
Butler Liao remained unmoved by the exquisite, perfect half-face, his tone troubled. "Second Young Master, this… Doctor Lin has already arrived."
