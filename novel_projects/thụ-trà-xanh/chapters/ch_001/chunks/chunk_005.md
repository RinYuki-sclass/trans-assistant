Resigned, the self-reproaching Shen Feizhi waved a hand. "Let's go, Uncle Liao."
If he couldn't avoid it, he'd just have to face it head-on.
Maybe the other person doesn't like handsome faces in general, just someone with the original owner's specific looks?
He tried to console himself.
He glanced at Liao Chen, whose expression remained unchanged, and lowered his eyelids slightly.
As someone intimately familiar with the original "Shen Feizhi," the butler showed no reaction other than astonishment at his beauty when faced with a face completely different from the original owner's.
It seemed his body had been automatically integrated into this Small World, replacing the original owner's existence and altering everyone's memories.
Shen Feizhi composed his features, his mind wandering as he maintained a facade of refined composure while following the butler downstairs. He occasionally probed for information, trying to piece things together.
Since the Main Star couldn't provide him with world data, he had to rely on himself.
After descending one flight of stairs, he remained calm and collected.
After three flights, his expression was still unruffled.

After five flights…

"Excuse me, I know this is a bit sudden," Shen Feizhi said, his tone cool but polite, "but… how many damn spirals does this staircase have?"
Uncle Liao looked utterly astonished. "Second Young Master, how could you forget? The Shen family's old mansion is the tallest villa in the First District! It has a full eight floors!"
Eight… floors?
Is this a villa or a residential tower?!
Of course, if being rich meant you could do whatever you wanted, there was still another problem.
"Eight floors, and no elevator allowed," Shen Feizhi said, clapping his hands together in mock admiration. "Is Grandfather trying to groom me into a sports star?"
