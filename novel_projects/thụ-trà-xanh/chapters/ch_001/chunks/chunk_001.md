Shen Feizhi, wrapped in a thick quilt on his bed, stared at the snowflakes drifting past the floor-to-ceiling window and the faint reflection of his own face on the glass. He sniffled sharply.

A Manager's job was to temporarily occupy the body of a "Child of Destiny" until the key node was confirmed to occur.
But two hours ago, just as Shen Feizhi arrived in this world, the energy channel he'd used to descend suddenly collapsed.
This meant he was completely cut off from the Main Star, trapped here forever.
