Chapter 18

In the Shen family's electronic equipment control room, Shen Feizhi stood before the surveillance monitors, his foot pressing down on a security guard lying unconscious on the floor.

Originally, he had only intended to knock the guard out temporarily, planning to wake him up before leaving. But before he could even enter the room, he overheard the man loudly cursing him and Gu Suizhou as a pair of "queer bastards."

Unfortunately for the guard, Shen Feizhi had never been one to repay evil with kindness.

Shen Feizhi calmly lit a cigarette, the tiny ember glaring brightly in the dimly lit control room. Simultaneously, he ripped down the "No Smoking" sign hanging above and tossed it to the floor.

He glared at someone on the monitor, his frustration palpable.

Gu Suizhou had slipped away too quickly earlier, evading a punch that had left Shen Feizhi seething with pent-up rage.

This won't do. The more I think about it, the more suffocated I feel.

Shen Feizhi took a deep drag of his cigarette, blowing smoke rings as he rapidly flicked the third-floor hallway light switch twice.

The lights flickered twice.

The figure on the monitor twitched in response.

A smirk curled Shen Feizhi's lips.

That's better.

He extravagantly stubbed out the remaining half of his cigarette, kicked the unconscious guard, and staged the scene to look like they had tripped over each other and fallen.

Before the guard fully regained consciousness, he used his smartphone to send Gu Suizhou a retreat message, then slipped out the door, intending to sneak to the back gate and wait for him.

The Shen family's villa was a model of traditional design, a perfectly orderly specimen of a wealthy residence according to Elder Master Shen's aesthetic, making it exceptionally easy to remember.

Shen Feizhi's plan was to slip from the rear garden to the front hall, meet Gu Suizhou, and escape together.

The rear garden was precisely where Gu Suizhou had just had his "talk" with Lin Sui.

As if taking a leisurely stroll, he circled the garden until he reached a secluded corner adorned with miniature rock formations, where he paused.

Shen Feizhi gazed at the small stones, leaned forward, and brushed his gloved hand across their surfaces.

In the still-bright night, he clearly saw crimson bloodstains smeared across the white glove.

The only people who had been in this area recently were Gu Suizhou and Lin Sui.

Since Gu Suizhou was uninjured, it was obvious whose blood this South Wind Knows My Intent belonged to.

Shen Feizhi stared at the bloodstains for a moment before silently removing his glove and meticulously wiping the affected stones clean.

Just a cub whose fur hasn't fully grown in, he thought. His cleanup work still lacks maturity—room for growth.

The white glove quickly became stained. After a moment's thought, he untied his tie, carefully wrapped the glove inside, and hurled it over the wall, landing precisely in the trash bin outside.

The garbage trucks in the wealthy district came like clockwork every day. Shen Feizhi waited a few more minutes until 8:30 PM, confirming the trucks had finished their rounds, before nonchalantly dusting off his clothes and leaving.

But on his way back, he encountered a formidable obstacle.

As expected, Shen Wei stood glowering menacingly in the front yard, his face contorted into a ferocious scowl.

Seeing Shen Feizhi approaching with an air of leisurely indifference, he lunged forward, his hand darting toward Shen Feizhi's collar!

If Shen Feizhi let himself be caught, his first half of life would have been a waste. With a slight sidestep, he effortlessly evaded the attack. "Shouldn't tonight's star be busy entertaining guests in the hall? What are you doing here, lurking to ambush me?"

"Don't play dumb with me, Shen Feizhi!" Shen Wei's face contorted with fury at his failed strike. "Grandpa moved too fast, kicking you out before I could settle accounts! What the hell gives you the right to touch my people?"

"Who exactly are 'your people'?" Shen Feizhi maintained a safe distance between them. "Lin Zhou?"

He hadn't forgotten to protect the little cub's alias.

"Enough with the bullshit!" Shen Wei's rage boiled over. "He's my Omega!"

"But you haven't even marked him yet," Shen Feizhi remarked casually. "Your Omega was quite good… but now he's mine."

Shen Wei: "……"

He was utterly flabbergasted. "Have you no shame?"

How could he say that with such self-righteousness?!

Shen Feizhi desperately wanted to break Shen Wei's fingers, but unfortunately, he wasn't wearing gloves. He considered touching Shen Wei's hand disgusting, so he abandoned the idea, instead raising his eyes to gesture toward the figure behind Shen Wei.

"I'd advise you to rein it in a bit. The Old Master's here."

Elder Shen commanded immense respect. At that single warning, Shen Wei dropped his hand and turned around obediently. "Grandpa."

"Hmm." Elder Shen didn't even glance at him, his slightly clouded eyes fixed on Shen Feizhi. "Come with me. I need to speak with you."

Shen Wei stared at them in astonishment. "Grandpa! You—"

"Shut your mouth," Elder Shen snapped, tapping his cane sharply on the ground. "Liao Chen, take Xiao Wei back. Tonight is the Shen family's event; we can't have no one running things."

"Grandpa, what could you possibly have to say to him?" Shen Wei tried to shake off Liao Chen's grasp, but it was futile. Liao Chen had been Elder Shen's right-hand man for years, a man of considerable skill and influence.

Forced to take a few steps forward, Shen Wei glared back resentfully. "Shen Feizhi, you fucking bastard! So what if you married Lin Zhou? Do you think the Lin family is still what it used to be? They've long since—"

"Shen Wei!"

Elder Shen finally rebuked him, his voice laced with anger, "Watch your tongue!"

Shen Wei had no chance to retort as Liao Chen forcibly dragged him away.

In the courtyard, only Shen Feizhi and Elder Shen remained.

Elder Shen gazed at Shen Feizhi, who stood before him with composed grace and dignified bearing, and suddenly found himself speechless. Compared to this man, his legitimate grandson seemed more like an illegitimate child unfit for public display.

Gu Suizhou must be waiting for me somewhere , Shen Feizhi thought, urging, "Since it's just the two of us here, Grandpa, let's cut to the chase."

The Old Master sighed. "Do you remember what I told you before about taking responsibility for Lin Zhou?"

"I remember," Shen Feizhi replied. "Isn't that exactly what I'm doing? I'm already seriously developing our relationship."

But Elder Shen's reaction wasn't the relief one might expect; his eyes held disapproval. "You… you should break things off with him."

The air around them instantly grew deathly still.

As if avoiding something, Elder Shen averted his gaze after speaking those words, shifting his eyes away from Shen Feizhi's.

Shen Feizhi stood motionless, adjusting his collar. "Reason?"

"Because the Shen family needs you," Elder Shen declared, his tone leaving no room for argument. "Shen Feizhi, you're not beyond redemption. If you're willing, the doors of the Shen family will always be open to you. Our family's legacy will undoubtedly have a place for you."

This sudden shift in attitude was… interesting.

"What's the catch?" he asked.

"There's no catch," Elder Shen said, as if reassuring him. "Just don't marry that Lin family brat. Xiao Zhi, the Shen and Lin families have some old grudges dating back to the previous generation. The two of you aren't well-matched; it wouldn't work. When you return, I'll arrange a more suitable marriage partner for you."

In other words, they just wanted to arrange a political marriage for him?

Old man, Shen Feizhi thought, you might as well just say the Shen family was involved in the deaths of Gu Suizhou's parents back then.

Just as he raised his eyes, a commotion erupted ahead.

Leaning to the side to peer past Elder Shen, he saw Gu Suizhou surrounded by a crowd of people.

You little bastard! This is exactly how the original plot starts with you getting… used!

Shen Feizhi could no longer afford to play along with Elder Shen. "I need time to consider this."

His answer wasn't exactly what Elder Shen wanted, but it was barely acceptable. "One month. I'll wait for your answer."

Shen Feizhi nodded. "But Grandpa, if I agree, can I have some say in choosing my marriage partner?"

Elder Shen asked curiously, "What's this? Someone here tonight caught your eye?"

"Not exactly." Shen Feizhi fidgeted with his fingers, his tone suggestive. "It's just been a while since I saw Miss Lu Qian. I noticed she's become even more beautiful than when we were children."

Elder Shen froze for a moment before understanding dawned. "I'll consider it."

He had initially planned to wait obediently by the door for Shen Feizhi, but before his target arrived, he was ambushed by another group.

It all started when several Omegas, irritated by their partners' lingering gazes, came looking for trouble.

But after being repeatedly silenced by Gu Suizhou's legendary silver tongue, they called in their Alphas and Betas for backup.

One Omega kept rambling incessantly, not pausing once from start to finish: "Honestly, with that fox-like face, he probably just snagged some sleazy sugar daddy."

"Exactly!" another chimed in. "He's definitely no match for my Lao Li—six-pack abs and all!"

"My Yao-shushu has those too! And a V-line!"

Gu Suizhou: "……"

Can't you guys have your little male competition without bothering me? Is comparing men something to be proud of? Shouldn't you be competing against yourselves before you have any right to talk? Can't these Omegas have some self-respect?

He didn't understand. He really, truly didn't understand.

What he understood even less was why those foul-smelling Alphas, who were being praised, still carried themselves with such arrogant "fatherly" airs!

He wasn't even competing. If he were to compete…

"My Big Brother Lu can blow perfect smoke rings!"

My brother's a Top Tier Alpha—tattooed with Azure Dragons and White Tigers, earns eight figures monthly, owns a mansion and luxury cars, and can down twenty bottles of Soju in one go without breaking a sweat!"

His words plunged the entire room into silence.

Shen Feizhi, who had just arrived, missed the best part. Staring at the deathly quiet scene, he hesitated, "What… what's going on here?"

Seeing him, Gu Suizhou's heart suddenly felt hollow with dread. "Br-Brother Shen."

Before Shen Feizhi could even respond, dozens of eyes, filled with either murderous intent or stunned admiration, turned toward him.

Gu Suizhou scurried over, clinging affectionately to his arm with a hint of feigned sincerity. "Feizhi-gege~"

"Get lost. Stop being clingy, you're creeping me out." Shen Feizhi shoved him away, momentarily stunned. "What are they…?"

"Oh?" one of the men with vividly colored hair chuckled theatrically. "Twenty bottles for you , huh?"

Shen Feizhi blinked, confused. "Who…?"

"Cut the act! Come on, pour him a drink! Tonight, whoever passes out first is a goddamn loser!"

Originally, they hadn't intended to get involved. But the moment this man arrived, their Omegas started staring at him like hungry wolves eyeing fresh meat. They couldn't possibly lose face like that!

Besides, this pretty boy had already struck a blow to their Alpha and Beta pride.

Gu Suizhou blinked innocently, forced out a dry, exaggerated laugh, and stammered an explanation: "I, uh, I was just bluffing… I didn't expect you to actually show up right then."

Shen Feizhi gritted his teeth. "Gu Suizhou, you—"
