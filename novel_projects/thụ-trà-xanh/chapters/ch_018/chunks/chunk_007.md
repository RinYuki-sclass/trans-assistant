Shen Feizhi took a deep breath.
Gu Suizhou pressed on, grabbing the wine glass from his hand. He gathered his courage and declared, "Look, don't worry. I'll drink it. I'll take them on. You—"
"Oh, chickening out? Going to let your little darling drink for you?"

"Pfft! Just a pretty face with no guts, huh?"
"Weakling!"
Shen Feizhi sighed, finally understanding why Gu Suizhou was so determined to drink.
A man's pride was indeed more important than the heavens.
He could relate to that.
With a single hand, Shen Feizhi stopped Gu Suizhou from bringing the glass to his lips. Then, right there, before Gu Suizhou's stunned gaze, he drained the entire cup in one gulp.
He tilted his head back slightly, and a single drop of wine, spilled from his hasty gulp, slid down his sharply defined jawline.
As if compelled by some unseen force, Gu Suizhou reached out with his thumb and wiped away the drop.
He suddenly regretted wearing gloves.
"Fuck, keep going—"
"No need to continue," Shen Feizhi interrupted the clamor with a scowl. His slender fingers curled, and he yanked off Gu Suizhou's necktie, tossing it to him. "Tie it around my wrist."
"Drinking won't solve the root problem. Let's settle this with our fists."
An hour later.
"Holy shit, Brother Shen, you were fucking awesome out there!"
If he hadn't reacted quickly and pulled Shen Feizhi away from the brawl, the Shen family would have needed at least three ambulances today.
