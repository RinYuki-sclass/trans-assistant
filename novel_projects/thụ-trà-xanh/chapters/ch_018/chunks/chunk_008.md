Of course, none of them would have been for Shen Feizhi.
People's eyes always follow the strong, especially sixteen- and seventeen-year-olds who naturally admire power.
This was the first time Gu Suizhou had genuinely praised Shen Feizhi.
Shen Feizhi's reaction was utterly indifferent: "Mm."
"You're so calm," Gu Suizhou remarked, impressed. "You must have gotten into a lot of fights before, huh? Ah, good thing you wrapped your tie around your hand. Otherwise, you'd definitely have gotten a cut."
Shen Feizhi remained equally aloof: "Mm."
"Brother, I really think you're seriously badass…"
"Brother Shen."
"You fought them for me, didn't you~?" Since Shen Feizhi hadn't laid a hand on him, Gu Suizhou lost all restraint. His little fang gleamed as he eagerly courted a scolding, "You love me so much~"
Shen Feizhi: "Mm."
Gu Suizhou's smile froze. "Huh?"
Shen Feizhi suddenly stopped walking.
"Gu Suizhou."
