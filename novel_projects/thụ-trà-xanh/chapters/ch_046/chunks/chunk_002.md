Shen Feizhi said to C2, "Once he wakes up, I’m taking him to live elsewhere. You do as you please."
C2 cried out, "Shen Feizhi! How can you be such a person who abandons friends for a pretty face!"
“It’s not choosing sex over friendship,” Shen Feizhi said. “Or would you rather see him go mad again?”
The kid was clearly beyond reason and refused to listen to explanations; he was in a completely manic state, ready to snap at anyone he saw.
C2’s ears drooped. “Fine. I’ll go arrange a car for you. Where are you staying?”
“Don’t know. We’ll decide once he wakes up. Go back to your room first. Don’t come out until we’ve left.”
Gritting his teeth, C2 shot Gu Suizhou a resentful glare before slinking away.
When Gu Suizhou opened his eyes, the first thing he saw was Shen Feizhi sitting by the open window, smoking and scrolling through his smartphone.
Like a heat-seeking missile, Gu Suizhou sat up and silently shuffled over to the man’s side, cautiously tugging at his sleeve.

“Big Brother, are you angry?”
Seeing him approach, Shen Feizhi snubbed out the remaining half of his cigarette. “If I were angry, I wouldn’t be sitting here waiting for you to wake up.”
Since he wasn't angry, Gu Suizhou grew bolder. He shamelessly rested his head on the man’s shoulder. “Then explain.”
Shen Feizhi: “?”
Gu Suizhou: “Explain your relationship with that guy. Who is he? What’s his deal? How do you know each other? The two of you…”
"Stop," Shen Feizhi interrupted coldly, pushing the other’s head away. "Who do you think you're interrogating?"
Gu Suizhou’s cheeks puffed out. "You abandoned me all alone for over four years! Don't you feel even the tiniest, tiniest, tiniest bit bad for me!?"
He knew Shen Feizhi must have remembered the past; otherwise, he wouldn't be this indulgent.
Most people with a conscience would avoid the subject in a situation like this, refusing to exploit the other person’s guilt and sympathy. But Gu Suizhou was different—he simply had no conscience.
He was going to milk this favor for all it was worth, throw a royal tantrum, and raise absolute hell!
