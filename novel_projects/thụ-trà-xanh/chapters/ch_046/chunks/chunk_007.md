Shen Feizhi asked, “Where did you buy a place?”
“It’s not exactly close. You can sleep for a bit.”
Shen Feizhi was a little curious.
Gu Suizhou should be quite wealthy now; he hadn't bought a house in the First District?
Soon, his confusion was answered.
Looking at the high-rise that looked no different from before, Shen Feizhi felt a slight tightness in his chest.
Gu Suizhou, on the other hand, acted perfectly natural, helping him carry his luggage. “Let’s go. All your things are still here, no need to prepare anything else.”
When he said everything was still there, he really meant not a single thing was missing.
For a split second, Shen Feizhi felt as if he had only been gone for a day or two.
With a complicated mix of emotions, he pushed the door open to enter the bedroom.
“Wait!” Gu Suizhou suddenly popped up behind him, his hand quickly bracing against the door.
Shen Feizhi glanced back sideways. “Did you do something bad in my room?”
"No." Gu Suizhou's fingers curled inward, his gaze flickering. "I… I made a mess yesterday. Let me go in and clean it up."
His chest was pressed tightly against Shen Feizhi's back. Unaccustomed to the proximity, Shen Feizhi ducked to the side to avoid him and jerked his chin toward the door. "Fine, go ahead."
Gu Suizhou hadn't expected him to be so easy to talk to. He pushed the door open in surprise—
