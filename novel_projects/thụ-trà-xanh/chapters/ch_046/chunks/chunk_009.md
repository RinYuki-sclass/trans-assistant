Shen Feizhi slammed the door shut with a loud thud .
The next day, as soon as Shen Feizhi woke up, he arranged to meet with Xu Zhong.
Xu Zhong was still fully masked, face hidden behind sunglasses and a surgical mask. To avoid drawing attention, Shen Feizhi met him in a private booth.
He cut straight to the chase. "What exactly is going on with Gu Suizhou's body? You still haven't figured out a way for him to differentiate normally?"
Xu Zhong gave him a strange look. "Didn't President Gu tell you? The medicine has actually been developed; he's just refusing to use it."
The piece of fish between Shen Feizhi’s chopsticks slipped back onto the plate. "Refusing? Why?"
"Well…" Xu Zhong put it delicately. "The doctor said that President Gu's differentiation this time will be extremely intense. Ordinary drugs won't be able to suppress it. Whether he becomes an Alpha or an Omega, if there isn't someone there to help him… he likely won't make it through. He's unwilling to do that sort of thing with someone he has no emotional foundation with. You understand."
Shen Feizhi was so exasperated he actually laughed. "A warrior of pure love?"

He hadn't seen that coming. The brat who couldn't keep his hands off him every day turned out to be such a pure-hearted little fledgling.
Xu Zhong was also somewhat helpless. "I've tried to convince him too, but it’s no use. Mr. Shen, he listens to you. Please, try to talk some sense into him."
After Xu Zhong left, Shen Feizhi sat alone facing a table full of nearly untouched food, his appetite completely gone.
So he had to be both father and mother to him, and now he had to be the brat’s matchmaker too?
And he wouldn’t do it with someone he didn't have an emotional foundation with, yet there was no telling what he would differentiate into—how was Shen Feizhi supposed to help him find a blind date?
It wasn't as if he could cultivate feelings with an Alpha and an Omega at the same time, then pick one out of the two once he differentiated, right?
That would be way too unethical!
Shen Feizhi was annoyed to death, but remembering what Xu Zhong said about how the longer this dragged on, the more dangerous it might become, he knew the matter had to be resolved.
He simply placed a call to C2: "Come out tonight. I'm taking you to Feiya."
Feiya, the largest nightclub on Blue Star.
Though it was called a nightclub, it wasn't "dirty"; it was so clean and high-end that it was almost an insult to the word.
