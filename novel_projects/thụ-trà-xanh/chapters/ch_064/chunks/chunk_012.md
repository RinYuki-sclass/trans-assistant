At first, Shen Feizhi just listened half-heartedly, but since a certain someone had already started sulking and radiating a heavy atmosphere, he had to urge, "Is there anything else?"
"Ah, are you busy right now?"
"Yeah." Shen Feizhi leaned lazily back into the sofa chair, prying Gu Suizhou’s averted head back toward him. "Busy smoothing out some ruffled fur."
"Oh, that dog of yours." C2 understood. "Then I won't bother you. Be nicer to your pup; pets are very healing. Don't keep a cold face all day."
Shen Feizhi gave a short laugh. "Alright, got it."
As if he’d heard their conversation, Lucky Dog wagged his tail and barked as he trotted around.
Gu Suizhou shot the dog a glare, then buried his face into Shen Feizhi’s shoulder with a wounded look. "Is chatting with him fun? Which Imperial Noble Consort made you smile so happily? Have I fallen out of favor?"
From being the most favored to becoming a has-been in just a few minutes—life's joys and sorrows were truly nothing more than this.
Shen Feizhi kissed his ear. "No. You're the Empress."
Gu Suizhou wanted to play up the drama a bit more, but he discovered to his despair that he had actually been coaxed into a good mood.
He lifted his head again. "Bro, kiss—"
“Gu Suizhou.” Shen Feizhi had a keen sense of smell. Catching a glimpse of something out of the corner of his eye, his expression darkened. “Your son took a shit in the bedroom.”
Gu Suizhou: “…”
Was everyone and everything more important than getting a kiss from him?!
For the first time ever, he felt that the name ‘Dog Shit Luck’ was incredibly cursed!
With a black expression, he got up and silently dealt with the mess.
Shen Feizhi leaned against the balcony door with a look of pure disgust, watching him work.
Usually, Gu Suizhou would have been disgusted too, but right now his temper was flaring so hot that he’d lost all reason—he didn't even care about the filth.
Wrapped in an insulating aura of low pressure, he threw out the dog shit and the dog named Luck. Then, with exaggeratedly loud movements, he sat cross-legged on the bed with his arms folded.
