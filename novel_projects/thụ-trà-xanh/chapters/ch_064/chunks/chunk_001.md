Chapter 64
"Shen Feizhi, isn't it?"
The man sat with Shen Feizhi in a private room of a tea house, helping himself to an order of the most expensive tea on the menu.
Shen Feizhi gave a flick of sarcasm. "Do you have the money for this place?"
The man’s hand paused slightly, but he remained composed. "Are you not planning to treat me?"
Shen Feizhi smiled, though the mirth didn't reach his eyes.
The meaning was clear: Not a chance.
The man sighed, stubbornly insisting, "I've already ordered it."
Shen Feizhi lowered his eyelids, neither agreeing to pay nor refusing. "Get to the point. I'm in a hurry."
The man thought it over, but ultimately still poured the tea. He cleared his throat and said solemnly, "You've already figured out my identity?"
"Why else do you think I got out of the car?" Shen Feizhi looked up. "Do I look like someone with time to kill?"
The man: "…"
Why was this guy's every sentence like a thorn?
Actually, under normal circumstances, Shen Feizhi wasn't usually this hostile. It just so happened that his current mood could hardly be described as good.
Anyone who got interrupted by some nonsensical occurrence at a moment like that would likely be fuming.

The man coughed and poured him a cup of tea. "I'm curious, how did you figure it out?"
"Blue Star traffic police aren't so blind that they'd ignore a living soul standing right in the middle of a motor vehicle lane." Shen Feizhi twirled the teacup with his fingertips, though he didn't drink. "Besides, C2 mentioned he has a superior—six feet tall, buzz cut, thick eyebrows, big eyes, a square face, and a high bridge to his nose. Most notably, he said the man has very thin lips, likely worn down by how often he opens his mouth to dock people's wages."
His gaze swept rudely across the man’s face. "Your reputation precedes you. I find myself suddenly thinking C2’s command of the language is actually quite good."
The man’s face darkened visibly. The teacup gripped in his hand wobbled precariously, making one worry it might shatter at any moment.
