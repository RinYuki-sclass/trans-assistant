Since the system had him return at such a time, it was inevitably because if he weren't there, the World Nodes would cease to appear from that moment forward.
From the very first moment Gu Suizhou laid eyes on the imposter, he was never going to fall in love with him.
He would certainly find out who his savior was in the future, but even so, it wouldn't be enough to reach the critical node.
The change in this man only occurred after Shen Feizhi's return; what made him do this was never the Child of Destiny who held the debt of saving his life.
So, Gu Suizhou had not followed the World’s Will to love "Shen Feizhi."
He had defied the World’s Will to love only Shen Feizhi.
Combined with what C2 had previously mentioned about the world’s rules making compromises, Shen Feizhi became even more certain of this conclusion.
He wouldn't doubt the sincerity of this man’s emotions, nor would he distance himself in silence through internal strife as K7 had imagined. Even if something were wrong, he would verify it personally and speak his mind.
Shen Feizhi detested the kind of relationship where people kept their mouths shut; that was nothing more than self-indulgent sentimentality.
He liked the way Gu Suizhou said whatever was on his mind.
