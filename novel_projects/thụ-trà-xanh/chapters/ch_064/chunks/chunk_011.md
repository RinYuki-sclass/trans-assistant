It was just a belated greeting from Leapton after seeing the morning news.
Gu Suizhou watched him take the call with a darkened expression.
As Shen Feizhi chatted, he reached out to stroke Gu Suizhou’s hair soothingly.
With great difficulty, he finally hung up, but before Gu Suizhou could even get a word in, another call immediately followed.
Shen Feizhi glanced at it and answered once more: "C2."
"Shen!" C2’s frantic voice came through. "My boss is fucking out of his mind today don't take a single word he said to heart!"
Look at that—the kid was so worked up he’d even stopped using punctuation.
Shen Feizhi’s voice held a faint hint of amusement. "Mm. Don't worry, I didn't take it to heart. I know."
He knew C2 and A11 meant well.
To be honest, if Shen Feizhi had known about this from the very beginning, he really might not have continued to associate with Gu Suizhou.
He was the type to prefer avoiding trouble whenever possible, and he certainly would have steered clear of the hassle from the start.
In a way, he actually owed these two a favor.
C2 said oddly, "…You know everything? That was fast."
He even knew about Gu Suizhou’s pheromones?
Shen Feizhi assumed he was talking about the World Nodes and replied, "Mm. It's not a big deal."
C2 was even more shocked. That wasn't a big deal?! He really was a man who had seen it all.
He said dryly, "Oh, well, that’s good then. That’s good."
C2 didn't hang up, instead babbling a bunch of other nonsensical, meaningless junk.
