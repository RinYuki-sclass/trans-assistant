He changed his shoes, took off his coat, and washed his hands before heading up.
Sure enough, Gu Suizhou was out on the open balcony outside the bedroom, lounging in a sofa chair, watching the nebulae with Lucky Dog.
He flicked Lucky Dog’s ear. "I bet your stepdad will be back before ten."

Lucky Dog let out two cooperative barks.
Gu Suizhou kept one hand on the dog while the other held a glass of shochu, already half-finished. He murmured to himself, "But what if he doesn’t come back? Why are there always so many people with ill intentions around him?"
Lucky Dog barked again.
Gu Suizhou clicked his tongue. "It’s fine. If he doesn't come back, I’ll go find him myself, knock him unconscious, and…"
“How about knocking me out?”
“!” Gu Suizhou jumped in fright. His hand, which had been pinching Lucky Dog’s ear, clenched too hard. The dog let out a sharp “Awooo,” gave him a kick, and scrambled over to Shen Feizhi’s side.
Shen Feizhi ruffled the dog’s head and said, his words clearly directed at someone else: “Bold as brass, aren't we?”
Gu Suizhou: “…”
He stood up abruptly, yet he didn’t pounce over as he usually did. “You're back?”
“Mm.” Seeing him looking like he was hesitating to speak, Shen Feizhi took the initiative. “That was a leader from C2.”
Gu Suizhou didn't look particularly surprised; he had likely guessed as much.
With his hands clasped and fingers twisting behind his back, he pursed his lips and asked, “What did he want with you?”
“To poach me,” Shen Feizhi said succinctly, not hiding it from him.
But he left it at that, not telling him anything more.
