Satisfied?
Fuck, he was more than goddamn satisfied.
“Shen Feizhi, you really are…” Gu Suizhou’s cheeks tensed as he clenched his jaw, trying to spit out some word of condemnation.
But after searching high and low, he found he couldn't bear to say anything too harsh, only managing to choke out four words: “You’re too much!”
Shen Feizhi patted the back of his head and laughed for quite a while.
It was the first time Gu Suizhou had seen him laugh so effortlessly, and he dithered for a moment.
It was beautiful. None of his usual coldness or aloofness remained; the curve of his eyes was gentle, and in those pitch-black pupils, aside from the nebula behind him, only two tiny reflections of Gu Suizhou remained.
The deep, hidden depths were no longer invisible; they had been dominantly claimed by him.
Gu Suizhou’s Adam’s apple bobbed as he leaned in, unable to help himself.
Shen Feizhi released his hold on Gu Suizhou’s hands, a silent, indulgent permission for him to draw closer.
It was at that exact, inopportune moment that the comms rang.
Neither of them had intended to answer, but just as Gu Suizhou grabbed the smartphone to toss it aside, Shen Feizhi caught a glimpse of the caller ID on the light screen.
"Wait, it’s Leapton." He immediately stopped the other man and took the phone to answer. "Good evening."
Shen Feizhi knew how much Leapton meant to Gu Suizhou, and the potential "ending" Leapton might have faced still left him with a lingering sense of dread. Naturally, he had to handle this with caution.
