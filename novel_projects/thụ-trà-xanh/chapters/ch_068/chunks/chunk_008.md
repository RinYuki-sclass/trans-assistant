Gu Suizhou scrambled to explain, his fingers typing so fast they were a blur of afterimages.
Only after the chat interface was filled with a dense forest of his green message bubbles did he finally receive a single word from Shen Feizhi—【Oh.】
What the hell did "oh" mean?
Gu Suizhou stared at that character, wishing he could turn back time.
In reality, he was overthinking it. Shen Feizhi didn't mean anything by it.
He knew exactly what kind of person Gu Suizhou was; the possibility of him wanting a child was zero, so the moment the question left his mouth, he had already dismissed it.
Since it wasn't about wanting a kid, then there had to be some other underlying meaning behind this sudden spree of links.
He couldn't even be bothered to poke holes in that clumsy excuse about a junior classmate.
Shen Feizhi was completely lacking the self-awareness that he had nearly believed it himself at the start.
So, what exactly were these nonsensical links hinting at?
He still hadn't figured it out by the time he got off work and went to the company to pick the guy up.
"Yo, President… Shen!" Leapton had been helping audit invoices all day and had only just finished. Seeing him, he looked a bit surprised. "Here to pick up President Gu from work?"
"Yeah." Shen Feizhi glanced at his watch. "It’s almost six. You’re just getting off work?"
"Yeah," Leapton said, rubbing his aching wrists. "It's the end of the month, time to pay the wage slaves. It's exhausting."
