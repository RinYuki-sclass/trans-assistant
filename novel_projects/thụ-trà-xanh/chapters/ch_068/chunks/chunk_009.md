"Nothing, nothing." Leapton waved his hands hurriedly.
He dared to mess around and joke in front of Gu Suizhou, but he didn't dare act out in front of Shen Feizhi.
How to put it? He felt that a man like Mr. Shen was simply untainted by worldly desires; it wasn't outside the realm of possibility for Old Gu to spend the rest of his life in a room alone, living out a purely Platonic romance.
Terrified his mouth would blurt out something else outrageous, Leapton stammered a goodbye and vanished like a puff of smoke.
Shen Feizhi watched his departing back, stroking his chin thoughtfully.
After waiting downstairs for a while without seeing any sign of Gu Suizhou, and receiving no reply to his messages, he thought about it and decided to go upstairs to find him.
When the people in the Secretariat saw him arrive, they didn't even wait for him to speak. One took the initiative to say, "President Gu just finished work and went to change. Are you going to see him?"
Shen Feizhi replied, "No rush. I'll wait in his office for a while."
Gu Suizhou's lounge and office were on different floors, one above the other; he was too lazy to climb an extra flight of stairs.
"Alright," the secretary said. "We'll let him know in a bit."
Shen Feizhi nodded. "Thanks."
