It was over.
Not only was this total social death, but he was definitely going to get beaten up again.
His terminal chimed twice.
It was Shen Feizhi.
【Husband~: Get in here.】
Gu Suizhou: "……"
With that brook-no-argument tone, he was doomed.
He took a deep breath, cautiously pushed open a sliver of the door, and peered inside: "Sh-Shen-ge."
"What are you acting so cowardly for?" Shen Feizhi crooked two fingers at him, beckoning. "You have the guts to do it, but not the guts to own up to it?"
Gu Suizhou gave a couple of sheepish laughs, hesitating and shrinking back as he took two steps forward and one step back. "I just thought about it, just thought about it…"
Shen Feizhi watched him with casual interest. "Thought about what?"
Gu Suizhou took a sip of water to mask his panic. "Noth—"
Shen Feizhi: "Thinking about topping me?"
Gu Suizhou: "Pfft—"
He choked violently, leaning on the table as he looked up in disbelief. "You… what did you say?"
"Is that not it?" Shen Feizhi kindly handed him a tissue. "Otherwise, why wrack your brains to give me all those hints?"
Gu Suizhou’s silver tongue instantly got tied in knots. "Sss, I indeed wanted to… No, wait…"
Stumbling and stuttering, with darting eyes and flushing ears, his behavior made it seem as if Shen Feizhi were the one acting like a rogue.
