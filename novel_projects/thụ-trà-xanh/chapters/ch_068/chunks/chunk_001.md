Chapter 68
"Morning, Old Gu."
Leapton pushed open the door to the W.N CEO's office, carrying a stack of magnetic cards.
He had always been in charge of the company's data; at the end of every month, the employees relied on his records to determine their salaries and bonuses.
This job allowed for a lot of flexibility and could usually be done from an internet cafe. Leapton only needed to come in once at the end of the month to report.
As soon as he entered, he saw Gu Suizhou propping up his head, looking weary and listless. "You don't seem to be in very high spirits today," he noted curiously.
"Yeah." Gu Suizhou pinched the bridge of his nose. "Just leave the stuff there."
Leapton made himself at home and poured a glass of juice. "What happened? You look like you've overindulged in carnal pleasures."
At the mention of this, Gu Suizhou's temper flared. "I asked you to find me a movie with 'atmosphere.' What kind of film did you give me?"
"What? The one I gave you was an art house film that won awards at the end of last year," Leapton said, feeling wrongly accused.
"Bullshit." Gu Suizhou frowned. "In your 'art house' films, the male and female leads fight each other without any clothes on?"
Leapton blinked, processing that for two seconds.
Suddenly, a thought struck him, and he slapped his thigh. "You didn't just open it, see that, and think it was some X-rated movie, did you? Give me a break, man. Plenty of arthouse films have scenes like that nowadays. It's only in the first minute of the movie—after that, it's so pure there isn't even any kissing!"
Gu Suizhou didn't want to believe it. "Who are you trying to kid?"
