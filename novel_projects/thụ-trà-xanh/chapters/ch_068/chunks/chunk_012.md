Shen Feizhi arched an eyebrow. "Why are you in such a panic? Did I say you couldn't?"
Gu Suizhou froze. "…"
His eyes went wide, and his mouth fell open in shock. "Huh??"
What "couldn't" he?
Was that a yes or a no?
The implications between those words were far too rich; he simply couldn't process it all at once.
He clearly knew how to read every single character, yet when they were strung together, they made him feel like an illiterate.
Like an infinitely patient mentor, Shen Feizhi repeated himself word for word: "If you want to, it’s not out of the question."
Gu Suizhou had been mistaken. Shen Feizhi’s notions and opinions regarding such matters were thin to begin with; he didn’t care at all about being the top or the bottom. One way or another, it was all just rolling around in bed—what difference did it make?
He didn't think being on the bottom was any more stifling or humiliating than being on top. Pleasure was pleasure, wasn't it?
If Gu Suizhou truly wanted to be on top, all he really had to do was act a little spoiled or roll around a bit, and Shen Feizhi would actually have nodded in agreement quite easily.
But seeing how cautious and wary the man was being, Shen Feizhi suddenly didn't want to agree so readily anymore.
He admitted he was truly wicked; seeing Gu Suizhou in such a state of anxiety was simply too entertaining.
Closing his left hand slightly and resting it on the table, his fair wrist bone peeking out from the shirt cuff against the edge of the wood, Shen Feizhi raised a slender index finger and tapped the table with the pad of his finger. "Come closer."
Drawn by the faint tapping sound, Gu Suizhou lowered his eyelids.
The moment his gaze touched that hand, the scene in the car from that day resurfaced in his mind. The difference was that the hand hadn't been wearing a watch back then, and those calloused fingertips…
Thump, thump.
The index finger tapped twice more.
It was Shen Feizhi's impatient warning: "Gu Suizhou."
"I hear you," Gu Suizhou answered softly. He reached out to close the floating light screen and obediently circled the table, coming to a halt and standing straight in front of him.
