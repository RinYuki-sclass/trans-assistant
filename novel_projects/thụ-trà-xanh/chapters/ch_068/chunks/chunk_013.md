One was sitting and the other was standing, yet the one standing possessed no aura of authority whatsoever.
With his head hanging low in grievance, he looked exactly like someone who had done something wrong.
But looking closer, those eyes, half-shrouded by long lashes, were brimming with aggression—nothing even remotely close to "well-behaved."
Shen Feizhi couldn't be bothered to call out his half-hearted acting. When his neck grew tired from looking up, he gave the other man’s knee a kick.
Once Gu Suizhou half-knelt, Shen Feizhi finally deigned to hook a finger under his chin, tilting it toward him. "Did I not tell you before? If you want something, just say it. There’s no need for these convoluted schemes."
"Anything I want?" Gu Suizhou’s left leg lunged forward as he dropped to his knees, straightening his torso to wedge himself between Shen Feizhi’s knees. He reached out to wrap his arms around the older man's waist, burying his head against the crook of his neck. "Even getting into bed?"
"Before, yes. But not now." With one hand propped against the armchair’s rest to support his temple, Shen Feizhi used the other to idly stroke the younger man’s hair. "A reward for doing something wrong? Since when did you become so insatiable?"
Gu Suizhou knit his brows and gave Shen Feizhi’s collarbone a vengeful nip. "Then give me a chance to make up for it?"
Shen Feizhi remained silent.
Gu Suizhou grew a little frantic. "Brother Shen."
Shen Feizhi still didn't respond.
"Big brother~"
"Husband!"
Shen Feizhi grit his teeth, suppressing a laugh. "Fine. Beg me."
Gu Suizhou’s grip tightened. "Beg you for what?" he asked expectantly.
It was just a matter of opening his mouth and begging; the requirement was simply too easy.
He was shameless enough to say anything, anyway.
Shen Feizhi seemed to see right through him. The hand resting on the back of Gu Suizhou's head suddenly tightened, grabbing his hair to force his chin up, bringing his left ear—and its stud—flush against Shen's lips.
"Beg me to speak the words," his voice was low, as calm as still water. "To let you take me."
