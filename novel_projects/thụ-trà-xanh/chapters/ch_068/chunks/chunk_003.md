But as he lay back in his armchair, Leapton’s question from a moment ago kept looping in his head.
【Which of you is on top, and which is on the bottom?】
That really did seem like a major issue they’d overlooked. They’d have to face it sooner or later, right?
He’d seen plenty of similar novels on Lai Gou's smartphone before. The protagonists in them would fight tooth and nail over who got to be on top; some even came to blows, neither willing to be the one underneath.
Applying that to himself and Shen Feizhi, they’d probably be at a stalemate for a long time.
Even though he called Shen Feizhi “Husband” smoothly enough, he’d still much rather be the one on top than be the one… well, that.

After all, the fantasy of being pinned didn't do much for him, but just the thought of being able to pin that man made his entire blood supply surge!
Gu Suizhou ruffled his hair.
Hmm… So, how should he go about convincing Shen Feizhi to let him be on top?
Shen Feizhi had been working at the W.N Research Institute since early that morning.
Gu Suizhou had handed over the entire scientific research department for him to manage. Ever since the incident with Ritter, the institute had laid off more than half its staff, leaving things so busy they were practically frantic.
It never would have crossed his mind what sort of unprofessional thoughts were occupying the head of the big boss surnamed Gu in the office building on the other side of the campus.
"Shen Feizhi." The Director knocked on his office door. "We recruited a batch of new employees a while back. There’s an orientation gala next Tuesday."
Shen Feizhi was aware of the event. "Have a good time."
"Tsk, are you playing dumb with me?" the Director said with dissatisfaction. "You don't understand my underlying meaning?"
How could he not understand?
But Shen Feizhi didn't really want to understand, so he spoke plainly: "I don't like these kinds of events. You should be well aware of that."
"I know you don't like them," the Director pleaded, circling around him. "But isn't this an exception? Our institute just went through such a major ordeal; this gala is also a chance to boost everyone's morale and provide some closure! You don't have to say much, we’ll do all the talking. You just need to show your face."
