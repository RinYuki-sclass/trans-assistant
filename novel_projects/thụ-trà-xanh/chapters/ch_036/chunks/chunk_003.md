Initially, Shen Wei had stubbornly refused, hurling sarcastic insults. But Shen Feizhi's next words left him completely dumbfounded.
Shen Feizhi had told him that Elder Master Shen was already preparing to transfer shares to him. If Shen Wei still wanted to inherit the family business, he'd better cooperate.
He couldn't tell if it was true or not, nor did he understand why Shen Feizhi would reveal this to him unprompted. But as he reviewed the share transfer documents and appointment papers, he had no choice but to believe it.
Shen Wei strode over with a grim expression and sat down in the chair across from him. "What exactly do you want?"
"Let me be blunt: I have no interest in the Shen family," Shen Feizhi said, pulling up recent market data on the electronic screen. "Your real rival isn't me—it's your dear cousin."
Elder Master Shen clearly disapproved of Shen Wei. He'd already begun searching among the extended family for a suitable heir.

"You're trying to drive a wedge between me and Grandpa, aren't you?" Shen Wei took the electronic screen with skepticism. As he read, the veins in his neck grew more prominent.
Shen Feizhi patiently waited for him to finish before asking, "Still think I'm trying to drive a wedge?"
Fine beads of sweat covered Shen Wei's forehead. He wasn't naive; that's precisely why he was so alarmed.
"Why come to me?"
"After all, we're blood relatives. I'd naturally choose you over a cousin I've never even met," Shen Feizhi said calmly. "I currently hold five percent of the company shares. If you agree to my request, I'll transfer them all to you unconditionally."
"What exactly do you want?" Shen Wei snapped, his patience wearing thin. He loathed people who beat around the bush without getting to the point!
Shen Feizhi lowered his gaze and said coolly, "I need you to apply for a research and development permit the moment you gain control of the Shen family. Leave the guarantor field blank; I'll fill in the name myself later."
He had considered it carefully. If the permit were signed to W.N., it would be uncertain which department would ultimately control it. Only by signing it to an individual could he ensure the drug's release.
Though Shen Wei wasn't the sharpest tool in the shed, he wasn't naive enough to take everything at face value. Wary and suspicious, he asked, "R&D? Since when did you get involved in this kind of thing? What exactly do you plan to research?"
"Just some minor projects. Not me personally, but a business partner," Shen Feizhi said with a smile. "I have no intention of meddling in the Shen family's affairs anymore. Surely you'll let me find some other way to make a living, right?"
Shen Wei remained unconvinced. "Unless you tell me exactly what it is, I can't agree. Who knows what consequences might befall the Shen family if we guarantee something like that?"
He wouldn't fall for Shen Feizhi's tricks!
