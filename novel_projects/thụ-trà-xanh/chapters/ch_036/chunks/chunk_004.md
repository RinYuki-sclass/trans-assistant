Yet Shen Feizhi showed no signs of anxiety. Instead, he casually pulled out his smartphone. "I can sign a contract with you," he said, "stating that this research won't directly affect the Shen family. Otherwise, I'll bear all the losses myself."
Of course, W.N's research wouldn't directly affect the Shen family. The real threat was the decade-old scandal involving Gu Lang and Lin Zizhuo.
But Shen Wei knew none of the details and had no chance of outmaneuvering Shen Feizhi in wordplay. Shen Feizhi was confident he could draft a contract that would deceive both Shen Wei and his lawyers.

Signing a contract meant involving the law directly.
Shen Wei wrestled with himself.
Going through legal channels posed no real problem, but something about this felt wrong.
It was too sudden, catching him completely off guard.
Shen Feizhi gave him no time to hesitate. With his finger hovering over his smartphone screen, he showed it to Shen Wei. "If you don't agree, I'll go find Shen Jiang instead."
Shen Jiang was Shen Wei's cousin.
When Shen Wei saw that Shen Feizhi had indeed found Shen Jiang's contact information, his lips paled visibly.
"N-no, at least give me some time to consider…"
Shen Feizhi ignored him. "10."
"Shen Feizhi!" Shen Wei growled.
Shen Feizhi continued counting down. "9."
Shen Wei sprang to his feet, lunging toward Shen Feizhi with a raised arm.
Shen Feizhi calmly counted down to "5."
Shen Wei: "…Can't you give me a moment to think?"
"I'm not stopping you from thinking." Shen Feizhi placed the electronic contract before him. "This contract guarantees that the research and development won't negatively impact the Shen family. It's beneficial to you, not harmful. You can sign it whenever you're ready."
