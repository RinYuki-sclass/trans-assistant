He stood his right index and middle fingers upright and made them "walk" like tiny men, step by tentative step, toward Shen Feizhi. With bated breath, he hooked his pinky around Shen Feizhi's little finger.
Shen Feizhi woke the instant his hand felt the cold touch.
He knew exactly who this was. Only one person he knew would dare such a familiar and audacious act.
Speechlessly, he turned his head and opened his eyes.
The little rascal lay there peacefully, eyes closed, completely unguarded—looking like a rabbit about to be slaughtered.
But that was just an illusion.
When this kid got worked up, even a giant Angora rabbit couldn't match his ferocity.
Shen Feizhi tugged his hand.
But the finger gripping his pinky suddenly tightened its hold, and the finger's owner furrowed his brow.
Shen Feizhi paused, then tugged again.
Gu Suizhou's long, thick eyelashes were damp, and he muttered incoherently in his sleep, restless and uneasy.
Having a nightmare?
Growing impatient, Shen Feizhi was about to yank his hand free and wake him up when the man beside him mumbled, "Bro…"
Shen Feizhi froze, stunned for a rare few seconds.

For a fleeting moment, the scene felt strangely familiar, as if he'd witnessed it before, yet impossibly distant.
His eyelids twitched involuntarily as they slowly lowered, his gaze fixing on their intertwined pinkies.
The hazy memory surfacing wasn't quite like this. His hand hadn't been loosely hooked by a pinky; instead, it had been tightly gripped in a scalding palm, the force nearly breaking his fingers.
Yet he hadn't resisted, returning the grip with gentle tenderness, as if offering reassurance.
Shen Feizhi closed his eyes, a headache throbbing behind them. He must be hallucinating.
