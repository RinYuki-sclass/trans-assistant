Chapter 16

Sunday, 5:55 PM.

Shen Feizhi passed by Gu Suizhou's bedroom door for the twentieth time.

His expression remained calm and detached, yet his footsteps were swift and silent.

Since that impulsive kiss the other night, the two hadn't interacted at all.

Shen Feizhi racked his brains trying to understand why he had acted with such… tenderness and intimacy back then.

The moment he woke up this morning, he blamed it on the world's influence warping his mind.

At 6:00 PM sharp, the alarm rang.

Shen Feizhi finally stopped pacing and knocked on Gu Suizhou's bedroom door.

After some rustling inside, Gu Suizhou hastily opened the door. "Coming, coming!"

Judging by his voice…

It seems I'm the only one who's been dwelling on what happened.

Realizing this, Shen Feizhi's expression instantly soured. He glared at the man in the doorway, his displeasure palpable. "Black suit, black tie? Why dress so old for your age? Why not wear something brighter?"

Gu Suizhou was dressed entirely in black, just like Shen Feizhi himself. The two tall men, both over 1.8 meters, standing side-by-side looked less like they were heading to a banquet and more like they were about to kick down the doors and start a brawl.

Gu Suizhou paused. "Don't you like black and white?"

Shen Feizhi's wardrobe consisted entirely of black and white clothes. Wasn't he just catering to his preferences?

The question prompted an uncharacteristic silence from Shen Feizhi, who stiffly changed the subject. "Is your leg okay?"

The leg with the minor fracture hadn't fully healed yet. To move more freely, Gu Suizhou had stubbornly removed the brace a few days earlier, only wearing it at night while sleeping.

"It's fine," he said, flexing his leg twice to demonstrate.

"Brother Shen, what's my role tonight at Shen Wei's graduation banquet?"

"You know perfectly well," Shen Feizhi replied, lowering his head to straighten his tie clip, finally forcing himself to bury the memories of that night.

Gu Suizhou tugged recklessly at his sleeve. "Tell me clearly, so I can figure out how to act later."

Having pushed him this far, Shen Feizhi finally spared him a glance.

"You're…" He recalled the term Gu Suizhou had used in front of Leapton that day, his thin lips twitching. "My kept pretty boy."

Gu Suizhou: "……"

Wait, what happened to the "My cherished, destined Omega" act we agreed on earlier?

How did we suddenly go from a genuine romantic relationship to a purely transactional, flesh-and-money affair?!

"Brother Shen, I don't think this is appropriate."

He tried to protest, but this time Shen Feizhi wasn't joking. He remained unmoved even after Gu Suizhou's throat went dry from arguing.

When they arrived at their destination and stepped out of the car, Shen Feizhi even quickened his pace to prevent Gu Suizhou from walking shoulder-to-shoulder with him.

"Get clear on your role here. What's your status compared to mine? How could we possibly walk on the same level?"

Gu Suizhou: "……"

He forced a twisted smile. "Brother Shen, you're truly something."

"Thank you for the compliment." Shen Feizhi chuckled softly, elegantly bent his right arm, and tilted his chin slightly, gesturing for Gu Suizhou to hurry up.

Suppressing his frustration, Gu Suizhou looped his white-gloved arm through Shen Feizhi's.

The gloves were Shen Feizhi's requirement. His obsessive cleanliness had reached an absurd level. Not only did he wear gloves himself, but he also forced Gu Suizhou to wear them too, refusing any physical contact without them.

That night, after the power went out, he had a period of mental fog and couldn't recall exactly what happened afterward.

All he remembered was being roughly tossed onto the bed by Shen Feizhi, who didn't even glance at him before striding into the adjacent bathroom.

The water ran for nearly three hours. The next day, the electricity bill doubled, making Gu Suizhou suspect the man was shedding his skin like a snake.

Listening to the sound of the water, he vaguely recalled Shen Feizhi… might have hugged him briefly?

Fuck! Does he despise me that much?!

Gu Suizhou's irritation grew. He surreptitiously tightened his grip on Shen Feizhi's arm.

Shen Feizhi remained unmoved, handing the invitation to the butler welcoming guests at the door.

"Uncle Liao, long time no see."

Squeeze again—

Shen Feizhi remained indifferent, not even deigning to acknowledge him. "If there's nothing else, we'll head in."

"Alright. Have a wonderful evening, you two."

I'll crush you to death—

Gu Suizhou: "……"

The Shen family placed great importance on Shen Wei, their only son. Even for his graduation banquet, Old Master Shen and Madam Shen had invited a host of big shots from various industries.

To put it bluntly, the banquet was meant to serve as a stepping stone for Shen Wei to enter society and take over the family's assets.

The banquet hadn't officially begun yet. Old Master Shen was seated in the lounge area when his gaze happened to meet Shen Feizhi's, who had just entered.

After a moment of eye contact, the Old Master nodded approvingly at him.

Knowing enough to attend the banquet shows he understands the big picture.

Gu Suizhou paid no attention to the Old Master. He whispered to Shen Feizhi, "Lin Sui is here too."

"I saw him." Shen Feizhi's expression didn't change. He nodded politely to Lin Sui before leading Gu Suizhou to the dining area.

"Once everyone arrives, we won't get a chance to eat. Let's grab something to line our stomachs first."

"Mm-hmm," Gu Suizhou said, looping his arm through Shen Feizhi's. "I want those boneless chicken wings."

Shen Feizhi gave him an odd look. "You don't need to report that to me. You have hands and feet—go get what you want."

Gu Suizhou clicked his tongue, his eyes seeming to scold: How utterly clueless are you?

"I'm your most beloved Omega now," Gu Suizhou declared, utterly shameless. "You love me so much, you have to feed me, right?"

Besides, Lin Sui was watching them.

He had to infuriate Lin Sui to death!

Shen Feizhi stared at him in stunned silence.

He hesitated for a moment. "Do all sugar daddies feed their Omegas?"

"Of course!" Gu Suizhou nudged him with his elbow. "Haven't you seen any domineering CEO novels or dramas? Feeding each other in public is basic etiquette—it's called 'showing off affection'."

Actually, he didn't quite understand it himself, but it was a common trope in the dramas Leapton made him watch every day. Even if you've never eaten pork, you've at least seen pigs running around.

Shen Feizhi nodded thoughtfully. "I guess I haven't done my homework. I'll have to brush up on this later."

"I'll help you study," Gu Suizhou said, his eyes curving into a smile as he bit into the chicken wing Shen Feizhi held out. "Brother Shen, did you read the document I sent you this morning?"

"I did," Shen Feizhi replied, tossing aside his fork and picking up a fresh one to cut his steak. "I've memorized everything."

That morning, Gu Suizhou had compiled all the guest information for the evening's gala into a document and sent it to Shen Feizhi, using the opportunity to re-add him as a friend.

This gathering included not only business titans of equal stature to the Shen family but also several high-ranking researchers from W.N.

Shen Feizhi casually scanned the crowd and, finding Ritter absent, confidently attended the event.

Among the attendees were several individuals who piqued his interest, including someone soon to join the harem of the original protagonist.

Shen Feizhi sliced off a massive chunk of steak and shoved it roughly into Gu Suizhou's mouth.

Lowering his voice, he said, "Your primary target tonight should be Shen Wei. He's been acquiring a lot of shares recently, and many of the sellers are long-standing shareholders of the Shen family. They likely know about W.N's past."

"Most of them have kept a low profile, making their identities difficult to trace. If we can obtain their transaction network IDs from Shen Wei, tracking them down will be much easier."

"I understand." Gu Suizhou swallowed the steak with difficulty, having already considered this point. "Brother Shen, I'm just a powerless nobody now. You wouldn't abandon me, would you?"

"Shen Wei's childhood sweetheart, Lu Qian, has a glandular disorder and is urgently seeking an Alpha to perform pheromone guidance."

Powerful Alphas can use their abilities to guide others through differentiation and pheromone release, regardless of whether the recipient is an Alpha or Omega.

Of course, this guidance is extremely taxing on the Alpha's energy and stamina, and careless application could even damage their body. Generally, no Alpha willingly volunteers for this task.

Gu Suizhou's brows furrowed, a hint of displeasure flickering across his face. "You want to become her guidance Alpha?"

Absolutely not! Our honey trap plan isn't even complete yet. How can we just hand him over like that?!

"No," Shen Feizhi shook his head. "My point is, if she sets her sights on me, you need to make sure you keep me firmly in your grasp—don't give her the slightest chance!"

In the original novel, Lu Qian was just a cannon fodder harem member. From every angle Shen Feizhi considered, it seemed impossible that she could be connected to any key plot points.

To avoid unnecessary trouble, he resolved to sever all ties with Miss Lu, nipping this budding romance in the bud.

A free Gu Suizhou? Might as well use him.

Annoying as he was, Gu Suizhou would make an excellent shield against unwanted advances.

Unaware of the underlying reasons, Gu Suizhou sighed in relief before asking, "Isn't it a bit premature to worry? You haven't even met her yet."

"Better safe than sorry," Shen Feizhi replied dismissively. "Enough chatter. Are you in or out?"

The banquet soon formally commenced.

Shen Wei, the evening's protagonist, was currently busy with various negotiations, so there was no rush to approach him.

Shen Feizhi and Gu Suizhou discreetly blended into the crowd, playing poker while observing their surroundings.

Shen Feizhi murmured, "How does it feel to watch your former fiancé shine so brightly?"

"We didn't even have a verbal engagement," Gu Suizhou retorted. "How can he be my former fiancé?" Initially, he had been willing to endure associating with Shen Wei to achieve his goals, even if it meant resorting to unscrupulous tactics.

But now that he'd seen Shen Feizhi's magnificent face, the mere sight of Shen Wei made him gag.

He stared curiously at Shen Feizhi's face, his mind wandering. "Brother Shen, your mother must have been a stunning beauty, right?"

"You lose." Shen Feizhi tossed down his last card, shaking his head. "My mother passed away early. I don't remember her."

This face isn't even the real Shen Feizhi's face, he thought. How would I know what the original owner's mother looked like?

Gu Suizhou let out an "Ah," then tactfully changed the subject. "Brother, that young lady beside Shen Wei keeps glancing at you."

"I noticed," Shen Feizhi replied calmly. "That's Miss Lu."

Gu Suizhou: "?"

Holy shit! She's actually got her eyes on him?

Remembering his mission, Gu Suizhou frowned and immediately squeezed closer to Shen Feizhi. "Brother Shen~ Brother Shen~"

"What?" Shen Feizhi, who detested physical closeness, swatted Gu Suizhou's hand away and tried to move away.

Lin Sui glanced at Gu Suizhou. "Nothing much. Xiao Zhou has been sulking and ran away from home recently, hasn't returned in ages. I never expected to see him attending tonight's banquet with you."

"Found him by the roadside a couple days ago," Shen Feizhi said casually, affectionately smoothing Gu Suizhou's hair. "Poor thing was squatting by the curb looking miserable, so I figured I'd bring him along for a free meal."

Gu Suizhou obediently lowered his head, making it easier for Shen Feizhi to fuss over him.

Lin Sui didn't notice their little exchange. His smile remained unchanged, though the stem of the wine glass nearly snapped in his grip.

"I see. Xiao Zhou is quite immature. Thank you, Second Young Master Shen, for looking after him these past few days," he said, nodding gratefully to Shen Feizhi. Then he pulled a small, broken wooden carving from his pocket. "Oh, Xiao Zhou, I was cleaning the house the other day and accidentally—"

"Lin Sui."

Gu Suizhou suddenly spoke, his voice barely concealing his rage. The oppressive atmosphere around him drew frequent sidelong glances from onlookers.

"Are you courting death?"

Shen Feizhi, genuinely surprised, widened his eyes, trying to get a better look at the broken wooden carving. What precious artifact could possibly provoke Gu Suizhou to drop his facade like this?

Before he could examine it properly, Gu Suizhou snatched the carving away, carefully smoothed it with his fingers, and wrapped it in a handkerchief like a priceless treasure before tucking it into his pocket.

Clenching his jaw, Gu Suizhou spoke each word deliberately: "We… need to talk elsewhere."

"Xiao Zhou, I didn't mean to," Lin Sui said, flustered, especially as more people turned to stare. He quickly stepped forward, reaching for Gu Suizhou's hand. "Don't be angry."

Gu Suizhou ruthlessly dodged his touch.

Lin Sui's eyes reddened slightly.

"In public, especially in front of… Give me some face, brother," he said, glancing awkwardly at Shen Feizhi, his meaning clear.

But Shen Feizhi acted as if he hadn't heard a word, giving Gu Suizhou's nape an ambiguous squeeze and murmuring in a low, nonchalant voice, "Control your emotions. Don't give anyone ammunition."
