Chapter 16
Sunday, 5:55 PM.
Shen Feizhi passed by Gu Suizhou's bedroom door for the twentieth time.
His expression remained calm and detached, yet his footsteps were swift and silent.
Since that impulsive kiss the other night, the two hadn't interacted at all.
Shen Feizhi racked his brains trying to understand why he had acted with such… tenderness and intimacy back then.
The moment he woke up this morning, he blamed it on the world's influence warping his mind.
At 6:00 PM sharp, the alarm rang.
Shen Feizhi finally stopped pacing and knocked on Gu Suizhou's bedroom door.
After some rustling inside, Gu Suizhou hastily opened the door. "Coming, coming!"
Judging by his voice…
It seems I'm the only one who's been dwelling on what happened.
Realizing this, Shen Feizhi's expression instantly soured. He glared at the man in the doorway, his displeasure palpable. "Black suit, black tie? Why dress so old for your age? Why not wear something brighter?"
Gu Suizhou was dressed entirely in black, just like Shen Feizhi himself. The two tall men, both over 1.8 meters, standing side-by-side looked less like they were heading to a banquet and more like they were about to kick down the doors and start a brawl.
Gu Suizhou paused. "Don't you like black and white?"
