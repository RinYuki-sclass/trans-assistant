He wasn't the original owner of the body; hearing "Second Young Master" always felt off.

His ears flushed crimson as he pursed his lips. "Alright, Feizhi."

Shen Feizhi felt like he was speaking to someone who couldn't understand plain language. "It's 'Feizhi,' my full name. Does your family not pronounce 'Shen'?"
Lin Sui: "……"
Gu Suizhou couldn't contain himself and collapsed against Shen Feizhi's shoulder, his entire body shaking with laughter.
Furious, Lin Sui pointed at him. "You!"
Shen Feizhi followed his gaze downward.
Gu Suizhou tilted his head up, two teardrops welling in his eyes as he blinked. "Brother Shen, he's so mean! I'm scared."
The corner of Shen Feizhi's mouth twitched.
What a drama queen.
But he had to play along, ruffling Gu Suizhou's hair haphazardly. "Don't worry, don't worry."
Lin Sui's chest heaved visibly.
Maintaining a strained composure, he said, "Mr. Shen, I came here to see you. Ritter told me you got promoted and have been observing here recently."
"You're too kind," Shen Feizhi said, peeling Gu Suizhou's arm off his own. "Actually, I owe this promotion to whoever reported me. If they hadn't given me the chance to prove myself to the Director, I might have been waiting years."
"Reported?" Lin Sui's feigned surprise sounded exaggerated. "You were reported?"
"Yep, yep," Gu Suizhou chimed in. "I wonder which bastard reported you~"
A vein bulged on Lin Sui's forehead.
