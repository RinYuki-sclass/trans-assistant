Despite the excruciating pain, Gu Suizhou paused for a moment, then unexpectedly burst into laughter, his amusement growing louder and more unrestrained.
It took him a long time to compose himself. Before Shen Feizhi could react, he abruptly leaned forward, maintaining his restrained posture, his head coming to rest against Shen Feizhi's chest.
The hand Shen Feizhi was gripping was twisted into an unnatural angle. Even if the bones hadn't fractured, dislocation was imminent.
As if oblivious to the pain, Gu Suizhou leaned even closer, his teeth gently tugging at the first button of Shen Feizhi's shirt. With a slight movement of his neck, he undid the button that had been tightly concealing Shen Feizhi's Adam's apple.
"Look, Ge," he said with a sly smile, "even without my hands, I can still undo it."
Shen Feizhi's exquisitely defined collarbone and sensual Adam's apple were now exposed to the air. Perhaps it was the car's excessive heating, but he didn't feel the cold.
"So you absolutely must protect yourself."
Gu Suizhou's lashes lowered gently, his warm breath ghosting across Shen Feizhi's skin. His voice was low, husky, and tender, like a lover's whisper.
