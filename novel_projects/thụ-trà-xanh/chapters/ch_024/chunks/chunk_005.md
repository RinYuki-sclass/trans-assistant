But just as their hands were about to touch, disaster struck.
The invigilator's "drinking water" was a ruse. He merely blew on the freshly poured hot water before snapping his head up.
Shen Feizhi: !
Without a moment's hesitation, he seized Gu Suizhou's hand and yanked it downward with brutal force!
The commotion of a desk and its occupant tumbling to the floor in the back row instantly drew the attention of every test-taker.
The invigilator, stunned, roared: "Gu Suizhou! What in the hell are you doing?"
Gu Suizhou sat amidst the wreckage, glaring daggers at Shen Feizhi.
Shen Feizhi met his glare without flinching, his handsome lips barely moving as he mouthed four words: Forced by circumstances.
Gu Suizhou squeezed his eyes shut.
When he looked back at the proctor, his voice dripped with barely restrained fury. "Sorry, I had a nightmare and accidentally kicked over the desk."
The proctor stared, speechless. "…You…"
He was speechless, at a loss for words to reprimand him.
Fortunately, there was still some time left in the exam. Gu Suizhou, wincing and clutching his rear, managed to right the desk and chairs and copy down most of the answers.

—Because after Gu Suizhou broke his femur during the morning history exam, he insisted that Shen Feizhi be responsible for supporting him.
Shen Feizhi disdainfully braced Gu Suizhou's arm with one hand. "You manage to get hurt this badly from a simple fall? Seventeen years old with the body of a seventy-one-year-old."
Gu Suizhou's expression teetered between a smirk and a wince. "Just a simple fall? Brother Shen, should I remind you who beat my hip black and blue last week?"
Shen Feizhi pondered for a moment, then fell silent.
Last week, after Gu Suizhou foolishly squeezed into a doorframe, Shen Feizhi had given him a beating. During the scuffle, Gu Suizhou had stumbled into a coffee table, leaving a sprawling bruise from his left waist down to his thigh—a close call that nearly landed him in the hospital.
