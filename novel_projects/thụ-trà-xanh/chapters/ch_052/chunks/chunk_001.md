Chapter 52
Shen Feizhi settled the score with C2.
In the end, C2 honestly admitted his mistake, conceding that he had indeed been the one to start the trouble, and compensated Shen Feizhi with a Main Star pass.
Shen Feizhi was somewhat puzzled. "I already have one."
"This one isn't for you, it’s for that little maniac of yours," C2 said crossly. "Didn't you notice it before? That kid was the only one unaffected by the world's rules after you left."
Shen Feizhi grew quiet.
It was true; he had discussed this point with C2 before, reaching the conclusion that "Gu Suizhou's willpower might already be strong enough to defy the world's will."
But that was merely a "might."
He didn't dare gamble Gu Suizhou's life on such an uncertain possibility.
Realizing this, Shen Feizhi suddenly felt a sense of poignancy.
In all his long years of living, this was the first time he had encountered something he "didn't dare" to do.
He sighed. "We'll see."
Despite his words, he didn't return the pass.
C2 shrugged indifferently. "Alright, I've made my apologies. Hurry back to your room; if you take any longer, that one of yours is going to go crazy."
"He isn't that petty," Shen Feizhi said.
