The first world C2 had selected involved helping the Child of Destiny of the entire Illusory Space endure twenty years of solitude.
Ten years had passed, and the Child of Destiny’s mental state had already snapped; he had self-mutilated to the point of being at death’s door. Out of necessity, the Main Star had to send someone in person to replace him for the final ten years until his body recovered.
In an empty, enclosed space, it was easy for a person to suffer a mental breakdown. Most would find an object to serve as a spiritual pillar to lean on while waiting for their return.
Ordinary items could not successfully pass through the energy field. Shen Feizhi had thought long and hard about what to bring, and ultimately decided to let Gu Suizhou leave a bite mark.
He knew better than anyone how long a scar could stay on the body. A scar and the pain of its birth were the marks least likely to be lost or forgotten.
Originally, this world hadn’t been the optimal choice, but its time flow was the fastest. Once ten years passed there, only five days would have elapsed on Blue Star.
Ten years in exchange for Gu Suizhou’s absolute safety during his secondary differentiation was a bargain.
Gu Suizhou pressed his teeth against the crook of Shen Feizhi's thumb and opened his mouth.
When Shen Feizhi met with C2 that evening, C2 looked at him with curiosity. "Did someone scam you out of your money?"
"Look at your face. It's as black as the bottom of a pot, like someone owes you eighty billion."
Shen Feizhi pinched the bridge of his nose. "No."

It was just that a certain wolf cub had turned vicious that afternoon. At the critical moment, he’d changed his mind; instead of biting Shen Feizhi's hand, he insisted on biting the back of his neck.
The area where the bandages wrapped around his gland still throbbed with a dull pain.
Gu Suizhou had originally let go as soon as he barely broke the skin, but Shen Feizhi felt that since he was already in pain, he shouldn't suffer for nothing. He had forced the boy to bite until he bled, only letting him go once he was certain the mark was deep enough.
The most exasperating part was that Gu Suizhou had stared at his bleeding neck with tears streaming down his face. If anyone else had seen it, they would have thought the scoundrel was the one who had been bitten.
He exhaled. "Let's go."
C2 nodded, started the shuttle, and sent a link message to A11.
Shen Feizhi watched the ground recede further away. From the corner of his eye, he caught a glimpse of the First District's most prominent landmark—the W.N logo—and the Third District's chocolate factory.
…Why did he suddenly feel like something bad was about to happen?
