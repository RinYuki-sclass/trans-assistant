Feeling a pang of unease, Shen Feizhi took the initiative to step forward and ruffled his hair. "Not this time."
Gu Suizhou buried his forehead into Shen Feizhi's palm. "Just twenty days. Not a day more, right?"
Shen Feizhi tried his best to be patient. "Mm."
Gu Suizhou looked up and took the initiative to let go of his hand. "Then go quickly."
Shen Feizhi was slightly surprised.
He was being this straightforward this time?
Gu Suizhou covered his eyes and turned around, his back to him. "Hurry up and go! While I’ve still got my mind made up. If you're a second later, I'm not letting you take your leave!"
Shen Feizhi pursed his lips, suppressing the curve of his mouth. He suddenly straightened up and pinched the back of the other's neck. "Turn around. Do me a favor."
Gu Suizhou turned back indignantly. "I told you, if you don't leave now, you won't be able to leave at all!"
"Are you helping or not?" Shen Feizhi asked instead of answering.
Gu Suizhou glared at him for two seconds before deflating like a punctured ball. "I’ll help," he said listlessly.
So obedient.
Shen Feizhi felt a sense of lament in his heart.
He didn't even ask what the favor was. If someone sold him off, he’d probably even help them count the money.
Fortunately, Shen Feizhi wasn't a human trafficker.

Shen Feizhi lowered his hand and placed it against Gu Suizhou's lips, commanding, "Bite."
Gu Suizhou didn't react for a moment, staring at him in shock.
“Hurry up, don’t ask why,” Shen Feizhi urged. “Bite hard enough to leave a mark—ideally, the kind that won’t fade for ten years.”
