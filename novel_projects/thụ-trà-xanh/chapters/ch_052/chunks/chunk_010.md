In the past, he had always been able to complete missions well on his own. This time, with C2 and A11 providing backup, the operation went even more smoothly.
Ten years in the first world passed in the blink of an eye. Shen Feizhi was brought back to the Main Star to undergo psychological rehabilitation.
As soon as his psychological scores barely met the requirements, he quickly entered the second world. He finished things swiftly and returned, only to spend two days in the ICU.
Once his body had recovered slightly and his remuneration was in hand, he didn't keep a single cent, transferring it all to C2.
C2 took the money and did his part, handing over the medicine without delay.
During this period, that bad premonition never manifested. For the first time, Shen Feizhi doubted his own intuition.
On the way back, he couldn't help mentioning it to C2.

C2 frowned. "Could it be that you're being too sensitive?"
"I hope so." Everyone hopes such premonitions are wrong, and Shen Feizhi was no exception. "Forget it, let's just go back first."
The agreed-upon twenty days were almost up.
C2 nodded. "Is your body okay?"
"I'm fine." Shen Feizhi's physical constitution was naturally excellent; he had only taken two bullets to the abdomen, and two days of rest were enough for him to move normally.
"Then let's head back!"
C2 pulled the shuttle's control handle, his cheerfulness lasting less than two seconds before his expression shifted abruptly. He said solemnly, "A11? What's going on? Where is the energy channel for World Number 69!"
Shen Feizhi, who had been resting with his eyes closed, snapped them open instantly.
As expected, the worst had happened.
C2 said hurriedly, “Don’t panic! A11 has already gone to investigate the cause. It doesn’t seem to be a problem with the Main Star; it’s something to do with your world’s interior. Damn, why is your world so ill-fated?”
Ill-fated?
A flash of insight suddenly crossed Shen Feizhi’s mind, and he gripped it tight. “Do you think the several accidents in World Number 69 were coincidences?”
