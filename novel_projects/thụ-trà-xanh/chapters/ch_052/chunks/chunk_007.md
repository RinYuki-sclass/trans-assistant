“Yeah, I actually do know a tattoo artist. My ex-ex-ex-ex-ex-boyfriend. If you want a tattoo, Mr. Shen, I can hook you up.”
Shen Feizhi closed his eyes for a brief moment, then said impassively, “Then I’ll trouble you to send me his contact info.”
Among the four people who had gone on this trip, none were idlers except for C2. Spending half a month away had already been a stolen moment of leisure from their hectic lives; they hadn’t been back on Blue Star for a single day before each returned to their respective posts.
After the W.N project was halted, Shen Feizhi’s work at the Research Institute became considerably lighter. Moreover, since there was no longer a need to contact the Main Star through Ritter’s team, he found himself with an abundance of time.

Gu Suizhou had carved out a management position for him at the company, and when he had nothing else to do, he would go over to help out.
Shen Feizhi’s efficiency was beyond reproach; in just one short week, he cleared a month’s worth of affairs.
Then, he ceremoniously submitted a leave application to Boss Gu.
“A month?” Gu Suizhou’s displeasure flared the moment he saw the duration. “That long?”
“I have some business to attend to with C2.” Shen Feizhi tapped the desk, cutting off any words of refusal. “I’m not consulting you; I’m informing you. You don’t have veto power over this.”
The veins on Gu Suizhou’s forehead throbbed. He endured it, then endured it some more, before finally failing to restrain himself. He offered a faint smile. “Brother Shen, don’t you think you’re getting a bit too close to him? I don’t mean anything by it, I just feel… won’t this affect your reputation?”
Shen Feizhi thought to himself: And whose fault is it that I’m forced to be around him every day?
He sighed. "It's not convenient to explain. Stop messing around. It’s only for a month. I’ll definitely be back by this time next month."
"Really?" Gu Suizhou pursed his lips. "But a month is way too long."
Shen Feizhi thought for a moment. "Then twenty days."
Time flowed differently in every world. He had specifically asked C2 to pick ones where time moved faster than in this world. By his calculations, he could finish everything in fifteen days at the earliest.
Accounting for any uncertainties, he was confident he could make it back within twenty.
Seeing his attitude, Gu Suizhou knew there was no room for negotiation.
The smile in his eyes faded until it was almost invisible, and his voice was very soft. "If you leave this time… will everyone forget you again?"
Those four-plus years had left him with too deep a psychological trauma. He couldn't handle another day of it, let alone more.
