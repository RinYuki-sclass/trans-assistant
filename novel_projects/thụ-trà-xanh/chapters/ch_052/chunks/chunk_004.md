Driven by his biological clock, Shen Feizhi woke up very early the next day.
The moment he opened his eyes, he noticed the man beside him was breathing heavily, his sleep far from peaceful.
He pulled back the quilt and saw that, as expected, the bandage on the man’s thigh had been rubbed nearly loose. The wound underneath likely wouldn't be a pretty sight either.
Such a troublemaker. He deserves to die from the pain.
Shen Feizhi thought coldly, yet his hands moved quickly as he brought over the medical kit. "Gu Suizhou, wake up. Time to change the dressing."
"Mmn…" Gu Suizhou mumbled groggily, "No… so sleepy."
Without a word, Shen Feizhi pressed down on his wound.
"Sss—ah!!" Gu Suizhou jerked from the pain. He bolted upright instantly, grabbing the medicine and heading toward the bathroom before his eyes were even fully open. "Fine, fine, fine. I’m doing it."
Watching the bathroom door close, Shen Feizhi withdrew his gaze and picked up his smartphone, planning to go downstairs for breakfast.
Then he noticed a roll of gauze sitting right next to the phone.
…Changing a dressing without bringing the gauze. He really was hopeless and scatterbrained.
Helpless, he picked it up and knocked on the bathroom door. "The gauze is out here. Open up and take it."
"Ah, it’s not convenient right now," Gu Suizhou said. "Just come in and leave it on the sink."
Since there was no gender difference between the two of them, there was no need to be prudish over such a small matter. Shen Feizhi didn't think twice, pressing down on the handle and opening the door.

The bathroom was divided into two separate sections: the inner room held the shower and toilet, while the outer area was for the sink. Gu Suizhou was sitting in the inner room; Shen Feizhi intended to set the gauze down, wash his hands, and leave.
But as luck would have it, the mirror above the sink was positioned directly opposite the person inside.
Shen Feizhi swore he hadn't done it on purpose. It was just that as he washed his hands, he happened to look up, catching a full view of the scene within.
