A momentary flicker of confusion crossed Shen Feizhi's face. "I don't know. You cursed just now. It was a reflex."
Had hitting him become such an ingrained muscle memory?
Gu Suizhou was on the verge of snapping. "Big Brother! You're my own brother! We're best buddies! Loving family! So it's perfectly normal for me to be at your place, you get it?"
Shen Feizhi remained unmoved, his voice icy. "Liar."

"How am I lying?" Gu Suizhou gripped Shen Feizhi's fingers tightly with both hands, desperate not to accidentally get strangled.
Yet this drunken, incoherent mess had the audacity to argue logically: "You're a Shen, I'm a Gu. We don't share a surname. How could we be brothers?"
Gu Suizhou paused for two seconds, then burst out laughing in exasperation. "Fine, at least you remember my name. I'm honored.
"Besides, I'm a Gu, you're a Shen. Big Brother, you're so drunk you can barely stand. Can you at least listen to reason?"
"Listen to your reason? Isn't my reason good enough?" Shen Feizhi's drinking habits were truly appalling—bursting with curiosity while refusing to listen to anyone else. "And besides…"
Gu Suizhou fell silent, throwing caution to the wind and crossing his arms, determined to see what other nonsense this man could spout.
But the moment he stopped struggling, Shen Feizhi unexpectedly released his neck, stumbled back two steps, and collapsed onto the sofa.
"And… I have no family."
Gu Suizhou froze.
Shen Feizhi lay back on the sofa, his naturally aloof eyes gazing up at him from below, somehow appearing…
He knew he shouldn't think that, but it was the truth.
Gu Suizhou suddenly marveled at the quality of Shen Feizhi's shirt. How had only one button come undone by now?
Oblivious to his disheveled appearance, Shen Feizhi enunciated each word carefully: "You're not my friend, not my family, and certainly not my lover."
Because I have none of those.
