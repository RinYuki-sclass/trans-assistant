A flicker of triumphant joy sparked in Gu Suizhou's eyes.
Finally, a way to overpower Shen Feizhi.
With deliberate patience, he explained meticulously, "Calling us lovers isn't entirely accurate. To be precise, our relationship hasn't reached that stage yet."
Shen Feizhi's alcohol-fogged brain struggled to process this. First, he slowly wrestled his hand free and scrubbed it repeatedly with a wet wipe. Then, his eyes widened as he stared at Gu Suizhou. "You wouldn't…"
"I'm the little lover you took in two days ago," Gu Suizhou finished for him, lowering his head. He ended by pressing a kiss to Shen Feizhi's wrist.
Shen Feizhi's wrist carried a subtle, intoxicating scent. Gu Suizhou, with his naturally keen sense of smell, could detect faint notes of bitter coffee pheromone.
Disliking coffee and fearing Shen Feizhi might beat him to death upon sobering up, Gu Suizhou deliberately kept his lips suspended just above Shen Feizhi's skin, feigning contact.
His feverish breath was enough to fool the drunk.
Shen Feizhi, seemingly struggling to reconcile his upright self with the idea of keeping a lover, murmured with disoriented sharpness, "Then why did you say we're brothers?"
Gu Suizhou couldn't hold back his laughter this time, chuckling beside him for a good while. When he finally calmed down, he leaned close to Shen Feizhi's ear and whispered, "Brother Shen, have you forgotten? This is between you and me…"
He tapped his fingers together suggestively, entwining them briefly before pulling them apart with lingering ambiguity.

"…our little game."
Shen Feizhi's eyes glazed over slightly, as if this concept lay beyond his current understanding.
Gu Suizhou continued to coax, "Even though you're older, sometimes you like to call me 'Big Brother' too. Come on, try it now?"
"You mean…" Shen Feizhi finally understood, "I'm paying you so I can call you 'Big Brother'?"
Gu Suizhou's eyes lit up. "Exactly, exactly!"
Unexpectedly, Shen Feizhi's expression darkened. He rolled over to face away from Gu Suizhou, lying on his side. "Return to sender!"
He wasn't about to call him "Big Brother."
Gu Suizhou fell numb from his brother's merciless rejection. "Big Brother, if you send me back now, my colleagues will mock me, and I'll be thrown out to scavenge trash daily."
