Shen Feizhi's voice remained flat. "What's that got to do with me?"
Oh-ho, that tone. How familiar.
Brother Shen is still Brother Shen, even drunk. Status doesn't change.
Gu Suizhou wiped his face, finally letting go of any hope of Shen Feizhi calling him "Big Brother." "But you've already paid. We don't offer refunds. Are you absolutely sure you want me to leave now?"
Shen Feizhi suddenly turned his head. "How much do you charge for a night?"
Gu Suizhou paused, calculating for a moment. "I don't come cheap. At least three to four hundred thousand yuan a night. You've already paid the deposit—188,000 yuan."
He clearly saw Shen Feizhi draw a sharp, almost imperceptible breath.

The next moment, Shen Feizhi sat up from the sofa and issued commands without a shred of politeness.
"Go make me a bowl of noodles, crack in two eggs, and clean the entire living room and kitchen. Pick out the grime from the range hood, table, and tile grout with a needle. Run a hot bath and make the bed…"
Since the money's already spent, might as well get my money's worth.
Thrift and frugality were virtues etched into Shen Feizhi's very DNA.
Gu Suizhou's smile gradually faded.
He wanted to stay, but not at the cost of his life.
"Brother Shen, your 'little lover' isn't your maid," he sighed helplessly. "Generally speaking, my 'services' are best delivered in bed. This… well, it's a bit of a waste of resources, isn't it?"
