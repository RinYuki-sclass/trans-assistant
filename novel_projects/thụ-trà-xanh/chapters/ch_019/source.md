Chapter 19

"Brother Shen! Brother Shen! That's not our place! Ours is across the hall!"

Gu Suizhou watched in horror as Shen Feizhi, unable to unlock the door, prepared to force his way in. Nearly jumping out of his skin, he frantically grabbed Shen Feizhi's leg just as he was about to kick down the door.

With a blank expression, Shen Feizhi turned to face the correct door and once again prepared to kick it in.

Gu Suizhou quickly restrained him with one arm while using his other to unlock the door with his fingerprint. "We can open this door normally. No need to kick it."

He never would have guessed that Shen Feizhi, who seemed to embody every vice with his smoking, drinking, and tattoos, was actually a lightweight who couldn't hold his liquor.

Was he drunk? It didn't seem like it.

He answered every question clearly and logically, remained calm and composed, and didn't even blush.

But if he wasn't drunk… That was even less likely. Gu Suizhou had never seen Shen Feizhi so rowdy before.

At every shop they passed, Shen Feizhi insisted on going inside for a look, his curiosity bordering on explosive. He even picked up a random dog to check its gender.

Gu Suizhou sighed and opened the door. "Brother Shen, what are you—"

He turned to find Shen Feizhi had vanished.

"I'm right here."

A chilling voice suddenly spoke from behind him.

Gu Suizhou was utterly baffled. "What else would it be?"

Shen Feizhi suddenly reached out and grabbed his neck, demanding fiercely, "Then why did you break into my house?"

Gu Suizhou's head slammed against the door with a dull thud, his ears ringing. He lowered his gaze to the hand gripping his neck, its veins bulging.

Wait, wasn't he being so docile just now?

Is this dog-man's drunken behavior episodic or something?

He couldn't help but sigh. "Brother Shen, your OCD seems to have vanished with the alcohol."

Usually he avoids me like the plague, wiping his hands raw with disinfectant after the slightest touch. But now he's suddenly okay with this?

Proof that alcohol truly is a miracle cure.

The sober Shen Feizhi is as stubborn as a mule. But what about the drunken one? Would he—

"Disgust me again, and I'll rip out your tongue," Shen Feizhi snarled.

Shen Feizhi froze. "You wouldn't be…?"

Gu Suizhou suddenly felt an inexplicable surge of anticipation.

"My son?" Shen Feizhi asked.

A hand landed on his head.

Gu Suizhou stared in disbelief. "What was that for?!"

A momentary flicker of confusion crossed Shen Feizhi's face. "I don't know. You cursed just now. It was a reflex."

Had hitting him become such an ingrained muscle memory?

Gu Suizhou was on the verge of snapping. "Big Brother! You're my own brother! We're best buddies! Loving family! So it's perfectly normal for me to be at your place, you get it?"

Shen Feizhi remained unmoved, his voice icy. "Liar."

Yet this drunken, incoherent mess had the audacity to argue logically: "You're a Shen, I'm a Gu. We don't share a surname. How could we be brothers?"

Gu Suizhou fell silent, throwing caution to the wind and crossing his arms, determined to see what other nonsense this man could spout.

But the moment he stopped struggling, Shen Feizhi unexpectedly released his neck, stumbled back two steps, and collapsed onto the sofa.

"And… I have no family."

Gu Suizhou froze.

Shen Feizhi lay back on the sofa, his naturally aloof eyes gazing up at him from below, somehow appearing…

He knew he shouldn't think that, but it was the truth.

Gu Suizhou suddenly marveled at the quality of Shen Feizhi's shirt. How had only one button come undone by now?

Oblivious to his disheveled appearance, Shen Feizhi enunciated each word carefully: "You're not my friend, not my family, and certainly not my lover."

No family…

Was it because among the many members of the Shen family, not a single one truly cared for him?

Gu Suizhou's fingers twitched. He licked his lips before stepping forward and dropping to one knee. "Alright, I admit it. I lied just now."

Shen Feizhi's expression seemed to say, "I knew it." "Then get the hell out now."

"Not a chance," Gu Suizhou grinned, his two sharp little canines seeming to taunt. "If I leave, who'll take care of you?"

"I don't need anyone to take care of me," Shen Feizhi retorted coldly.

"But I want to take care of you." Gu Suizhou boldly reached out, and while Shen Feizhi was lost in thought, he deftly stroked his cheek, then sighed longingly, "Damn!"

He'd been craving to touch Shen Feizhi's magnificent face for ages, and today he'd finally gotten his wish!

Shen Feizhi slapped his hand away in disgust, grabbed a wet wipe, and frantically scrubbed his face. "Didn't I just tell you not to swear?"

"Alright, alright, I'm sorry." Having already gotten what he wanted, Gu Suizhou didn't press his luck, apologizing obediently. "Brother Shen, actually… I really am your lover."

Seeing his stubborn refusal to relent, Shen Feizhi scowled and swung his fist at Gu Suizhou's face.

Fortunately, Gu Suizhou's reflexes were sharp. He caught Shen Feizhi's fist and pinned it against his chest.

To his surprise, he realized his hand was actually larger than Shen Feizhi's.

Gu Suizhou's skin was even paler than Shen Feizhi's, smooth and delicate. His knuckles weren't as prominent as Shen Feizhi's, yet his hands were slender and shapely, as exquisite as those of a professional hand model for a high-end jewelry brand.

At first glance, their hands clasped together might appear to belong to an Alpha and his cherished Omega lover.

But a closer look revealed Gu Suizhou's palm to be a full fist larger than Shen Feizhi's, capable of enveloping half the other man's fist.

A flicker of triumphant joy sparked in Gu Suizhou's eyes.

Finally, a way to overpower Shen Feizhi.

With deliberate patience, he explained meticulously, "Calling us lovers isn't entirely accurate. To be precise, our relationship hasn't reached that stage yet."

Shen Feizhi's alcohol-fogged brain struggled to process this. First, he slowly wrestled his hand free and scrubbed it repeatedly with a wet wipe. Then, his eyes widened as he stared at Gu Suizhou. "You wouldn't…"

"I'm the little lover you took in two days ago," Gu Suizhou finished for him, lowering his head. He ended by pressing a kiss to Shen Feizhi's wrist.

Shen Feizhi's wrist carried a subtle, intoxicating scent. Gu Suizhou, with his naturally keen sense of smell, could detect faint notes of bitter coffee pheromone.

Disliking coffee and fearing Shen Feizhi might beat him to death upon sobering up, Gu Suizhou deliberately kept his lips suspended just above Shen Feizhi's skin, feigning contact.

His feverish breath was enough to fool the drunk.

Shen Feizhi, seemingly struggling to reconcile his upright self with the idea of keeping a lover, murmured with disoriented sharpness, "Then why did you say we're brothers?"

Gu Suizhou couldn't hold back his laughter this time, chuckling beside him for a good while. When he finally calmed down, he leaned close to Shen Feizhi's ear and whispered, "Brother Shen, have you forgotten? This is between you and me…"

He tapped his fingers together suggestively, entwining them briefly before pulling them apart with lingering ambiguity.

He wasn't about to call him "Big Brother."

Gu Suizhou fell numb from his brother's merciless rejection. "Big Brother, if you send me back now, my colleagues will mock me, and I'll be thrown out to scavenge trash daily."

Shen Feizhi's voice remained flat. "What's that got to do with me?"

Oh-ho, that tone. How familiar.

Brother Shen is still Brother Shen, even drunk. Status doesn't change.
