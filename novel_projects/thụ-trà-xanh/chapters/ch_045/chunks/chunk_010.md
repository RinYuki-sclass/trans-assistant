Ah, they hadn't prepared an answer for that question.
Shen Feizhi and C2 exchanged a glance and spoke in unison: "A friend."
They had known each other for a long time; in a sense, they really were friends. But this was the first time Gu Suizhou had ever heard Shen Feizhi call someone a "friend."
It felt as if he had been plunged into an ice cellar. "What kind of 'friends' shower together?"
C2 scrambled to explain, "No, no, no! We aren't showering together, it's just that we're living together right now."
Shen Feizhi thought, We're screwed.
Sure enough, the air pressure around Gu Suizhou dropped even lower. He spoke through gritted teeth, "Lived… to… gether?"
This time, Shen Feizhi wasn't going to give C2 another chance to make things worse. He spoke up first: "Only for one day. He’s planning to settle down here, so I’m helping him pick out a house."
But Gu Suizhou was beyond all reason now.
His mind was filled with images of C2 and Shen Feizhi living together.
He had lived with Shen Feizhi before; he knew exactly what it was like to share a space with this man.
