The neck was a fragile and sensitive area for anyone. Even with his eyes tightly shut, Gu Suizhou felt the discomfort; he frowned and moved his head, lifting an arm as if to swat away the meddling hand.
With this movement, his loose sleeve slid down to his elbow.
Shen Feizhi froze, then suddenly lunged forward to grab his wrist.
How did he get all these scars?
The marks of "1642" were even seeping blood!
The intricate, messy scars were clearly man-made. Other than himself, no one else could have done this to him.
What kind of game was this kid playing? Had he learned self-harm over the past few years?
Shen Feizhi gripped his thin wrist tightly, not letting go until the hand was flushed with congested blood. Only then did his fingers go limp, allowing that well-defined hand to fall lifelessly onto the bed.

Watching the child—who used to whimper and cry just from being hungry—torture himself into this state, Shen Feizhi couldn’t tell if he was more angry or heartbroken. Recalling the words Lin Zizhuo and Gu Lang had told him before they died, he felt only a profound sense of frustration.
Hurting himself without even bandaging it, leaving scars without ever trying to remove them—was this his idea of being obedient?
This time, Shen Feizhi didn’t just go through the motions. Like he had done a long time ago, he carefully tended to the other's wounds.
There was a knock on the bedroom door, and C2’s muffled voice came through: "Shen Feizhi? Are you free right now? There’s something I need to tell you."
Shen Feizhi gave a low "Mm." "Wait for me, we'll talk outside."
He set Gu Suizhou’s wrist down, tied a knot the way the younger man liked to tie bowknots, and went to C2’s room.
C2’s bedroom and study were integrated into one space. He was sitting at the desk, idly flipping through a guide on how to tie hair ties.
"No thanks." Shen Feizhi had only flipped through it on a whim, thinking of Gu Suizhou’s long hair. "Go ahead, what did you want to talk about?"
