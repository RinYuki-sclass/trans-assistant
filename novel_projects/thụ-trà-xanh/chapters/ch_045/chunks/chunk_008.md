Gu Suizhou was curled against him in a wretched mess, his fingers clutching Shen Feizhi’s waist so hard they nearly sank into the flesh. Shen Feizhi winced at the pain, but he didn't stop him.
He moved his hand to the back of the other's neck, giving it a gentle squeeze just like he used to, trying to help him stabilize his emotions. "Calm down first. Let's talk."
"Talk about what?" Gu Suizhou’s nerves had been pulled taut for over four years, and in this moment, they snapped completely. It wasn't that he didn't know his strength was hurting Shen Feizhi, but he was more afraid that if he let go, this person would disappear forever.
"Did you come back this time planning to leave again? To make everyone forget you again—to make me forget you, too?"
Shen Feizhi gave a "no" that lacked any shred of conviction.
Gu Suizhou’s hand clamped around his jaw, staring him down with savage intensity. "Shen Feizhi, do you know what I was thinking when I was carving those words into my arm?"
Shen Feizhi froze, not expecting him to suddenly bring up the scars on his arm.
But then he recalled those sixteen hundred-odd days…
His heart constricted instantly, a sharp pang of pain radiating through him. "Are you insane? What did you carve?! Who taught you to treat yourself like that?"
The kid was a complete lunatic!
Shen Feizhi hated it when people sacrificed themselves for him; it always left him feeling indebted. Faced with Gu Suizhou’s reckless, nearly suicidal devotion, he was momentarily at a loss.
He suddenly wanted to ask: Was it worth it?
He was just one man. If he was forgotten, he was forgotten. They hadn't even had much of a foundation for their relationship to begin with—what was the point?

"Then what do you want me to do?" The corners of Gu Suizhou’s eyes were so red they looked ready to bleed. "I keep thinking about how every one of these cuts is because of you. I know you were the one who gave me that injection. I should hate your guts. Every time I see these scars, I should remember how cruel you are. I should want to tear you to pieces!"
This boy had always been one to throw fastballs.
He wasn't like others who would hide their wounds and pretend to be unfazed. He insisted on ripping his own injuries open right in front of the person responsible, exposing the raw, bloody mess.
Because he knew Shen Feizhi would feel the sting of heartache; he knew he would cave.
Gu Suizhou was no gentleman. He didn't care about "face," nor did he have any lofty pride that would trap him in a stalemate. Since he was a child, he had known exactly how to exploit people's weaknesses, and he utilized that knowledge perfectly on Shen Feizhi.
