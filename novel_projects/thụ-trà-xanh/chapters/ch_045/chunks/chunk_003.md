He rarely met his match; aside from A11, only the man before him could constantly make him hit a dead end.
He rubbed his nose helplessly. "In every small world we've known so far, once someone discovers our existence, they never escape unscathed. Those who learn of us are quickly obliterated by the laws of the world's order. If you tell Gu Suizhou, even if we don't move to erase him ourselves, there's no guarantee the rules of order won't come for him."
This was a problem.
Shen Feizhi frowned. "Is there no other possibility?"
"It's not entirely impossible," C2 smiled. "Unless that person’s willpower and mental state are strong enough to defy the world’s will. It’s far too painful—from the forceful external intervention on the body to the internal psychological resistance… In all my time, aside from you, I've never seen a second person capable of reaching that point."
Shen Feizhi let out a breath.
Having endured the torture of the energy channels himself, he knew exactly how much it hurt; naturally, he didn't want Gu Suizhou to suffer through it as well.
But lying was a tall order for him.
Especially if he wanted to deceive that little brat Gu Suizhou, who was sharper than a damn monkey—one lie wouldn't be enough.
C2 knew he was troubled and didn't say much more. "I’m going to take a shower first. Once you’ve thought of something, talk it over with me. I’ll need to coordinate with you."
Both were Alphas, so there was no need to avoid each other by running upstairs just for a shower. Shen Feizhi gave a casual "mm" and leaned back in his chair to rest with his eyes closed, rubbing his temples with his knuckles.
C2 was quick; before long, the sound of water stopped.
Then, Shen Feizhi heard a knock.
He was just about to ask, "Why the hell are you knocking to come out of the bathroom?" when he suddenly realized the sound was coming from the room's main door.
