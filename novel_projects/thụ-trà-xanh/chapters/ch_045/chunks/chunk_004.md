He looked up. "You… wait a second."
The knocking paused.
Before Shen Feizhi could even stand up, he heard a voice from outside: "Is it inconvenient? I understand. Sorry to disturb you."
Shen Feizhi: "…" Such a calm voice? He had expected the guy to throw a massive fit once he woke up.
The bathroom door was ajar, and C2 indicated that he could do as he pleased without worrying about him.
Shen Feizhi then said, "Come in."
The words had barely left his lips when the door was pushed open.
Leaning against the bookshelf, Shen Feizhi tilted his head lazily to look at him. "You woke up fast. Do you feel unwell anywhere?"
Gu Suizhou opened his mouth, his beautiful amber glaze-like eyes staring straight at him, as if trying to see right through him.
Even Shen Feizhi felt uncomfortable under his gaze. "What is it?"
Gu Suizhou let go of the door handle and walked toward him step by step, his indifferent expression appearing as if it had been tempered by ice.
Shen Feizhi’s Adam's apple bobbed; for the first time in his life, he had the urge to retreat.
Unfortunately, Gu Suizhou didn't give him the chance. When there were still a few meters between them, he suddenly accelerated his pace, his burning hot hands clamping firmly around Shen Feizhi’s neck!
Shen Feizhi could have dodged, but after a thought, he let his breathing be held captive.
He adjusted the rhythm of his chest's rise and fall, meeting the man's gaze without a hint of fear.
