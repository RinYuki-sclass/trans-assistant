Chapter 45
Xu Zhong looked at Shen Feizhi—the man who had "finally returned from a four-and-a-half-year business trip"—and, knowing full well his standing in Gu Suizhou’s heart, didn’t lift a finger to stop him.
Shen Feizhi successfully abducted this high-profile figure and brought him home.
The moment they got into the car, he looked at C2 with a dark expression. "How did you let yourself get tricked into going up there?"
C2 scratched his cheek awkwardly. "I wasn't tricked. I went up there voluntarily. That guy was pretty good at cards, and I got the itch, so I went up to play a couple of rounds with him."
Shen Feizhi took a few deep breaths. "And then?"
"They weren't playing fair. They kept trying to feel me up while we played." C2 frowned. "Gu Suizhou seemed to think I was some kind of escort, so he didn't bother to intervene."
Shen Feizhi covered his face.
Relying on Gu Suizhou to save a life—not in ten thousand years.
"Later, he realized I was refusing, and I think he intended to stop them," C2 said uncertainty. "But I couldn't hold back at the time and punched the guy first. After that, he completely lost interest in helping and just let me argue it out with them."
Shen Feizhi: "."
C2 looked resentfully at the person passed out in the backseat. "But this man of yours has a truly unpleasant mouth. Otherwise, I wouldn't have picked a fight with him."
Shen Feizhi glanced sideways.
Unpleasant? Why did it always seem like his mouth was coated in honey whenever he was around him?
C2 appeared small and cute, but as a Main Star inhabitant, his strength was actually no less than Shen Feizhi’s; a full-force strike was enough to keep a kid like Gu Suizhou unconscious for half the night.
