He released the grip he had on the other’s collar and gently wiped away the endless stream of tears, sighing, "You're in your twenties, what are you crying for?"
"Brother Shen?" Gu Suizhou called out weakly, his voice trembling with uncertainty.
Shen Feizhi’s anger vanished instantly. "Yeah."
That single response seemed to flip a switch in Gu Suizhou, and he suddenly began to sob even harder.
Shen Feizhi couldn’t wipe them away fast enough. "Hey! No, calm down for a second."
If Gu Suizhou could calm down, he wouldn't be Gu Suizhou.

He suffered from chronic insomnia and rarely ever dreamed.
Even when he did, for some reason, he seldom saw Shen Feizhi in them.
On the rare occasion he did, it would be a face that looked nothing like the real Shen Feizhi, doing things that were completely out of character.
Gu Suizhou knew those were just his own delusions; he never for a second believed those people were actually Shen Feizhi.
They were all imposters! All fakes!
Shen Feizhi had abandoned him long ago; he’d been gone for years! Why couldn't his dreams at least be realistic?
Today was the same. The moment he came down, Shen Feizhi was actually speaking to him with such a good temper, not even fighting back when he was being strangled—how was that possible?
Gu Suizhou tortured his own psyche with near-masochistic cruelty, thinking to himself that this was just another imposter.
Until he was kicked to the ground.
Absurd delusions suddenly turned into reality, and he couldn't help but choke out, "Brother Shen?"
Shen Feizhi answered immediately, "I'm here."
"Brother Shen! Brother Shen! Brother Shen!!"
Like a dying pup suddenly catching sight of an oasis that could grant him life, he let out one desperate, yearning cry after another.
