He narrowed his eyes and sank back down.
Shen Feizhi had gone to great lengths to hack into the education system's network and find Gu Suizhou's information.
He rushed to High Tier High School, only to be greeted by the sight of Gu Suizhou being held by the neck and slammed against a utility pole.
He took a moment to catch his breath, then said coldly just before the fist could land on Gu Suizhou's face, "If you let him beat you to the ground, don't bother coming home."
Gu Suizhou instinctively jerked his head to the side.
Yang Rui hadn't expected him to dodge at that moment. His fist slammed into the utility pole behind Gu Suizhou, and he cried out, clutching his hand as he squatted down in pain.
Gu Suizhou seized the chance to sidestep away and whipped his head around.

Their eyes met.
Shen Feizhi looked into Gu Suizhou's deep, amber eyes and felt a rare twinge of guilt.
He cleared his throat and, without a hint of blush or a quickened heartbeat, walked over to Yang Rui. He lifted his foot and stomped down hard on the injured hand.
This time, Yang Rui's scream was even louder.
"Sorry, I accidentally stepped on it," Shen Feizhi said, looking down. "Is your hand broken?"
Judging by the unnatural angle of Yang Rui's hand, it was almost certainly fractured.
Tears of pain streamed down Yang Rui's face as he lay on the ground, his face and neck flushed with rage, roaring, "Are you fucking insane?"
"Cursing?" Shen Feizhi frowned. "How rude."
As he spoke, an elegant, exquisitely crafted butterfly knife just so happened to slip from his sleeve, landing right beside Yang Rui's hand.
Gu Suizhou's eyebrow arched slightly.
Hearing the crisp clatter of the knife hitting the ground, Shen Feizhi adjusted his trouser leg and knelt on one knee, pretending to pick it up.
He said to Gu Suizhou, "Stand a little to your left."
