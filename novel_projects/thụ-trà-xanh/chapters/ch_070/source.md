Shen Feizhi disliked wasting time on meaningless things, but this time, he sat on the sofa with extraordinary patience, trimming the sharp thorns from each rose.

Lucky Dog hadn't seen his other master today and was uncharacteristically quiet. He cleverly stepped on the remote several times to turn on the large light screen, then nestled by Shen Feizhi’s feet, wagging his tail.

The cheerful sounds of a variety show emanated from the speakers, but Shen Feizhi ignored them.

One man and one dog stayed together like that, accompanied by the laughter from the show and the rhythmic ticking of the alarm clock's second hand.

Finally, at eleven-thirty at night, Shen Feizhi finished trimming the last rose.

Right then, the door opened.

"Still sitting out here this late? You really did leave the door open for me." The moment Gu Suizhou stepped inside, the air instantly became lively. "Brother Shen, come have some late-night snacks!"

As he spoke, his eyes lit up, spotting the vibrant roses in the vase. "For me?"

"Mm." Shen Feizhi nonchalantly tossed the trimmed hangnail into the trash. "Do you like them?"

"I love them! I love them so much." Gu Suizhou leaned in with a beaming smile to give him a hug. "They’re still dripping with dew. Did you buy them specifically to celebrate my exoneration? Ge, you love me so much~"

Shen Feizhi noticed the bag he’d placed on the table.

Inside were green tea cakes—ten boxes in total, still piping hot.

Shifting his gaze back to Gu Suizhou, he noticed the hair at the younger man’s temples was slightly damp, likely from sweat.

Shen Feizhi’s lashes fluttered. He brushed that lock of hair back with his fingertips. "Why were you in such a rush?"

"I was afraid of making you wait too long." Gu Suizhou glanced at his watch.

It wasn't midnight yet; he’d made it in time.

Shen Feizhi gave a soft sigh. "What did you say right before that?"

Gu Suizhou repeated obediently, "I was afraid of making you wait too long."

Shen Feizhi: "Before that."

Gu Suizhou thought for a moment, then said hesitantly, "You… love me so much?"

"Mm." Shen Feizhi let out a soft chuckle. His fingers toyed with the other’s earlobe, deftly unfastening the stud before leaning close to his ear. "I love you very much."

"I love you very much. Did you hear me?"

No confession of love was more direct or fervent than "I love you."

He figured Gu Suizhou needed to hear it.

Gu Suizhou’s eyes suddenly brimmed with red.

That pitiable flash of red was more striking than any nebula or rose.

His Adam’s apple bobbed as he swallowed back a weak, unintended sob.

He tilted his head slightly, placing a solemn kiss upon the pulse of the wrist held near his ear, and murmured, "I heard you."

The confession and the heartbeat—he heard them both.

The sound of the heartbeat was faint, almost imperceptible.

Yet it was deafening.

November 5th.

In the predetermined trajectory of the original world, "Shen Feizhi" and "Gu Suizhou" should have been at each other's throats in the Administrative Court.

But now, they were kissing passionately amidst the fragrance of roses.

Shen Feizhi suddenly felt a flicker of curiosity: what kind of little tag would the Main Star give the data for their world now?

Was it Mutual Salvation? Or Fated Together? Or perhaps Reunion After a Long Parting?

It seemed every one of them applied, yet none of them felt quite accurate.

There were so many inevitabilities in the world, yet Shen Feizhi and Gu Suizhou were partial to chasing coincidences born of following one's heart.

It was just that they happened to love each other enough.

After a life of wandering, they finally belonged to one another.
