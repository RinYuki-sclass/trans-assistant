The cigarette had already burned down to the filter. That faint, flickering spark—weak yet refusing to die—scorched the smoker’s fingertip, but he remained utterly oblivious.
It wasn't until the sweetness of the chocolate vanished from his tongue that he finally snapped back to his senses. His fingers loosened, dropping the cigarette butt into the ashtray.
By the time C2 submitted the report, Shen Feizhi had emerged from the private room.
He paid the bill as if nothing had happened. "A11 told me to tell you: your vacation is over."
By the time C2 submitted the report, Shen Feizhi had also stepped out of the private room.
He paid the bill on his own initiative. "A11 asked me to tell you that your vacation is over."
A11 had just messaged him asking if he was with C2 and requested that he pass along the next assignment.
"Ugh, so annoying. It’s the end of the year, so everyone’s rushing for bonuses and performance targets." C2 curled his lip. "The task in your world is just about finished, and so is the vacation I managed to snag under the guise of official business."
Shen Feizhi gave a low laugh and handed over a card.
C2 took it. "What's this?"
"The third pass," Shen Feizhi said.
C2 froze once he saw the pattern on it.
Shen Feizhi currently held two passes granted by the Main Star.
Those two passes allowed for free entry and exit to the Main Star, making it convenient for Gu Suizhou and Shen Feizhi to go there for vacations whenever they liked.
But the one he was giving C2 was for free entry and exit to the Small Worlds, and it could be bound to a hundred of them.
It was essentially a travel pass.
The Main Star managed its employees very strictly. Despite the fact that they spent every day traveling all over the place, every trip required approval from superiors; they couldn't just vacation wherever they pleased.
Only those in the K-tier—the highest level of management—were entitled to three of these passes each.
Right now, one such card lay in C2’s hand.
“This is a thank-you gift,” Shen Feizhi said earnestly.
