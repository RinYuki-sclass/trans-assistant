"Why did you feel the need to do something so redundant?"
"You know how it is. A lot of those high-level leaders can't stand me. My conscience is clear, so rather than hiding it and leaving them leverage for later, it’s better to set off the fuse myself now. I’ll feel more at ease once it's dealt with once and for all." He offered some comfort, "See? They’re all being perfectly pleasant to me. There won't be any major problems."
"Change your clothes first." Ten minutes was too short, gone in the blink of an eye. Shen Feizhi urged, "Are you certain your conscience is clear?"
"Certain!" Gu Suizhou nodded with immense gravity. "I swear that in the development of W.N, I haven't conducted any illegal human experimentation."
His gaze was so steadfast that Shen Feizhi couldn't bring himself to voice any suspicion. He sighed and nodded.

To lighten the heavy atmosphere, he cracked a casual joke: "Not on W.N, but on something else?"
"Hmm…" Gu Suizhou rubbed his chin, looking innocent. "There’s no helping that, but the other stuff is tacitly approved by the Federation. They won't investigate me; in fact, they’ll even support me, so you don't have to worry!"
"If you don't want me to worry, stop pulling these stunts in the future." Shen Feizhi’s brows furrowed. "When… are you coming back?"
"Today." Gu Suizhou looked down at him. "I’ll definitely be back before midnight."
Shen Feizhi had no choice but to believe him. "Fine."
They stepped out just a second before the administrative review personnel could break down the door.
In front of everyone, Gu Suizhou gave Shen Feizhi a quick kiss. "Remember to leave the door unlocked for me tonight, husband."
Shen Feizhi gave a perfunctory nod.
The leader of the group couldn't suppress his gossipy nature, glancing from the visibly pleased Gu Suizhou over to Shen Feizhi.
But with that one glance, he broke into a cold sweat.
The man, with his cold and stunning features, was staring at him with the icy gaze one might reserve for a corpse.
Having spent years in the political sphere, he recognized it immediately: it was a look of threat and intimidation, a warning to be sensible and not go too far with the person being taken away.
It was obvious who Shen Feizhi was doing this for.
The leader swallowed hard.
