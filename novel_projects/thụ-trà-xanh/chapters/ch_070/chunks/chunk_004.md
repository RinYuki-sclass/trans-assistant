Love really does make people blind.
Shen Feizhi had lunch with C2, who had just returned from a business trip.
"Holy shit, the target of this mission is going to be the death of me!"
C2 ranted endlessly about the job he’d just finished. "Do you have any idea how much of a dumbass that guy was? Even A11 cursed! That's A11 we're talking about—do you realize what that means? To piss him off so much he starts spouting 'fragrant language,' the guy must be a world-class specimen!"
C2 really had a gift for gab; even without Shen Feizhi engaging, he could carry on a solo comedy routine.
Usually, when they were together, C2 did most of the talking.
But today, Shen Feizhi was exceptionally silent. Even C2, accustomed to his own monologues, noticed something was off. "What’s up? Got something on your mind?"
Shen Feizhi mechanically swallowed a bite of steak and set down his knife and fork, not bothering to hide it. "Gu Suizhou was arrested."
What? He’d only been gone for a few days; what the hell had happened?
Shen Feizhi gave him a brief overview of the situation.
"I know Gu Suizhou wouldn't lie to me, but I'm afraid his actions weren't authorized. Even if he didn't hurt those people, they might catch him on a technicality and lock him up for a few years."
C2's expression shifted from serious, to relaxed, and finally to sheer shock.
He couldn't help but interrupt. "Wait, you… you don't know?"
Shen Feizhi sensed an information gap between them. "Don't know what?"
