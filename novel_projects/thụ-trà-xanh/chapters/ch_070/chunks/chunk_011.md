What if something had gone wrong with the experiment? What if the compatibility results were incorrect? What if…
He suddenly wanted to grab the kid by the collar and beat him.
Such reckless measures were truly infuriating.
Yet Shen Feizhi was the person least qualified to reprimand him for it.
He sat quietly on the table, the pad of his index finger unconsciously rubbing against his thumb.
C2 knew he wanted to smoke.
So, he kindly fished one out of his pocket: "Want me to light it for you?"
"No." When Shen Feizhi first spoke, he couldn't get the words out. He cleared his throat before saying slowly, "I won't. It leaves a smell."
Gu Suizhou was coming home tonight.
If he smelled smoke, he would likely make a scene.
C2 fell silent.
He placed the cigarette on the table: "Do you want to see what the original settings of your world looked like?"
Shen Feizhi looked up, blinking once.
C2 had seemingly prepared for this, handing him a magnetic card containing the documents before getting up to go buy some milk tea.
“I snuck this out behind A11’s back, so hurry it up.”
Shen Feizhi had seen this kind of data countless times before, but this was the first time he lacked even the courage to pick it up decisively.
He dug his nails hard into his palm before finally opening the reading mode.
