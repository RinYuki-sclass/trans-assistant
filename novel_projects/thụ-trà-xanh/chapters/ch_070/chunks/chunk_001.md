Chapter 70
70. End of the Main Story
The next day was a day off. Two people with poor sleep quality and a habit of rising early managed a rare feat, sleeping soundly until noon.
The small wooden fox carving hanging by the window swayed gently, its wooden grain plated in a layer of golden light, quiet and cozy.
Inside the room, the bitter yet refreshing scents of coffee and matcha intertwined, somehow giving off an unprovoked hint of sweetness.
In the end, they were startled awake by a knock at the door.
Annoyed by the persistent ringing of the doorbell, Shen Feizhi fished out a scent-blocking patch and stuck it onto the bite-mark-covered nape of Gu Suizhou’s neck. He gave his leg a kick. "Get lost and open the door. See who it is."
Gu Suizhou wasn’t fully awake either. "Which idi—"
A palm landed on his head.
It was too cold outside the covers. Shen Feizhi retracted his arm as quickly as he had delivered the blow. "Watch your language."
Gu Suizhou gave an obedient "oh." "Which idiot is disturbing people's sweet dreams this early in the morning?"
Shen Feizhi let out a short laugh. "Go take a look."
Both of them were busy men burdened with affairs; it wouldn't do if they actually delayed something important just because they stayed in bed.
Gu Suizhou grumbled under his breath about how he "couldn't stand another day of this job" before reluctantly throwing on a bathrobe to go answer the door.

Shen Feizhi couldn't sleep anymore either. He lay in bed with his eyes narrowed for a while, and once the drowsiness cleared, he opened them and picked up his smartphone to search for a place to eat lunch.
His whole body was sore and aching; he didn't want to cook himself.
But even after he had picked out three backup restaurants, Gu Suizhou still hadn't returned.
A sharp sixth sense told him something was wrong. He slapped on an isolation patch, threw on some clothes, and walked out.
The rest of the words were swallowed back into his stomach the moment he saw the row of Administration Department personnel at the door.
