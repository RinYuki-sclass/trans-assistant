Category: Romance – Anguish – BE (Bad Ending)
Shen Feizhi’s brow twitched when he saw those last two letters, nearly crushing the light screen in his grip.
In this world’s original setting, Shen Feizhi was not supposed to meet Gu Suizhou so early. Both had "beautiful, strong, and miserable" backgrounds, and both had differentiated into Alphas at a young age.
They were meant to rise through the ranks of the Federation Research Institute individually, and only after their first meeting would they begin to cherish each other's talents, gradually developing feelings.
However, the mutual repulsion between Alphas and a 0% compatibility rate meant they could never be together. Furthermore, the Shen family stood between them; Gu Suizhou wanted the Shen family destroyed, while Shen Feizhi wanted to seize control of the family's finances.
The two parted on bad terms after an argument. Their next meeting was as enemies on opposing sides of the Prosecutor’s Office.
Shen Feizhi didn’t want to read any further.
What kind of mess was this?
It felt like reading some mind-blowing, out-of-character fanfiction.
Even without all those complications, Shen Feizhi would never have coveted the Shen family’s meager wealth.
But if this was the original setting of the World’s Will…
The world’s trajectory had completely shifted, its rules thoroughly rewritten; no wonder the Main Star cared so much about them.
Shen Feizhi sighed, pulling a piece of chocolate from his pocket and popping it into his mouth.
While he was at it, he lit a cigarette left on the table.
He didn't smoke it, though. He merely held it lit between his fingertips, the faint red spark reflecting in his pitch-black pupils. It flickered in and out of existence, causing one’s heart to ebb and flow along with it.
C2 was crouched by the door, writing the work report for this world.
[World 6/9 has undergone a total of three plot deviations…]
[The first was when Shen Feizhi saved Gu Suizhou by a twist of fate and used pheromones to guide his differentiation.]
From that moment on, the pheromone compatibility between the two would no longer be 0%.
[Second, Gu Suizhou loves Shen Feizhi instead of following the World’s Will to love the "Child of Destiny."]
