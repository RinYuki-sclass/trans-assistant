But because it was so late, the shop owner said apologetically that the remaining roses hadn't been de-thorned yet, so it might be inconvenient.
Shen Feizhi shook his head and said it was fine; he’d take them all.
He didn't count exactly how many roses were left, but he estimated there were twenty or thirty—not some romantic, significant number.
But that was fine. Life was rarely filled with so many coincidences.
Romance has no fixed answer; it shouldn't be bound by the shackles of a "standard."
As long as it comes from the right person, what difference does the meaning between twenty or thirty roses and ninety-nine roses truly make?
Shen Feizhi disliked wasting time on meaningless things, but this time, he sat on the sofa with extraordinary patience, trimming the sharp thorns from each rose.
Lucky Dog hadn't seen his other master today and was uncharacteristically quiet. He cleverly stepped on the remote several times to turn on the large light screen, then nestled by Shen Feizhi’s feet, wagging his tail.
The cheerful sounds of a variety show emanated from the speakers, but Shen Feizhi ignored them.
One man and one dog stayed together like that, accompanied by the laughter from the show and the rhythmic ticking of the alarm clock's second hand.
Finally, at eleven-thirty at night, Shen Feizhi finished trimming the last rose.
Right then, the door opened.
"Still sitting out here this late? You really did leave the door open for me." The moment Gu Suizhou stepped inside, the air instantly became lively. "Brother Shen, come have some late-night snacks!"
As he spoke, his eyes lit up, spotting the vibrant roses in the vase. "For me?"
"Mm." Shen Feizhi nonchalantly tossed the trimmed hangnail into the trash. "Do you like them?"
"I love them! I love them so much." Gu Suizhou leaned in with a beaming smile to give him a hug. "They’re still dripping with dew. Did you buy them specifically to celebrate my exoneration? Ge, you love me so much~"
Shen Feizhi noticed the bag he’d placed on the table.
Inside were green tea cakes—ten boxes in total, still piping hot.
Shifting his gaze back to Gu Suizhou, he noticed the hair at the younger man’s temples was slightly damp, likely from sweat.
Shen Feizhi’s lashes fluttered. He brushed that lock of hair back with his fingertips. "Why were you in such a rush?"
