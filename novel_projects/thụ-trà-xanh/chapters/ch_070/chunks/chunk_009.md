Gu Suizhou waited by the machine, anxious and frantic.
But when the result finally appeared on the screen, it was like a bolt from the blue, a strike of lightning that left him reeling.
A 0% flashing in warning red.
What kind of circumstances could possibly lead to such a value?
In utter disbelief, Gu Suizhou seized the doctor and demanded, "What does this mean? Why is it flashing red?"
The doctor was equally bewildered. "How could it be this low? Even between members of the same sex, the Federation has recorded no more than three such cases!"
Gu Suizhou’s eyes were bloodshot. "Give it to me straight!"
The doctor blurted out immediately, "Your pheromones aren't just completely incompatible with his—they actually trigger… an allergic reaction."
To put it simply, it was like water and fire—innately incompatible, naturally diametric. If they met, it would be a fight to the death.
Most likely, both sides would end up destroyed.
