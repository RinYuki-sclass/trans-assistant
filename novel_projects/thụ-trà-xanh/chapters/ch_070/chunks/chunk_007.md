Gu Suizhou kept these words buried deep in his heart.
Because he knew that no one else remembered the existence of "Shen Feizhi."
He asked the doctor, "If I don't find someone, what will happen?"
The doctor looked at him with a complex expression, a trace of admiration in his eyes. "You will go through repeated bouts of pseudo-estrus until the differentiation is complete. Each time may be more painful than the last, and more… controlled by instinctive desire."
"Understood." Gu Suizhou’s reaction was flat. "Thank you."
He dismissed everyone and sat alone in the hospital room, closing his eyes as he traced the features of Shen Feizhi’s face a hundred times in his mind.
By the hundredth time, the sun had risen.
He sat up and placed a call to Leapton. "You have a new tattoo artist boyfriend? Give me his contact info."
Gu Suizhou learned how to tattoo himself and designed his own pattern.
The thorn vines coiling up around the base of his thigh were never meant to represent longing, but rather, restraint.
They restrained his instincts and reinforced his yearning.
At first glance, the tattoo appeared to be a mess of aggressive, thorn-riddled vines tightly entwining and threatening a cross, but that wasn't the case.
It was a bruised and battered, blood-soaked whip, pleading for a sliver of comfort from the cross that represented hope and salvation.
The thorns of a rose are never meant to hurt others, but to protect oneself.
The cross magnanimously allowed the thorns to entwine it, neither leaving nor showing any sign of loathing.
