In exchange for agreeing to help the Main Star with the occasional favor, he had obtained this card from K7 to repay his debt of gratitude to C2 and A11.
Overcome with emotion, C2 was suddenly a blubbering mess of tears and snot. “Holy shit! Shen! I love you!!”
He loved traveling; to him, this was more precious than striking it rich overnight!!
“Waaaah… did you sign an indentured servitude contract with my heartless boss just for me? Shen, how can I ever thank you? Waaaah…”
Shen Feizhi pushed him away with a look of disdain. “This is for both you and A11. Don’t hog it.”
“Don’t worry, what’s his is mine, and what’s mine is still mine.” C2 caressed the card as if he couldn’t bear to let it go. Without a moment’s hesitation, he bound World 6/9 to the first slot.
Shen Feizhi arched an eyebrow. “You haven’t had enough of this place yet?”
“You’re asking what you already know!” C2 gave him a playful, coy shove. “I just can't bear to leave you guys!”
Shen Feizhi felt a wave of revulsion and got a case of goosebumps. "I'm leaving."
C2 wiped his tears. "Not even going to see me off?"
Shen Feizhi shook his head. "It’s getting dark."
The night outside was deep. Rose-red starlight mingled with the warm yellow glow of the streetlamps, spilling onto the ground and weaving together into a crimson sea of light, looking very much like clusters of crowded roses.
He suddenly remembered that the roses in the vase at home were about to wither; it was time to replace them.
C2 dropped his theatrical act and waved at him. "See you next time, then."
"Mm. Stay safe at work."
C2 smiled and called out to him, "Shen Feizhi."
Shen Feizhi stopped and turned around.
C2 said with genuine feeling, "May you two last a long, long time."
Shen Feizhi’s lips curled into a faint smile. "Thanks. We will."
After saying goodbye to C2, he went to a florist to buy a large bouquet of roses.
