"I was afraid of making you wait too long." Gu Suizhou glanced at his watch.
It wasn't midnight yet; he’d made it in time.
Shen Feizhi gave a soft sigh. "What did you say right before that?"
Gu Suizhou repeated obediently, "I was afraid of making you wait too long."
Shen Feizhi: "Before that."
Gu Suizhou thought for a moment, then said hesitantly, "You… love me so much?"
"Mm." Shen Feizhi let out a soft chuckle. His fingers toyed with the other’s earlobe, deftly unfastening the stud before leaning close to his ear. "I love you very much."
"I love you very much. Did you hear me?"
No confession of love was more direct or fervent than "I love you."
He figured Gu Suizhou needed to hear it.
Gu Suizhou’s eyes suddenly brimmed with red.
That pitiable flash of red was more striking than any nebula or rose.
His Adam’s apple bobbed as he swallowed back a weak, unintended sob.
He tilted his head slightly, placing a solemn kiss upon the pulse of the wrist held near his ear, and murmured, "I heard you."
The confession and the heartbeat—he heard them both.
The sound of the heartbeat was faint, almost imperceptible.
Yet it was deafening.
November 5th.
In the predetermined trajectory of the original world, "Shen Feizhi" and "Gu Suizhou" should have been at each other's throats in the Administrative Court.
But now, they were kissing passionately amidst the fragrance of roses.
