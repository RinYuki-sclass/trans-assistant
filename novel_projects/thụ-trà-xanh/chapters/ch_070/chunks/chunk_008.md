Relying on this tattoo, Gu Suizhou endured one agonizing pseudohit after another.
He thought he could keep enduring forever, holding out until the day a miracle occurred and Shen Feizhi returned to see him.
But it seemed the heavens loved to spite Gu Suizhou. Not only did no miracle descend, but his differentiation indicators intensified.
"Your pheromones are starting to faintly manifest." The doctor had a massive headache; this was the first time he had ever seen such a situation.
“Here, the pheromone data is all here.”
Gu Suizhou took it and looked it over. Facing data that was exclusively S-Rank, the first question he asked was actually—
“Can I perform a pheromone data match with someone else now?”
The doctor didn’t even understand what he meant by the question. “Ah? Do you have someone you like? Sure, just give me their pheromone sample and I’ll check it for you.”
“I don’t have a sample, only data.” Gu Suizhou looked into his eyes with a streak of paranoia that others couldn't detect. “Can it be cross-checked through the machine using data?”
The doctor was speechless. “…It’s, well, not impossible.”
“Good. Let me borrow your machine.”
As he spoke, he got out of bed and went to the computing room next door. He quickly entered his own data under the “No. 1” profile, then switched to the “No. 2” interface.
Before proceeding, Gu Suizhou paused for a few seconds.
Only those familiar with him would know that this was a sign of nervousness and fear.
He was tense. What would the data match between himself and Shen Feizhi be?
