Gu Suizhou nearly fainted, his eyes rolling back in his head.
He'd been terrified of ghosts since childhood, a fact Lin Zizhuo never tired of teasing him about. Gu Lang, however, had always been fiercely protective, shielding him entirely from cemeteries and burial sites.
Who could have imagined that one day he'd be lying in a grave pit with a handsome Alpha?
Listening to the footsteps outside, Gu Suizhou whispered, his voice barely audible, "Big Brother, does this mean we're… sharing a bed in life and a grave in death?"
He paused, then added, "Wait, we haven't even shared a bed in life yet. I need to work harder."
The grave pit was cramped, barely fitting one person. Shen Feizhi, pressed against Gu Suizhou, couldn't help but wish he could weigh down on him even more.
If he could just crush this man to death, he wouldn't have to listen to all this ridiculous nonsense.
"That's strange… I could've sworn I heard something moving over here just now…"
The person outside muttered to himself, pacing back and forth.
The pit they had fallen into was so deep that no sane person would suspect anyone would be crazy enough to jump in.
Being scared was one thing; figuring out how to get out later was another.
The gravedigger shivered twice. "Fuck, did I actually run into a ghost?"
His voice trembled, clearly terrified by his own overactive imagination.
"If it weren't for the high pay here, no one would take this job in a million years…"
His muttering faded into the distance until Shen Feizhi could no longer hear it. Straightening up, he patted the face of the man beneath him. "You okay?"
