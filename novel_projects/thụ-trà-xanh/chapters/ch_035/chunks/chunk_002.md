Clutching the bubble tea, Lin Sui lowered his head and sneered, "And what business is it of Mr. Shen's whether I withdraw my investment or not?"
"You might be mistaken. I'm not trying to control you. I have no intention of interfering with your decisions." Shen Feizhi glanced at his constantly vibrating smartphone, his brow furrowing. "I'm just curious: what exactly did the Shen family offer you to make you betray the hard work of your adoptive parents like this?"
Lin Sui instinctively retorted, "I didn't!"
"Alright, you didn't." To Lin Sui's surprise, Shen Feizhi agreed. "If you say the Shen family didn't offer you any benefits, that's not impossible either."
Lin Sui was taken aback. "Then…"
Shen Feizhi: "Then you're doing this purely to protect yourself?"
Terror flooded Lin Sui's face, his ears burning crimson. "No! I just want to cut all ties with W.N.!"
"Perhaps you haven't fully grasped the situation. It was always you who sought involvement with W.N. If you hadn't exploited Gu Suizhou's inheritance to ride on the tiger's coattails, who at W.N would have recognized your status?" Shen Feizhi silenced his buzzing smartphone for the third time, his gaze never leaving Lin Sui. "Now you're claiming you want to sever ties and withdrawing your investment? Isn't that a bit hypocritical?"
Lin Sui shot to his feet. "I did it all to protect Gu Suizhou! If it weren't for me, the Shen family would have killed him countless times by now!"
Shen Feizhi raised an eyebrow. "But if you hadn't helped the Shen family slander Professor Lin and Professor Gu back then, Gu Suizhou wouldn't have lost his parents at such a young age."
"I don't know what you're talking about!" Lin Sui roared, his voice shaking. "They were my parents too! How could I ever…"

His voice was cold. "Why do you care so much?"
Six simple words, yet Gu Suizhou choked on them.
