Gu Suizhou's face was as pale as a corpse in a coffin. "Not really."
Shen Feizhi stood up, showing no sympathy. "Then lie here and wait for me to come back?"
"Absolutely not!" Gu Suizhou would rather die than be left alone in this place. He struggled to his feet, this time not clinging to Shen Feizhi's arm, but instead wrapping his arms tightly around his waist like a koala clinging to a tree. "Please, Brother Shen, don't push me away! Protect me!"
Shen Feizhi: "…"
He paused for two seconds, then sent Gu Suizhou a message on his smartphone.
The crisp, resolute ringtone—"Don't worry, Bro, I'll protect you!"—echoed in the small earthen pit.
Gu Suizhou had gone all-in, completely abandoning his dignity. "I'll protect you… spiritually!"
Shen Feizhi scoffed, untangling Gu Suizhou's arm from his waist. "Stay put. I'll go up first and then pull you up."
Gu Suizhou reluctantly let go, clinging to the edge of the pit and gazing up with his uselessly pretty, blinking eyes.
Using small rocks embedded in the pit wall, Shen Feizhi scrambled up in a few swift leaps. He leaned down, extending his hand. "Grip me tight."
Unexpectedly, Gu Suizhou was so terrified that his strength failed him. He fumbled the first attempt, his hand sliding loosely down, his delicate fingertips brushing across Shen Feizhi's calloused palm.
Instinctively, Shen Feizhi recoiled, jerking his hand back.
Gu Suizhou panicked. "Bro, I didn't mean to! Don't abandon me!"
Shen Feizhi bit his lower lip. "I'm not abandoning you. Stop whining and hurry up."
