"Delicate," Shen Feizhi grumbled, yanking Gu Suizhou up. He tossed the word over his shoulder as he strode ahead.
Gu Suizhou curled his lip, but reluctantly followed.
What choice did he have? He was just plain scared.
The Shen family's ancestral rites had been going on for days, nearing their conclusion. For the past two days, they had to attend daily ceremonies and prayers.
The area was heavily guarded, but the pair managed to sneak in unscathed, just as Elder Master Shen knelt at the front, reciting the eulogy.
Shen Feizhi stood behind a nearby tombstone, listening to the hypocritical words—"Throughout his life, he lived diligently and virtuously, hoping the Shen family's legacy would endure forever, the clan prospering eternally"—and found it all utterly laughable.
Beside him, Gu Suizhou's eyes nearly popped out of their sockets, clearly sharing the same sentiment.
Shen Feizhi whispered, "Do you think threatening Elder Master Shen with digging up his ancestors' graves would make him apply for development rights for W.N?"
"I doubt it," Gu Suizhou replied. "Elder Master Shen is selfish and hypocritical to the core. He's unlikely to care about the peace of his ancestors' spirits."
After a moment's reflection, Shen Feizhi realized Gu Suizhou was right.
"Then what method do you think I should use?" he asked.
Gu Suizhou glanced at him in surprise. "Brother Shen, are you actually taking my advice?"
