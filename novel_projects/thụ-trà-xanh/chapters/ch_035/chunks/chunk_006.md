Shen Feizhi, expressionless, said, "I brought you here to protect me. What's with the dead weight?"
Gu Suizhou swallowed hard. "N-no, Bro… You didn't mention the cemetery was this huge…"
And at night, no less!
The entire cemetery was pitch-black except for a few oil lamps scattered around. The wind kept whistling through the gravestones, sending chills down his spine. Who wouldn't be terrified?!
If Gu Suizhou hadn't been clinging to his willpower, he would have collapsed on the ground and never gotten up.
Shen Feizhi dragged him forward a couple of steps, but feeling the increasing resistance, he abruptly let go and strode into the cemetery alone.
"Hey!" Gu Suizhou nearly lost his balance from the sudden loss of momentum. He stumbled after him, grabbing his arm. "Wait for me~"
Shen Feizhi had grown almost accustomed to Gu Suizhou's occasional hugs. Seeing how genuinely scared he was, he didn't push him away this time. "You're practically taller than me already. How can you be such a coward?"
He'd only noticed a couple of days ago that this damned brat had shot up like a weed, already towering slightly over him at a glance.
Good thing he wasn't the type to equate height with dignity; he couldn't care less.

Gu Suizhou clung to him stubbornly. "As long as Brother Shen's got the guts, I can just be a spineless dodder-vine~"
Dodder-vine my ass.
More like a Rafflesia in a white lily's disguise.
Shen Feizhi spat inwardly and led him through the labyrinthine paths toward the cemetery's central, most brightly illuminated structure.
Gu Suizhou stuck to him like a burr. His eyes were fixed on the path ahead, terrified of unseen horrors.
But life loves to serve up your worst fears.
Barely two steps later, before either of them could react, Gu Suizhou stepped into a freshly dug grave.
Shen Feizhi immediately clapped a hand over his mouth. "Shut up!"
A patrolling cemetery guard, alerted by the noise, rushed over, flashlight in hand. "Who's there?!"
