Shen Feizhi saw Lin Sui again at W.N.'s anniversary gala at the end of the month.

The Director explained that if Lin Sui were the only one withdrawing funding, it wouldn't be too difficult to manage. Plenty of other companies were eager to invest in W.N., and securing additional funding would have been straightforward.

The real problem was the lack of sufficient development authorization.

Advancing an experimental project required development authorization from The Federation. This authorization typically required corporate sponsorship or endorsement from influential individuals before being granted to private research institutes.

Lin Sui's withdrawal triggered two other sponsors to pull out as well, instantly stripping away three authorizations. Securing these authorizations was no easy feat; most entrepreneurs preferred spending their time earning money rather than pursuing scientific research.

Compounding the crisis, several other sponsors were teetering on the brink of withdrawal.

After hearing this, Shen Feizhi knew Lin Sui wasn't acting alone.

The Shen family, having somehow obtained inside information, was undoubtedly behind this, deliberately sabotaging their efforts at every turn.

After the evening banquet, Shen Feizhi successfully cornered Lin Sui at the emergency exit of the underground parking garage.

"Let's talk," Shen Feizhi said.

Lin Sui glanced at him and gave a wry smile. "I don't think Mr. Shen and I have anything to discuss."

With that, he tried to squeeze past Shen Feizhi.

As he brushed past, his expression suddenly twisted, and his legs buckled, sending him to his knees.

Shen Feizhi rubbed his nose, calmly retracted the pheromone he had just released, and looked down at him, reiterating, "Let's talk?"
