The guide readily agreed. "Alright, the Herb Garden is up on the mountain. I'll book the cable car reservations right away."
As it turned out, paying more for a guide had its advantages. Their tickets were secured in no time.
The cable cars were two-seater cabins, each requiring real-name authentication to ensure proper identification of victims' families in case of mid-ride emergencies.
Just as Gu Suizhou swiped his card and naturally moved to sit next to Shen Feizhi, a staff member grabbed him and steered him to the row behind.
Gu Suizhou: "?"
The staff member, seeing his bewildered expression, shook his head disapprovingly. "Hey kid, even if you're close to your brother, you shouldn't interrupt his date!"
Gu Suizhou let out a "Ha!" and instinctively glanced at Shen Feizhi and Leapton.
Lai Gou, having just swiped his card, barged in and dragged Gu Suizhou away from the staff member, whispering, "I just told him Brother Shen and I are in the 'getting to know you' phase, and asked him to seat us together."
Gu Suizhou: "?"
"Didn't you two sign up as brothers?" Lai Gou, oblivious to Gu Suizhou's darkening expression, continued, "I made sure to tell the staff. Don't worry, your honey trap will work perfectly! We'll kill two birds with one stone—you get the trade records, and I get Shen Feizhi!"
Gu Suizhou: "……"
For a moment, he was utterly speechless.
And then, this clueless oaf had the audacity to slap him on the shoulder with an air of righteousness, giving him a reassuring wink before sashaying over to Shen Feizhi.
When Shen Feizhi turned around, he saw not the familiar face he expected. "What are you doing here?"
Leapton replied bashfully, "Gu Suizhou wanted to switch seats with me. He said he needed to ask the guide some questions, so he asked me to keep you company."
