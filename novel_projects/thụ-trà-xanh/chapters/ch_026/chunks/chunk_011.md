"I can't find any information about Shen Feizhi before he turned ten. I suspect someone deleted it. See if you can find anything—even a scrap will do."
Leapton sensed something was amiss. "Could it be—"
Before he could finish, Gu Suizhou spotted Shen Feizhi returning from his errand. With lightning reflexes, he went limp. "Agh! Brother Shen, I think I hit my leg too! It hurts like hell~"
Leapton only caught a blur as Shen Feizhi's figure flashed before him.
Shen Feizhi, his face impassive, grabbed Gu Suizhou by the scruff of his neck and yanked him upright. "Pathetic."
His movements were far from gentle—verging on disdainful and rough.
Yet Leapton could swear he saw a flicker of contentment on Gu Suizhou's face.
Leapton: "……"
Brother, a heart full of gratitude? How could it come to this?
