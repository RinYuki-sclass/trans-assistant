At first, Gu Suizhou stirred restlessly, but after some rustling noises, he fell silent.

Hearing him gradually settle down, Shen Feizhi finally drifted into a light slumber and began to dream.

He rarely dreamed, and when he did, they were usually bizarre, unrealistic fantasies.

Dreaming of the past, as he did tonight, was exceptionally rare.

Perhaps Gu Suizhou's earlier question had unlocked the floodgates of his memories. Shen Feizhi dreamed of the first time he took a human life.

It was a zombie world, and he was still young, lacking the experience and skills he possessed now.

The Small World's Child of Destiny was about to be killed, and Shen Feizhi had been forcibly thrust into the plane to take his place, tasked with escaping until the rescue team arrived.

The reality of the Zombie World bore no resemblance to the half-hopeful, half-desperate, dramatic portrayals in movies and novels. It was an endless nightmare of terror, despair, and the relentless cacophony of screams and wails.

Shen Feizhi had never been so utterly wretched. He crawled through filth, devoured raw meat, drank muddy water—he did whatever it took to survive.

After two years on the brink of mental collapse, he finally collapsed in the mud, awaiting the rescue team's arrival.

Just then, the unexpected struck.

The Small World's critical junctures were fated to occur, but because the Child of Destiny had changed its core, many details diverged from the script.

Earlier, his soft heart had prevented him from burning his teammate's corpse, which had now transformed into a zombie and was charging at him with unimaginable speed!

Shen Feizhi's pupils constricted sharply.

The zombie died.

Perhaps calling a zombie "dead" was inaccurate, but to Shen Feizhi, it was no different from killing a person.

His entire body was drenched in foul-smelling, acidic blood, yet the fastidious man seemed utterly oblivious.

He simply knelt there, unmoving, until the helicopter's whirring blades announced its arrival.

With the critical juncture of the world's narrative reached, Shen Feizhi was about to leave.

His consciousness drifted in mid-air, gazing down at the twisted, grotesque corpse on the ground, and finally understood something.

He had always believed he lived solely to survive.

But in truth, perhaps he existed only to enable others to live. To achieve this, he had to cast aside parts of his humanity: compassion, kindness, conscience, morality…

These were precious aspects of humanity, but not ones he was meant to possess.

He could not allow precious humanity to become the knife that might destroy the world.

Shen Feizhi's dream ended precisely as he left the Zombie World, his eyes snapping open at the exact moment, as if perfectly timed.
