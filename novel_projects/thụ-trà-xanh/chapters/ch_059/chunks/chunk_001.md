Chapter 59
Gu Suizhou didn’t like hospitals, so Shen Feizhi didn’t linger. After contacting the director to collect the hospital's data, he led him away.
The two didn't choose to call a cab for the return trip, instead walking aimlessly through the streets wearing masks.
One reason was to let the hospital smell cling to their clothes dissipate; the other was because Gu Suizhou was in a bad mood, and Shen Feizhi wanted to accompany him on a walk to clear his head.
Gu Suizhou wanted to take his hand, but Shen Feizhi discreetly shoved his hands into his pockets. Left with no choice but to settle for the next best thing, Gu Suizhou grabbed the corner of the other man's coat. "We won't be able to wear these clothes anymore once we get back."
The smell was too mixed and unpleasant.
Shen Feizhi looked pointedly at the hem of his coat. "You seem to be quite fond of them, though."
Gu Suizhou smiled without saying a word, but his hand didn't loosen its grip in the slightest.
Shen Feizhi didn't bother saying more.
As they passed a small stall selling candied fruits, Shen Feizhi suddenly stopped and asked, "Want some tanghulu?"
Gu Suizhou paused, startled.
Shen Feizhi was patient and asked again, "Do you want a stick?"
Gu Suizhou pursed his lips. "I haven't eaten them in a long time."
Not since the year his parents died, when his tanghulu fell to the ground.
He had never eaten them again.

Gu Suizhou was about to ask if he liked this kind of thing too, only to see both sticks of hawthorn candy placed in front of him.
Shen Feizhi said, "Eat. They’re both yours."
In that instant, Gu Suizhou’s eyes suddenly stung.
