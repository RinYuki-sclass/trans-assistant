Gu Suizhou muttered, “There’s barely a five-year gap between us. You make yourself sound so ancient.”
“That’s the age gap according to our Blue Star ID cards,” Shen Feizhi said. “In reality, I’ve probably lived thousands of years longer than you.”
Belatedly, Gu Suizhou recalled the things Shen Feizhi had told him before.
The corners of his mouth sank.
Some might envy Shen Feizhi’s long lifespan, but all Gu Suizhou could think about was how much pain Shen Feizhi must have endured—to have met so many people over such a vast stretch of time, only to end up all alone in the end.
Shen Feizhi paid no mind to his sudden bout of gloom. "Heads up. They’re losing patience."
The two intentionally steered their path toward the street with the ATM. As expected, the presence of those tailing them grew markedly more palpable.
Gu Suizhou snapped out of it. "Wipe 'em out?"
Shen Feizhi: "…"
He shot him a sharp glare.
Gu Suizhou offered a placating grin. "Just a joke. Should I knock them out and dump them back where they came from, or what?"
"If you have to ask me for everything, what use are you?" Shen Feizhi retorted testily.
