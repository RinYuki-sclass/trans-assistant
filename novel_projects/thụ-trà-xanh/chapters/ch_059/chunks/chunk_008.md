"Wait!" The woman hurriedly grabbed his sleeve. "Are you… truly in that much of a hurry?"
Shen Feizhi’s gaze dropped surreptitiously to where her hand gripped Gu Suizhou's sleeve.
Unaware, Gu Suizhou tilted his head. "Yeah, it’s a big deal."
The woman struggled with herself for a moment before her eyes shifted. She let go of his arm. "…It’s not twenty-four hours. It’s only open from ten in the morning until seven in the evening."
