When he didn't take them, Shen Feizhi personally brought one to his lips. "Open up."
Gu Suizhou lowered his long, thick lashes, masking whatever was behind them, and took a bite of the first hawthorn.
The sugar coating was crisp, followed by a faint tartness that surfaced after the initial sweetness, perfectly cutting through the cloying richness.
There was a reason people had been eating these for so long, and why they still sold so well even now, amidst a staggering variety of modern delicacies.
Gu Suizhou was usually very good at playing weak in front of Shen Feizhi, but this time was an exception; he didn't want to be sad.
Taking the sticks, he waited a moment before softly saying, "Thank you."
Shen Feizhi helped him tuck a stray strand of hair behind his ear. "Let’s go."
They didn't head toward the hotel, instead wandering aimlessly for a couple of laps.
Gu Suizhou couldn't finish two sticks of candied hawthorns by himself, of course, so in the end, Shen Feizhi helped him finish half.
He brandished the empty bamboo skewer in his hand. "How much longer do you think they’re planning to tail us?"
"My bet is two more blocks before they lose their patience." Gu Suizhou took the skewer from him and meticulously wiped his hands with a wet wipe.

Shen Feizhi raised an eyebrow and gave an approving nod.
They had realized they were being followed the moment they stepped out of the hospital. It was surprising how much restraint the tail was showing; a full hour had passed, and they still hadn't made a move.
"They aren't sure of our objective yet, so of course they won't risk tipping us off," Gu Suizhou said. "It’s a good thing we had the company’s market research to use as a cov—"
He broke off mid-sentence, looking at Shen Feizhi with a sudden realization. "You did this on purpose?"
It wasn't that he’d decided to check the banks in District Seven because they were here on business. Rather, he had planned to investigate the banks from the very start, using the trip as a convenient way to draw the snakes out of their holes.
No wonder Shen Feizhi had agreed so readily when he asked him to accompany him to the meeting. At the time, Gu Suizhou's entire focus had been on Shen Feizhi, so he hadn't noticed that the other man had actually been controlling the rhythm of the entire meeting!
"You're only realizing this now?" Shen Feizhi chided. "What exactly were you doing during that meeting?"
“I was looking at you…” A wave of lingering fear washed over Gu Suizhou. “Thank goodness you’re on my side.”
