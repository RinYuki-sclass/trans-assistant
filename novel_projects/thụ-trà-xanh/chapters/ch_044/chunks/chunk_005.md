Oblivious to Shen Feizhi’s complicated shift in mood, C2 caught sight of a gaudy, uniquely decorated building in the city district out of the corner of his eye. He pointed excitedly. "Shen Feizhi! Can we go there? Let's go check it out!"
Shen Feizhi, his brooding interrupted, glanced over. His brow furrowed. "That’s not a place for you to play."
"Why not?!" C2 protested.
Shen Feizhi replied flatly, "That's a bar."
"Then I have to go even more!" C2 showed no intention of backing down. "I read in the files before that Blue Star's bars are enchanting. I’m definitely going to see for myself tonight! Shen Feizhi, come with me, pleeease~~"
Shen Feizhi had zero interest. "No."
"Shen Feizhi!!" C2 gripped his sleeve tightly. "Come with me! Come with me, come with me, come with me! If you come with me, I’ll help you look into what happened back then, okay?"
Shen Feizhi hesitated.
Indeed, he still suspected that the events of that year weren't as simple as just the Shen family and Lin Sui's doing.
Rather than masterminds, those two seemed more like tools being pushed along. Who exactly was behind them, orchestrating the whole thing, had yet to come to light.
Thinking of Gu Suizhou, he gave a reluctant nod. "Fine."
"Hell yeah!!" C2 jumped for joy. "Let's go buy clothes!"
The two headed happily toward the mall, completely failing to notice a tall man passing by just as they stepped through the entrance.
The man had the same face as the one on the billboards.
