Chapter 44
The next day, Gu Suizhou was dragged out of bed by Xu Zhong.
“President Gu, you’re going to be late for the meeting.”
Gu Suizhou hadn’t even opened his eyes yet. He kicked at the quilt in irritation. “Another meeting? We just had one last night. What is it for today?”
Xu Zhong replied, “It’s about the allocation of research funds for Luvi Tisqi’s side. They’re arguing again over the funding the company provided for their research.”
Luvi Tisqi, the Director of the W.N Research Institute.
This name had remained low-profile until the end of '23, when the cases of Lin Zizhuo and Gu Lang were overturned. She had suddenly emerged, single-handedly rallying all of their former students to file a petition for redress, pushing the reputations of those two men to their peak.
Only then did Gu Suizhou learn that the loli Director of W.N was Gu Lang’s most prized student, and that she had sacrificed a fortune in wealth and time to clear their names.
Because of this, he maintained a decent attitude toward her and her requests.
However, Gu Suizhou’s morning grumpiness was so severe that even the King of Heaven wouldn’t be able to soothe it. He lifted his eyelids with immense impatience, his eyes brimming with hostility and violence.
Four years ago, he had used the inheritance left by Lin Zizhuo and Gu Lang to invest in and start a company with the same name as the research institute, all to tighten his grip on the ownership of the W.N Research Institute.
Ever since Lin Zizhuo and Gu Lang’s case had been overturned years ago, many powerful and influential families had come to curry favor with him. Using those connections to make a comeback had not been difficult.
Now, the company had successfully entered the ranks of the Interstellar Top 200.
Gu Suizhou sat up, pressing his hand against his temple. He froze for a moment when he saw the numerical scars, still tinged with traces of blood, on his arm, but he quickly recovered. "Have you found Shen Feizhi yet?"
