The lights were dim, and Gu Suizhou couldn't see the face of the person behind him.
But he could smell that faint scent of coffee.
There was no mistaking it.

It was this very pheromone that had tormented him for four years. Longing for a scent he couldn't have, he could only resort to buying coffee incessantly.
He clearly loathed coffee the most, yet for these past four years, he had been addicted to it as if his life depended on it. It was as if he were constantly soaking in coffee beans—buying it recklessly, drinking it recklessly, drinking until he was plagued by insomnia, eyes wide open as he waited for the next day's sunrise.
But every sunrise told him the same thing: Shen Feizhi hadn't come back.
The sunrise on Blue Star wasn't warm. However, for the people of Blue Star who lived in perennial darkness, the dappled spots of light falling on the ground symbolized hope.
Only Gu Suizhou felt as if his entire body were being pricked by needles whenever he walked out.
Gu Suizhou’s eyes suddenly stung.
At this moment, memories of Shen Feizhi surfaced in his mind with unprecedented clarity, playing frame by frame like a silent film. Every page turned felt like a slow death by a thousand cuts to his heart.
In reality, only three seconds had passed.
He heard the man say, "Fighting, swearing, getting provoked so easily… Who did you learn all this from while I was gone?"
His back, which had always been straight and unyielding, slumped at that moment. It was as if by hunching his spine, he could alleviate the dull ache in his chest.
Under the gaze of a crowd of subordinates, Gu Suizhou’s eyes reddened, yet he could only lower his head in disarray. He lacked the courage to look back, terrified that if he did, he would find it empty once more.
With a glimmer of hope, he spoke in a small voice, "Shen—"
"I’ll fucking smash you to death!" C2’s wine bottle landed on his head at the most inopportune moment.
The entire room fell silent, stunned by this sudden turn of events.
As people snapped out of it and tried to stop the two intruders, they were held back by a hurriedly arriving Xu Zhong.
Shen Feizhi was quick enough to catch Gu Suizhou’s limp body as it slumped, shooting two terrifying glares at C2.
