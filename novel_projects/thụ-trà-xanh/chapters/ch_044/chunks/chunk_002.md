Every trace of Shen Feizhi’s existence was silently fading away. Driven to a dead end, Gu Suizhou resorted to an extreme measure—
He used an electric chair to force both his physical instincts and his mind to remember that day’s existence, then carved into his arm every single day how much time had passed since then.
This way, the moment he thought of that day, he would inevitably think of Shen Feizhi as well.
The electric chair was excruciating—so agonizing that his heart stopped multiple times, forcing Xu Zhong to rush him into the emergency room. The hospital practically became his daily haunt.
Standard voltages meant for ordinary people had no effect on him; he had to crank up the current again and again until that day was finally etched into the depths of his mind.
He spent an entire year coming to terms with the fact that Shen Feizhi had vanished. For the remaining three-plus years, he spent every waking day remembering.
Even with no one left who remembered Shen Feizhi, he continued to pour massive amounts of manpower and wealth into searching for this "nonexistent person." Lai Gou and Xu Zhong both claimed he had gone mad, but only he knew just how lucid he truly was.
Gu Suizhou let out a breath and put on a pendant made of a halved wood carving. "Let’s go."
"This is the 'great method' you were talking about?"
Shen Feizhi stood at the entrance of W.N Company with his arms crossed, his tone cold enough to cut glass.
C2 shrank his neck guiltily. "Well, we didn't have much of a choice. You've been gone for almost five years; we had to find a way to make sure nobody noticed anything was off, right?"
