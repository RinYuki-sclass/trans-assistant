In the original plot, Lin Sui's family suffered a tragedy when he was young. Both his parents died, leaving him with only a younger brother.

Lin Sui despised his younger brother. Fearing the original host would fall for his brother, he kicked him out of the house and cut off all contact after they got together.

When Shen Feizhi read the files, he couldn't understand this kind of paranoid anxiety. Now, seeing Lin Sui and his brother in person, it all clicked.

The man in the white coat exuded a scholarly, gentle aura—that had to be Lin Sui. The boy in the school uniform beside him must be his brother.

The two brothers were in completely different leagues when it came to looks.

If Shen Feizhi was a 10, Lin Sui was a 6. His brother…

It was hard to say who was more handsome between him and Shen Feizhi. One was strikingly heroic, the other ethereally androgynous—their styles were entirely different.

But based on Shen Feizhi's personal taste, he'd give the boy an 11.

The boy somehow managed to make Blue Star's notoriously ugly school uniform look like something out of a teen idol drama. With his flawless, cool-toned skin and deep-set features, he was already devastatingly beautiful despite not having fully matured. No one would believe he wasn't the male lead.

As if sensing the gaze, the boy's hand, poised to turn a page, froze mid-air. He snapped his head up.

Their eyes met.

Shen Feizhi couldn't help but raise an eyebrow again.

Those are clearly aggressive, predatory fox eyes—and in the sharpest amber hue, no less. How do they look so docile on that face?

He looked like a little white rabbit, no wonder his brother bullied him.

Gods above, I was terrified that profligate would follow his brother's example and pounce on the kid like a starving wolf, right here and now!

At this, Elder Master Shen's face darkened even further.

He was a feudal old traditionalist who disliked all his juniors equally. His initial reprimand of Shen Feizhi had been routine, but now, reminded of the boy's illegitimate status, his disdain instantly sharpened with prejudice.

"You have a point. You…"

"My mother never taught me high-society etiquette," Shen Feizhi cut in. He hadn't intended to speak, but he refused to be insulted without cause. "She only taught me not to go into heat over people anytime, anywhere."
