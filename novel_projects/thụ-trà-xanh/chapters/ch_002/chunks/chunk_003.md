"Second Young Master, please lie down."
Shen Feizhi agreed, then flopped onto the medical chair like a stiff corpse.
"Second Young Master, sit up a little." Lin Sui pushed up his glasses again. "Take off your clothes."
Shen Feizhi: "?"
He glanced around the hall, which was teeming with servants. "Right here? I'm supposed to strip here?"
To make him strip for an examination in front of everyone… the Shen family truly doesn't treat this "Second Young Master" like a human being.
Lin Sui reassured him calmly, "Just your shirt. I need to attach these to your spine and chest."
As he spoke, he gestured with hands full of wires. One end of each wire connected to the machine, while the other had a small metal patch for skin contact.
The patches were only the size of a fingernail, and applying them would inevitably involve physical contact.
Lin Sui's gaze swept unintentionally over the restraints on the medical chair, a flicker of some unreadable emotion crossing his eyes. "Second Young Master, please place your hands in the wrist straps yourself."
