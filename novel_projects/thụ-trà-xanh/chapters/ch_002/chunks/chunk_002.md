Thankfully, he hadn't.
Uncle Liao discreetly let out a breath of relief. He approached the old man seated at the head of the table with utmost respect. "Elder, the Second Young Master has arrived."
As he spoke, he shot Shen Feizhi a frantic look.
Shen Feizhi, who had just finished sizing up Madam Shen—who was pointedly ignoring him—caught the signal and gave a perfunctory nod. "Grandpa," he said, his tone cool and detached.
Elder Shen rapped his cane sharply on the floor. Thump. Thump. "Making your elder wait an extra ten minutes, Shen Feizhi! Where have your manners gone?"
Madam Shen, who was pouring tea for him while enjoying the spectacle, wore a gloating expression. "Dad, don't bother with him. After all, Feizhi's mother was just a low-class actress who couldn't grace a proper stage. She probably never taught the boy any etiquette."
At this, Elder Master Shen's face darkened even further.
He was a feudal old traditionalist who disliked all his juniors equally. His initial reprimand of Shen Feizhi had been routine, but now, reminded of the boy's illegitimate status, his disdain instantly sharpened with prejudice.
"You have a point. You…"
"My mother never taught me high-society etiquette," Shen Feizhi cut in. He hadn't intended to speak, but he refused to be insulted without cause. "She only taught me not to go into heat over people anytime, anywhere."
