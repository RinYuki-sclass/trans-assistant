But he was just a delicate Omega. How could he possibly handle so much physical labor?
Just as he was about to feign weakness to catch the man's attention, a malicious gaze swept over him.
He looked up to see his own younger brother, seated on the other side of the sofa.
The boy's language textbook had been tossed aside ages ago. He was now slumped bonelessly in the corner of the sofa, his eyes flickering between Shen Feizhi with amusement and Lin Sui with mockery.
Only after Lin Sui had finished did the boy slowly sit up straight, meeting his gaze. His rosy, thin lips parted, silently mouthing the words—
【Are you that desperate for attention, Big Brother?】
Lin Sui's molars clenched, and he lost his composure, snapping, "Lin Zhou!"
His voice, uncontrolled, was loud enough to draw the attention of everyone in the room, including Shen Feizhi.

Uninterested in Lin Sui's contorted face, Shen Feizhi focused on the other side of the sofa, two meters away.
The boy—the "Lin Zhou" Lin Sui had just named—was slowly rising from the sofa, his head bowed.
His expression was completely obscured by his slightly long hair, but his back was ramrod straight.
When he reached Lin Sui, he spoke softly, "Big Brother, let me help you calibrate the equipment."
His voice was quiet but clear, audible to everyone nearby.
Shen Feizhi, who had initially shown no interest in meddling, found his mind immediately turning at the mention of calibrating the equipment.
"You know how to run the examination?"
Lin Zhou, clearly not expecting Shen Feizhi to intervene, instinctively glanced at Lin Sui before stammering, "Maybe… I do."
"What do you mean, 'maybe'?" Shen Feizhi stepped forward, stopping at a polite distance. He leaned down slightly to meet the boy's eyes. "You either know or you don't. There's no such thing as 'maybe I do.'"
Lin Sui's face had turned as black as the bottom of a pot the moment Shen Feizhi asked his first question.
