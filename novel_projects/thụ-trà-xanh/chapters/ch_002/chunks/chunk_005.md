The deep, cold tone sent a jolt through Lin Sui's heart. He looked up in shock.
But before he could scrutinize him, Shen Feizhi had already reverted to his usual cool, detached demeanor, lounging lazily against the sofa. "Call me when you're done moving it."
"Could I trouble you to help me…"
Lin Sui's attempt to create physical contact died on his lips the moment he met the other man's ink-black eyes.
He had dealt with many dangerous individuals, but for a fleeting instant, the look in this man's eyes was more perilous than any he had ever encountered.
Was I imagining things…?
Lin Sui kept his head bowed, utterly lacking the courage to look up again.
He could only grit his teeth secretly. "Yes, Second Young Master."
Shen Feizhi was satisfied. "You've worked hard."
Excellent. An Omega who knows self-respect is a good boy.
He sat elegantly on one side of the sofa, crossing his legs as he watched Doctor Lin bustle back and forth, moving the equipment. He showed no intention of helping.
No one likes an unchivalrous rogue, but fortunately, he wasn't a gentleman to begin with. Playing the part was effortless.

Shen Feizhi waited on the sofa for a while. Confirming there were no fluctuations in the world's energy, he finally breathed a sigh of relief.
His earlier actions were not just to evade the examination but also to test the waters.
It seemed this level of plot deviation wasn't a critical node yet.
Shen Feizhi pondered this, his long, thick lashes fluttering shut as he closed his eyes to rest.
"Second Young Master."
Lin Sui, panting after moving all the equipment, saw the man on the sofa still sitting as immovable as a mountain. He pouted, a sense of grievance welling up inside him.
Without the Patriarch's order, not a single servant in the grand hall had come to help him.
