Before Lin Zhou could speak, Lin Sui cut in sharply, "Second Young Master, he doesn't."
Shen Feizhi remained silent.
Lin Sui rushed to add, "This kid is lazy and disobedient. He barely passes his exams. How could he possibly understand such complex equipment?"
Still, Shen Feizhi said nothing, not even sparing Lin Sui a glance. He maintained his posture, his gaze fixed quietly on the boy.

The boy, however, wouldn't meet his eyes.
Only after Lin Sui finished did he nervously fidget with the hem of his shirt. "I'm sorry, Big Brother."
"What are you apologizing for?"
Shen Feizhi scoffed, straightened up, and shoved his hands into his pockets. He cast a lazy, sidelong glance at Lin Sui. "Was I talking to you?"
For no apparent reason, a thin sheen of cold sweat broke out on Lin Sui's forehead.
His smooth voice turned into a stammer. "Second… Second Young Master Shen, Xiao Zhou isn't very talkative. Please don't…"
"Come in. Calibrate the equipment and run my examination."
Shen Feizhi cut him off rudely, not even giving the brothers a chance to refuse. He turned and walked straight into the room with the equipment.
Lin Zhou froze for a moment, then called out helplessly, "Second Young Master!"
"It's fine," Shen Feizhi reassured him. "Whether you know how or not, just do it boldly."
Both Lin brothers stared, stunned.
Lin Sui's eyes turned red with jealousy.
Lin Zhou's eyes also reddened, his facial muscles trembling slightly. At first glance, it looked as if he were fighting back tears of overwhelming gratitude.
Before he could respond, Shen Feizhi continued in the same flat, conversational tone, as if discussing the weather.
"Worst case, if something goes wrong, we'll just die together."
