He finished assembling the surfboard’s leash and fins, then applied a layer of wax to both sides. After confirming there were no flaws, he handed it to the man. "Let's go. Get used to it on the shore first."
"Wait." Gu Suizhou stopped him, pointing to his hair. "It’s getting in the way."
Shen Feizhi was somewhat speechless.
Always finding something to complain about. A real living ancestor. What a total pain.
"It won't be in the way if you shave it all off," he said. Yet despite his words, his hands moved nimbly to untie the cord with the small wooden sword charm from his wrist to use as a hair tie. "Lower your head."
Gu Suizhou obediently lowered his head, breathing in the pleasant scent of coffee on him with a touch of infatuation.
His hair had already grown past his collarbones; pulling it all back into a ponytail, bangs and all, was no problem at all.
Shen Feizhi didn’t tie it particularly well, but it was sturdy. It wouldn't come loose no matter how much he shook his head.
He always had a knack for making things look hideous yet practical.

"Anything else you're missing, 'little princess'?" Shen Feizhi asked, his voice thick with sarcasm.
"Nope." Gu Suizhou blinked. "Thanks, Big Brother~"
"Then keep up."
Shen Feizhi led him to a patch of water where the waves were breaking into white foam. "Lie on it."
"Lying down?" Gu Suizhou didn't get it. He pointed at the people further out. "Why are they all kneeling when they get on?"
Shen Feizhi was speechless. "Those are professionals. Are you?"
"I could be." Gu Suizhou was a bit resistant to paddling; it wasn't cool at all, and it would ruin his image in front of Shen Feizhi. "I can start at the advanced level!"
A nearby child overheard him and puffed out his chest, telling his coach, "I want to start at the advanced level too!"
The coach glared at Gu Suizhou, then began a painstaking lecture, repeating "No" at every turn.
But Shen Feizhi didn't try to persuade him.
