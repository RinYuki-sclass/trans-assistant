Chapter 51
Shen Feizhi was a man of his word. Since he had promised to indulge Gu Suizhou’s proximity for half a month, he would do so unconditionally.
Even though sleeping together was more difficult for him than being killed, he still agreed, leaving Gu Suizhou in high spirits all afternoon.
Eating an ice cream, C2 watched Gu Suizhou as he bought a surfboard and asked, bewildered, "Did you inject him with hormones?"
"No," Shen Feizhi said expressionlessly. "I just agreed to sleep with him."
"Pfft— cough cough cough!! " C2 nearly choked to death on his ice cream. "No, you agreed to what?! "
"Sleeping together," Shen Feizhi repeated.
C2 sucked in a sharp breath.
He knew that when Shen Feizhi said "sleep," he meant literal sleep.
"May I take the liberty of asking why?"
Shen Feizhi gave him an account of the events leading up to it.
Actually, the suggestions and ideas C2 provided were sometimes quite useful, so he didn't mind communicating and exchanging thoughts with him.
Upon hearing this, C2 remained silent for a long while.
Shen Feizhi inquired humbly, "Is something wrong?"
C2 shook his head with a sigh of emotion. "It’s nothing. It’s good. Just keep it like this."
