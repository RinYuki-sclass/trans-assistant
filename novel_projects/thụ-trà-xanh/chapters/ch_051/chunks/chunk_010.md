C2 was going out of his mind: "Don't treat your life like a joke! Just wait and see if Shen Feizhi chews you out when he gets back!"
"Scold me?" Gu Suizhou suddenly laughed, his two canine teeth flashing provocatively at him. "Brother Shen should know I'm only playing because you provoked me. Guess who he'll scold—me or you?"
He had indeed intended to tease Gu Suizhou into a few snide remarks at the start; there was no defending that.
He opened his mouth, but no words came out. He could only watch helplessly as the kid charged straight into the waves.
The moment the boy's figure was swallowed up, C2 heard an urgent shout: "Gu Suizhou!"
He jolted. "You're done that fast?"
"What is he doing?!" Shen Feizhi had just finished a turn on his surf and saw Gu Suizhou’s death-defying stunt. The hair on his neck stood on end. "You just let him go in like that?!"
"I…" C2 felt stifled, unable to voice his grievances. Compared to being a cold bystander, the truth would likely make Shen Feizhi even angrier.
"Just wait, we'll settle this later." Shen Feizhi gritted his teeth, having no time for further talk. He grabbed a nearby jet ski and sped toward the tail of the wave.
This wave was indeed massive, on par with the one he had just caught.
The crashing spray slammed into the sea’s surface, a chaotic mess that made it impossible to see what was happening inside. Under these circumstances, no matter how anxious Shen Feizhi was, there was nothing he could do but wait outside, hoping for Gu Suizhou to be washed out by the waves.
