Human society is always inextricably linked with nature, but it is when one's life is truly tethered to the ocean that the sensation of living is felt most profoundly.
Shen Feizhi enjoyed surfing partly for the thrill and pleasure brought by the dopamine rush, and partly because, in this moment, even the sea breeze blowing past was free.
He belonged entirely to himself.
As the churning, surging whitecaps began to swell, he dove straight in without a moment's hesitation.
The wave was massive, drawing the eyes of surfers and tourists alike along the shore.
Gu Suizhou sat cross-legged on his board, head tilted back as he watched him.
Obscured by the spray, the view was blurred and indistinct, yet he could vividly imagine exactly how the man looked inside that wall of water.
He felt a sudden heat in his chest, a heavy, swelling throb that pulsed rhythmically against his ribs.
C2 appeared out of nowhere beside him. "What’s the matter? You want a turn too?"
Gu Suizhou didn't spare him a glance.
C2 wasn't deterred. "Forget it. With Shen Feizhi’s level of skill, it’ll take you at least three to five years just to catch up."
Gu Suizhou remained silent, wordlessly pushing himself up from his board to paddle further away to practice.
