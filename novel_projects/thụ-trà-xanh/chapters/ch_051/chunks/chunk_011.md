It was terrible.
This feeling was absolutely terrible.
For the first time, Shen Feizhi discovered that surfing could produce such a sensation of being completely out of control.

Watching the surge of the waves gradually level out without seeing the man or his board being swept out, his heart climbed higher and higher in his throat; a conservative estimate would put his heart rate at 140.
If something really happened to Gu Suizhou, he would tear the Main Star apart until they were forced to wind back the timeline!
Shen Feizhi closed his eyes, already beginning to plot the plans and routes for wreaking havoc on the Main Star.
Yet, just as his hands and feet were turning ice-cold, he suddenly heard a very familiar call—
"Shen Feizhi!"
He didn't call him "bro" this time, and for a moment, Shen Feizhi didn't even react.
Shen Feizhi snapped his eyes open. That wave had completely subsided, and the person who emerged from it was actually still standing steadily on his board!
…He actually succeeded?
Shen Feizhi opened his mouth, unable to describe the cocktail of emotions he felt at this moment.
Survival, relief, anger?
Perhaps a bit of each, but none were quite right.
He had thought himself a competent guardian—the type who, like any other parent, prioritized a child’s life and safety above all else.
But facts proved otherwise; he wasn't competent, and was perhaps even a bit reckless.
Because at this moment, his first instinct was actually admiration—a sense of joy for the boy's success. Only after that feeling ebbed did the delayed terror and worry finally set in.
This was clearly not the reaction a proper guardian should have.
Before he could sort out the right and wrong of it, Gu Suizhou had already stumbled his way over.
