His skills weren't quite up to par yet, after all; his "brakes" were unsteady, and he tripped several times before crashing into Shen Feizhi: "Shen Feizhi! Did you see that?!"
Caught off guard, Shen Feizhi took the full brunt of the embrace. He instinctively raised his hands, loosely circling the younger man’s waist.
A young man in his early twenties is a furnace of vitality; his entire body was burning hot. When Shen Feizhi’s hand brushed against his back, the boy was still trembling slightly from the lingering rush of adrenaline.
He understood that reaction.
It wasn't fear, but excitement. Like him, the boy had never known how to fear death.
When no response came, Gu Suizhou realized belatedly that the man might actually be angry.
He hurriedly straightened up and explained anxiously, "I wasn't being disobedient! I just… I just didn't realize that wave was so high! By the time it came over, it was, it was already too—"
"No need to explain," Shen Feizhi interrupted flatly.
How could Gu Suizhou listen to that? He grew even more panicked. "Brother Shen!"
"I said, there's no need to explain." Shen Feizhi sighed. For the first time, he gave him a genuine smile, the corners of his mouth curving into a beautiful arc. He reached out to brush away the water-slicked hair from the other's face. "You did very well."
Gu Suizhou's body stiffened.
"You did very well, it was brilliant," Shen Feizhi continued. "You're very impressive, Gu Suizhou."
He was never stingy with praise for those who were exceptional.
"However," he pivoted, "taking other factors into consideration, this kind of reckless stunt…"
"Let it be the last time."
