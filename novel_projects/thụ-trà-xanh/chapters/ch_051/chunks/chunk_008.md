"Yo?" C2 paddled over at some point. "Not playing in the whitewater with your little friend today?"
"It’s his first time stepping up; he’s already doing quite well," Shen Feizhi said, subtly saving some face for Gu Suizhou. "He’s practicing on his own today."
C2 laughed. "Then how about heading further out with me?"
Shen Feizhi didn’t refuse. "Let's go."
To relieve stress, the Managers of the Main Star had tried all sorts of extreme sports, and Shen Feizhi was no exception. He had learned everything from skydiving and skiing to surfing.
But his favorites were still surfing and skiing.
Skydiving, bungee jumping, and paragliding involved too much weightlessness, which always gave him a sense of being out of control.
Surfing and skiing were better; they allowed his brain to experience excitement and challenge more stably, providing a proper sense of release.
"Here it comes!" C2 said excitedly. "Whoa! This one's pretty high. Shen—"
He turned his head, only to find that Shen Feizhi was already at the crest of the wave. "Holy shit! How can you be like this?!"
The person closest to the peak of the wave's break has the right to catch it and choose their line—this is known as wave priority. At this moment, if anyone else tried to take the same point, it would be considered "snaking" or dropping in.
In the industry, snaking is considered extremely unethical and rude. Since C2 was a step too late, there was no way he was going to crowd him.
Shen Feizhi arched an eyebrow at him, his tone tilting upward. "This one's mine."
C2 made a face at him but gave him plenty of safe space.
