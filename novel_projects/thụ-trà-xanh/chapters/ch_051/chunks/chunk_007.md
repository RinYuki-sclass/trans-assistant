Shen Feizhi stood by with his arms crossed, watching him with a cold gaze. "Skipping levels already?"
Gu Suizhou weakly raised a hand. "I was wrong, Bro."
He had nearly choked to death; he hadn't even managed to touch the edge of the board the entire time.
Shen Feizhi watched as that hand mischievously grabbed his ankle again. He had to endure several impulses before he managed not to kick him away.
The coach and the kid they had encountered earlier returned for a break. Glancing at the disheveled Gu Suizhou, the coach cried out in surprise, "You really took him to catch green waves?"
"Yeah." Shen Feizhi suppressed the hint of a smile in his eyes. "He only learns to behave after a firsthand experience of taking a beating."
He lowered his head slightly, addressing the kid. "Still want to skip levels?"
"…" With a living example of what not to do right in front of him, the kid instantly became sensible. "Not anymore. Thank you, Big Brother."
Gu Suizhou no longer had the strength to curse.
For several days in a row, Shen Feizhi stayed with Gu Suizhou in the beginner's area.
Surfing was a physically demanding sport. Both Gu Suizhou and Shen Feizhi were the type of people who either didn't do something at all or did it to perfection. They studied with the intensity of people competing in a tournament the next day, which naturally made them far more exhausted than C2 and Lai Gou.
Gu Suizhou had originally thought he could stir up some trouble at night, but who would have guessed that after returning to the hotel, he would fall asleep the moment his head hit the pillow, feeling as though a layer of skin had been peeled off his entire body.
He and Shen Feizhi occupied opposite sides of the two-meter-wide bed, with a clear line drawn between them.
