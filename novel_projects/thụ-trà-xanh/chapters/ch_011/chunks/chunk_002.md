"I'm no expert," Shen Feizhi replied humbly, pulling a thin sheet of white paper from behind him. "This rudimentary data table is all I managed to produce."
Ritter took the paper and examined it for a moment.
The next second, Professor Ritter's voice, cracking with excitement, echoed through Laboratory 7.
"Oh, my darling! You're an absolute genius! A godsend, an angel sent to me by heaven itself!" He clutched the paper to his chest, kissing it repeatedly, before whirling around the lab. He even lunged at Shen Feizhi, trying to pull him into a passionate embrace. "My sweet—"
"Professor," Shen Feizhi said, suppressing the urge to slap him and taking a step back. "Is this data really that valuable?"
Hearing such a naive question snapped Ritter back to reality. "Of course it's valuable! Darling, with your exceptional talent, how could you possibly be just a janitor?"
"I…" Shen Feizhi lowered his gaze, his fingers lingering wistfully over a distillation flask. "I couldn't afford school, so I have no qualifications. Finding any job at all was lucky."
"Oh, heavens… With your innate brilliance, once you master the theory, your future prospects are limitless!" Ritter stared at the buried genius with a mix of anguish and regret. After a moment's thought, he slammed his fist on the table.
"Darling! Come be my assistant!"
Looking at his newly updated "Energy Group A Assistant Work Permit," Shen Feizhi hummed a satisfied tune.
All the exhausting work this past week, rushing to Group A every day, had finally paid off.

He'd finally managed to ingratiate himself with Ritter.
After observing the Research Institute for the past few days, Shen Feizhi realized that drug development was the institute's primary focus, consuming half of its resources. The remaining resources were divided among other research projects.
Among these projects, Group A, the one Ritter oversaw, had the highest likelihood of encountering the Energy Storm Eye.
However, after helping Shen Feizhi with his job transfer that morning, Ritter had also sent him a letter that afternoon. Shen Feizhi only had time to open it now, after work.
The first thing that fell out was Ritter's handwritten note.
The top line, written in large, striking letters, read: 【Dear Darling】 .
Shen Feizhi stared at it for two seconds.
Donning disposable gloves, he tore off the line, crumpled it into a ball, and tossed it into the trash can.
