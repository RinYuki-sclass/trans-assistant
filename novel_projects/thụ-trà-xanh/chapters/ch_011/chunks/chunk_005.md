"Gu Suizhou."
Jiang Che suddenly appeared, standing on the podium and calling out to him.
With lightning reflexes, Gu Suizhou hid his smartphone and looked up. "Huh? Teacher, what's up?"
Jiang Che, long accustomed to Gu Suizhou's lack of diligence, merely sighed and waved his hand. "Move your desk to the side. We'll have a visiting observer next week."
Having a visiting observer in high school was a big deal.
These were either children from wealthy families seeking an easy credential or well-connected individuals passing the time.
Gu Suizhou frowned. "Why beside me?"
"Because there's the most space there," Jiang Che replied.
Gu Suizhou scanned the classroom and realized it was true.
His desk was at the very back, making the entire corner his territory.
Hearing this, he could only reluctantly mutter "Oh" and slowly drag his desk and chair against the wall.
Who's the old fossil coming to observe this time? he wondered. This high school material is so dry and boring. I finished it all in middle school. What's even the point of studying this?
