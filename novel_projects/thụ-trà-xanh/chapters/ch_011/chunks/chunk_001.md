Chapter 11
As the year drew to a close, W.N buzzed with frenetic energy.
Nearly every research group was racing to meet their year-end performance targets, the atmosphere in the Research Institute rivaling that of students cramming for their college entrance exams. Every available hand was put to work.
Even Shen Feizhi, a janitorial supervisor, occasionally found himself pulled into tasks like printing documents.
Heaven rewards the diligent. After his thirty-seventh deliberately engineered encounter, Shen Feizhi finally caught the attention of Group A, the team he had been targeting.
"Hey, you over there, the one sweeping!"
A middle-aged man with thinning hair, handsome in a scholarly way, stood by a distillation flask and waved him over. "Come here a moment!"
Shen Feizhi pointed at himself to confirm he was the one being called, then set down his cleaning tools and jogged over. "Professor Ritter, do you need me to clean the experimental area?"
"Huh?" Ritter glanced at his bare name tag, surprised. "You know me?"
Duh , Shen Feizhi thought. You're the head of Group A, the guy I've spent days researching down to the last detail. How could I not know you?
"Yes, I read your paper on quantum communication in the Great Science of the Modern Era journal last year. I deeply admire your work."
The words were polite enough, but the flat tone betrayed not a hint of warmth.
Ritter: "Young comrade, you seem… not particularly adept at expressing yourself."
But a professor is a professor, after all. Experienced with such cases, he quickly composed himself and asked enthusiastically, "Would you like an autograph?"
"If it's not too much trouble, could you sign my employee badge when you have a moment, Professor?" Shen Feizhi pointed to his badge. "I wouldn't want to interrupt your work now."

"Of course!" Ritter greatly admired such perceptive young people.
He gave Shen Feizhi a thorough once-over, then suddenly noticed the burn scar on the web of his left hand. "What happened there?"
Shen Feizhi's expression shifted to one of mild guilt. "Lack of expertise, I'm afraid. Last night, while attempting to link the energy flows between two isolated spatial realms, I accidentally injured myself."
"You understand Spatial Energetics?" Ritter's eyes gleamed with excitement.
