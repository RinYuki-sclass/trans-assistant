"Is something I buy myself the same as something you buy for me?" Gu Suizhou replied righteously. "I just like it when you provide for me."
Despite his verbal disdain, Shen Feizhi’s actions told a different story; he let the other man go on a shopping spree, silently swiping his card and carrying the shopping bags for him.
The two took the clothes directly to the villa in the First District, filling the new wardrobes to the brim.
"Whoa," C2 said, looking at their overloaded car. "Anyone who didn't know better would think you two just got back from a heist."
Gu Suizhou snorted. "It’s for two people, get it? Don’t be so jealous."

Shen Feizhi ignored him. "Just moved into a new place, we're stocking up."
Moving was Shen Feizhi’s idea.
The Research Institute and the W.N. offices were both close to the villa. For convenience, they had moved out of the flat.
Gu Suizhou had originally lived in that apartment only for Shen Feizhi's sake; now that Shen Feizhi himself was here, he certainly didn't need a house to serve as a substitute, so he had followed him out without the slightest hesitation.
Only after they finished moving did they realize that they and C2 were neighbors, separated by only a single street.
Ever since he learned that C2 was also from the Main Star, Gu Suizhou’s hostility toward him had only intensified, fearing that if he looked away for even a second, Shen Feizhi would be spirited away by the man.
So, when C2 offered to see them off the next morning, Gu Suizhou had righteously refused. He dragged Shen Feizhi out of bed before dawn and headed straight for the airport.
Shen Feizhi asked patiently, "Do you have everything?"
"The assistants packed it all." Gu Suizhou did a quick double-check. "Once we arrive, should we head to the site for an investigation first, or go straight to the bank?"
"Investigation first. It won't take long, we can have it done in a day, then we'll hit the bank," Shen Feizhi said. "Is the hotel booked?"
"I couldn't get a booking." Gu Suizhou sighed. "District Seven’s economic development is too backwards, and the environment is harsh. There are frequent power outages and network failures. The secretariat contacted several places but didn't get any response. We'll just have to figure it out once we get there."
Shen Feizhi’s brow furrowed, appearing somewhat incredulous.
In this day and age, there were still places where the network was unusable?
"District Eight and District Nine are both in better shape than District Seven," Gu Suizhou explained. "District Seven is like a transition zone; they built a lot of high-pollution, high-load factories there. The environment of the entire region is terrible."
