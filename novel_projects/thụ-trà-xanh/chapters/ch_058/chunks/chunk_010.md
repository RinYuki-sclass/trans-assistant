"It was a failure resulting from a mutation in the 172nd version of the serum; it would change the gender of the secreted pheromones. It was originally intended to be permanently destroyed, but someone leaked the news and it reached the ears of those outside."
"Once those powerful and noble families found out, they couldn't help themselves. They took control of my parents first. You know how it is—money makes the world go 'round. No matter how stubborn or unyielding my parents were, they couldn't keep those drugs hidden."
"And yet… their initial research was only ever meant for the common people."
The reason he specified "common people" was because the class divisions of the entire interstellar galaxy mirrored those of Blue Star.
The wealthier the class, the fewer the people; the poorer the place, the more crowded it was. The rich grew richer, and the poor grew poorer. The vast majority of people lived in the "Fifth District" and below.
Second gender differentiation was not only the most difficult ordeal for the poor, but also the only way for those below the Fifth District to change their lot in life. High-quality Alphas and Omegas would have the chance to enter the upper echelons of society.
This had been the original intention behind Lin Zizhuo and Gu Lang’s research, but the results had unfortunately veered in the opposite direction.
They had underestimated human greed, realizing that even if the medicine was developed exactly as they envisioned, it would likely still be exploited by others for nefarious ends.

Disappointed, the two had hoped Gu Suizhou would terminate the research and wash his hands of the matter entirely.
Shen Feizhi had always been emotionally detached, but even he couldn't help but feel a pang of regret now.
Lin Zizhuo and Gu Lang were scientists worthy of respect, yet because of that fire, they had been transformed into "selfish, narrow-minded egoists" in the eyes of others.
Though he didn't know why Lin Zizhuo and Gu Lang had set fire to the entire Research Institute, he instinctively felt there must have been more to the story.
A hint of heartache he hadn't even noticed himself flickered in Shen Feizhi's eyes as he squeezed the hand in his palm. "Then why did you restart this research?"
He had assumed Gu Suizhou had found a new methodology, but it turned out he intended to carry on his parents' original vision.
Gu Suizhou hugged him at the entrance of the isolation room—a threshold between life and death—and asked in a low voice, "Do you think it's right?"
Shen Feizhi didn't respond, clearly waiting for the other to answer first.
Gu Suizhou fell silent for a moment. "I… don't agree with my parents' final stance. Something that could clearly benefit everyone shouldn't be blocked from existence just because of some people's darkness."
Otherwise, it would be unfair to those who need this medicine.
Everything has two sides; he was a bold gambler, and he chose to embrace the positive.
