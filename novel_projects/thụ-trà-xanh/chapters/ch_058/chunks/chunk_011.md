"It’s not 'others.'" Gu Suizhou looked at him fixedly. "Only you. I only listen to you. If you think I’m wrong, I’ll change it immediately."
His trust in Shen Feizhi was absolute, exceeding even the trust he had in himself.

But the words reached his lips and were swallowed back down as he looked into those light amber eyes.
Gu Suizhou was cautiously observing his expression.
Shen Feizhi thought: I wonder where all that usual boldness of his went.
He grabbed the man's collar and pulled him a step forward, avoiding a wheelchair that had almost collided with them from behind.
He said, "Whatever you want to do, go do it."
No matter the perspective, Shen Feizhi's choice would always lean toward Gu Suizhou.
His ideals came from the heart; his emotions belonged to his personal bias.
