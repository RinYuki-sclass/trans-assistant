Chapter 58
Shen Feizhi, of course, did not agree to his unreasonable request.
In fact, to make him realize his ordinary status, he even brought both of their secretaries along for the business trip to District Seven.
It was said that the overall environment of District Seven wasn't much different from a slum: dirty, chaotic, and noisy enough to give anyone a headache.
Before the four of them set off, they made a special trip to a supermarket to buy some affordable clothes.
The shopping cart was filled with blacks, whites, and grays handpicked by Gu Suizhou. Shen Feizhi’s lips tightened. "Didn't you used to wear rainbow-colored clouds?"
Come to think of it, since his return, he really hadn't seen Gu Suizhou wear many bright colors.
Gu Suizhou was in the middle of tossing a pair of black cargo pants into the cart. Hearing this, he froze for a moment. "…Ah, I got used to it. I just haven't changed back yet."
After Shen Feizhi left, every aspect of his life—what he ate, what he wore, where he lived—carried the shadow of that man.
So it wasn't that he no longer liked bright colors; he had simply grown accustomed to this.
Shen Feizhi knew the kid was playing the pity card again, but he was a sucker for it anyway. He put most of the clothes back into the bin and said, "Pick again."
Gu Suizhou pointed at the section of clothes belonging to Shen Feizhi.
Shen Feizhi caught the hint and compromised. "Put those back too. You can just pick out my clothes for me."
The moment the words left his mouth, he had a vague sense that something was wrong.
Sure enough, for the second half of the trip, it was as if Gu Suizhou had been unsealed. He tossed clothes into the cart one after another, all of them styles Shen Feizhi had never touched before.

By the calendar of Blue Star, he was nearly twenty-six, yet he had never worn this kind of youthful, vibrant campus-casual style.
But once said, words were like spilled water. Shen Feizhi, who had always been a man of his word, could only endure it.
The two assistants followed silently behind, acting like invisible people, occasionally glancing at them and whispering a few words.
Shen Feizhi’s manager sighed with emotion. "President Gu… really looks like a little wife picking out clothes for her husband."
