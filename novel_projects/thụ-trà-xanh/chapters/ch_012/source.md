Chapter 12

After the 9 p.m. study hall ended, the students from Class 9 swarmed out of the classroom like bees escaping a hive.

As if their shoes had been greased, the room emptied in the blink of an eye, leaving only Gu Suizhou and Shen Feizhi behind.

Shen Feizhi watched Gu Suizhou dawdling over packing his bag and rapped his knuckles twice on the desk.

"Instead of paying attention in class, you were napping. Think you already know everything? The Federation invests all these resources in your education—just so you can find a new place to sleep?"

Gu Suizhou pressed his lips together, silent.

His parents had died early. In his sixteen years, this was the first time anyone had ever pointed a finger in his face and scolded him for not paying attention in class. He found it…

Strangely refreshing.

Shen Feizhi wasn't usually one for words, but when he started berating someone, his tirade was relentless, like a machine gun firing without pause.

Listening to Shen Feizhi's scolding, Gu Suizhou felt less anger than a sense that his missing childhood was being somehow compensated.

Watching Shen Feizhi's lips move as he spoke, he suddenly realized that this man's mouth could actually utter beautiful words.

He couldn't help it, a grin spreading across his face, "Heh heh~"

Shen Feizhi's words trailed off abruptly.

Gu Suizhou waited a moment for him to continue, then couldn't resist urging, "Come on, keep yelling at me, bro. Why'd you stop?"

Shen Feizhi's expression turned complicated. "I don't talk to idiots."

It lowers my IQ.

He pinched the bridge of his nose and stood up. "Wipe that grin off your face. Come on, let's eat out tonight."

Gu Suizhou nearly tripped in shock. "Did you rob a bank or something?"

"……" Shen Feizhi gave him a fleeting glance. "I got promoted."

"A cleaner… can get promoted?"

"Sure. Everything has room for advancement." Shen Feizhi pulled a work ID from his bag. "If it weren't for the training required for my new position, what makes you think I'd be here?"

"Starting next week, I'll be a research assistant at W.N."

Gu Suizhou hadn't expected Shen Feizhi to splurge so extravagantly, actually dining at the renowned century-old restaurant in the First District.

This establishment had been operating for over 170 years, with five generations of managers. Its prices had skyrocketed alongside time.

Watching Shen Feizhi order lavishly without a hint of reservation, Gu Suizhou sucked in a sharp breath, his gaze drifting absently to the violinist on stage.

He couldn't even remember the last time he'd been here.

Eleven years ago, perhaps?

Gu Suizhou propped his cheek against his hand, his fingers tapping an erratic rhythm on the table.

He might have amassed considerable wealth—hundreds of millions in assets—but Xu Zhong had diverted most of it for overseas ventures, leaving him with little to indulge in life's pleasures.

While he could afford a meal here, the extravagance seemed unnecessary.

Only upon coming of age and inheriting Gu Lang's legacy would he finally be able to loosen his grip on the purse strings and reap the rewards of his efforts.

"Any dietary restrictions?" Shen Feizhi suddenly asked.

Gu Suizhou opened his mouth. "Yes."

Shen Feizhi leaned in, listening intently.

"No spicy food at all. Absolutely no onions, garlic, ginger, or scallions. No fatty meat, skin, or organ meats. No overly greasy fried foods. No carrots, green peppers, celery, lettuce, spinach—especially cilantro! No fish with heavy, pungent flavors or too many bones…"

"Oh, right, and no red bean paste! Absolutely no red bean paste products!"

Shen Feizhi stared at him, speechless.

Shen Feizhi shot him a sharp glare. "Starve, then."

Despite his words, he still avoided most dishes Gu Suizhou disliked.

As the electronic menu hovered on the verge of confirmation, Gu Suizhou's hand, which had lingered for so long, finally reached out to tap the screen.

"Brother Shen, I want sea salt lemon ice cream."

Shen Feizhi glanced at the steadily falling snowflakes outside the window. "Are you sure?"

Gu Suizhou nodded. "I want it."

Shen Feizhi added it to the cart without hesitation. "Alright."

Gu Suizhou's handsome, fox-like eyes instantly narrowed into two beautiful crescent moons. "Brother Shen, you didn't even try to dissuade me! You love me so much~"

Shen Feizhi looked puzzled. "Why would I need to dissuade you?"

"Well…" Gu Suizhou racked his brain for an explanation. "It's snowing so heavily outside, and it's twenty to thirty degrees below zero. Shouldn't you try to stop me from eating something so cold, so I don't get a stomachache or cramps?"

"Are you some clueless kid?" Shen Feizhi retorted. "Do I have to remind you? If you know all that and still want it, it means you're prepared to suffer the consequences. Any stomachache or cramps will be your own fault."

Gu Suizhou's gaze, which had been brimming with tenderness, instantly became as empty as the void: "…Brother, if you didn't have that face, you'd get stomped into the ground and beaten up the moment you stepped outside."

He couldn't fathom how Lai Gou had been so blind to fall for someone like Shen Feizhi.

"I wouldn't get beaten up like that," Shen Feizhi said, taking an elegant sip of the drink the waiter had just served. "And more importantly, don't you want it?"

Gu Suizhou pressed his lips into a thin line, staring blankly for a long moment, his mind struggling to grasp the meaning behind those words.

No one had ever… taught him this.

To achieve a goal, one always needed tactics to earn another's favor, to use some kind of strategy.

Before asking for the ice cream, he had prepared a whole arsenal of reasons to persuade Shen Feizhi, showering him with lavish praise and flattery.

He never imagined none of it would matter. All those meticulously crafted arguments paled in comparison to a simple "want."

"Gu Suizhou?" Shen Feizhi waved a hand in front of him, frowning at his dazed expression. "What's wrong with you today? You're completely out of it."

"Huh?" Snapped back to reality, Gu Suizhou instinctively averted his gaze. "Nothing. Just tired from a long week of classes."

Shen Feizhi let out a cold laugh.

Tired? Judging by how brazenly you were sleeping in class this afternoon, I doubt you've been working yourself too hard these past two weeks.

But he didn't pursue the topic, content to savor his hot drink.

Gu Suizhou's ice cream hadn't arrived yet, leaving him restless and bored. Noticing Shen Feizhi's relaxed demeanor, he couldn't resist stirring up some conversation.

"Brother Shen, have you always been a coffee drinker?"

"Coffee?" Shen Feizhi glanced at the steaming dark liquid in his cup, understanding the source of the question.

He shook his head. "This isn't coffee. It's hot chocolate."

Gu Suizhou: "?"

He stared in disbelief. "That deep color… chocolate? You drink it straight like that?" Won't it be overwhelmingly sweet?!

"Mhm." Shen Feizhi remained calm. "I like sweet chocolate and dislike bitter coffee. What's wrong with that?"

Gu Suizhou looked utterly bewildered. "But your pheromone smells like bitter coffee! And Leapton said you always ordered coffee at the internet cafe."

"So you two really are close," Shen Feizhi remarked, raising an eyebrow. "I only ordered coffee from him once—a casual choice just to start a conversation. Before I could even take a sip, something came up, and I never drank it. After that, he started bringing me coffee unprompted."

"Unfortunately, his attempt to win me over backfired spectacularly."

"As for pheromones…"

"I don't think there's any rule that says you have to like the scent of your own pheromones."

He was right. Perfectly, infuriatingly right.

There wasn't a single point Gu Suizhou could refute.

Gu Suizhou stared silently at Shen Feizhi's self-assured face for a long moment.

Then he shifted his crossed legs, switching which one was on top.

So all that acting he'd done—pretending to be obsessed with the coffee scent—had been a misguided attempt to please Shen Feizhi?

Gu Suizhou felt like he'd been slapped so hard it echoed, both cheeks burning with the sting.

Across the table, Shen Feizhi crossed his legs in imitation of Gu Suizhou's posture.

He was incorrigibly mischievous, relishing Gu Suizhou's frustration.

"Excuse me, gentlemen. Here's the sea salt lemon ice cream you ordered."

A waiter suddenly approached, carrying an enormous glass, nearly half a meter tall, filled with ice cream.

Shen Feizhi stared as the ice cream landed on the table, completely blocking their faces. "…"

He'd never imagined it would be this massive.

"Could you please split it into two bowls?" Gu Suizhou, ever adaptable, asked smoothly, already arranging two small bowls. "It's been ages since I've had your ice cream. It's still as generous as ever."

"It absolutely has to be," the waiter said with a perfect smile. "After all, the price has increased twentyfold. Skimping on the portion would seem rather… unethical."

Shen Feizhi and Gu Suizhou exchanged a glance.

A vein throbbed at Shen Feizhi's temple. "You're quite ethical yourself."

"You flatter me, sir," the waiter said, placing two brimming bowls of ice cream before them. "Is there anything else I can assist you with?"

"No, thank you."

"You're welcome, sir. I'll be taking my leave then. Please don't hesitate to call if you need anything."

As soon as the waiter left, Gu Suizhou eagerly piped up, "Brother Shen, you have to try this! Their sea salt lemon ice cream is seriously amazing!"

Watching Gu Suizhou devour his ice cream with gusto, Shen Feizhi took a small spoonful but didn't immediately put it in his mouth. "Have you been here before?"

"Mhm," Gu Suizhou hummed, not bothering to hide it. "You know, before my family went bankrupt, we were pretty well-off. My parents used to bring me here all the time."

"Back then, you probably just came for the food," Shen Feizhi murmured, savoring the ice cream slowly. It was a pointless question.

The ice cream was soft, fluffy, and melted in his mouth, but after the rich, hot chocolate he'd just had, the sea salt and lemon combination tasted surprisingly bland.

He wasn't particularly interested and set down his spoon after only one bite.

"I didn't bring you here just for a meal."

Gu Suizhou's hand froze mid-scoop, amber eyes flickering with caution.

Shen Feizhi reached across the ice cream, his slender index finger pressing against Gu Suizhou's temple, turning his head to the right.

"See that old man in the white suit on the far left?"

Forced to look, Gu Suizhou's pupils dilated violently the moment their eyes met.

"W.N's largest shareholder besides you," Shen Feizhi said casually, dropping the bombshell he'd spent weeks painstakingly uncovering.

Gu Suizhou immediately raised his arm to grab Shen Feizhi's hand, but the man withdrew it too quickly, so fast that Gu Suizhou couldn't even brush against it.

Shen Feizhi leisurely wiped the finger that had touched Gu Suizhou with a napkin, his tone casual, as if discussing the menu: "He'll likely leave after the violinist finishes their thirteenth piece. That's plenty of time for you to finish eating. Before he leaves, I want you to find a way to get his contact information."

"You have one meal to decide what to do."

Gu Suizhou's grip on the spoon loosened, and the broken utensil clattered onto the table in two pieces.

He abruptly relaxed, leaning back in his chair. "The W.N shares aren't in my hands right now. As for that man… what does he have to do with me? Brother Shen, are you trying to get a promotion by using him? If you really want it, I wouldn't mind selling my charms a bit."

"If you can get it with your charms, that's your skill." Shen Feizhi saw through Gu Suizhou's tense shoulders and the warning signs of an impending outburst, but he remained utterly unfazed. "Get his contact information, and I might give you an account to infiltrate their company's internal network."

"Don't play dumb. I know you've been wanting to steal W.N's current information for a long time, but no one else could do it for you."

Gu Suizhou couldn't find the words to describe his current mood.

His interlaced fingers clenched together tightly, and the veins on the back of his hand bulged uncontrollably.

This man was a complete monster.

Shen Feizhi showed no fear whatsoever under his intense gaze.

Seriously? After enduring so many malevolent gazes back in that post-apocalyptic mystery world, how could I possibly fear some kid who hasn't even grown his first pubic hair?

After calmly finishing his meal, Gu Suizhou finally spoke in a hoarse voice, his tone dropping several notches lower than before. "You've uncovered W.N.'s founder and current ownership."

He spoke with unwavering certainty.

Shen Feizhi readily admitted, "More than that. I also discovered that W.N. originally started as a specialized pharmaceutical research group, and…"

"That drug's research was terminated ten years ago."

Gu Suizhou had regained his composure, and at this, he even cracked a smile.

"Indeed. Many people died ten years ago, which led to the termination of the research."

"You really do know a great deal."

Shen Feizhi lowered his lashes with quiet satisfaction. "That's why I'm certain you want to know the truth about what happened back then more than anyone else. You won't refuse my request."

Gu Suizhou didn't deny it. "Why?"

Why investigate these things? Why reveal this to me proactively? …Why involve me in something Shen Feizhi could accomplish alone?

Shen Feizhi sensed his unspoken questions but selectively answered only the last one.

"Because this will determine whether I exploit you or cooperate with you in the future."

The probing and calculation were laid bare.

This was the most sincere invitation he could extend to Gu Suizhou.
