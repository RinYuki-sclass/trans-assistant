"A cleaner… can get promoted?"
"Sure. Everything has room for advancement." Shen Feizhi pulled a work ID from his bag. "If it weren't for the training required for my new position, what makes you think I'd be here?"
"Starting next week, I'll be a research assistant at W.N."
Gu Suizhou hadn't expected Shen Feizhi to splurge so extravagantly, actually dining at the renowned century-old restaurant in the First District.
This establishment had been operating for over 170 years, with five generations of managers. Its prices had skyrocketed alongside time.
Watching Shen Feizhi order lavishly without a hint of reservation, Gu Suizhou sucked in a sharp breath, his gaze drifting absently to the violinist on stage.
He couldn't even remember the last time he'd been here.
Eleven years ago, perhaps?
Gu Suizhou propped his cheek against his hand, his fingers tapping an erratic rhythm on the table.
He might have amassed considerable wealth—hundreds of millions in assets—but Xu Zhong had diverted most of it for overseas ventures, leaving him with little to indulge in life's pleasures.

While he could afford a meal here, the extravagance seemed unnecessary.
Only upon coming of age and inheriting Gu Lang's legacy would he finally be able to loosen his grip on the purse strings and reap the rewards of his efforts.
"Any dietary restrictions?" Shen Feizhi suddenly asked.
Gu Suizhou opened his mouth. "Yes."
Shen Feizhi leaned in, listening intently.
"No spicy food at all. Absolutely no onions, garlic, ginger, or scallions. No fatty meat, skin, or organ meats. No overly greasy fried foods. No carrots, green peppers, celery, lettuce, spinach—especially cilantro! No fish with heavy, pungent flavors or too many bones…"
"Oh, right, and no red bean paste! Absolutely no red bean paste products!"
Shen Feizhi stared at him, speechless.
Shen Feizhi shot him a sharp glare. "Starve, then."
