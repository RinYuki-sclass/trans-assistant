Chapter 12
After the 9 p.m. study hall ended, the students from Class 9 swarmed out of the classroom like bees escaping a hive.
As if their shoes had been greased, the room emptied in the blink of an eye, leaving only Gu Suizhou and Shen Feizhi behind.
Shen Feizhi watched Gu Suizhou dawdling over packing his bag and rapped his knuckles twice on the desk.
"Instead of paying attention in class, you were napping. Think you already know everything? The Federation invests all these resources in your education—just so you can find a new place to sleep?"
Gu Suizhou pressed his lips together, silent.
His parents had died early. In his sixteen years, this was the first time anyone had ever pointed a finger in his face and scolded him for not paying attention in class. He found it…
Strangely refreshing.
Shen Feizhi wasn't usually one for words, but when he started berating someone, his tirade was relentless, like a machine gun firing without pause.
Listening to Shen Feizhi's scolding, Gu Suizhou felt less anger than a sense that his missing childhood was being somehow compensated.
Watching Shen Feizhi's lips move as he spoke, he suddenly realized that this man's mouth could actually utter beautiful words.
He couldn't help it, a grin spreading across his face, "Heh heh~"
Shen Feizhi's words trailed off abruptly.
Gu Suizhou waited a moment for him to continue, then couldn't resist urging, "Come on, keep yelling at me, bro. Why'd you stop?"
