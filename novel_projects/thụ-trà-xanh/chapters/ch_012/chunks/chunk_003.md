Despite his words, he still avoided most dishes Gu Suizhou disliked.
As the electronic menu hovered on the verge of confirmation, Gu Suizhou's hand, which had lingered for so long, finally reached out to tap the screen.
"Brother Shen, I want sea salt lemon ice cream."
Shen Feizhi glanced at the steadily falling snowflakes outside the window. "Are you sure?"
Gu Suizhou nodded. "I want it."
Shen Feizhi added it to the cart without hesitation. "Alright."

Gu Suizhou's handsome, fox-like eyes instantly narrowed into two beautiful crescent moons. "Brother Shen, you didn't even try to dissuade me! You love me so much~"
Shen Feizhi looked puzzled. "Why would I need to dissuade you?"
"Well…" Gu Suizhou racked his brain for an explanation. "It's snowing so heavily outside, and it's twenty to thirty degrees below zero. Shouldn't you try to stop me from eating something so cold, so I don't get a stomachache or cramps?"
"Are you some clueless kid?" Shen Feizhi retorted. "Do I have to remind you? If you know all that and still want it, it means you're prepared to suffer the consequences. Any stomachache or cramps will be your own fault."
Gu Suizhou's gaze, which had been brimming with tenderness, instantly became as empty as the void: "…Brother, if you didn't have that face, you'd get stomped into the ground and beaten up the moment you stepped outside."
He couldn't fathom how Lai Gou had been so blind to fall for someone like Shen Feizhi.
"I wouldn't get beaten up like that," Shen Feizhi said, taking an elegant sip of the drink the waiter had just served. "And more importantly, don't you want it?"
Gu Suizhou pressed his lips into a thin line, staring blankly for a long moment, his mind struggling to grasp the meaning behind those words.
No one had ever… taught him this.
To achieve a goal, one always needed tactics to earn another's favor, to use some kind of strategy.
Before asking for the ice cream, he had prepared a whole arsenal of reasons to persuade Shen Feizhi, showering him with lavish praise and flattery.
He never imagined none of it would matter. All those meticulously crafted arguments paled in comparison to a simple "want."
"Gu Suizhou?" Shen Feizhi waved a hand in front of him, frowning at his dazed expression. "What's wrong with you today? You're completely out of it."
