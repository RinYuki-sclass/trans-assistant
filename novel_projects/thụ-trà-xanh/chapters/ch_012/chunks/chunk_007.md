After calmly finishing his meal, Gu Suizhou finally spoke in a hoarse voice, his tone dropping several notches lower than before. "You've uncovered W.N.'s founder and current ownership."
He spoke with unwavering certainty.
Shen Feizhi readily admitted, "More than that. I also discovered that W.N. originally started as a specialized pharmaceutical research group, and…"
"That drug's research was terminated ten years ago."
Gu Suizhou had regained his composure, and at this, he even cracked a smile.
"Indeed. Many people died ten years ago, which led to the termination of the research."
"You really do know a great deal."
Shen Feizhi lowered his lashes with quiet satisfaction. "That's why I'm certain you want to know the truth about what happened back then more than anyone else. You won't refuse my request."
Gu Suizhou didn't deny it. "Why?"
Why investigate these things? Why reveal this to me proactively? …Why involve me in something Shen Feizhi could accomplish alone?
Shen Feizhi sensed his unspoken questions but selectively answered only the last one.
"Because this will determine whether I exploit you or cooperate with you in the future."
The probing and calculation were laid bare.
This was the most sincere invitation he could extend to Gu Suizhou.
