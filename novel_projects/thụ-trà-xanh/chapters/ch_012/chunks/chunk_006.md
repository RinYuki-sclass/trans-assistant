"Mhm," Gu Suizhou hummed, not bothering to hide it. "You know, before my family went bankrupt, we were pretty well-off. My parents used to bring me here all the time."
"Back then, you probably just came for the food," Shen Feizhi murmured, savoring the ice cream slowly. It was a pointless question.
The ice cream was soft, fluffy, and melted in his mouth, but after the rich, hot chocolate he'd just had, the sea salt and lemon combination tasted surprisingly bland.
He wasn't particularly interested and set down his spoon after only one bite.
"I didn't bring you here just for a meal."
Gu Suizhou's hand froze mid-scoop, amber eyes flickering with caution.
Shen Feizhi reached across the ice cream, his slender index finger pressing against Gu Suizhou's temple, turning his head to the right.
"See that old man in the white suit on the far left?"
Forced to look, Gu Suizhou's pupils dilated violently the moment their eyes met.

"W.N's largest shareholder besides you," Shen Feizhi said casually, dropping the bombshell he'd spent weeks painstakingly uncovering.
Gu Suizhou immediately raised his arm to grab Shen Feizhi's hand, but the man withdrew it too quickly, so fast that Gu Suizhou couldn't even brush against it.
Shen Feizhi leisurely wiped the finger that had touched Gu Suizhou with a napkin, his tone casual, as if discussing the menu: "He'll likely leave after the violinist finishes their thirteenth piece. That's plenty of time for you to finish eating. Before he leaves, I want you to find a way to get his contact information."
"You have one meal to decide what to do."
Gu Suizhou's grip on the spoon loosened, and the broken utensil clattered onto the table in two pieces.
He abruptly relaxed, leaning back in his chair. "The W.N shares aren't in my hands right now. As for that man… what does he have to do with me? Brother Shen, are you trying to get a promotion by using him? If you really want it, I wouldn't mind selling my charms a bit."
"If you can get it with your charms, that's your skill." Shen Feizhi saw through Gu Suizhou's tense shoulders and the warning signs of an impending outburst, but he remained utterly unfazed. "Get his contact information, and I might give you an account to infiltrate their company's internal network."
"Don't play dumb. I know you've been wanting to steal W.N's current information for a long time, but no one else could do it for you."
Gu Suizhou couldn't find the words to describe his current mood.
His interlaced fingers clenched together tightly, and the veins on the back of his hand bulged uncontrollably.
