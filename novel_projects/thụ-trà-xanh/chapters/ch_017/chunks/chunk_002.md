Having just fended off an admirer, Shen Feizhi watched the commotion with detached clarity.
Truth be told, he didn't much care.
Or rather, the entire Shen family had yet to earn his attention.
What mattered more right now…
Shen Feizhi's spine tensed, his focus almost entirely on the graceful young woman approaching from behind.
The original story had described Lu Qian as a woman of captivating allure yet disarmingly shy, like a lily—outwardly radiant but inwardly pure.
Shen Feizhi had always been remarkably tolerant of women. The methods he used to torment Gu Suizhou were entirely unsuitable for Lu Qian; it would be utterly ungentlemanly, especially toward a suitor so deeply infatuated with his original persona.
He began nervously counting on his fingers, feeling conflicted.
"Handsome, mind if I get to know you?" Lu Qian's clear, melodious voice cut through the air. "I've been noticing you all evening."
Shen Feizhi turned slowly, as if in slow motion. "I'm sorry, I…"
