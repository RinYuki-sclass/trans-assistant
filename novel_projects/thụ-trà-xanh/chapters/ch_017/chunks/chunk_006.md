But Shen Feizhi moved faster.
The glass brushed past Lu Qian's hand and landed in Shen Feizhi's grasp.
She looked back in surprise to see a red cocktail being offered to her.
Holding the glass, Shen Feizhi's cool voice carried a hint of concern. "I think a lower-proof cocktail would be more suitable for you. What do you think?"
Lu Qian froze, realizing he had taken the soju—one of the strongest liquors.
She gave Shen Feizhi a grateful look. "Thank you so much, Feizhi!"
Shen Feizhi shook his head gently. "You're welcome."
Holding the drink he had given her, Lu Qian hurried off in the opposite direction.
Shen Feizhi silently locked eyes with Elder Shen across the room for a moment, then abruptly turned, brushed off the shoulder Lu Qian had touched, and headed toward the restroom.
"Follow me," he ordered Gu Suizhou in a low voice.
Gu Suizhou, who had been pretending to be invisible the entire time, paused briefly before obediently trailing after him.

You'll use that opportunity to get to the third floor. You have fifteen minutes to memorize the entire floor plan. Understand?"
Gu Suizhou replied with a simple "Mm."
Unfazed by his cold demeanor, Shen Feizhi continued, "You don't have much time. When you come down, head straight to the front courtyard and wait for me. I'll clean up any loose ends and meet you there. Got it?"
Gu Suizhou gave another curt "Mm."
"And one more thing—" Shen Feizhi suddenly stopped and turned around.
Gu Suizhou nearly collided with him, his footsteps faltering as he narrowly avoided crashing into a marble pillar to his right.
With lightning reflexes, Shen Feizhi steadied him. "Careful."
Gu Suizhou blinked.
