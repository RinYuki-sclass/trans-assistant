"Also," Shen Feizhi added, "remember to stay safe. If anything goes wrong, slip back here immediately. Above all, don't get caught."
This time, Gu Suizhou's response was far from indifferent, nodding rapidly like a chicken pecking at rice.
"Good," Shen Feizhi said. "Go into the restroom first. I'll keep watch at the door. Climb out the window, then take the back stairs to the third floor—that route has fewer cameras."
"Okay," Gu Suizhou agreed, pushing open the restroom door. Just as he was about to step inside, he suddenly paused, his gaze burning into Shen Feizhi.
"What is it?" Shen Feizhi asked, puzzled.
This kid's expression… it just didn't look like he was up to anything good.
Sure enough, a strange smile curled at the corner of Gu Suizhou's lips.
