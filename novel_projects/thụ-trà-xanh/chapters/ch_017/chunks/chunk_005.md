The notoriously strained relationship between the two young Shen heirs was public knowledge.
Lu Qian noticed his subtle hesitation and patted the illegitimate son's shoulder sympathetically. "Ugh, to be honest, it's anything but close."
"I can't stand that idiot. All that talk about childhood sweethearts is utter bullshit. His mother just wanted to marry me off to him for the sake of our families' power, but I'd sooner die than agree."
"So that's how it is," Shen Feizhi murmured. "I'd always assumed Madam Shen was genuinely kind to you." He trailed off, realizing his slip, and lowered his gaze apologetically. "My apologies. I spoke out of turn."
"It's nothing," Lu Qian said dismissively. "You thought she was being nice to me because she took me to the Shen family's study? That's all bullshit. That 'study' is just a room for storing extracurricular books."
She raised a slender hand and pointed toward a certain direction on the third floor. "See that? That's their family's real feng shui treasure spot. The third floor—I've never even been up there."
Shen Feizhi's lips curled into a silent smile. "Is that so?"
"Exactly!" Lu Qian nodded vigorously. "So seriously, Shen Feizhi, don't let that idiot Shen Wei prejudice you against me, okay?"
She genuinely liked this handsome man.
It wasn't just superficial attraction to his looks; she admired his refined demeanor, his way of speaking, and his entire presence.
Shen Feizhi replied with his usual gentlemanly tone, "Of course. I never had any prejudice against you before, and I certainly won't start now."
Seeing that Lu Qian had dropped the formal address, he tactfully adopted a more familiar tone himself.
"Come on, let's exchange contacts!" Lu Qian eagerly pulled out her smartphone. "We can—"
