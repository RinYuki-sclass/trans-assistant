He moved with painstaking care, afraid of waking the sleeping man with any sudden movement.

By the time he finally managed to put them on, he was nearly drenched in sweat.
Gu Suizhou shifted his shoulders, ready to settle back into his seat.
Just then, Shen Feizhi, who should have been asleep, abruptly opened his lips and mouthed two words.
Gu Suizhou froze, his movements arrested.
With the noise-canceling headphones on, he couldn't hear what Shen Feizhi had said.
But he could read lips.
Shen Feizhi had mouthed—
I've killed before.
Gu Suizhou settled back into his seat. After a moment of absentminded staring, he reached out again to touch Shen Feizhi's left wrist.
How could Shen Feizhi not notice his movements? But he was truly exhausted and certain Gu Suizhou wouldn't do anything foolish with it, so he cooperatively pulled up his sleeve and let him fuss.
Gu Suizhou got what he wanted, his eyes curving into a delighted smile like a child who'd received candy. He twirled the crude little knife between his fingertips, then reached to touch his chest.
There hung the other half of the wooden fox carving.
[ These two are truly ugly in the exact same way, ] he thought.
The first-class cabin of the shuttle had both single and double rooms. Leapton and the guide had opted for single rooms, while Gu Suizhou and Shen Feizhi chose a double room for easier communication.
