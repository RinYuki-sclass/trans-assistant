"Almost there… ten minutes," Gu Suizhou said, his voice slightly breathless.
Leapton's features scrunched up in confusion. "What are you two up to?"
"Don't even ask," Gu Suizhou panted, his heavy breathing echoing in Leapton's ear. "I'll explain when we get there. We're in the lobby now. Gotta hang up."
Ten minutes later.
Shen Feizhi and Gu Suizhou arrived precisely on time to meet the other two.
Seeing Shen Feizhi with his rarely disheveled hair, Leapton's curious eyes nearly glued themselves to him. "Wait, Brother Shen… did you guys run here?"
Shen Feizhi shot Gu Suizhou a glacial glare that could kill. "Ask him."
Gu Suizhou coughed twice. "We took a cab, but I forgot my luggage in the car. Ended up chasing it down for five kilometers."
Leapton was used to Gu Suizhou's antics, but he was more curious about something else. "Did Brother Shen run with you too?"
"I wasn't planning to," Shen Feizhi's aura grew colder with each word, carving out a small, isolated space for the four of them. "He tripped halfway and fell. I had no choice but to chase after him to help."
"……" Thanks for the image, Leapton thought dryly, already picturing the scene.

Leapton's mouth twitched violently, while the tour guide silently averted his gaze.
After an awkward silence, Gu Suizhou, who had nearly buried his head in the ground, successfully remolded his face and brazenly sidled up to Shen Feizhi. "Let's go, Brother Shen. We'll miss the boarding if we don't hurry."
Shen Feizhi merely snorted, choosing not to dwell on the matter.
The four of them had purchased First Class tickets, which meant the lines were significantly shorter than at the regular check-in counters.
It wasn't long before their turn came.
The tour guide led the way, and the first three passed through smoothly.
But when it came to Shen Feizhi, the robotic scanner paused at his left wrist, circled several times, then flashed a yellow light.
The Security Checkpoint's smart scanners were highly advanced. A green light indicated no anomalies, a red light confirmed prohibited items, and a yellow light signaled an unidentified object.
