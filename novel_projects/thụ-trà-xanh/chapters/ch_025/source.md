Chapter 25

Gu Suizhou and Shen Feizhi arrived at the apartment building just a step behind Lin Sui.

Neither Lin Zizhuo nor Gu Lang were extravagant people. The top-floor apartment, at 260 square meters, was more than sufficient for the family of four, especially with the small attic above. They had never seen a need to move to a villa.

Before meeting Shen Feizhi, Gu Suizhou usually stayed at school and rarely returned home. On the rare occasions he did, he often found Lin Sui and other Alphas making a mess of the place. Disgusted, he stopped coming back altogether.

Seeing the apartment again now, it felt strangely unfamiliar.

Shen Feizhi stood a meter away, arms crossed, scrolling through his smartphone, his expression grim.

"One hour. Make it quick."

"Got it, Brother Shen." Gu Suizhou concealed the gauze wrapping his right hand with his sleeve and smiled warmly. "That's plenty of time. I won't need nearly that long."

Lin Sui glanced between the two, puzzled. Only a couple of hours had passed since they last saw each other, yet the atmosphere between them felt… strange. Had they quarreled?

He cautiously asked Shen Feizhi, "Mr. Shen, would you like to sit down and have some coffee?"

Shen Feizhi shook his head. "No, thank you."

Lin Sui didn't press the matter. Instead, he walked up to Shen Feizhi and struck up a casual conversation. "Did Mr. Shen and Xiao Zhou have a fight?"

Shen Feizhi replied, "If you have something to say, just spit it out."

Lin Sui sighed dramatically. "You really don't like me much, do you?"

Shen Feizhi's words carried a pointed barb: "It's wise to know oneself."

Lin Sui chuckled bitterly. "I understand your meaning. But I'm not here to hit on you. I just wanted to talk about Xiao Zhou. He's my brother, after all, and I want him to be happy."

That's an interesting way to put it, Shen Feizhi thought. If he didn't know the truth, he might have believed Lin Sui was a well-meaning older brother, helpless against his rebellious younger sibling.

He didn't call Lin Sui out on his lies, nodding instead. "I will."

"I trust you, Mr. Shen. Xiao Zhou is lucky to have you," Lin Sui said, lowering his head and tapping his toe against the ground. "My brother's still young and immature. Our parents died early and never taught him proper manners. Please forgive any rudeness on his part."

So, he's indirectly calling Gu Suizhou ill-mannered, is he?

Shen Feizhi loathed such manipulative types and saw no need to play along. "Doctor Lin, you're meddling too much. My Omega doesn't need anyone's approval—I have eyes of my own."

Lin Sui frantically waved his hands, panic rising in his voice. "Mr. Shen, you've misunderstood! I didn't mean to imply anything negative about Xiao Zhou. It's just—"

"Brother Shen," Gu Suizhou suddenly appeared behind him, "I'm all packed!"

So quickly?

Shen Feizhi raised an eyebrow and tilted his head to look.

The scene he'd imagined—piles of luggage—didn't materialize. There was only one large suitcase on the floor.

"Hiss." Gu Suizhou shook his aching right hand, sore from lugging the suitcase all the way here. He glanced at his bandaged, sprained left hand with frustration, gritting his teeth as he prepared to lift it anyway.

After all, this injury is my fault.

Shen Feizhi clicked his tongue and swiftly stepped forward, hoisting the suitcase with one hand. "Let's go."

Gu Suizhou smiled faintly. "Brother Shen, would you mind waiting for me by the elevator? I just need to say a quick word to him."

Shen Feizhi glanced at him, nodded, and left, pulling the door nearly shut as he went.

The room was now occupied only by the two brothers.

"You can't even lift such a light suitcase? You're even weaker than before," Lin Sui sneered, his gaze fixed on Gu Suizhou's heavily bandaged left hand. "Did Mr. Shen do this?"

"No," Gu Suizhou replied cheerfully. "Don't let your filthy mind sully my gentle Brother Shen."

Lin Sui: "……"

No matter how much he admired Shen Feizhi's face, he couldn't bring himself to lie through his teeth and call him gentle.

Gu Suizhou didn't care whether Lin Sui believed him. He glanced around the room, his gaze settling on the glass door leading to the balcony.

"You've renovated almost everything in this apartment, except for this door. My father had it custom-made for my mother's birthday."

Lin Sui hadn't expected him to bring this up. After a moment of silence, he nodded stiffly. "Yes, I was the one who arranged for it to be moved here."

"That's why I've tolerated you using it until now." Gu Suizhou walked slowly over, touching the folding glass door with a hint of tenderness.

The glass wasn't particularly high-quality by modern standards, but thirteen years ago, it had been the best money could buy.

Anything Lin Zizhuo gave Gu Lang was always the finest.

Watching his movements, Lin Sui felt a growing unease. "Xiao Zhou, what are you planning to do?"

"Don't call me that. It's sickening," Gu Suizhou said languidly, his injured left hand clenching into a tight fist.

The same young man who had struggled to lift a suitcase now moved with decisive force, his fist smashing into the glass door!

"Lin Zhou!!"

Lin Sui shrieked, his voice cracking.

Shen Feizhi, standing by the elevator, didn't even glance up at the commotion. He continued scrolling through stock market updates on his smartphone.

Sounds like Gu Suizhou's holding his own. Let him play his games.

Inside the apartment, Gu Suizhou surveyed the glass door spiderwebbed with cracks and let out a cheerful whistle.

Crimson blood seeped through the bandage on his left hand, but he seemed oblivious. Taking two steps back, he kicked the door with a vicious thud.

The glass shattered with a resounding crash, showering the floor with shards.

Lin Sui watched the scene unfold, his face pale with shock.

He didn't have time to react. Who could have predicted such a sudden outburst?

This guy's a lunatic!

"My parents bought this big house just to adopt you," Gu Suizhou said, brushing off the glass shards clinging to his clothes. "Call it their karmic debt—I won't fight you for it. But right now, I don't think you deserve any of this."

With the last remaining item belonging to Lin Zizhuo and Gu Lang finally gone, Gu Suizhou felt a wave of liberation wash over him.

"Stay out of my way from now on, and I won't bother you."

"Oh, and one more thing." Before opening the door, he cast Lin Sui a chilling backward glance. "Stay away from Shen Feizhi. Don't let me catch you clinging to him again."

With that, he slammed the door shut.

He hadn't lied. The condition for not acting against Lin Sui was that Lin Sui himself didn't court death.

But knowing Lin Sui's true nature, he couldn't believe the man would honestly live out his remaining years with his tail between his legs.

Besides, Lin Sui hardly seemed innocent in that incident ten years ago.

Gu Suizhou ran a hand through his hair, his agitation growing. But as he rounded the corner and spotted Shen Feizhi at the end of the corridor, a smile bloomed on his face.

He jogged over and shielded Shen Feizhi's eyes from the glare of the screen with his hand.

Shen Feizhi looked up.

Gu Suizhou pouted, waving his blood-soaked left hand. "Brother Shen, it hurts."

Since Gu Suizhou had no pheromones, the only scent emanating from his wound was the metallic tang of blood.

Shen Feizhi pressed the tip of his tongue against his barely noticeable canine tooth. "Lin Sui's work?"

Having spent so much time together, Gu Suizhou had gotten a feel for Shen Feizhi's temper. Playing the pitiful act wouldn't work; it might even get him kicked to the ground and cursed for being pathetic.

So he puffed out his chest. "What do you take me for? I'm the one who beat him!"

As expected, Shen Feizhi chuckled softly. "Come on, let's get this treated at the hospital."

Gu Suizhou's eyes sparkled.

Watching him effortlessly pick up the luggage, Gu Suizhou couldn't resist nuzzling his shoulder. "Brother Shen, you're carrying my luggage? You're such a sweetheart~"

Shen Feizhi had long since learned to ignore these remarks.

He dropped Gu Suizhou off at the hospital near their home and returned to unpack first.

Their vacation from W.N had dwindled to just one week, and further delays would make their trip impossible.

Neither of them knew much about Pashimi Star, so they decided to join a one-way tour group. They'd stick with the guide to get their bearings and learn the basics, then venture out on their own.

But the only reputable travel agency offering departures required a minimum of three participants, and they couldn't find a third person on such short notice.

Gu Suizhou idly fiddled with his heavily bandaged hand, spinning around on his suitcase while his gaze drifted toward Shen Feizhi on the sofa. He said nothing, yet somehow conveyed everything.

In a flash of intuition, Shen Feizhi understood. "…Leapton?"

Gu Suizhou gave him a thumbs-up. "Smart cookie!"

And just like that, their duo trip became a trio.

Gu Suizhou explained that Leapton was familiar with W.N and its inner workings, so they didn't need to be secretive around him during their conversations.

Shen Feizhi's expression, hovering between a smile and something more sinister, was unsettling. "I recall someone mentioning you two weren't particularly close."

Gu Suizhou chuckled awkwardly. "Back then, my cultural literacy was lacking. I've since redefined my understanding of 'familiarity'."

Honeyed words and deceptive charm, Shen Feizhi thought inwardly, before closing his eyes to rest, magnanimously letting the matter drop.

The travel agency had customized a one-way, second-highest-tier VIP travel plan for them.

The guide created a group chat for the three travelers, arranging to meet at the Third District airport the next morning at 8:00 AM sharp.

When Leapton arrived punctually at 8:00 AM the next day, he found only the guide waiting anxiously.

"Huh? Where are the others?" The guide glanced at the time, his expression growing increasingly worried.

The airport was crowded that day, and security checks would likely take a while. With just over forty minutes until their flight, where were Gu Suizhou and Shen Feizhi?

How are these two even less reliable than me? Leapton wondered, cursing under his breath as he dialed Gu Suizhou's communicator.

Just as the call was about to disconnect, Gu Suizhou finally answered.

"Almost there… ten minutes," Gu Suizhou said, his voice slightly breathless.

Leapton's features scrunched up in confusion. "What are you two up to?"

"Don't even ask," Gu Suizhou panted, his heavy breathing echoing in Leapton's ear. "I'll explain when we get there. We're in the lobby now. Gotta hang up."

Ten minutes later.

Shen Feizhi and Gu Suizhou arrived precisely on time to meet the other two.

Seeing Shen Feizhi with his rarely disheveled hair, Leapton's curious eyes nearly glued themselves to him. "Wait, Brother Shen… did you guys run here?"

Shen Feizhi shot Gu Suizhou a glacial glare that could kill. "Ask him."

Gu Suizhou coughed twice. "We took a cab, but I forgot my luggage in the car. Ended up chasing it down for five kilometers."

Leapton was used to Gu Suizhou's antics, but he was more curious about something else. "Did Brother Shen run with you too?"

"I wasn't planning to," Shen Feizhi's aura grew colder with each word, carving out a small, isolated space for the four of them. "He tripped halfway and fell. I had no choice but to chase after him to help."

"……" Thanks for the image, Leapton thought dryly, already picturing the scene.

Leapton's mouth twitched violently, while the tour guide silently averted his gaze.

After an awkward silence, Gu Suizhou, who had nearly buried his head in the ground, successfully remolded his face and brazenly sidled up to Shen Feizhi. "Let's go, Brother Shen. We'll miss the boarding if we don't hurry."

Shen Feizhi merely snorted, choosing not to dwell on the matter.

The four of them had purchased First Class tickets, which meant the lines were significantly shorter than at the regular check-in counters.

It wasn't long before their turn came.

The tour guide led the way, and the first three passed through smoothly.

But when it came to Shen Feizhi, the robotic scanner paused at his left wrist, circled several times, then flashed a yellow light.

The Security Checkpoint's smart scanners were highly advanced. A green light indicated no anomalies, a red light confirmed prohibited items, and a yellow light signaled an unidentified object.

All three pairs of eyes turned toward him.

The man himself remained unfazed. He casually rolled up his sleeve, revealing a black cord bracelet. At the end of the bracelet hung a small, carved wooden knife, about six or seven centimeters long.

"It's just a souvenir I bought on vacation," Shen Feizhi explained, demonstrating its harmlessness by scraping the wooden blade across his skin. "Can't I bring it?"

The Big Sister conducting the further inspection examined the small knife for a while before finally declaring it harmless. "It's fine. You can bring it through."

The three of them breathed a collective sigh of relief.

A false alarm. Leapton and the guide quickly dismissed the incident from their minds.

Only Gu Suizhou remained, his gaze fixed on Shen Feizhi's wrist with a complex expression.

After they were seated and buckled in, Gu Suizhou boldly placed his palm over Shen Feizhi's left wrist. "Brother Shen, where did you get this bracelet? It's really cool! Can I take a closer look?"

Shen Feizhi's hand stilled.

He stared at Gu Suizhou with an utterly bewildered expression. "Cool?"

How can this kid lie through his teeth like that?

Even Shen Feizhi himself thought his craftsmanship was utterly hideous.

Yet Gu Suizhou nodded earnestly. "Let me see it! I want to get one too."

"It's not store-bought. I made it myself." Despite the rare compliment, Shen Feizhi never fell for flattery. "It's for self-defense. I can't just give it away."

He was being honest about this. There was no need to hide it from Gu Suizhou.

Gu Suizhou's eyes flickered. "Didn't the Big Sister at security say it wasn't a weapon?"

"It depends on who's holding it," Shen Feizhi said, pulling down his eye mask to catch up on sleep. He hadn't slept well the night before and was already drifting off. "To them, it's just a toy or decoration. But in my hands, it can kill."

"I see…" Gu Suizhou watched his hand slide from Shen Feizhi's palm. A sudden thought struck him, and he asked almost inaudibly, "Brother Shen, have you ever killed anyone?"

Shen Feizhi didn't answer, seeming to have already fallen asleep.

The engines roared to life as the shuttle prepared for takeoff.

Gu Suizhou put on his noise-canceling headphones and hesitated, looking at Shen Feizhi's bare head. After a moment's hesitation, he picked up another pair of headphones and leaned over to gently put them on him.

He moved with painstaking care, afraid of waking the sleeping man with any sudden movement.

By the time he finally managed to put them on, he was nearly drenched in sweat.

Gu Suizhou shifted his shoulders, ready to settle back into his seat.

Just then, Shen Feizhi, who should have been asleep, abruptly opened his lips and mouthed two words.

Gu Suizhou froze, his movements arrested.

With the noise-canceling headphones on, he couldn't hear what Shen Feizhi had said.

But he could read lips.

Shen Feizhi had mouthed—

I've killed before.

Gu Suizhou settled back into his seat. After a moment of absentminded staring, he reached out again to touch Shen Feizhi's left wrist.

How could Shen Feizhi not notice his movements? But he was truly exhausted and certain Gu Suizhou wouldn't do anything foolish with it, so he cooperatively pulled up his sleeve and let him fuss.

Gu Suizhou got what he wanted, his eyes curving into a delighted smile like a child who'd received candy. He twirled the crude little knife between his fingertips, then reached to touch his chest.

There hung the other half of the wooden fox carving.

[ These two are truly ugly in the exact same way, ] he thought.

The first-class cabin of the shuttle had both single and double rooms. Leapton and the guide had opted for single rooms, while Gu Suizhou and Shen Feizhi chose a double room for easier communication.

With partitions surrounding them on all sides and the only other living person wearing an eye mask, Gu Suizhou brazenly unbuttoned his shirt, revealing his smooth, sculpted chest muscles.

He gently moved the fox pendant aside, then deftly flipped the wooden knife in his hand, its blunt tip now pressed directly against his chest.

In the next moment, he ruthlessly stabbed it down!

The fingers gripping the knife handle whitened with strain as the blunt tip pressed against his heart.

Gu Suizhou lowered his gaze, his face impassive as he watched the small knife press a faint mark into the skin over his heart. Only when the wood wobbled precariously, on the verge of snapping, did he mercifully withdraw his hand.

The small knife landed safely back in his palm, completely undamaged.

The spot on his chest where the knife had pressed, aside from a tiny prick bleeding slightly, was entirely unharmed.

Gu Suizhou smirked, his lips curling into a satisfied smile as he smoothed his clothes.

He meticulously cleaned the small wooden knife inside and out, wiping it spotless before fastening it back around Shen Feizhi's neck.

Brother Shen is talking nonsense.

How could this little knife possibly kill anyone?

He stubbornly thought: Even if someone did die, it would only be because they deserved it—because they willingly chose to die, actively sought out their own demise.

In any case, it would have nothing to do with Shen Feizhi.
