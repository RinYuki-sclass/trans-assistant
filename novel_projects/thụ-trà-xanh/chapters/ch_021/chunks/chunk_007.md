Shen Feizhi idly pondered these thoughts before pushing them aside as temporarily unimportant.
His mind lingered on how Gu Suizhou had brazenly lied to his face last night, and how he'd even entered Shen Feizhi's bedroom.
Countless past survival experiences had ingrained into his muscle memory the instinct to remain vigilant, even when utterly drunk.
He'd sensed Gu Suizhou approach the bed, but remained motionless, curious about the man's intentions.

Thankfully, after some furtive, thief-like fumbling, Gu Suizhou ultimately chose the path of honesty and restraint.
If he hadn't, Shen Feizhi thought, I might have accidentally killed him in my alcohol-fueled, rationality-depleted state.
He sighed.
I really shouldn't drink alcohol, he thought. Not even to save face.
After lying down for a while until the dizziness subsided, Shen Feizhi staggered to his feet and prepared for work.
The living room still held the remnants of Gu Suizhou's failed breakfast and that hideously scrawled note. As usual, Shen Feizhi tossed it all into the trash without a second glance.
While discarding the note, he hesitated briefly between sticking it on Gu Suizhou's bedroom door to mock him or simply throwing it away. In the end, he chose the latter.
The reason was simple: the handwriting was so atrocious that even taping it to Gu Suizhou's door would only offend his eyes.
Only after cleaning up everything did Shen Feizhi leave.
Today, there was no class taught by Jiang Che at Gu Suizhou's school, so he had to go find Ritter to conduct experiments at the Research Institute.
Having been in this Infinite Flow World for two months, he felt it was time for some progress, whether with W.N or the Ability Channel Research.
As he passed a certain street, he spotted an adorable black-and-white stray dog.
But as soon as the dog noticed Shen Feizhi, its wagging tail instantly tucked between its legs, and it scurried away on its short legs.
For the first time, Shen Feizhi witnessed the very embodiment of "utter panic" in a dog.
After a moment's contemplation, he suddenly remembered that this was the very dog he had forcibly checked the gender of last night.
