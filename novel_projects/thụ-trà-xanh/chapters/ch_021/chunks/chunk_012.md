Arrogant. Utterly arrogant!
She tried to warn him with her eyes to restrain himself. "Young man, excessive pride isn't a virtue."
"Rest assured, I'm well aware of my limitations," Shen Feizhi replied, his eyes betraying a hint of amusement. "But you didn't refute the second half of my statement, so someone did report me."
The Director fell silent.
Agh, I hate talking to these cunning bastards with eight hundred schemes in their heads!
Shen Feizhi leisurely observed her subtle changes in expression. "Let me guess," he said casually. "Was it because a photo of me attending the Shen family banquet with a young man was taken yesterday?"
The Director stiffly retorted, "No!"
"Then that settles it," Shen Feizhi nodded. "Now, let me guess again: was the person who reported me W.N's largest shareholder's representative, Lin Sui?"
The Director fell silent again.
She refused to speak.
Lin Sui's reason for reporting him was quite simple.
His "wants" were merely fantasies of lust and desire. Without Gu Suizhou in the picture, Lin Sui's tolerance for him would have been high. But when Shen Feizhi threatened Lin Sui's own interests, those "wants" became utterly worthless.
If falling into Lin Sui's hands before would have meant becoming a mere plaything in bed, physically unharmed, now it likely meant suffering the fate Gu Suizhou had described: reduced to a half-dead, living mannequin.
Shen Feizhi confirmed his suspicions once more: "Just as I thought. Then let me try another—"
"Enough with the fucking guessing!" the Director interrupted rudely. "You're right, I admire your talent and don't want to fire you. Since you want to stay at W.N too, let's make a deal."
That's exactly what I was waiting for.
Shen Feizhi smirked. "Go on."
The morning sun streamed through the floor-to-ceiling windows of W.N's top-floor office, instantly gilding the minimalist space in a blinding, golden light.
The Director frowned and instructed a mechanical claw to draw the curtains closed. "What I'm about to say is… not for public ears. You're not wearing any recording devices, are you?"
Shen Feizhi chuckled. "Of course I'd say no if you asked me so directly."
