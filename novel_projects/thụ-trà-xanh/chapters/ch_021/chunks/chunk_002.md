After Xu Zhong left, the room suddenly felt much emptier.
If not for the constant hum of the kitchen appliances, Gu Suizhou might have thought he was the only one left.
Wait a minute…
Could Shen Feizhi have unconsciously passed out in the room?!
Gu Suizhou sprang to his feet with a carp-like flip, dashed to the study door, and shoved it open with a crash.
But seeing the scene inside, he quickly swallowed the rest of his words.
Shen Feizhi was already lying in bed, fast asleep.
Steam still rose from the bathroom connected to the bedroom, its fresh bath gel scent mingling with the damp air, making the room feel warm and cozy.
This guy's such a neat freak, he remembers to shower even when he's drunk.

Leaning against the doorframe, Gu Suizhou quietly watched the man in bed.
Shen Feizhi's sleeping posture was eerily perfect, almost like a zombie cosplay: hands neatly folded across his abdomen, lying ramrod straight facing the ceiling.
His breathing was so shallow that without careful observation, one wouldn't even notice the rise and fall of his chest. Occasionally, his eyelashes would flutter slightly.
His sleep is so light. Gu Suizhou frowned.
His own sleep wasn't deep either, forcing him to catch up on sleep at school during the day. But based on what he saw, Shen Feizhi's sleep seemed even lighter than his own.
Is this innate, or something he developed over time?
Gu Suizhou pursed his lips, decided not to disturb him further, and quietly retreated, closing the door behind him.
As he walked into the living room, he spotted Leapton emerging with a bowl of noodles.
"What took you three years to cook this? Even Shen's already asleep."
"Huh?" Leapton's expectant expression instantly vanished, replaced by a pitiful look as he stared at the bowl brimming with chicken, duck, fish, and meat. "I just wanted to make it extra special for Brother Shen, so he could have a really good meal."
