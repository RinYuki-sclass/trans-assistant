Shen Feizhi stood rooted to the spot, his expression blank, debating whether to kill the dog to silence it.
Finally, Shen Feizhi sighed softly and decided to spare the creature's life.
What's the harm? Dogs can't talk. Letting it go won't ruin my reputation.
If I were going to silence someone, I'd choose a human with a mouth.
Like that Student Gu, the only witness from last night.
Next time we meet, that brat won't escape a beating.
With this thought, Shen Feizhi waved a cheerful goodbye to the dog.
Mondays at W.N were as dreary as ever, the air thick with apathy. Everyone moved with listless resignation, numb to their surroundings.
Especially those in lab coats—their resentment could fuel ten evil gods.
In such an environment, being singled out felt especially conspicuous.
The moment Shen Feizhi stepped into the Research Institute, he could feel almost every eye turning toward him.
He paused, scanned his ID card, and poked the familiar guard at the entrance. "Anything happening at the Institute lately?"
Unexpectedly, the guard, who had been chatting amiably with him just moments before, completely ignored him.
With a pitying and complicated expression, he took Shen Feizhi's ID card and tossed it into the security safe.
