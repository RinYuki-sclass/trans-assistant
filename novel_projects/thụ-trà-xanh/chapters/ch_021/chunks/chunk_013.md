"You don't strike me as someone who would lie." The beautiful little girl's expression suddenly turned serious as the curtains drew closed, her adorable appearance unable to conceal her overwhelmingly authoritative aura.
But Shen Feizhi wasn't intimidated in the slightest.
His eyes fixed intently on the Director, waiting for her next words.
"We investigated your identity as Shen Feizhi. Apart from some moral lapses, your record is relatively clean."
Shen Feizhi: "…Thank you for your acknowledgment."
If only I didn't have to carry the original owner's "moral failing" baggage.
"I admit, even in a place like W.N, talents like yours are exceedingly rare. That's why I value you so highly." The Director's gaze remained fixed on him. "I need your abilities, and you need a proper platform."
"Now, an opportunity is before you. I wonder if you'd be willing to try."
Shen Feizhi straightened his posture. "Please elaborate."
The Director averted her gaze, her eyes drifting downward. Her voice softened. "Restart the W.N project, which was terminated ten years ago."
When Shen Feizhi emerged from the Director's office, his earlier composure had vanished.
To be honest, he had anticipated the Director keeping him behind to discuss a specific project, but he never imagined it would be about reviving the W.N. research—a project dormant for a decade.
He had been promoted from research assistant to Junior Research Scholar, and the Director had secretly granted his ID card the highest project authority.
The Director strictly forbade him from revealing this information to anyone, not even Ritter.
W.N. was a secret within secrets. The research team hadn't been officially established yet; it would only be formed once she had confirmed all participants, at which point they could openly disclose their identities to each other.
Standing before the archives on the first basement level, Shen Feizhi silently repeated the Director's "Ten-Day Mission":
Retrieve all materials related to W.N. within ten days.
This archives room hadn't been entered in ten years.
His task was simple: find all available information about W.N within ten days. Only then would he pass the evaluation and be released.
During these ten days, he would not be disturbed, except for daily meal deliveries at fixed times.
