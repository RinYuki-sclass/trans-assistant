Shen Feizhi , not Shen Zhi .
So someone had exposed his true identity after all.
W.N was a towering 22-story building.
Using the Underground City's foundation as its base, the upper 15 floors housed small-scale project research labs, while the lower 7 floors, excluding the parking garage on the bottom level, comprised the High-Tier Restricted Area—experimental grounds for major research projects. Entry was strictly forbidden without explicit authorization.
As a regular janitor, Shen Feizhi's previous work area had been limited to the first three above-ground floors and the underground parking garage.
The rest of the building remained uncharted territory for him, like undiscovered continents on a map.
This time, however, the Director himself had summoned him, a figure of considerable importance. For the first time, Shen Feizhi rode the elevator to the top floor.
The top floor's facilities were far from extravagant; they exuded an air of minimalist high-tech efficiency, particularly the soundproof walls and doors…
"I've said it before! No! Absolutely not! No means no! I will not allow it!"
Shen Feizhi: "……"
He stood expressionless outside the Director's office, silently retracting his earlier half-formed evaluation.
