Chapter 21
Ever since he was six, this illness would flare up periodically.
Each time he regained consciousness, all he could see were bloody scratch marks covering his body, especially on the back of his neck, which often became so severely eroded that he needed debridement at the hospital.
He knew it was because his pheromones couldn't be properly discharged. He had consulted countless doctors, but none could offer an effective solution.
Xu Zhong had gone to extraordinary lengths to procure numerous medications, having Gu Suizhou try them one by one. Finally, through a black market supplier in the Federation's Tenth Star System, they found a rare, illicit injectable drug that barely managed to reduce the frequency of the attacks.
Hearing that Gu Suizhou had relapsed, Xu Zhong's normally steady voice immediately rose in alarm: "What happened? When? Did you hurt yourself?"
"Don't worry, Uncle Zhong, I'm not hurt," Gu Suizhou replied, his tone still relatively gentle. "A power outage suddenly plunged my room into darkness the other night. I didn't react quickly enough, and since I hadn't taken my medication in a while, I lost control."
Xu Zhong was stunned. "Nothing else happened?"
He knew that Gu Suizhou's attacks were far more severe in complete darkness compared to his usual episodes!
Last year's incident—when Gu Suizhou had frantically clawed at his neck, severing his carotid artery during a power outage, landing him in the ICU—remained vividly etched in Xu Zhong's memory. He could scarcely believe that this time, the episode had passed with such startling ease.
Yet that was indeed the case.
Gu Suizhou shook his head, admitting his own confusion. "This episode seems much milder than the previous ones. I only lost consciousness briefly and regained clarity quickly."
From the moment the power went out to when Shen Feizhi roused him, no more than twenty minutes had elapsed.
He couldn't recall ever experiencing such a brief episode, leaving him thoroughly perplexed.
Xu Zhong leaped to his feet, his voice trembling with emotion. "Young Master, is your condition improving? Will you recover completely soon?"
