Dragging a soundly sleeping Shen Feizhi out of bed to dry his hair would be no different than sticking his head out for a slap. He wasn't a masochist, and he had no intention of doing something so thankless and strenuous.
Still, ensuring the room's temperature remained warm enough to prevent Shen Feizhi from catching a cold—that was basic human decency.
Only when he saw the damp ends of Shen Feizhi's hair cease dripping under the warm air did Gu Suizhou shift his attention back to the perfectly flawless nape of the man's neck.
Alphas typically have prominent pheromone glands, but Shen Feizhi's were remarkably inconspicuous.
He never wore the pheromone-suppressing patches commonly used by ordinary people. Yet, as long as he didn't actively release his pheromones, few could detect them.
This demonstrated an extraordinary level of control and mastery over his own body and pheromone manipulation.
Such ability could never be innate; even other Top Tier Alphas couldn't achieve this level of precision. It must have been honed through relentless effort and training. Only Shen Feizhi himself could truly know the sacrifices and perseverance required to reach such mastery.
A flicker of emotion crossed Gu Suizhou's eyes.
His hand, hidden in his pocket, quietly clenched the small wooden sculpture that had split into two halves. He gently rubbed it twice before silently relaxing his grip.
He couldn't recall what had happened the night of his psychotic episode, but he vividly remembered the overwhelming bitter coffee scent of pheromones that permeated the room after he regained consciousness—there was no mistaking it.
Was my milder episode this time due to this man's pheromones?
Gu Suizhou crouched until his legs grew numb, then tentatively reached out.

Just a fist's width from Shen Feizhi's face, Gu Suizhou suddenly noticed the muscles in Shen Feizhi's neck and shoulders tensing into a dangerous arc, his eyes still peacefully shut.
This was a primal reaction—the body bypassing the brain's higher commands to execute an immediate response to imminent danger.
Outside of special operations personnel, few would exhibit such an acute stress response.
Gu Suizhou had no doubt that if he moved even a millimeter closer, he would awaken the man before him, and might even be soundly beaten.
He wisely changed course, instead straightening the blanket beside Shen Feizhi's arm and shoulder, tucking it in meticulously.
Shen Feizhi was more dangerous than anyone Gu Suizhou had ever encountered. For now, reckless action was out of the question.
Gu Suizhou rose and casually flipped through the half-read book on the table. After confirming its harmless content, he returned it to its original position.
