Lai Gou said it contained his love, Gu Suizhou thought. If Shen Feizhi really wakes up hungry, I must make sure he finishes every last bite of that "love."

But that's ridiculous! he thought. This kind of "love" only leads to trouble!
To return Leapton's misplaced affection, Gu Suizhou resolutely and with a fearless spirit of sacrifice, grabbed the noodles and devoured them in a few hasty mouthfuls.
After finishing, he snapped a photo of the empty bowl and sent it to Lai Gou, then muted notifications and tossed his phone aside with a satisfied air.
What's all this fuss about Shen Feizhi needing to eat?
If he wakes up hungry, I can just make him instant noodles, right? I can cook too, you know!
Satisfied with his meal, Gu Suizhou's mood brightened considerably.
