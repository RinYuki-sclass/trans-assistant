"Who knows?" Leapton shrugged. "You're always pulling this kind of shady shit."
"You motherfucker—"
"Alright, alright, never mind. I won't stay then," Leapton quickly backpedaled, his survival instincts kicking in.
Muttering under his breath, he added, "They said it was a 'honey trap,' but they didn't even give me a chance to work my magic. What kind of honey trap is this? I don't even know who's supposed to be using it—me or you…"
Gu Suizhou didn't quite catch that. He tilted his head. "What did you say?"
"Nothing," Leapton quickly replied, knowing better than to repeat his suicidal remarks. "Just complimenting your good looks."
After tidying up the kitchen a bit more and noticing the late hour, Leapton said his goodbyes and prepared to leave.
Before leaving, he tried to peek in on Shen Feizhi one last time, but Gu Suizhou stopped him, warning that Shen Feizhi was a light sleeper.
