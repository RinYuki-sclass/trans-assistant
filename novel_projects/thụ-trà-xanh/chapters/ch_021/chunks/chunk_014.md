Shen Feizhi had come to W.N specifically to study Lin Zizhuo and Gu Lang's research from years ago, so he had no objections.
He ran his tongue over his dry lips and pulled out his smartphone to send Gu Suizhou a message.
S: I'll be busy for the next few days and may be unreachable. Don't worry.
After confirming the message had sent successfully, he put away his phone and swiped his ID card at the data room's entrance.
It is now 10:37 AM, Blue Star First District time. Welcome, Junior Research Scholar Mr. Shen Feizhi.
Identity verification successful.
Your access level is: S+.
Shen Feizhi stared at the "S+" rank for a moment before slowly stepping into the data room.
Meanwhile,
Ritter, holed up in a single-stall restroom, was on a call with a number Shen Feizhi recognized well.
"Hello? Uncle Ritter?" The young man's deliberately deepened voice crackled through the earpiece.
"Mm-hmm, I hear you," Ritter replied, his voice also lowered. "Zhouzhou Baby, don't worry. The person you asked me to protect should be fine. The Director himself values his abilities highly."
Miles away in the Blue Star Ninth District Villa Area, in a secluded corner villa, Gu Suizhou's hand clenched his smart device so tightly the veins stood out on the back of his hand. His expression twisted fiercely.
Damn it! After all this time, I still can't get used to Ritter's pet names for people.
Gu Suizhou pursed his lips, suppressing the urge to retort, and said politely, "Thank you. I've troubled you again."
"Hey, it's nothing," Ritter chuckled. "Besides, with his exceptional abilities, I would have protected him even if you hadn't asked!"
"Really?" Gu Suizhou countered ambiguously.
Not long ago, he had unexpectedly learned from Ritter that a new research assistant named Shen Zhi had recently joined the institute, and that Shen Feizhi himself had just been promoted.
What a fortunate coincidence.
"Absolutely!" Ritter exclaimed excitedly. "Zhouzhou Baby, you wouldn't believe how utterly captivating Little Shen is! He's the most brilliant pearl I've ever seen!"
