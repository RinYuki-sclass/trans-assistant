"Uncle Ritter, you should really brush up on your Chinese when you have the time," Gu Suizhou sighed. "Alright, I get it. Please take good care of Brother Shen for me from now on."
Ritter casually replied, "No problem! I'll protect him like he's in my own mouth!"
Gu Suizhou shuddered inwardly. "……"
That's really not necessary.
After exchanging a few more pleasantries, Gu Suizhou ended the call.
When the screen returned to the main interface, he noticed a new message from Shen Feizhi.
Unavailable for contact for a few days?
Ritter had just said he was fine, so it couldn't be an accident. He must be focused on his work.
Gu Suizhou breathed a sigh of relief.
Perfect. I'm busy these next few days anyway. No need to make excuses.
Knowing the situation, he didn't press for details. Instead, he quickly searched for and downloaded a "Little Fox Nods and Kisses" emoji pack, sending it along with a flurry of messages: Kiss Kiss , Throwing a Tantrum , Rolling Around , Being Stubborn . He then switched off the screen.
"Uncle Zhong, is the medicine ready yet?"
"Almost, Young Master. Just a moment longer."
Xu Zhong, silent as ever, stood before a cluttered array of bottles and jars, meticulously manipulating various mixing vials and syringes with a jingling of glass.
Gu Suizhou lay back in the medical chair, the sterile, medicinal scent of antiseptic filling his nostrils and making him uncomfortable.
He reached for a sea salt lemon-flavored bubblegum from a nearby dish, tore open the wrapper with one hand, and popped it into his mouth. The refreshing citrus burst against his tongue, surging through his taste buds to his brain, finally offering a measure of relief.
This morning, before Shen Feizhi awoke, he had deliberately risen at dawn, taking a high-risk Express Shuttle to the Ninth District solely to test the new medicine Xu Zhong had mentioned—said to regulate pheromones.
The raw ingredients had been acquired recently, and his personal physician had spent weeks refining them into this latest iteration, specifically tailored to his unique needs.
Because of this medicine, Xu Zhong, who had already been en route to the Tenth Star System, had abruptly turned back mid-journey.
Xu Zhong watched as Gu Suizhou nonchalantly removed his shirt, revealing the spine on his back riddled with needle marks. He couldn't conceal the heartache in his eyes.
