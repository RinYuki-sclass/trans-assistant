The raw ingredients had been acquired recently, and his personal physician had spent weeks refining them into this latest iteration, specifically tailored to his unique needs.

Because of this medicine, Xu Zhong, who had already been en route to the Tenth Star System, had abruptly turned back mid-journey.

Xu Zhong watched as Gu Suizhou nonchalantly removed his shirt, revealing the spine on his back riddled with needle marks. He couldn't conceal the heartache in his eyes.

There was a reason Gu Suizhou hadn't differentiated yet.

No one knew that Gu Suizhou was actually a genius.

From the age of five, he had shown faint signs of differentiating into a Top Tier Alpha.

But no one could have predicted the tragedy that would later befall his family.

The orphaned young master was devastated. In a moment of carelessness, he slipped away, and no one could find him for over half a year. Only then did the young master return on his own.

Xu Zhong still remembered finding the young master on that rainy day nine years ago, his face caked with mud and barely a patch of skin remained unscathed, including…

His swollen, broken nape.

Clearly, Gu Suizhou had differentiated.

But it seemed the differentiation had failed.
