Chapter 49
After Shen Feizhi left back then, Gu Suizhou had Xu Zhong take the dog back to raise.
He was attempting to keep everything in the world related to Shen Feizhi within his grasp.
In truth, there was something he hadn't told Shen Feizhi: the fact that the drugs in his system hadn't been cleared sooner was partly due to his own deliberate choice.
Because the drug was connected to that man, only through the pain could he sharpen the man's impression in his mind.
Lucky Dog had been well-trained over the years and was very intuitive, making him the perfect tool to coax Shen Feizhi.
Gu Suizhou leaned against the dog, his fingertips idly playing with his smartphone.
On the screen was his chat interface with Shen Feizhi; the other man had pushed over a contact card, which belonged exactly to that "handsome young guy" he had met last night.
He said, "Lucky Dog, quick, wish your dad success in his pursuit of love."
Lucky Dog cooperated with two barks.
Gu Suizhou seemed to have gotten busy; his shadow hadn't been seen for many days in a row.
Shen Feizhi observed silently for two days and found that the man was indeed more diligent about going to work. It didn't look like an act in the slightest, suggesting that what he said before was likely true.
Shen Feizhi was glad to see this development. He breathed a sigh of relief and gradually reduced the attention he paid to Gu Suizhou.
