“Are you letting me in or not?” Shen Feizhi didn’t want to waste words.
Gu Suizhou knew when to quit and stopped being cheeky, stepping aside to make room for him to enter.
The moment he stepped inside, the door was suddenly shut.
The lights in the room weren’t on. Shen Feizhi knitted his brows, and remembering this man's fear of the dark, he moved to find the nightlight switch.
He hadn’t taken two steps before a hand wrapped around his waist.

By reflex, he grabbed that hand, ready to throw him over his shoulder.
But Gu Suizhou leaned against his ear, his wrist trembling incessantly, his voice sounding very weak. “Forgot to turn on the light. My legs gave out.”
Shen Feizhi loosened the grip he had on him and steadied the other by the shoulder. "You didn't think to activate the Smart Butler the moment you walked in? Get up. Turn on the power for the mechanical claw behind you and have it get the lights."
"I can't. No strength." Seeing that he didn't resist, Gu Suizhou pushed his luck. He wrapped both arms tightly around Shen Feizhi’s waist, hugging him from behind. "What should I do, Gege? I don't even have the strength to carry on an affair with you anymore."
Shen Feizhi fell silent.
A wave of panic washed over Gu Suizhou.
Did I take the joke too far? Am I about to get kicked out?
Just as he was about to say something to make amends, he heard Shen Feizhi let out a scoff. Suddenly, Shen Feizhi turned around and reached out, giving Gu Suizhou’s hair a tug that was neither too light nor too heavy.
