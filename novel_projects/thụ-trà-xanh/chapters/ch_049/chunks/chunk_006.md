After Gu Suizhou had quieted down for a period of time, a message finally arrived one day.
【Bastard: Bro, we're planning to move in together to nurture our feelings, but the house we just bought is still being renovated. Can we stay with you for a while?】
Lately, the messages from Gu Suizhou rarely featured those tildes anymore.
Shen Feizhi stared at the question mark for a few seconds before typing.
【S: Sure. Use the villa.】
It was the villa he’d bought before he left, and no one had lived in it yet.
Subconsciously, he didn't really want others staying in his apartment, so he offered up the villa instead.
【Bastard: Thanks, Brother Shen.】
【Bastard: Are you staying there too, Brother Shen?】
【S: Why would I go? To be your third wheel?】

At that moment, Shen Feizhi was playing cards at C2's place. Seeing this message, he suddenly spoke up: "Say, Gu Suizhou wants to move in with his partner, but he asked me to stay there too. What do you think he means by that?"
C2 immediately tossed his losing hand of cards aside and said solemnly, "Holy shit, is the kid sending you a distress signal?"
Shen Feizhi asked, "What?"
"Gosh! Haven't you seen those news reports?" C2 said anxiously. "There are tons of people who get hurt in relationships without even realizing it, and their subconscious starts hoping for friends or family to help them out!"
This was truly a blind spot in Shen Feizhi's knowledge. "Can't a person feel for themselves whether a relationship is comfortable or not? He's not that stupid."
"Can matters of the heart be viewed rationally?" C2 said, frustrated by his friend's lack of insight. "They say the onlooker sees most of the game. By the time they realize it themselves, it'll be too late!"
Shen Feizhi: "…"
C2 was always reliable when it came to matters of love and romance, so Shen Feizhi decided to trust him this once.
