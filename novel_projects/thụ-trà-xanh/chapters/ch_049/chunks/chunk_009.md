Shen Feizhi dodged his touch.
Gu Suizhou’s hand froze in mid-air.
Shen Feizhi said flatly, "Respect your partner. You need to maintain proper boundaries and distance from everyone else."
Gu Suizhou: "…"
He truly understood now what it meant to shoot oneself in the foot.
He was currently harboring serious doubts about whether his recent play had been the right move.
After a short while, Number 9 came over to call the two of them. "Brother Shen, Gu… cough , dinner’s ready."
He didn't know how to address Gu Suizhou.
Shen Feizhi’s keen senses caught the slip, and he shot Gu Suizhou a questioning look.
Gu Suizhou gave a calm smile. "He’s reserved. Usually, the way he addresses me is too sappy to say out loud."
Shen Feizhi tentatively believed him.
That calm composure vanished completely the moment he saw the spread on the table.
Spicy diced chicken, green onion tofu, pork belly, fried chicken skin, stir-fried carrots and spinach… there were even red bean glutinous rice rolls.

It was a precision strike on every single one of Gu Suizhou's dislikes.
They had lived together for nearly a month, yet he didn't even know what his partner liked to eat?
Shen Feizhi was utterly incredulous.
He himself had memorized Gu Suizhou’s preferences since the very first time they ate together, back when he still disliked the guy.
But what came next was even more unbelievable.
Gu Suizhou, the brat, actually sat down as if nothing were wrong. His first move was to pick up a red bean roll, which he ate while praising Number 9’s excellent cooking.
