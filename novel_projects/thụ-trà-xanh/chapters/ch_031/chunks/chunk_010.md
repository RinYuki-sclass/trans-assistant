Shen Feizhi turned his head. "Hmm?"
"Something fell over there. Could you pick it up for me?" Gu Suizhou asked.
Following Gu Suizhou's gaze, Shen Feizhi spotted half of a small wooden carving lying on the ground.

He walked over and picked it up, immediately noticing its resemblance to the one Lin Sui had been holding last time.
So this ugly little thing is important to the kid?
Shen Feizhi's lips twitched. This fox-headed carving is even worse than my own work.
He tossed the small wooden carving back. "If it's so important, keep it safe yourself."
Gu Suizhou lowered his head, muttering a muffled "Mm."
Assuming he was wiping the carving, Shen Feizhi didn't disturb him further.
But where Shen Feizhi couldn't see, Gu Suizhou's expression darkened.
Shen Feizhi had shown no reaction whatsoever to the wooden carving he himself had crafted—not even a furrow of his brow.
Lai Gou had just messaged him, saying Shen Feizhi had discovered he was investigating him. Hearing the described reaction, Gu Suizhou felt something was terribly wrong.
At first, Gu Suizhou thought Shen Feizhi was deliberately concealing their past connection, pretending not to recognize him. But after calming down and thinking things through, it didn't add up.
If he hadn't stumbled upon Shen Feizhi's scar and the wooden carving, he wouldn't have detected the slightest flaw in Shen Feizhi's behavior. Could someone truly act so perfectly? And if it were all an act, why would Shen Feizhi tell him he carved the wooden sword? It would have been easier to maintain the facade otherwise.
Therefore, Shen Feizhi couldn't possibly be pretending not to recognize him.
Either he genuinely forgot their past, or… Gu Suizhou had mistaken him for someone else.
But Gu Suizhou never entertained the second possibility.
Though Shen Feizhi's current personality was completely different from the one he remembered, he remained certain he hadn't mistaken his identity.
