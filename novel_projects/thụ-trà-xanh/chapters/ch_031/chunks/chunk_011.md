Lai Gou reported that all records of Shen Feizhi's life before age ten had been completely erased, leaving only the security footage from the Shen family gate to investigate.
The footage showed ten-year-old Shen Feizhi leaving the estate with luggage.
That day happened to be the departure date for C1907.
The next clip jumped forward more than a year, showing Shen Feizhi returning.
He couldn't believe in such coincidences.
Gu Suizhou pinched the bridge of his nose.
He still couldn't figure out why Shen Feizhi had lost his memory, but since the other man didn't remember any of this, his recent attitude toward Gu Suizhou must have been incredibly awkward.
He couldn't just blurt out the truth. Shen Feizhi might just think he was making things up again.
He didn't want such an important memory to become just a story Shen Feizhi had heard from someone else.
Lost in thought, Gu Suizhou slowed his pace.
Watching Shen Feizhi maintain his steady stride, showing no intention of waiting for him, Gu Suizhou ground his back teeth.
If I don't want to control the distance between us, I'll just have to find an excuse for my actions.
"Brother Shen!" he called out.
Shen Feizhi stopped impatiently. "What is it now?"
As he turned around, a massive bouquet of roses was shoved right into his face.
"……" Shen Feizhi felt more and more eyes converging on them. He said coolly, "What are you up to now?"
Gu Suizhou held the roses ostentatiously, completely unfazed by the stares around them. "There's something I've been thinking about for a long time. I can't keep it to myself any longer. I thought I should tell you first."
Shen Feizhi watched Gu Suizhou's mouth open and close, a bad feeling surging through him. Before another shocking remark could escape, he swiftly grabbed Gu Suizhou by the collar and dragged him into a deserted alleyway.
Even before Shen Feizhi had steadied his feet, Gu Suizhou's brash voice rang out: "Brother Shen, I think my differentiation is coming soon. The doctor said I'm highly likely to differentiate into an Omega, and the later it happens, the more dangerous it'll be. My heat cycles and all that won't be manageable with just medicine."
Shen Feizhi's unease deepened. "So?"
