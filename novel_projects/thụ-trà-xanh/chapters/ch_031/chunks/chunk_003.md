Shen Feizhi suddenly felt a pang of sentimentality.
This kid had a self-destructive streak, just like Shen Feizhi used to. Always chasing the ultimate thrill and pain, always resorting to the bloodiest, most brutal solutions.
After all this time spent trying to change, so long he'd almost forgotten his old self, he never imagined he'd one day see his younger self reflected in someone else.
This isn't a good habit, he thought. It needs to be corrected.
He shifted from crossed legs to a straddle, pulling Gu Suizhou—who was lying on the floor—between his knees and forcing him to kneel on one leg. His five fingers, now gloved in white silk, gently pinched Gu Suizhou's neck.
"You guessed correctly. But I'll say it again: relying on self-harm to achieve your goals is the most foolish approach. I don't want to see you do it again."

Gu Suizhou smiled. "So there'll be a next time? Does that mean Bro's forgiving me?"
Shen Feizhi raised an eyebrow, his gaze pointedly drifting to the pulse monitor showing "3895" heartbeats.
Gu Suizhou understood instantly. He leaned forward, his bathrobe hanging loosely as if it might slip off at any moment, and buried his face in Shen Feizhi's lean abdomen, which was devoid of even the slightest excess fat.
"Please, please, please, Big Brother," he murmured. "Forgive me."
His words flowed effortlessly, his emotions natural and unburdened.
Shen Feizhi ignored the exquisitely sculpted lines of Gu Suizhou's upper body, nearly bare, and kicked his knee. "Your hand."
Gu Suizhou obediently raised his arm.
With a practiced motion, Shen Feizhi gripped his wrist and effortlessly snapped the joint back into place with two sharp cracks . "Don't strain it for a while. Move aside, I'm going to bed."
Seeing Shen Feizhi about to rise, Gu Suizhou swiftly wrapped his arms around his waist and forced him back down. "Wait! You haven't said whether you forgive me yet!"
Shen Feizhi stood up forcefully, his face dark. "Get lost. Don't make me repeat myself."
But Gu Suizhou, that brat, bloomed with sunshine at the slightest hint of warmth, sticking to him like a leech, never more than a centimeter away. "Bro~ Do I still have to move out?"
"Are you still mad at me?"
