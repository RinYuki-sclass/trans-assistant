He rubbed his slender wrist between his palms. "Brother Shen~ Have you made up your mind—"
A sudden ringtone cut him off.
Shen Feizhi glanced at the caller ID, gestured for him to be quiet, and answered directly. "Hello?"
"Yes, speaking."
"The house is finalized? Then I'll probably… come take a look this Saturday."
"Alright, yes, you can handle the rest."
Gu Suizhou pricked up his ears, listening intently.
Brother Shen is… buying a house?
We're going to have our own house?!
Gu Suizhou's canines peeked out in a joyful grin. "Brother Shen, you really don't need to spend so much…"
"Wait," Shen Feizhi calmly held up a silencing hand, then dialed another number. "Lu Qian, are you free this Saturday?"
Gu Suizhou: "?"
Shen Feizhi didn't notice the sudden glint vanish from Gu Suizhou's eyes.
"Mm, it's settled. I'll pick you up on Saturday, and we can go look at houses together."
Gu Suizhou: "……"
His face darkened, and he abruptly ended the call.
Shen Feizhi frowned. "What was that about?"
"You're buying a house?" Gu Suizhou demanded.
"Yeah," Shen Feizhi replied, bewildered by his sudden outburst. "What's wrong? Do you want one too? Then study hard, earn your own money, and buy one yourself."
Gu Suizhou, infuriated, snapped, "It's not about the house!"
