As Gu Suizhou watched him discard the can, he asked, "Why are you smoking so much?"
"Things came up," Shen Feizhi replied tersely, unwilling to elaborate.
"So you deal with problems by smoking?" Gu Suizhou retorted, displeasure in his voice. "Smoking is bad for your health. You told me yesterday that any method of harming oneself is foolish. Isn't relying on cigarettes to relieve your anxiety just another foolish way of harming yourself?"
Shen Feizhi clenched his jaw. Damn brat. Meddling in everything—heaven, earth, and now my personal affairs?
He skirted the issue, standing at a distance to let the smoke dissipate.
"Skipping class to come here?"
"Yeah," Gu Suizhou admitted. These days, he avoided lying to Shen Feizhi whenever possible.
Shen Feizhi rubbed his fingertips, still warm from pinching the cigarette butt. He refrained from scolding him. "Didn't I tell you not to come?"

Gu Suizhou refused to back down this time. "Then why were you waiting here?"
Shen Feizhi knew Gu Suizhou too well. He'd known the kid wouldn't listen; telling him not to come would only make him more determined.
They fell into a shared silence.
Maintaining a tacit meter's distance, they walked back side-by-side.
Gu Suizhou was worried that Shen Feizhi was facing some trouble, forcing him to chain-smoke like this.
Meanwhile, Shen Feizhi was fretting over how to handle his interactions with Gu Suizhou.
I can't just tell this kid, "Your benefactor is dead because I had to complete a mission and took his place."
Given Gu Suizhou's ruthless, familial ties-be-damned temperament when enraged, such a confession would be tantamount to begging the boy to kill him.
But keeping the truth from Gu Suizhou…
A lie always requires countless more lies to maintain. Shen Feizhi couldn't bring himself to deceive the boy into staying away, especially with Gu Suizhou's sharp intellect. He couldn't guarantee his lies would remain flawless.
Lost in his thoughts, Shen Feizhi was startled by a soft click . "Brother Shen," Gu Suizhou called out.
