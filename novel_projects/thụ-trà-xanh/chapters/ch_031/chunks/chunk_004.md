"Enough!" Shen Feizhi, thoroughly annoyed, slapped on the overhead light and finally relented. "I forgive you."
He just wanted to kick the brat out, take a shower, and get to bed early—he had work tomorrow.
Unexpectedly, the little bastard was truly fearless. Seizing his unguarded moment, Gu Suizhou swiftly grabbed Shen Feizhi's right hand and pressed a kiss to the back of it. "Thanks, Bro. Good night~"
Gu Suizhou winked at him before slipping out like greased lightning.
Left alone, Shen Feizhi raised his right hand, his eyes darkening with murderous intent.
The next morning, Gu Suizhou noticed Shen Feizhi's right hand was wrapped in bandages.
He was taken aback. "What happened? Did you get injured overnight?"
Shen Feizhi snapped, "A dog bit me."
Gu Suizhou was flattered beyond belief. "Just one bite and you're bandaging it up? Afraid it'll rub off? Aiya, there's really no need to be so precious. If you want me to be on standby, I'll kiss wherever you point!"
His earnestness was disarming; most people wouldn't have the heart to slap his grinning face.
But alas, he was dealing with Shen Feizhi, a man with a heart of iron and stone.
The steely-hearted Mr. Shen calmly unwrapped the bandage, revealing a broken-skinned, slightly inflamed wound beneath. "I scrubbed too hard while showering last night."
Gu Suizhou stared in silence.
Are you that disgusted? Over just one kiss?
