Simultaneously, the pulse rate on the screen spiked sharply.
This time, it was from the pain.
Shen Feizhi clicked his tongue, watching the young man before him pant in shallow breaths, his hands hanging limply at his sides as he walked toward him with slow, unwavering steps.
He didn't try to stop him, remaining seated as the other man knelt before him, lowering his head and biting Shen Feizhi's wrist—the one holding the antidote—through the long-sleeved shirt.
That slender, pale leg was exposed to the air, the robe draping down to barely cover his vital areas and the base of his thigh.
Shen Feizhi lowered his lashes, concealing the amusement in his eyes.
"Is this your idea of playing fair?"
Gu Suizhou couldn't speak, but he bit down harder, though not enough to hurt.
Shen Feizhi followed his silent demand, plunging the syringe into his neck.
As the drug took effect, Gu Suizhou let out two sharp gasps, finally regaining his voice.

The moment he could speak, he surged to his feet, throwing himself onto Shen Feizhi like a boneless creature, giving him no time to react.
Shen Feizhi grabbed him by the scruff of his neck and threw him back to the ground. "Quit flailing around."
"Brother Shen," Gu Suizhou's voice was still hoarse, "wasn't this whole performance today for exactly this?"
He raised his arm, shaking his wrist and thumb, both forcefully dislocated while trying to break free of the handcuffs. "To teach me a lesson."
Shen Feizhi was slightly surprised, but he didn't deny it.
He had indeed intended to teach the younger man a lesson.
He had assembled the code from four different ciphers. Someone untrained like Gu Suizhou couldn't possibly crack it. From the start, he never considered the possibility of the younger man succeeding.
He simply wanted to see how this little troublemaker would react in such a situation.
Sure enough, Gu Suizhou had chosen the most brutal method.
