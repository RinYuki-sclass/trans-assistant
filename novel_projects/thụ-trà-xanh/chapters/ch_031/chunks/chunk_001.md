Chapter 31
As soon as Shen Feizhi finished speaking, he noticed the young man across from him's heart rate not only failing to decrease, but instead skyrocketing exponentially, reaching 120 beats per minute.
Such poor psychological resilience?
He didn't realize that Gu Suizhou wasn't afraid, but rather thrilled.
It wasn't just the cold touch of the handcuffs that excited him; Shen Feizhi's expression and posture as he spoke those words also sent a thrill through him.
Gu Suizhou twisted his head away awkwardly, forcing himself to stop looking at the man across from him. He tried to focus his attention on the screen displaying the password.
After two rounds of password sequences flashed across the screen, his face fell.
What kind of god is Shen Feizhi? What the hell is this?!
Gu Suizhou had never studied cryptography. He only knew basic Morse code and similar deciphering techniques. Faced with this incomprehensible mess, even if he forced himself to try, he doubted he could crack even one code in half an hour.
At this rate, he was certain to lose.
Shen Feizhi observed his dejection and tapped the screen with his fingertips, his interest piqued.
Tap, tap, tap, tap…
The rhythmic tapping sounded like a death countdown, its relentless beat stirring panic in his heart.
Gu Suizhou felt a pang of helplessness. Even in this state, why is he kicking me when I'm down?
