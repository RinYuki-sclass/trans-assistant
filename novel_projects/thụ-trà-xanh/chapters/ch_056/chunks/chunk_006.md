“Friends, I suppose,” Shen Feizhi said airily.
Without even thinking, Gu Suizhou blurted out, “Fuck!”
A palm landed on his head.
Shen Feizhi’s smile was faint. “Care to say that again?”
“…I’m sorry, I won’t say it again.” Gu Suizhou pursed his lips, rubbing his head with a look of aggrieved pouting. He hunched his back and buried his head in Shen Feizhi’s shoulder. “Shen Feizhi~”
“If you have something to say, say it properly. Don’t act like a spoiled child.”
The guy had cut his hair into long layers; although it was much shorter than before, it was even pricklier against the skin.
It wasn’t quite long enough for a braid, so he could only gather the loose strands in the front and tie them into a tiny ponytail at the back.
It was much less of a hassle than long hair. If truth be told, based on Shen Feizhi’s personal taste, he really liked this length.

Gu Suizhou brushed aside some stray hairs to stop them from getting in the way. “Big Brother, I’m begging you, date me.”
Shen Feizhi: “…”
Were kids these days all this direct?
He found it somewhat amusing. “On what grounds?”
“On the grounds that I really, really like you.” Gu Suizhou gave his neck a light nip and mumbled, “You don’t reject me anyway, so why won’t you give us a try?”
Shen Feizhi wanted to ask: Do you even understand what dating means?
Love was a relatively stable relationship. In Shen Feizhi’s eyes, a healthy romance needed to be built upon a foundation of mutual familiarity and trust.
He and Gu Suizhou were indeed very familiar with each other, yet it always felt as if something was still missing.
Shen Feizhi narrowed his eyes. "No."
Gu Suizhou let out a pained gasp. "That heartless??"
