Shen Feizhi reached out and pulled a single white rose from the bouquet of red ones. After deftly stripping away its thorns, he tucked it into the breast pocket of Gu Suizhou’s suit, right over his heart.
Satisfied, he said, "There. Go on."
This white rose was the finishing touch he’d spent ages picking out at the florist by the entrance.
Thorny red roses were passionate and romantic, perfectly suited for Gu Suizhou.
But in the eyes of outsiders, this man’s appearance was aloof and pure—perhaps a white rose suited him better.
Shen Feizhi was also a dominant Alpha. Having bitten this man’s gland, he had developed a more or less wicked sense of possessiveness. In the eyes of others, he preferred Gu Suizhou to be matched with a white rose.
As for the red rose, that was his private property.
Of course, setting all that aside.
He admitted he simply enjoyed the aesthetic of a seductive creature like Gu Suizhou being defiled amidst pure white roses.
It was a powerful visual impact, wasn't it?
