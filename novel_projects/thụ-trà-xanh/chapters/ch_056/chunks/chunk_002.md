Shen Feizhi’s hand paused. "On Gu Suizhou's orders?"
"Yes, but the research direction has changed this time. Before, it was focused on inducing differentiation, but now it's for assisting differentiation—mitigating its effects and the pain," the Director said.
Shen Feizhi’s nerves relaxed. "Isn't that a good thing?"
"Yes, but that's not the point." Luvi Tisqi was more serious than ever before. "Do you know what President Gu gave me when he came to see me two days ago?"
Shen Feizhi’s heart skipped a beat.
Luvi Tisqi said, "Getting those research fanatics in the Pharmacology Team to abandon all their previous results and switch directions is an incredibly difficult thing to do. But what Gu Suizhou provided was enough to convince them."
“He gave us one hundred and twenty-seven sets of research data on W.N.”
“The conclusion is that this drug is utterly incapable of fully inducing differentiation. The probability of differentiation can only infinitely approach 1, but can never be guaranteed at 1. At the same time, it brings nothing but agony to the test subjects—pain levels that… all exceeded the limit of human endurance by more than 200%.”
These results were practically a declaration that the entire direction of the first version of W.N’s research was a complete failure.
But Shen Feizhi knew that wasn’t Luvi Tisqi’s point.

“Where on earth did he get this data?”
These were live-subject experiments. They were criminal acts.
Shen Feizhi closed his eyes, recalling Lin Sui’s words and the woman he had encountered while exiting the elevator that day.
Lin Sui had said Gu Suizhou’s hands were filthy, stained with the lives of an unknown number of people.
In truth, he could guess some of it. Gu Suizhou’s relationship with the underground Black Market was significant; otherwise, he wouldn’t have survived to adulthood unscathed, let alone caused such a stir.
These might have been part of the inheritance Lin Zizhuo and Gu Lang had left to protect him, but in Gu Suizhou’s hands, these shields could be wielded as spears.
He asked, “Where is the data now?”
“President Gu only let us take a look at them during the meeting before taking them away.” Luvi Tisqi had a strong legal consciousness and was also Gu Lang’s student, so she naturally didn't want to see Gu Suizhou dabbling in such illicit business.
She could see that Shen Feizhi was the same type of person as her—both wanting what was best for Gu Suizhou—so she gambled on the hope that Shen Feizhi would take her side.
