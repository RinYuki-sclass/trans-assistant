Chapter 56
W.N had recently been plagued by massive internal disagreements regarding the Research Institute’s funding.
Ever since a new project for the institute's pharmaceutical department was handed down two days ago, Gu Suizhou's stance was to pour the bulk of the capital into pharmacological R&D. However, another faction placed much higher importance on quantum technology research.
Projects involving tens of billions weren't something Gu Suizhou could decide on his own, and he didn't want things to turn too ugly. Every day at work was spent bickering with that group of old fogies.
To keep him from getting distracted, Shen Feizhi accompanied him to and from work every day. Once home, he let the boy play with all sorts of locks as he pleased. On the rare occasion Shen Feizhi had to go to the institute to sign in, he even had to file a report; it was truly an inexhaustible nuisance.
Yet, he couldn't bear to let anything happen to the kid. Since he had chosen this for himself, he had to endure it.
But this couldn't go on forever.
Shen Feizhi leaned back in his chair, beginning to contemplate a solution.
"President Shen." Someone from the secretariat suddenly knocked on the door. "Ms. Luvi Tisqi is here to see you."
"Let her in." Shen Feizhi sat up straight.
By the time the Director entered, Shen Feizhi looked every bit the picture of a refined gentleman.
She unceremoniously filled a disposable cup several times, draining it each time. "Damn, I'm parched. Why has the weather been so dry lately?"
It was autumn now, and the air was indeed quite dry; Shen Feizhi had already turned on the humidifying climate control system at home.
"You’ll have to ask the meteorological department for that." Shen Feizhi flicked his fingers, signaling a mechanical claw to bring her a chair. "You’re here for something?"
"Well, what else? Would I dare step foot into your inner sanctum without a reason?" The Director looked at him with a complex expression.
