Shen Feizhi would never use the guise of "doing what's best for Gu Suizhou" to persuade him into doing what he personally thought was right.
Including this time.
He would investigate to find the truth first, and then make a decision.
Even if Gu Suizhou really had done those things, as long as he had a sufficient reason to convince him, Shen Feizhi wouldn't blame him. He would even help clean up the mess.
His heart had always been biased beyond all measure.

Thus, Shen Feizhi merely nodded. "I understand. Carry on with your research. I’ll look into this matter."
Luvi Tisqi didn't doubt him. "I hope you can bring good news."
Though he said he would ask and resolve it, Shen Feizhi didn't want to bring it up while Gu Suizhou was already overwhelmed with work.
The next day, after checking in with Gu Suizhou, he made a trip to the research institute.
"Morning." Luvi Tisqi nodded to him, then turned back toward her office, gesturing for him to make himself at home.
Shen Feizhi didn’t stand on ceremony. Strolling through the place as if he owned it, he swaggered into the Pharmacology Team’s wing. "Who was present at the meeting Gu Suizhou called the other day?"
His abilities preceded him; he held considerable sway within the Research Institute.
As soon as he spoke, many hands went up.
Shen Feizhi nodded. "Write down as much of the research data Gu Suizhou showed you during that meeting as you can remember. Group leader, send a copy to me by the end of this weekend."
No researcher was ever indifferent to experimental data. The crowd didn't ask questions, assuming he was simply unwilling to accept having missed out. They nodded, indicating it wouldn't be an issue. "We probably won't be able to reproduce all of it, but thirty to forty percent is definitely doable."
"That’ll work. Thanks for the hard work," Shen Feizhi said. "Afternoon tea is on me. Group leader, go pick it up at the entrance in a bit."
He gave a few more instructions before Gu Suizhou’s demanding call came through.
Shen Feizhi sent a location pin to soothe him, then packed his things to head back.
