“The flowers in the office have withered; it’s time for a change.” Ritter blinked. “Here, baby Shen, these roses are for you! I had these imported flowers specially air-freighted from the botanical gardens of Pashimi. Beautiful flowers for a beautiful person. Love ya~”
Shen Feizhi’s fist tightened.
He could turn a blind eye to Gu Suizhou’s “love yous,” but coming from people like Ritter…
It only made him want to vomit.
Shen Feizhi originally intended to refuse, but when the words reached his lips, they shifted for some inexplicable reason: “Thanks.”
Not long after, a handsome man holding a bouquet of roses appeared at the entrance of W.N.
Passersby stole frequent glances, but Shen Feizhi kept feeling that these red roses were far too gaudy. They were beautiful, yet something about them felt off.
After a moment’s thought, he stepped into a nearby flower shop.
On the top floor, Gu Suizhou had just finished tearing the finance department a new one because the accounts didn’t balance, leaving him in a foul mood.
He was gulping down water, feeling as if his throat were about to catch fire from sheer irritation.
Consequently, when the office door was knocked, his tone was harsh: “Come in!”
The person pushing the door open paused. “Why so fierce?”
“Brother Shen?” Gu Suizhou’s expression vanished instantly, and he immediately stood up to greet him with a beaming smile. “You’re back~”
Before the final syllable of his greeting could even leave his lips, he was rooted to the spot, stunned by the roses in Shen Feizhi’s arms.
