Gu Suizhou raised a hand to rub his lips.
After a long while, he reached out and turned the water temperature to its lowest setting.
When he finished his shower and stepped out, Shen Feizhi happened to be receiving a transmission from Number 9 Handsome Guy.
He tossed the towel in his hand to Gu Suizhou: "Yeah, it’s me."
Gu Suizhou’s hands stiffened, his heavy gaze fixing on him: 【Who is it?】
Shen Feizhi ignored him: "I’ve got some things to handle these next two days, so it might not work. Let’s say next week."
Gu Suizhou’s expression grew increasingly grim as he listened.
The moment Shen Feizhi hung up, Gu Suizhou snatched the smartphone from his hand and tossed it onto the bed. "Who was that, Brother Shen? A date? Next week?"
"Someone I met tonight," Shen Feizhi replied, making no effort to hide it. "Xu Zhong said if you don't differentiate soon, it might damage your health."
Gu Suizhou threw his head back. "I can go my whole life without differentiating!"
Shen Feizhi tapped the headboard, his expression displeased. "Don't talk such nonsense."

"I’m only considering your health. If you don't like this one, we'll move on to the next. You can't keep putting it off," Shen Feizhi said calmly. "I can't stand by and watch you hurt yourself over this."
Hearing that it was for his own sake, Gu Suizhou’s expression softened slightly. "Are you that worried about me?"
What he actually wanted to say was: If you're so worried, why don't you do it yourself?
Shen Feizhi nodded candidly. "Yes. Otherwise, I won't be able to face your parents."
Gu Suizhou: "…"
The atmosphere, which had just begun to thaw, instantly froze over again.
Shen Feizhi didn't know which part of his words had struck a nerve, but Gu Suizhou’s expression turned so sour it looked as though someone owed him eighty billion.
