Shen Feizhi coldly withdrew his hand. “No.”
The dog seemed to understand human nature; it immediately let out two whimpering whines, as if pleading.
Gu Suizhou, his courage bolstered by the alcohol, actually dared to glare at him. “Why not!”
Shen Feizhi completely ignored the dog’s pitiful whimpers and said seriously, “Are you sure you can take care of him?”
Gu Suizhou was adamant: “I can.”
“Words are easy,” Shen Feizhi said. “Injections, medication, feeding, walks, and handling all sorts of miscellaneous chores—where are you going to find the time?”
What he said was true. Without the commitment to do these things, meddling in a life on a whim was an act of extreme irresponsibility.
Shen Feizhi was never one to go looking for that kind of trouble.
But Gu Suizhou was being persistent this time. "I can handle it. Besides, if I occasionally go on a business trip, Brother Shen, would you really just stand by and watch him starve to death at home?"
Right on cue, the dog let out a "woof," tucking its tail between its legs dejectedly.
Shen Feizhi’s fist tightened slightly.
