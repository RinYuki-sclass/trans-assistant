Gu Suizhou smiled, neither confirming nor denying. "In short, don't worry about me anymore. I can handle my own affairs. You don't owe my parents anything anymore, so don't dwell on it. Just live your own life."
"That would be for the best." Shen Feizhi calmly finished the last sip of his milk. "That dog of yours…"
"I'll take him with me once I find a house." Gu Suizhou had finished eating as well, and promptly handed him his packed briefcase. "I'll raise him well with my future partner. I won't be irresponsible."
"It's just that I’m going to be too busy these next few days, so I might have to trouble you to look after him for me."
Gu Suizhou tossed a piece of meat to the dog. After finishing it on the floor, the dog proactively pressed a rag down with its paws and wiped the spot clean.
This time, Shen Feizhi was genuinely shocked.
Gu Suizhou smiled. "See? He’s very intelligent. He won't cause you any trouble."
Shen Feizhi didn't say anything, but it was taken as a silent agreement.
Gu Suizhou imperceptibly breathed a sigh of relief.
Throughout the morning, he had tactfully prepared breakfast for Shen Feizhi, readied his things, and held out his coat to help him into it. There had been no physical contact the entire time; at first glance, he truly seemed much more mature.
Shen Feizhi nodded, unable to quite put his finger on how he felt.
It was subtle, sudden, and somewhat unsettling.
He didn’t dwell on it for now, as the dark circles under Gu Suizhou’s eyes didn’t look fake. He simply assumed the other man had truly reflected on his behavior. "Then I’ll head out first."
"Alright." Gu Suizhou watched his back with a smile in his eyes. Just before Shen Feizhi pushed the door open to leave, he suddenly called out, "Wait."
Shen Feizhi paused. "What is it?"
