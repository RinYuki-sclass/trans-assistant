"Alright, I'll fix that next time," Gu Suizhou accepted the lecture with a pleasant smile.
Once the door closed, he leaned against it, listening to the footsteps vanish down the hallway. Only then did the smile on his face abruptly dissolve, replaced by displeasure and a brooding gloom.
Dammit, I spent the whole morning holding back from hugging him. I’m dying inside!
Irritated, he sank into the sofa that still held the lingering aroma of bitter coffee. He stared blankly at the news on the TV in a funk, one hand petting the dog while the other slowly moved to his lips.
He used his tongue to gently lick away the drop of milk he had wiped off.
As the sweetness climbed from his taste buds to his sensory nerves, his expression finally normalized a bit. He lazily scratched the dog's head. "Lucky Break, good job."
Hearing the name "Lucky Break," the dog barked twice in excitement.
Evidently, this dog had possessed its own name for quite some time.
