Gu Suizhou choked back a sob. "I actually understand. My brother just thinks keeping you requires responsibility, and he doesn't want to be responsible for you. After all, he might just up and leave us again without a word at any moment. He won't even take responsibility for me, so he definitely won't take responsibility for you!"
The dog gently and considerately nuzzled his palm, then trotted over to Shen Feizhi, cautiously nipping and tugging at his pant leg.
Shen Feizhi: "…"
Gu Suizhou forced himself to let go, looking like a martyr marching toward his execution. "Stop biting! No matter how much you bite, we aren't adopting you! Go! Just go! You leave first, and when I get dumped, I'll come find you. We can roam the streets together like brothers!"
Shen Feizhi’s temple throbbed, teetering on the edge of an explosion. "That’s enough out of you."
Gu Suizhou stood up, refusing to spare another glance at the dog watching them with longing eyes. "Farewell forever!"
Finally pushed past his limit, Shen Feizhi turned around. "Take your dog and keep up!"

And so, their duo became a trio.
It took a monumental effort for Shen Feizhi and the dog to get Gu Suizhou home safely. The moment they stepped through the door, Gu Suizhou bolted straight for Shen Feizhi’s bedroom, unstoppable.
With a dark expression, Shen Feizhi let him do as he pleased. He led the dog to the balcony and placed two sausages on the floor.
He maintained a cold, safe distance from the animal. "We’ll decide whether you stay or go tomorrow, once your 'brother' sleeps off the booze."
The dog seemed to understand, yet not quite. It let out a few whimpers, and for the sake of the two sausages, it tried to affectionately nuzzle him to show its friendliness.
But Shen Feizhi distanced himself and dodged the dog, returning inside and shutting the balcony door, leaving the poor pup to scratch piteously at the glass.
He mentally prepared himself, persuading his inner self not to act on impulse and refrain from physical violence, before pushing the door into the bedroom.
"Gu Suizhou, get your ass in the shower."
The person currently wriggling like a maggot on the bed heard his voice and sat up, clutching a pillow. "Why doesn't your bed smell like you?"
"Because I slept on the sofa yesterday." Shen Feizhi walked over, reaching out with the intent to forcibly toss him into the bathroom.
But Gu Suizhou was born with a rebellious streak and struggled incessantly in his grip.
"I don't want to shower!"
