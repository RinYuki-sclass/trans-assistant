He closed his eyes and counted to ten. Once the grogginess faded, he sat up from the sofa.
"Brother Shen, you're awake?" Gu Suizhou brought fried eggs and milk to the table. "Perfect timing. Come have breakfast."
There was no smell of burning coming from the kitchen today, which was a rarity.

Shen Feizhi walked over and flipped an egg. He found that even the underside wasn't burnt; it was a perfectly fried egg.
He offered a rare word of praise: "Not bad."
"Practice makes perfect. After practicing for a few years, I can't exactly fail at frying an egg forever." Gu Suizhou smiled at him. "Go get washed up, Bro."
Shen Feizhi gave him a strange look.
He couldn't put his finger on it, but he felt like there was something odd about this guy.
By the time he finished washing up, Gu Suizhou had just finished feeding the dog. Seeing him come out, Gu Suizhou wiped his hands clean, set out the utensils for him, and sat down across from him.
He even took the initiative to bring up the previous day's events: "I'm sorry, Brother Shen. Thank you for the trouble of coming to pick me up yesterday."
Shen Feizhi wasn't used to him being this polite and civil. "It's fine. It was on the way."
Gu Suizhou chuckled. "Regardless, it was a hassle for you. I thought about it all last night and realized I really have made you worry too much in the past. You can't look after me forever; I need to change as soon as possible."
Shen Feizhi raised an eyebrow. "Oh? And how do you plan on changing?"
"At the very least, I can't let you exhaust yourself over me anymore," Gu Suizhou said. "Give me the contact info for that person you mentioned. I'll go personally next week; no need for you to make an extra trip."
"Also, regarding the house, I already had Xu Zhong look into it this morning. Don't worry, I'll definitely move out within a month."
Shen Feizhi’s motion of drinking milk paused for two seconds, and he nearly choked.
A well-shaped hand timely offered him a tissue. He reached out to take it, but the other party skillfully avoided any fingertip contact.
Shen Feizhi didn't pay it much mind. "What exactly did you go through last night?"
