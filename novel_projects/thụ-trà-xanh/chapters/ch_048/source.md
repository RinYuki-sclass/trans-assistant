Shen Feizhi loathed drunks, especially those who couldn't keep still.

Gu Suizhou was the first one he hadn't left on the side of the road to fend for himself.

The road near their apartment complex had been under construction recently, making it difficult for cars to enter. Shen Feizhi had no choice but to park on the curb and walk the final stretch.

As they passed the woods near the entrance, a faint whimper of a dog caught a certain drunkard's attention.

Shen Feizhi stopped along with him. "You know it?"

The drunkard nodded, mumbling, "An old acquaintance. You know him, too."

Shen Feizhi's lips thinned slightly.

Sure enough, a second later, a medium-sized black-and-white dog came running out.

The dog’s coat was beautiful, but it was caked in mud and dust, making it look quite filthy. When standing normally, it reached about his knees.

Seeing Shen Feizhi, the dog first circled around with its tail between its legs. Once it determined the stranger showed no signs of aggression, it cautiously stepped forward to nuzzle Gu Suizhou’s pant leg before circling around Shen Feizhi’s legs, acting very much like an old friend.

Only then did Shen Feizhi remember who this dog was.

"Five years later, and it's grown this much." He raised an eyebrow in surprise. "It's actually still alive."
