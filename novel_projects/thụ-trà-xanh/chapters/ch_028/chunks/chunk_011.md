With Gu Suizhou nursing multiple hairline fractures and Shen Feizhi suffering from severe soft tissue damage, they made a perfect pair—one broken bones, the other torn flesh.
Gu Suizhou sighed, carefully applied the medicine, and then meticulously wrapped the gauze in a beautiful spiral, tying a neat bow at the waist.
After cleaning up, Shen Feizhi looked much tidier.
He draped the thermal jacket directly over Shen Feizhi, his gaze lingering for a long moment on the spot where Shen Feizhi's chest pressed against the sofa.
While bandaging the wound, his fingers had repeatedly brushed against Shen Feizhi's skin, feeling the uneven, cratered texture of his chest.

He stared intently for a long while before muttering softly, his voice small and timid, "Brother Shen, you once taught me that the ends justify the means."
"Now I can tell you, I've learned that lesson very well."
He had never been a righteous gentleman. His restraint toward Shen Feizhi stemmed solely from gratitude and respect for his benefactor.
But Shen Feizhi had always kept him at arm's length. What choice did he have but to resort to such desperate measures? If he missed this opportunity, who knew when he'd get another chance to corner him?
"I'm sorry, Brother Shen. I never meant for it to come to this. But don't worry, I'll try my best to avoid any physical contact with you."
"Brother Shen, Brother Shen, I'm being so careful. Don't be mad at me when you wake up, okay?"
Gu Suizhou was utterly insincere. Sweet words flowed from his lips, but his hands moved with unwavering purpose as he gently scooped Shen Feizhi into his arms.
Those amber eyes flickered slightly, long lashes trembling like a sieve.
He'd lied earlier about being afraid to look at the electronic document, his real intention being to get Shen Feizhi's hand to touch the screen where he'd just applied the topical sleep aid.
But this time, he really was a little afraid to look.
Gu Suizhou's literary skills weren't his strong suit. After rummaging through his mental lexicon for a long moment, he finally sighed, "It's like the proverb says: 'Nearing home, one's heart grows timid.'"
If Shen Feizhi were awake, he'd definitely mock him for that.
Thinking of the man's cold voice ordering him to "get lost," Gu Suizhou chuckled softly, feeling strangely pleased.
Tsk, my mindset's getting a bit twisted. I'll have to hide this better in the future, can't let Shen Feizhi figure it out.
