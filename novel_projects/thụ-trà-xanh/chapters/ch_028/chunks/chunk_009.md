Shen Feizhi froze for a moment, then swiftly raised his arm to strike Gu Suizhou's hand.
But Gu Suizhou reacted even faster.
"Brother Shen, haven't you noticed how utterly exhausted you've become, how heavy your body feels?"
Easily dodging Shen Feizhi's attack, Gu Suizhou raised the spray and spritzed it toward his nose and mouth.
Shen Feizhi's vision blurred, and his head began to spin violently.
He stumbled back two steps, his knees buckling, and nearly collapsed to the ground.
But Gu Suizhou caught him.
Shen Feizhi hung limply in Gu Suizhou's arms, gritting his teeth. "You fucking suicidal idiot?" he rasped.
He rarely cursed, but this time, he was truly enraged.
Though he wouldn't admit it, his guard against this brat had relaxed slightly. He never imagined that the moment he let his guard down, he'd be betrayed so ruthlessly.
A hint of amusement flickered in Gu Suizhou's melodious voice. "Bro, you sound so cute when you curse."
He gently covered the unconscious man's eyes with his cool, slender palm. "Sleep now, Bro," he murmured softly. "Don't resist. Trust me—I won't hurt you."
Shen Feizhi mentally devised at least ten ways to tear Gu Suizhou limb from limb.
