The old Gu Suizhou had been easier to handle—he'd curse with no hesitation. But this shameless version was becoming too much to bear.
Gu Suizhou crossed his arms, then used his wrists—sleeves still covering his skin—to wrap around Shen Feizhi's neck, avoiding skin contact. "Why not? Am I not beautiful enough?"
"All looks, no substance," Shen Feizhi retorted. "A princess wouldn't have a mouth like yours."
"Shut up."
Gu Suizhou stopped teasing him. He rested one hand on Shen Feizhi's shoulder while obediently holding the torch aloft with the other, lighting their way.
Guided by Leapton, Shen Feizhi discovered the cave wasn't particularly long, but it had many branching paths, forming a regular, orderly maze. There were also numerous footprints left by people, likely staff from the Herb Field who frequented this place.
Gazing at the rough, uneven stone wall beside him, a flash of insight struck Shen Feizhi, bringing him to an abrupt halt.
Gu Suizhou, nestled in his arms, narrowed his eyes. "Brother Shen noticed it too, huh?"
"Mm-hmm," Shen Feizhi replied.

"What?" Leapton's brow furrowed in confusion. "What's with the cryptic talk?"
Shen Feizhi, unable to free his hands while carrying Gu Suizhou, kicked the wall lightly with his foot. "These stones are too easily dislodged. They're not natural formations—they're man-made. The size and order of each passage are remarkably uniform, suggesting systematic management."
"And the height here is exactly that of a standard floor," Gu Suizhou added. "The wood is so dry it's flammable, and the ground lacks the usual dampness and grime of caves… Lai Gou, I taught you this before."
Leapton pondered for a moment, then exclaimed in shock, "You're saying this could be their internal office space?"
Gu Suizhou had previously explained that many places were designed to mimic natural environments. If such elaborate craftsmanship appeared within a high-tech facility, this place would be more valuable than any vault in a skyscraper.
"Smart," Shen Feizhi praised. "Even though this place mimics nature, it'll be crawling with surveillance. Leapton, find the cameras and delete all footage of us. Don't forget to erase the automatic backups in the central control room."
Leapton, slightly embarrassed by the praise, grinned. "Heh heh, you can count on me!"
The two were chatting amiably when Gu Suizhou, clearly disgruntled, tightened his grip around Shen Feizhi's neck, forcing him to turn and face him. "Brother Shen! I taught him everything! Shouldn't I be the one getting praised for being smart?"
"Why are you still acting like a child?" Shen Feizhi didn't quite understand why Gu Suizhou was so upset and had no intention of coddling him. He bent at the waist, ready to put Gu Suizhou down and start searching the walls for hidden doors or switches.
But even after his feet touched the ground, Gu Suizhou kept his arms wrapped tightly around Shen Feizhi's neck, refusing to let go.
