He wrapped his arms around Shen Feizhi from behind, his head leaning over the man's shoulder.
Before he could even get the best view, a thick, dark brown scar filled his peripheral vision.
Gu Suizhou froze.
…Too obvious.
The scar was far too conspicuous.
Even partially concealed by the bandage, its shape remained clearly discernible.
Etched against his snow-white skin, it seared the eyes.
Gu Suizhou's lips parted slightly as his hand fluttered awkwardly, making a rough measurement in the air—
Palm-sized.
The dimensions matched perfectly.
Gu Suizhou's breathing quickened sharply.
He closed his eyes urgently, abandoning all restraint, and vigorously rubbed his numb fingers before reaching out with a trembling hand to touch the scar.
Though he was already eighty percent certain, when his index finger traced the familiar pattern he had traced countless times before, tears welled up in his eyes.
"Shen Feizhi… it really is you."
Gu Suizhou had a high tear threshold, but his lacrimal glands were highly developed. He was prone to crying uncontrollably—if the right nerve was struck, tears would flow at once.
He hastily withdrew his hand, wiping his cheeks frantically.
Amid his excitement, a sudden wave of resentment washed over him.
Years ago, Shen Feizhi had claimed to know his name, to recognize him, to understand him, and to promise to treat him well…
Why did he now act as if he had just met him?
He refused to believe anyone could forget a comrade with whom they had faced life and death together!
