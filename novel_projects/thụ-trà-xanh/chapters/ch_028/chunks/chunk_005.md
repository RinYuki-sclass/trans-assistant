Shen Feizhi shifted his neck uncomfortably. "Sorry," he said coolly, "I couldn't help myself."
Most of the people in the room had already discreetly excused themselves to find doctors and security guards, leaving only the Alpha waiting for the robotic arm to administer the isolation injection. Just shut up already, Shen Feizhi thought. Why do you talk so much?

But the Alpha was even more shocked. "Holy shit, kid! You're even more badass! Your voice is steady too! Did you train for this in countless battles?"
Gu Suizhou: "……"
