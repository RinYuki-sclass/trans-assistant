Chapter 28
"Take off your shirt. I'll clean your wounds."
After Gu Suizhou uttered those words, Shen Feizhi felt an overwhelming urge to kick him out and send him plummeting to his death.
The two men stared at each other in silence, the only movement being Shen Feizhi's increasingly clenched fist.
After what felt like an eternity, a soft cough broke the tense atmosphere.
"Um… just saying… is there a chance I'm still here listening?"
Shen Feizhi slammed his eyes shut, then reopened them, his expression back to its usual impassive mask. "My apologies. Could you give us directions?"
Gu Suizhou watched Shen Feizhi's retreating figure, sighing regretfully.
But he knew when to back down; pushing further would only backfire.
The mere sight of Gu Suizhou's face made Shen Feizhi's head ache. He strode forward a few steps, but when he heard the retreating footsteps growing fainter, he turned back with a dark expression.
The little brat might be infuriating, but his injuries are real.
He considered crouching down to carry Gu Suizhou on his back, but remembering his own blood-soaked back, he had to resort to another method.
"Arms out," Shen Feizhi commanded flatly.
Gu Suizhou obeyed instantly, his eyes sparkling as if he had guessed what was coming.
Shen Feizhi forcefully ignored his gaze, tore strips from his pant leg to tightly wrap both hands, ensuring the wound on his right hand wouldn't reopen. He then scooped Gu Suizhou up into a bridal carry.
