From the start of the reality show filming to their "accidental" fall into this cave, everything had gone too smoothly to be believed.
This unsettling feeling reached its peak when everyone began fleeing as if rehearsed, the moment they entered.
He'd always been unlucky, and even sticking by Shen Feizhi hadn't improved his fortunes much. So, rather than attribute this to luck, he suspected someone had deliberately arranged for him to see these records.
"Lai Gou."
Leapton had been monitoring their every move through the surveillance cameras, yet he still couldn't make sense of the unfolding events. Trusting Gu Suizhou implicitly, he refrained from intervening when Gu Suizhou moved against Shen Feizhi.
Hearing the other finally acknowledge him, he responded dully, "What's up?"
"Check the Herb Garden's surveillance footage from the past few days—all of it," Gu Suizhou said. "Also, investigate who the management team members have met recently. Someone might have gotten here before us."
Leapton exclaimed in surprise, "Do you have any suspects?"
Gu Suizhou's expression darkened ominously, as if a name had surfaced in his mind. "I do. Check the footage first. They haven't mastered the art of erasing all traces yet. We should find something."
