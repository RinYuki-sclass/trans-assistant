Gu Suizhou lowered his head, his soft sobs muffled against Shen Feizhi's neck and shoulder. His voice was hoarse.
"Brother Shen, why did you hide it from me?"
"Why wouldn't you admit you knew me? Why did you pretend we were strangers?"
"Brother Shen, have I disappointed you? Did I not grow into the person you hoped I'd be? Is that why you don't want to see me anymore…?"
The more Gu Suizhou spoke, the more aggrieved he felt, nearly choking on his tears.
He even considered that if Shen Feizhi ever confessed, "Yes, I am disappointed," he would imprison him, lock him away, and force him to teach him exactly what kind of person he wanted him to be.
Just tell me, and I'll change!
Gu Suizhou trembled violently, the muscles in his arms tensing as if he were holding Shen Feizhi tightly.
In reality, he didn't dare apply any pressure at all. Shen Feizhi slept soundly in his arms, his brow uncreased.
Only after his tears had dried somewhat did Gu Suizhou's emotions subside. He awkwardly wiped his hands on his own clothes and even dabbed away the tear streaks he'd left on Shen Feizhi's neck.
Never mind, never mind.
If Brother Shen doesn't want to acknowledge me, so be it.
I'm not even an adult yet; I can still change. If Shen Feizhi doesn't like it, then I'll change it.
One day, I'll change enough to satisfy him, to make Brother Shen acknowledge me willingly.
Gu Suizhou repeated these thoughts to himself relentlessly, his complexion gradually improving.
"Bro, let's get you out of here. We can take things slow, outside."
The previous composure and madness had vanished entirely. He helped Shen Feizhi to his feet, handling him as carefully as a porcelain doll, meticulously dressing him.
His hand paused subtly as it passed over the scars, his index finger tracing their patterns once more, lingering at the very center of the cross.
The color was deepest there, the injury most severe, and closest to the heart.
Gu Suizhou's hand slid downward from the cross's center, grasping Shen Feizhi's right hand, which was wrapped in bandages. He lowered his head and pressed a reverent kiss to the back of his hand.
