"Lin Zizhuo! We were classmates for ten years, and you don't trust me even this much?" Ritter’s eyebrows shot up in fury. "I have a feeling there's something behind this Energy Vortex that we haven't discovered yet! Don't you want to bring about a major technological breakthrough? Isn't the advancement of the world what you wanted?"
Lin Zizhuo frowned. "You… Ritter, calm down. Let's discuss this first."
"What exactly is there left for you two to discuss?!" Ritter was usually friendly toward others, but as soon as it involved his research, his entire persona shifted. "Gu Lang, are you against me too?"
"We need to deliberate," Gu Lang sighed. "Funding isn't something the two of us can decide alone."
This was the truth; the Research Institute was a collective entity, not a personal one.
Ritter couldn't refute this. He paced around irritably for several laps, and finally, without a word, he stormed out with a dark expression, slamming the door behind him.

Gu Lang and Lin Zizhuo exchanged a look, seeing a mutual sense of helplessness in each other's eyes.
Gu Lang asked, "What do you think?"
"Let's not allocate the funds to him for now; let him see what he can do on his own," Lin Zizhuo said, pinching the bridge of his nose. "If it really proves to be of great significance… we'll find another way."
After all, they were old classmates. They remained as understanding as they could be, and since the two of them stopped arguing, the matter settled down for the time being.
Ritter's research continued; it was the project group with the largest expenditures and resource consumption in the Institute at the time.
Many people were keeping an eye on his results, and many among them knew the trade well.
Before long, people began reporting to Lin Zizhuo and Gu Lang with grim expressions.
It was a data report, simple and stark. It showed that since the start of Ritter’s research, the energy levels of the cosmic magnetic field had become unstable. It had already caused over a thousand deaths and countless aerospace traffic accidents.
Lin Zizhuo and Gu Lang immediately realized the gravity of the situation.
“Ritter! You are making everyone pay the price for your research with their lives!” Gu Lang was rarely this angry. “You cannot be this selfish.”
“I’m selfish?” Ritter reacted as if he had heard the world’s biggest joke. “Gu Lang, for someone who was a top graduate in our class, surely you can see what lies behind that Energy Vortex? It’s a High Civilization! That is where we belong! That is the place for people like us!”
“Ritter, don’t take this too far.” Lin Zizhuo looked at him with disappointment and heartache. “You have far too little regard for human life.”
“That’s because they aren’t worthy!” Ritter slammed his hands on the table, flipping it over in a sudden outburst. “People like us aren’t even in the same class as them. Why should we care if they live or die? What meaning is there in their existence anyway?”
