Gu Suizhou answered without a second thought: "Yes."
He paused, then added, "But not if it's from you."
When some people went mad, they could apply a double standard to anything.
Shen Feizhi gave a short laugh. "Good that you aren't."
The moment he finished speaking, Gu Suizhou felt a sharp pain in his earlobe. He reached up to touch it and found a stud had appeared there out of thin air.
Reflected in the glossy surface of the wardrobe behind him, he could see the earring was diamond-shaped, jet-black and textured. At its center sat a red diamond no larger than a grain of rice, shimmering in the dark, exceptionally dazzling.
Gu Suizhou touched his earlobe, unable to take his hand away. "Is this… a consolation prize?"
"It's an earpiece." Shen Feizhi licked the tip of his index finger, where a single drop of blood had just been drawn from Gu Suizhou’s earlobe. "A one-way transmission earpiece."
"One-way?" Gu Suizhou was stunned. "Where is the sound coming from?"
Wearing it on his earlobe, he wouldn't be able to hear anything at all.
Shen Feizhi saw the confusion in his eyes, arched an eyebrow, and took his hand, pressing it against his own chest.
Gu Suizhou realized something vaguely. His heart rate suddenly spiked, his breathing grew ragged, and the corners of his eyes flushed hot.
Shen Feizhi said, "I have a micro-monitor of the same size embedded in my heart. It's also one-way, a pair with the one on your ear."
Gu Suizhou’s hand, pressed against the other man’s chest, couldn't stop trembling. Thump-thump, thump-thump. One beat after another, it was impossible to tell if it was the rise and fall of Shen Feizhi’s chest or the frequency of his own hand’s tremors.
He opened his mouth, but couldn't make a sound.
"Don't understand?" Thinking the other hadn't caught on, Shen Feizhi explained patiently, "Simply put, this earring is linked to the frequency of my heartbeat."
Since he lacked such a sense of security, he would give him a dose of reassurance.
Shen Feizhi was never stingy with his favoritism.
"President Gu, work hard." He flicked the man’s bright red earlobe, a faint smile lingering in his eyes. "Don't slack off. I’m supervising."
