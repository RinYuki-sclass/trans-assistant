Before he could voice his doubt, he suddenly felt something cold and slimy wrap around his ankle. Looking down, he realized it was the Director’s pet snake.
Leapton hiked up his pant leg, his expression shifting drastically.
This pet snake was actually venomous?!
“Director, you—!” No matter how slow he was, Leapton now realized that the man’s motive for calling him here was anything but simple.
The snake coiled tightly around his ankle. Not daring to provoke the creature, he kept his body rigid, staring at the man in a mix of rage and shock. “What are you trying to do?”
"Little Leapton, if you're going to blame anyone, blame Gu Suizhou." The Director sighed. "You shouldn't have gotten involved with him."
With that, he gave a signal, and the snake bit down hard on Leapton’s ankle.
"Motherfucker!" Leapton cursed. He whipped out a miniature launcher, aimed it at the Director, and pulled the trigger without hesitation.
His aim was terrible; trying to hit the snake on his ankle would have likely resulted in him shooting his own leg. But the Director was a grown man—as useless as Leapton's aim was, he could at least hit the broad side of a barn.
Sure enough, the Director hadn't expected this move. His eyes widened in shock, and both men collapsed to the ground simultaneously.
Cold sweat broke out on Leapton’s forehead as the toxin began to spread through his body. His vision blurred, but seeing the other man go down before him, he finally breathed a sigh of relief and slumped to the floor.
