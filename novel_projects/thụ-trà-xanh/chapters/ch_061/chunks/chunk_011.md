The Main Star would teach him a lesson he’d never forget.

C2 clicked his tongue. "You really… understand him, don't you?"
"Yes." The corners of Shen Feizhi's lips curled up, a faint glint of a smile in his eyes. "Because if it were me, I would do the same."
Truly a little madman raised by his own hand.
C2 stuck out his tongue and playfully poked his arm. "With him being so supportive, wouldn't it be wrong not to give him a little reward?"
It was originally meant as a joke, but to his surprise, Shen Feizhi actually nodded. "You're right. A reward is in order."
Shen Feizhi strode toward the building. "Keep watch for me. Five minutes is all I need."
They couldn't meet for long; Ritter had too many eyes and ears, so they had to remain vigilant at all times.
C2 watched his retreating back with keen interest, then picked up a communication device that had been ringing incessantly.
"When are you coming back?" A11’s voice was as calm and flat as ever. "The solution you came up with—why was it me who filed the report to the higher-ups?"
"Oh, stop~" C2 teased playfully. "What’s mine is yours, and what’s yours is… well, still yours! Why draw a line between us?"
C2 added coaxingly, "I worked so hard to play matchmaker; you have to at least let me see how it ends, right? A11~ Eleven~ One-One~ Yao-Yao~~~"
"Fine," A11 interrupted, a rare sigh finally audible in his voice. "Let it be."
Shen Feizhi, of course, was oblivious to their conversation.
