Chapter 61
The timeline returns to one day ago.
In the middle of the night, Leapton received a message from Gu Suizhou, saying he wanted to investigate information regarding their former orphanage.
Before he even had a chance to start looking, he received a letter from the Director.
In the electronic message, the Director wrote that he was already aware they were currently facing difficulties. He had compiled all the orphanage's financial information; however, the magnetic card was damaged, and Leapton would have to come and repair it himself.
Leapton had stayed in contact with the Director over the years, and the Director had been the one who doted on him most back then. Thus, without much suspicion, he took his equipment and went to visit the Director, who lived in seclusion on Changyu Snow Mountain.
"Director!" As soon as he stepped through the door of the small villa, he couldn't wait to open his arms. "Long time no see!"
"Oh my, Little Leapton, you've gotten thin." The Director gave him a hug with a chuckle, patting his head affectionately. "It’s snowing so hard outside, your hands are freezing. Come over and warm up by the fire."
"I'm alright," Leapton said with a smile. "I can't stay long, though. I just came to see you; I have to head back tomorrow."
The Director said regretfully, "In such a hurry?"
"Yeah, it's not like I'm unemployed." Leapton began massaging the Director's shoulders. "You said you could give me the billing and expenditure records. Is that true?"
“You dared to come straight to me without even being sure if it was true?”
“Well, I also missed you and wanted to pay a visit, didn’t I?”
“You always were a smooth talker.” The Director laughed as he pulled out a magnetic card. “The records are all here. I heard from others that you’ve been looking into things lately; I’m just glad I can help.”
“This helps immensely! Thank you!” Leapton took the card. “But where did you hear that from?”
