While he maintained an immovable facade, his pace was nearly fast enough to leave afterimages. He arrived swiftly at Gu Suizhou’s room and swiped his card to enter.
The lights weren't on. Gu Suizhou was crouching on the floor with his back to him, his silhouette rimmed in rose-colored light from the nebula outside the window. He looked exhausted and despondent, possessed of a certain dejected beauty.
It was only natural. Anyone would find it impossible to stay calm after discovering that the elder they had depended on for over a decade was actually the culprit who had killed their parents.
He had spent the past few days pretending that it didn't matter, but he still couldn’t hide it from Shen Feizhi.
Hearing footsteps behind him, Gu Suizhou didn’t move. He only said in a hoarse voice, "Uncle Zhong, take Leapton to the hospital."
"Uncle Zhong? Can’t even recognize my footsteps anymore?"
The figure stiffened visibly before standing up abruptly, looking back in disbelief. "…Brother Shen?"
"I’m here." Shen Feizhi stood a meter away from him, his pitch-black eyes watching him. Just by standing there, he brought an unprovoked sense of calm.
He said softly, "It's been hard on you."
These past few days, all these years—it had been hard on him.
Those four simple words caused Gu Suizhou, who had remained strong and clear-eyed until now, to burst into tears instantly.
He always thought he had a high threshold for tears despite his "tear-incontinent" constitution, yet in front of Shen Feizhi, these useless eyes found a threshold lower than any limit.
Seeing his tears fall, Shen Feizhi felt a dull tightness in his chest, a sharp tugging sensation that struck every now and then, aching with a bitter sting.
He took a step forward, and as if by magic, produced a piece of chocolate, raising his hand to place it against the younger man’s lips.
Gu Suizhou instinctively curled his tongue to take the cloyingly sweet chocolate, accidentally brushing against the pads of Shen Feizhi’s fingers.
The man’s fingertips weren’t smooth like his own; they were covered in a thin layer of calluses, and the sensation wasn't particularly pleasant.
Yet Gu Suizhou felt that this was the most comforting hand in the world.
He pursed his lips, wanting to hasten the melting of the chocolate, but accidentally tasted his own tears.
The salty, bitter tears dripped onto lips that had just tasted sugar; as it turned out, they were sweet.
Shen Feizhi rubbed his left earlobe incessantly until it grew hot, then asked in a low voice, "Are you afraid of pain?"
