Shen Feizhi had his reasons for letting this clueless kid perform the check-up.
First, to avoid physical contact with Lin Sui.
Second, he needed to know if his body had undergone any changes after the world fusion.
If the results were abnormal, Lin Sui, as the Shen family's private doctor, would immediately report them to Elder Master Shen.
But his younger brother wasn't a professional and lacked any sense of professional ethics. A few glib words would probably be enough to brush him off.
With this in mind, Shen Feizhi calmly unbuttoned his shirt, the black fabric hanging loosely on his frame.
Lin Zhou didn't spare a single glance at his taut, perfect muscles, focusing entirely on adjusting the equipment.
The little white rabbit was indeed clumsy with the controls. Shen Feizhi's exposed chest was starting to feel cold, yet the machine still wasn't ready.
Bored, he decided to figure out how to attach the electrode wires himself.
"There are seventeen wires in total," Shen Feizhi said, holding the tangle of cables. "Where do they all go?"
"Two over the heart, three under the ribs…" Lin Zhou recited the instructions like a textbook. "…one on the back of the neck, and two along the spine."
Shen Feizhi nodded, attaching the metal pads as instructed. He then turned, lifting the hem of his shirt to expose his back. "Like this?"
Lin Zhou glanced over, slightly surprised.
The positions were perfectly accurate.
