Shen Feizhi: "…Heh."
Damn kid. You've done what your brother only dared to dream of.

Shen Feizhi: "…"
He didn't want to sleep.
But it was no use. Even the strongest willpower was useless against a potent anesthetic.
His eyelids fluttered twice before gently closing.
His vision gone, Shen Feizhi slowed his breathing, fighting against the anesthetic's effects. He tried to delay passing out, to find out what the little drama queen planned to do next.
But before he could fully regulate his breathing, another sharp pain shot through his neck.
Lin Zhou roughly yanked out the syringe needle, his voice deceptively gentle. "The Second Young Master is in such good shape. A single needle couldn't even pierce all the way through."
Shen Feizhi: "…"
Just before succumbing to complete unconsciousness, he summoned every last ounce of his willpower to utter a single, resonant syllable.
Lin Zhou clicked his tongue. Only when Shen Feizhi's body finally went limp did the furrow in his brow smooth out.
But a moment later, his handsome brows knitted together again.
Ever since the Shen family had driven the Lin family to bankruptcy and his parents had committed suicide under the weight of their debts, he had been meticulously gathering information on the Shens. He had even uncovered every detail about their sole illegitimate son.
