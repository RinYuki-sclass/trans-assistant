Chapter 22
In the ABO system, the three secondary genders—Alpha, Beta, and Omega—are clearly defined. Once differentiation occurs, there's always a definitive outcome. On Blue Star, there had never been a recorded instance of failed differentiation.
Yet Gu Suizhou defied this established precedent. He exhibited faint traces of scent glands, and his body undeniably produced Alpha pheromones and displayed indicators of Alpha physiology.
However, no matter how the instruments were calibrated, the final readings consistently identified him as an Omega.
Initially, Xu Zhong dismissed it as his own misjudgment, assuming the Young Master had genuinely differentiated into an Omega. He reasoned that whether A or O, it made little difference; Gu Suizhou would always be his Young Master.
But gradually, he noticed anomalies.
Gu Suizhou lacked both the rut period and heat cycle characteristic of Alphas and Omegas.
Apart from the extraordinarily high levels of Alpha pheromones in his body and the Omega classification from the tests, he appeared indistinguishable from a Beta.
Later still, Gu Suizhou's madness began to surface.
The Alpha pheromones within him, unable to be discharged through rut or sexual activity, accumulated relentlessly. When they reached a critical threshold, they would periodically trigger his madness, yet no means could be found to eliminate them.
Xu Zhong was nearly frantic, unable to find a permanent solution. Gu Suizhou, for his part, had no clue what had caused it—he'd woken up from a coma like this, with no discernible cause.
Currently, their only options were the acupuncture needles purchased from the Black Market and regular pheromone drainage sessions administered by external means.
Previous drainage medications had only managed to extract less than half of his pheromones, leaving him with a tormenting, half-relieved sensation that even Gu Suizhou's iron will couldn't endure.
Hopefully Uncle Zhong's latest concoction will be more effective this time.
Gu Suizhou sighed and dutifully laid prone on the medical chair.
