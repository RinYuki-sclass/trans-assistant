So what if I can't see? I can use my sense of touch!
A thorough palpation from head to toe would reveal any injuries.
The man, unable to dissuade him, finally nodded and said he'd go wash off the grime first.
Gu Suizhou agreed.
He was gravely ill, and as fate would have it, he was also going through his differentiation period. His consciousness faded in and out, blurring his sense of time. When thirsty, he'd groggily take a sip from the nearby water glass; when hungry, he'd grab some dried fruit from the pile within reach. He didn't notice anything amiss.

Only later, reflecting on those days, did he realize the water glass never seemed to empty, and the dried fruit appeared inexhaustible.
Many days must have passed before the man reappeared before him.
He stood openly before Gu Suizhou, letting the boy paw at him freely.
The child, lacking all restraint, immediately stripped the man's clothes off and groped him thoroughly. Sure enough, his fingers encountered a series of rough, uneven textures on the man's chest.
"You lied! You're hurt!"
Gu Suizhou burst into tears, his already hoarse voice cracking in distress.
The man quickly soothed him, "It's not a wound, not a wound."
The child slowly stopped crying. "Not a wound?"
"No, it's not a wound. It's a tattoo," the man said. "An old one, meant to bring blessings and redemption. It's come in handy now, protecting you and me both. We'll make it back alive."
He gripped Gu Suizhou's hand, tracing circles upon circles around the mark on his palm.
"You have to live, Gu Suizhou."
Gu Suizhou didn't recognize the design; he'd only seen it once, and the memory was blurry.
All he knew was that later, they did make it back.
After a long, long time, he returned to Blue Star, completely unconscious due to his illness. He woke up at the entrance to Xu Zhong's apartment complex.
