It was almost exactly like…
Ten years ago, when the shuttle he was on crashed, and someone had rescued him in an escape pod, leaving him stranded on a barren planet.
He was instantly paralyzed.
Is this a dream?
The moment the thought crossed his mind, his senses and the voice began to fade. He was waking up—his consciousness was already resurfacing.
Gu Suizhou panicked.
Perhaps that period had traumatized him too deeply. He rarely dreamed about it in the years since. But heaven knew how fervently he prayed every day for a chance to see that person again, even if only in a dream.
Reality's sensations steadily replaced the dreamlike visions. Gu Suizhou desperately tried to force himself to recall the events of ten years ago.
2012… 2012…
In the year 2012 of Blue Star's New Era, the C1907 Shuttle, en route to the Tenth Star System, encountered an energy fluctuation. The uncontrolled magnetic field hurled the aircraft into a debris field, where countless meteoroids pulverized it, leaving not even a single fragment behind as it vanished into the cosmos.
Official reports declared that no survivors remained.
Only Gu Suizhou knew the truth.
He was a survivor of that catastrophe.

He would never forget that morning. Lin Sui and he had argued fiercely over whether to accept financial support from the Shen family. Furious, with the help of his old colleagues Lin Zizhuo and Gu Lang, he secretly bought a Shuttle ticket to the Tenth Star System, hoping to clear his head.
Less than two hours into the flight, disaster struck.
Watching the debris field outside—the endless collisions and annihilations—the six-year-old child felt the chilling threat of death for the first time.
He was truly terrified.
Tears of terror streamed down his face as he clutched his safety harness with all his might, calling out for his parents. He had even forgotten that they would never answer him again.
Gu Suizhou watched helplessly as space debris battered the Shuttle's hull, the entire vessel shaking violently.
