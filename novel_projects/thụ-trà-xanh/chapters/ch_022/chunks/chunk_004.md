As expected, drowsiness soon washed over him under the drug's influence. His eyelids began to flutter.
Just before drifting off, he suddenly recalled the pop-science book on dreaming he had seen on Shen Feizhi's desk the previous night.
It claimed that if a person repeatedly suggested a particular topic to themselves before sleep, they were highly likely to dream about it.
Gu Suizhou didn't fully believe in such mystical theories, but this time, for some inexplicable reason, he found himself mentally dwelling on it for quite a while.
In a hazy daze, Gu Suizhou faintly heard a voice.
"Are you alright, kid?"
Kid? Is someone calling me that?
"Wake up. Can you hear me?"
The voice sounded like another teenager. Who could it be?
He struggled to open his eyes, only to find himself still enveloped in darkness.
Am I blind?
Gu Suizhou was disoriented, his arms flailing unconsciously.
He tried to speak, but couldn't utter a single word.
Yet the other voice remained clear.
"Don't panic," the person said. "I'm not a bad guy. Just stay calm."
