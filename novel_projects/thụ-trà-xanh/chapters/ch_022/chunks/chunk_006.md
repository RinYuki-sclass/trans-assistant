Drawing on survival skills Lin Zizhuo had taught him, he groped for the Escape Pod within the Shuttle. But the terrified passengers were crammed together in a panic, making it impossible for his small frame to maneuver.
When the crew members joined the chaos, the pandemonium reached its peak.
The final asteroid struck amidst the deafening screams and shouts.
At that moment, Gu Suizhou's heart nearly stopped from fear.
He stared blankly at the blinding flames outside, numb and motionless like a frozen statue.
Then, a warm hand wrapped around his waist.
The C1907 Shuttle had ejected numerous Escape Pods, and everyone assumed none had survived.
No one could have imagined that, in such near-zero odds of survival, one lucky boy had been accidentally ejected.
Gu Suizhou woke up in that person's arms.

Though he'd narrowly escaped death, the fire's glare had damaged his eyes, leaving him completely blind.
Since then, his eyes had remained incredibly fragile, and the psychological trauma persisted to this day. Even the slightest darkness could trigger a relapse.
Severely injured and delirious at the time, he remembered little clearly.
He only recalled the man telling him he was five years older, came from Blue Star like him, and even knew him.
Gu Suizhou lived with this man on an unnamed Barren Planet for nearly a year. Despite constant hunger and poor sleep, life was surprisingly stable.
The man treated him with such extraordinary kindness that even a six-year-old couldn't help but suspect ulterior motives.
Yet all the man ever did was gently prepare meals, help him wash up, coax him to sleep, carve countless whimsical wooden sculptures for him to play with, and teach him a vast array of unfamiliar knowledge and current affairs—including news about his parents and the Shen family.
The man knew him intimately and devoted everything to helping him, never asking anything in return.
One day, when the man returned, he reeked of blood. He insisted he was fine, claiming it was just animal blood splattered on him from hunting.
Gu Suizhou was running a fever, but his mind hadn't yet been completely addled. He stubbornly grabbed the man's hand, insisting on examining him.
