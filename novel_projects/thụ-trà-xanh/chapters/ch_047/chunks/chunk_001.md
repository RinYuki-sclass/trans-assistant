Chapter 47
As Shen Feizhi read through these descriptions, he harbored serious doubts regarding Lin Zizhuo and Gu Lang’s parenting.
Between the two children, one was an S and the other was an M—wasn't that a bit too warped?
With a heavy heart, he arrived at W.N. Looking up at those two overbearing letters, he felt a wave of stifling gloom.
Now that the business trip was over, the work at the research institute had to continue.
Since it was a workday, he had no choice but to show up and clock in.
Director Luvi Tisqi had been waiting for him on the top floor for quite some time. "Was the trip smooth?"
Predictably, Shen Feizhi thought to himself, no one besides Gu Suizhou had even realized he had been gone for so long.
He nodded. "Smooth."
Noticing that Ms. Tisqi’s twin tails were tied lower than usual, he asked keenly, "Did something happen at the institute?"
"I wouldn't exactly call it 'something happening,'" the Director said, dabbing concealer under her eyes to reveal startlingly dark circles. "It’s just that there have been some disagreements with the board lately."
Shen Feizhi clicked his tongue. "How many days has it been since you last slept?"
"Only three or four," the Director replied nonchalantly. "Professor Shen, I heard that you and President Gu are on quite good terms?"
After that incident, Shen Feizhi’s rank was promoted, and he was now on equal footing with Ritter and the other team leaders.
He leaned back against his chair and narrowed his eyes. "Does this have something to do with Gu Suizhou?"
