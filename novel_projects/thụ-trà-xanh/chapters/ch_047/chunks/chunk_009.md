He greeted the people at the door and was smoothly led into the hall.
The drinking party was nearly over. Most of the few remaining people had their eyes fixed on Gu Suizhou.
The arrival of a strikingly handsome man walking toward Gu Suizhou with a look of pure hostility drew even more stares.
With a foul temper, Shen Feizhi kicked away the man who was trying to touch Gu Suizhou’s face.
"Ah! What the hell are you doing?" the man glared at him. "Even if you're trying to pick someone up, there's a—"
He didn't finish. The cold, sinister look in Shen Feizhi’s eyes made his skin crawl, and the rest of his sentence died in his throat.
Shen Feizhi glanced back at the others. "Get lost on your own, or do I have to help you?"
Those people were only there for the money and had no desire to lose their lives. Without a struggle, they stammered, "W-we’ll go ourselves…"
Once the irrelevant people had scattered, Shen Feizhi finally looked at the protagonist of the photo. "What happened? Are you drunk?"
Gu Suizhou’s gaze was pinned on him. "No."
"Drunkards always say they’re not drunk." Shen Feizhi took a step forward and tilted the other’s chin up with one hand. "Open up."
Gu Suizhou obediently opened his mouth.
Shen Feizhi dropped a sobering pill into his mouth. "Hold it in your mouth, I—"
The words came to a sudden halt.
