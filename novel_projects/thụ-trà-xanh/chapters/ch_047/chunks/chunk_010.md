Little brat, what a sharp nose.
He offered a simple explanation: "I went out for a bit just now. I was in contact with a lot of people."
Gu Suizhou lowered his eyes, his belief in the statement unclear.
"Busy? Who were you out with?"
The words had barely left his lips when he felt a needle-like sting where his hand was being bitten.
He instinctively tried to pull his arm back.
Gu Suizhou only bit down harder.
Fearing he might hurt the boy’s teeth, Shen Feizhi went stiff and stopped moving.
Perhaps realizing he had caused pain, Gu Suizhou carefully opened his mouth. He took Shen Feizhi’s hand in both of his, cradling it in his palms as he squinted, stroking the tooth marks on the fingertip.
