Gu Suizhou was as good as his word. He not only gave Ritter the best test site available but also assigned him a massive workforce.
Previously, Ritter could afford to work at his own leisurely pace. However, Gu Suizhou's current attitude made it clear: as long as Ritter wasn't dead, he was expected to work himself to death. He didn't have a single moment to spare, spending night after night accompanied only by the energy fields, unable to worry about anything else.
Shen Feizhi and C2 took advantage of this time to weed out every single mole Ritter had planted around them.
To make Ritter believe that "Shen Feizhi" was dead, not a single spy could be overlooked.
"I’m saying, is this really necessary?" Ritter asked, dark circles under his eyes as he looked helplessly at the man standing in the doorway. "Are you really in that much of a hurry?"
Gu Suizhou curled his lip. "Your parents' bodies aren't even cold yet, and you're not in a hurry?"
"I'm really not. They're already dead; they're just corpses." Ritter shrugged, completely ignoring the fury in the other man’s eyes. "There's no need for you to keep watch over me here. If you're truly anxious, you'd be better off helping me find Shen Feizhi. After all, if that kid doesn't die, I can't return your person to you even if everything on my end is ready."
He understood the connection between Shen Feizhi and the Energy Vortex better than anyone.
Though the reason remained unclear, he would eventually figure it all out.
Gu Suizhou gave a cold snort. "I told you, I won't interfere in Shen Feizhi's affairs."
"You still have feelings for him?" Ritter exclaimed. "I thought you were just playing around."

He had practically watched Gu Suizhou grow up, so he knew exactly what kind of brat the kid was behind closed doors. He never imagined someone like him would ever develop a useless emotion like love.
Gu Suizhou lowered his eyes, offering no denial.
Ritter said mockingly, "Ah, you like him and yet you're letting me kill him? Seems you aren't that devoted after all. Good. That's a good boy."
"Are you done?" Gu Suizhou closed his eyes in annoyance. "You only have ten days left."
His irritation wasn't an act. He was genuinely frustrated.
He and Shen Feizhi hadn't seen each other for twenty whole days!
He had practically memorized the rhythm of Shen Feizhi's heartbeat for every hour of the day, yet the anxiety hadn't been relieved. Instead, he missed the man more and more—it was driving him insane.
Ritter opened his mouth to say a few more words, but the young man irritably kicked over a trash can and stalked out with his hands in his pockets.
