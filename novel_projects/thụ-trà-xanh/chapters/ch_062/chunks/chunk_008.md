Gu Suizhou’s volatility was nothing new; it wasn't impossible for him to have a sudden lapse in sanity.
Shen Feizhi watched him with a hint of amusement. While the other man wasn't looking, he swiftly lifted the cup and brought it to Gu Suizhou’s lips.
Gu Suizhou took a sip and took the cup, his fingers intentionally brushing over Shen Feizhi’s wrist. "It's so late. You’re quite bold."
He meant that Shen Feizhi actually dared to walk in here so brazenly, but the assistant only thought he was commenting on an employee's boldness for staying so late at the office alone.
Using the light screen on the desk as cover, Shen Feizhi gripped the back of Gu Suizhou’s wrist and replied, "There’s no one at home. I’d rather stay at the company than go back."
Gu Suizhou pressed his tongue against the roof of his mouth, trying to break free from the restraint. "Is that so? What about your family?"
Neither of them would yield an inch, both vying to gain the upper hand.
Yet their voices remained incredibly steady, as if nothing were out of the ordinary.
Naturally, the assistant sensed nothing amiss. "Mr. Gu, our professor said his side will be finished tomorrow and asked if you could drop by when you have time."
"Mm," Gu Suizhou responded casually. Looking down at their increasingly entangled hands, he added teasingly, "The boss is asking you a question. Why aren't you answering?"
Shen Feizhi thought to himself that this man was truly pushing his luck and courting death.
He let out a cold laugh. "I don't have family. I'm all alone."
Gu Suizhou’s eyes immediately drooped. In a vengeful move, he reached out with lightning speed and pinched Shen Feizhi’s thigh.
Caught off guard, Shen Feizhi let out a soft groan.
It wasn't loud, but in the silence of the office, it was enough to attract the third person's attention.
