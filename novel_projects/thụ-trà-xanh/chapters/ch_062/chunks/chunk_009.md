The man looked over, puzzled. "Are you alright?"
"I'm fine. Just a leg cramp," Shen Feizhi said with a forced smile, tightening his grip and ruthlessly crushing the other man's fingers.
Gu Suizhou hissed, drawing in a sharp breath of cold air. "…You're something else, aren't you?"
"Average." Shen Feizhi quietly shook off his hand. Catching something out of the corner of his eye, he frowned. "President Gu, your ear is inflamed."
"Then help me take care of it," Gu Suizhou said.
Watching Shen Feizhi drop to one knee, Gu Suizhou smiled and leaned down, whispering four words into his ear with a breathy voice: "Thank you, Hubby."
Shen Feizhi’s hand tightened.
"Sss!" Gu Suizhou let out a sharp cry of pain. As the assistant nearby looked over, he barked, "If you don't know what you're doing, then don't do it!"

"Serves you right," Shen Feizhi leaned in close to his ear as well, replying in the same breathy whisper. "Keep acting up, then."
He shouldn't have been soft-hearted enough to make this extra trip today.

Shen Feizhi turned the tables. "You're not happy about it?"
