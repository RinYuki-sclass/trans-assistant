He burst through the door: "Where are the Pheromone Storage Spheres? Give me one!"
Leapton jumped in fright, watching him frantically fish out a sphere as if it were some kind of miraculous life-saving medicine.
Leapton stared at him and the sphere for a long time before suddenly speaking.
"Old Gu, are Pheromone Storage Spheres still the 21st century's biggest failure on Blue Star?"
These words were hauntingly familiar.
Gu Suizhou: "…"
"Get lost."
Days without Shen Feizhi’s actual company were so lonely. Gu Suizhou returned to the company with the pheromone sphere, looking like he’d lost his soul.
It wasn't that he didn't want to go home, but there had been too much to deal with lately. He had no time for rest and had been working several all-nighters in a row.
He wasn't some heartless capitalist; except for a select few who stayed at the office for their own reasons, no one else was working overtime.
He was the only one left in the entire building, along with Ritter’s assistant, who had been "permitted" to stay by his side to "monitor" him.

To put Ritter at ease, this assistant practically lived at W.N, terrified that he might tamper with things at the company.
Without even looking up, Gu Suizhou said, "Go make five copies of that stack of documents behind me, and brew a cup of coffee while you're at it."
Free labor was a waste not to use, and Gu Suizhou showed no mercy, saddling the man with everything from cleaning to menial chores. The man suffered in silence, forced to comply with every demand.
Soon, the documents were brought back.
Gu Suizhou glanced sideways. "Where’s the coffee?"
"Someone is working overtime today," the assistant replied. "When they heard you wanted coffee, they volunteered to make it. I’ll bring it to you in a moment."
"Working overtime at this hour?" Gu Suizhou frowned. Had some slave-driver been privately exploiting the staff? "Go call them in here for me."
"Fine." The assistant rolled his eyes and left the office.
