He curled his lip and gave an indifferent smile.
While Gu Suizhou was supporting him, the boy was also secretly searching for his parents' bodies. But Ritter had hidden them away long ago; he was absolutely confident they would never be found.
Meanwhile, after shaking off everyone tailing him, Gu Suizhou arrived at a crematorium alone.
In today's technologically advanced society, many people choose to preserve their bodies long-term after death or opt for high-end methods of handling their remains.
However, Gu Lang and Lin Zizhuo’s wills left only a single sentence, expressing their wish for dust to return to dust.
They had ended that year’s conflict with their deaths, nipping an even greater threat in the bud; now, they could finally be truly at peace.
Gu Suizhou walked into the crematorium. Attendants led him to a room upstairs, where two bodies lay in the center.

Ritter had preserved the bodies well over the years, and with the mortician's touch, they looked as though they were merely sleeping.
Gu Suizhou reached out and smoothed Gu Lang’s hair.
He actually wanted to ask if they regretted adopting Lin Sui, or if they regretted being soft-hearted toward Ritter…
But he knew the answer.
Lin Zizhuo would tell him gently: No one can predict the future. What’s done is done, and every person must pay the price for every choice and action they take.
On this point, his parents’ philosophy was identical to Shen Feizhi’s.
They never fretted over irreversible outcomes or impractical hypotheses.
Gu Suizhou said softly, "Dad, Mom, your luck in this life was truly terrible. Your only stroke of good fortune was probably having me—and meeting Shen Feizhi."
He knew his parents all too well. He knew that if Shen Feizhi hadn’t been in that basement back then, they likely wouldn't have survived long enough to make it out. It was Shen Feizhi who had risked his life time and again to secure them chances to move and to relay information.
Lin Zizhuo and Gu Lang had saved Shen Feizhi, and Shen Feizhi had saved them in return.
And even after that, Shen Feizhi had saved him, too.
"I originally planned for him to be here with me when you were cremated." Gu Suizhou’s eyes turned cold. "But I'm sorry; something unexpected came up recently, and I’m afraid of delaying you any longer. I couldn't wait. He can’t make it today, so don't blame him."
