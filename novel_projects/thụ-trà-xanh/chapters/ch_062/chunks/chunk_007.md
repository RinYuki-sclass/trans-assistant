"Tch," he muttered, displeased. "Are you a mute—"
"Cleaning Department," a familiar voice came from right beside his ear, tinged with a hint of playfulness. "Does President Gu have any instructions?"

The person standing beside him spoke politely: "What is it, President Gu? Is it not to your taste?"
"…No." Gu Suizhou set aside his work and leaned back against the chair, looking up at him slightly. His tone was flat. "Feed me."
Shen Feizhi: "…"
The assistant looked over in surprise.
Shen Feizhi was dressed in loose, casual clothes that hid his physique, wearing a white mask and a hat. For a cleaner, this attire was normal and wouldn't draw unwanted attention.
Only Gu Suizhou, standing so close to him, could see those deep, bewitching eyes.
Shen Feizhi’s eyelids lowered halfway. "Does President Gu not have hands of his own?"
"Work is too exhausting." Gu Suizhou blinked, shaking his two hands back and forth. "I'm so tired, I can't lift them."
The assistant nearby almost had his eyes pop out of his head.
He had been monitoring this man for over twenty days, and never once had he seen this mercurial CEO act so… coquettish?
He couldn't help but draw closer to the two, wanting to catch a glimpse of the cleaner's true face.
