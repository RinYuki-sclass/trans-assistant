Chapter 62
Shen Feizhi was generally quite frank and open about the things he did; if C2 asked, he would usually tell the truth.
After learning that this man had gone and had a hole cut into his heart for no reason at all, C2’s dark eyes rolled back, and he nearly keeled over.
"Holy shit! Shen Feizhi, you’re finished. I’m serious, you're beyond help."
Shen Feizhi replied nonchalantly, "It’s just a micro-device."
It was only the size of a grain of rice, made of the most advanced materials, and powered by a permanent natural energy source. Aside from the pain of the anesthesia and the incision during surgery, it had virtually no impact on his life.
He and Gu Suizhou wouldn't be seeing each other for a whole month. If he didn't find a way to keep the guy pacified, there was no telling what state he would be in when he returned.
Thinking of this, Shen Feizhi felt his decision and execution were perfectly correct.
C2 was utterly dumbfounded, staring at him as if looking at a terrifying new species. "To be honest, the first time I saw you, I was already mourning for your future partner, wondering what kind of saint could ever put up with dating a cold, heartless prick like you."
He let out a heartfelt sigh. "I realize now that you can't judge a book by its cover."
To be able to date Shen Feizhi—the kid must have literally saved the world in a past life, hadn't he?
Shen Feizhi cast a cold glance at him. "We are not dating yet."
"Right, right, you're not." C2’s dismissal was perfunctory, but internally he was thinking: If this isn't dating, then once they actually start, won't that Gu kid's ego soar straight into the stratosphere?
…Wait, that wasn't right.
