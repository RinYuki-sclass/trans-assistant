This was a longer sentence, and the person on the other side paused after hearing it. When he spoke again, his tone was a bit strange. "Do I… know you?"
Shen Feizhi thought to himself that he was quite sharp. Even C2 might not have been able to detect his voice modulation, yet this person had caught on so quickly.
He didn't intend to keep up the act any longer, reverting to his original voice and saying lazily, "I'll take the deal. Five billion, and you do the ghost marriage yourself—hiss! Gu Suizhou, are you a dog?"
Before he could even finish, the coffin in front of him slid open with a harsh scraping sound. Someone shot out like a firecracker freed from its fuse, slamming straight into his chest. "Brother Shen!"
Talking didn't stop him from biting; before his voice even trailed off, he snapped his teeth onto Shen Feizhi’s neck.
Shen Feizhi rubbed the hands wrapped around his waist. "Why are your hands so cold?"
Gu Suizhou’s voice was muffled. "What do you think? My soul from my previous life was about to leap out of my body from the shock!"
"From shock?" Shen Feizhi mocked him heartlessly. "Weren't you full of vigor just a moment ago when you were trying to strike a fifty-billion deal with me?"
"That was all an act." Gu Suizhou showed weakness without a shred of dignity, finally lifting his head. "I didn't know it was you back then. Of course I couldn't lose face!"
The cool fringe of a hair ornament brushed against his cheek. Shen Feizhi lowered his eyelashes, finally getting a clear look at the other man's current getup.
He made no attempt to hide the admiration and wonder in his eyes.
Gu Suizhou wore a layer of light makeup; the red eyeshadow swept slightly upward, blending perfectly with his fox-like eyes. A tiny cinnabar beauty mark sat by his red lips, enchanting and seductive.
His hair was already somewhat long, so it didn't look out of place at all when paired with feminine hair ornaments and a bridal gown. It gave him a unique, exotic charm.
Shen Feizhi pinched his chin with two fingers to make him face him. "You… could grow your hair a bit longer in the future."
