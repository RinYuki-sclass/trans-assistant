Shen Feizhi narrowed his eyes. "We're in."
Following the instructions on the invitation, the two of them successfully wired a hundred-million-dollar "sponsorship fee" to the Federation’s official account.
Naturally, the Federation wasn't about to provide this kind of perk out of the goodness of their hearts. The invitations had been sent, but if one actually intended to attend, they had to cough up fifty million per person. It was a blatant cash grab, plain and simple.
However, there wasn't much to be said about such transparent fleecing. These people were all as sharp as tacks; did they not understand exactly what was happening?
They were simply paying for a bit of amusement.
The invitation had listed the name of the event and absolutely nothing else.
The Federation maintained their radio silence until December 30th—the day before the event—before finally using private channels to inform them of the specific location and rules.
It was to be an interactive, immersive musical held aboard a cruise ship; the entire vessel would serve as their playground.
the event would run from 3:00 PM on December 31st until 10:00 PM on January 1st.
Participants would interact closely with the performers to complete various side quests; only upon completion could they unlock the treasure chests the actors guarded.
The boxes might contain treasures worth a fortune, or they might be completely empty—it all depended on luck.

At two o'clock the following afternoon, they arrived at the cruise ship’s reception desk right at the scheduled gathering time.
“Mr. Shen Feizhi, Mr. Gu Suizhou, good afternoon.”
The receptionist said respectfully, “Please extend your left hands. we will now fit you with your identity cards.”
The identity cards were metal bracelets engraved with their assigned numbers and names.
Shen Feizhi was No. 6, and Gu Suizhou was No. 9.
Shen Feizhi raised an eyebrow. “These numbers aren't assigned based on check-in order?”
“No.” The receptionist adjusted his glasses. “Our numbers are certainly not assigned at random…”
