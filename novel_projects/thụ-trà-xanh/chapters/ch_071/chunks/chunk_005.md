Shen Feizhi’s brow furrowed slightly.
He remembered.
Gu Suizhou seemed to be afraid of ghosts.
That wasn't ideal.
"Mr. Shen Feizhi," someone said, knocking before entering, "the game is about to begin. Please board the ship."
Shen Feizhi stood up. "Is this a solo mission?"
"To make the game more interesting, it starts that way," the person replied with a smile. "Of course, once the game begins, you are free to seek out companions on the cruise ship to travel together."
Shen Feizhi nodded, signaling his understanding.
But once he stepped aboard, he fell silent for a moment.
This atmosphere, this setting… wasn't it a bit too dark?
When watching the plot unfold before, it never occurred to him that the entire cruise ship would look like something straight out of a horror movie. Wouldn't that kid, Gu Suizhou, be reduced to crawling on the floor?
Feeling a sense of urgency as the sound signaling the game’s start rang out, he followed the stairs toward the areas where there was light.
The actors were fully immersed in the stage play, their silhouettes flickering amidst the shifting lights. Finding someone in this mess was going to be an ordeal.
With great effort, he finally found a quiet spot to catch his breath, but before he could even exhale, his path was blocked.
