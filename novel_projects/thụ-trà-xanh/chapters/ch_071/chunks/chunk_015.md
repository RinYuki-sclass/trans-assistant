The sound of fireworks exploding was endless, and the cheers of the crowds echoed to the heavens. The red robes Shen Feizhi and Gu Suizhou hadn’t had time to take off fluttered wildly—brazen and passionate.
At fifteen thousand meters in the air, they shared a quiet kiss.
Shen Feizhi called his name: “Gu Suizhou.”
Gu Suizhou’s wandering soul finally returned to his body from the helicopter doorway just before the parachute opened.
He raised his arm and gripped the hand at his collar.
Since they weren't wearing gloves, both their hands had been stripped of warmth by the wind, their sense of touch greatly diminished.
But Gu Suizhou felt his palm was scorching hot, warmer than any treasure.
The corners of his mouth curled up, his eyes shining bright. His gaze met those pitch-black eyes through his transparent goggles, and his thin lips parted, bringing the New Year's ritual to a perfect close.
“Happy New Year, Shen Feizhi.”
