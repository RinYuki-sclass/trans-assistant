Before the game started, he had luckily obtained information about this crystal by unlocking three locks, which was why he had headed straight for the tomb from the very beginning.
Few people were suicidal enough to start a game by running downward. He had been the only one in the tomb, and he’d been scared nearly to death. Several times his eyes had been on the verge of rolling back 180 degrees, but at the thought of this thing, he had forced them back into place.
