His brother was truly all-powerful!
Shen Feizhi didn’t respond, instead asking meaningfully, "Don’t you know how, too?"
"?" Gu Suizhou froze. "How did you know?"
After the plane crash back then, he had studied all flight-related knowledge and skills, including skydiving.
"In the villa where you used to live, there were certificates and trophies for it." Shen Feizhi moved his fingers, grabbed two sets of skydiving gear, and tossed one over. "If you want to jump, jump yourself. Don't get any ideas about me."
Tandem skydiving involved a lot of physical contact. What Gu Suizhou was playing dumb for was obvious without even having to think.
Shen Feizhi had no intention of coddling him.
Sure enough, Gu Suizhou curled his lip in disappointment and grumbled, "A flaw in an otherwise perfect plan. How do you notice everything?"
"Be sharper next time." Shen Feizhi glanced at his watch. "Let's jump at midnight. Think of it as… a fresh start for the New Year."
Gu Suizhou smiled. "Alright."
They sat by the helicopter door, watching the massive floating countdown outside as it finally reached the final minute.
Shen Feizhi had just finished checking his safety rope when a slender hand suddenly appeared before him, holding the crystal they had just retrieved.
His long-overdue bangs were whipped by the wind, repeatedly brushing across his eyes and obscuring his view of the other man.
He swept his bangs back. "You—"
"Brother Shen," Gu Suizhou spoke up suddenly. "This might be a bit sudden, but I couldn't think of a better New Year's gift."
"What?" Shen Feizhi looked at the crystal in his hand, a realization beginning to dawn on him.
Gu Suizhou said, "After the C1907 explosion in 2003, a small ring of energy vortexes was left behind."
Shen Feizhi’s mouth hung open in shock.
"Fortunately, it’s finally in my hands."
He smiled and pulled a delicate little band from his pocket, snapping the crystal into the pre-prepared slot on top.
