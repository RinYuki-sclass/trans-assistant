With the addition of the crystal, the band began to shimmer with a starlight glow, looking remarkably like…
Shen Feizhi’s fingers, propped beside him, suddenly curled inward.
"Is this… a proposal?"
The other three people on the helicopter sat with eyes cast down, noses to hearts, pretending to be invisible while their peripheral vision drifted uncontrollably toward the pair.
Gu Suizhou shook his head. "Strictly speaking, not yet."
The subtle tension in Shen Feizhi’s chest was dispelled by his words. He found it amusing. "What do you mean, 'not yet'?"
“I have a glass heart; I can’t handle rejection,” Gu Suizhou hummed. “If you say yes, then this is a proposal. If you don’t, then it’s just a New Year’s gift.”
Unreasonable as always.
Shen Feizhi thought to himself that he had never seen such a hardcore way of giving someone a ring.
He had to admit he was quite a sucker for this.
So, as the first firework of 2029 exploded against the night sky, he raised his left hand. “I accept.”
Gu Suizhou’s heart thudded in sync with the fireworks. His voice was strained, and the hand holding the ring wouldn't stop shaking. “Accept… what?”
Or the proposal?
Shen Feizhi smiled and lowered his gaze, wordlessly rejecting both options to provide a third answer—
He leaned down slightly and, before the other man could react, proactively slid his ring finger through the band.
The ring sat snugly at the base of his finger; the size was perfect. Its translucent amber color looked exactly like a certain someone’s eyes.
The next second, Gu Suizhou’s shoulder was shoved hard. Enveloped by a sudden sense of weightlessness, he fell backward out of the cabin door.
“Hey!” the pilot cried out, startled. “Fuck! I haven’t even descended to a proper altitude yet, goddammit!”
Shen Feizhi ignored him and jumped right after.
That left hand, now adorned with a ring, grabbed a certain someone’s collar. With a slight tug against the wind, during their minute of freefall, he swiftly and precisely captured the other’s breath.
