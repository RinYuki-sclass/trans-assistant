Shen Feizhi looked down. "Leapton?"
"What a coincidence!" Leapton greeted him cheerfully. "Huh, you're not with Old Gu?"
Shen Feizhi asked, "You saw him?"
"I saw him once before coming in," Leapton said. "He might have gone to the basement first."
The basement. There wasn't even a single lamp there.
Standing at the top of the stairs, Shen Feizhi had never considered the possibility that a certain someone would actually come here first.

He hadn't gone two steps down the narrow one-way path when he heard an aged voice say, "Are you the groom waiting for his lover?"
Shen Feizhi paused. "…Priest?"
He remembered this plot point. The Third Miss's groom's heart belonged to another; on the eve of the wedding, he met with his lover and was discovered by the Third Miss, leading the pair to commit suicide together.
After death, the groom arrived at the place of reincarnation, but he didn't see his lover for the longest time.
The Priest who presided over restless souls told him that his lover was being kept alive by the Third Miss with medicine; because they weren't fully dead yet, they couldn't come here.
Unwilling to reincarnate early, the groom insisted on staying to wait until his lover finally died. Only after the two held a ghost marriage would their wishes be fulfilled so they could move on to their next life.
The groom’s plotline was one of the most melodramatic, scandalous, and morally questionable romantic arcs in the entire stage play.
And naturally, it was the one Shen Feizhi got stuck with.
Shen Feizhi’s current mood was no better than that of the deceased groom.
With a stoic expression, he took the wedding robes from the priest’s hands and threw them on half-heartedly. He grabbed a pen and paper to wait for the unlucky soul who would trigger the "lover" script, idly doodling to pass the time.
It was almost pitch-black down here in the basement; why would Gu Suizhou come here?
Seeking a thrill was out of the question. The only possibility was that the tomb held something he wanted.
Speaking of which, that man's level of interest in the cruise ship was indeed a bit strange.
