71. Extra 1
New Year's Eve on Blue Star was generally a grand affair, steeped in a sense of ritual.
Not long after Gu Suizhou celebrated his twenty-second birthday, the Federation released its New Year's Eve announcements.
Two messages: one for the public, and one for the wealthy.
The public one was the same old story—gala performances, social mixers, and carnivals—nothing particularly interesting. However, the event held for the wealthy this time was much more intriguing: it was an invitation.
Both Gu Suizhou and Shen Feizhi received one.
“A large-scale immersive song and dance treasure hunt?” Gu Suizhou knit his brows. “What kind of crap is that? I’d rather stay home and have sex.”
Shen Feizhi: “……”
He glanced at the trash can, overflowing with tissues and wrappers—every bit of it evidence of someone’s unbridled behavior under the guise of celebrating a birthday.
“Aren’t you afraid of premature kidney failure?”
“Don’t worry, I get regular checkups.” Gu Suizhou’s tone was actually quite proud, heavily implying a subtext of ‘Hurry up and praise me for my self-discipline.’
Shen Feizhi pushed away the hand that was creeping toward his waist again. “Not planning on going? Then I’ll write a letter of refusal.”
“Hey, hey, wait!” Gu Suizhou grabbed him. “Who said we aren’t going? We’re going!”
Shen Feizhi found it a bit strange. “Didn’t you think it sounded boring?”
“The format is boring, but the prizes aren’t.” Gu Suizhou smiled. “I had someone look into it. The ‘treasures’ in this event are all rare items you can't find on the market. We might just scavenge quite a few gems if we go.”

“Rare items?” That phrase piqued Shen Feizhi’s interest as well.
The joys of the wealthy were actually far fewer than those of ordinary people. An ordinary person would be happy for a long time upon getting a house or a car, but for them, projects worth tens of billions were as mundane as drinking plain water; it was difficult to feel a sense of surprise.
Compared to the multi-billion-dollar deals they handled at the drop of a hat, it was the things money couldn't buy that truly intrigued them.
Gu Suizhou nodded. "Are we in?"
