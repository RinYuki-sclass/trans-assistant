A moment later, the pen stopped at his fingertips.
Someone was coming.
He arched an eyebrow, intending to greet the other unlucky soul.
Unexpectedly, before he could even open his mouth, the other party spoke first.
"Hey, buddy, let’s make a deal."
Shen Feizhi: "?"
That voice…?
He couldn't help but chuckle silently.
What a coincidence.
The lighting was far too dim, and with a long coffin separating them, neither could see the other's face or figure clearly.
The person continued on his own: "Our task is to follow the ghost marriage process and perform the wedding bows. How about this: you forfeit, I’ll finish the ceremony alone to get the chest, and I’ll compensate you once we’re out. The terms are negotiable; you won't lose out."
Shen Feizhi felt like teasing him, so he deliberately altered his voice. "Let me think about it."
The other person grew a bit impatient. "Five billion. Deal or no deal?"
This time, Shen Feizhi was genuinely surprised.
What on earth was in that box for this guy to be so generous?
