Both were still wearing their wedding robes. Gu Suizhou took Shen Feizhi’s hand; the crimson fabric swayed with their movements, casting dappled shadows on the ground as the light hit it.
They had walked the long Path to the Afterlife, only to find that the finish line was actually the top deck of the cruise ship.
The place was deserted; it seemed no one else had arrived yet.
Several helicopters were parked on the massive platform, with people standing guard nearby, waiting for them.
"Mr. Shen, Mr. Gu, please put on your protective gear."
The helicopters would fly to the best vantage point on Blue Star to view the city’s nightscape and the upcoming celebration.
It was currently December 31, 2028, 23:41, on Blue Star.
Only nineteen minutes remained until the massive interstellar New Year's Eve fireworks display.
Shen Feizhi and Gu Suizhou had luckily made it in time for this grand celebration.
They sat in the helicopter with the doors open, the wind whipping past them as they listened to the clamor below.
The night wind was arrogant; the people were free.
"How much do you guys get paid to fly for a night?" Gu Suizhou had already started chatting with the pilot.
The pilot thought for a moment. "A bit over a million after taxes."
"That’s not bad," Gu Suizhou said with a nod.
The two chatted idly, and the pilot’s originally stiff and uneasy state dissipated quite a bit. "By the way, Mr. Gu, would you like to try skydiving?"
"We can skydive?" Gu Suizhou asked in surprise. "Are the facilities that well-equipped?"
"The organizers insist on making guests feel at home," the pilot said. "The two people behind us are professionals; if you’d like, they can take you down."
Gu Suizhou turned to look at Shen Feizhi. "Bro, do you know how?"
"Yes," Shen Feizhi nodded.
Gu Suizhou clicked his tongue. "Just as I expected!"
