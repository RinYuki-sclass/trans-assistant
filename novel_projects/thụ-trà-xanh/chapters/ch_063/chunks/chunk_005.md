But then again, it was only natural—after all, he and this crowd were simply not on the same level.
Ritter let out a contemptuous snort, then turned his gaze toward the capsule.
The capsule's frosted glass was slowly rotating, revealing an inner layer of ordinary glass.
Gu Suizhou stood behind it, swaying the transmitter in his hand with a faint, mocking smile.
It was just like a month ago, the way he had aimed when he burst into the hotel.
The only difference was…
Ritter’s eyes suddenly bulged. His face turned deathly pale as he lunged forward, his entire body pressed against the glass.
His voice was so sharp it cracked: "Shen Feizhi?!!"
How could he be here? Wasn't he dead? How was he still standing here, alive and well!
Both Gu Suizhou and Shen Feizhi were fluent in lip-reading; they naturally understood what he was shouting.
Shen Feizhi heard Gu Suizhou mutter something in displeasure.
Curious, he asked, "Why the long face?"
"He’s saying your name." Gu Suizhou’s possessiveness was practically exploding. "It doesn’t sit right with me."
Shen Feizhi said helplessly, "Plenty of people say my name."

Shen Feizhi wanted to tease him a bit more, but then he felt the wrist resting on his shoulder suddenly tighten.
It was the precursor to action.
He stopped talking, quietly cooperating with the other man.
