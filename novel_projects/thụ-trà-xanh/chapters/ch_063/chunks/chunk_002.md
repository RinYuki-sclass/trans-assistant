After a brief discussion, Shen Feizhi decided he would be the one to do it.

Yet on the day of the operation, Shen Feizhi never expected that Gu Suizhou would be the one to stop him.
"Let go." He frowned, looking at the youth before him who had grown noticeably thinner. "If I don't go now, it’ll be too late."

In that brief moment of distraction, Shen Feizhi lost his best chance to resist.
