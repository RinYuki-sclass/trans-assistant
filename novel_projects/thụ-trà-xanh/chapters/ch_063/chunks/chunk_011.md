He had envisioned these scenes many times, so he wasn't surprised.
Yet, looking at the countless comments apologizing to Lin Zizhuo and Gu Lang, he still felt a sense of unreality—a hollow, drifting feeling with nowhere to land.
Until his pinky brushed against another source of warm body heat.
Gu Suizhou looked down, and his heart finally settled.
He leaned his body slightly, resting his head on Shen Feizhi’s shoulder. This time, he wasn’t pushed away; the other man even adjusted his posture to make it more comfortable for him to lean against.
Gu Suizhou smiled soundlessly. He opened his comms and said to the panicked Ritter on the screen, "Did you forget someone?"
Both Ritter and Shen Feizhi were taken aback.
After a moment, Ritter’s lips trembled as he spoke: "I’m sorry… Shen Feizhi."
Shen Feizhi’s heart tightened abruptly, and he pressed his lips together.
He had almost forgotten it himself.
Gu Suizhou said with a beaming smile, "How about a couple more kowtows?"
Shen Feizhi said tonelessly, "That’s enough."
"Alright then," Gu Suizhou agreed obediently. "True, we’re running out of time."
The livestream was cut off, leaving only Ritter’s roar through the speakers in their room.
"Gu Suizhou! You’re going back on your word! Let me back right now!"
"Going back on my word?" Gu Suizhou sneered. "When did I ever promise to let you back?"
In truth, if he were alone, he would have let the lies fly without a second thought. But he didn't want to leave Shen Feizhi with the impression of a villain who was all talk and no integrity, so he could only play word games with a straight face.
He glanced sideways every now and then as he spoke, making Shen Feizhi want to laugh a little.
