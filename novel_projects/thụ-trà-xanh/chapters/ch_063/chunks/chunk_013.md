They were not people who dwelled on the past or allowed themselves to be held hostage by pain. Now that they knew the outcome, they didn’t want anything more to do with Ritter.
Having finally dealt with all that, there was still a mountain of messes waiting to be resolved; there was no time to spare for someone like that.
Gu Suizhou let out a long sigh and sprawled across the seat, resting his head on the other man’s lap.
Shen Feizhi adjusted his legs to make him more comfortable.
Sensing the subtle shift, Gu Suizhou kept his eyes closed and chuckled. "So considerate. Bro, you love me so much~"
This wasn't the first time he'd been flippant with Shen Feizhi.
But this time, Shen Feizhi didn't tell him to get lost, nor did he respond with silence.
After looking down at him for a brief two seconds, he gave a soft, "Mm."
Gu Suizhou: "…"
Gu Suizhou bolted upright, his head slamming into the car roof with a loud thwack . It sounded painful, but he didn't care. "Did you just say 'mm'?!"
They were at a traffic light, and the red light was a long one.
Shen Feizhi’s eyes lidded slightly. His lips moved, but then he suddenly heard someone tapping on the car window.
Both of them looked over at once.
It was a man dressed in a suit and leather shoes.
He stood right there in the motor lane, smiling amiably at Shen Feizhi. The passing traffic police acted as if he were invisible; not a single one came to intervene.
Shen Feizhi locked eyes with him through the glass for two seconds before turning to Gu Suizhou. "Go back first. Wait for me at home."
Gu Suizhou felt an instinctive surge of panic and grabbed his hand.
The man was merely standing outside the car… yet he exerted a powerful, overbearing sense of pressure.
The windows were clearly one-way glass, yet the man’s dark gray eyes had just locked onto theirs with unerring precision.
It was uncomfortable, suffocating—especially the way he looked at Shen Feizhi. He had clearly locked onto his target.
