The sound of Gu Suizhou’s index finger hooking around the trigger was exceptionally clear.
His heartbeat suddenly quickened by several beats; he had never felt this way even when he was the one taking the risk.
Shen Feizhi couldn't help but hold his breath.
The shuttle had already started; there was no way Ritter could bring it back down so quickly.
The moment it was about to fly out of the test site’s airspace, a medium-caliber bullet grazed past his ear.
The launcher was equipped with a silencer, its sound drowned out by the deafening roar of mechanical operations, yet the shattering blast of it striking glass still drew the attention of many.
More than half the crowd watched as the bullet tore through the air, unerringly blowing apart the control panel at the very bottom of the shuttle.
Shen Feizhi’s tension suddenly eased.
But a second later, his nerves pulled taut again. "Why are you letting him see me now?"

Gu Suizhou hurried to massage his shoulders. "I know what you're worried about."

The shattered glass was quickly recovered by the original one-way glass, turning the space back into a private sanctuary for the two of them.
Shen Feizhi pushed his head away and sat down on a sofa chair brought over by a mechanical claw.
Gu Suizhou refused to sit alone, insisting on squeezing into the single-seater sofa with him.
Shen Feizhi’s mind was on the task at hand, so he didn’t argue.
Gu Suizhou got his wish, pressing close against him, his hand resting loosely on his shoulder, occasionally stroking his neck and getting handsy.
Until he was slapped upside the head.
"Behave, or get out."
