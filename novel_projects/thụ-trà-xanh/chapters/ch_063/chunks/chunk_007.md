At those two simple words, Gu Suizhou’s eyes crinkled with a wide smile.
Meanwhile, on the shuttle, Ritter still had no idea what was happening.
His mind was entirely consumed by Shen Feizhi’s indifferent gaze from moments ago.
Why? Why?!
He was clearly dead! The Energy Orb couldn't be wrong. If that man hadn't died, why would the energy field of the Storm Eye have…
Suddenly, he realized something.
If Shen Feizhi’s life and death were linked to the energy field, then if he knew that himself, could he also control it?!
Ritter panicked, a massive wave of palpitations and terror enveloping him.
Why were those two doing this?
Was Gu Suizhou… seeking revenge?
Other than this, he could find no other logical explanation.
Ritter’s eyes were so bloodshot they looked ready to drip; his already sunken sockets appeared even more ghastly.
He absolutely could not die like this!
His existence was of such great significance—how could he die so easily?
