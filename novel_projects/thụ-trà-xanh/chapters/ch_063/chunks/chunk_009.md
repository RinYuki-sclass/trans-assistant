Listening to the dial tone in his earpiece, Ritter’s expression went blank.
There were only thirty minutes left before they flew beyond the Blue Star's sphere of control. Time was running out. His gaze drifted uncontrollably toward the button Gu Suizhou had mentioned, his Adam's apple bobbing rhythmically.
At this moment, he was finally forced to admit that he was truly terrified of dying.
It had nothing to do with any grand, high-sounding reasons; he simply didn't want to die. It was as simple as that.
The news broadcast reached the masses, including Blue Star and every star system under the Federation's rule.
Saturday at seven in the evening on Blue Star was the time of peak viewership.
It was under these circumstances that Ritter abruptly broke into the channel, crashing into the public's field of vision.
"Good evening, everyone…" He took a deep breath. "I am Ritter Naons."
[Isn't that Professor Ritter? I was just reading his academic papers yesterday.]
[? I only stayed off the grid for a day, what’s going on? Can someone fill me in?]
[To the person above: he got busted for embezzlement. A massive sum of money, too—I’ve never seen that much in my entire life.]
[To be honest, I feel like if the Federation’s funding had been sufficient, Professor Ritter wouldn’t have done something like this, right?]
Read at Novelib.com
[Are you out of your mind? Federation funding has to be allocated rationally based on the situation; they can’t just give all the money to him alone.]
The online arguments grew louder, but most were still discussing the embezzlement of public funds.
Thus, Ritter’s very first sentence silenced the bullet comments.
"Next, I shall make a public statement regarding the events of fifteen years ago."
"Regarding the illegal sale of substandard pharmaceuticals by Professor Lin Zizhuo and Professor Gu Lang of the W.N Research Institute, I bear an inescapable responsibility…"
He offered a pious confession, yet every word between the lines sought to absolve his own crimes. Terms like "oversight," "negligence," "accident," and "unforeseen" were strung together, leaving the audience in a daze.
[Uh… why can't I understand what he's saying?]
