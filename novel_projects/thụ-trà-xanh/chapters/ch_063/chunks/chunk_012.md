Was that really necessary? It wasn't as if he were some kind of moral paragon.
Shen Feizhi raised a hand and cut the communication. "Don’t waste your breath on him."
C2 reported that everything was going smoothly on his end. Relieved, the two began contacting various departments to prepare the press releases for the media after Ritter's departure.
The omnipresent reporters had already blocked the front gate of the test site until it was airtight. They could only leave through the back door; though there were reporters there too, there were less than half as many as at the front.
It was a rare moment of disarray for the two of them. On the way out, Shen Feizhi was even bumped while steadying a female reporter, nearly knocking his head against the doorframe.
Fortunately, Gu Suizhou caught him by the waist in time. "Are you okay?!"
"I’m fine." Shen Feizhi pried his hand away and said in a low voice, "Mind your image. There are cameras."
"Who cares about the cameras," Gu Suizhou grumbled.
This brief pause gave the reporters the chance they were looking for, and they surged forward, cameras propped on their shoulders.
"Mr. Gu, do you plan to file a lawsuit against Ritter Naons?"
“Mr. Gu, are the facts exactly as Ritter said? Is there anything else hidden?”
Amidst the heap of rude and useless, noisy questions, one suddenly popped out.
“Mr. Gu, Ritter Naons mentioned Mr. Shen Feizhi in his final sentence. May I ask what role Mr. Shen played in this matter? Was he a victim?”
Gu Suizhou’s footsteps halted.
He accurately locked onto the reporter who asked the question, his tone frigid. “I will say what needs to be said at the press conference in a few days. Until then, if any of you dare to disturb Shen Feizhi or dig into his private information, you will bear the consequences.”
Shen Feizhi raised an eyebrow.
By the time they finally escaped the crowd and sat in the car, a thunderous explosion suddenly erupted from the horizon.
Looking up, that was exactly the location of the world’s Energy Storm Eye.
C2 sent a message asking if he wanted to come watch the site of Ritter’s execution.
Shen Feizhi and Gu Suizhou glanced at each other, and both refused.
