With a distorted face, he switched on the communication device, attempting to contact his friends at the jump point.
"Requesting contact with the station guards at coordinates A-13."
But after a burst of static, the electronic voice replied:
【We are sorry, Mr. Ritter. All of your communication devices and personal assets are currently under Federation surveillance. Outgoing transmissions are disabled.】
The smile on Ritter’s face froze.
The amount of funds he had misappropriated this time was staggering, reaching into the hundreds of billions—a sum more than enough to see him sentenced to death.
A crackle of static erupted from somewhere within the shuttle, and Gu Suizhou’s voice soon followed.
"Good morning, Ritter Naons."
He no longer bothered with hypocritical honorifics.
Ritter slammed his palm against the control console. "What do you think you're doing? Gu Suizhou, I’m warning you, your parents' bodies—"
"My parents will be buried tomorrow," Gu Suizhou interrupted nonchalantly. "It’s a pity you won't be there to see it."
Ritter spoke with great difficulty, his voice raspy and strained. "You… how…"
"Ritter," another voice interrupted him. "You are going to die."
The communication channel fell into a dead silence.

Ritter suddenly roared, "Shen Feizhi! What are you trying to do? I can't die! I’m telling you, if I die, all of you will—"
"Who are you shouting at?" Gu Suizhou’s tone was sharp. "The button to your left connects directly to the Central News live broadcast. You know what to do, don't you?"
He finished speaking rapidly, cutting off the communication before the other man even had time to react.
