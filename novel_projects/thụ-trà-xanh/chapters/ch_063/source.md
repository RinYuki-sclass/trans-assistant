They say you can't be soft-hearted; if you are, you've already lost.

As it turned out, that saying held true.

Shen Feizhi had learned this lesson through painful firsthand experience.

He returned home with a look of displeasure and called C2. "How are things on your end?"

"Everything's going smoothly," C2 said. "Ready to interface with you guys at any time. A11, you're ready on your side, right?"

A11 gave a cold "Mhm" in response.

Now that someone reliable had appeared, Shen Feizhi finally felt at ease upon hearing his voice.

Because they had acted on their own initiative without authorization, the big boss on the Main Star had given them a severe lashing. Even with A11 stepping up to take the fall, C2 still had to return to the Main Star to submit a report.

A11's year-end bonus had been docked to the last cent. For a model employee like him, this was nothing short of a stinging humiliation.

C2 felt a bit guilty facing him. "It's fine, I've still got half of my year-end bonus left."

A11 felt a headache coming on and didn't even bother looking at him. "Keep it and spend it yourself."

It was already a blessing if this guy didn't come to him to borrow money.

C2 offered a few more insincere protests before giving up. He clapped A11 on the shoulder, calling him a true brother.

When word of this reached Shen Feizhi and Gu Suizhou, neither was the least bit surprised; it was exactly the kind of underhanded stunt C2 would pull.

Before daybreak the following morning, Shen Feizhi rose and headed to Ritter’s test site in the Underground City.

To prevent any complications that might allow Ritter to turn back mid-flight, they planned to sabotage the shuttle itself. At the exact moment of ignition, they would manually sever the external connection lines of the joystick controller.

It was a maneuver of extreme difficulty. After several simulations, Shen Feizhi couldn't guarantee a hundred percent success rate by shooting the wires; only pulling them by hand was truly foolproof.

But while manual precision was higher, the danger increased accordingly.

After a brief discussion, Shen Feizhi decided he would be the one to do it.

The Main Star had always favored him; with luck and skill stacked in his favor, nothing major should go wrong.

Yet on the day of the operation, Shen Feizhi never expected that Gu Suizhou would be the one to stop him.

"Let go." He frowned, looking at the youth before him who had grown noticeably thinner. "If I don't go now, it’ll be too late."

“Don’t move.” Gu Suizhou was sitting on the control console, his arms tightly encircling the other’s waist. “Don’t worry, I’ve arranged everything.”

Arranged everything?

What exactly did you arrange?

Gu Suizhou let out a soft sneer.

Ever since he was young, Ritter had been pretentious. He was a genius—a conceited, contemptuous genius.

He had established a strict social circle for himself, surrounding himself only with the elite of the elite, like Lin Zizhuo and Gu Lang. Everyone else, in his eyes, was mere trash that lowered his quality of life.

He had always felt that he didn’t belong here. Someone like him clearly belonged in a more advanced world; there were too many idiots in this world, to the point where even breathing felt like an ordeal.

As for Gu Suizhou, a junior with immense talent, Ritter felt a sense of pity. He thought it was a shame that such a perfect physical vessel was trapped by a depraved and decadent soul.
