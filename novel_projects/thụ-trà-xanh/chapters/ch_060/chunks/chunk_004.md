They enjoyed a rare period of peace and quiet there.
Usually, Shen Feizhi would cook or tend to some flowers, while Gu Suizhou would cling to him, playing cards, going shopping, or gaming together—occasionally holding remote meetings with the other side. It was a comfortable, leisurely existence.
When C2's communication came through, Gu Suizhou was busy volunteering in the kitchen, attempting to cook a proper meal for the first time.

"I’ve found everything," C2 let out a long sigh. "The computers here are seriously a pain to use. I ended up burning through three of Leapton's optical computers."
Shen Feizhi’s lips quirked into a faint smile. "No wonder he came to us for a reimbursement a few days ago."
C2 huffed. "Good thing I managed to dig it up for you. Tracking down the founder directly wasn't realistic, so I could only trace the source accounts for Lu Fan’s transfer records. And guess what?"
"Which acquaintance is it this time?"
"Shen Wei!!"
C2 said excitedly, "Damn, that kid just got out of the Detention Center, didn't he? He starts plotting against you the second he's out. How much does he hate you?"
"Shen Wei…" Shen Feizhi stared blankly at a single point, one hand tapping against his smartphone. "Is it really him?"
"Yeah, the name on the first account that transferred the money is his. I couldn't have made a mistake," C2 said.
"I’m not saying you made a mistake," Shen Feizhi explained. "I mean, perhaps he's just being used as a pawn, too."
C2 seemed to have an epiphany. "Tsk, that’s troublesome."
Shen Feizhi raised an eyebrow. "We'll be back as soon as—"
Gu Suizhou: "Brother Shen, time to eat."
Shen Feizhi: "…As soon as I finish eating, I’ll head back."
C2, having heard everything: "…"
He said snidely, "Yeah, yeah, yeah. Eat, eat. Of course you can't work on an empty stomach."
