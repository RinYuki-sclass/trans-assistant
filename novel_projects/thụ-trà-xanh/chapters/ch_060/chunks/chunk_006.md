"No manpower?" Shen Feizhi was puzzled. "The Physics Group has plenty of people, doesn't it?"
"Yeah, but he’s dissatisfied with those useless hacks. He can't seem to find an assistant who's actually up to par."
Ritter's high standards were common knowledge throughout the Research Institute.
When he first took a liking to Shen Feizhi, everyone had been in a state of utter disbelief.
After leaving the office, Shen Feizhi headed to the Underground Energy Test Site to find him.
Seeing him approach, Ritter greeted him warmly. "You're finally here!"
Shen Feizhi nodded. "How is the progress?"
"Not too bad," Ritter said, showing off his results. "Look, this is the data for the outer energy layer. I feel like I'm close to figuring out the pattern!"
To anyone else, the winding line graphs might have looked complex, but Shen Feizhi understood what he was looking at in a single glance. "This data is enough to publish a paper. What are you still working on?"
"That’s where you’re wrong." Ritter stared at the charts, entranced. "Look here, babe. This segment—the data values are clearly different from the rest! I have a feeling this kind of energy field doesn't follow fixed natural laws. It can be manipulated!"
Shen Feizhi’s fingers, hanging at his side, suddenly curled inward.

He masked the shock in his eyes and stared intently at that specific segment of data.
The timeframe corresponding to this data was exactly when Gu Suizhou had differentiated—the same time he and C2 had returned.
…So the energy field issue they encountered was actually caused by Ritter, purely by accident?!
The discovery left him momentarily speechless.
Events were piling up one after another; faced with the truth that Ritter was very likely the one on the verge of discovering the Main Star's existence, he couldn't even muster a facial expression.
Shen Feizhi didn't want to say much more. He hurriedly memorized the data, packaged it off to C2, and bid Ritter farewell.
Autumn in Blue Star's First District was still warm. Walking along the road, listening to the rustle of wind through the leaves, a sense of inexplicable weariness often took hold.
Even Shen Feizhi, who was rarely affected by his environment, felt his head throbbing. His legs nearly buckled, almost sending him tumbling into an open manhole by the side of the road.
