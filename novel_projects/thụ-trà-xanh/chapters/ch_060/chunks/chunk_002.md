Shen Feizhi was amused. "Alright, go wash up. I'll buy you some ointment to put on later."
Gu Suizhou’s eyes crinkled. "Okay."
Once they were both dressed, they made a special trip to the next street to withdraw one million in cash, then headed back the same way with the money in hand.
Shen Feizhi asked, "Did your people catch those guys yesterday?"
"Just as we expected," Gu Suizhou replied. "You couldn't beat a word out of them; they genuinely have no idea what's going on, nor do they have any info on the employer."
It was to be expected, so there was no real sense of disappointment.
Timing their arrival for the moment the lady finished her lunch break to head back to work, they reached the ATM.
"We meet again," Gu Suizhou greeted her warmly. Using his sleeve as cover, he flicked out a thin chip.
Unnoticed by the woman, the chip adhered to the inner hem of her trouser leg.
She gave a polite smile. "What a coincidence. I have to get to work now, please make yourselves at home."
Gu Suizhou nodded with a smile.
