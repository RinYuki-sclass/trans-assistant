Chapter 60
When Shen Feizhi woke up, he habitually reached for his waist. Finding nothing there, he quietly opened his eyes.
Habit truly was a terrifying thing; he had actually begun to grow accustomed to Gu Suizhou crawling into his bed in the middle of the night to hold him.
Suddenly feeling nothing there actually felt a bit strange.
It was rare for Gu Suizhou to be lying so obediently in his own bed, sleeping so deeply.
Shen Feizhi walked over, intending to wake him. Gu Suizhou gave a muddled response, sounding tired beyond words.
It was unclear why he was so exhausted.
Shen Feizhi didn't try to wake him again. He adjusted the room’s ventilation and temperature, asked his secretary to buy breakfast, then opened his portable brain by the window to handle some official business.
He organized the documents and data for District Seven and submitted them to the official website of the Federation General Administration Department.
He hoped the ruling class wouldn't turn a blind eye to it.
The sunlight outside grew increasingly dazzling. By the time Shen Feizhi finished his breakfast, the person on the bed suddenly bolted upright. "Brother Shen!"
"I'm here," Shen Feizhi replied. "Awake?"
Without even putting on shoes, Gu Suizhou ran over and hugged him, staying quiet for a moment. Only after confirming he was really there did he heave a sigh of relief. "Did you rest well last night?"
"Not bad," Shen Feizhi said. "But look at those dark circles. Didn't sleep well?"
"Yeah. Couldn't sleep without holding you," Gu Suizhou said with a half-joking, half-serious smile.
