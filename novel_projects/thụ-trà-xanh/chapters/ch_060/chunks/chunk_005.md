Shen Feizhi didn't bother arguing with him and cut the communication directly.
Gu Suizhou pulled out a chair for him. "What is it? Did something come up?"
Shen Feizhi gave a brief overview of the situation.
Although Shen Wei had unconsciously played a part in this affair, with a brain like his, it was unlikely he was a primary participant.
He probably hadn't been involved when Elder Master Shen and Madam Shen imprisoned Lin Zizhuo and his wife to ensure his differentiation into an Alpha. He likely found out later, but he hadn't reported it either.
"Shen Wei is no saint," Shen Feizhi said, "but he's half a mama's boy. The Old Master is already behind bars. If someone tells him now that Lin Sui meeting with me will get his mother sent away too, how could he possibly just sit by and do nothing?"
"Eat first." Gu Suizhou began fiddling with his smartphone. "I'll have someone go grab him."
"Keep it discreet. Don't alert the enemy." Shen Feizhi picked up a piece of broccoli. Noticing the other's phone vibrating incessantly, he frowned. "What's going on? So many messages."
"Oh, this," Gu Suizhou scratched his head, frustrated. "Didn't we decide to invest the R&D funds and copyrights into the Pharmacology Team? The leaders of the other two teams in the Research Institute are hounding me."
Shen Feizhi nodded. "Leave it. I'll go take a look when I get back."
He had always been a man of swift action. Having said he would look into it, he headed straight for the Research Institute the moment he stepped off the shuttle.
Gu Suizhou was no incompetent; he didn't need to worry about every little thing. He trusted that the man could handle the situation with Shen Wei perfectly.
Inside W.N, the Director's office was on the verge of exploding.
When Shen Feizhi walked in, Ritter and the Biology Team Leader happened to be coming out. Seeing Shen Feizhi, they both offered weary greetings before trudging away listlessly.
