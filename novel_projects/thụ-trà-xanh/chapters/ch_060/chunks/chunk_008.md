Shen Feizhi looked up, only to see a heavy-duty truck careening out of control, flying toward the cafe at high speed!
His terrible sixth sense reached its peak at this moment. He lunged to his feet and threw himself to the side.
Before moving, he didn't forget to grab his untouched chocolate milk tea.

"Holy shit!"
Amidst a flurry of screams, the heavy truck slammed violently into the cafe’s glass windows. Following a series of sharp, splintering crashes, it was finally brought to a halt by a fallen tree at the entrance.
"My God, where did that truck come from?"
"No idea. How did it suddenly lose control? Didn't they do safety checks before it set off?"
"Ouch, thank goodness that lad sitting there just now ran fast. There weren't many people today either, otherwise, who knows how many would’ve died."
The spot where Shen Feizhi had been sitting was now smashed beyond recognition; it had taken the brunt of the impact.
The cafe was in total chaos. The sirens of ambulances and fire trucks blended together, grating on everyone's nerves.
Although no one had died, the number of injured was not small.
Shen Feizhi stood to the side, shielding a little girl. His expression was frighteningly grim.
A paramedic stepped forward, looking at his arm with concern. "Sir, your hand is injured. Do you need a bandage?"
The hand he had used to protect the little girl’s head had been sliced by several shards of glass, making for a gruesome sight.
Shen Feizhi thought for a moment, then nodded. "If you wouldn't mind."
"Not at all, it's our job."
He followed the medical staff onto the ambulance for emergency treatment. While the nurse was occupied clearing away the glass shards, he used his body as cover, dipped a hand into the milk tea residue, and smeared it onto a nearby substance detector.
