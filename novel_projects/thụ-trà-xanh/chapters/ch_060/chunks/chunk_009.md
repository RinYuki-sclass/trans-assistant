Shen Feizhi closed his eyes briefly.
"Sir, your wound is quite deep. I’ll apply some debridement and anti-inflammatory medication for you." The doctor changed into a new pair of gloves and tossed the blood-stained glass tray aside.
Shen Feizhi snapped back to reality. Just as he was about to speak, a sudden flash of insight struck him.
He lunged without warning, grabbing the doctor’s hand and wrenching it back with brute force!
The doctor let out a pained cry, dropping the medicine bottle. The liquid that spilled out instantly corroded the floor.
Shen Feizhi cursed under his breath. "Who sent you?"
The man clamped his teeth down hard, refusing to speak as blood began to seep from the corners of his mouth.
Just as Shen Feizhi was about to stop him from committing suicide, people from outside began swarming toward them.
"Hey! You in there! What are you doing to the patient?!"
In this situation, there was no way to interrogate him, nor any way to drag him along.
He delivered a sharp chop to the man’s nape, knocking him out to stop him from ending his own life. Then, he leaped over the driver's seat and slipped out through the front door.
