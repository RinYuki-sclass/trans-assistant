The High School was located in a remote area, especially eerie at night. Aside from the humming of the AI security guard at the main gate, the silence was as unsettling as a scene from a horror movie.
Gu Suizhou knelt on the ground, staring up at Shen Feizhi's ambiguous expression, a sudden chill gripping his heart.
He seems to have provoked the King of Hell himself.
The King of Hell, with his model-like long legs, lifted one foot and tapped the tip of his shoe against Gu Suizhou's kneeling knee. "Get up quickly. We need to get your leg treated at the hospital first."
"Take tomorrow off. I'll take you to work."

Gu Suizhou was taken aback by the sudden change of subject. "Work? When did you get a job?"
"Recently," Shen Feizhi said. "Aren't you curious how I knew you skipped class to go to the Underground City today? You'll find out tomorrow."
Gu Suizhou grunted in response, gritting his teeth as he forced himself to stand, enduring the pain.
The next moment, a thick, long trench coat was draped over his shoulders.
He looked up in surprise. Shen Feizhi, wearing only an undershirt, straightened Gu Suizhou's collar. "You didn't even change into your winter uniform? If you catch a cold, we don't have the money for another hospital visit."
This guy isn't as heartless as the King of Hell after all.
Gu Suizhou tightened his grip on the coat. "Thank you, Shen…"
"And," Shen Feizhi's gaze lingered with amusement on Gu Suizhou's perfectly curved rear, "you must have changed in a hurry and didn't notice—there's a hole in the back of your pants."
"Red underwear… is it your lucky year?"
