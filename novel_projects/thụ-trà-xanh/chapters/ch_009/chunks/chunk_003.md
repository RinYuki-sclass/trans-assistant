Shen Feizhi rented a hoverbike and sped through the wind to the entrance of the High School.
Knowing the Little Brat wouldn't make it back in time, he rode at a leisurely pace, taking two hours to arrive.
He pulled out his smartphone, snapped a photo of the school gate, and sent it to the Little Brat, adding a loving message: Here. Come out.
After the message sent successfully, Shen Feizhi took out a cigarette he had bought on the way, ready for a smoke to pass the time.
Just as he was about to spark the flame—
"Second Young Master Shen!"
A joyful, surprised call came from behind him.
Shen Feizhi frowned imperceptibly at the sound. He twirled the cigarette between his index and ring fingers before tucking it back into the pack.
