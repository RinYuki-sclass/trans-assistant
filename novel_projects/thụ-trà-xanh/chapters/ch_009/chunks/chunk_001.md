Leapton found Gu Suizhou at his workstation, his brow furrowed in concentration as he stared intently at the illuminated screen.
"What's wrong with you?" Leapton asked, approaching him. Just then, the screen of Gu Suizhou's smart device lit up.
"Hey, a new message. Someone's trying to reach you."
"Just leave it there. Ignore it," Gu Suizhou said, not even glancing away from the screen. "Have you been following the stock market lately?"
Leapton shook his head. "Not really. Didn't you buy a bunch of blue-chip stocks that were guaranteed to make money?"
"That's right…" Gu Suizhou's gaze remained fixed on the subtly fluctuating red line. "But I expected much higher returns. This tiny profit doesn't make sense."
Leapton, not understanding much about finance, could only nod dumbly. "So… what should we do?"
"Sell everything first," Gu Suizhou said grimly, finally tearing his eyes away from the screen. He scribbled a string of characters on a scrap of paper and handed it to Leapton. "Look up this ID for me. If you can get their personal details, even better."
He had spent the entire afternoon trying to understand how this newcomer, who had suddenly appeared in the market a few days prior, had managed to disrupt his predictions. After analyzing the user's recent trading history, Gu Suizhou realized that this individual's market prediction skills rivaled his own.
Gu Suizhou was intensely curious about this mysterious figure.
If I could recruit them to work for me…
He narrowed his eyes, his finger circling the mouse.
Soon, the screen filled with pages of stock sales.
Once the market closed, he lazily leaned back in his chair. "You said someone sent me a message earlier?"
