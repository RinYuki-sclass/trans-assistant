"Indeed, quite a coincidence!" Lin Sui panted as he rushed over, his starry eyes shining brightly as he gazed at Shen Feizhi. "I never imagined I'd run into you here."
When he'd received that mysterious communication, he hadn't believed it at first.
But then he considered: whoever it was had somehow obtained his contact information and knew Shen Feizhi's name, going out of their way to inform him. They must possess some unusual means.
Lin Sui hadn't had time to ponder the communicator's motives. All he knew was that if he didn't see Shen Feizhi again, his mental state would completely collapse.
After all, several people who bore a striking resemblance to Shen Feizhi lay in that basement, their fates uncertain.
As Shen Feizhi met his gaze, Gu Suizhou's words flashed through his mind.
He recoiled a step, his voice dripping with disdain. "What do you want?"
Lin Sui rubbed his icy hands together. "Just passing by. Saw you here and thought I'd say hello."
"We're hardly on such familiar terms," Shen Feizhi snapped, his words as brutal as possible when he wanted to make his point. "You can leave now."
That brat Gu Suizhou must be racing back to school after seeing my message, Shen Feizhi thought. It'll be bad news if they cross paths.
Lin Sui pretended not to hear, gazing curiously at the school gate. "What brings you to school so late, Second Young Master?"
Shen Feizhi tapped his arm. "Immersing myself in the aura of knowledge."
It seemed Lin Sui was unaware Gu Suizhou attended this school.
What could have driven these brothers to such a bitter rift?
Lowering his lashes, Shen Feizhi suddenly leaned in close, bending at the waist until their gazes met. "Speaking of which, I visited the Underground City today and saw someone who looked remarkably like Doctor Lin."

The moment the words left his lips, Lin Sui's pupils visibly contracted to pinpoints. He forced a calm tone. "Second Young Master, you must have mistaken someone else for me."
"A case of mistaken identity?" Shen Feizhi didn't press the issue, raising the topic high only to let it drop gently. "Perhaps."
