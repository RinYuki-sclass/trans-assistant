He glanced at the time. "Does Doctor Lin have other matters to attend to?"
Ever since Shen Feizhi mentioned the "Underground City," Lin Sui had been completely out of sorts.
Hearing this, he didn't insist on staying any longer. Forcing a smile, he said, "Since Second Young Master has other matters, I won't disturb you any further."
Watching him turn to leave in the direction he had come, Shen Feizhi suddenly realized something. A cold, silent smile tugged at the corner of his lips.
Ah, so that's how Lin Sui suddenly appeared here.
He called out abruptly, "Wait!"
Taking two quick steps forward, he opened his smartphone to the add friend interface. "Shall we exchange contact information?"
Lin Sui stared at him in astonishment.
He had been fully prepared to distance himself from this man, yet suddenly… What was going on?
"I'll be returning to the Shen family eventually, and I figure interactions with you will be unavoidable. Might as well get acquainted now." Shen Feizhi paused for two seconds, noting Lin Sui's hesitation, before adding nonchalantly, "If you'd rather not, that's fine."
With that, he dimmed the screen and prepared to put the device away.
Lin Sui finally snapped out of his daze, frantically pulling out his own smartphone. "Yes! I want to!"
Lin Sui seemed to enjoy sharing his life on social media.
Read at Novelib.com
Today it was the Celi grasslands; tomorrow, the sunset on Havini Star… the quintessential upper-class lifestyle.
Shen Feizhi glanced through the feed, found nothing useful, and switched to another interface, losing interest.
He opened a chat window with someone else: [ I've been waiting for you for almost an hour. What are you doing? ]
To his surprise, the other person replied almost immediately.
Brother Shen, look up.
The response sounded surprisingly sincere.
