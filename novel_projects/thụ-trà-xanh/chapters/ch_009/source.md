Leapton found Gu Suizhou at his workstation, his brow furrowed in concentration as he stared intently at the illuminated screen.

"What's wrong with you?" Leapton asked, approaching him. Just then, the screen of Gu Suizhou's smart device lit up.

"Hey, a new message. Someone's trying to reach you."

"Just leave it there. Ignore it," Gu Suizhou said, not even glancing away from the screen. "Have you been following the stock market lately?"

Leapton shook his head. "Not really. Didn't you buy a bunch of blue-chip stocks that were guaranteed to make money?"

"That's right…" Gu Suizhou's gaze remained fixed on the subtly fluctuating red line. "But I expected much higher returns. This tiny profit doesn't make sense."

Leapton, not understanding much about finance, could only nod dumbly. "So… what should we do?"

"Sell everything first," Gu Suizhou said grimly, finally tearing his eyes away from the screen. He scribbled a string of characters on a scrap of paper and handed it to Leapton. "Look up this ID for me. If you can get their personal details, even better."

He had spent the entire afternoon trying to understand how this newcomer, who had suddenly appeared in the market a few days prior, had managed to disrupt his predictions. After analyzing the user's recent trading history, Gu Suizhou realized that this individual's market prediction skills rivaled his own.

Gu Suizhou was intensely curious about this mysterious figure.

If I could recruit them to work for me…

He narrowed his eyes, his finger circling the mouse.

Soon, the screen filled with pages of stock sales.

Once the market closed, he lazily leaned back in his chair. "You said someone sent me a message earlier?"

Speaking of coffee…

That man's bitter coffee-scented pheromone was so strong, it had almost made him break character and drop his act of tender affection.

A bottle of juice quickly emptied in his hand.

After a long day of work, Master Gu finally remembered his smartphone, which had been neglected for half the day. He picked it up with the speed of a sloth…

…and then shot to his feet like a rocket.

Leapton jumped in fright. "What is it?"

Gu Suizhou stared at the two chilling lines of text with a face as pale as death: "Shen… Shen Feizhi is coming to bring me clothes?!"

Over an hour had passed since Shen Feizhi sent the message. Damn it! Even if I rode on flaming wheels, I'd never make it back in time!

Gu Suizhou paced frantically around the room. "Leapton! Think of something, quick!"

Leapton trailed after him, turning in circles. "Wait, Big Brother, you haven't even told me what's going on! How am I supposed to come up with a plan? Ah, shit!"

Gu Suizhou gripped his shoulder fiercely. "Hurry! Get Lin Sui on the line!"

Shen Feizhi rented a hoverbike and sped through the wind to the entrance of the High School.

Knowing the Little Brat wouldn't make it back in time, he rode at a leisurely pace, taking two hours to arrive.

He pulled out his smartphone, snapped a photo of the school gate, and sent it to the Little Brat, adding a loving message: Here. Come out.

After the message sent successfully, Shen Feizhi took out a cigarette he had bought on the way, ready for a smoke to pass the time.

Just as he was about to spark the flame—

"Second Young Master Shen!"

A joyful, surprised call came from behind him.

Shen Feizhi frowned imperceptibly at the sound. He twirled the cigarette between his index and ring fingers before tucking it back into the pack.

He crossed his arms and turned slightly. "Doctor Lin, what a coincidence."

"Indeed, quite a coincidence!" Lin Sui panted as he rushed over, his starry eyes shining brightly as he gazed at Shen Feizhi. "I never imagined I'd run into you here."

When he'd received that mysterious communication, he hadn't believed it at first.

But then he considered: whoever it was had somehow obtained his contact information and knew Shen Feizhi's name, going out of their way to inform him. They must possess some unusual means.

Lin Sui hadn't had time to ponder the communicator's motives. All he knew was that if he didn't see Shen Feizhi again, his mental state would completely collapse.

After all, several people who bore a striking resemblance to Shen Feizhi lay in that basement, their fates uncertain.

As Shen Feizhi met his gaze, Gu Suizhou's words flashed through his mind.

He recoiled a step, his voice dripping with disdain. "What do you want?"

Lin Sui rubbed his icy hands together. "Just passing by. Saw you here and thought I'd say hello."

"We're hardly on such familiar terms," Shen Feizhi snapped, his words as brutal as possible when he wanted to make his point. "You can leave now."

That brat Gu Suizhou must be racing back to school after seeing my message, Shen Feizhi thought. It'll be bad news if they cross paths.

Lin Sui pretended not to hear, gazing curiously at the school gate. "What brings you to school so late, Second Young Master?"

Shen Feizhi tapped his arm. "Immersing myself in the aura of knowledge."

It seemed Lin Sui was unaware Gu Suizhou attended this school.

What could have driven these brothers to such a bitter rift?

Lowering his lashes, Shen Feizhi suddenly leaned in close, bending at the waist until their gazes met. "Speaking of which, I visited the Underground City today and saw someone who looked remarkably like Doctor Lin."

The moment the words left his lips, Lin Sui's pupils visibly contracted to pinpoints. He forced a calm tone. "Second Young Master, you must have mistaken someone else for me."

"A case of mistaken identity?" Shen Feizhi didn't press the issue, raising the topic high only to let it drop gently. "Perhaps."

He glanced at the time. "Does Doctor Lin have other matters to attend to?"

Ever since Shen Feizhi mentioned the "Underground City," Lin Sui had been completely out of sorts.

Hearing this, he didn't insist on staying any longer. Forcing a smile, he said, "Since Second Young Master has other matters, I won't disturb you any further."

Watching him turn to leave in the direction he had come, Shen Feizhi suddenly realized something. A cold, silent smile tugged at the corner of his lips.

Ah, so that's how Lin Sui suddenly appeared here.

He called out abruptly, "Wait!"

Taking two quick steps forward, he opened his smartphone to the add friend interface. "Shall we exchange contact information?"

Lin Sui stared at him in astonishment.

He had been fully prepared to distance himself from this man, yet suddenly… What was going on?

"I'll be returning to the Shen family eventually, and I figure interactions with you will be unavoidable. Might as well get acquainted now." Shen Feizhi paused for two seconds, noting Lin Sui's hesitation, before adding nonchalantly, "If you'd rather not, that's fine."

With that, he dimmed the screen and prepared to put the device away.

Lin Sui finally snapped out of his daze, frantically pulling out his own smartphone. "Yes! I want to!"

"Then remember this pain," Shen Feizhi said pointedly, taking a step back. "Next time you pull a stunt like this, it won't just be a kick. Now get up yourself."

Shen Feizhi was dressed lightly, with only a single-rider hoverbike behind him… There was no sign of the clothes he had supposedly brought, as mentioned in Gu Suizhou's information!

"Shen Feizhi, are you fucking playing me?" Gu Suizhou's face darkened as if he was owed eight million yuan. "You… you knew all along, didn't you?"

"Do I know you skipped class today? Or do I know you went to the Underground City instead?"
