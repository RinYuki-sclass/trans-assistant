A weak voice called from inside, "I said I don't want visitors. I feel awful. Go away if you have nothing important to say."
"It's me," Shen Feizhi said good-naturedly.
A sound of something dropping to the floor came from inside.
Immediately afterward, the door swung open.
Shen Feizhi quickly entered and shut the door behind him, frowning at the overwhelming honey scent that filled the room. He pulled out a prepared mask from his pocket and put it on. "Quite the performance you put on."
"You bet," Lu Qian replied, crossing her legs on the bed, showing no sign of weakness as she looked at him smugly. "If it weren't convincing, how could I have tricked those old fogies into calling you back? You really are something, disappearing like that for a whole day with no way to reach you."
Remembering the day and night he'd spent unconscious, Shen Feizhi's mood sank like a stone into a deep sea trench. "What's so urgent that you needed to call me back?" he asked, his voice low.
"Come here and look at this." Lu Qian beckoned him closer, carefully suppressing her pheromones. "Check out this designer's profile."
Shen Feizhi walked over and took the electronic tablet from her.
The screen showed an elderly man, alongside a comparison photo of him in his youth. "Lu Fan, this year… 50 years old?"
He was somewhat surprised.
The photo looked like it was at least a century and a half old.
Since the New Era began, the average human lifespan had stabilized at over 180 years. People in their fifties or sixties often looked barely different from those in their twenties or thirties.
Of course, some aged prematurely, but to see someone this old… it was truly rare.
