Though the Lu family was wealthy, she only received a small allowance. To her, a villa on Lanxie Road was a pipe dream.
Shen Feizhi gave her a "you're dreaming" look. "Don't get your hopes up. You're just coming to help me choose it. We need to keep up appearances."
Lu Qian awkwardly scratched her nose. "Oh, I see. Who knew you were so loaded?"
"I'm not rich," Shen Feizhi said flatly.
Lu Qian: "?"
Shen Feizhi: "But Elder Master Shen is."
Elder Shen wanted him to take over the family business and help Shen Wei, didn't he? He had shown his sincerity. The old man couldn't refuse to reciprocate.
"I'll secure the funds in three days. In the meantime, start looking at properties on Lanxie Road and try contacting Lu Fan."
Three days—the exact duration of Shen Feizhi's final days of vacation at W.N.
During those three days, Shen Feizhi had stayed in the room with Lu Qian under the pretense of helping her regulate her pheromones.
Elder Shen had struck a deal with him, depositing a billion yuan into his account. Shen Feizhi promptly invested it in the stock market, nearly doubling the amount—enough to purchase a duplex villa.

"Got it. I'm also following up on the house," Shen Feizhi nodded. "I'll be off now."
"Alright." Lu Qian suddenly looped her arm through his and playfully winked. "See you later, darling~"
Shen Feizhi instantly broke out in goosebumps.
But seeing Madam Shen's displeased expression and Madam Lu's worried gaze, he forced himself to endure it. "Yeah, see you later."
Lu Qian looked at him expectantly.
Yet Shen Feizhi couldn't bring himself to say "darling," turning to leave with a complete lack of romantic flair.
