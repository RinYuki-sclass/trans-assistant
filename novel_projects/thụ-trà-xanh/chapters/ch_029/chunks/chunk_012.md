He never imagined Shen Feizhi would be this angry.
That very night, he'd spent a fortune on a black market shuttle ticket to rush home, only to find the place empty, not even a suitcase in sight.
No surprise there. He never packed much to begin with. He probably just ditched everything on the way back.
He'd haunted the place for days, numbly tending to the Herb Garden each morning. Only this morning did he remember W.N and call Ritter, who confirmed Shen Feizhi would be returning to work tomorrow.
His work ID and other essentials are here. He'll definitely come home before going in.
That's when Gu Suizhou decided to groom himself meticulously, determined to greet Brother Shen with the image of a radiant, polished beauty.
Warm water cascaded over his head, and a wave of sorrow washed over him. He couldn't help but hum a couple of lines from a tragic love song, hoping to soothe his troubled emotions.
The more he sang, the more heartbroken he became. By the climax of the song, his emotions reached a fever pitch, his tear-choked voice vibrating like an electric tremor.
"It's only because I loved you so deeply that I resent you— ah! "
Suddenly, ice shards cascaded from the ceiling like a waterfall, crashing down on him.
Gu Suizhou's entire being, heart and soul, froze to the core.
"F-f-f-f-fuck!!" His teeth chattered uncontrollably as fury surged to his head. Wrapping himself in a towel, he stormed out of the shower, snarling, "Which fucking bastard…"
His voice died in his throat.
Shen Feizhi leaned lazily against the main control console, cradling a mug of hot cocoa, without even glancing up.
"Sing on. Keep going."
