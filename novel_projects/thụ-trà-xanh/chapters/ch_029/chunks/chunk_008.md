"He doesn't look his age, does he?" Lu Qian pointed to the adjacent photo. "Can you believe it? This picture was taken just ten years ago."
In just a decade, he had aged as if a century had passed.
Shen Feizhi studied the man's resume and architectural works for a moment, then murmured, "I don't see any projects here that would warrant such extreme dedication."
If it wasn't work pressure or financial hardship, then it could only be self-inflicted mental torment.
Lu Qian snapped her fingers. "Brilliant!"
Lowering her voice, she continued, "Feizhi, the Shen family estate was originally a large courtyard complex. Twenty-five years ago, to keep up with the times, Elder Shen had it demolished and rebuilt, hiring this man as the architect."
"The mansion is magnificent, and its design made the architect famous. Given its precision engineering, the Old Mansion should have remained structurally sound for centuries. But just ten years ago, Elder Shen suddenly invited the designer back, claiming it was for a 'routine inspection.'"
Ten years? Again, that time frame keeps cropping up.
Shen Feizhi nodded knowingly. "I understand."
Lu Qian asked, "I'm not sure if this information is related to the Shen family's dealings with W.N ten years ago, but I think we should invite the designer over to probe him. What reason should we give?"
Shen Feizhi lowered his gaze. "Let's say it's for the renovation of our 'honeymoon suite.' What do you think?"
Lu Qian froze, then blurted out, "Fuck."
A vein throbbed at Shen Feizhi's temple. He disliked profanity, but since it was Lu Qian, he held his tongue. If it had been Gu Suizhou, he would have slapped him across the face without hesitation.
That devilish, grinning face flashed unbidden into his mind again. With a touch of helplessness, he pinched the bridge of his nose.
"Judging by his resume, Lu Fan has rarely taken on projects in recent years. As a renowned spatial structure designer, asking him to design a small apartment of a few hundred square meters would be like using a sledgehammer to crack a nut. He'd likely send an apprentice to do a perfunctory job."

Lu Qian's limited intellect showed through. "Then what do we do? Offer him a fortune?"
"A fortune, definitely a fortune," Shen Feizhi replied. "Also, our 'honeymoon suite' can't be a simple apartment. Hiring a designer like Lu Fan for such a small space would be too conspicuous. I'll buy a villa on Lanxie Road soon. Find some time for us to go sign the property deed together."
"Holy shit!" Lu Qian jumped up. "Sign the deed with me? You're really giving it to me?!"
