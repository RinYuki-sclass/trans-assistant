"You mean Mr. Gu and Young Mr. Leapton?" Xu Zhong replied. "They're in the room downstairs. To avoid disturbing you, they had a new room prepared specifically for you."
"For me?" Shen Feizhi sneered, mocking the words.
He loathed this kind of presumptuousness, especially the feeling of being kept in the dark. It was utterly infuriating.
No matter Gu Suizhou's motives for targeting him, Shen Feizhi would never forgive him.
"It seems your Young Master is quite capable, handling everything perfectly without anyone's intervention." Shen Feizhi rose from the bed, his expression darkening further as he noticed the neatly bandaged wounds. "Did you do this?"
"No." Xu Zhong pushed up his sunglasses and pointed to the silent mechanical arm nearby. "After the program was set, the arm handled your wound. It's mostly healed now."
Shen Feizhi's expression finally softened.
"Fine. The wound's patched up, so we're even," he said flatly. "Tell Gu Suizhou I'm done with this. I won't meddle in his affairs anymore. He shouldn't bother me again."
He was interested in W.N, but not to the point of needing Gu Suizhou specifically.

Seeing Shen Feizhi actually get up to change, Xu Zhong hastily tried to smooth things over for Gu Suizhou. "Mr. Shen! Mr. Shen! The Young Master said he's coming to see you soon! Please wait until he's explained everything before you leave?"
As if on cue, a knock sounded at the door the moment Xu Zhong finished speaking.
Shen Feizhi glanced at Xu Zhong, who was already scrambling to escape, and lowered his eyelids.
In the next instant, he seized Xu Zhong's arm with one hand and executed a swift shoulder throw.
Xu Zhong's eyes widened in shock.
He was no slouch in combat, yet against this young man, still under the effects of the herbal medicine, he was completely outmatched!
"It's best not to resist. You can't beat me," Shen Feizhi said calmly as he bound Xu Zhong's hands with the remaining bandages. "Bear with it. I don't want to be too rough with you."
Xu Zhong was frantic. He wasn't completely without a chance to fight back, but if he hurt this man…
The Young Master would surely settle the score with him!
