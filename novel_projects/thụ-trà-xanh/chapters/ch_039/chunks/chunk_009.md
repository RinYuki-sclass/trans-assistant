With that explanation, the people outside stopped insisting. Their main concern was preventing an incident that would tarnish the establishment's reputation. Since the person involved didn't seem to mind, his life or death was no longer their business.
"That's better." Shen Feizhi stood up, patting away nonexistent dust from his clothes. He lifted Lu Qian from the chair. "Doctor Lin, people always have to pay for their actions. I'm taking Lu Qian. We'll see each other in a week."
Cold sweat drenched Lin Sui's forehead. His hand was pinned to the floor by the syringe, immobilizing him. "Shen Feizhi, do you really think killing me will solve everything?"
Shen Feizhi didn't break his stride. "At the very least, it'll let Gu Suizhou vent some of his anger."
Lin Sui froze.
He never expected such an answer.
Gu Suizhou? It's always Gu Suizhou.
"What the hell is Gu Suizhou?!" he roared.
Shen Feizhi ignored his screams, kicked the door open, and strode out with swagger. Many eyes turned to him, but he had no time for them. The longer he lingered, the more dangerous it would become.

So she's not an enemy.

Shen Feizhi started. "Is he alright?"
"I don't know. The notification came from Xu Zhong." The woman shrugged and proactively showed him the screen of her smart device. "We'll handle things here. Rest assured, we've been monitoring Lin Sui's recent activities. The most important thing now is for you to get back safely."
