Lin Sui was stunned by the sudden turn of events.
He stared at Shen Feizhi in disbelief. "You… How are you here?"
"Where else should I be? The home of your adoptive parents that you burned down?" Shen Feizhi scoffed. "You really didn't hold back, did you?"
Lin Sui's face went deathly pale, and he couldn't utter a word.
"Open the door!"
The tightly shut door suddenly slammed from the outside.
"Guest inside, if you don't come out now, don't blame us for using force!"
Lin Sui's eyes lit up as if he'd found an escape route, and he bolted toward the door.
Shen Feizhi grabbed him by the hair and threw him back. "Do you think I'm blind? You have ten seconds. Get rid of them."
Lin Sui shrieked, "Shen Feizhi! What the hell did Gu Suizhou promise you? What do you gain by going to such lengths to help him against your own family?"
"You don't need to know." Shen Feizhi advanced slowly, picked up a syringe, and plunged it viciously into the back of Lin Sui's hand. "If that door gets hit again, don't blame me for what happens next."
The pain was so intense that Lin Sui's consciousness began to fray.
He had clung to a sliver of hope, but now…
This man might actually kill him.
A madman, Lin Sui thought. Just like Gu Suizhou, a complete madman!

Lin Sui hesitated for a second.
Shen Feizhi's lips curved into a humorless smile. "Do you think I can handle them?"
Glancing at the mercenary lying on the floor, his fate unknown, Lin Sui said in despair, "It's nothing! He's… a friend of my younger brother."
