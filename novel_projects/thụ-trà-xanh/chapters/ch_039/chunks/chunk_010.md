If Gu Suizhou had been monitoring this place all along, Lin Sui's crimes would have been exposed sooner or later. Shen Feizhi hadn't needed to take this risk.
If he hadn't rescued Lu Qian today, her glands might have been permanently damaged.
Shen Feizhi's heart sank slightly.
Those people Lin Sui hurt before… Gu Suizhou must have known about them, right?
It was as if Gu Suizhou had tacitly allowed Lin Sui's actions, waiting for the perfect moment to unleash all his crimes at once.

There were better ways to handle this. They could have investigated thoroughly without sacrificing lives. Why choose the most brutal method?
Shen Feizhi didn't pity those people; he'd never been one for empathy.
But this wasn't the optimal solution.
The longer things dragged on, the more it resembled an unpredictable bomb strapped to everyone involved, with a high chance of backfiring in the end.
If it were him, he would have sought a swift, decisive resolution, not dragged the battle out for a decade like Gu Suizhou.
Shen Feizhi had no answers, only silence as he left. He and Leapton boarded a car bound for the hospital.
Xu Zhong was already waiting for them at the door of the hospital room.
"Mr. Shen, the Young Master is still unconscious."
"Mm," Shen Feizhi nodded. "Lu Qian has been taken for treatment. You handle the analysis of the drugs in her glands."
"Understood."
Inside the room, Gu Suizhou was half-wrapped in bandages. One leg and one arm were in casts. His eyes were tightly shut, his breathing so faint it was nearly inaudible, the only sound the steady beep… beep… of the ECG monitor.
Shen Feizhi casually pulled over a chair, sat down, crossed his legs, and watched him in silence for a moment. "Still pretending?"
The man on the bed's eyelashes fluttered. He slowly opened his eyes and said with a sigh of resignation, "I thought Brother Shen would at least check on my injuries when you came in."
"I already discussed your condition with the doctor outside," Shen Feizhi said. "Aren't you going to explain?"
