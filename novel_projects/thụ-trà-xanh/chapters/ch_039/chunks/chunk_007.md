Shen Feizhi didn't rush out immediately.
The Lu family doted on their youngest daughter; Lin Sui wouldn't dare kill her. He was curious to see what the man was planning.

Soon, the data on the monitors began to fluctuate wildly.
Both Lin Sui and Shen Feizhi watched the numbers intently, committing every value to memory.
Sets of data flashed across the monitors. After an indeterminate amount of time, Lin Sui finally stopped his observation, disconnected the instruments, and flipped Lu Qian onto her stomach.
A sharp scalpel instantly pressed against the nape of her neck, aimed directly at her fragile scent gland.
Shen Feizhi sighed, pushed open his own door, and stepped out.
The mechanical voice immediately spoke up. "Mr. Shen, you—"
"I'm joining them," Shen Feizhi replied casually.
The mechanical voice paused. "…Enjoy your time."
Without knocking, Shen Feizhi simply raised his foot and kicked open the tightly shut suite door.
An alarm instantly blared through the noisy club.
A fist, propelled by a gust of wind, shot through the door's opening, aimed straight for his face.
Shen Feizhi's brow furrowed. With a single hand, he snapped the arm.
"Ugh…" The mercenary grunted, his eyes wide with disbelief. He couldn't fathom how someone could so easily dismantle his attack.
"You're strong," Shen Feizhi stated nonchalantly. "But you're no match for me."
Before the mercenary could react, Shen Feizhi kicked him back into the room, swiftly shut the door before security could arrive, and knocked the man unconscious with a precise chop to the neck.
