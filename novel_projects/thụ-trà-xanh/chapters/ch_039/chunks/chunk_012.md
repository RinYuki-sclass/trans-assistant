Shen Feizhi considered it. Deciding such a trivial matter wasn't worth arguing over, he didn't insist on moving.
He said, "Lu Qian is in a severe coma, so next week's engagement ceremony is definitely off. We plan to release W.N.'s research findings tomorrow and anonymously submit Lu Qian's fraudulent accounts to the court to initiate a full search and investigation of the Shen family. While this won't immediately go public online, it's the best solution we have right now."
Gu Suizhou nodded emphatically as he listened. "As expected of Brother Shen, you've arranged everything."
"Which means," Shen Feizhi continued, "you have only one day left to confess everything to me voluntarily. Or would you prefer I investigate everything you've done over the years myself?"
Gu Suizhou fell silent.
He sighed. "Big Brother, you're truly ruthless."
"All I can say is, before today, I genuinely didn't know Lu Qian was also involved back then. Besides the Black Market Bar incident, I haven't hidden anything else from you."
Shen Feizhi was about to press further when Gu Suizhou suddenly gasped sharply, sucking in two ragged breaths.
A flush of red crept into the corners of his eyes, his lashes glistening with moisture as if he were about to cry.
Shen Feizhi glanced down and saw that the wound on Gu Suizhou's neck—the one he had pressed earlier—was inexplicably bleeding much more heavily.
The man in question raised a hand hooked up to an IV and monitors to rub his eyes, forcing himself to continue his explanation and apology with a face full of sincerity and remorse.
Shen Feizhi watched for a moment, gaining a whole new appreciation for his acting skills.
He's improved drastically, Shen Feizhi thought. Almost enough to fool me.
He raised an eyebrow with amusement, the earlier irritation from being deceived dissipating considerably. Leaning down slightly, he cupped Gu Suizhou's jaw with one hand, holding his head still.
Gu Suizhou blinked. "Bro?"
"Mm," Shen Feizhi hummed, his face dangerously close. With his free hand, he carefully unwrapped the bandage on Gu Suizhou's neck, wiped away the blood, readjusted the needle, and then slowly re-bandaged it.
Gu Suizhou hadn't expected him to handle it personally and remained completely rigid throughout the process.
After finishing, Shen Feizhi didn't immediately straighten up. Instead, he maintained his dominant posture, looking down at Gu Suizhou with one arm braced beside his shoulder, lazily propping up his head.
The hand that had held his jaw slowly slid upward, tracing across his face until the pad of his thumb pressed against Gu Suizhou's eye.
Gu Suizhou instinctively squeezed that eye shut.
