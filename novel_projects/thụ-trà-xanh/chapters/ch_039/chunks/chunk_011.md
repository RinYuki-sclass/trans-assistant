Shen Feizhi suddenly moved, sitting on the edge of the bed beside him. His long, elegant fingers gently stroked Gu Suizhou's bandaged neck, where an IV needle was taped down.
Gu Suizhou narrowed his eyes, but before he could savor the touch, his eyes shot wide open.
Shen Feizhi's hand tightened, pressing precisely on Gu Suizhou's wound.
The patient's agitation sent the monitors into a frantic, continuous alarm.
The chaotic footsteps of medical staff immediately echoed from the hallway outside.
"Mr. Gu! Mr. Gu, are you alright?"
Someone tried the doorknob, then cried out in alarm.
"Who's in there?"
"Why is it locked? Open the door! Doctor!"
Shen Feizhi's gaze remained lowered, his hand unmoving. "They're calling for you."
Gu Suizhou was drenched in a cold sweat from the pain, yet he managed a smile, his voice laced with amusement as he called out, "It's nothing! I just bumped the machine! You can go back. I'm just talking with my brother. Don't worry, my brother dotes on me!"
His tone must have been convincing enough, as the staff didn't seem overly suspicious.
After instructing Shen Feizhi to unlock the door, they muttered amongst themselves and left.
Shen Feizhi loosened his grip. The pad of his thumb was stained with blood that had seeped from Gu Suizhou's wound.
"I dote on you?"
Gu Suizhou blinked nonchalantly. "Brother Shen, don't be angry. Let's talk properly."
"Fine," Shen Feizhi nodded, starting to rise to return to his chair.
But Gu Suizhou caught his sleeve, murmuring softly, "Let's just talk here, okay?"
