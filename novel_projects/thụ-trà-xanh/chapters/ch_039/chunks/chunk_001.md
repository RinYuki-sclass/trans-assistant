Chapter 39
Shen Feizhi had only suffered a scrape on his arm; otherwise, he was unharmed.
He used a passing tour boat to clamber ashore. Suddenly, a deafening explosion ripped through the air from the overpass above, startling passersby who all whipped their heads around to look.
Shen Feizhi's breath hitched violently.
Realizing the kid's intention, he swore viciously, "Little lunatic."
Who the hell risks their own life so recklessly?
Shen Feizhi's mind reeled. He couldn't fathom why the kid would go to such lengths.
But this was no time to dwell on it. He had no time to think, no time to go back and check if the kid was dead or alive. He could only grit his teeth and contact Leapton, ordering a grid search to track Lu Qian's whereabouts.
"Found her, Bro!" Leapton wasn't actually younger than Shen Feizhi, but he'd gotten used to calling him "Bro"—he called Gu Suizhou "Bro," and Shen Feizhi "Bro" too. "I'm about a hundred or two hundred meters from Miss Lu Qian right now. Lin Sui's here too, but… there are too many people, and the area's too chaotic. We can't risk making a move."
"Wait for me," Shen Feizhi said, glancing at the coordinates. "Twenty minutes. Use any means necessary to ensure Lu Qian's safety."
She's the last witness. Nothing can happen to her.
Shen Feizhi couldn't afford to wait for a car. He tore off a strip from the hem of his shirt and wrapped it around the gash on his arm—a wound so deep it had nearly exposed bone. Then he hopped on a shared motorcycle and sped toward the coordinates.
The wail of police sirens from the overpass grew louder and clearer. As he passed the entrance ramp, he couldn't avoid seeing the thick smoke billowing from above. Several cars were engulfed in flames, their occupants' fates unknown.
Shen Feizhi glanced once, then forced his gaze away.
He realized he was still susceptible to his emotions overriding his reason.
