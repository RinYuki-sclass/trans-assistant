Suddenly, she spotted a bird with iridescent plumage—emerald green mixed with gold—perch on the windowsill.
"What a beautiful bird!" Lu Qian exclaimed, her voice filled with delight.
She reached out toward it through the glass.
The bird tilted its head, then abruptly pecked hard against the glass!
Lu Qian blinked, stunned.
She awkwardly withdrew her hand and sat back down.
"What's wrong?" Shen Feizhi asked casually.
"Nothing, just ran into a very aggressive bird."
Shen Feizhi glanced curiously outside. He saw the bird fluttering its wings, circling around the window again and again as if showing off its magnificent plumage, flying with obvious delight.
Lu Qian: "?"
This bird has two faces?!
Shen Feizhi, however, withdrew his gaze.
He had no interest in the stupid bird. Better to focus on ordering.
Having ordered a large pizza, Shen Feizhi, out of habitual courtesy, moved to sit beside Lu Qian, leaving space for the waiter to serve and make slicing easier.
