Gu Suizhou, acting like a dutiful little wife, asked obediently, "What is it?"
Shen Feizhi didn't answer. He took Gu Suizhou's wrist and led him a few steps, stopping in front of the building next to the café. "Look at the ground ahead. There's a dead green bird. Go pick it up."
Gu Suizhou: "……"
He forced himself to go over and pick it up, feigning surprise. "Brother Shen! This bird has no blood!"
"That's not all," Shen Feizhi said, strolling over to stand beside him. "Not only is there no blood, but its eyes are actually miniature cameras."
Gu Suizhou's heart trembled.
He had finally tracked down their location and rushed to the café entrance with his newly invented bird-shaped surveillance drone, only to witness the pair sharing an intimate meal.
Consumed by fury, Gu Suizhou lost his composure and bribed a staff member to repeatedly harass them.
Watching Gu Suizhou's slightly parted lips, Shen Feizhi chuckled softly. "Tell me, who do you think owns this surveillance drone?"
"I have no idea," Gu Suizhou replied with a smile.
Shen Feizhi neither confirmed nor denied his answer, switching tactics. "How did you find me?"
Gu Suizhou's eyes darted around. "Telepathy, fate. We're soulmates, a perfect match."
Before Shen Feizhi could even speak, an old man carrying a vegetable basket happened to pass by.
Seeing Gu Suizhou, he exclaimed in surprise, "Eh? Did you finally find that handsome, dashing, and unmarried fiancé of yours who was tricked into joining a pyramid scheme by that wicked woman?"
Gu Suizhou: "……"
Shen Feizhi's gaze pierced like two icy daggers. "What fiancé?"
The old man clicked his tongue, admiring Shen Feizhi's face. "Just now, a young woman walked a thousand miles on foot, all to find her innocent, naive fiancé who's clueless about the ways of the world!"
Shen Feizhi: "?"
The old man turned to Gu Suizhou. "And she even carried their keepsake—a beautiful, emerald-green little bird, if I remember correctly… Ah! Just like this dead bird here!"
