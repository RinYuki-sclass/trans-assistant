Shen Feizhi fell silent.
Yellow roses, yellow carnations, datura, daisies, rock roses, dandelions…
He recognized many of the flowers, but to his dismay, none carried auspicious meanings. Every single bloom symbolized unrequited or impossible love.
"Who sends flowers like this ?"
The waiter pressed on, "Go on, deliver them!"

Shen Feizhi sneered, "Get out."
The waiter swallowed nervously, his voice trembling. "Sir, is there something you're dissatisfied with…?"
Shen Feizhi shot him a glacial glare.
The waiter: "…Right, I'll just be going then."
The door slammed shut again. Just as Lu Qian was about to ask what was going on, Shen Feizhi abruptly stood and flung open the window.
The beautiful bird was still fluttering outside. Seeing the window open, it excitedly flew toward him.
Shen Feizhi reached out—
And grasped the bird's fated head.
Lu Qian: What are you doing…?
Before she could finish, Shen Feizhi bent his arm and violently flung the bird against the opposite building!
"Holy shit!" Lu Qian exclaimed, stunned. "Feizhi! Why would you slaughter an innocent creature like that?"
"Slaughter?" Shen Feizhi smiled enigmatically. "I can only slaughter something that's alive. Let's eat first. I'll take you home afterward."
Lu Qian didn't understand his words, but she trusted him enough not to press the matter. After finishing her meal, she walked out of the cafe with him, side by side.
The moment they stepped outside, they saw someone who shouldn't have been there standing beneath the crooked tree at the entrance.
