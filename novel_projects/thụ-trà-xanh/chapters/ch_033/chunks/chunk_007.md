Lu Fan shook his head violently. "No! No way! Impossible! Don't even think about it! I'll die! He'll kill me!"

Lu Qian's hand trembled as she sipped her mocha.
She knew Shen Feizhi was acting, but his performance was so convincing it sent a chill down her spine. Anyone who didn't know better would think he was truly capable of murder.
Lu Fan stared fixedly at Shen Feizhi, pleading desperately, "Please, I'm begging you! Please, let me go… please?"

Shen Feizhi shook his head. "I'm sorry, but no."
Lu Fan's reactions confirmed Shen Feizhi's suspicions: during the renovation of the Shen family's old mansion years ago, something sinister had been structurally concealed. Elder Shen, fearing exposure, had been using special methods to control people all these years.
Indeed, it was a regimen of constant medication.
History had even recorded cases of hair turning white overnight from grief. How much more rapid would the decline be under the dual torment of drugs and mental manipulation, capable of aging a young man in his prime to decrepitude in mere months?
Lu Fan resigned himself to his fate.
With the aura of death clinging to him, he sketched a crude diagram. However, he stubbornly refused to answer any further questions, even when threatened with death.
"I hope you keep your word," Lu Fan muttered after finishing the last stroke, his spirit completely broken. "I really don't want to die."
Shen Feizhi ignored him, studying the diagram. Suddenly, he chuckled, his elegant fingers tapping the area beneath the ground-floor kitchen. "Are you sure you've drawn everything?"
Lu Fan's pupils constricted, his neck stiff as he nodded. "Yes."
"It seems you don't value your life as much as you claim," Shen Feizhi remarked. "Are you certain the house won't collapse if nothing supports it here?"
Lu Fan never expected his opponent to be so knowledgeable.
Shen Feizhi's smile vanished completely. "Are you absolutely sure there's nothing else you can add?"
