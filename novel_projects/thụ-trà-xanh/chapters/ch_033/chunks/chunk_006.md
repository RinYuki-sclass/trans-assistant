Judging by Lu Fan's demeanor, any further questioning might make him storm out with the blueprints.
But without pressing him, they might never pry a single word of information from him once the house was finished.
Better to cut to the chase…
Shen Feizhi cut to the chase: "Designer Lu, you stopped working in this style because my grandfather traumatized you all those years ago, didn't he?"
Instead of beating around the bush, it would be faster to force him to reveal the truth directly.
Sometimes a sudden, unexpected strike yields greater results.
Lu Fan's head snapped up, his deep-set, clouded eyes glaring fiercely at Shen Feizhi.
Shen Feizhi met his gaze openly, clearly seeing the flicker of concealed fear and terror in the other man's eyes.
"I don't know what you're talking about," Lu Fan snapped, rising abruptly and rudely tossing down the blueprints. "Young Master Shen doesn't seem genuinely interested in buying my designs, so I won't waste my time."
Shen Feizhi remained calm and unhurried. Only after Lu Fan had staggered toward the door did he casually remark, "If I told you Elder Master Shen sent me to discuss what happened back then, would you still leave?"
This time, not only Lu Fan but even Lu Qian stared at him in shock.

She quickly recovered, however, and chimed in with feigned indignation, immediately changing her tone: "Teacher Lu, you certainly have a high opinion of yourself! Even Grandpa had to send us personally to invite you."
Lu Fan stood frozen, his complexion even worse than when he'd first entered.
This time, the fear in his eyes was completely undisguised. "Y-you… What else does the Old Master want from me? I told you I wouldn't reveal anything! I've been taking my medication every week on time! What more do you want from me?!"
Shen Feizhi lowered his gaze and chuckled softly. "Mr. Lu, I advise you to sit back down now. Otherwise, if I tell Grandpa what you've said to me today, your life won't be worth a straw."
Lu Fan instantly began to tremble violently.
He wasn't easily intimidated; otherwise, his secret would have been exposed long ago. For years, he had avoided meeting anyone outside his circle. This time, he'd only agreed because his employer was Shen Feizhi—another Shen family member. He had even consulted Elder Master Shen, who had given his approval for the job.
So when faced with Feizhi's calm, assured questioning, he never suspected his interrogator was utterly clueless.
How could someone who knows nothing speak with such unwavering confidence?
