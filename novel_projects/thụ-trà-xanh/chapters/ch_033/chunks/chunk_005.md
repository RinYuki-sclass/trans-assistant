Lu Qian rose eagerly and ushered him in, "Master, you've arrived! Please, have a seat!"
Shen Feizhi: "……"
What's with this summoning a quack vibe? Shen Feizhi wondered.
Lu Fan sank heavily into a chair. "Young Master Shen, Miss Lu. Do you have any specific requirements for the house's renovation style?"
He cut straight to the point, clearly uninterested in small talk.
Shen Feizhi and Lu Qian exchanged a glance before ordering a warm glass of milk for him.
"We're not experts," Shen Feizhi said. "Here's the structural plan. We'd like to hear your opinion."
Lu Fan grunted in acknowledgment and slowly took the blueprint to examine it.
As he studied the plans, he remained utterly silent, his breathing so faint it was barely audible. His eyes, hidden beneath drooping eyelids, revealed nothing of his thoughts. His entire demeanor radiated an aura of deathly stillness.
While Lu Fan was engrossed in the blueprints, Shen Feizhi casually remarked, "Speaking of styles… I actually quite liked the design you did for my grandfather's house."
The moment these words left his lips, Lu Fan's hands, still gripping the blueprints, began to tremble violently.
His breathing grew heavy, and he replied stiffly, "That… I can't do it anymore."
Shen Feizhi observed this without betraying his reaction. "Why not?" he asked evenly. "Is it the budget? Don't worry about that—tell us what it would take. If it's possible, money is no object."
Lu Fan's only response was to hunch his shoulders even further.
His tone remained rigid: "I won't do it."
