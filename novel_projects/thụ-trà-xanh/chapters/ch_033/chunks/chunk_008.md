Shen Feizhi: "Still a problem?"
"No! None!" Lu Fan scrambled to his feet, hunched over in terror, and fled through the door like a hunted animal.
Lu Qian watched Shen Feizhi casually playing the stock market, her curiosity piqued. "You're just letting him go like that?"
"We won't get anything more out of him now," Shen Feizhi replied. "Let's see him again in a while."
After all, they had to maintain the facade—their house still needed renovating.
Shen Feizhi turned to Lu Qian. "Can your people guarantee his safety?"
Lu Qian considered for a moment. "Protecting one man? No problem."
Shen Feizhi tapped his fingers on the table.
He understood Lu Qian's implication.
Lu Fan's obedience to Elder Shen likely stemmed from some leverage the elder held over him. If this "leverage" involved his family, the situation would become exceedingly difficult.
Though Shen Feizhi possessed considerable influence, his personal network was woefully thin. He couldn't cultivate truly trustworthy allies in such a short time.
He sighed. "Alright, let's end things here for today. I'll think of another way. This café has desserts. Want some?"
Lu Qian, already lightheaded with hunger, nodded eagerly. "Yes, yes, yes!"
Shen Feizhi signaled a waiter.
