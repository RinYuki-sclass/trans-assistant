Shen Feizhi frowned. "Gu Suizhou."
The man squatting under the tree had been idly playing with mud, but at the sound of his name, he snapped his head up with the speed of a large dog spotting a bone and sprinted toward them.
He tripped over his own feet in his haste.
Shen Feizhi initially wanted to shove him away in disgust, but remembering their established roles, he reluctantly allowed Gu Suizhou to presumptuously grab his arm.
Gu Suizhou took full advantage of this audacity, saying, "Didn't I rush here to pick you up because I was worried waiting at home?"
Lu Qian watched the two of them fawning over each other, her lips twitching violently.
I'm the rightful fiancée here! Where did this little fox spirit come from?!
Just as the thought crossed her mind, the "fox spirit" innocently blinked at her. "Am I interrupting?"
You bet you are, Lu Qian thought, but Shen Feizhi spoke first.
"No, we were just finishing up. You came to pick me up? How did you get here?"
Gu Suizhou blushed shyly. "I took a taxi."
Shen Feizhi stared in disbelief.
Taking a taxi to pick up someone who owns a car? What kind of idiot does that?
He turned to Lu Qian. "Want to walk together?"
Lu Qian instinctively glanced at Gu Suizhou.
Gu Suizhou's eyes were practically shooting sparks.
Lu Qian sighed inwardly.
Not one to lack social awareness, she spread her hands. "I'll just walk alone."
Shen Feizhi didn't press her. He called a car for her.
After watching the car pull away, he turned back and affectionately patted Gu Suizhou's head. "Come on, let's go pick something up."
