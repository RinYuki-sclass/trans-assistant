"Sir," the waiter smiled, once again squeezing between them. "Here's your cream of mushroom soup."
"…Thank you," Shen Feizhi replied.
The third time, Shen Feizhi reached for a napkin near Lu Qian.
Again, the waiter squeezed in. "Here's your ice cream."
"Wait a minute," Lu Qian said, puzzled. "There's clearly a designated space over there for you to serve. Why do you insist on squeezing in here?"
The waiter seemed genuinely surprised. "Oh, there's a space over there!"
The two of them stared in silence.
Lu Qian, speechless, finally asked, "I think we've received all our dishes, right?"
The waiter smiled stiffly. "Yes… I believe so."
"Alright, you can go now," Lu Qian said.

Shen Feizhi watched the waiter's retreating figure, his eyes narrowing. Then he deliberately reached across the table toward Lu Qian…
The door burst open again.
Both of them looked up to see a new waiter.
Shen Feizhi's hand froze mid-air. "I think we've already received all our dishes."
The waiter smiled, copying the previous one's forced cheerfulness as he wedged himself between the two to place a bouquet on the table. "As our 886th customers, we'd like to present you with this special bouquet."
He then dropped a garish, haphazardly arranged bouquet into Shen Feizhi's lap.
Shen Feizhi: "?"
The waiter grinned. "You can give it to the lady beside you."
Lu Qian: "?"
