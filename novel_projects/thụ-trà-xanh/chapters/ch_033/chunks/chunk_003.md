When Shen Feizhi first arrived in this world, he had been somewhat restrained. Now, having grown accustomed to it, he was exuding his full aura, naturally appearing quite different.
Moreover, compared to the "Shen Feizhi" the noblewoman remembered, he had likely changed even more dramatically.
Shen Feizhi offered a vague explanation: "I've been through a lot these past four months."
The noblewoman sighed with a hint of relief. "Ah, you've matured and grown up so much!"
But when she noticed Lu Qian standing beside him, her mood soured. "Xiao Shen, is this your—"
"Auntie, I'm Feizhi's fiancée." Although Lu Qian wasn't sure of their exact relationship, she maintained a polite smile and greeted the woman respectfully.
The moment those words left her lips, Shen Feizhi knew they were doomed.

Predictably, the noblewoman's already sour expression completely collapsed. "Hmph! Xiao Shen, I'm just saying, you can't forget your roots just because you have money now! That beautiful child from before was so much better!"
Beautiful child? Was she talking about Gu Suizhou? What did Gu Suizhou have to do with any of this?
Lu Qian looked utterly bewildered.
Shen Feizhi sighed inwardly. "…Big Sister's reprimand is well-deserved."
Lu Qian: "……"
The moment he called her "Big Sister," she understood: she had lost, completely and utterly.
That brat knew exactly how to win people over.
The wealthy woman glared at Lu Qian as she walked away.
Shen Feizhi and Lu Qian exchanged a silent glance for a moment.
Lu Qian gave him a thumbs-up, her voice genuinely impressed. "He's still the one with the deeper game."
Remembering how that kid had swindled them out of the gold pendant and jade ring, Shen Feizhi couldn't help the corners of his eyes crinkling slightly. "Mm, he's certainly capable."
Lu Qian's eyes widened. She had never seen Shen Feizhi smile so beautifully before!
