Lu Fan was notoriously difficult to persuade. It had taken Shen Feizhi's audacious gamble of directly appealing to Elder Shen, his grandfather, to finally coax him out of retirement.
His audacious methods had always proven surprisingly effective.
As the hour approached, Shen Feizhi glanced at his watch. "Hungry?"

Shen Feizhi raised his hand and ordered a small cake, croissants, and other pastries to be brought up. "Have something to tide you over."
Lu Qian was touched. "You're so thoughtful!"
Shen Feizhi calmly sipped his hot cocoa, accepting the compliment without a flicker of embarrassment.
