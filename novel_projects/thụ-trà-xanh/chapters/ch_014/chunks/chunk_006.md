Just as, from the moment he could remember, Shen Feizhi had known he was a Manager tasked with safeguarding the order of time and space.
He ceaselessly traversed various Small Worlds, familiarizing himself with their unique characteristics and interacting with individuals of countless identities.
Initially, Shen Feizhi had been deeply curious about his origins and purpose.
While other Managers also toiled tirelessly, they enjoyed holidays on the Main Star, dining with family, fishing with parents, or traveling with friends.
But he alone toiled relentlessly, one mission immediately following another. He seemed to have no parents, no friends, not even superiors who communicated with him beyond issuing mechanical orders.
Gradually, he learned to suppress his curiosity. By stifling his inquiries, he could numb the emptiness, sadness, and sense of loss that had plagued him.
Thus, when an accident prevented his return from this mission, he felt no despair, even a hint of relief at the prospect of rest.

The crucial nexus point of this world remained elusive, and his reasons for staying here were still unclear. With his fate hanging in the balance, contacting the Main Star for assistance remained the best course of action.
Lost in thought, Shen Feizhi momentarily forgot to finish buttoning his shirt.
The door burst open violently.
"Good evening, Brother Shen!" Gu Suizhou stood in the doorway, tossing him a flamboyant air kiss. "Five days without contact—did you miss me?"
Snapping back to reality, Shen Feizhi's lips curled downward. His hands flew to fasten his collar. "Take off your shoes."
"Oh," Gu Suizhou replied, withdrawing the foot he'd almost stepped inside. As he changed his shoes, he glanced at Shen Feizhi. "Huh? Since when did you start checking yourself out in the mirror? And the angle of that mirror… it seems a little…"
His words trailed off abruptly.
He saw Shen Feizhi straightening his collar, turning to look at him with a half-smile.
Gu Suizhou instantly understood.
He'd been exposed.
How sharp are this man's eyes?!
He'd spotted the subtle shift in the mirror's position!
