Since he was alone at home, he decided to change clothes right there in the living room.
But as he buttoned his crisp white shirt, his peripheral vision caught sight of the full-length mirror beside him.
That flashy Gu Suizhou had insisted on installing it—ostensibly for grooming before going out. Shen Feizhi had found the fuss pointless and let him have his way.
Only today did he realize the mirror's angle was no accident. From Gu Suizhou's favorite armchair, one could see Shen Feizhi's usual spot perfectly reflected.
Little Brat, Shen Feizhi thought, over six feet tall with an even taller rebellious streak.
He strode over, shirt unbuttoned, and scoffed. With a sharp kick, he shifted the mirror's angle.
From Shen Feizhi's usual seat, the adjusted mirror angle now offered a clear view of Gu Suizhou's bedroom.
Satisfied, he clenched his fist, raised it, and gently bumped knuckles with his reflection.
The man in the mirror's hair had grown slightly longer since their last meeting, his stray bangs now completely obscuring his eyebrows. Yet his features remained exquisitely refined, his complexion and the sculpted lines of his physique flawlessly perfect—not a single imperfection…
…if one ignored the pale scar etched across his chest.
Shen Feizhi's gaze settled quietly on the deep brown mark etched into his chest in the mirror.
This scar had been the first undeniable sign that this body was indeed his when he had transmigrated to this small world.
