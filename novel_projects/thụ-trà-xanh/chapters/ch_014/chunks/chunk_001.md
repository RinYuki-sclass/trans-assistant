Chapter 14
"Brother Zhou, what's wrong? Why the long face?"
Song Zhe and Leapton flanked Gu Suizhou like a pair of mascots, one on either side.
"If—and I mean if ," Gu Suizhou sighed heavily, his expression grim. "If a friend of mine accidentally offended someone and got blocked, what would he have to do to get their contact information back?"
Song Zhe gasped in alarm. "Brother Zhou! Did you get blocked by someone?!"
"Why don't you shout a little louder?" Gu Suizhou shot him a sharp glare. "I already said it's about a friend, not me!"
To his left, Song Zhe silently clamped a hand over his mouth.
To his right, Leapton was already laughing hysterically, almost choking. "Holy shit… this is killing me! Gu Suizhou, did you get blocked by Mr. Shen?!"
Gu Suizhou took a deep breath.
He desperately wanted to deny it.
But alas, the truth was… he had , goddamn it, been deleted by Shen Feizhi and hadn't been added back yet.
Today was already Friday. Why hadn't that man contacted him yet?! How could he be so patient?
During Jiang Che's class last time, Gu Suizhou had used the account Shen Feizhi had given him to discover that the Shen family was hosting a gala this Sunday, and he'd even seen a list of many of their business partners.
They'd agreed to sneak into the gala this Sunday to gather intel.
But since that day, Shen Feizhi hadn't sent him a single message. Even when Gu Suizhou pretended to be sick and had Jiang Che contact Shen Feizhi, the man refused to engage.
