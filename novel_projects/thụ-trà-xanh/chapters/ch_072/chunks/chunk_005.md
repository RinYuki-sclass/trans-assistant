From the corner of his eye, the rearview mirror reflected the figure of Gu Suizhou’s assistant jogging toward him, something clutched in his hand.
If he wasn't mistaken, it was the meal from the private kitchen he’d been craving half an hour ago.
The assistant, having rushed back with the food, let out a massive sigh of relief when he spotted Shen Feizhi’s car.
Thank goodness, he made it!
He tapped tentatively on the car window. "President Shen, President Gu asked me to give this to you."
The engine hummed twice, its roar gradually fading.
The assistant lifted the packed meal, preparing to slide it through the slowly descending gap of the window.
To his surprise, the window stopped after lowering only two finger-widths.
He blinked in confusion.
A frigid voice drifted from inside: "Move back."
The tone was cold enough to freeze boiling water. The assistant’s spine shuddered, and he instinctively took two steps back.
Before his heels had even firmly met the pavement, the car abruptly surged forward. With a whoosh , it was gone, leaving only a faint trail of dust.
The taillights flickered twice, and in the blink of an eye, the vehicle vanished completely.
