Before he could even get excited, a cool sensation brushed against his neck.
Looking down, he realized that the leather collar had actually been fastened around his own neck.
Shen Feizhi caressed his neck, unable to put it down. "But it wouldn't be quite fair if I was the only one using those things, would it?"
Having spent so much time with him, Gu Suizhou could more or less grasp the secondary meaning behind his words.
He narrowed his eyes and asked, "Fifty-fifty?"
"Works for me," Shen Feizhi accepted readily. "I pick one, and you can pick one."
Gu Suizhou stared him down in silence for two seconds. In the next instant, he grabbed him by the waist, hauled him up, and they tumbled onto the bed together.
"Husband," he forced the words out through gritted teeth, "you really know how to have a good time."
Shen Feizhi looked at the vibrant red ribbon in the other man's hand and returned the compliment: "Thank you. You’re not doing so bad yourself."
Gu Suizhou didn't actually like the rain, but this particular rainy season in March, he loved it to his very core.
Especially that one day during those few days when he had no choice but to take some time away for Federation business and return to the office.
Just as he finished changing and was preparing to head out, a slender, handsome hand reached out from under the duvet beside him and tugged at the corner of his clothes.
He looked down to check on him. "What's wrong? Hungry? Tell me what you want to eat and I'll go fix it."
The person inside didn’t answer; based on the movement beneath the covers, he was likely shaking his head.
Gu Suizhou mentally reviewed the traits of an Alpha’s estrus period.
…Does possessiveness really get so intense that it causes such a severe lack of security?
With a sense of understanding, he pulled back the quilt, revealing the flawless face of the man beneath.
The sudden rush of fresh air made Shen Feizhi knit his brows as he tried to shrink back into the covers.
Gu Suizhou preemptively stepped to the side to block out the harsh sunlight, stopping him from retreating. He leaned down slightly and pressed a lingering, tender morning kiss against the corner of his lips.
Shen Feizhi’s rare bout of morning grumpiness was finally quelled.
