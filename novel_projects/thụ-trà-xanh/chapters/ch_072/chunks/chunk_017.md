Kissing in that position wasn’t particularly comfortable; it was only after a strained breath of air that he managed to get out a single sentence: "Come back early."
Basking in the favor shown to him, Gu Suizhou teased him mischievously. "Why’s that, Brother Shen? Can't bear to let me go? Don't want me to leave? This won't do, husband. You were the one who taught me not to be a 'faint-hearted monarch who neglects the morning court' for his love."
Shen Feizhi opened his eyes in silence.
The icy lethargy with which his eyelids lifted already conveyed exactly what he wanted to say: Are you looking for a death wish?
Seeing that the Shen Feizhi who could deliver a fatal blow the moment he spoke was about to return, Gu Suizhou quickly gathered what little was left of his survival instinct and said, “Alright, alright, I get it. I’ll go and come back quickly. I’ll bring you lunch.”
Only then did Shen Feizhi close his eyes again, his voice raspy as he said, “You’d better be a man of your word.”
Gu Suizhou couldn’t help but laugh. “I know, Bro.”
He stood up reluctantly. After pouring some water, peeling an egg, and preparing some bread, he packed everything into an insulated box. Only after ensuring everything was in order did he start heading out, looking back every few steps.
By then, Shen Feizhi had shaken off his drowsiness and was preparing to get up to handle some work.
Hearing the sound of the door opening, he suddenly spoke: “Gu Suizhou.”
Gu Suizhou looked back.
Shen Feizhi beckoned him. “Come closer.”
Without a moment’s hesitation, Gu Suizhou walked over, kicked back the hem of his trousers, and half-knelt by the bedside.
Shen Feizhi grabbed him by the neck to pull him close, ruthlessly sinking his teeth into his gland to leave a bite mark.
Canines pierced the flesh, steadily pumping in pheromones.
Gu Suizhou cooperated, reining in his own restless pheromones and obediently accepting the invasion.
Shen Feizhi did not let him go until the scent of coffee completely overwhelmed the taste of matcha, his tongue sweeping over the skin of the other's neck to lick away a few stray drops of blood.
As the matcha-flavored blood spread across his tongue, a sense of satiation instantly seized his entire brain.
It felt wonderful.
"I hope that when I return, there will be no other Alpha or Omega pheromones—or any other scents—on you except for the smell of coffee. And…" Shen Feizhi looked into his eyes and spoke with unquestionable authority, "Remember to miss me."
