He really must be angry!
“Yes, yes, okay!” he agreed as he scrambled toward the door, so flustered he forgot he was holding a corded office phone. He was nearly yanked off his feet by the wire before he realized his mistake. “Wait for me for half an hour… no, twenty minutes, just twenty minutes! I’ll be home right away!”
With that, he shoved the receiver back onto the desk and bolted.
He didn't forget to toss a parting word to the bewildered President Zhang: “I’ll send you the address when I get back!”
It was currently peak clock-out time for the majority of W.N’s employees. Consequently, nearly half the company witnessed their usually aloof and elegant President Gu sprint out the main doors at the speed of a hundred-meter dash, his long legs swinging over his motorcycle without a second's pause.
His expression and posture were more frantic than if he were racing home to put out a fire.
