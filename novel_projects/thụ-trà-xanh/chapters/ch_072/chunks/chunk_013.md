Gu Suizhou muttered, "What's the point of washing so thoroughly when I'm just going to get dirty again later anyway?" but after receiving a fierce glare, he rubbed his nose and dejectedly scurried off to the bathroom.
Shen Feizhi threw on a bathrobe, slapped on a pheromone isolation patch, and went to open the door.
"Hello, are you Mr. Gu?"
Unexpectedly, it wasn't takeout, but a delivery.
Specifically, an encrypted private delivery.
"I am not." Shen Feizhi raised an eyebrow. "Do you need a signature for confirmation? I'll do it."
"But this delivery requires the recipient to sign for it in person with identification," the delivery guy said, looking at him helplessly.
Shen Feizhi said, "Wait here," went back into the room, and quickly returned to toss a marriage certificate at him. "Can I accept it on his behalf now?"
The delivery guy had likely never seen a marriage certificate between two Alphas before; he nodded in shock. "Y-Yes, you can…"
Shen Feizhi left a beautiful signature on the receipt.
Signing inevitably required using his right hand, causing his sleeve to slip back and reveal the faint glimpse of half a handcuff around his wrist.
The delivery guy realized something in a daze, his ears turning completely red as he lowered his head and pretended to be blind.
As soon as he finished signing, the courier snatched the receipt, blurted out a quick "Don't forget the positive review!" and bolted away like a rabbit.
Shen Feizhi politely reminded him to stay safe on the road.
Only after the front door closed did he begin to turn the massive package over in his hands.
What kind of item required encryption? How mysterious.
He went upstairs and knocked on the bathroom door. "Your delivery is here. Can I open it?"
"Go ahead," Gu Suizhou replied without a second thought.
With permission granted, Shen Feizhi had no further reservations.
With a rare hint of curiosity, he sliced open the seal…
