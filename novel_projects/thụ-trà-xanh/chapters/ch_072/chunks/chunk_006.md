The assistant squeezed his eyes shut and steeled his heart. "President Gu, I’m sorry. I failed the mission."
Gu Suizhou was in the middle of signing a contract with the man surnamed Zhang. Hearing this, his head snapped to the side, causing the "dots" in the character for "Zhou" to trail off high across the page.
His business partner was a sharp one. He hurried to sign his own name. "Then thank you, President Gu, for being willing to pull us out of the fire at such a critical moment! Rest assured, eighty percent of our profits for the next fifty years will belong to W.N. We absolutely will not let you take a loss!"
"Mhm." Gu Suizhou withdrew his gaze distractedly, his mind entirely occupied with how to explain himself to Shen Feizhi. "In the first quarter, I hope your sales figures will satisfy me."
"Don't you worry!" President Zhang patted his chest with confidence. "Our products are among the best in the entire Federation! You might not be very familiar with our company yet, but I’ll send a set of samples over later. Once you see them, you'll understand."
"Samples?"
The image of the sample the other party had sent him not long ago flashed through Gu Suizhou’s mind… Right, Shen Feizhi had almost seen it. That was a close call.
He cleared his throat with feigned composure. "Fine. The address is—"
"President Gu, there’s a call for you."

Shen Feizhi gave a hum of affirmation, not dwelling on the topic. He simply gave a calm command: "Half an hour. Get back here."
Gu Suizhou bit the inside of his cheek in terror, nearly dropping the handset.
