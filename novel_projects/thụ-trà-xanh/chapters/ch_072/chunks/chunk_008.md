The group chat was suddenly swamped with the same refrain.
Naturally, the two parties involved had no way of knowing any of this.
Gu Suizhou, who was supposed to be "thrilled to death," was currently pacing hesitatingly in front of his own front door, his overwhelming anxiety palpable despite being drenched in rainwater.
He had taken a shortcut to rush home in fifteen minutes, leaving himself five minutes to mentally prepare.
How was he supposed to explain this? If he told the truth, would Brother Shen ban him from the bedroom for a month? Surely not… the man couldn’t be that heartless, right…?
Five minutes passed in the blink of an eye.
Gu Suizhou gritted his teeth, steeled his nerves, and threw the door open: "Shen—"
"Get in here." Shen Feizhi’s voice drifted out from the bathroom.
Gu Suizhou immediately caught the raspy edge to his voice. He rushed over in three quick strides. "Are you okay?"
He pushed open the bathroom door, but there was no expected cloud of warm steam. Instead, the air was chilly, and the scene inside was laid bare.
Including the shoulder of the man in the bathtub, exposed to the cold air.
A surge of heat rushed to Gu Suizhou’s brain.
