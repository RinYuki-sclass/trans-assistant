Inside the bathroom, Gu Suizhou had just finished washing his hair when a faint sense of dread washed over him.
Wait a minute.
He hadn't done any online shopping lately!
Where did a package come from?
Hot water streamed down his forehead. In a flash of insight, his eyes snapped open as he remembered something—
“You might not be very familiar with our company. I’ll send you a set of samples once I'm back, and you'll understand once you see them.”
“I’ll send you the address later.”
Gu Suizhou: "……………………"
Holy! Shit!!!
I'm dead!!!!
He didn't even have time to wipe the water droplets off his body; he burst out with only a towel wrapped around his waist. "Brother Shen!! Don't open it yet—"
His words came to a dead halt.
He was still.
One step too late.
Shen Feizhi was leaning against the wardrobe, looking at the postcard in his hand with a half-smile. The enormous delivery box in front of him had been completely opened, and its contents were spread all over the bed.
“To show our gratitude for President Gu’s support of our company, we have specially selected our highest quality and most acclaimed products from recent years for you to experience…” He read the contents of the postcard aloud, enunciating every word with deliberate clarity.
With every syllable spoken, Gu Suizhou’s heart gave a violent tremor.
“I—I can explain…”
“You’ve had plenty of explanations already. What else is there to say?” Shen Feizhi replied, his voice laced with irony.
His critical gaze swept over the items strewn across the bed one by one.
