"President Gu, Mr. Zhang has been waiting for you for half an hour."
An assistant came over to urge him along.
Truly no sense of timing.
Gu Suizhou turned his head, displeased. "I know. I'm coming."
Shen Feizhi watched his hurried departure, thinking of heading up to the lounge to order some takeout while waiting for him to get off work.
As he turned, he noticed a smartphone sitting all alone on a nearby table.
Gu Suizhou didn't suffer from technology dependency. Usually, his biggest hobby was clinging to Shen Feizhi, so he often forgot his phone; if not for work-related calls, Shen Feizhi ended up using it more than its owner did.

This time was no different.
After a moment's thought, Shen Feizhi reached out to pick it up for him, lest he forget to take it home later.
Just as he moved, a flurry of hurried footsteps sounded from behind.
Gu Suizhou had doubled back, heading straight for the phone.
"Almost forgot this." Whether intentional or not, the screen lit up the moment the phone was picked up, only to be quickly flipped over, its back facing Shen Feizhi.
Shen Feizhi: "?"
The third time.
This was the third time today he felt a prickle of annoyance.
Sensing a gaze as sharp as a blade, Gu Suizhou rubbed his nose and asked cautiously, "What's wrong, Bro?"
"Nothing," Shen Feizhi said woodenly, turning to head downstairs. "Go back to work."
Gu Suizhou’s hand, hanging by the seam of his trousers, curled inward.
His Brother Shen’s intuition was truly too sharp.
