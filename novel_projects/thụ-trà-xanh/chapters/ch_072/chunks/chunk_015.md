From ears, bit-gags, collars, leather leads, and restraints… to the countless internal gadgets. It truly lived up to the sentence on the back of the postcard:
There is nothing we cannot create, only what you cannot imagine.
“This is the company you went behind my back to acquire?”
No wonder he’d kept it a secret from everyone; it was scandalous.
Gu Suizhou’s only thought right now was to jump off the third floor and spend a month in the hospital to avoid this situation.
His ears burned hot as he shifted his gaze away uncomfortably. “I really… if I didn't owe them a favor, I wouldn't have even looked their way.”
Shen Feizhi, who knew him all too well, let out a scoff. “The truth?”
“…” Gu Suizhou’s voice weakened significantly. “Fine. Actually, if this financial crisis is resolved, the value they’ll bring to W.N will be at least this much.”
He held up five fingers.
It was indeed a considerable turnover.
But Shen Feizhi still didn't let him off: "Are you sure… there isn't… any other reason?"
Gu Suizhou: "…"
Shen Feizhi's pitch-black eyes were far too piercing, as if they could see right through a person's soul.
Unable to withstand it, Gu Suizhou gave up and crouched on the ground, burying his head firmly between his knees. "Fine, fine, I admit it! I'd heard of their brand before and originally planned to have someone buy some of their last products before they went bankrupt. I didn't expect that, by some stroke of luck, someone would ask me for help, so I just went with the flow and acquired the whole company."
Shen Feizhi’s lips curled into a silent smile.
As expected, this was the quintessential Gu Suizhou answer.
He playfully picked up a collar from the bed. "They said they’d let you 'experience' it. Who were you planning to experience it with?"
"Who else could it be?" Gu Suizhou continued his impression of a guilty ball. "But I was only thinking about it…"
Shen Feizhi let out a low chuckle, holding the collar as he crouched down across from him. "Actually, it's not entirely out of the question."
"?!" Gu Suizhou snapped his head up.
