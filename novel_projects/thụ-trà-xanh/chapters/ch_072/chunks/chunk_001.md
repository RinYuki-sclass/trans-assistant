72. Extra 2
When Shen Feizhi pushed the door open and entered, Gu Suizhou was lying lazily on the office sofa, admiring the ring on the ring finger of his left hand.
Not long after they returned from New Year's Eve, Shen Feizhi had made it up to him with a ring.
It didn't carry a significance as monumental as that crystal, but it was a pair of very simple matching rings. They were crafted from a rare raw stone that Shen Feizhi had C2 bring from the Main Star, making them one of a kind.
From then on, two rings appeared on Shen Feizhi's left hand: the one Gu Suizhou had given him on his ring finger, and this simple band on his middle finger.
Following the Federation's review meeting after the New Year, the Same-Sex Marriage Law was amended to be more comprehensive. On the first day of February, they registered their marriage.
At the time, Shen Feizhi was "26" and Gu Suizhou was 22. In an era where the average age for marriage was around seventy or eighty, they fell into the category of "extremely, extremely early" marriages.
For a time, major media platforms were flooded with reports and tabloid news about them.
The parties involved, however, couldn't have cared less. After getting their certificate, they each returned to their respective posts and worked diligently.
Especially Gu Suizhou.
His recent enthusiasm for work seemed as though it had been injected with hormones, leaving even Shen Feizhi feeling quite puzzled.
"The Legal Department on the second floor has a document that needs your personal verification and approval. You need to head over there now," Shen Feizhi said, leaning against the doorway as he succinctly explained the situation. "Also, someone was just looking for you."
Gu Suizhou stood up instantly. "Who?"
"Why so agitated?" Shen Feizhi glanced at him, surprised. "They said they're here to discuss a partnership. I haven't met them before, so I had someone take them to the waiting lounge. What project have you been working on lately?"
"Ah, I'm not sure. Probably some minor project I forgot about," Gu Suizhou said, letting out a barely perceptible sigh of relief as he regained his casual tone. "I'm heading down now. Do you want anything to eat? I can stop by the breakroom on the way."
