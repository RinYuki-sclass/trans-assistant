Chapter 10

Gu Suizhou didn't speak to Shen Feizhi all night.

The next morning, Shen Feizhi bought some quick breakfast and, splurging on an autonomous taxi, took the Little Brat to the Underground City.

"Watch your splint," he said as Gu Suizhou clumsily climbed into the car. "I'm not buying you another few-thousand-yuan splint again."

Gu Suizhou's injured leg throbbed.

He could still vividly remember Shen Feizhi applying the splint last night.

He carefully propped his limping left leg on the front seat and turned to stare out the window, still refusing to acknowledge Shen Feizhi.

Shen Feizhi responded to his silent treatment with magnanimous silence, unhurried, and sat in the front passenger seat with his eyes closed, resting.

It won't be long before this kid starts talking to me, he thought.

The scenery outside the car window flashed by. Soon, after passing through a pitch-black tunnel, the familiar architecture of the Underground City came into view.

Gu Suizhou had been so angry last night that he didn't fall asleep until dawn. Though still a bit groggy, the sight of familiar landmarks instantly banished his drowsiness.

"Brother Shen," he said, his voice betraying no emotion, "you… work here?"

Shen Feizhi opened his eyes. The W.N headquarters loomed ahead.

He yawned, got out of the car, and opened the door for the disabled passenger in the back. "W.N, huh? Isn't it obvious?"

Gu Suizhou nimbly hopped out on one leg, propping himself against the car door as he gripped Shen Feizhi's sleeve tightly. "How could you possibly work here?"

Shen Feizhi, an aimless loafer with no skills? How could he ever get into a place like this?!

Shen Feizhi gave him an odd look. "Is it really that hard?"

With that, he tugged his sleeve free from the young man's pale, slender fingers and confidently strode to the gate. Under the guard's inscrutable gaze, he swiped his employee ID card.

Beep—Authorization confirmed.

Gu Suizhou stared in shock at the man ahead.

Cleaning Supervisor Shen Zhi, Identity Verified.

Gu Suizhou: "……"

C-Cleaning… Supervisor?!

Gu Suizhou clenched his fists tightly, seething with a sense of betrayal. He hadn't even noticed that the name listed wasn't three characters long.

The guard looked at him with sympathy, sighing as if he understood the feeling all too well.

Ah! Wonderful! Finally, someone who understands how I felt that day!

But Shen Feizhi remained unfazed. "What's wrong? Look down on cleaning?"

Gu Suizhou forced a strained smile. "Of course not! The working class is the most glorious—I've known that since I was a kid."

"That's more like it."

"No unauthorized personnel are allowed here. Go wait at the internet cafe across the street. I'll get someone to cover my shift and come out."

Gu Suizhou watched the man's proud, upright back disappear through the gate.

The next moment, his face darkened, and he limped briskly across W.N Street and burst into the internet cafe opposite the street.

"Lai Gou!" he shouted.

The internet cafe had just opened, and Leapton hadn't even finished washing up. He emerged, bewildered, with a mouthful of toothpaste foam.

"It's only eight o'clock! What brings you here so early, old man?"

"You think I want to be here?" Gu Suizhou ruffled his hair irritably. "Tell me, did anyone named Shen Feizhi come to your place yesterday?"

"Isn't that the name that made you bolt out of here yesterday?" Leapton's eyes gleamed with gossip. "Nope, never heard of him. Who is he? What's he to you?"

Gu Suizhou hesitated.

After a moment's thought, he gave an answer that would shock Leapton for the next eight centuries: "Probably… my sugar daddy?"

Someone who pays for my schooling and daily expenses? Isn't that what a sugar daddy is?

Leapton stared at him in stunned silence, then wordlessly raised his hand and slapped himself lightly.

"Fuck! This isn't a dream!"

This ancestor with assets worth hundreds of millions actually has a sugar daddy?!

"Don't overthink it," Gu Suizhou said. "If you haven't met him before, that's fine. He's coming over later—you know, the really handsome guy I mentioned. When you see him… unleash your full flirtatious charm and try to win him over."

He hadn't forgotten his honey trap plan!

Leapton: "?"

It took him a second to process before he erupted into curses: "Fuck you, Gu Suizhou! Do you think I'm some kind of floozy? I'm saving my purity for my destined one—"

Ding-dong.

The crisp, familiar doorbell rang again.

Shen Feizhi had barely stepped inside when he heard Blondie's familiar tirade from behind the counter. He paused, leaned against the doorframe, and knocked lightly. "Am I interrupting something?"

Gu Suizhou's lips twitched. "N-no—"

"Absolutely not! Never!" A saccharine, high-pitched voice—completely different from the earlier outburst—cut him off with lightning speed. "Mr. Shen, you're never an interruption, no matter when you come~"

Gu Suizhou: "……"

You little shit, how can you change your attitude so fast?

…Wait a minute.

His brow furrowed. "Didn't you say you didn't know him?"

Leapton looked slightly puzzled. "What? You mean Mr. Shen Zhi? This is his third time visiting my shop."

Gu Suizhou suddenly realized something and plastered on a dazzling smile, turning to face the man. "Brother Shen!"

Shen Feizhi looked at him, amused. "Hm?"

Gu Suizhou practically hissed through clenched teeth, his words muffled. "So the 'Fei' in your name is silent, huh?"

Shen Feizhi pressed a knuckle against his forehead and, for once, let out a chuckle.

"Wait, hold on, you two!" Leapton was utterly baffled. "You know each other?"

Gu Suizhou said coldly, "Ah, my sugar daddy."

Shen Feizhi's smile vanished.

"You're tarnishing my reputation?"

"No, no, not at all!" Leapton waved his hands frantically, but the corner of his mouth twitched uncontrollably beneath his nose. He grabbed a cup of hot coffee and handed it over. "Aiya, look at the commotion you've caused! I almost misunderstood. Mr. Shen, I knew you were single!"

Shen Feizhi adored the label "single."

Blondie's cowlick perked up. "Mr. Shen, I'm Guinness Dan Leapton!"

Coffee splashed onto the floor.

Shen Feizhi rarely lost his composure. "What?"

Blondie obediently repeated, "Guinness Dan Leapton."

Shen Feizhi: "……"

The original owner's second harem member had appeared.

Leapton hadn't appeared as early as Lin Sui in the original plot, but his role was equally significant, making him the original owner's favorite Omega.

Shen Feizhi's gaze darkened as he stared at Blondie's cowlick, a sudden weight settling over his expression.

"How old are you?" he asked.

Leapton didn't understand the sudden shift in Shen Feizhi's gaze but answered anyway, "Twenty-five this year."

He was nine years older than Gu Suizhou, yet in both appearance and demeanor, Gu Suizhou seemed the elder.

Shen Feizhi's fingers twitched slightly.

Blondie is also connected to Gu Suizhou, he mused. He must have been involved in W.N and the Gu family's affairs. Since he's a major plot character, could preventing Leapton's death be a critical turning point in this world?

With this question lingering, Shen Feizhi remained silent as Gu Suizhou introduced the pair. From a detached, almost godlike perspective, he observed their expressions and conversation.

Gu Suizhou explained that he had known Leapton since childhood, lost contact for a while due to school, and only recently reconnected.

His words carried little credibility. Shen Feizhi listened casually, then waved away the topic. "Where do you usually stay?" he asked Blondie.

"Right here. This internet cafe is my home; there's a rest area in the back," Leapton gestured toward a pillar.

"So you live alone? Who do you hang out with in the Underground City?"

Watching Shen Feizhi and Leapton suddenly become engrossed in conversation, Gu Suizhou's handsome face grew darker by the minute.

Gu Suizhou exuded a palpable aura of suppressed anger. Only when their conversation paused did he clear his throat twice to assert his presence.

"Finished chatting?"

"Mm-hmm," Shen Feizhi replied, taking a sip of the freshly served hot coffee. A faint, almost imperceptible curve tugged at the corner of his lips. "Tired of standing?"

"Not really, just my legs are a little sore," Gu Suizhou said with an understanding smile. "You can keep talking; don't worry about me. It's just my legs might give out after standing so long. Of course, that's nothing compared to the importance of your conversation. I can endure."

"Spare me the sarcasm. It won't work on me." Shen Feizhi set down his coffee and pointed upstairs to a private booth. "I was there at noon yesterday. Now you know how I caught you ditching school."

Gu Suizhou curled his lip. "I'll be more careful next time."

Shen Feizhi gave his braced leg a casual kick. "I brought you here today to show you where I work."

As if I'm not more familiar with this place than you are, Gu Suizhou thought, but he replied obediently, "Oh."

"So you can rest assured that I have a legitimate job. You don't need to worry about me dragging you into any illegal schemes that could implicate you."

Shen Feizhi straightened his ID badge and work pass. "W.N. employees aren't allowed to use smartphones during work hours. I'll be on full-shift duty for the next week, so don't panic if you can't reach me—it's normal."

He looked up, the lazy glint in his eyes vanishing completely when they met Gu Suizhou's gaze.

"So, don't let me find out you're investigating me behind my back."

Gu Suizhou froze, his hand poised over his smartphone, ready to ask Leapton to investigate "Shen Zhi." He couldn't decide whether to continue or put it away.

Shen Feizhi made the choice for him.

He reached out and turned off the man's smart device, then waved dismissively without turning around as he walked leisurely across the street toward the Research Institute.

Leapton stared dreamily after his retreating figure, giggling foolishly to himself.

Gu Suizhou's gaze remained fixed on that retreating back until the man disappeared through the Institute's entrance. Only then did he reply, his voice as light as air after Leapton's elbow grew weary of poking him: "He's from the Shen family."

"Oh, so he's a Shen… Holy shit?! The Shen family?!" Leapton exclaimed in horror. "You mean that Shen family?"

"Yes, the same Shen family I asked you to investigate," Gu Suizhou confirmed, his eyes shifting back to Leapton. "Going after him will likely yield evidence much faster."

"So that's why you wanted me to seduce him?" Leapton asked.

"Exactly," Gu Suizhou replied bluntly, his words laced with an even sharper chill. "And that's why I'm warning you. Play with him if you want, but don't catch feelings."
