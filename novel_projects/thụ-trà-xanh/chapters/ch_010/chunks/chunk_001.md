Chapter 10
Gu Suizhou didn't speak to Shen Feizhi all night.
The next morning, Shen Feizhi bought some quick breakfast and, splurging on an autonomous taxi, took the Little Brat to the Underground City.
"Watch your splint," he said as Gu Suizhou clumsily climbed into the car. "I'm not buying you another few-thousand-yuan splint again."
Gu Suizhou's injured leg throbbed.
He could still vividly remember Shen Feizhi applying the splint last night.
He carefully propped his limping left leg on the front seat and turned to stare out the window, still refusing to acknowledge Shen Feizhi.
Shen Feizhi responded to his silent treatment with magnanimous silence, unhurried, and sat in the front passenger seat with his eyes closed, resting.
It won't be long before this kid starts talking to me, he thought.
The scenery outside the car window flashed by. Soon, after passing through a pitch-black tunnel, the familiar architecture of the Underground City came into view.
Gu Suizhou had been so angry last night that he didn't fall asleep until dawn. Though still a bit groggy, the sight of familiar landmarks instantly banished his drowsiness.
"Brother Shen," he said, his voice betraying no emotion, "you… work here?"
Shen Feizhi opened his eyes. The W.N headquarters loomed ahead.
He yawned, got out of the car, and opened the door for the disabled passenger in the back. "W.N, huh? Isn't it obvious?"
Gu Suizhou nimbly hopped out on one leg, propping himself against the car door as he gripped Shen Feizhi's sleeve tightly. "How could you possibly work here?"
