Gu Suizhou froze, his hand poised over his smartphone, ready to ask Leapton to investigate "Shen Zhi." He couldn't decide whether to continue or put it away.

Shen Feizhi made the choice for him.
He reached out and turned off the man's smart device, then waved dismissively without turning around as he walked leisurely across the street toward the Research Institute.
Leapton stared dreamily after his retreating figure, giggling foolishly to himself.
He nudged Gu Suizhou with his elbow. "Hey, how did you and Mr. Shen meet?"
Gu Suizhou's gaze remained fixed on that retreating back until the man disappeared through the Institute's entrance. Only then did he reply, his voice as light as air after Leapton's elbow grew weary of poking him: "He's from the Shen family."
"Oh, so he's a Shen… Holy shit?! The Shen family?!" Leapton exclaimed in horror. "You mean that Shen family?"
"Yes, the same Shen family I asked you to investigate," Gu Suizhou confirmed, his eyes shifting back to Leapton. "Going after him will likely yield evidence much faster."
"So that's why you wanted me to seduce him?" Leapton asked.
"Exactly," Gu Suizhou replied bluntly, his words laced with an even sharper chill. "And that's why I'm warning you. Play with him if you want, but don't catch feelings."
Around ten o'clock, the Underground City began to fill with people. Watching the bustling crowds outside, he added as a final warning, "There isn't a single decent person in the Shen family."
