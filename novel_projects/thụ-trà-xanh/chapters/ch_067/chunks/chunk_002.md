For Ritter, the most important things in life were the adoration of others and his aspiration toward a higher-tier world.
The Main Star intentionally delayed his sentencing. C2 threw him into the Main Star’s black market industry; they didn’t physically abuse him, as that would have violated the Main Star’s humanitarian regulations.
Instead, they simply placed him in the hands of the unsavory "talents" of the black market, forcing this fool who fancied himself a genius to live every day amidst the indifference and contempt of the Main Star’s citizens.
Occasionally, he would even be hauled away to serve as a subject for a few human experiments.
To those people, that was the only use Ritter had left.
When the Federation’s public trial was broadcast, C2 set up a 24-hour non-stop stream and locked Ritter in a cell, forcing him to watch. He had no food or water, his life sustained solely by the nutrient solutions A11 provided.
A man as desperate to live and as afraid of death as Ritter tried to commit suicide over forty times in a single month.
