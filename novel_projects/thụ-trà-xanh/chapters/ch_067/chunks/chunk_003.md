But since the garden's renovations would take some time, they were in no rush to perform the burial.
Time flew by until it was October. On the day of Shen Feizhi’s birthday, Gu Suizhou left work early to pick him up.
Half of the management rights for the W.N Research Institute had fallen onto Shen Feizhi's shoulders, leaving him so busy lately that his feet hardly touched the ground.
By the time he finally finished his work and clocked out, Gu Suizhou had already fallen asleep while waiting in his office.
He lightened his footsteps, walking over to rub the other’s ear.
Gu Suizhou pried one eyelid open with great effort. "Finished?"
"Yeah," Shen Feizhi said. "It’s still early. Sleep a bit longer; I’ll wake you in half an hour."
"Alright." Gu Suizhou rolled over, hugging Shen Feizhi’s pillow as he drifted off again.
