Strictly speaking, this was the first time Shen Feizhi was celebrating his birthday while actually being aware of it; previously, he had thought the date belonged to the "original owner."
Back at the house, the two of them stared at the small cake in silence.
Gu Suizhou asked, "How many candles are we lighting?"
Twenty-six, or several thousand?
Shen Feizhi lowered his eyelids. "Just light one."
Simplicity was the ultimate sophistication.

"Good idea," Gu Suizhou agreed.
He had originally booked a restaurant to take Shen Feizhi to, but the man had zero interest in such things, his mind filled only with work. After some discussion, they reached a consensus to just spend the day casually at home.
Sometimes, after experiencing too much, one yearned exceptionally for the quiet passage of time.
Leaning against the kitchen doorframe, Gu Suizhou watched Shen Feizhi and suddenly asked, "What are we exactly?"
He had asked this question once before, and the answer he’d received back then was—friends.
It had nearly infuriated him to death.
If he got "friends with benefits" this time, he might actually cough up blood and die on the spot.
Fortunately, Shen Feizhi wasn't that heartless. He countered with a question of his own: "What do you think?"
Gu Suizhou thought for a moment and said earnestly, "I like you."
Shen Feizhi turned off the stove and looked at him with a hint of amusement. "I know."
Gu Suizhou said with certainty, "You like me, too."
"So, we’re in a relationship now, right?"
As Gu Suizhou spoke, his eyes shimmered with a bright light that felt almost loud.
