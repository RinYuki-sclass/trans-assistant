The last of Gu Suizhou’s sleepiness vanished instantly, and his ears suddenly felt a bit hot.
Shen Feizhi happened to turn around. "Is there anything else you want to buy?"
Startled, Gu Suizhou instinctively shoved the boxes into the gaps between the watermelons.
"Nothing."
"Then let's go pay."
Gu Suizhou’s movements had been too fast for Shen Feizhi to see clearly; he assumed it was just a pack of gum.
Gu Suizhou cleared his throat. "I'll pay. It’s crowded, why don't you wait for me outside?"
Shen Feizhi gave him a strange look.
Usually, whenever the two of them went out to buy things, he was always the one who paid.
This scoundrel usually insisted on experiencing the feeling of being "kept," happily mooching off him without a shred of shame. Why the sudden urge to pick up the tab?
Supplying a strained sense of composure, Gu Suizhou forced his eyes not to glance at the bottom of the cart. "I want to eat grilled beef skewers."
"I'll go out and buy those first, then," Shen Feizhi said, understanding.
Once he saw Shen Feizhi’s figure disappear through the exit, Gu Suizhou let out a sharp sigh of relief. He looked down at the two small boxes at the bottom of the cart, torn.
