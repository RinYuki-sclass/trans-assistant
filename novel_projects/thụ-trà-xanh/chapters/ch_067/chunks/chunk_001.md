Chapter 67
Gu Suizhou’s press conference was concise; not a single word of superfluous nonsense was uttered. He left immediately after addressing and answering all the reporters' questions.
Blue Star had been exceptionally bustling these past few days. The people at the Prosecutor's Office were driven to the brink of madness; arrest warrants and detention orders were being issued one after another, leaving officials at every level running themselves ragged.
In the beginning, the arrest warrants were first served to dozens of wealthy families and major enterprises.
Some of those people had been happily eating New Year’s Eve dinner with their families one moment, only to be handcuffed by intruding investigators the next; others were still in the throes of passion with their lovers at private clubs or nightclubs when they were pinned directly to their beds…
The scandals within the upper-class social circles were explosive. Whether they were in the business world or the entertainment industry, almost no one was spared.
It was truly quite unbelievable. Had it not been for this thorough investigation, those who had participated in the illegal purchase of prohibited drugs all those years ago would have shown absolutely no signs of abnormality in their daily lives.
What kind of mentality did they possess to be able to continue living their lives of luxury and dissipation with a clear conscience after indirectly causing someone's death?
Aside from the parties involved, perhaps no one would ever know.
After several heavyweights were exposed, the next wave targeted those who knew of Ritter’s actions but failed to report them—those who, instead, assisted him by giving false testimony.
What was truly heart-wrenching was that half of W.N’s employees back then were involved in this. They were elites discovered and nurtured by Lin Zizhuo and Gu Lang, yet under the lure of financial bribes, every single one of them chose betrayal.
When they were arrested, Gu Suizhou was standing right outside the Prosecutor’s Office.
Those people didn't dare look him in the face; some even fell to their knees in public.
Gu Suizhou’s reaction was flat: "Don't kneel to me, I can't afford it. When you get out, don't go kneeling to my parents either; they’d find it disgusting."
No one dared to retort, and no one was worthy of retorting.

Soon, the partners who had dealings with Ritter were also caught in the net.
After the Federation dealt with the first batch of people, they immediately issued an announcement regarding Ritter.
His crimes included intentional homicide, kidnapping, illegal detention, illegal development, and intentional injury, totaling twenty-three counts. He was directly listed by the Federation Supreme Court as a major S-Rank case and a criminal archetype. His relatives' civil rights were restricted for life, and the announcement was published across the entire network, never to be deleted.
From then on, the name Ritter Naons remained permanently at the top of the condemnation and vitriol lists of every major influencer on Starnet.
