Those watermelons were bizarrely shaped; sitting on the display racks, some were the size of four human heads.
Seeing his silent stare, the vendor patted a rind proudly. "Genetically modified. Seedless and super sweet! Want one, handsome?"
Shen Feizhi replied politely, "No, thank you."
Who on earth would buy a melon that big?
He turned his head and continued walking.
In the time it took to pick up some strawberries, he looked down to find a massive watermelon sitting in his cart, hogging every inch of space.
"How come you didn't call me when you came to the supermarket?" Gu Suizhou’s voice was still thick with sleep.
With an extra body suddenly hovering behind him, Shen Feizhi said speechlessly, "You picked this?"
"Yup!" Gu Suizhou patted the melon twice with an air of pride. "Look, isn't it square? I searched through the whole pile to find one this symmetrical. You don't like it? Should I go swap it for another one?"
"…" Shen Feizhi’s lips twitched. "What, are you planning to find a triangular one next?"
Sometimes, he really wanted to crack Gu Suizhou’s head open just to see what was inside.
After a long moment, he resigned himself to it. "Fine, just leave it."
Since he’d picked the melon, he had to carry the weight—and so, the task of pushing the cart naturally fell to Gu Suizhou.
Gu Suizhou followed slowly behind him. As they reached the checkout counter, his peripheral vision caught a row of small, exquisitely packaged boxes.
Yawning, his hand acted on its own and snagged a box.
