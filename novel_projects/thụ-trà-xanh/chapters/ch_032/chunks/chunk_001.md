Chapter 32
After Gu Suizhou's hundredth tantrum-fueled demand, Shen Feizhi finally relented and explained the situation on the way home.
"It's not a real engagement, and there won't be a ceremony. We just need to spread the rumor to convince Elder Master Shen. Plus, it's the best excuse to bring Lu Fan over."
Gu Suizhou sulked the entire way, refusing to open the door for Shen Feizhi even after they arrived home.
"Oh, so that promise about helping you prune the peach blossoms? Guess that's out the window then, huh? Ooh, look at Brother Shen, turns out you don't always keep your word."
Shen Feizhi had been barely restraining his temper since the moment they left.
No need to treat this little brat with kid gloves.
Gu Suizhou snorted through his nose.
Shen Feizhi, his heart sealed with ice, retorted, "Upset? Deal with it. Get out."
Gu Suizhou, of course, refused to budge. "I'm coming with you on Saturday!"
"What for?" Shen Feizhi frowned. "To those people, you're still my ex-lover."
Taking my ex-lover along to buy a wedding home with my fiancée? That's some underworld shit I'd never do.
Gu Suizhou snapped fiercely, "I'm going to establish my rightful place as the official spouse!"
Shen Feizhi stared at him calmly.
