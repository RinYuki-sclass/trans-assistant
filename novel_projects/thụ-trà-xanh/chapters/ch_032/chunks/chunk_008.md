Lu Qian scoffed, a mirthless smile twisting her lips.
She nearly blocked and deleted him in one swift motion, but on a whim, she asked, "Feizhi, when you chat with people on your smartphone, how often do you use emojis?"
As she spoke, she surreptitiously activated the recording function on her phone.
Shen Feizhi, unaware of her motives, guessed she had stumbled upon some more odd internet trends and replied casually, "Not often."
"Ah, so you do use them sometimes?" Lu Qian pressed. "When would you use emojis?"
Shen Feizhi pondered for a moment, setting aside the one he had proactively sent Gu Suizhou earlier in a good mood…
"Usually when I'm too lazy to type, I'll send a perfunctory emoji."
"Oh, I see."
Lu Qian nearly burst out laughing, hurriedly stopping the recording.
Revisiting Gu Suizhou's WeChat Moments, she realized the emoji was far more than just an emoji.
After scrutinizing it for ages, she finally pieced together the scattered fragments of the long image. Each pair of characters spelled out the word "Perfunctory."
Lu Qian barely suppressed her triumphant grin as she sent the recording to Gu Suizhou.
Love Without Commitment : Just had a quick chat with Feizhi. Didn't want you to overthink things, so I'm sharing our conversation openly with you~
Love Without Commitment : Don't worry, even though Feizhi is incredibly gentlemanly and sweet to me, I won't be "deluding myself," okay?~
A long, tense silence stretched out on the other end.
