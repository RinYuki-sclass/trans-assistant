Gu Suizhou had accompanied him to pick out the car, insisting on helping choose it. As Shen Feizhi sat in the driver's seat, ready to leave, Gu Suizhou muttered thickly, "The car I picked out… and the first person you drive isn't me."
Shen Feizhi gave him a look that said, What nonsense are you talking about? "Getting in?"
Gu Suizhou blinked. "You want me to come along?"
"I'm giving you a ride home," Shen Feizhi replied.
"How disappointing," Gu Suizhou said, but his hand moved swiftly to open the car door. Just before getting in, he paused dramatically, feigning hesitation, and asked, "Brother Shen, won't this delay you? Isn't that a bit much?"
He had expected Shen Feizhi to reassure him with a domineering, "Don't worry about it."
Instead, Shen Feizhi actually glanced at his watch. "You're right. I'm already late."
With that, he slammed the door shut, stomped on the accelerator, and left Gu Suizhou choking in a cloud of exhaust fumes.
And, to add insult to injury, he called out, "Take a taxi home yourself."
