Not terribly high, but for an ordinary person, it could easily be fatal.
But Shen Feizhi was no ordinary person.
Of course, he wasn't actually going to crush Gu Suizhou to death. As they plummeted past the trunk of the Mahogany Medicinal Tree, he slammed his own back against it to break their fall.
Gu Suizhou didn't even see the move. All he felt was a sharp pain behind him as he crashed heavily onto the ground.
Fortunately, his head had landed on something soft, sparing him a concussion. Still, his vision went black, and he gasped weakly, "Bro~~ I'm dying…"

Shen Feizhi rose to his feet, disgusted. "Useless."
He looked completely unscathed, his hair only slightly mussed, as if he'd just casually ridden a roller coaster.
Gu Suizhou lay on the ground, eyes squeezed shut, rubbing his dazed head. His entire body ached as if it were about to fall apart.
He didn't complain much about being used as a human cushion. After all, if Shen Feizhi truly was that person , Gu Suizhou would die for him without batting an eye.
Still, seeing the considerate Big Brother who had once been so gentle and kind to him now transformed into this cold, ruthless, mixed-blood Demon Lord, he couldn't help but sigh with emotion. "So cruel, Big Brother."
Shen Feizhi ignored him, fumbling around in the darkness.
Gu Suizhou's eyelids felt glued shut, and he kept them closed. He was still afraid of the dark, but the self-imposed blackness behind his eyelids was more bearable than opening them to an endless void.
The next moment, however, the darkness before him shifted, warming into a gentle glow.
Gu Suizhou's beautiful lips parted slightly as he blinked his eyes open in surprise.
Shen Feizhi's black thermal jacket was draped over him. The man himself wore only a thin shirt, his hands busy feeding firewood into a newly kindled fire.
In the flickering firelight, Gu Suizhou clearly saw that the back of Shen Feizhi's right hand was mangled, especially near the ring finger, where the wound was deep enough to expose bone.
A hazy memory surfaced: when they fell, his head had been cushioned by something soft.
Seeing color return to Gu Suizhou's lips, Shen Feizhi averted his gaze and fashioned a simple torch.
He held up one finger. "You have ten minutes to pull yourself together."
