His tone was harsher and more serious than ever before.
He wasn't a block of wood. On the contrary, someone who had been through as much as he had was always highly sensitive to emotions. He had sensed the shift in Gu Suizhou's attitude over the past two days.
It was no longer the previous disgust and pure exploitation. Instead, there was something more… no, there was much more genuine feeling.
He knew this emotion wasn't the "like" or "love" the kid casually tossed around, but whatever it was, it was entirely superfluous to him.
Emotions cloud judgment and behavior. He was using Gu Suizhou, but he didn't want to exploit someone who was kind to him. Only a relationship based on mutual interest was truly reliable.
He had no desire to explore Gu Suizhou's baffling emotions or question their origin. As long as the other man was sensible enough to retreat to his proper place, Shen Feizhi could magnanimously pretend he hadn't noticed.
With that final, cutting remark, Shen Feizhi turned and walked away without a moment's hesitation.
He always wore white shirts. Even the finest fabric of that color would reveal any dark stains beneath. Now, the mangled flesh and blood from the impact on his back were starkly exposed to the person behind him.
The sticky blood clung to his skin, and the wind blowing from the cave entrance sent a chill through him.
Even with his extraordinary constitution, Shen Feizhi felt a deep discomfort.
He considered his words severe enough to humiliate anyone, ensuring they wouldn't dare approach him again…
The next second, a warm body slammed into him from behind.
Shen Feizhi: "…" How is that little brat still standing after a fall like that?

But Gu Suizhou seemed to anticipate the move. A broad palm covered his elbow joint and gently pushed it back.
As his hand retreated, it audaciously scratched at the bunched-up fabric of Shen Feizhi's shirt at his waist.
Whether intentional or not, the fabric, without touching the wound itself, lightly wiped away a drop of blood that had trickled down his waist.
Even cleaning just that single drop made Shen Feizhi feel significantly cleaner.
Gu Suizhou had already figured out the man's mysophobia trigger: as long as there was no direct skin-to-skin contact, Shen Feizhi wouldn't truly lash out.
