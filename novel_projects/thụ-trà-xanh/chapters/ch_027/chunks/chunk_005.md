"I just checked that spot," Leapton said. "No good. The path's been excavated. It's now a cliff—not incredibly deep, but still a cliff."
Gu Suizhou and Shen Feizhi spoke in unison: "Can we jump down?"
Leapton's fingers trembled. "Old Gu, I expect you to be reckless, but Mr. Shen, why are you joining his madness?"
His tone turned serious. "Impossible. Don't even think about it. A jump from that height is certain death. I advise you to come back today and wait until the film crew leaves the day after tomorrow."
"We can't wait that long," Gu Suizhou said, frowning.
He scanned the area, desperately searching for something to help them climb down the cliff.
But this was an herb garden, not a botanical garden. The barren fields offered nothing resembling a long, slender vine.
While Gu Suizhou was searching, Shen Feizhi tested the ground with his foot.
The entire ecosystem was dedicated to cultivating medicinal herbs, so the ground wasn't paved with sturdy concrete. Footsteps left clear imprints and depressions.
Perhaps, he thought, surviving the jump isn't as impossible as it seems.
If he were alone, he would have acted already. But with this "weak and helpless" invalid trailing behind him—an utter nuisance—it was infuriating.
"Brother Shen!"
The invalid suddenly shouted his name.

"Brother Shen, hidden cameras are moving toward us," Gu Suizhou whispered, his voice low. "I assume you, like me, don't want to be exposed on camera."
