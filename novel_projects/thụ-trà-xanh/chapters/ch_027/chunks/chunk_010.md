So he maintained a precise one-centimeter distance, his warm breath brushing against Shen Feizhi's ear. "Shen Feizhi, how can a man as heartless as you exist in this world?"
Sure enough, Shen Feizhi didn't strike him. He stiffly turned his neck. "If you can't stand it, get lost."
"I don't mind," Gu Suizhou said, his voice laced with heartache as he lowered his head to gently blow on the still-bleeding wound on Shen Feizhi's back. "Because I just happen to be the most shameless man in the world!"
Shen Feizhi sneered. "You're barely a boy, calling yourself a man?"
"Fine, I'm a child," Gu Suizhou conceded, backing down with surprising speed.
His hand silently snaked around Shen Feizhi's waist and came to rest on his chest. Plump, rosy fingers slipped between two tightly fastened buttons on the shirt, tugging indecisively as he whined pitifully.
"Children can't stand being wronged. Brother Shen~ My whole body feels like it's about to fall apart. Won't you take pity on me?"
His voice was so melodramatic and drawn-out that anyone overhearing would have thought Shen Feizhi had snatched his last lollipop, thrown it on the ground, and stomped it to pieces.
A headache began to throb in Shen Feizhi's temples. "What the hell do you want?"
