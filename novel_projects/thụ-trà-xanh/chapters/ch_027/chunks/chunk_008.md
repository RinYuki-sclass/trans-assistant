His tone was equally unpleasant.
The fire blazed brightly, illuminating the shallow cave as if it were daytime.
At least he didn't have to fear the dark anymore.
Gu Suizhou rubbed his ears.
A ringing had started in them.
He couldn't hear a word Shen Feizhi was saying; he could only see the man's features softened by the firelight.
Deep-set and flawlessly sculpted, his face was unrealistically handsome.
Gu Suizhou blurted out, "Brother Shen, your face is seriously to die for."
"…" The "to die for" face instantly darkened. "Looks like you don't need ten minutes to rest after all."
How wonderful.
Not even two minutes had passed, and that useless mouth was already spouting nonsense.
Gu Suizhou forced his aching body to sit up, cupping his face in his hands. His tone was deceptively obedient. "Bro, do you think I have Stockholm syndrome? You crushed me on the way down, but when I saw your hand, I actually felt… touched."
Shen Feizhi tossed a stick at him. "Let me cure you, then."
"First," Shen Feizhi began, his voice glacial as he listed his reasons, "I pinned you down during the fall because your left wrist and hip were already injured. If I'd been too hurt to move, you wouldn't have been able to carry me. And for the record, I don't like being carried by anyone."
"Second, I used my hand to shield you because I was afraid you'd actually die, and I'd spend the rest of my life rotting in prison."
