Shen Feizhi had promised Gu Suizhou that he would head to the company to pick him up after dropping off the dog. Naturally, he wouldn’t go back on his word. He arrived at W.N right at the appointed time, heading to the top floor under the curious and starstruck gazes of the employees.
Gu Suizhou was still in a meeting, so he went to the reception room. Given his special status, the secretary told him that if he wished, he could turn on the light screen to watch President Gu’s meeting.
Since Shen Feizhi had nothing better to do, he turned it on.
Gu Suizhou was dressed in a black suit, looking unusually less cavalier than usual. His beautiful brows were knitted together, his eyes brimming with impatience.
"What the hell is your verbal guarantee worth? You flap your lips and I’m supposed to grant you a two-billion-yuan appropriation? Since when did your words become so damn valuable?"
Shen Feizhi raised an eyebrow.
Even a suit couldn't mask that roguish streak; his speech was as crude as ever.
The person being mocked by Gu Suizhou looked rather unsightly, and even after the meeting adjourned, he continued to glare fiercely at Gu Suizhou’s retreating back.
Shen Feizhi took it all in, then turned off the light screen and stepped out to wait for him.

The board of directors consisted of both young and old members, so it was only natural for their ideas and viewpoints to clash. With a crowd of people packed together, it was nothing but one argument after another. Gu Suizhou was nursing a headache when he spotted Shen Feizhi by the elevator.
His eyes lit up instantly. Completely ignoring a woman trying to strike up a conversation with him, he trotted over.
"Have you been waiting long?"
"Just arrived." Shen Feizhi pressed the elevator button for him. "I'm here to pick you up from work."
The mirth in Gu Suizhou’s eyes deepened as he addressed the silent crowd. "Take the next elevator."
With that, he swiftly ushered Shen Feizhi inside and closed the doors.
The group: "…"
Was Young President Gu under some kind of spell?
Gu Suizhou was naturally talkative, and as soon as he saw Shen Feizhi, his complaints about work poured out like an overflowing bucket.
Shen Feizhi listened quietly. When Gu Suizhou finally took a breather, he asked, "President Xu—is that the one who was asking you for investment funds during the meeting?"
