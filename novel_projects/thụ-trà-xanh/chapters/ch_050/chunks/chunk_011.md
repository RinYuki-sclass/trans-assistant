Thinking back to his own unsolicited confessions, he felt a wave of regret.
He’d truly lost the battle and the war.
Shen Feizhi had used a basic attack, and he’d countered by blowing his ultimate.
"I really did have Fluke before, but wasn't I raising him alone? I was afraid you wouldn't accept him, so I took a roundabout way to make you feel some responsibility for him." He didn't dare bullshit anymore, explaining himself honestly.

After finishing, he muttered under his breath, "And to make you feel some responsibility for me while we're at it."
Shen Feizhi caught that last part too. His pace faltered, and he said bluntly, "Gu Suizhou, don't you think you're too clingy?"
As soon as those words left his mouth, Gu Suizhou panicked. "You think I'm annoying?"
"No, I just think," Shen Feizhi struggled to find a more tactful way to put it, "you should perhaps look for the underlying cause. It's not a good thing to be too dependent on one person. You—"
"Brother Shen," Gu Suizhou’s smile faded as he interrupted him for the first time. "Indulge me for another half a month. Once we go back, I’ll change. All right?"
Shen Feizhi looked him straight in the eye, silently demanding a reason.
Gu Suizhou was the first to look away. He lowered his head and boldly pulled him into an embrace. "Don’t ask, Bro. I admit I’ve been a bit off lately. But you were gone for so long… every day was an agonizing struggle for me."
"Don’t they say online that if you eat your fill of something you crave all at once, you won't want it anymore? By that same logic, if you just make it up to me for a week and let me have my fill, I’ll change after that. Okay?"
Shen Feizhi had actually suspected this might be the reason before. Hearing the explanation now, he felt a sense of relief instead.
When he didn't answer immediately, Gu Suizhou asked, "Am I putting you in a difficult position?"
"Then… am I troubling you?"
"…Not that, either."
"I’ll be as good as my word."
Shen Feizhi had truly met his match in him.
"…Fine. Let it be."
