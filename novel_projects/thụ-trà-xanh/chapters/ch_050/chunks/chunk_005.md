Shen Feizhi opened his mouth, struggling to force out those three words.
"Fluke"—he really didn't want to say that name out loud.
That day, when Gu Suizhou had smugly introduced the dog’s name to him, he’d almost wanted to rip the man’s mouth shut.
But out of respect for another person's freedom to name their pet, he had patiently asked, "Why that name?"
Gu Suizhou had replied, "The fact that he ran into you and then got adopted by me—isn't that just a massive stroke of dog-shit luck?"
Shen Feizhi had turned and walked away immediately.
Fortunately, Leapton had no reservations about the name. He called out "Fluke" over and over in a crisp, clear voice without the slightest hesitation.
There was a street food market below Xu Zhong’s apartment. Leapton was starving, so he stayed downstairs to eat while waiting for Shen Feizhi to head up and drop off the dog alone.
Xu Zhong greeted him warmly. "It just so happens that Fluke is due for his shots today. You’ve brought him at the perfect time."
"Yeah." Shen Feizhi leaned casually against the doorway, watching Xu Zhong put the muzzle on Fluke. While he waited, he picked up the diagnostic records sitting on the cabinet to look them over.
Since he intended to keep the dog long-term, Gu Suizhou had signed Fluke up for an annual membership at the pet hospital. The records detailed every medication used for each injection and the frequency of the shots.
Every pet had a certain number of mandatory vaccinations. On Fluke’s chart, only the last two remained.
…What kind of dog was healthy enough to finish almost an entire course of shots in a single month?

Excluding the possibility of genetic mutation, it meant this dog had started its vaccinations a long time ago.
Shen Feizhi used his smartphone to scan the date the medical record was opened.
—December 28, 2023.
He stared at it for a moment, then suddenly let out a low chuckle.
Hearing it, Xu Zhong, who was currently buckling the dog’s leash, felt an inexplicable chill run down his spine.
Why did it feel like… his Young President Gu was about to be in deep trouble?
