Shen Feizhi sighed. "We’ll find a way eventually."
Blue Star might not have a solution, but the Main Star certainly would.
In any case, Xu Zhong’s method was just a guess and wasn't absolutely safe. Rather than gambling on probabilities, it was better to let C2 think of something.
He was lost in thought, weighing various possibilities and methods, when he suddenly felt a hand grip his ankle, sliding upward.
“Watch your hand. Behave yourself.”
Gu Suizhou sat at the edge of the bed, his fingers encircling Shen Feizhi's ankle with nimble, forceful restraint. “Brother, did you just finish bathing? Your legs are so cold.”
“Let go.” Shen Feizhi’s tone was unfriendly; the touch sent a shiver racing up his spine.
Gu Suizhou, of course, had no intention of listening. He used the hold on the other's leg to pull himself up.
That dexterous hand brushed against the skin of his leg, sliding directly past the hem of his bathrobe and over his thigh before finally, reluctantly, moving away.
“It seems Brother Shen has lived quite well these past few years. Your skin is so well-kept.”

Shen Feizhi surely had no idea how beautiful his face looked right now.
There was a knock on the bedroom door.
“I heard a noise in here. Are you alright?”
Subconsciously, Shen Feizhi loosened his grip, intending to answer the door.
Before he could take a step back, his lower back was hooked. Gu Suizhou forcibly locked him in an embrace, pressing a finger against his lips. “Shh—don’t say a word, Brother Shen. We’re still in the middle of an affair, after all.”

Shen Feizhi slapped his hand away. "Enough."
