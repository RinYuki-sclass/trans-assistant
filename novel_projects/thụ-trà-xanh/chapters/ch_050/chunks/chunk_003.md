"I’m not lying." Gu Suizhou switched to grabbing both of his arms, looking innocent. "On the surface, at least, he and I are still in a relationship. If he sees you in my room, wouldn't our breakup be blamed on me?"
Shen Feizhi wasn't about to be led in circles by him. "We didn't do anything. How could it be your fault?"
"Brother Shen, you still don't understand men well enough." Gu Suizhou sighed, adopting the air of someone who’d seen it all as he rested his head on Shen Feizhi’s shoulder. "They're always so unreasonable."
He said it as if the two of them weren't men themselves.
But wanting to avoid unnecessary trouble, Shen Feizhi didn't insist on speaking up again.
Where Shen Feizhi couldn't see, a hint of a smile appeared in Gu Suizhou's eyes as he said loudly, "It's fine. You go back and get some sleep."

He applied a bit of force with his hands. Caught off guard, Shen Feizhi took an unsteady step forward, his knee accidentally bumping into something.
Gu Suizhou let out a sharp gasp of pain.
Shen Feizhi froze, staring at him in disbelief. "You…"
"Me what?" Gu Suizhou sounded helpless. "I’m a normal man, too."
Shen Feizhi was still in a state of shock, unable to wrap his head around it. "A normal man gets a reaction from being kicked?"
He really was a masochist, wasn't he?!
Seeing his expression, Gu Suizhou knew he had been misunderstood. His mouth twitched; for a moment, he was at a loss for words.
He couldn't exactly say he’d had a reaction since the moment Shen Feizhi walked in, could he? If he did, he might as well kiss his life goodbye.
He cleared his throat. "Maybe… my pseudo-heat is coming up soon."
At the mention of this, Shen Feizhi’s focus shifted. "We’re going to the hospital tomorrow."
"It’s fine, I went a few days ago. I’ll have Uncle Zhong send you the report later." Gu Suizhou thought to himself that this was torture; lying really did come with a price. "Brother Shen, since I’m free the next few days, why don't you come out and have some fun with me?"
Hearing the footsteps outside fade into the distance, Shen Feizhi finally pushed him away. He grabbed a lollipop from the bedside table and unwrapped it. "Where do you want to go?"
"Let's go to the beach," Gu Suizhou said with a smile. "The ocean on Blue Star is ugly. I haven't seen a blue sea in almost five years."
