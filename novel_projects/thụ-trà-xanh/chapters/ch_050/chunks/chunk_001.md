Chapter 50
Shen Feizhi had finally figured it out. All that talk about PUA was complete bullshit. This kid had been full of bad intentions from the very start; God only knew what kind of scheme he was cooking up.
Now that his heart had settled back into his chest, he finally had the energy to settle scores.
Over the past week, the way the two of them had interacted had been a bit strange—like a bull's head grafted onto a horse's tail, it felt wrong no matter how you looked at it.
It wasn't until this moment, after he kicked Gu Suizhou onto the bed, that he found that familiar rhythm again: "If I go one day without beating you, you just have to start acting out, don't you?"
Gu Suizhou was actually amused by the kick. It took quite an effort to press down the corners of his mouth before he said piteously, "Then what was I supposed to do? Didn't I cooperate with your arrangements just because I wanted Brother Shen to feel at ease?"
"He was so mean to me, bullying me every day and making me eat things I don't like. You saw it just now, right? I told him so many times what I like to eat, but he just craves my body and my face—he doesn't care about me at all!"
His ability to play the victim was master-class, instinctive and effortless; even someone like Shen Feizhi couldn't spot any flaws.
Recalling the awkward, distant interaction between the two earlier, he actually believed him.
He sighed, stepped forward, and rubbed the boy’s head. "I'm sorry. I was inconsiderate and didn't arrange things properly this time."
Gu Suizhou was the type to take an inch and go a mile; he wailed even louder, "Brother Shen, do you have any idea how miserable I’ve been this past month? I never want to date an Omega again for the rest of my life!"
No way. He didn't actually develop PTSD from this, did he?!
Shen Feizhi grew a bit anxious. "I think you should calm down. This kind of thing…"
Gu Suizhou’s face fell. "Are you saying you want me to go through it again?"
