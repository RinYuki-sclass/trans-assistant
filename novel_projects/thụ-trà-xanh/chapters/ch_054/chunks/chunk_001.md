Chapter 54
The next morning, the scents of coffee and matcha remained intertwined in the room, lingering with an unexpected harmony.
Shen Feizhi was not a man of excessive carnal desires. Having indulged once, he refused to let the other man back into the bedroom. After injecting two more doses of suppressants, he had managed to regain some semblance of stability.
While the pheromones were under control, his physical state remained unstable; the doctor suggested he stay home for another two days.
He could afford to laze around at home, but Gu Suizhou could not. Scores of people at both the company and the research institute were waiting for him to return to work.
Leaning against the headboard and scrolling through his light computer, Shen Feizhi didn’t even spare him a glance. "Work hard."
Gu Suizhou gritted his teeth resentfully, his jaw still feeling a bit sore.
They had done everything they should and shouldn't have, yet after it was over, Shen Feizhi acted as if nothing had happened, treating him exactly the same as before. This wasn't right!
He snatched the other man's light screen away. "That’s it? Nothing else to say?"
"What else do you want to hear?" Shen Feizhi asked tonelessly. "Do you need me to take responsibility for you?"
He had turned the tables. Gu Suizhou closed his eyes and smirked. "And shouldn't you?"
"First of all, we didn't go all the way. You were the one who insisted on provoking me, and I simply made you pay a small price," Shen Feizhi said heartlessly. "Second, I don't believe there will be a next time."
Gu Suizhou blinked, looking pitiful and wronged. "Why won't there be a next time?"
"Because your technique is terrible, and I have no desire for a second experience." Shen Feizhi spoke the bluntest words with the most indifferent expression. "I can write yesterday off as you losing your head because you were unwell. Don't cause trouble like that again."
Gu Suizhou took a deep breath and gave an understanding response. "Fine."

Shen Feizhi listened to the footsteps fading in the hallway before tossing aside the book he hadn't managed to read a single word of. Frowning, he rubbed his temples.
It was one thing for Gu Suizhou to be senseless yesterday, but how could he have gotten carried away and joined in on the madness?
Now things were a mess—pure brotherhood had turned into a one-night stand, making for quite the difficult situation to resolve.
