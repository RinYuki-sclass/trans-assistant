He didn't deny that his feelings for Gu Suizhou were complicated; it could be considered a form of liking, though not quite love. At the very least, he didn't dislike the other man’s advances. If they were both normal people, perhaps trying out a relationship wouldn't be out of the question.
But the key issue was.
Neither of them was normal.
Gu Suizhou was an Alpha; he shouldn't be this dependent on him.
Moreover, the man had clearly been acting abnormal yesterday. Rather than romance, Shen Feizhi was more inclined to believe that some external factor had influenced Gu Suizhou, triggering a desire akin to "possessiveness."
Agitated, Shen Feizhi lit a cigarette.
Before he could even take a drag, the bedroom door was flung open again with a loud bang .
He was nearly burned by the cherry. "Why are you back?"
Expressionless, Gu Suizhou snatched the cigarette and snuffed it out without a word. He produced a pair of exquisite handcuffs and, under Shen Feizhi’s bewildered gaze, locked the man's left hand to the handle of the nightstand. "The caregiver will bring Lucky Dog over in a bit to keep you company. Wait for me to come back. You aren't allowed to leave."
Then, he left as abruptly as he had arrived.
