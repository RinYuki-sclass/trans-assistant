Xu Zhong and Leapton rushed to the address only to find the kitchen filled with smoke.
The two men exchanged glances, speechless.
"Gu Suizhou, are you trying to kill my Mr. Shen?" Leapton asked, leaning against the kitchen doorway with a complex expression as he watched Gu Suizhou dump another pot of sludge-like batter into the trash—his third attempt.
"If you really can't cook, just have the robotic maid make him something. There's no need to torture yourself like this."
"You think I haven't tried that?" Gu Suizhou snapped, his face darkening. "That old grump won't eat it!"
He had initially instructed the AI to prepare the meal, but Shen Feizhi hadn't even put a noodle in his mouth before disdainfully setting the plate on the nightstand. "I pay you to operate machines, not have them do everything for you!"
Gu Suizhou had nearly smeared the noodles across his face in fury.

Fortunately, his rationality prevailed. He knew he was to blame for Shen Feizhi's drunken state tonight and had to swallow his anger, retreating sheepishly to the kitchen.
"Perfect timing," he exhaled heavily, shoving the apron into Leapton's hands. "Your Mr. Shen is holed up inside, half-dead from his academic pursuits, and hasn't eaten dinner yet. Here's your chance to impress him."
Gu Suizhou had already briefed them on the situation via his smartphone, and Leapton immediately understood.
He rolled up his sleeves. "Don't worry! Leave it to me! Tonight, I'll make Mr. Shen fall head over heels for my apron!"
Gu Suizhou wanted to wish him luck, but the words caught in his throat. For some reason, he swallowed them back.
"Oh," he replied, and returned to the living room with Xu Zhong.
"Uncle Zhong," he said, handing over an electronic file, "here's the detailed layout of the Shen family's old mansion and all the surveillance feeds I could access."
"Understood, Young Master," Xu Zhong replied, his face still covered by a thick mask and black-framed glasses even indoors, leaving only a pair of murky, small eyes visible. "I'll have a detailed analysis map for you within three days."
"Thank you for your hard work," Gu Suizhou said, pinching the bridge of his nose wearily.
Neither of them spoke again.
They sat on opposite ends of the sofa, the silence between them unnervingly heavy.
After a long while, Gu Suizhou suddenly picked up the black suit he'd worn earlier that evening. He carefully pulled out a handkerchief-wrapped object from the inner pocket and unwrapped it.
