On his 22nd birthday, Fang Yushi, the prince of nightlife, decided to represent his family in a strategic marriage alliance.
His betrothed, Liang Jimu, was a university professor—a humorless, old-fashioned stick-in-the-mud, the quintessential aloof intellectual.
Fully aware of the immense benefits this union would bring his family, Fang Yushi clicked his tongue audibly while reviewing Liang Jimu's profile.

To maintain their business ties, Fang Yushi meticulously maintained his image after the wedding. No matter how wild he got outside, he always returned home looking like a clean-cut, innocent college boy.
They treated each other with formal respect, their marriage a refined affair between cultured individuals.
