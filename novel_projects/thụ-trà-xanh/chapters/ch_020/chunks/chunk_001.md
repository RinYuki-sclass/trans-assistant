Chapter 20
After his initial attempts to weasel out of it, Gu Suizhou had worried Shen Feizhi might impulsively force himself on him.
But now he truly understood: his concerns had been utterly unfounded.
Shen Feizhi paid no heed to Gu Suizhou's evasive tactics, acting like an ascetic monk, detached from worldly desires and untainted by earthly concerns.
"What business is it of yours how I use you? Are you defying your benefactor now? Even if I ordered you to empty a toilet, could you refuse?"
Gu Suizhou: "……"
Fine, you win.
Once Shen Feizhi had finally finished giving his instructions, Gu Suizhou immediately sent location messages to Xu Zhong and Leapton.
"Then go take a shower first. I'll get a couple of helpers to come cook noodles for you. How's that?"
"Helpers? Is this some kind of forced-purchase scheme?"
"I'll cover their expenses myself—no extra charge."
"That works." Shen Feizhi glanced at his watch. "But I'm not showering now. I need to study."
It was study time.
Knowledge was the only true currency that allowed a person to stand tall anywhere!
Gu Suizhou's gaze gradually shifted from numb indifference to utter horror.
