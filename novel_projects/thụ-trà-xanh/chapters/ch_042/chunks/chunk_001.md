Chapter 42
Shen Feizhi hesitated for a long time, but in the end, he didn't ruin the mood by telling the other man that it wasn't actually his birthday.
In truth, he didn't even know when his birthday was; he had no memory of such things.
He accepted Gu Suizhou’s sentiment, but he did not accept the chocolate factory.
Gu Suizhou had given him the ownership contracts and all the necessary paperwork, but Shen Feizhi never took them to be notarized.
In the days that followed, Gu Suizhou was incredibly busy—so busy his feet barely touched the ground, and he wouldn't return home for days at a time.
Having accepted the "birthday gift," Shen Feizhi felt he had to settle the debt before leaving. Thus, he quietly began managing the inheritance left behind by Gu Lang and Lin Zizhuo.
It was a massive sum of money, an amount most people couldn't spend in a lifetime even if they indulged in every luxury imaginable.
He managed these funds expertly, even utilizing them flexibly to earn a profit. By the time he was ready to return it all to Gu Suizhou, the amount had nearly doubled.
As for the Shen family, the court was proceeding through the case methodically.
By the time the final judgment was handed down, it was already December. Shen Feizhi finally received word that he could meet with Elder Master Shen.
He stood outside the detention center, a sudden sense of trance washing over him.
Time had passed so quickly; an entire year had already gone by.
If not for his deteriorating health constantly reminding him of his displacement, he might have almost integrated into this world completely.
Shen Feizhi rubbed his frozen, stiff hands and stepped through the main gates.

The atmosphere inside the detention center was oppressive—though that was to be expected; it was impossible for a place filled with criminals to be lively.
A guard led him to the innermost glass partition. The once-formidable Elder Master Shen now looked like any other ordinary old man, sitting there lifelessly, meeting his gaze.
Shen Feizhi felt no particular sentiment or emotional upheaval. He sat down calmly and cut straight to the chase: "In your basement back then, besides Professor Lin Zizhuo and his wife, who else did you have locked up?"
Elder Shen seemed a bit dazed. "You came to see me, and you have nothing else to say but this?"
