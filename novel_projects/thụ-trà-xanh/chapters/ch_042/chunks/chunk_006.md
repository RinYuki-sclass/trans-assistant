When the magnetic card was pulled out, it dropped to the floor.
Gu Suizhou looked down, only now realizing his hands were shaking.
He opened his mouth, but his throat felt impossibly parched.
The envelope Xu Zhong had brought was still lying nearby. What was inside? Was it that medicine? Or chocolate?
A moment ago, Gu Suizhou hadn’t wanted to open it, but now he was desperate. He desperately needed Shen Feizhi to tell him that he hadn't harmed him.
But the reality disappointed him.
Inside the envelope, there was nothing but a stack of electronic data cards and a single information magnetic card.
Facing this magnetic card, Gu Suizhou was suddenly seized by a sense of dread.
It felt as if… something was about to be lost forever.
With fumbling hands, he slotted the magnetic card into the reader.
Read at Novelib.com
Inside was a string of data and a voice recording from Shen Feizhi.
"Gu Suizhou."
the man’s voice was lower than usual. Transmitted through the electronic device, it lost some of its usual coldness and gained a low, husky magnetism.
"This is the data Lin Sui gathered when he ran a diagnostic on Lu Qian back at the speakeasy. I don’t know as much about medicine as he does, but I thought these few values he checked might be helpful to you, so I noted them down."
Gu Suizhou stared blankly at the data, feeling as if he could hear something shattering.
The data Lin Sui had checked was identical to what was monitored during his own routine physical exams.
Even the fluctuations were the same.
The last sliver of hope vanished. The final vial of medicine had indeed been injected into his body.
Gu Suizhou had no memory of being injected with an unknown drug.
