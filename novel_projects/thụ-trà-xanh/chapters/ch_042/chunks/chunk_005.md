His slender fingers toyed with the magnetic card, his expression gloomy.
Before today, he never knew he was the type of person who liked to run away.
In truth, the evidence was already clear. The Shen family’s butler said he had seen the Second Young Master Shen in the basement; Shen Feizhi’s activity records in the surveillance footage from back then reflected the same—during the period when Lin Zizhuo and Gu Lang were imprisoned, he seemed to have vanished entirely.
So, it was self-evident who the other person locked in that basement was.
He suddenly thought of that man. If it were him, what would he do now?
Gu Suizhou’s lips quirked downward slightly. Without further hesitation, he slotted the magnetic card into the quantum card reader.
The cost and technical difficulty of decrypting video and audio were far too high, so what Gu Lang had left for him was a piece of encrypted text.
【Xiao Zhou, it’s been a long time. Since Mom and Dad aren't around, have you been taking good care of yourself?】
Gu Suizhou stared at those two lines for a long while before continuing to read.
The preceding section was filled with the trivial ramblings of Lin Zizhuo and Gu Lang. Gu Suizhou read every word with solemn focus, only reaching the crux of the message near the end.
【Son, I regret that we cannot leave you anything useful; we might even have to destroy it all. You must remember that what you possess the power to create, you may not necessarily be able to control. Please, at all costs, do not challenge human nature, and do not attempt to defy the laws of nature. This is a lesson your father and I have already learned.】
【If you are still in the dark about the truth by the time you receive this card, I can only tell you this: go find the child from the Shen family—his name is Shen Feizhi. I was lucky enough to steal a defective serum, and I gave that final dose to him.】
【He is a good child. I cannot guarantee what he will become in the future, but at least while we were still alive, he was a child worthy of trust.】
The truth was not unexpected.
Gu Suizhou thought to himself: You used to say Lin Sui was a good kid, too.
