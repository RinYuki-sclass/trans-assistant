Shen Feizhi paused. "I’m afraid right now isn't possible."
Gu Suizhou’s voice took on a pleading tone. "Brother Shen, it’s my birthday today. Let me come find you, okay?"
Shen Feizhi parted his lips slightly.
He hadn’t known it was Gu Suizhou’s birthday today. If he had known in advance…
He still wouldn't have stayed, but he would have prepared a gift beforehand.
Unfortunately, there were no "ifs" in reality.
He could only say, "I’m sorry, I can’t see you."
The person on the other end seemed to sense something, his voice rising: "Shen Feizhi, where are you?"
Watching the countdown enter its final five minutes, Shen Feizhi softened his voice for once. "Gu Suizhou, I have to leave for a while. If we get the chance to meet again, we'll talk then, okay?"
"No, I won't allow it!" There was a series of clattering sounds from the other end, as if something had been knocked over. "Where are you? I'm coming to find you right now!"
"You can't come here." Shen Feizhi sighed, finally realizing what he had forgotten before leaving.
He had forgotten to say goodbye.
He had always left the moment a mission was complete; he didn't have the habit of saying goodbye. It was the one flaw in his otherwise meticulous planning.
Gu Suizhou’s voice was trembling. "Shen Feizhi!"

Gu Suizhou didn’t understand why, but his body reacted to Shen Feizhi’s words with instinctual muscle memory. If he said it, Gu Suizhou did it.
"…I’m there."
Shen Feizhi said, "Look up."
Gu Suizhou tilted his neck back, just in time to stop a single tear from falling.
