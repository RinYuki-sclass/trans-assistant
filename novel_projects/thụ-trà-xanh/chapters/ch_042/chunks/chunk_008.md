He was clearly someone with a high threshold for tears, but at this moment, his defenses easily crumbled.
“Brother Shen…”
Xu Zhong had the sense to retreat from the room.
Shen Feizhi hadn’t expected Gu Suizhou to call him at a time like this.
Glancing at the remaining half-hour of his journey, he gave a low "Mm" and asked, "What is it?"
"Brother Shen," Gu Suizhou whispered, "if there were someone who was very good to you, but you knew that in the past, they might have done something to hurt you… what would you do?"
The moment the question was asked, Shen Feizhi understood.
He had already vaguely guessed that the other person locked in the basement was the original "Shen Feizhi."
If it were before, he could have simply said, "That wasn't me," but now, looking at the photo of the woman in his hand, the words wouldn't come out.
There was a long silence over the earpiece.
Neither of them spoke again.
An unknown amount of time passed before Shen Feizhi finally heard his own voice, sounding rough and dry. "…I think that person, at least for now, would absolutely never harm me."
Regrettably, he didn’t know what the "self" of the past had actually done, so he couldn't give Gu Suizhou the answer he truly wanted.
This time, even the sound of breathing vanished on the other end.
Shen Feizhi was unusually patient. He stood up and leaned against the door, watching the fragments of space rock streaking past the shuttle’s window.
