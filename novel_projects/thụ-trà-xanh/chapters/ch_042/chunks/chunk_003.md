But it wasn’t her beauty that shocked him; it was that face—one that was almost an exact replica of his own.
With his head bowed, Elder Shen couldn't see his expression as he remarked, "You don't look a bit like your father, but you're the spitting image of your mother."
Indeed, they were alike.
Especially the eyes, the mouth, and the mole on the tip of the nose—it was as if they had been copy-pasted.
Shen Feizhi didn’t even know how he managed to walk out of there.
He was in a total daze.
How could they look so much alike?

He clearly wasn't the original owner of this body, so why did he look so much like the original owner's mother?
Had the worlds automatically merged?
Shen Feizhi hardly dared to entertain an even more absurd possibility.
Fortunately, at that moment, a communication from Ritter caught his attention.
"Baby Shen! The fuel you developed has passed ten consecutive experiments! It can be officially launched on the market now!"
Shen Feizhi suddenly snapped back to his senses, his fingers tightening around his smartphone. "I understand… Professor Ritter, could I trouble you to send the fuel to me? I'll send you the address in a moment."
Ritter was slightly puzzled. "The test site is right downstairs from W.N. Where else would you want it sent?"
Shen Feizhi skirted the question. "To a place I'm familiar with. If you would, please."
Ritter was very fond of him and generally granted his requests. Seeing that the address was a derelict test site, the professor assumed he was looking for an open outdoor space and readily agreed.
A short while later, Shen Feizhi arrived at the location himself.
However, the wasteland was not as empty as Ritter had imagined. It was filled with small engines, and at the very center sat a high-spec, high-density synthetic shuttle.
This was a private passenger shuttle he had purchased a few months ago and modified; its functional specifications were now on par with military grade.
