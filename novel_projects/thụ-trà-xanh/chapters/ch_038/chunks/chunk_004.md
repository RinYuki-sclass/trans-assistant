Smoke billowed relentlessly from the windows of the high-rise, the flames gradually spreading upward from the first floor.
Shen Feizhi rested his head against his hand, a sense of eerie familiarity washing over him.
Gu Suizhou quickly confirmed his intuition.
"That's Lin Sui's place!"
Shen Feizhi froze. "What?"
Gu Suizhou's lips pressed into a thin, grim line. "So he's dropping the act now?"
Ten years ago, a massive fire at W.N had burned everything to ashes.
Now, Lin Sui was clearly mimicking that pattern, intending to erase all traces of his dirty deeds with a single blaze. It was a blatant declaration to the two men: So what if I did it? You have no proof.
Bloodshot veins spiderwebbed across Gu Suizhou's eyes as he abruptly switched the car to manual control.
Shen Feizhi watched his movements, his muscles tensing. "Do you even know how to drive?"
"No license," Gu Suizhou growled, "but I'm close enough."
If he'd said that to anyone else, they'd probably be scared out of their minds right now.
Shen Feizhi sighed, remaining firmly seated and closing the car window. "This is the highway," he said calmly. "Don't be reckless."
The speedometer had already soared past 400 kilometers per hour, and he tacitly permitted this reckless behavior from his companion.
