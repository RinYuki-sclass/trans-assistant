If they were lucky, they might even risk a quick run through the burning building to retrieve some useful items.
Shen Feizhi frowned, resting his hand on his knee, his peripheral vision fixed on the burning skyscraper. His fingers tapped a rhythmic pattern.
Something feels off.
The car's speed continued to climb.
Gu Suizhou drove as if he had nothing to lose, completely disregarding the difficulty of braking and the physical strain on his body. The knuckles of his hands, gripping the steering wheel, turned white.
The only restraint keeping him from pushing the car beyond its limits was the thought of the person in the back seat.
The turn signal sign was just ahead. He moved his little finger, preparing to activate the blinker.
But just then, the seatback suddenly reclined. Gu Suizhou's back lost support, and he nearly lost his grip on the steering wheel.
His heart clenched. He immediately tried to turn around and grab the person behind him to prevent injury if the car flipped.
But before he could fully turn, a hand brushed past his face from behind and gripped the steering wheel.
Shen Feizhi knelt on one knee on the overturned seat back, leaning over to wrap his arms around Gu Suizhou from behind. He tightened the seatbelt further and pressed down hard on Gu Suizhou's hand still gripping the joystick with his other hand. "Go straight. Don't change course. Find Lu Qian."
The distinctive aroma of Shen Feizhi's bitter coffee breath washed over Gu Suizhou. Momentarily stunned, his back snapped ramrod straight as if burned. "What's the point of finding Lu Qian now?! Over there…"
"Quiet." Shen Feizhi's face was so close Gu Suizhou could feel his warm breath on his earlobe. "Do you trust me?"
Gu Suizhou fell silent, momentarily speechless.

They were only two minutes away from the fork in the road. Turning now risked an accident.
Gu Suizhou opened his mouth. "What if I choose to turn?"
Shen Feizhi replied casually, "Then we'll turn."
"Whether it's possible isn't your concern. If you choose to turn, I'll even take us back in reverse if we've already passed the turn," Shen Feizhi said. "The only thing you need to decide now is whether you trust me or not."
