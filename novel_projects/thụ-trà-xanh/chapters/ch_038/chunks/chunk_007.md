W.N Second Clinical Trial: Builds upon the first trial; temporarily suppresses further development of glands showing signs of differentiation; side effects include a high risk of permanent tissue damage.
W.N.'s seventy-third clinical trial, building on all previous versions, introduced a new capability: reversing the development of glands showing clear signs of differentiation. However, the side effects worsened, causing permanent damage to the glands and leading to blockages.
As Gu Suizhou recalled this, he suddenly remembered Shen Feizhi mentioning earlier that Lu Qian, the Lu family's young mistress, had a glandular defect—apparently, a blockage preventing proper drainage.
He exclaimed in shock, "You mean Lu Qian has it too?!"
"It was just a suspicion before, but now I'm almost certain," Shen Feizhi said, tugging at his cuff. "Help me roll up my sleeves, then sit down and buckle your seatbelt."

While contacting Xu Zhong to alert the Traffic Bureau to leave them alone, Gu Suizhou helped Shen Feizhi roll up his sleeves.
Operating with one hand, he kept inadvertently brushing against Shen Feizhi's skin.
Shen Feizhi grew irritated by the constant contact. "Enough! Just leave it."
This guy is more trouble than he's worth.
Having been snapped at for no reason, Gu Suizhou felt a little wronged. He stubbornly finished rolling up both sleeves neatly. "Don't be mad, Brother Shen. Next time, I'll think before I act—logic over emotion!"
Shen Feizhi didn't follow his lead. "I didn't tell you to prioritize reason over emotion. There's nothing wrong with embracing your human qualities."
He had never believed that emotionality was a flaw.
On the contrary, it was precisely because humans possessed these seemingly weak emotions that they had become a unique existence in the universe.
The moment a person could freely express their emotions without restraint, that was the truest form of happiness—a testament to a life lived freely, without constraints or reservations.
Gu Suizhou was still young. He could become such a person in time, with no need to emulate Shen Feizhi.
Gu Suizhou, unaware of these thoughts, chuckled and retorted, "You're talking like you're not human yourself, Brother Shen."
Shen Feizhi fell silent.
Am I even human anymore?
