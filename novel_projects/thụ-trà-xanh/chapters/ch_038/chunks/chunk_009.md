He understood what Shen Feizhi meant.
With this reckless maneuver, their car risked being crushed between the pursuers. In such a situation, preserving one person's safety was the only option—a choice that lay entirely with the driver.
It was clear Shen Feizhi had chosen to protect him.
While Shen Feizhi only had his own abilities to rely on, Gu Suizhou had powerful allies backing him: Xu Zhong, Leapton… all capable of providing crucial support and resources for their future actions.
This was the most rational choice under the circumstances.
Just as they had anticipated, the moment Shen Feizhi made his move, the pursuers sensed his intentions. Abandoning all pretense of concealment, they gradually closed in.
Shen Feizhi immediately swerved to the far right, creating ample space for Gu Suizhou to escape safely.
The wide avenue was nearly deserted, likely due to the Shen family's abuse of their influence.
Dozens of cars relentlessly pursued the silver-gray vehicle, closing in step by step. Yet the silver-gray car stubbornly refused to slow down, even ramming violently into the safety barrier and smashing the lead blue sports car aside!
The roar of engines mingled with the screech of tires and the smell of burning rubber. What should have been a routine interception had, thanks to the protagonists themselves, devolved into a tense life-or-death standoff.
Shen Feizhi tightened his grip on the wheel as he stared at the synthetic gold rear of the car ahead.
He carefully shifted lanes, focusing his vision straight ahead to brace for the impending impact from the passenger side.
Never one to hesitate, Shen Feizhi stomped on the accelerator the moment he had aligned his position.

The man in the passenger seat suddenly grabbed the other side of the steering wheel and twisted it violently!
Shen Feizhi, rarely caught off guard, exclaimed, "Gu Suizhou!"
With a screech of tires against asphalt, the silver-gray car swerved into a graceful arc, completely reversing its direction.
Now driving against traffic, the car hurtled backward. The most dangerous position had shifted from the driver's seat to the passenger seat.
Gu Suizhou gripped Shen Feizhi's hand, his eyes sparkling with amusement as he apologized. "Didn't Brother Shen say I don't need to change? That letting emotions override reason isn't always a bad thing?"
