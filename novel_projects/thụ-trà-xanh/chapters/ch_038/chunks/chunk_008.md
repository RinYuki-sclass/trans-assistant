His fingers tapped the steering wheel. "Buckle up. I won't say it a third time."
Gu Suizhou raised an eyebrow, withdrew his hand, and settled back into his seat. Just as he was about to put on some relaxing music to ease Shen Feizhi's mood, a sudden flash of headlights in his peripheral vision made his pupils constrict sharply. "Brother Shen!"
Shen Feizhi glanced in the rearview mirror, his jaw clenching tightly. "They're moving fast."
"That's a Shen family car," Gu Suizhou said, having thoroughly investigated the Shen family and memorized all their vehicles and purchases. "Damn it, how did they get the news so fast?"
"Don't let me hear you curse again," Shen Feizhi warned. He had a vague hunch but kept it to himself for now, tilting his chin forward. "What about that one ahead?"
Now, not only was the silver car behind them tailgating them, but a discreet white car had suddenly appeared ahead.
"That's a Shen family car too."
Gu Suizhou's head began to throb as he quickly scanned their surroundings. "Right now, there are two cars ahead, three behind, and one to the left. These are all confirmed to be Shen family, but we can't rule out the possibility of others being involved."
Conservatively estimating about a dozen cars surrounding them, they were completely boxed in.
Shen Feizhi hadn't expected to experience the thrill of an apocalyptic scenario in an ordinary interstellar setting.
He suddenly smirked, a hint of amusement in his eyes. "I recently made a small modification to this car. The front end can withstand impacts with a coefficient of around 500. Crashing through two cars at 300 kilometers per hour shouldn't be a problem."
"Gu Suizhou, do you think I should stop and surrender now, or just ram straight through and force a path?"
Gu Suizhou's eyes flickered. He cautiously replied, "Ramming through might not be the best idea?"
Shen Feizhi pressed, "The truth?"
"A lie," Gu Suizhou confessed, finally unable to suppress a muffled laugh. "Brother Shen, smash them!"
