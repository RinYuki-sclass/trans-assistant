Shen Feizhi's eyes widened, as if struggling to grasp the meaning behind those words.
Gu Suizhou's fox-like eyes, brimming with deliberate, ambiguous flirtation, locked directly with Shen Feizhi's deep, black gaze.
"At our current speed, we'll collide with that car in two minutes. We'll likely be forced to the edge and into the turn. It's right by the sea—the perfect spot to jump."
"Big Brother, there's no turning back now. You have two choices: either join me in mutual suicide or escape and wait for me to create a miracle."
Not long ago, it was Shen Feizhi who had posed the choice.
Now, their roles had abruptly reversed.
