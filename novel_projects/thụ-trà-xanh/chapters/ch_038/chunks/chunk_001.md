Chapter 38
After severe interrogation, Gu Suizhou finally confessed honestly to his methods and motives.
He had snuck back home while Shen Feizhi was at work, grabbed the clothes, and fled. Afraid of being discovered, he even replaced the missing item with a brand-new identical one.
Shen Feizhi's head buzzed. "Do you even realize what kind of behavior this is?"
Gu Suizhou nodded earnestly. "It's perverted."
Shen Feizhi: "…"
How could he say it so calmly, so clearly?
His face turned cold. "Knowing that, you still did it?"
Gu Suizhou thought for a moment, then said, "It's fine. I don't mind being a pervert."
Shen Feizhi: "……"
He desperately wanted to say, "But I do mind."
Gu Suizhou's mindset and behavior were becoming increasingly dangerous, crossing the line.
Could it be that I've been too kind to him lately, leading him to develop this unhealthy dependence? Shen Feizhi wondered, falling into self-reflection.
Seeing his silence, Gu Suizhou panicked and apologized profusely the entire way home, finally managing to soften Shen Feizhi's expression a little by the time they arrived.
Sitting in the car, Gu Suizhou pressed his face against the window, pleading pitifully, "Bro, you're really, really not mad anymore, right? I only resorted to such drastic measures because I missed you so much ."
