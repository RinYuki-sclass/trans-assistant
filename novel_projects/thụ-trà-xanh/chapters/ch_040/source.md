Shen Feizhi had only been teasing him; he didn't actually intend to make him cry.

He pulled away quickly, standing there with a glacial expression as he waited for him to continue his explanation, appearing utterly cold and detached.

Gu Suizhou had indeed known what Lin Sui was doing for a long time now.

The information he held was far more extensive than what he let on.

After listening to it all, Shen Feizhi suffered a rare bout of insomnia that night.

It wasn't that the story itself kept him awake, but rather a sense of doubt. He felt as if there was a specific point in everything Gu Suizhou said that didn't quite align, though he couldn't put his finger on exactly what it was.

The next day, he threw himself headlong into the pursuit of Lin Sui and the preparations for the W.N press conference. This busy streak lasted an entire week, until everything was finally finalized. He, Gu Suizhou, and Lu Qian sat together in the hospital ward to watch the live broadcast of the conference.

It was a public trial ten years overdue.

Lin Sui’s face appeared on the defendant's stand without any concealment. He stared blankly at the camera as he recounted his actions from years ago.

The day Lin Sui was taken from the orphanage by Lin Zizhuo and Gu Lang, the weather had been beautiful.

That day, Lin Sui felt he was likely the luckiest child in the world.

His adoptive parents treated him so well that they surpassed biological parents, giving him the best education and the finest living environment.

Though they were busy with work, they still celebrated his birthdays like any ordinary family and would take him to amusement parks during the holidays when he achieved good grades.

Lin Sui had thought this life would continue forever, until one day, Gu Lang placed Lin Sui's hand on her belly and told him gently that he was going to have a younger brother or sister.
