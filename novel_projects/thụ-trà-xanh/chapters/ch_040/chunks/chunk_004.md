And so, gradually, Lin Zhou grew up.
In truth, Lin Zizhuo and Gu Lang still celebrated Lin Sui’s birthdays, went shopping, strolled the streets, and visited amusement parks just as they always had. Aside from the sudden addition of a little sprout to the family, there was no difference at all.

Lin Sui also knew that the two of them were actually very conscious of the matter of fairness.
For instance, if the little sprout wanted an ice cream, Gu Lang and Lin Zizhuo would be sure to buy one for him as well; if the little sprout had a twenty-square-meter nursery, Lin Sui would have a forty-square-meter bedroom…
It could be said that not only was Lin Sui not neglected, he was actually held in even higher regard.
Both were meticulous people. The moment they realized a second child was on the way, they had already anticipated this issue and avoided the pitfalls skillfully. They were a pair of highly competent, gentle parents.
But Lin Sui didn't see it that way.
To him, their kindness felt like a sword suspended over his head.
Were Lin Zizhuo and Gu Lang doubling down on their affection now just so that when he reached adulthood, they could tell him they had fulfilled their duties and ask him to stop bothering them?
Sooner or later, they would abandon him for their biological son!
What Lin Sui wanted was to live happily with his parents forever, with no outsiders involved and no one else to disturb them.
This sense of crisis reached its peak the day Gu Suizhou was tested and found to have the potential to differentiate into a Top Tier Alpha.
With a biological son so outstanding, would they even notice an adopted son like him anymore?
It was also at that time that he happened to discover that Lin Zizhuo and Gu Lang’s research involved drugs meant to control the direction of differentiation.
Lin Sui felt as if the world were collapsing. It seemed obvious to him now: his adoptive parents despised "useless" Omegas; they weren't as indifferent toward the second gender differentiation as they pretended to be.
That weekend, Lin Sui emboldened himself for the first time and moved to kill Lin Zhou.
Taking advantage of the time Lin Zizhuo and Gu Lang were taking their afternoon nap, he carried his younger brother to the bathroom.
