But to Lin Sui’s ears, it was a rebuke.
In all the years since he had been adopted, this was the first time he had been criticized.
Lin Sui hated this younger brother even more.
He dreamed of getting rid of him.
It wasn't until he reached high school and presented as an Omega that the opportunity finally arose.
His pharmaceutical research won first prize in the Interstellar Youth competition, and the Shen family came looking for him.
That was the first time Lin Sui met Elder Master Shen.
The solemn old man sat in the seat of honor and told him, "Name your price. We want the product of your research."
Lin Sui's mindset was already close to that of an adult. Faintly realizing something, he asked, "Do you… want the drug for directional differentiation?"
His award-winning work was merely a shoddy imitation he had analyzed and puzzled out based on the research of Lin Zizhuo and Gu Lang.
Elder Master Shen did not deny it.

Lin Sui grew bolder. "…My drug is not yet mature and won't be of help to you, but I know of a drug that can satisfy your needs."
He surrendered the secretly developed W.N.
In exchange for this secret, he secured a promise from the Shen family—to eliminate Lin Zhou without a sound.
To kill a living, breathing human being was, of course, no easy task.
Especially when that person was the child of their targets.
Elder Master Shen promised Lin Sui that once they had the drug in hand, they would cut off Lin Zhou's head and deliver it to him.
Lin Sui readily agreed.
But he had not expected that the Shen family would go as far as to imprison Lin Zizhuo and Gu Lang.
