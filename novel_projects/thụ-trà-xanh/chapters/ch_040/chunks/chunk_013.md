If they had actually followed Shen Feizhi's plan, wouldn't he have really had to go through with the engagement ceremony with Lu Qian?

Did Shen Feizhi not care about his own reputation at all? Didn't boys know they had to protect themselves when they were out and about?
He lay there for a long while before looking up to ask, "Is he gone?"
"Yes." Xu Zhong’s expression was complicated. "Young Master, aren't you… being a bit too good to him?"
Gu Suizhou shook his head, offering no further explanation.
Xu Zhong didn't push the matter. He placed the small box he had been clutching for a long time in front of him. "Mr. Shen shoved this to me just now and told me to give it to you."
Gu Suizhou held it without opening it, only asking, "Have you found a method for differentiation yet?"
