She gave a miserable laugh. "Shen Feizhi, you really are heartless."
Through their interactions during this period, Lu Qian had thought she and Shen Feizhi could at least be considered friends.
Shen Feizhi said, "This has nothing to do with whether I'm heartless or not. The one you've wronged is Gu Suizhou, and I have no right to influence his actions."
Within the boundaries defined by modern social civilization and law, no one has the right to stand in the victim’s place and make decisions for them.
Lu Qian shook her head. "Fortunately, I didn't come to you to beg you to let my family go."
Shen Feizhi raised an eyebrow. "Then what is it?"
Lu Qian’s expression suddenly turned solemn. "Lin Sui just said that he produced one hundred and eighty doses of the drug. He’s been clearing them out in numerical order, and I am the last one."
"But I remember it very clearly. When a private doctor came to give me an injection back then, that syringe was labeled 179."
Inside the hospital ward.
As soon as Xu Zhong entered, he saw Gu Suizhou clutching the bedsheets with a death grip, his eyes nearly bursting from their sockets.
He cried out and ran over: "Young Master? Young Master, are you alright?!"
"The medicine…" Fearing that the people at the door might hear the commotion, Gu Suizhou bit his lower lip so hard that blood seeped out. He whispered, "Give me the medicine."
Xu Zhong hurriedly injected the drug into the back of Gu Suizhou’s neck.
Gu Suizhou lay on the bed trembling for a long while before he finally began to quiet down.
He had originally thought Lin Sui was only responsible for the cleanup and cover-up.
He had never imagined the man could actually be cold-hearted enough to personally manufacture the drugs used to frame his foster parents.
To say he wasn't grieving would be a lie; he just hadn't dared to show it in front of Shen Feizhi.
Xu Zhong asked him, "Mr. Shen said before that he wanted to release the situation regarding the basement to the public at the first opportunity. By doing this… his engagement ceremony with Miss Lu Qian might not be able to proceed."
This meant they wouldn't have a suitable reason to get the Central News Channel to broadcast live.
Xu Zhong was somewhat worried.
