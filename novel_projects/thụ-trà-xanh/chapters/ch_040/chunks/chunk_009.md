Since he lived on campus, he had no chance to go home.
When the weekend finally arrived, Gu Lang unexpectedly took the initiative to call him.
“Xiao Sui, I heard you took first place in your final exams.”
Lin Sui was overwhelmed by the sudden attention. “Yes! That’s right, Mom! I scored over twenty points higher than second place!”
“Wonderful.” Gu Lang gave a small laugh. “Come home. Your father and I have a gift for you.”
“Okay, okay! I’m coming back right now.” Lin Sui’s eyes lit up.
He bolted for home the second school let out.
But then, struck by a sudden thought, he took a detour to pick up Lin Zhou.
Lin Zizhuo had told him that he didn't need to bring his younger brother for today’s celebration, but he insisted on it; he wanted to bring Lin Zhou along so the boy could see who their parents truly loved most.
Because he was in such a good mood, he even went against his usual nature and bought Lin Zhou a candied hawthorn stick.
He never imagined that the gift Lin Zizhuo and Gu Lang had for him would be two corpses hanging from the rafters.
The thunderstorm was heavy that day, the rain battering the windows with a clatter like an earthquake.
The candied hawthorns shattered on the floor, their sugar coating splintering everywhere.
