Lin Zhou was two years old then and could already speak. He blinked at him blankly. "Big Brother, Zhou… already washed."
"I know," Lin Sui said calmly as he placed him in the bathtub and abruptly turned on the showerhead.
Ice-cold, bone-chilling water pelted the child's tender, fragile skin. Lin Zhou choked on several mouthfuls of water, his mouth crumpling as he prepared to cry.
Lin Sui suddenly bent down and clamped his hand tightly over his mouth. "Don't cry!"
The water level had already risen to the child's neck.
Lin Zhou was suffocating, unable to catch his breath. Only his beautiful eyes remained visible, tears streaming from them incessantly.
Lin Sui stared at those fox-like eyes that so closely resembled Gu Lang's, and for a fleeting moment, his mind drifted.
In that split second, the strength in his hand wavered. He failed to keep Lin Zhou suppressed, and the child's shrill wail rang out.
"Waaaaah—"
Lin Sui panicked. In his desperation, he snapped the water temperature regulator and cried out loudly, "Mom! Dad!"
"What happened?"
Gu Lang hadn't even finished dressing; he hurriedly threw on a shawl and ran out with Lin Zizhuo.
Lin Zhou sat in the bathtub, half his body submerged in the water, crying uncontrollably.
Lin Sui struck first. "Little brother just fell into the trash can! I didn't want to wake you up, so I thought I'd bring him in for a wash. I didn't expect…"
His eyes reddened slightly. "I accidentally snapped the temperature regulator, and Xiao Zhou got chilled. I’m sorry, Mom…"

Lin Zizhuo hurriedly scooped Lin Zhou out of the water. He let out a sigh and gently patted Lin Sui’s head. "It’s alright, you didn't do it on purpose. Just don't be so reckless next time. If something happens, just call for us."
"That's right, Xiao Sui." Gu Lang felt a lingering sense of dread. "Luckily, your father and I hadn't left for work yet, otherwise there might have been a real accident."
There wasn't a hint of blame in their voices; instead, they lightheartedly comforted the "frightened" Lin Sui.
