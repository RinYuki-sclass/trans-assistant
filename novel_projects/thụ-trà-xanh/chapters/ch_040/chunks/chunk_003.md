Shen Feizhi had still been in a cold war with him, but he couldn't bring himself to maintain a cold face anymore.
He poured a cup of hot water and pressed it into Gu Suizhou's trembling hands.
Gu Suizhou gripped the cup with one hand and suddenly seized two of Shen Feizhi's fingers with the other, his beautiful eyes brimming with tears.
This time, he truly was a pathetic little white rabbit.
Shen Feizhi sighed. He didn't say a word, but for the first time ever, he didn't immediately pull his hand away.

Gu Lang didn’t have a miscarriage.

Back then, Gu Suizhou wasn’t called Gu Suizhou yet; he was called Lin Zhou.
“Xiao Sui, look, it’s a little brother.” Gu Lang held the infant, smiling beautifully.
Lin Sui forced a smile. “That’s wonderful! I’ll have someone to play with from now on!”
But in his heart, he was thinking: Why did it have to be a brother?
If it were a little sister, he might have been able to tolerate it, but since they were both boys, would his parents really not neglect him?
Looking at the newborn infant, Lin Sui suddenly felt that life was incredibly fragile.
So fragile that with a mere reach of his hand, he could strangle the baby.
But Lin Zizhuo and Gu Lang protected the child all too well.
Lin Zizhuo adored the child and could not bear to be apart from him, staying by his side at all times and constantly bringing the baby over to play with Lin Sui.
