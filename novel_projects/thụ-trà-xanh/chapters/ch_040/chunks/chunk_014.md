Gu Suizhou recognized the mold; it was the baking equipment he had insisted on buying when he dragged Shen Feizhi out shopping before.
So, the chocolates were handmade by Shen Feizhi himself.
Like a man discovering a long-lost treasure, he picked one up and placed it in his mouth. The saccharine flavor instantly flooded his palate.
That man was right.
Eating sweets really did make one happy.
Gu Suizhou’s eyes curved into a smile. After a long moment, he picked up the small card at the bottom of the box.
Pasted onto the card was a heart pieced together from scraps of wrapping paper, accompanied by Shen Feizhi’s handsome and elegant handwriting:
【Returned to you.】
