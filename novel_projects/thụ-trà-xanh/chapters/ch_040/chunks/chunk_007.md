He was utterly stunned when he saw the thugs burst into their home and drag the two away.
Lin Zhou cried and wailed incessantly, the noise making him even more frustrated.
Unable to restrain himself, he slapped Lin Zhou hard across the face.
He had seen Lin Zizhuo and Gu Lang being dragged away, but so what?
Once Lin Zhou was dead, he would still be their only child.
After the initial shock, Lin Sui chose cold indifference.
He knew Lin Zizhuo and Gu Lang must be suffering in the Shen household, but it served them right. Why did they have to have another child?

Wasn’t he enough on his own?
He had thought it through; his plan was foolproof.
Thus, he never imagined that the Shen family would actually approach him to produce counterfeit medicine.
"What are you talking about?"
Lin Sui was incredulous. "My parents' drugs are more than enough. I couldn't possibly do better than them!"
The one who had come to him wasn't Old Master Shen, but a middle-aged man who was far less patient than the patriarch.
"Just do as you're told. Your parents aren't handing over the medicine, and we can't exactly let them go just like that."
Lin Sui grew cautious. "What exactly are you trying to do?"
"Nothing much." The man smiled. "Haven't you always wanted to be the only person your parents care about? If you're the only one in the family who is successful and capable of making money, wouldn't their eyes have to be fixed on you alone?"
Lin Sui opened his mouth but said nothing.
The man shrugged. "I'll give you ten days. I want 150 vials."
He finished speaking and left briskly.
