When his leg finally bumped against the edge of the bed, he held his breath, leaned down slightly, and squinted at the figure on the mattress.
The boy's slender body was curled up in a tight ball, his face buried between his knees, arms wrapped tightly around his legs—a stark contrast to his earlier languid posture.
Shen Feizhi couldn't see clearly, only a vague outline.
He reached out, his hand slowly landing on the mattress close to the boy.
That spot was trembling slightly.
…Gu Suizhou was shaking.
Afraid of the dark?
With no experience in comforting others, Shen Feizhi pursed his lips. "Gu Suizhou, look up and talk to me."
Gu Suizhou remained unresponsive.
His body shook even harder.
Left with no choice, Shen Feizhi leaned in closer.
