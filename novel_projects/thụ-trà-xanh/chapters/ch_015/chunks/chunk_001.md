Chapter 15
Shen Feizhi raised a hand and neatly buttoned the last unfastened button at his collar. "That's none of your concern."
"Come on, Brother Shen, you're making me sound like I have ulterior motives," Gu Suizhou retorted, quickly shifting his gaze to arrange the cutlery. "A tattoo's just a tattoo. I don't discriminate."
"Enough chatter. You must not be hungry after all."
The dining table was a long wooden one with three seats on each side. Shen Feizhi watched Gu Suizhou casually settle in the middle seat. After a moment's thought, he took the seat diagonally opposite him.
Gu Suizhou clicked his tongue and shifted one seat to the right.
Shen Feizhi glanced at him and immediately moved to the opposite diagonal corner.
Gu Suizhou: "…Brother, this is going too far."
"Too far?" Shen Feizhi shook his head. "I just want to enjoy my meal. I can't eat with you sitting across from me."
Just looking at that face made his appetite vanish.
Gu Suizhou choked on his words, reluctantly abandoning his plan to sit directly across from him.
"Tch, double standards."
Shen Feizhi's ears twitched. "What double standards?"
Gu Suizhou retorted, "To Lai Gou, you're the kind, gentle onii-chan next door. But to me, you're the tyrannical bully boss, pushing me around."
The words slipped out without a second thought, revealing long-simmering resentment.
