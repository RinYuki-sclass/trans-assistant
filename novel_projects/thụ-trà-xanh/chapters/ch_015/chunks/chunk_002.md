He waited for Shen Feizhi to take the first bite before picking up his chopsticks and grabbing a seaweed shrimp cake, stuffing it into his mouth in a show of frustration.
Three seconds later…
"Holy shit!!"
Shen Feizhi tapped the table with his chopsticks, making a sharp thunk .
"What did I tell you about the first rule of our agreement? Forgotten already?"
"Sorry, Brother Shen. I'll remember next time." Gu Suizhou was too stunned to argue, gazing tearfully at the shrimp cake. "Brother Shen, your cooking… it's just too amazing!"
It was a perfect match for his tastes—no garlic, no scallions—and even the other dishes on the table were flawless!
Could it be anything less? Shen Feizhi thought to himself. He'd briefly worked as a private chef for a domineering CEO, specializing in customizing meals to his clients' preferences.
The Little Brat's lucky; his preferences align closely with mine. I just combined our common ground.
Gu Suizhou's translucent amber eyes seemed to glow with an otherworldly light. "So, my handsome, dashing, charming, and irresistible Brother Shen~ will you cook for me often from now on?"
Shen Feizhi chuckled, studying him amusedly. "It depends on your behavior."

Gu Suizhou immediately straightened up, his attitude earnest and proactive. "I'll do the dishes!"
Since Gu Suizhou had volunteered to do the dishes after dinner, Shen Feizhi went straight back to his study to read General Principles of Blue Star Criminal Law and Crime Analysis .
He loved absorbing knowledge; the feeling of intellectual fullness brought him a profound sense of security. Unless it was a life-or-death situation, Shen Feizhi made it a habit to devote an hour or two each day to learning something new.
By the time he finished his studies for the day, pinching the bridge of his nose in concentration, it was already past ten at night.
The lights in the living room and dining area had long been dimmed, and no one was sitting outside. Gu Suizhou must already be in bed, preparing to rest.
Shen Feizhi went out to pour himself a glass of water. As he passed the living room on his way back, he deliberately walked by the full-length mirror.
Through the crack beneath Gu Suizhou's bedroom door, he could see the man lying in bed reading. His left hand, resting on the edge of the mattress, was absently fiddling with a few small stones.
Shen Feizhi swirled his water, drained the glass in one gulp, and then turned around to fetch another glass from the kitchen. After pouring another cup, he knocked on Gu Suizhou's bedroom door.
