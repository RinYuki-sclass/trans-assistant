"Just kidding!" Gu Suizhou burst out laughing at his indescribable expression. "I wanted to dig it up, but Lin Sui and the Shen family stopped me. Brother Shen, do you think that's normal?"
Leaning against the window, Shen Feizhi instinctively intertwined his fingers, kneading them together. "You suspected them of foul play in this even right after you returned? How old were you then? Six?"
"Of course I didn't think of it back then. Someone reminded me while I was away." For a fleeting moment, Gu Suizhou's usually serene expression seemed tinged with sorrow. "It's precisely because I was so young. It took years for me to realize."
"Even so, you weren't much older," Shen Feizhi murmured, secretly noting the person who had reminded him. "So, you've been this devious since you were a kid, huh?"
"I'll take that as a compliment," Gu Suizhou said, his eyes crinkling at the corners as he tugged the blanket closer. "That's why I've always wanted to thoroughly investigate Shen—"

The room was now pitch-black.

But no response came.
