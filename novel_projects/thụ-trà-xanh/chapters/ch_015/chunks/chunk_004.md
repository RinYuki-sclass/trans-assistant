The public outcry reached fever pitch. Families of the victims rallied outside W.N's headquarters, demanding answers and accountability.
But instead of answers, they received a cataclysmic fire that shook Blue Star to its core.
The blaze engulfed the entire Research Institute, inside and out. Surveillance footage quickly identified the arsonists—Lin Zizhuo and Gu Lang themselves.

As the institute's highest authorities, setting the fire was trivial. Their motive was clear: cornered like dogs, they sought to destroy the shameful research, eliminating evidence of the victims' claims.
After reviewing the surveillance footage, the Prosecutor's Office immediately dispatched agents to arrest Lin Zizhuo and Gu Lang.
But before dawn, they received distress calls from Lin Sui and Gu Suizhou.
The two had committed suicide.
They were found hanged in their homes.
"Back then, the Shen family was W.N.'s largest benefactor. They oversaw everything after the incident, from handling the remains to managing the suicide scene of my parents. You could even say they handled it all personally."
Gu Suizhou spoke with unsettling calm, his voice utterly devoid of emotion.
Shen Feizhi noticed Gu Suizhou's lips were cracked and dry, yet he seemed oblivious to his own discomfort. He rose himself, brought the lukewarm water from the bedside, and held it to Gu Suizhou's lips. "Drink a little first."
Gu Suizhou nearly spilled the water.
He stared at the ripples on the water's surface in the cup, but made no move to take it. Instead, he drank two gulps directly from Shen Feizhi's hand.
Shen Feizhi pressed his lips together, suppressing the urge to pour the entire cup down Gu Suizhou's throat. He slowly fed him the rest of the water.
Once Gu Suizhou had quenched his thirst, he continued.
"A few days after my parents died, I had to leave. When I returned, all traces of W.N.'s involvement had vanished, as if none of it had ever happened—as if my parents had never existed."
"The Shen family explained that everything had been taken care of. Years later, when they finally took me to the cemetery to see my parents' graves, they were empty tombs."
Shen Feizhi frowned slightly. "An empty grave? How are you so sure it was empty?"
