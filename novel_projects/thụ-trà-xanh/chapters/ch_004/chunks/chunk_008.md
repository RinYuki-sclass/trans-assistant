Lin Zhou shamelessly drew out the word, "Bro~ther~"
Shen Feizhi's smile didn't reach his eyes. "Little brother, watch your mouth. Otherwise, I'll just stand here in the middle of the road until the green light turns red and let you get run over."
As he spoke, his pace actually slowed.
Staring at the crosswalk timer ticking down from seven seconds, Lin Zhou realized Shen Feizhi was genuinely capable of such a deranged, mutually destructive act. His mouth twitched. "Brother, I was wrong."
Shen Feizhi was satisfied.
With a long stride, he yanked the little brat along in a mad dash, finally reaching the other side of the road just as the light turned yellow.
After that incident, the rest of the walk was remarkably quiet.
Shen Feizhi only stopped when they reached the entrance of a less crowded alley.
He released his grip and turned to face the boy.
Lin Zhou wasn't much shorter than him. At 185 cm, Shen Feizhi estimated Lin Zhou was at least 180 cm. They could look each other in the eye without any strain.

He cleared his throat, once again the picture of a calm, composed man, unfazed by praise or humiliation. "You can follow me."
Lin Zhou's face lit up. "Really?!"
"Mm," Shen Feizhi nodded. "But you need to think this through…"
"I've thought it through!"
Before he could finish, Lin Zhou answered without a second thought.
This man is riddled with suspicious points, Lin Zhou thought, but I've finally found a breakthrough into the Shen family. I have to seize this chance, no matter what!
Shen Feizhi let out a soft snort, crossing his arms and leaning against the wall. He unhurriedly finished his sentence: "…Since you're the one choosing to follow me, then no matter what happens later, you're not allowed to run off on your own until I decide to ditch you."
This little brat's motives are unclear, Shen Feizhi mused, but I've finally found a plot point. I can't let him get away, no matter what!
Lin Zhou nodded repeatedly.
