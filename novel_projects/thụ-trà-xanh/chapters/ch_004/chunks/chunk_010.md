"I wouldn't dare," Lin Zhou said with an obedient smile. "I just think you're brilliant."
Shen Feizhi scoffed. "You've really mastered the art of passive-aggression, kid."

With that, adhering to the principle of "out of sight, out of mind," he squared his shoulders and strode forward, head held high against the biting wind in his single-layer shirt. "Keep up, Lin Zhou. Lead the way."
This was the first time since they'd met that he'd used the boy's full name.
But this time, the boy didn't take the opportunity to act cute and push his luck.
Shen Feizhi walked a few steps before realizing no one was following him.
He couldn't help but stop and turn around. "What's wrong?"
The boy stood rooted to the spot, his worn, bulky winter school uniform failing to conceal his slender frame.
The fake smile had vanished from his beautiful, amber fox-like eyes. He simply met Shen Feizhi's gaze in silence.
For once, Shen Feizhi showed a rare flicker of patience and didn't march over to kick him. He just looked at the boy with a questioning gaze.
After a long moment, the boy finally broke into a smile. "Brother Shen, my name isn't Lin Zhou."
He jogged a few steps to catch up, stopping beside Shen Feizhi.
"My name is Gu Suizhou."
