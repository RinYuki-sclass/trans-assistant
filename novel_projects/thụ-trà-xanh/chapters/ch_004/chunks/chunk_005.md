Everything has multiple sides?
Shen Feizhi stared at the noodles in his hand, now almost completely cold. He repeated the phrase in his mind and suddenly understood something.
He had been biased from the start.
His ingrained way of thinking had made him subconsciously assume that the plot he knew was the real plot, causing him to overlook a well-known fact: the information provided by the Main Star was a storyline constructed from the perspective of the Child of Destiny.
If even what the Child of Destiny saw wasn't the truth, could this story still be real?
Shen Feizhi's expression darkened.
The feeling of suddenly transforming from a director who held the script to an actor holding the wrong script was beyond awful.
Lin Zhou noticed the subtle shift in his expression and raised an eyebrow slightly.
"Brother Shen, what do you think? Shouldn't you be thanking me for saving you from Lin Sui?"

Shen Feizhi snapped back to reality and narrowed his eyes at the boy who was supposed to be nothing more than minor cannon fodder.
Instead of answering, he countered, "You and Lin Sui don't look alike at all."
Lin Zhou shrugged nonchalantly and nodded frankly. "Yeah, we're not blood-related. He's the ungrateful bastard my parents adopted."
"Oh," Shen Feizhi said.
He wasn't particularly curious and had no interest in the Lin family's private affairs.
So, after Lin Zhou finished speaking, he simply asked calmly, "Why did you help me?"
He would never believe this little brat was so kind-hearted that he couldn't bear to see a poor soul fall prey to Lin Sui.
"Besides," Shen Feizhi subconsciously patted his pocket, then casually withdrew his hand when he realized there were no cigarettes inside, "what makes you so sure Lin Sui could have tricked me into bed after taking an interest in me?"
"I was sure before. I know better than anyone how dirty his methods are." Lin Zhou finished the last of his noodles and scooted closer with a cheeky grin. "But now that I know how smart you are, Brother Shen, I'm not so sure anymore."
"Then you were just meddling." Shen Feizhi shifted away, maintaining the exact distance Lin Zhou had tried to close. "Don't even think about using this to demand a favor."
