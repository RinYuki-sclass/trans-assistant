Shen Feizhi nearly choked to death on the water caught in his throat.
He clutched his mouth, coughing wretchedly for what felt like an eternity before finally glaring at the culprit with a look that screamed, Are you fucking kidding me? "Do you have a death wish?"
The boy, however, remained completely unabashed. "I just like you. I can't stand the thought of you being wasted on Lin Sui. I want to pursue you. Is that not allowed?"
The blunt confession drew stares from two passersby who had just exited the convenience store.
For once, Shen Feizhi was speechless.
A man who valued his pride more than his life, he couldn't bear the countless gazes from the bustling street. He retracted his hand into his shirt sleeve and, using the fabric as a barrier, seized the boy's wrist and dragged him away.
"Hey, hey, hey!" Caught off guard, Lin Zhou frantically tossed aside the instant noodle container he'd nearly spilled, stumbling a few steps to keep up. "Brother Shen, you're holding my hand!"
"This is grabbing ," Shen Feizhi seethed, radiating an icy chill. He popped his shirt collar, a futile attempt to shield himself from the onlookers' scrutiny. "Did you even pass your language classes?"
"I don't care." Lin Zhou flashed a brilliant smile, revealing his two small canine teeth, and cheerfully wiggled the hand now marked with five red finger imprints. "This is holding."
Shen Feizhi couldn't be bothered to argue. "Whatever—"
Lin Zhou: "You're so in love with me."
Shen Feizhi: "…"
He instantly let go.
For the rest of the walk, no matter how much Lin Zhou bantered, Shen Feizhi remained utterly silent.
Under the weight of countless probing gazes, he wanted nothing more than to dig a hole and crawl into it.

Finally, as they were crossing the street, Lin Zhou noticed his strange behavior.
He leaned in recklessly, his curious head practically begging for trouble. "Brother Shen, are you really that concerned about your pride?"
Having reached his breaking point, Shen Feizhi decided to throw caution to the wind. For the first time, a smile touched his eyes as he turned his head. "Little brother."
