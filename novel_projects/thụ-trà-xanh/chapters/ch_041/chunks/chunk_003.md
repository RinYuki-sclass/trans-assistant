By sheer luck, he had managed to pull through.
He was almost certain this current crisis was connected to the Main Star.

The moment he stepped inside, Shen Feizhi darted into his bedroom and rummaged through his medicine cabinet.
This body couldn't withstand much more. He needed medication to stabilize his condition, but he hadn't yet developed a fuel sufficiently compatible with the Energy Storm Eye. Judging by his progress, it would take at least another two months.
He dumped the contents of the medicine cabinet onto the floor with a clatter, only to find, to his frustration, that he had run out of suppressants.
He clenched his fists, his pheromones spiraling out of control.
He couldn't possibly go out in this state.
