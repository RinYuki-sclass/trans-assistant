Xu Zhong stood rooted to the spot for a moment, then sighed softly and left.
After he was gone, Gu Suizhou's irritation intensified. His knuckles turned white as he gripped his smartphone.
Finally, he violently raked his hand through his disheveled hair and swore loudly, "Fuck!"
Having cursed, he awkwardly scrubbed at his lips and face.
Ever since Shen Feizhi took charge of him, he'd truly turned over a new leaf.
It had been so long since he'd cursed, he was almost out of practice.
Still burning with fever, Shen Feizhi's sleep was restless.
He had always preferred quiet environments for rest, but today, for some inexplicable reason, the excessive silence only kept him awake.
So he turned on the electric screen in Gu Suizhou's bedroom and tuned it to the news channel, hoping to lull himself to sleep with its drone.
But the news channel turned out to be excessively noisy.

Is there a world war going on, for all this racket?

What the hell?
Today's news protagonist was none other than an old acquaintance: Gu Suizhou.
The young man's slightly long hair had been styled into a flamboyant updo, pinned back on one side. Dressed in a tailored suit that gave him an air of mature sophistication, he looked the picture of respectability.
Even under the unforgivingly unflattering close-up shots of the news channel's death-angle camera, Gu Suizhou's dazzlingly handsome face remained flawless, without a single imperfection.
But Shen Feizhi, who knew his true nature, could only scoff in disdain.
After W.N. was developed, Shen Feizhi had replaced the title of "sole authority" with the names of Lin Zizhuo and Gu Lang, giving them the recognition they deserved.
