Chapter 41
The scandal had erupted into a full-blown crisis, requiring individual interrogations of every Shen family member.
By the time Shen Feizhi emerged from the Investigation Bureau's dark holding cell, a week had passed.
The Prosecutor's Office had treated him reasonably well, not stinting on food or drink—likely due to Gu Suizhou's influence and backdoor dealings.
Gu Suizhou had even bribed the Chief Prosecutor to allow Shen Feizhi to accompany them to the Shen family's old mansion for the investigation.
"You know, it's really strange," the Prosecutor mused beside Shen Feizhi, scratching his head. "Why does Elder Master Shen still refuse to admit his mistakes, even now?"
Knowing the comment was directed at him, Shen Feizhi replied, "Perhaps it's his last act of stubbornness."
The Prosecutor chuckled. "I didn't realize you had such a sense of humor."
Shen Feizhi: "……"
Where's the humor in that?
Having been paid well, the Prosecutor performed his duties diligently, proactively opening the basement door for Shen Feizhi. "You wouldn't believe how stubborn that old man is. He flatly denies ordering Lin Sui to manufacture the drugs, pinning all the blame on Lin Sui and refusing to confess."
"Can they still prosecute him for the drug manufacturing?" Shen Feizhi asked.
"Right now, all we have is Lin Sui's testimony that he sold drugs to these people," the Prosecutor admitted bluntly. "Without concrete evidence beyond his word, it'll be tough to convict him of illegal pharmaceutical manufacturing and murder." He clicked his tongue. "But… tsk tsk tsk, look at this basement. Illegal imprisonment charges are a lock. The once-glorious Shen family's about to fall hard!"
As he spoke, his voice dripped with undisguised schadenfreude.
Only after finishing did he realize there was a Shen family member standing right beside him.
