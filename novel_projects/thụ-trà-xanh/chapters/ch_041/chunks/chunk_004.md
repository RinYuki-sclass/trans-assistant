Forgetting all decorum, Shen Feizhi mentally apologized to Gu Suizhou for the intrusion and pushed open the door.
The medicine cabinet was conspicuously placed. Opening it, he found it indeed well-stocked with a comprehensive range of medications.
He quickly administered the injection, feeling his pheromone levels gradually stabilize, and finally exhaled in relief, collapsing onto the window-side divan.
The familiar scent of Gu Suizhou's bath gel was absent from the air. Shen Feizhi realized with a start that Gu Suizhou hadn't been home for a long time.
Having grown accustomed to someone chattering incessantly by his ear, the sudden quiet felt jarringly unfamiliar.
Yet he couldn't deny that Gu Suizhou's absence made it exceptionally easy to recuperate.
Shen Feizhi sighed contentedly and closed his eyes.
It wasn't that he didn't want to return to his room, but the injection had left him utterly drained, forcing him to rest temporarily.
Barely had his eyelids drifted shut when his mind slipped into sleep.
Ever since his physical condition deteriorated, Shen Feizhi had been catching up on all the sleep he'd missed.
He was roused once by his smart device's ringtone, groggily glancing at the screen—a rare call from Gu Suizhou.
He answered with mild irritation, "What do you want?"
Gu Suizhou's voice remained as energetic as ever. "Brother Shen, are you busy right now? Did you get off work yet? Wanna hang out?"
The familiar three-part question.
Shen Feizhi replied with forced patience, "Busy, no, no."
