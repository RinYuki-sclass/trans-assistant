As they walked through the labyrinthine corridors, the Prosecutor's exclamations of astonishment never ceased. "All this is money!"
Shen Feizhi ignored him, stopping before the isolation cell Elder Master Shen had identified, his brow furrowed in a deep frown. "Why are there two rooms here?"
The basement resembled a vast labyrinth, each cell isolated by countless walls. Yet this particular isolation cell was actually two rooms pressed together.
"Professor Lin and Professor Gu each had their own room, that's perfectly normal," the prosecutor said.
Shen Feizhi remained noncommittal.
If they had each had their own room, the Shen family could have easily isolated them separately. But these two rooms—one large, one small—looked as if… the smaller space had been carved out of the larger one with iron bars.
He pushed open the door and stepped into the small cubicle.
The cubicle was spotless, devoid of any traces that might offer a clue.
Shen Feizhi kicked the corner of the wall.
Who exactly was imprisoned here?
