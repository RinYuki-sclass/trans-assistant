Why does that kid seem to be everywhere these days?
Shen Feizhi reached for the remote, intending to change the channel.
But just then, the smoothly proceeding signing ceremony suddenly took an unexpected turn.
A cocky young pup, all dressed up like a businessman, suddenly interrupted the insincere speeches. He snatched the contract and scribbled his name across the page with lightning speed, his hand a blur of motion.
Why's he rushing like he's got a date with reincarnation?
Shen Feizhi's lips twitched.

He reached for his smart device to call the brat, only to realize it was on Do Not Disturb mode.
Whatever, he thought, too lazy to disable it. He watched with a stony expression as the brat finished signing, grabbed his briefcase, and bolted out.
Looks like a slacking tyrant.
Has he been keeping some bewitching concubine abroad these past few months, a vixen who could topple a nation?
Shen Feizhi found the thought utterly bizarre.
What kind of breathtaking beauty could make a finicky little drama queen like Gu Suizhou this devoted?
He pondered this, but his muddled mind felt like pudding—he couldn't conjure a single coherent thought.
So he simply closed his eyes again.
An hour later, Shen Feizhi was jolted awake by a violent shake.
He lifted his head groggily, his gaze meeting the flawless face on the holographic screen.
For a fleeting moment, he wondered if he was dreaming.
But he quickly realized it was no dream.
Gu Suizhou glanced at him, then at his own bedroom, and let out a pleased chuckle. "Brother Shen, aren't you supposed to be working?"
