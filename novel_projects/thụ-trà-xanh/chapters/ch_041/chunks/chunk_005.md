Undeterred, he pressed on: "Brother Shen, it's such a beautiful day! Won't you come out and play? I even cleared my schedule just for this!"
Shen Feizhi forced his eyelids open to glance out the window. "Black clouds smothering the stars, gale-force winds lashing with drizzle… 'Beautiful day'?"
Gu Suizhou: "…"
Gu Suizhou: "No, Bro, don't be so cynical! It's just the weather. What matters is maintaining a positive, sunny, and warm mindset!"
Shen Feizhi couldn't be bothered. "I'm not going. Anything else? If not, I'm hanging up."
Every inch of his body ached. He had no patience for this brat's antics.
In the immediate aftermath of the incident, he had exhausted every ounce of his patience, trying to be gentle and avoid upsetting the boy. He had indulged the boy's tearful demands for hugs and his insistence on sharing a room.
But now that the crisis was over and the boy was still causing such a fuss, Shen Feizhi had run out of patience.
He ruthlessly ended the call, immediately setting his smartphone to silent and Do Not Disturb mode.
When Gu Suizhou tried to call back, he was met with nothing but a busy signal.
Xu Zhong crouched beside Gu Suizhou, studying his ashen face. He asked cautiously, "What… what do we do now?"
Gu Suizhou pressed his temples, headache throbbing. "Forget about getting him out. Send a message to the others—tell them tonight's signing ceremony will proceed as planned."
Xu Zhong nodded and rose to leave, but glanced back before departing.
