As soon as he finished speaking, he noticed the listless expression on Shen Feizhi's face. Realizing something was wrong, he tugged up his suit pants, dropped to one knee, and pressed his slightly cool palm against Shen Feizhi's forehead.
"Fuck!" he blurted out, his composure cracking. "How are you so burning hot?"
"If you don't want to catch it, stay away from me," Shen Feizhi thought to himself. As expected, the air grows noisy the moment this guy returns.
Gu Suizhou's gaze swept over the open medicine kit on the table, his face etched with worry. "Are you on sick leave?" he asked anxiously.
"What else would it be?" Shen Feizhi retorted sarcastically. "Do you think I'd just slack off? Toss and turn thinking of you and then sneak into your room to sleep?"
Gu Suizhou brushed aside the barb. "Have you been to the hospital? Seen a doctor? Taken any medicine? Did you eat dinner?"
"Why so many questions the moment you get back?" Shen Feizhi snapped. "Just be quiet for a while and let me rest."
Still worried, Gu Suizhou opened his mouth to speak again, only to be silenced by a searing-hot finger pressed against his lips.
"Leave me alone," Shen Feizhi said, his voice chillingly cold, a stark contrast to his feverish body. "Instead of this, you should reflect on your behavior tonight. You turned a public signing ceremony into a star's encore. What could possibly be worth rushing like that?"
Having raised children for so long, Shen Feizhi felt a mix of exasperation and disappointment at his protégé's shortcomings.
Gu Suizhou blinked, surprised. "You watched the news broadcast of my signing?"
Shen Feizhi nodded frankly. "Unfortunately, you were the news channel's lead story today."
"Ah…" Gu Suizhou didn't dwell on the "unfortunately." He grasped Shen Feizhi's burning palm with his cool hand. "Brother Shen, I'd originally declined tonight's signing ceremony. It was only because you didn't show up that I went."

This explanation clearly failed to satisfy Shen Feizhi. "Since you went, you shouldn't let anyone disrupt your performance."
Gu Suizhou lowered his gaze. "Do you think my return was unnecessary?"
Shen Feizhi considered briefly before nodding rationally. "Yes."
Gu Suizhou shot to his feet. "You really mean that?"
"Ask me a hundred more times," Shen Feizhi said, "and the answer will still be yes."
Seemingly enraged, Gu Suizhou turned and stormed out.
