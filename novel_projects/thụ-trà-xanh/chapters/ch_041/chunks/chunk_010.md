Hearing the door slam shut, Shen Feizhi closed his eyes again.
Two minutes later, the door burst open again.
Gu Suizhou charged back in, demanding, "You still have the strength to walk?"
Shen Feizhi opened his eyes, which hadn't fully closed, thinking, What kind of stupid question is that?
Being sick doesn't mean I'm crippled. Of course I can walk.
But Gu Suizhou didn't wait for an answer, shaking his head dismissively. "With a fever that high, you must be too weak to walk."
Shen Feizhi: "?"
No sooner had the words left his mouth than Gu Suizhou stood up, leaned down, and scooped Shen Feizhi up into his arms, carrying him horizontally.
Shen Feizhi: "? Gu Suizhou!"

"Mhm, mhm, I'm here," Gu Suizhou replied, his long legs eating up the distance as he carried Shen Feizhi to the living room's floor-to-ceiling windows. Before Shen Feizhi could struggle, he gently and swiftly settled him onto the beanbag chair.
Then, standing behind him, Gu Suizhou gently massaged his shoulders.
"Brother Shen, don't get angry yet. Just look."
Before Shen Feizhi could react, the city center beyond the floor-to-ceiling windows suddenly transformed from dimness to brilliant illumination.
Myriad colored lights flickered on one by one, so large they were clearly visible even from this distance.
The shimmering lights slowly coalesced into an "S" shape, as balloons and streamers suddenly filled the sky, seizing everyone's attention.
Shen Feizhi was momentarily stunned. "What's all this?"
"A chocolate factory," Gu Suizhou said, his eyes crinkling into a smile. "Happy birthday, Brother Shen."
Shen Feizhi's eyes widened.
He couldn't muster a rational reaction. Should he be excited? Happy?
