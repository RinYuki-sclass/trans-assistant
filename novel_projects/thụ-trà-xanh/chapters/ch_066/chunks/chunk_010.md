Because right now, he truly lacked any such worldly desires.
Compared to Gu Suizhou, who caught fire the moment he was teased, Shen Feizhi was practically a celibate monk.
His hypersensitive skin only ever made him feel uncomfortable and averse to touch; it didn't lead his thoughts in that direction, especially since no one usually had the chance to touch him anyway.
In the past, aside from his estrus periods, he basically never felt any impulses. Every month was like completing a mandatory chore, something he’d take care of himself just for the sake of physical health.

Although Gu Suizhou was different from others to him, a mere kiss was still far from enough to stir him.
When he didn’t speak, Gu Suizhou lowered his head and ate his ice cream in a sullen funk. If he had ears, they would be drooping all the way back to his neck right now.
He looked pathetically pitiful.
Realizing he was the one who had started the trouble in the first place, Shen Feizhi felt that sitting by and doing nothing would be somewhat irresponsible. He sighed and shoved the ice cream into Gu Suizhou’s hands.
Gu Suizhou turned his head, confused.
It was the sound of a watch buckle being released.
Shen Feizhi unhurriedly stowed the watch in the car door’s storage compartment and calmly rolled up his left sleeve.
The autopilot had been engaged. From the navigation system ahead came a mechanical female voice: [Destination confirmed. Estimated travel time: forty-seven minutes.]
“We have plenty of time.” Not a trace of emotion showed on Shen Feizhi’s face. His voice was cool and thin, his tone even flatter than someone reciting a menu.
His gaze swept lazily toward Gu Suizhou’s custom-made belt. His left knuckles, chilled by the ice cream, flexed once.
“Are you going to unbuckle it yourself, or do you want me to help you?”
