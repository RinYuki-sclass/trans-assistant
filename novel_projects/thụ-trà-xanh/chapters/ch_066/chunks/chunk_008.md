Shen Feizhi soon released him, his thumb brushing a trace of moisture from the corner of the other's mouth. "Still angry?"
"You're not playing by the rules." Gu Suizhou's voice was a bit hoarse as he leaned in and stole two more kisses. "That’s cheating, Brother."
"It’s called using one’s wits. Why wouldn't I take a shortcut if there is one?" Shen Feizhi didn't refuse his request for a kiss, playing along with a languid indifference.
"I didn't add her contact info, we barely spoke, we didn't eat ice cream together, and we didn't have cocoa. I have no reaction to her pheromones, and she feels the same about mine…"
"Are you placated now?"
With every sentence he added, the corners of Gu Suizhou’s mouth quirked up another fraction, until he finally broke into a grin, biting into the other man’s neck as he laughed.
Starting from the base of the ear, he kissed his way down to the collarbone.
He was quite satisfied with the masterpiece he had created. "Barely."
"Give you an inch and you take a mile." His tie hanging loosely around his neck, Shen Feizhi tried to sit up.
Gu Suizhou caught his hand and dragged him back. "In an hour, I have to go to a press conference."
"And?" Shen Feizhi narrowed his eyes.
"The way I am now, I might not be able to make it." Gu Suizhou sighed dejectedly and pulled down the blackout curtains inside the car, completely shutting out the light from outside. "What should we do, Big Brother?"

Then he reconsidered—so what if he was found out? He wished he could let the whole world know that a certain someone was his now.
