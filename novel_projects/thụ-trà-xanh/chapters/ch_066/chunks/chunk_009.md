“You’re very welcome, Mr. Shen,” the man said with a smile. “As our distinguished SVIP, you can reach out to us online anytime. We can always arrange for specialized delivery for you~”
Shen Feizhi nodded and closed the window again.
The intrusive light finally made its exit.
Gu Suizhou’s lower abdomen was so tight it felt ready to burst, but he tried his best to maintain a look of strained endurance. “What is that? SVIP?”
“Don’t you like this shop?” Shen Feizhi pulled a small cup of sea salt lemon ice cream from the bag. “I topped up a membership card on my way out.”
This shop really was a master at fleecing people.
Coincidentally, the person who processed his card was the same sincere waiter he and Gu Suizhou had met during their first visit. The waiter had offered a kind reminder with a beaming smile: "Mr. Shen, if you’re getting a card, a VIP membership requires a deposit of 100,000, while SVIP requires 200,000. Additionally, only SVIPs enjoy delivery service, though the delivered items are all 'mini' versions~"
They were mini, all right.

A serving of ice cream that was normally so large ended up being a mere 360ml glass when delivered, something that could be held in one hand.
If it weren't for the fact that Gu Suizhou liked it, no matter how wealthy Shen Feizhi was, he wouldn't be so out of his mind as to waste money like this.
He still remembered the waiter's utmost sincerity when handing him the card: "Congratulations, Mr. Shen, on becoming the 250th VIP guest since our shop opened."
Read as "VIP," written as "sucker."
There sure were a lot of rich people.
To think that before him, there had been 249 other massive suckers.
He knew full well that he was simply blinded by infatuation; he just didn't know if the others were genuinely stupid or just that rich.
Shen Feizhi suddenly felt a sense of balance. With a sigh of emotion, he held the ice cream out to Gu Suizhou. "A single serving is just right. Eat it to cool off, then we’re heading to the press conference."
Gu Suizhou choked slightly, his heart a mess of joy and gloom. Unsure whether to be moved or frustrated, he took the small spoon and scooped a mouthful right from Shen Feizhi’s hand. "Bro, have you ever considered that maybe there are other, more effective ways to 'cool off'?"
How could it be possible for Shen Feizhi not to know what he meant?
But he didn't want to.
