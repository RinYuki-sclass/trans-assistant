Chapter 30
Humans die in many ways: physical death, spiritual death, social death…
Undoubtedly, this was the third kind.
As Gu Suizhou stared at Shen Feizhi's cold, aloof expression, he suddenly wished he could cut out his own vocal cords.
"Brother Shen…"
"Don't call me 'Brother'," Shen Feizhi interrupted. "It's just as well you're back. Saves me the trouble of finding you."
Gu Suizhou couldn't help himself. "No, Brother Shen, wait! Let me explain."
"No explanation necessary," Shen Feizhi said after finishing his last sip of cocoa. "Pack your things. I'll give you one night. Starting tomorrow, get the hell out of here and never let me see your face again."
"!" Gu Suizhou panicked instantly. Forgetting he was barefoot, he charged toward Shen Feizhi. "No, Brother Shen! Brother Shen, listen… No, Brother Shen, I was wrong! I really was wrong!"
Shen Feizhi raised an eyebrow.
He's finally showing some sense, apologizing before trying to explain.

Gu Suizhou's expression shattered. "Bro, please, forgive me."
Shen Feizhi remained unmoved, carrying him like a chick and tossing him onto the sofa. Arms crossed, he looked down at him from above. "Forgiving you isn't impossible."
