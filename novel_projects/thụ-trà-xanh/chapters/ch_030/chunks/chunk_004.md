Gu Suizhou rolled over, turning his back to Shen Feizhi and curling up, his voice muffled.
"Brother Shen, do you think if I had wished for them to go on a trip with me or something, they might not have…"
"Don't indulge in such pointless hypotheticals," Shen Feizhi said flatly. "The primary cause of their deaths wasn't their mental state, but external factors. In such circumstances, your answer would have been meaningless."
Gu Suizhou was silent for a moment, then chuckled.
"Shen Feizhi, you're… truly utterly devoid of gentleness."
He was foolish, attempting to feign vulnerability to soften someone's heart.
Still, there was an unexpected silver lining. Though Shen Feizhi's words lacked emotional warmth, he found himself liking them immensely—more than any comfort he'd ever received before.
Gu Suizhou flipped back over, grinning, "Bro, you suspect my parents' deaths might be connected to Lin Sui too, right?"
"Maybe," Shen Feizhi replied vaguely, rising and heading toward the bedroom. "I get it. Let's get some sleep."
"Aiyo!" Gu Suizhou yelped, scrambling up to follow him. "Bro, does this mean you've forgiven me?"
Shen Feizhi remained silent.
Gu Suizhou stuck to his heels, refusing to let up. "Bro, even if you don't ask, I still need to explain! Knocking you out that day was a last resort. Xu Zhong came to pick me up without his mask. His face… it's badly disfigured, and he didn't want anyone to see him like that. Uncle Zhong worked for my parents, and I respect him deeply. I didn't want to hurt his feelings."
After saying this, Gu Suizhou silently apologized to Xu Zhong in his heart.
