Gu Suizhou was caught off guard, meeting his gaze.
Shen Feizhi sighed inwardly.
In just three or four months, this kid's acting skills have improved so much. You can't even tell if his gaze is sincere or fake anymore.
He suddenly changed the subject. "Go make my bed. After you're done… I'll consider whether to forgive you or not."
"You got it!"
Gu Suizhou's ears perked up, and he immediately scampered off to make the bed.
Shen Feizhi stood behind him, his pitch-black eyes watching silently.
Each corner of the bedsheet had a small tie, meant to be fastened to the bedposts to keep it from slipping.
Gu Suizhou watched Shen Feizhi's slender, elegant fingers deftly tie a perfect bow.
Shen Feizhi chuckled softly, but the sound was icy—the kind that could freeze water at -100°C.
Even Gu Suizhou, who was smoothing the sheet, felt the chill and shivered involuntarily.
"Brother Shen?" he murmured, curious, turning to look.
Just then, the bright chandelier dimmed abruptly, leaving only the bedside lamp casting a dim, blurred glow across the room.
Before Gu Suizhou could react, a warm body pressed against him from behind, mimicking his earlier actions—one hand clamped around his waist, the other gripping his neck, forcing him to tilt his chin upward.
