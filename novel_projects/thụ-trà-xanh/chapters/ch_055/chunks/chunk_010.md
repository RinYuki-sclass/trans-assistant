"Shen Feizhi," Lin Sui called out to stop him. "Do you really think he just runs a company and a research institute? Do you have any idea what Lin Zizhuo left for him? Do you know how filthy the things in his hands are?"
Shen Feizhi looked back. Lin Sui met his gaze, only to realize with disappointment that there was neither anger nor curiosity in his eyes.
Instead, they were terrifyingly calm. "His affairs are not for you to comment on."
"I don't have time to play these bargaining games with you. A reduced sentence is the best offer I can make. If you agree, I'll be back to see you next week. If not, then we’re done here."
After Shen Feizhi finished speaking, he didn't stay a moment longer, regardless of how loudly Lin Sui shouted behind him.
The atmosphere of the detention center was oppressive. Once Shen Feizhi stepped outside, he lit a cigarette, smoked it slowly, and then took a car to the W.N Company.
He needed to tell Gu Suizhou about meeting Lin Sui today.
"President Shen?" Gu Suizhou's secretary said, surprised to see him. "Didn't you have business out of the office today?"
"The business is finished. I'm here to have a word with President Gu," Shen Feizhi said. "Where is he?"
"Uh…" The secretary thought for a moment. "He should be in a meeting in the conference room. Why don't you wait in the guest lounge for a moment while I go find him?"
