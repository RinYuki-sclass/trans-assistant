Gossip about the strange mark on his wrist began to spread through the crowd. Many were speculating whether this fallen illegitimate son was using dirty money to keep someone, or if he was being kept for money himself.

Shen Feizhi ignored the rumors. Once his ownership of the assets was confirmed, he sent all the medical equipment directly to W.N.
The official handling the matter kept a neutral expression, but he was secretly reeling.
A member of the Shen family sending things to W.N… this was fucking surreal.
“You’re certain? A total donation?” The man swallowed hard. “These instruments are worth at least a hundred million.”
“I’m certain.” Shen Feizhi signed his name without a hint of regret, then looked up. “Keep your mouth shut. I don’t want to hear any rumors.”
The man broke into a cold sweat instantly. “Y-yes, sir.”
Shen Feizhi nodded. After a moment’s hesitation, he turned his attention toward a certain building by the window. “Are Lin Sui and Old Master Shen still in there?”
“Ah, yes. It’s unlikely they’ll get out in this lifetime. From what I’ve seen, Lin Sui and the old man are both on the verge of insanity. You were right not to accept their applications to visit all these years.”
Shen Feizhi’s pace faltered. “What applications?”
How had he never heard C2 or anyone else mention this?
“You don’t remember? These people are given one chance every month to write to the outside world. Every year, Lin Sui’s letters were addressed to you. We’ve audited the contents; he just hoped you would come visit him.”
Shen Feizhi’s heart sank slightly. He sent a message to Gu Suizhou.
【S: Have you received any letters from Lin Sui over the past few years?】
After receiving a negative response, he let out a breath.
“I’ll go see them now.”
