Leapton had seen through Gu Suizhou’s little feelings for Shen Feizhi ever since the "Grateful Heart" incident back then.
But since Gu Suizhou had never explicitly told him, he had pretended not to know, watching his brother sink deeper and deeper into it step by step.
So he was quite surprised to receive a transmission from Gu Suizhou today.
He knew more or less what Gu Suizhou had been up to over the years. To be honest, he felt that besides himself, no one in this world could treat Mr. Shen better. Hearing that the pheromone compatibility between the two was so high, he was sincerely happy for his friend.
However, Gu Suizhou’s voice held no hint of joy as he said blankly, "But he doesn't seem to feel anything for me at all."
"Ah." Leapton couldn't figure Shen Feizhi out either.
Gu Suizhou asked, "Is it because we’re both Alphas?"
"I don't think Mr. Shen is the kind of person who cares about gender." Leapton sighed inwardly; it was truly rare to see Gu Suizhou like this. "Maybe… you just need to try a little harder?"
"How much harder can I try?" Gu Suizhou sounded a bit depressed. "I've done everything I can."
"Perhaps…" Leapton thought for a moment. "With someone like Mr. Shen, you should be more direct. And I mean in every way—not just in your actions, but in your words, too."
Gu Suizhou fell into deep thought.
