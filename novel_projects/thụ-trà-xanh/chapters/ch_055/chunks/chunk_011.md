Shen Feizhi stood in place for a few seconds before turning back to his office.
He opened his light screen and pulled up the surveillance footage for the top floor today.
W.N's surveillance network was exceptionally comprehensive, making it nearly impossible for an average person to hack, but Gu Suizhou had given all access permissions to Shen Feizhi.
Without a second thought, he locked onto Gu Suizhou’s office.
Since arriving at work this morning, the man had been completely absent-minded. His movements were listless, and he was visibly anxious and impatient, constantly checking the clock and his smartphone.
Zooming in, it became clear that every time Gu Suizhou tapped into his phone, the screen was on his chat logs with Shen Feizhi.

It wasn't until a short while ago, when Shen Feizhi sent him a message, that his expression finally softened.
But it didn't take long for the restlessness to return.
Shen Feizhi crossed his arms, his expression solemn as his fingers tapped the keyboard to switch to yesterday’s footage.
Yesterday morning seemed fine, but from the afternoon—after receiving a message from someone unknown—his entire demeanor soured, and he had hurried off work early.
At that time… he himself had seemed to be at the hospital.
Shen Feizhi suddenly realized.
Wasn't it right after he had met Ritter?
Recalling Gu Suizhou’s check-in message yesterday and his maneuver of getting off work early, it turned out he had known all along that Shen Feizhi wasn't home.
The moment he wasn't home, this man turned into a walking corpse, scratching his head in agitation—just how much did he lack a sense of security?
It wasn't until now that he realized just how severe Gu Suizhou’s psychological issues were, and to what extreme his lack of security had reached.
There were two knocks on the door, followed by Gu Suizhou’s voice, sounding in a good mood: "Brother Shen? You're here? Can I come in?"
"Come in." Shen Feizhi closed the light screen. "What were you doing just now?"
"Meeting an employee." Even though Gu Suizhou had received the secretary’s testimony, he chose to tell the truth.
