A hint of a smile drifted into Shen Feizhi's eyes. "Sit."
He gave a brief summary of what Lin Sui had said. "What do you think?"
"Tell him to get lost." Gu Suizhou didn't spare him any face at all, his tone very sharp. "I don't need him to investigate anything for me."
"My thoughts exactly," Shen Feizhi said. "But at least from this meeting, we have a direction to investigate."
Gu Suizhou asked, "You mean the people who helped intercept Lin Sui's letters all these years?"
"No, tracking that down would be too much trouble. Besides, that person is cautious; even if we found them, it might not lead us to the one pulling the strings." Shen Feizhi took a sip of the chocolate milk Gu Suizhou had brought him. "I've already contacted someone. I'm preparing to leak the news of my meeting with him today."
Gu Suizhou instantly understood his meaning.
He grinned. "Bro, you're devious."
Once the news was out, that person would certainly make a move.
What they needed to do wasn't to mend the fold after the sheep were gone, but to wait for the rabbit to run into the post.
Shen Feizhi arched an eyebrow. "I haven't decided exactly when to release it yet, though. Do you have any lingering grievances with Lin Sui?"
He was certain that once this news broke, Lin Sui’s life would be forfeit ten to one.
However, before he died, he wanted Gu Suizhou to vent his frustrations—whether it was killing him or carving him up, he should have his fill.
Gu Suizhou raised an eyebrow. "No. I've already done everything I needed to do over the past few years."
Shen Feizhi replied, "Alright, then I’ll move as soon as possible."
Gu Suizhou nodded, then stood up and moved behind him to massage his shoulders. "Bro, you're so good to me. Aren't you afraid of spoiling me rotten?"
"You're already rotten, you can't get any worse." Shen Feizhi enjoyed his service with a clear conscience. "Use more strength."
Gu Suizhou helplessly increased the pressure.
Just then, the secretary knocked on the door.
"President Gu…" Seeing the scene inside, the secretary faltered. "Um, there's an emergency meeting to attend."
