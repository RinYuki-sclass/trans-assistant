The sandwiches went down quickly; Shen Feizhi finished them in a few bites, gulped down a glass of milk, and hurried to the door.
As he was changing his shoes, Gu Suizhou was still dawdling, staring blankly at Lucky Dog.
Only when he heard the sound of the door latch did he stand up and grab Shen Feizhi's hand. "Wait."
Shen Feizhi turned back, his eyes asking: Something else?
Gu Suizhou looked aggrieved. "You haven't said goodbye to me yet."
Shen Feizhi: "…"

What a delicate drama queen.
Speechless, he said, "See you this afternoon."
…How had their relationship become so subtle?
Shen Feizhi walked down the street, unable to figure out the answer to that question.
Had it started that night? Truly, one should never succumb to desire; it was too easy for things to go sideways.
"Mr. Shen, I presume?" The receptionist at the Prosecutor's Office had been waiting for a long time. Upon seeing him, they immediately came forward to greet him. "Please come sign in and register first."
"Mm." Shen Feizhi nodded and took the pen.
When he raised his hand, the receptionist let out a gasp. "Sir, is your left hand injured? Do you need me to get a first-aid kit to treat it?"
Shen Feizhi followed his gaze and saw the mark left by a certain someone earlier that morning.
His skin reddened easily; even when it was nothing serious, it looked quite alarming.
Shen Feizhi calmly lowered his sleeve. "It’s fine."
The receptionist froze. As the shape and nature of the mark registered, his face belatedly flushed, and he cursed himself for being a busybody. "I—I'm sorry."
"It's alright." Shen Feizhi quickly finished signing his name. "Let’s go."
