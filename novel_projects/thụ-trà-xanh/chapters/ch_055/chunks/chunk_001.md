Chapter 55
"Not finished yet?"
Shen Feizhi looked with displeasure at the worker squatting on the floor repairing the nightstand handle. Behind him, the second hand of the clock ticked away, sounding like a death knell.
The worker was equally speechless. "It’s only been five minutes. This break was total, and you want a seamless repair? It’ll take at least another ten minutes."
As he explained, he couldn't help but complain to himself: Young people these days. He looks so dignified and refined, but who knew he played so wild in private!
Out of sight, out of mind—Shen Feizhi closed his eyes.
Finally, after a long ten minutes, the worker stood up and wiped his sweat. "All done!"
At that exact moment, the doorbell rang outside.
Shen Feizhi: "…"
He looked at the worker seriously. "Can you leave through the window?"
Worker: "?"
He had brought ropes, but this was a high-rise; that was a bit of a tall order, wasn't it?!
Shen Feizhi opened the nightstand and took out a stack of bills. "For your trouble."
The stack looked to be about ten thousand yuan.
The worker solemnly accepted it. "Don't you worry. Even if I hadn't brought my ropes, I'd be jumping out of here today!"
