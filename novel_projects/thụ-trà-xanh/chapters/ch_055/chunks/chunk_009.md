Shen Feizhi requested to go in alone, so the guards at the door yielded the space to him, keeping watch from the monitoring room instead.
After waiting on the other side of the glass for a moment, Lin Sui was finally led out.
His hands and feet were shackled. Having not been seen for several years, his hair was filthy and matted, his body bloated, and his complexion sallow. He lacked even a shred of the prestige Doctor Lin once possessed.
Upon seeing him, the corner of Lin Sui’s mouth curled. "You're finally willing to come see me."
Shen Feizhi didn't immediately bring up the fact that he hadn't received the letters. Instead, he impassively tilted his chin. "You have something to say to me?"
Lin Sui remained motionless, watching him with a stooped posture.
Shen Feizhi understood. He beckoned to the personnel nearby. "Please turn off the monitoring. We're going to discuss some private matters."
Given his background, they naturally didn't dare to offend him. After exchanging looks, they compromised: "Ten minutes."
Once everyone had left, Lin Sui spoke up immediately. "The fact that you’ve come to see me means you’ve made no progress."
Shen Feizhi arched an eyebrow. "You sound so certain?"
"Otherwise, you wouldn't have been willing to see me, would you?" Lin Sui gave a bitter laugh. "Shen Feizhi, I really did quite like you."
Shen Feizhi replied indifferently, "If this nonsense is all you have to say, then save your breath."
Lin Sui fell silent for a moment, pulling back his smile. "Don't you want to know who’s backing me?"
Shen Feizhi’s brows lowered. "Your terms?"

"You don't, but my 'dear' younger brother does." Lin Sui smirked.
