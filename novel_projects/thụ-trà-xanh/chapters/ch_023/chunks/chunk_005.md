Gu Suizhou: "…?"
His voice immediately flattened. "What makes you say that?"
"You sound normal now," Shen Feizhi said, relaxing his brow. "Your voice was too low just now. I thought you'd been beaten up."
Gu Suizhou took a deep breath. "Brother Shen, what were you doing this morning? You kept hanging up on my calls."
He sounded genuinely aggrieved, but Shen Feizhi remained unmoved.
"Something came up at the Shen family's old mansion."
"Oh," Gu Suizhou replied dryly. "So, what did you want when you called me so many times the other day?"
"Mm, I'll tell you when I get back. I'm hailing a cab right now," Shen Feizhi said. "Where are you?"
Gu Suizhou answered obediently, "At home."
Shen Feizhi rubbed his thumb across his smartphone's screen. "If I remember correctly, today's Thursday. You're still in school, right?"
Gu Suizhou: "…………"
He ground his teeth slightly. "Brother, you know I've been busy these past few days. Are you doing this on purpose?"
Shen Feizhi neither confirmed nor denied it. "I'll be home in half an hour."
With that, he abruptly ended the call, his expression gradually turning serious.
