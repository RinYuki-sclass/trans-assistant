How many coincidences could there possibly be in the world?
If it truly was Shen Feizhi, judging by the other man's attitude, he clearly didn't remember him. He couldn't afford to spook him; he wasn't even certain if Shen Feizhi wanted to be reunited.
If it wasn't…
He'd have to wait until he was absolutely certain before giving up. After finally finding a lead, he refused to abandon the trail so easily.
After searching for that person for ten years, how could he possibly settle for such a half-hearted conclusion?
The problem was… he still didn't know how to act around Shen Feizhi.
Noble Shen's identity remained highly suspicious, yet his words were undeniably venomous. Until he confirmed it was "him," Gu Suizhou couldn't suppress his urge to resist.

Gu Suizhou sighed, secretly urging himself to remain patient.
With conflicted emotions, he sat at the table and tore open the takeout bag.
Shrimp fried rice was his favorite.
Gu Suizhou had seen the prices at this restaurant before. How could Shen Feizhi, that stingy lower-class guy, have specifically gone there to buy it for him? Could it be that spending every day together had made him fall for his face?
Gu Suizhou pressed his lips together.
He'd had this thought two months ago too, but back then he'd only felt disgust. Now, there was a complicated, indescribable feeling mixed in.
Even if Shen Feizhi really was "that person," his feelings for that person were merely gratitude and a sense of repaying a debt. If that person asked him to repay with his body…
Wouldn't that be a bit awkward?
Gu Suizhou felt himself getting tangled up in his thoughts, and his movements became slightly rougher as he grabbed the lunch box.
Then, a receipt slipped out from between the box and the packaging bag, fluttering slowly down to his feet.
…The number on it—2 followed by four zeros—seared into his eyes like a brand.
Gu Suizhou's face darkened ten shades in half a second.
