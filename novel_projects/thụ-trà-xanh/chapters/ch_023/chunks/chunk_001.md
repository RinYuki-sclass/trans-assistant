Chapter 23
Shen Feizhi was locked in a confrontation with Elder Master Shen at the family's old mansion when Gu Suizhou called. Without hesitation, he immediately hung up.
"If it's urgent, you should answer it," Elder Master Shen said with an understanding tone.
"It's nothing important," Shen Feizhi replied, immediately setting his smart device to Do Not Disturb mode. "The month isn't up yet, Grandpa. What brings you here?"
Elder Shen shook his head. "I can't wait a month. Early next month, your mother and I will take Shen Wei to the ancestral rites, which will last at least ten days. The family can't be left unattended during that time."
The Shen family maintained an entire cemetery in the Third Star System, where a grand ancestral rites ceremony was held every decade.
Shen Feizhi interlaced his fingers on his knees and drawled, "The Shen family's ancestral rites… Shen Wei can attend, but I can't?"
Elder Shen fiddled with the walnut in his hand and closed his eyes. "Feizhi, your status… isn't suitable for now. Once you inherit control and no one can hold authority over you, you can add your name to the family register yourself."
The scene felt strangely familiar—much like the empty promises HR recruiters used to dangle during job interviews.
Shen Feizhi's mind was crystal clear, but he kept his thoughts to himself, readily agreeing, "Alright, when can I join the company?"
His immediate compliance only deepened Elder Shen's suspicion. "Feizhi, if you harbor any resentment, I would understand."
"You worry too much, Grandpa," Shen Feizhi replied, his gaze drifting meaningfully toward the floor-to-ceiling windows. Through them, a luxury car was slowly pulling into the Shen family's old mansion.
His memory was sharp; he clearly recalled that Lu Qian had arrived in the very same car at the banquet half a month earlier.
"Joining the family will give me opportunities to interact with Miss Lu," Shen Feizhi explained. "It's a win-win situation. Of course I wouldn't refuse."
Elder Master Shen studied his expression, finding no hint of falsehood. He rubbed the rim of his teacup with a complicated mix of emotions. "I don't object to you pursuing a marriage alliance with the Lu family, but remember, they're not our own people. Be cautious in your words and actions."
