"You suspect the Shen family framed you and are now gathering evidence. Am I right?"
Lu Qian's expression darkened, so preoccupied she forgot to thank the waiter who had just served their dishes.
Once the waiter left, she asked, "What else have you uncovered?"
"Nothing more about your family," Shen Feizhi replied. "But regarding the Shen family… I've shown my sincerity. What about yours, Miss Lu?"
After a moment's silence, Lu Qian pulled a magnetic card from her pocket. "This contains our family's entire transaction history with the Shen family from recent years. Shen Feizhi, I want to cooperate with you."
"Your goal is to bring down the Shen family." Shen Feizhi took the card and idly flipped it over in his hand. "My goal is simply to get revenge on Shen Wei and his ilk."

Lu Qian hummed in acknowledgment, her fingers drumming restlessly on the table. "So, what do you suggest?"
"My advice is to start with the period when W.N collapsed," Shen Feizhi said. He needed an informant who could freely enter and exit the Shen family's old mansion and interact with him frequently without arousing suspicion.
It was obvious that Lu Qian, whom he "liked" and who already had good relations with the Shen family, was the most suitable candidate.
He slid her a magnetic card, demonstrating his sincerity: "This contains Old Man Shen and Shen Wei's entire network of connections."
Lu Qian's jaw dropped in shock.
This far surpassed the sincerity she had offered!
Shen Feizhi, however, remained magnanimous. "If you accept this, can we exchange contact information?"
Lu Qian gave him a deep, complex look before silently pulling out her smartphone. "…Pleasure doing business with you."
Lu Qian's expensive meal wasn't particularly enjoyable.
