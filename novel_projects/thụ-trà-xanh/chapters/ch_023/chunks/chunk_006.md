He watched in stunned silence as his coat was stripped off, then this brat's hands actually started reaching for the buttons on his shirt.
"Are you insane?!" he demanded, dodging in disbelief and grabbing Gu Suizhou by the scruff of his neck to throw him away.
"Tsk," Gu Suizhou clicked his tongue, his gaze lingering regretfully on Shen Feizhi's chest. He sighed theatrically, "I was just trying to help you change thoroughly , you know?"
"Get lost," Shen Feizhi snapped coldly.
What kind of shock has he suffered in the past two weeks?
Gu Suizhou pursed his lips but took the insult in stride, temporarily backing down. His gaze shifted to the High Tier takeout bag in Shen Feizhi's hand. "Is this… food you bought specially for me?"
Shen Feizhi had been staring at him with a look reserved for imbeciles for quite some time. Only at this did he reluctantly tear his gaze away.
He considered the bag for a moment before nodding without hesitation. "Yeah."
Is there really any difference between buying something "on the way" and buying it "specially"?
Round it up, and they're practically the same thing anyway, right?

Gu Suizhou clenched his jaw.
Remembering the mark he'd accidentally glimpsed on Shen Feizhi's chest, his heart felt like it housed a restless cat, its claws raking his chest with both pain and an maddening itch.
After leaving Xu Zhong's place that day, he'd gone to the Ninth District's Underground Music Group to vent his frustration for two days before finally calming down.
He hadn't been idle during those two days, investigating everything he should and shouldn't have. Yet the information he uncovered was scant.
Naturally, Shen Feizhi had been a prime suspect. After all, besides that scar, Shen Feizhi was nearly five years his senior, and his records before the age of ten were a complete mystery—a perfect match for everything Gu Suizhou currently knew.
