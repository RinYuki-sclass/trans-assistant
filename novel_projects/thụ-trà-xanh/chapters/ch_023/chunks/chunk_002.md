She playfully hooked her finger into the pocket of Shen Feizhi's shirt. "Are we going out to play today?"
"Yeah, I'm taking you somewhere." Knowing the mansion's surveillance cameras were watching his every move, Shen Feizhi suppressed the urge to push her away. "Let's go."
Prices in the First District were absurdly high—a casual meal for one could easily cost four figures.
Yet Shen Feizhi sat in a seven-star restaurant, ordering with unwavering composure.
He calmly ordered four or five dishes and handed the menu to Lu Qian. "See if there's anything you'd like."
As the Lu family's most pampered young miss, Lu Qian didn't hold back. She ticked off several items, causing the nearby cleaning staff to gasp audibly.
Shen Feizhi remained unfazed. "While we're waiting for the food, we can chat."
Lu Qian blinked her beautiful eyes mischievously. "Chat about what? Love? Romance?"
"Let's talk about the feud between you and the Shen family," Shen Feizhi said, dropping a bombshell.
Lu Qian's expression faltered momentarily, then she casually shrugged. "Shen Wei and I? I already told you about that."
"Don't play coy with me," Shen Feizhi said, lowering his gaze to her hand resting on the table. "Your fingers are a shade paler than before. You're nervous."

Lu Qian instinctively withdrew her hand into her sleeve.
Only after retracting it did she realize her gesture had tacitly confirmed his observation.
Her smile faded. "Little heartthrob, you're too sharp for your own good. Not cute at all."
Though four years older than Shen Feizhi, she suddenly felt she couldn't quite read this younger man.
Shen Feizhi remained impassive. "You deliberately leaked information about the Shen family's third floor, didn't you? All to get me to steal their confidential documents?"
Lu Qian clicked her tongue. "Should I admit it or deny it?"
"I've looked into your family's history," Shen Feizhi continued, speaking as if to himself. "Ten years ago, when the Shen family withdrew its investment from W.N, your family took over the subsequent funding. But W.N soon suffered a catastrophic setback, causing your family to lose a fortune."
