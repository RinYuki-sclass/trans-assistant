Chapter 13

"Forty-seven minutes."

Shen Feizhi squatted by a pillar near the parking garage exit on the second basement level of the venerable old establishment, finally finishing his first cigarette since arriving in this world.

"Twelve minutes slower than I expected."

He glanced at Gu Suizhou, who was approaching slowly, and tapped the time displayed on his smart screen.

Gu Suizhou shot him a look but remained silent, stopping a meter away and extending his hand.

Shen Feizhi raised an eyebrow, understanding immediately, and placed his smart device in Gu Suizhou's palm.

Gu Suizhou tapped the screen and remarked casually, "You don't even have five contacts in your address book?"

The unspoken implication was clear: What's wrong with your social life?

Shen Feizhi shrugged.

How would I know how the original owner of this body lived?

He'd checked the original owner's smart device when he first arrived—it was practically empty. Even the posts on his social media received likes only from himself.

Gu Suizhou wasn't expecting an answer anyway. After a few quick taps, he tossed the device back to Shen Feizhi.

"I've added the man's contact information, his assistant's contact information, and the identity and contact details of his current business partner."

"Not bad," he said.

Assuming the boy was just throwing a childish tantrum, Shen Feizhi paid him no mind. "I'll get you the account details by Monday."

"Fine," Gu Suizhou said. "So, you'll be busy these next few days?"

"Mm-hmm." Shen Feizhi stood up and hailed a nearby shared hovercar. "Don't bother me too much in the meantime."

"Relax, I won't," Gu Suizhou replied with a mirthless smile, his eyes curving into crescents. "I'm heading out tomorrow."

"To the Underground City?" Shen Feizhi thought of the blond-haired kid. "Looking for Leapton?"

"You remember him clearly enough," Gu Suizhou scoffed. "But you'll be disappointed—I'm not going to see him. You're so all-knowing and omniscient—how could you possibly not know what I'm doing tomorrow?"

Shen Feizhi's hand froze mid-motion as he reached for another cigarette.

He hadn't thought of it earlier, but now that the brat mentioned it, a faint memory surfaced.

Tomorrow, it seemed, marked the tenth anniversary of Lin Zizhuo and Gu Lang's suicides.

He recalled a passage from the original documentation describing the circumstances of Lin Sui's parents—no, Gu Suizhou's parents' suicides.

Lin Sui later told the original owner about it himself, after their relationship had deepened.

Lin Sui boarded at school and rarely needed to return home. To make it easier for Gu Suizhou to commute, Lin Zizhuo and Gu Lang bought a new house near the school.

The new house was very close by. One day, Lin Sui finished school early and picked up his younger brother on the way home. They walked hand-in-hand beneath streetlights that glowed as bright as daylight. Lin Sui even bought Gu Suizhou a string of candied hawthorns, intending to share them with their parents when they got home.

But they never got to eat the candied hawthorns.

As soon as they opened the front door, they saw two bodies hanging from the rafters.

One was their father, the other their mother.

Just then, it started to rain, and a flash of lightning illuminated Lin Zizhuo and Gu Lang's deathly pale faces.

They were very quiet, just looking a little sad.

Shen Feizhi closed his eyes briefly, his thumb gently rubbing the lighter's button as he lit a cigarette. "I'm sorry," he said.

Before entering this world, he had needed to grasp these basic facts to find its critical junctures. He hadn't intentionally pried into anyone's private affairs.

But whether intentional or not, the information he now possessed had violated the original inhabitants of this world.

He really should apologize.

Gu Suizhou looked somewhat surprised. "Brother Shen, are you apologizing to me?"

"Did you need a subject to understand?" Shen Feizhi didn't shy away from the topic. "I'm truly sorry. I didn't intentionally pry into your affairs. The W.N. matter is crucial, but the reason… I can't tell you yet."

Gu Suizhou narrowed his eyes, looking quite pleased.

He even boldly stepped forward, snatched the cigarette from Shen Feizhi's hand, stomped it out on the ground, and kicked it into the nearby trash pile. "If you won't tell me, I won't ask. Anyway, since you want to know about W.N.'s past, and I want to know too, that means we're on the same side now, right?"
