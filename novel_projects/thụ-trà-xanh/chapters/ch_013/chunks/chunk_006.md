Shen Feizhi observed the entire scene, thoughtfully twirling his pen between his fingers.
He'd initially assumed Gu Suizhou was just a school bully.
But Jiang Che's extraordinary tolerance for this troublemaker suggested something more. There could only be one explanation:
Gu Suizhou was a troublemaker with connections.
But the Gu family had long fallen into decline. Even Lin Sui had been reduced to working as a doctor for the Shen family. Where could this barely-grown minor possibly find any connections?
It wasn't as if High School lacked wealthy investors. Before coming here, Shen Feizhi had done his research. There were only a handful of families across Blue Star whose influence made the teachers here think twice. If it were a business dynasty, they'd have to be on par with the Shen family.
If it were a research or military clan… Gu Suizhou was keeping quite a lot from him.
Shen Feizhi glanced up and down at the boy buried in his smart device, head bowed, and said nothing further.
Whatever. His grades are none of my business. As long as he doesn't interfere with my plans, it doesn't matter if he becomes a success or a failure.
