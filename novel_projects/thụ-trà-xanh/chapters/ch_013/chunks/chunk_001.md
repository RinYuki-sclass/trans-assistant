Chapter 13
"Forty-seven minutes."
Shen Feizhi squatted by a pillar near the parking garage exit on the second basement level of the venerable old establishment, finally finishing his first cigarette since arriving in this world.
"Twelve minutes slower than I expected."
He glanced at Gu Suizhou, who was approaching slowly, and tapped the time displayed on his smart screen.
Gu Suizhou shot him a look but remained silent, stopping a meter away and extending his hand.
Shen Feizhi raised an eyebrow, understanding immediately, and placed his smart device in Gu Suizhou's palm.
Gu Suizhou tapped the screen and remarked casually, "You don't even have five contacts in your address book?"
The unspoken implication was clear: What's wrong with your social life?
Shen Feizhi shrugged.
How would I know how the original owner of this body lived?
He'd checked the original owner's smart device when he first arrived—it was practically empty. Even the posts on his social media received likes only from himself.
Gu Suizhou wasn't expecting an answer anyway. After a few quick taps, he tossed the device back to Shen Feizhi.
"I've added the man's contact information, his assistant's contact information, and the identity and contact details of his current business partner."

Shen Feizhi clicked his tongue.
No wonder it took longer than expected. He went above and beyond the call of duty.
"Not bad," he said.
Despite receiving this rare praise, Gu Suizhou's brows remained tightly furrowed. He even took two steps back, unwound his red scarf, and stuffed it into his bag.
Assuming the boy was just throwing a childish tantrum, Shen Feizhi paid him no mind. "I'll get you the account details by Monday."
