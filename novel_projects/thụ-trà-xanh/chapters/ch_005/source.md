Four hours later, the two men, having walked all the way home, stared at the small attic before them and fell into a shared silence.

Gu Suizhou's expression was wooden. "I thought a place on Lanxie Road would at least be a single-story flat or a duplex."

In Blue Star's First District, every inch of land was worth its weight in gold. The price per square meter for residential property could nearly match the annual living expenses of someone from outside the Fifth District.

Within a ten-mile radius centered on Lanxie Road, property prices skyrocketed exponentially.

The residents here weren't short on money; their homes were naturally single-story flats, duplexes, or villas.

Someone like Shen Feizhi, living in a 50-square-meter attic… was a rare sight indeed.

Shen Feizhi felt just as numb.

Before seeing this dilapidated attic, he'd shared Gu Suizhou's assumption.

But the outcome was both unexpected and logical. The original owner had been broke, surviving on a meager allowance from the Shen family. He'd squandered most of his money on food, drink, women, and gambling, so of course he wouldn't live anywhere decent.

The fact that he'd managed to buy this attic at all was already…

"Well, well, Mr. Shen? Back so late today?"

The two men, in the middle of unlocking the door, froze in unison. They turned their heads as if copy-pasted.

It was a noblewoman.

The keys in her hand instantly revealed her identity—the neighbor from across the hall on the floor below.

The noblewoman gave Gu Suizhou a strange look, then shot Shen Feizhi a glance filled with disdain.

Shen Feizhi opened his mouth to greet her.

But Gu Suizhou beat him to it. "Good evening, Big Sister."

Shen Feizhi's tongue tied itself in a knot as he swiftly swallowed the "Auntie" he'd been about to say.

The noblewoman immediately choked back her nosy remarks, her face beaming. "Yes, yes, good evening to you too."

She fumbled in her pocket, pulled out a gold pendant, and strode forward to shove it into Gu Suizhou's pocket. "Good boy, a gift from Big Sister. Take it, quickly!"

Shen Feizhi: "…?"

That works?

Gu Suizhou quickly tried to refuse. "Big Sister, I know you're beautiful and kind-hearted, but this is really inappropriate!"

Lost in the chorus of "Big Sister," the noblewoman completely forgot herself.

She shot Shen Feizhi a disgusted look, her tone and actions leaving no room for argument. "Relying on others for your meals is unreliable. Take it! Go buy yourself something nice to eat. You're at that age where you need to grow!"

Shen Feizhi, the target of the veiled insult, turned livid.

Gu Suizhou hesitated for a split second. In that brief opening, the gold pendant was successfully pocketed, and the noblewoman, quick as a flash, also slipped the jade ring from her middle finger into his pocket.

The noblewoman was satisfied. "Now that's more like it."

The next second, her tone shifted abruptly as she addressed Shen Feizhi. "By the way, your landlord said if you don't pay this month's rent, you can take your trash and get out!"

Shen Feizhi: "…Right. Thank you."

So, this shabby attic wasn't even bought—it was rented.

The plan to sell the attic and buy a house in another district was officially shattered.

The noblewoman exchanged a few more enthusiastic pleasantries with Gu Suizhou before finally departing, her curls bouncing as she clicked away in her high heels.

Her parting glare at Shen Feizhi seemed to scream: If you're broke, stop corrupting this innocent boy!

Shen Feizhi squeezed his eyes shut.

In all his years, he'd never been subjected to such passive-aggressive scorn without being able to curse back.

As the lock clicked shut, Gu Suizhou opened the door with a beaming smile. "Please, come in, Brother Shen."

Shen Feizhi glanced at the jewelry Gu Suizhou was now toying with in his hands and sneered, "Have you no shame?"

"Earning money with my face is still a skill. What's there to be ashamed of?" Gu Suizhou shrugged nonchalantly.

Unexpectedly, Shen Feizhi actually nodded. "You have a point."

With that, he entered the room and shut the door in one fluid motion. As he passed Gu Suizhou…

…he also smoothly snatched the gold pendant from his hand.

Gu Suizhou: "…"

Shen Feizhi leisurely changed into his slippers, sat down on the small central sofa, and tossed the pendant in the air. He flashed Gu Suizhou a rare smile. "Snatching is also a skill. Let's just say this covers your rent, how about it?"

The corner of Gu Suizhou's eye twitched. He forced a smile. "Sure, why not? Brother Shen, forget a gold pendant—even if you wanted the jade ring, I'd…"

His voice died abruptly.

Shen Feizhi's gaze was fixed on the remaining jade ring in Gu Suizhou's hand. He prompted, "Go on. You'd what?"

Gu Suizhou suppressed the urge to lunge at him. "…I'd hold onto it for you."

Shen Feizhi propped his head on his arm, lowered his head, and chuckled.

On the way over, the little brat had told him: [Lin Sui might be trying to harm me. I can't go back for now. Brother Shen, please take me in.]

Since Shen Feizhi had decided to let him stay, he'd naturally anticipated the whole "eating and living together" situation and readily agreed.

But they couldn't just drift along aimlessly like this. The kid was still in school. While Shen Feizhi wasn't exactly a humanitarian, he possessed the basic decency not to interfere with a student's education. No matter what, he had to find a paying job tomorrow.

Shen Feizhi sighed softly and rose, stretching his shoulders as he faced the window.

The small attic was sparsely furnished and lacked any lived-in warmth—likely the result of the original owner's constant carousing and staying out all night.

At least it saved him the trouble of being disgusted by the original owner's traces.

"There's only one bedroom. I'll take the bed, you take the sofa. Any objections?"

Gu Suizhou rolled his eyes dramatically at Shen Feizhi's back. "None, Brother Shen."

Shen Feizhi: "I don't have any new pajamas. Are my worn ones okay?"

Gu Suizhou's brow furrowed, and he threw a few phantom punches into the air. "They're fine, Brother Shen. I'm not picky."

Shen Feizhi: "If that hoof of yours even leaves the ground, I'll separate your head from your neck."

"Huh?" Gu Suizhou froze mid-kick, stuck in an awkward, half-raised pose.

His gaze, fixed on Shen Feizhi's back, was filled with utter disbelief.

Shen Feizhi turned around calmly and pointed at the window. "Reflection."

Gu Suizhou's lips curled downward. "Fuck."

" Tsk ." Shen Feizhi frowned, strode forward with his long legs, and rapped Gu Suizhou on the head. "If you're living with me, we're setting three ground rules. Rule number one: no swearing."

Gu Suizhou scoffed. "Hold on, you never mentioned any rules before."

"You have them now," Shen Feizhi said. "Since you're under my care, I have to be responsible for you."

If the future flower of the motherland wilts on my watch, it'll ruin my karma for the next life.

Behind his back, Gu Suizhou's hands clenched into tight fists, veins bulging on their backs. He asked politely, "So what are the other two rules?"

Shen Feizhi: "They're blank. I'll fill them in when I think of something."

Fill in my ass, Gu Suizhou thought furiously.

He watched helplessly as the certain someone —who clearly had no regard for the young—brushed past him. Seething, Gu Suizhou kicked the sofa several times.

Only when the sound of running water echoed from the bathroom did his expression turn cold. He reached out and snatched the backpack from the sofa.

A man like Shen Feizhi wouldn't just walk away from the Shen family empty-handed, he reasoned. He might have hidden crucial items, but there should at least be some documents or something, right?

Gu Suizhou carefully unzipped the backpack.

—It was completely empty.

Gu Suizhou: "…"

He squeezed his eyes shut.

Such a cautious bastard.

He left no physical evidence. He must have memorized everything.

Listening to the rushing water from the bathroom, Gu Suizhou's senses were flooded with the bitter coffee pheromones Shen Feizhi had left clinging to him.

Fuck, this is so damn annoying.

He hated coffee more than anything.

When Shen Feizhi emerged from the shower, he found Gu Suizhou sprawled on the sofa, dead to the world.

He was fastidious about cleanliness, yet he was still wearing his own clothes. His shirt was buttoned only at the chest, hanging loosely on his frame as he walked over, casually drying his hair with a towel in one hand.

He had to admit, Gu Suizhou's face was flawlessly beautiful.

Even from this unflattering high angle, he was so striking that a casual snapshot could pass for a professional portrait.

Shen Feizhi gazed at his long, dense eyelashes, recalling what the boy had said on the way here.

Before Gu Suizhou's mother passed away, she had changed his name but died before she could tell Lin Sui.

It was something Lin Sui could have easily discovered by glancing at a homework assignment or an ID card, yet he had always called him "Lin Zhou." Clearly, he couldn't care less about this younger brother.

Shen Feizhi slowly crouched down.

The boy had no gland on the back of his neck and didn't react negatively to his pheromones, yet he had instantly identified their scent.

Besides the possibility that his pheromones were simply too aggressive, the only other explanation was that the boy hadn't presented yet but was naturally sensitive to pheromones.

The corner of Shen Feizhi's mouth curved into a barely perceptible smile.

"What exactly did you do to convince Doctor Lin you're an Omega?"

The boy's breathing was steady, and he appeared to be sound asleep, ignoring him completely.

"When you wake up, remember to take a shower."

Shen Feizhi couldn't be bothered to expose his clumsy act. After tossing out the words, he casually threw the blanket from the sofa over him and walked back to the bedroom, hands in his pockets.

The sound of the door closing echoed through the room. Gu Suizhou silently opened his eyes.

His brows furrowed tightly as he stared at the closed door, a flicker of uncertain hesitation in his gaze.

When Shen Feizhi had leaned over earlier, his shirt collar had gaped open. Peeking through a sliver of his eyelids, Gu Suizhou had seen a striking mark on the man's chest.

It was dark, a stark and jarring contrast against his fair skin.

What a ruthless bastard, getting a tattoo in such a dangerous spot.

Gu Suizhou shook his head, momentarily dismissing the thought. He lay there for a while longer, staring at the ceiling, before finally slipping into the bathroom.

When Shen Feizhi woke up the next morning, he was alone in the apartment.

A note lay on the living room coffee table, the handwriting a chaotic scrawl—

【Brother Shen, I've gone to school. Looking forward to seeing you again on Friday. I'll wait obediently for you at the school gate~ Don't worry about me ^ ^】

Shen Feizhi blinked several times, his eyes stinging from the sheer ugliness of the script, before he managed to extract the useful information.

So the little brat attended a boarding high school and only came home once a week.

That certainly saved him a lot of trouble.

Pleased, he tore the note in half, tossed it into the trash, and grabbed his smartphone before heading out.

No matter which world he was in, making money was never easy.

Jobs in the First District were scarce, and after three or four days of searching, not a single employer would hire a punk with only a middle school education. Even the foreman at a construction site rejected him, complaining he was too fair-skinned and delicate for manual labor.

To think that he, Shen Feizhi—who had been an emperor, a savior, and the world's richest man—was now reduced to being unable to find a job.

If this continued, he'd have no choice but to dust off his old hacking skills and earn some black money to invest in the stock market.

He really didn't want to resort to such illegal activities unless it was absolutely necessary.

He sat on the stone steps, wearily pinching the bridge of his nose.

The sky had darkened, and a rose-colored nebula gradually materialized in Blue Star's night sky.

Shen Feizhi tilted his head back, his gaze fixed for a long moment on a point beyond the nebula, invisible to the naked eye.

Few knew that an Energy Storm Eye existed there—a nexus connecting various world planes. It was through this energy channel that he had descended into each Small World.

Perhaps… I can use this to proactively contact the Main Star?

His long, slender fingers tapped a rhythmic pattern on his knee. After completing two full cycles with all five fingers, he rose unhurriedly, dusted off the back of his clothes, and headed toward the First District's underground city.

There, nestled deep beneath the surface, was Blue Star's highest-level research institute: W.N.

A dedicated shuttle bus ran to the underground city. Shen Feizhi splurged on a standing-room-only ticket and endured a full day and night of travel to reach the institute.

He stared at the heavily fortified gate and, without hesitation, raised his hand to press the doorbell.

"Who is it?"

A holographic screen flickered to life, revealing the impatient face of a guard inside.

Shen Feizhi replied, "I'm here to apply for a job."

The guard's expression went blank for a split second before twisting into a sneer. "To apply for a research position here, you need a PhD from a top-two global university, at least ten publications in top-tier journals, and a minimum of three years of work experience…"

He rattled off the requirements until he was practically foaming at the mouth, finally pausing to gasp for air. "…Are you aware of these requirements?"

"I am," Shen Feizhi replied, having listened politely before giving a calm nod.

So composed? The guard stared in astonishment at the young man, who looked barely twenty.

He must be a hidden prodigy, a young genius!

The guard instantly snapped to attention, his posture rigid. "Sir, please state your name and upload your resume through Screen 1!"

"I don't have a resume," Shen Feizhi said.

So brilliant he doesn't even deign to write a resume?

The guard's expression could now only be described as one of high alert. "Could it be that you—"

"I don't have any of the qualifications you mentioned."

Shen Feizhi's tone was neither servile nor overbearing. He even lowered his gaze kindly, as if to reassure the guard not to be so tense.

"I'm here to apply for a janitor position."
