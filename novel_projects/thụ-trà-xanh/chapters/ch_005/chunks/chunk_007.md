Jobs in the First District were scarce, and after three or four days of searching, not a single employer would hire a punk with only a middle school education. Even the foreman at a construction site rejected him, complaining he was too fair-skinned and delicate for manual labor.
To think that he, Shen Feizhi—who had been an emperor, a savior, and the world's richest man—was now reduced to being unable to find a job.
If this continued, he'd have no choice but to dust off his old hacking skills and earn some black money to invest in the stock market.
He really didn't want to resort to such illegal activities unless it was absolutely necessary.
He sat on the stone steps, wearily pinching the bridge of his nose.
The sky had darkened, and a rose-colored nebula gradually materialized in Blue Star's night sky.
Shen Feizhi tilted his head back, his gaze fixed for a long moment on a point beyond the nebula, invisible to the naked eye.
Few knew that an Energy Storm Eye existed there—a nexus connecting various world planes. It was through this energy channel that he had descended into each Small World.
Perhaps… I can use this to proactively contact the Main Star?
His long, slender fingers tapped a rhythmic pattern on his knee. After completing two full cycles with all five fingers, he rose unhurriedly, dusted off the back of his clothes, and headed toward the First District's underground city.
There, nestled deep beneath the surface, was Blue Star's highest-level research institute: W.N.
A dedicated shuttle bus ran to the underground city. Shen Feizhi splurged on a standing-room-only ticket and endured a full day and night of travel to reach the institute.
He stared at the heavily fortified gate and, without hesitation, raised his hand to press the doorbell.
"Who is it?"
A holographic screen flickered to life, revealing the impatient face of a guard inside.
