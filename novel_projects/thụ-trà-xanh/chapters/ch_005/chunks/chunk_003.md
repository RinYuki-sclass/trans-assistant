"Earning money with my face is still a skill. What's there to be ashamed of?" Gu Suizhou shrugged nonchalantly.
Unexpectedly, Shen Feizhi actually nodded. "You have a point."
With that, he entered the room and shut the door in one fluid motion. As he passed Gu Suizhou…
…he also smoothly snatched the gold pendant from his hand.
Gu Suizhou: "…"
Shen Feizhi leisurely changed into his slippers, sat down on the small central sofa, and tossed the pendant in the air. He flashed Gu Suizhou a rare smile. "Snatching is also a skill. Let's just say this covers your rent, how about it?"

The corner of Gu Suizhou's eye twitched. He forced a smile. "Sure, why not? Brother Shen, forget a gold pendant—even if you wanted the jade ring, I'd…"
His voice died abruptly.
Shen Feizhi's gaze was fixed on the remaining jade ring in Gu Suizhou's hand. He prompted, "Go on. You'd what?"
Gu Suizhou suppressed the urge to lunge at him. "…I'd hold onto it for you."
Shen Feizhi propped his head on his arm, lowered his head, and chuckled.
On the way over, the little brat had told him: [Lin Sui might be trying to harm me. I can't go back for now. Brother Shen, please take me in.]
Since Shen Feizhi had decided to let him stay, he'd naturally anticipated the whole "eating and living together" situation and readily agreed.
But they couldn't just drift along aimlessly like this. The kid was still in school. While Shen Feizhi wasn't exactly a humanitarian, he possessed the basic decency not to interfere with a student's education. No matter what, he had to find a paying job tomorrow.
Shen Feizhi sighed softly and rose, stretching his shoulders as he faced the window.
The small attic was sparsely furnished and lacked any lived-in warmth—likely the result of the original owner's constant carousing and staying out all night.
At least it saved him the trouble of being disgusted by the original owner's traces.
"There's only one bedroom. I'll take the bed, you take the sofa. Any objections?"
Gu Suizhou rolled his eyes dramatically at Shen Feizhi's back. "None, Brother Shen."
