When Lin Zizhuo and Gu Lang were captured and imprisoned, Shen Feizhi had already been forgotten in the basement for several days.
Having gone without food for days, his breaths were coming shallow and ragged, a sight that left Lin Zizhuo and Gu Lang momentarily stunned.
They saved Shen Feizhi.
Since going back up meant facing further inhumane abuse, Lin Zizhuo and Gu Lang figured they might as well nurse the child back to health first. After all, no one in this dungeon recognized Shen Feizhi's face, so they simply kept him in the small room next to theirs.
Every day, the two would skimp on their own meals to save a few bites for Shen Feizhi. By the time Shen Feizhi’s fever broke and his spirits lifted, the two of them had grown significantly thinner.
Shen Feizhi was already ten years old at the time; filled with gratitude, he helped Lin Zizhuo and Gu Lang in whatever ways he could.
It could be said that the Lin couple saved Shen Feizhi, and in return, they found their only source of solace in that dungeon.
Unfortunately, the good times did not last, and Lin Zizhuo and Gu Lang were eventually taken away.
Shen Feizhi waited for a long time before they finally returned. The light had completely vanished from Gu Lang’s eyes as she pressed an injection vial into his hand with a pleading look: "Sweetheart, can Auntie ask a favor of you?"
Back then, there was nothing Gu Lang could ask of Shen Feizhi that he would ever refuse.
Aside from his own mother, this couple was the most important people in his life.
And so, before leaving, he gave them his word—on his very life—that he would ensure Gu Suizhou grew up safely, and that he would deliver the medicine and their message to the child.
He had been a man of action since he was young; having given Gu Lang his promise, he immediately broke his own arm. His cries of pain finally drew the butler’s attention, and he was taken out for medical treatment.
It was while he was at the hospital that he heard the news of their double suicide.
Shen Feizhi stood frozen for a long time. He never could have imagined that two people as gentle as Lin Zizhuo and Gu Lang would have such a rebellious son—one who didn't even give him time to grieve before buying a shuttle ticket to go off on a solo trip to clear his head.

A six-year-old child, going out all by himself?!
Shen Feizhi was truly driven to the brink of madness. He used what little pocket money he had to buy a ticket for the same shuttle.
To prevent any accidents from happening, he spent that entire period cramming knowledge on shuttle safety.
It was originally meant as a precaution, but as fate would have it, the very thing he feared most came true. They really did encounter a crash. Fortunately, his luck held out; he managed to escape with the child, and although they plummeted onto a barren planet, they at least kept their lives.
