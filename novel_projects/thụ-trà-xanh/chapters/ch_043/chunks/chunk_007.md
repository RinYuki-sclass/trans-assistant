Thus, the Main Star dispatched someone in an attempt at a final salvation for this world.
Since Shen Feizhi’s half-dead body was no longer usable, the Main Star took a risk and sent a "Manager" over along with their physical body. Little did they know that the individual would harbor malicious intent, directly throwing Shen Feizhi’s body into the energy channel during the return journey.
From that moment on, their lives and identities were swapped.
When A11 saw Shen Feizhi emerge from the interior, he took a token step forward to ask, "Are you alright?"
"Not really," Shen Feizhi admitted truthfully, his face pale. "I feel like vomiting."
"That's normal." A11 looked at him with admiration. "It's already quite remarkable that you can still stand after direct contact with the Energy Orb. Shen Feizhi, you are very well-suited to join A-tier Management."
"No need," Shen Feizhi said. "I'd still rather go back."

One of the compensations the Main Star had offered him was actually a direct appointment to their high-ranking officials. Putting this oversight aside, his abilities and experience were more than sufficient to excel in the role.
But Shen Feizhi wasn't interested; he had long since grown tired of it all. Moreover, now that he knew his true identity, staying here held no meaning.
He had his own home.
He had to go back.
The promises he made to Lin Zizhuo and Gu Lang were still unfulfilled, and he didn't even know how that brat was doing.
At the thought of Gu Suizhou's face, Shen Feizhi let out a silent breath.
He finally understood why that guy was always so clingy.
He asked A11, "Do you have the memories from when your people took over my identity after I left?"
A11 shook his head sheepishly. "We completely lost contact with your world during that period."
Even the recent energy field had only been forced open after they noticed something was amiss.
Shen Feizhi asked, "Then was the critical node of my world actually clearing Lin Zizhuo and Gu Lang's names?"
"No." A11 shook his head. "But you don't need to worry. That critical node… has already been reached."
