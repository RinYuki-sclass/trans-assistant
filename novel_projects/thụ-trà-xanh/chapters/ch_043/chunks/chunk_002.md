There was nothing here—just a vast, sterile expanse of white in every direction.
Was he supposed to sit around like an idiot doing nothing, staring at the ceiling while his body mended itself?
A11 weighed the logic of his statement and eventually decided he had a point.
With a wave of its hand, the space instantly transformed into a sprawling villa packed with amenities—a swimming pool, a garden, a gym, and a game room. It had everything.
"Any other requests?" it asked.
Shen Feizhi: "…"
That was the convenience of being a high-dimensional being: total freedom to do as one pleased.
In the past, he wouldn't have cared about such luxuries. He wasn't the impatient type; even with nothing to do, he could have stayed quiet and still for a very, very long time.
But things were different now.
He had thought everything on Blue Star was settled before he arrived here, but he had overlooked one very serious problem: Gu Suizhou.
He couldn't shake the feeling that he hadn't handled his departure properly. It would be best to go back and check as soon as possible.

A11 couldn’t bear the weight of his gaze any longer: "I’ll arrange for doctors to come and get your body back in shape as soon as possible. Once you reach the threshold, everything that belongs to you will be returned."
This was likely the best solution A11 could come up with.
Shen Feizhi didn’t push any further: "Fine."
A11 left, and not long after, a youth with a beauty so striking it bordered on androgynous suddenly appeared in the space. He was a head shorter than Shen Feizhi.
He wore a sweet smile, showing off two distinct little snags: "Shen Feizhi, right? Hello! I’m C2. Your body is under my protection for the time being!"
Shen Feizhi arched an eyebrow: "An Omega?"
C2’s smile vanished instantly: "I’m an Alpha!"
Shen Feizhi let out a soft chuckle.
