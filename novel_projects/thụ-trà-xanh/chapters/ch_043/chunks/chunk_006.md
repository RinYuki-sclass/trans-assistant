Before accessing these memories, Shen Feizhi never imagined he once had such a good temper.
He had actually possessed the patience to let Gu Suizhou run wild, tell him bedtime stories, and cook for him.
He also finally understood where his wood-carving skills and the scar on his chest had come from.
No matter how precocious he had been back then, he was still only eleven years old. His hunting skills were learned on the fly; because Gu Suizhou had spent that day sobbing and mumbling about how long it had been since he’d eaten meat, Shen Feizhi had plucked up the courage to hunt a wild boar.
Who would have thought that while he’d successfully killed the boar, he would be attacked by a wild rhino? Its sharp horn had gouged a massive wound right across his chest.
In that moment, Shen Feizhi thought he was going to die.
Yet, he had dragged his blood-soaked body back to the cave, clutching the boar meat, and silently cooked it for Gu Suizhou.
He had bandaged himself haphazardly and stubbornly held on for two days until the wound began to fester and the bleeding stopped.
Perhaps it was because there was simply nothing left to bleed. As the inflammation worsened, Shen Feizhi’s consciousness began to drift, yet when he heard Gu Suizhou crying from a nightmare, he still forced himself to speak and comfort him.
As it turned out, the kid had a sharp nose; even without being able to see, he realized Shen Feizhi was injured.
He didn’t want to worry the kid, so he simply went out and personally tore the scabs open again, drained the pus, and made two extra cuts along the shape of the scar, making it look like a cross clawed deep into the position of his heart.

He told Gu Suizhou it was a tattoo, a blessing to ensure they could return home safely.
In hindsight, it was a truly foolish thing to do.
But if time were to repeat itself, Shen Feizhi would likely do it again.
That cross wasn’t just to comfort Gu Suizhou; it was to comfort himself.
When driven to a desperate corner, a person always needs to grasp at a piece of driftwood—even if they’re just lying to themselves, it gives them something to hope for.
He had indeed honored his oath, protecting the child of Lin Zizhuo and Gu Lang with his life.
The Shen Feizhi at the end was in a near-death state; otherwise, how could he have attracted the attention of the Main Star?
When a Child of Destiny dies, the world is not far from destruction.
