Gu Suizhou’s company was located in the First District, yet he insisted on living in this house in District Six, adding an extra four hours of commuting every day.
It had been this way for over four years.
The only strange thing was that the doorknob to Gu Suizhou’s own bedroom was covered in dust, looking as though no one had entered it in a very long time.
Gu Suizhou shed his black-and-white suit, removed his silver-gray watch and necktie, and without even turning on the lights, walked listlessly into Shen Feizhi’s bedroom.
After a hurried shower, he habitually took a white shirt out of the wardrobe, pulled it on, and collapsed onto the bed.
The shirt was tight on him and clearly didn't fit; it was obvious the owner wasn't him, and it couldn't have been comfortable to wear.
Gu Suizhou himself felt none of it; the moment the clothes touched his body, his perennially furrowed brows smoothed over.
He closed his eyes and remained motionless for a long time, looking as though he had fallen asleep.
But after a short while, those clear, icy eyes suddenly snapped open. He sat up in the darkness and took a small wooden knife from the nightstand.
Unlike the small wooden knife belonging to a certain someone, this one, though ugly, was exceptionally sharp.
