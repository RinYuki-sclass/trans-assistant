Inside the Main Star Temporary Space Station.

Shen Feizhi stared at the coffee in front of him, motionless. "Don't you think," he said tonelessly, "that you owe me an explanation right about now?"

It had been a long time since Shen Feizhi had self-destructed his shuttle to force his way into the Energy Vortex.

The moment he entered, he had blacked out; that sensation of his body being torn apart had returned.

Fortunately, it didn't last long this time before he felt a warm glow of light guiding him into another space.

After an unknown period of self-repair, he finally opened his eyes again. The first thing he saw was the impeccably dressed, cold-faced man sitting across from him.

Shen Feizhi considered his own features to be quite cold, yet here was someone who looked even colder than him.

The man had no name, only a work code: A11. He was one of the twenty people holding the highest management authority on the Main Star.

A11 looked at the cooling coffee in front of Shen Feizhi and knitted his brows. "This blend is the most expensive one on the Main Star. You’re being very wasteful."

"I didn't ask you to brew it for me," Shen Feizhi replied, unmoving. "If you want to cater to someone's tastes or offer an apology, you need to apply the right remedy for the symptoms. Did you fail to investigate beforehand and find out that I don't like coffee?"

A11, knowing he was in the wrong, fell silent. He picked up his cold coffee and drained it in one gulp. "There's nothing to explain. The fact is, you were originally this world’s Child of Destiny, but due to an accident, one of ours slipped through the cracks and usurped your identity. This was indeed an oversight on our part. You have our apologies, and we will do our best to compensate you."

The explanation was concise and clear, but Shen Feizhi was far from satisfied. "I remember nothing of my past, and your explanation is just this one sentence?"

A11 was ill-suited for social interaction; his face remained expressionless, his voice stiff. "We will naturally return the memories that belong to you. It is just that your current physical condition has been so badly depleted by the energy field that you cannot withstand the process yet."

Shen Feizhi took a deep, nearly imperceptible breath.
