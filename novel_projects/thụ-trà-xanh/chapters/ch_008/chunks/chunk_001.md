Leapton had been eagerly anticipating this man's arrival all week.
Ding-dong.
The doorbell rang again.
He immediately straightened his hair and stood up straight, but his shoulders slumped in disappointment the moment he saw who it was.
"Oh, it's just you."
"What's with that tone?" Gu Suizhou walked over, his face expressionless. "You seem awfully disappointed."
"Yeah, really disappointed! Utterly disappointed!" Leapton rolled his eyes. "Coming to an internet cafe in the middle of the day to find me? Don't you think you're being a bit conspicuous?"
"Who cares? No one bosses me around anyway, and no one in the Underground City knows me." Gu Suizhou shrugged, his long, straight legs striding behind the service counter in a single step. "Do you have any food here? I skipped breakfast and had to run over to Song Zhe's place to grab something. I'm starving."
"Why didn't you eat breakfast? Did Lin Sui fire the housekeeper?"
"Nah, I ran away from home. I'm not staying with Lin Sui anymore." Gu Suizhou dropped the bombshell casually.
Leapton choked on his spit. "Holy shit?! You ran away from home? Aren't you afraid he'll destroy all the stuff Aunt Gu Lang left you in a fit of anger?"
"He'd probably be glad to see me gone," Gu Suizhou retorted, rolling his eyes again.
"So, where have you been staying the past few days?"
"Staying…" Gu Suizhou's thoughts drifted involuntarily to a certain face, his lips twitching. "Staying in a penthouse, freeloading off someone else."
