Unbidden, his thoughts drifted to that beautiful poppy he had taken in.
Keeping that kid around seemed to have been a fortunate misstep after all.
When Leapton skipped back to his room, clutching his face, Gu Suizhou was sitting there, fingers flying across Blondie's smartphone screen as he slurped down a bowl of instant noodles with gusto.
Watching Gu Suizhou's ravenous eating, Leapton curled his lip in distaste. "What a waste of such a handsome face."
"Just admit you're jealous," Gu Suizhou said between slurps, his brow furrowed as he stared intently at the glowing screen. "Any news on the person I asked you to find?"
"Big Brother, you gave me next to nothing—'male, currently twenty years old, passenger on C1907 ten years ago.' Where am I supposed to even start looking?" Leapton practically wanted to kneel in frustration. "No one survived that accident. You're asking me to find a dead—"
His words froze in his throat.
Gu Suizhou's gaze had locked onto Leapton's neck. His fox-like eyes, usually languid and half-lidded, were now unnaturally clear, like still, lifeless water. Their natural amber clarity had become murky, obscuring the emotions swirling within.
Gu Suizhou's voice was cold as ice, so light it seemed about to drift away. "Do I need to teach you how to speak?"
Leapton's face paled. Almost unconsciously, he clenched his fists. "S-sorry, Brother Zhou."
"Think before you speak next time," Gu Suizhou said, shifting his gaze as if nothing had happened. "Mentioning death—it's just bad luck."
Leapton and Gu Suizhou were close, but sometimes Leapton still found himself intimidated by him.
Like now. Even though Gu Suizhou's tone was casual, as if they were just chatting, Leapton only dared to laugh awkwardly twice before falling silent.
Gu Suizhou finished his noodles in silence, then tossed a data disk into Leapton's hand. "This contains all the Shen family's recent financial transactions I could find. Investigate which account is funding the drugs for him."
"Right away." At the mention of business, Leapton's expression turned serious. "Just digging through records won't be enough, Gu Suizhou. You need to find your parents' bodies. Otherwise, even if we prove the accident had nothing to do with your family, we still won't be able to convict the Shen family."
