The Shen family had handled the aftermath of the accident years ago. Gu Suizhou had watched as their men carried his parents' bodies out and load them into a car, and then they had vanished without a trace.
He couldn't shake the feeling that the Shen family wouldn't have simply burned his parents' remains.
Though there was no concrete evidence, Gu Suizhou's intense intuition left him unshakably convinced.
Just as he sank deeper into thought, a chair scraped beside him. He glanced up to see Blondie preening in front of the mirror, meticulously fixing his hair.
Gu Suizhou's eye twitched sharply. "…Do you have a date?"
Ever since they'd become partners, Leapton had always been flamboyantly stylish, his string of male companions flowing like water, averaging one every two weeks with no gaps in between.
He had an adorable face but lacked softness, with distinctly masculine features. His style leaned toward the pure, good-student look of a high school boy.
Yet even that couldn't conceal the "wild" aura that radiated from every inch of him—truly a peculiar character.
Leapton let out a light hum, his ears surprisingly flushing a faint pink.
"Let me tell you, I have a feeling—this time, I might have finally met my soulmate!"
Gu Suizhou sneered. "That's the nineteenth time you've said that this year."
Leapton's eyes widened, clearly displeased by the retort. "Every relationship should be treated as if it's your last—that's how you make it last! What do you know about it?"
Gu Suizhou: "Your idea of 'lasting' is a record of 27 days?"
Leapton: "…Can't you have a worse memory?"
