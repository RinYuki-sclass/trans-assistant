Gu Suizhou wanted to stay by his side, most likely because of the original owner's identity—that is, his connection to the Shen family. It had nothing to do with Shen Feizhi himself. As long as it posed no threat to him personally, he couldn't be bothered to ask further.
"Then, the last one…"
"The last one," Gu Suizhou cut in, recklessly grabbing Shen Feizhi's sleeve, "Brother Shen, I just wanted you to come pick me up."
Shen Feizhi's eyebrow twitched violently.
He felt he should shut that little brat up, but when he tried to yank his sleeve free, it wouldn't budge.

So Gu Suizhou's words spilled out unchecked:
"I thought… you didn't want me anymore."
Shen Feizhi scoffed lightly. "You flatter yourself too much—"
Gu Suizhou: "This is the first time anyone's ever come to pick me up from school."
Shen Feizhi swallowed the rest of his sentence.
After a long pause, he finally blurted out four words:
"Abandonment is illegal."
During his two-day hospital stay, Shen Feizhi had already secured a new residence in District Six through the Starnet. For half the rent of his previous place, he'd leased a 180-square-meter penthouse apartment in a high-end residential complex—an absolute steal.
As he surveyed the apartment's interior, Shen Feizhi thrust the newly purchased disinfectant into Gu Suizhou's hands. "Let's get to work."
Gu Suizhou's lips twitched.
He knew Shen Feizhi was a neat freak, but he hadn't realized just how extreme it was.
Watching Shen Feizhi spray the disinfectant without the slightest hesitation, Gu Suizhou resigned himself to joining the cleanup crew.
Three hours later, the pair collapsed onto the sofa.
Shen Feizhi hadn't done a thorough cleaning in ages. After this top-to-bottom overhaul, he felt like every muscle and bone in his body had been revitalized.
