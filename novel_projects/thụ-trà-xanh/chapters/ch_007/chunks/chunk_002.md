What's the point of asking me now that you've already canceled the lease?
Shen Feizhi glanced sideways at him, then casually turned his gaze forward again.
"It's been two days. Don't you have anything you want to explain to me?"
Gu Suizhou looked genuinely puzzled. "Explain? About what?"
"Do I have to drag the answers out of you?" Shen Feizhi didn't mince words, holding up a hand and ticking off the points on his fingers. "Who is Yang Rui? Why did you fight? Where did you get the money for a lawyer? …And why didn't you wait for me after school? Why go straight back to the apartment?"
Gu Suizhou's eyes curved into crescents, his lips lifting in a mischievous grin that revealed two small fangs.
"Is that all, Brother Shen? If you want to know, of course I'll tell you everything!"
Shen Feizhi remained skeptical. "You'd better."
Gu Suizhou seemed oblivious to the sarcasm. "The Yang family used to be suppliers for the Gu family. After my parents died, Yang Rui got cocky and started picking fights with me."

In poverty, even kin turn away; in wealth, strangers gather.
The harsh realities of human nature must have sunk deep into Gu Suizhou's young mind.
Shen Feizhi offered no empty platitudes, his gaze urging him to continue.
Gu Suizhou continued, "You can probably guess why we fought, Brother Shen. He just didn't like me, so he started throwing punches."
Shen Feizhi pressed him, his tone ambiguous. "Just because he didn't like you?"
"Yup!" Gu Suizhou replied firmly. "I didn't do anything to provoke him."
"I just teased him a little bit. Rounded down, that's practically nothing, right?"
Shen Feizhi: "Alright, carry on."
"Don't worry about the lawyers, Brother Shen. I have an uncle who's an old friend of my dad's; he'll definitely help with something this small."
Shen Feizhi had no intention of delving into the truth of that statement.
