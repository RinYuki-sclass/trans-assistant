The guard on duty today was the same one from yesterday. Noticing Shen Feizhi absentmindedly twirling his wooden stick and poking at the floor-cleaning robot, he couldn't help but remind him, "Hey, straighten up! The Institute's top brass is coming for inspection today!"
"Top brass?" Shen Feizhi instantly lost interest in Gu Suizhou, his full attention now focused on the Research Institute. "Professor-level figures, you mean?"
"Not exactly." The guard lowered his voice, checking his surroundings before whispering conspiratorially: "You probably don't know this, but the Institute's largest shareholder isn't actually the Federation—it's an individual."
Shen Feizhi was genuinely surprised. "The Federation trusts them with that?"
The most authoritative and capable research institute on the entire planet was under the control of a single person. He couldn't help but wonder what the Federation's high-ranking officials were thinking. How could they be so confident?
The guard sighed. "What choice do they have? This institute was practically founded because of that family. The two founders died suddenly before the Federation could gradually buy back the shares, so everything fell into the hands of their descendants."
"Can't they just keep reclaiming the shares?" Shen Feizhi asked, a vague suspicion already forming in his mind.
Sure enough, the answer confirmed his hunch.
"No way. Their legal heir is still a minor, and the Federation strictly enforces its Minor Protection Law. Snatching shares from a kid would be a PR disaster. So we're just waiting for the boy to turn eighteen."
"But today's inspector is his older brother." The guard lowered his voice even further. "You know how these big families are—full of tangled relationships. Everyone knows what's going on. The older brother's probably already scheming to seize his younger brother's shares."
Shen Feizhi had no interest in such gossip.
After extracting the useful information, he cut the guard's chatter short and herded the little cleaning robots into the warehouse.
He hadn't come here to be a janitor.
He needed to find a chance to contact the Research Institute's core personnel. Only then could he hope to find a way to reach the Main Star.

Surveillance cameras were everywhere in the Research Institute. As Shen Feizhi stirred up a ruckus with his cleaning stick, poking at the cleaning robots, his eyes darted around, scanning his surroundings.
Back when he was exploring and colonizing new worlds, Shen Feizhi had always favored warehouses. Sometimes, messy places yielded more valuable discoveries than libraries or research labs.
This belief proved true once again.
Soon, in a corner pinned down by a crate, he discovered a duty roster.
The janitorial robot, kicked halfway around, advanced into the corner and swept the duty roster out.
