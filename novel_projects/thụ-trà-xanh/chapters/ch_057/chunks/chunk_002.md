He had been candid to this extent; if Gu Suizhou continued to be vague, it would be pushing it.
But Gu Suizhou was truly afraid.
Even knowing that Shen Feizhi wasn't some saint himself, he didn't want his image in the other man's heart to be too filthy.
Shen Feizhi looked at his reddening eyes and sighed silently. "I'll ask, you answer."
"How much of that 'harmful to heaven and reason' business have you touched?"
Harmful to heaven and reason—nothing more than murder, arson, trafficking, and the like.
Gu Suizhou immediately said anxiously, "Bro, don't misunderstand, I haven't touched a single one of those!"
Shen Feizhi chuckled. "Since you haven't done them, what are you afraid of? My only requirement for you is to stay out of that kind of business. Appropriate methods are necessary sometimes; it's not like I'm going to lecture you."
Having said all that, Gu Suizhou lowered his head and said weakly, "I have a hand in all the business on the Black Market, and I have a mercenary group under my command, so I also have some contact with the military…"
The more Gu Suizhou spoke, the more helpless Shen Feizhi’s expression became.
This kid had hidden quite a lot.

However, he had no intention of blaming him. After listening, he simply nodded. "You've done well these past few years."
Gu Suizhou suddenly felt a wave of grievance. He squatted down by Shen Feizhi's legs, leaning against him in silence.
Shen Feizhi patted the head resting on his knees. "There's one more thing I want to ask you. How did you get your hands on that W.N drug experiment report?"
Gu Suizhou's voice was muffled. "Luvi Tisqi really did tattle on me."
"Even if she hadn't told me, did you think I wouldn't find out?"
Shen Feizhi gave him a light slap.
The Pharmacology Team had shown him the data this morning; the vital signs of all those test subjects were stable. This meant that the experiments hadn't cost a single life.
That was the only reason he could ask about it so calmly now.
