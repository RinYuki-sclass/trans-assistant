Shen Feizhi thought to himself that it certainly wouldn't.
But he couldn't quite put into words why he wouldn't.
He wasn't the type to chase after some supreme, perfect romance. In fact, thinking rationally, his personality should have led him to settle their relationship decisively—either be together or stay far away from each other—rather than letting things become the tangled mess they were now.
After thinking for a while, he felt the root of the problem lay in this man’s 100% pheromone dependence on him.
