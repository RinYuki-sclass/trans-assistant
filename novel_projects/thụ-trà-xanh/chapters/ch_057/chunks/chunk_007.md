"Meeting adjourned."
Gu Suizhou’s cold voice crackled through the earpiece.
Before Shen Feizhi could react, he heard a flurry of hurried footsteps. A second later, his office door was flung open.
"What's wrong?"
The man’s long legs carried him across the room in just a few strides. He leaned over, gripping Shen Feizhi’s shoulders, his eyes bloodshot. "What do you mean by telling me all this?"

Shen Feizhi gave the back of his neck a steadying squeeze. "Do you believe what I’ve told you?"
"If you say it, I believe it," Gu Suizhou said resolutely.
"Don't worry," Shen Feizhi said. "I'm not from the Main Star. I… because of an accident, I wasn't here for a few years, but I’m back now."
"When?" Gu Suizhou asked bitterly. "When you were ten? Was it because of me?"
"It wasn't because of you." For some reason, Shen Feizhi instinctively denied it. "There are some things even I haven't figured out yet. Don't go taking the blame for everything."
“How could it not be because of me?!” Gu Suizhou’s emotions were spiraling out of control. “It’s because my mom told you to protect me that you ended up in that accident with me. If it wasn’t for me, you definitely would have made it back safely! You—”
“And then what?” Shen Feizhi’s gaze was deep, his voice as cold as if he weren’t even the one involved. “If it weren’t for your parents, I might be dead already. If I hadn’t left with you, I might have been dragged down by the Shen family and be rotting in prison with them right now.”
“Gu Suizhou, haven't I taught you not to make such pointless hypotheses?”
He lowered his eyes. “You’re being disobedient.”
The moment those words left his mouth, Gu Suizhou snapped out of it. Panic-stricken, he clung to Shen Feizhi, hanging off him. “I’m not, I’m being good. I won’t do it again, please don’t abandon me!”
“I’m not abandoning you.” Shen Feizhi patted his back. “I’m telling you this so you have the right context to think about your own affairs, not so you can let your imagination run wild here.”
The very reason he had kept this from Gu Suizhou for so long was for fear that he would overthink things.
“I understand.” Gu Suizhou rubbed against his neck, asking tentatively, “Brother Shen, are you… still going to leave?”
Five years had been too long.
