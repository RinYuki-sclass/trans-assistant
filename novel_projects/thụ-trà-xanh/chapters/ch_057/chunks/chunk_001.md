Chapter 57
Due to Shen Feizhi's egregious behavior, Gu Suizhou had unilaterally declared a huff.
He didn’t have any particularly extreme reactions; it was just that whatever Shen Feizhi did, Gu Suizhou would follow right behind him with a look that screamed "I'm angry." Then, pointing at Shen Feizhi, he would stubbornly force Lucky Dog to call him "Stepdad."
It was honestly…
Incredibly childish.
Shen Feizhi let him throw his fit for two days. Finally, on the third day, the two of them began to communicate normally and calmly again.
Because Lin Sui was dead.
"The Prosecutor’s Office summoned me for questioning." Gu Suizhou’s smartphone was chiming incessantly, enough to give anyone a headache. "Bro, how’s it looking on your end?"
"I was summoned too." Shen Feizhi knit his brows. "They said I was the last person to see Lin Sui before he died."
"Meaning no one else went in over the last few days?" Gu Suizhou began sorting through the surveillance data from the past few days. "I haven't found any outsiders contacting him. I’m leaning towards the theory that someone on the inside killed him."
"My thoughts exactly," Shen Feizhi said. "You have a lot of connections. Any leads?"
"Not yet. I haven't looked into the roster of personnel held at the detention center. However, I’ve already sent my people in to investigate. We should have results within the next two days."
Shen Feizhi asked tonelessly, "You can even arrange for people to get in there?"
"Yeah," Gu Suizhou hummed in response, offering nothing more.
Though he wasn’t intentionally hiding it, he clearly didn't want to dwell on the subject—or rather, he didn't want Shen Feizhi to know.
