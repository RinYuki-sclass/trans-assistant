The so-called Law of Conservation of Happiness: when Shen Feizhi was angry, Gu Suizhou would good-naturedly sit by his side and massage his shoulders.
He didn't look like a boss at all.
As those people threw one barb after another, Gu Suizhou just sat there taking it like a pathetic wretch, occasionally offering shallow opinions.
Shen Feizhi looked at him coldly before speaking: "Enough, all of you shut up."
His voice wasn’t loud, but it was enough to be intimidating.
"The public has a greater demand for medicine. High-end technology has nothing to do with the middle and lower classes. There is really no need to waste money on this right now."
"Next week we will go to District Seven for a field investigation. I hope that after you all see the report upon our return, you won't be so blind to the situation anymore."
District Seven?
Gu Suizhou arched an eyebrow; he had already heard about those transfer records.
As expected, this man’s actions never had a simple motive.
Shen Feizhi spoke with such authority that no one dared to voice an objection.
Exactly the effect he wanted, he packed his things and stood up: "Meeting adjourned."
Gu Suizhou followed him out: "It’s gotta be you, Bro. They’re usually so energetic when they're bullying me!"
"Is that so?" Shen Feizhi didn’t give him a pleasant look. "Do you know what you looked like just now?"
"An elementary schooler who knows nothing of the world."
Gu Suizhou shrugged nonchalantly and smiled. "It's fine. Don't I have my Brother Shen? If I'm a kid, then I'm—"
"Think carefully before you speak." Shen Feizhi came to a sudden halt, their bodies inches apart.
He tapped the other man’s chest twice with his fingertip. "I don't like kids."
"…" Gu Suizhou’s smile vanished. "Once I’m back from District Seven next week, I’ll make sure this is settled once and for all."
"I'll be watching," Shen Feizhi said. "Also, how did that balding guy even make it into the upper management? Does he even have a brain? I'm giving you half a month to get him fired."
