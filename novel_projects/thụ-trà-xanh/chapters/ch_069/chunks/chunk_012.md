Major scientific research conferences were usually held at noon or 6:00 PM. This time, because they were in such a rush, Shen Feizhi hadn't been able to make the 6:00 PM slot no matter how hard he hurried, so he had to settle for 10:00 PM instead.
"The conference will wrap up before midnight," he said. "I promised you it would launch today, so it’s going to launch today."
Gu Suizhou pursed his lips, a swelling warmth filling his chest.
He knew his request earlier that day had been unreasonable, but he hadn't expected Shen Feizhi to actually agree—and even less expected him to actually pull it off.
Actually, he didn’t want Shen Feizhi to work so hard, if not for tomorrow…
Shen Feizhi was currently patting Lucky Dog's head, asking softly, "Satisfied?"
Satisfied?
He was more than satisfied.
Gu Suizhou thought: He couldn't write an eight-thousand-word self-reflection, but he could certainly try an eight-thousand-word love letter.
He pulled the man gently into his arms. "You’ve worked hard."
Shen Feizhi was a bit shorter than him, just enough to rest his chin on Gu Suizhou's shoulder. "I'm craving green tea cakes. Go buy some for me tomorrow."
Gu Suizhou agreed readily, "Alright, I'll buy ten boxes!"
Never mind green tea cakes; as long as this man spoke the word, even if it were an asteroid, he’d find a way to blow one down from the sky.
Shen Feizhi was very satisfied. "Let’s go home."
Under Gu Suizhou's insistent demand, Shen Feizhi reluctantly walked home with him, fingers intertwined as they walked the dog.
The moment they stepped through the door, Gu Suizhou accidentally stepped on one of Lucky Dog's droppings and was shoved into the bathroom with look of total disdain.
By the time he hurriedly finished washing up and came out, Shen Feizhi was lying on the sofa, fast asleep.
He hadn't even taken off his clothes; he must have been exhausted.
Gu Suizhou crouched by the sofa and watched him for a while, gently kissing the small mole on the tip of the other's nose before reaching out to help him undress.
The coat was easy to remove, but the shirt underneath took some effort; it was quite a struggle to finally tug the hem out from the belt.
