"No, you were ignoring me." As Shen Feizhi dodged, Gu Suizhou continued to chase his lips for a kiss. "I'm dying of sadness here."
"Cut the crap…"
The sidewalk was narrow, and no matter how much he tried to escape, there wasn't much room to go. Moreover, he couldn't bring himself to actually kick the guy away with any real force, so he could only awkwardly tilt his head to avoid him.
"Wait a second!"
"Stop… we're still outside."
"No one’s looking at us."
"Gu Suizhou!" Shen Feizhi barked in a low voice.
Gu Suizhou finally stopped. His affectionate fox-like eyes were glistening and wet, looking for all the world as if he would burst into tears if he were rejected again.
Shen Feizhi wanted to say something harsh, but looking into those eyes, he couldn’t help it and let out a snort of laughter. "You’re truly shameless."
Oh well, if it was three minutes short, then it was three minutes short. He’d broken so many precedents for this man already; one more wouldn't hurt.
Like an elementary schooler who had just been given candy, Gu Suizhou leaned in for a kiss with a grin. "Like I said, you can't chase a husband if you're worried about saving face."
Shen Feizhi didn’t pull away this time. Half-leaning against the bridge railing, he let the river breeze brush over him and shared a lingering, gentle kiss amidst the barking of Lucky Dog.
Ten o’clock was the time for the nightly news, broadcast live on the floating screens of the Central Broadcasting Station across the river.
Hearing the familiar news intro, Shen Feizhi bit Gu Suizhou’s lower lip, bringing the kiss to a silent pause. "Turn around."
Gu Suizhou turned dissatisfaction, only to see the sole news item appearing on the screen tonight.
It was the launch event for W.N’s public listing.
