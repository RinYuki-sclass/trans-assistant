Amidst the sudden cacophony, only two people remained calm and indifferent.
One was Gu Suizhou, and the other was Shen Feizhi, who was currently staring him down.
Shen Feizhi’s gaze was like a physical blow: What are you playing at?
Gu Suizhou offered a silent smile and tilted his chin, gesturing for him to look down.
Look at what? What was there to see? Wasn't it just a passcode?
Despite the thought, Shen Feizhi indulged him without a word and looked down.
As he looked closer, his expression grew increasingly solemn.
It really wasn't an ordinary passcode. It was incredibly complex—a string of characters composed of at least four different types of ciphers layered together.
Strings of numbers scrolled across the screen, refreshing every thirty seconds with a completely new set.
After watching a few more cycles, Shen Feizhi sensed something was off.
It was so familiar…
He pursed his lips. From the corner of his eye, he caught Gu Suizhou removing his left earring and twirling it between his fingertips before pressing it against his ear.
In a sudden flash of realization, he finally remembered the "lesson" he’d learned back when he’d used electronic handcuffs to track the man’s heart rate and calculate the time.
