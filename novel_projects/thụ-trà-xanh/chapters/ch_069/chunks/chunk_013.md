This man usually wore casual shirts and never used shirt stays, which was a bit of a pity.
As the buttons were undone one by one, Gu Suizhou's hand accidentally brushed against his abdominal muscles.
The lines and the feel were so distinct they were impossible to ignore.
Gu Suizhou, who truly hadn't had any ulterior motives before he started undressing him, found himself harboring a few little thoughts at this moment.
He knew he shouldn't look further down, yet his eyes remained fixed, as if possessed, on the V-line hidden beneath the waistband. His fingers hooked slightly inward, trembling ever so slightly; he wanted to undo it
Gu Suizhou didn’t immediately catch the hidden meaning behind those words.
Shen Feizhi chuckled. "Giving up now? Then wouldn't the methods you racked your brain for tonight to beg me for mercy go to waste?"
Gu Suizhou’s hand, resting on Shen Feizhi’s thigh, suddenly tightened. "Are you… drunk?"
Shen Feizhi patted his hand and said calmly, "I’m going to take a shower first."
Gu Suizhou let go blankly. Listening to the rushing water from the bathroom, he sat on the floor in a daze for a while until his smartphone blinked twice.
With a hollow expression, he picked it up—
[Lai Gou: Old Gu! Did you see the video of your husband at the gala today?]
[Lai Gou: /Video/]
The corners of Gu Suizhou’s mouth couldn't help but twitch downward.
His gaze suddenly turned cold as he clicked on the video.
The footage was a surveillance clip. Inside, Shen Feizhi sat in a booth in the lounge area with an air of total indifference. People occasionally approached to greet him, and he mostly responded with a few polite, casual words.
Until a fair-skinned, beautiful little Omega walked over.
Gu Suizhou’s knuckles turned white as he cranked up the volume.
"Teacher Shen, thank you for your guidance and care before!" The Omega’s eyes were sparkling, his admiration written plainly across his face.
Shen Feizhi nodded. "There’s no need to call me teacher. It was only right."
