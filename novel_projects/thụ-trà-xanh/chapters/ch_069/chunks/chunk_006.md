Gu Suizhou sighed and flipped down the sun visor in front of Shen Feizhi to block out the glare of the neon lights outside. "Close your eyes for a bit. I’ll wake you when we’re there."
Shen Feizhi wasn't actually drunk, and he couldn't fall asleep in a mere ten minutes. After a brief rest with his eyes closed, he was wide awake the moment the car came to a stop.
As he leisurely unbuckled his seatbelt, Gu Suizhou had already made his way around to open the door for him, shielding the top of the doorframe with his hand to keep him from bumping his head. "We’ll make this quick once we’re inside. The summary isn't worth listening to anyway."
Shen Feizhi let out a lazy yawn. "I don't need you to tell me that. I'm not wearing a suit, though. Do you have a spare jacket? I'll just throw one on to make do."
“It’s in the trunk.” He popped it open, then knit his brows. “Ah, the black one was taken by my assistant for dry cleaning. There’s only the wine-red one left.”
“It’s fine, I’ll make do.” Shen Feizhi took it without the slightest hesitation.
The man usually scented his clothes with a woody fragrance; as soon as Shen Feizhi took hold of it, he caught that familiar scent. It was pleasant, not at all overpowering.
Gu Suizhou had just closed the trunk when he saw the man, looking weary and listless, slipping into the deep red suit jacket.
The suit wasn't an overly stiff cut; it was naturally a bit loose, but his clothes were a size larger than what Shen Feizhi usually wore. On the other man, it became oversized.

Moreover, the color made Shen Feizhi’s skin appear even paler. The small mole on the tip of his nose made that aloof, cold face look exceptionally gorgeous.
He looked up at Gu Suizhou. “What are you dazing off for? Let’s go, we’re going to be late.”
Gu Suizhou gave a low, muffled grunt of affirmation. “Okay.”
He felt that he might not be able to wait much longer.
Alcohol didn't affect Shen Feizhi’s brain; during the meeting, his speech was clear and his critiques were sharp. Not a single person could tell the difference between him and his usual self.
Only Gu Suizhou, sitting directly across from him, could see the rare flicker of irritation and speechlessness in his eyes every time someone made an idiotic comment.
He chuckled and suddenly shifted his posture.
Shen Feizhi was currently busy calculating stocks when he felt a kick to his calf.
He looked up at the culprit, his face expressionless: 【Is this fun?】
Gu Suizhou nodded with a beaming smile, his foot sliding up Shen Feizhi’s calf to hook behind his knee.
