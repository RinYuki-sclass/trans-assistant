The man is really trying to turn the world upside down.
Whatever, he was done washing anyway.
So, he nodded readily. "Fine."
He had no idea what kind of mood Gu Suizhou was in today.
In his mind, he had assumed that for their first time, Gu Suizhou would definitely take it slow and not go too far.
The reality, however, was quite the opposite.
Gu Suizhou acted as if he were trying to dismantle his very bones.
Something was wrong—very wrong.
There was definitely something going on!
For hours, Shen Feizhi’s muddled brain couldn't figure out what could have happened to make this man as crazed as if he’d been drugged.
It wasn't until the final round, when one of his wrists was bound by a necktie, that the perpetrator suddenly halted. Tightening his grip on the other end of the tie, he whispered into Shen Feizhi’s ear with a sob in his voice, "Bro, do you not like me anymore?"
Shen Feizhi nearly felt like cursing.
Asking a question like that at a time like this was simply asking for a beating.
He clamped down hard, and only after successfully eliciting a muffled groan from the other man did his expression soften slightly. "What exactly is the matter?"
Gu Suizhou sounded utterly aggrieved. "I saw it. You were drinking with so many people tonight, and you didn't refuse a single one of them!"
Shen Feizhi: "…"
Leapton had been the one monitoring the surveillance footage that night. Realizing this, Shen Feizhi immediately guessed who had snitched and exactly what they had snitched about.
He said helplessly, "Those are all apprentices from the Research Institute's Pharmacology Team."
They were the ones who had worked the hardest over the last few days, ordered around by him day in and day out. Letting them have a few drinks was only reasonable.
Gu Suizhou gritted his teeth, his waist and core tightening slightly. "That first one is a total 'white lotus' at first glance! And 'Teacher Shen'? Hah! How intimate, Teacher Shen !"
