The layout of this garden was complex, its paths shifting from time to time. Anyone wishing to pay their respects needed enough patience to navigate the labyrinth before they could reach the very center.
Since their burial site couldn't be kept a secret from the public, Gu Suizhou had installed these architectural mechanisms to ensure they wouldn't be disturbed. Unless one was truly sincere, they would never reach the heart of the maze.
On the tombstone was a photograph of the two of them, with their names and titles hand-carved below by Shen Feizhi.
Originally, Gu Suizhou was supposed to do it, but he had enough self-awareness to know his handwriting was far too unsightly to be shown in public, so he delegated the task to Shen Feizhi.
Shen Feizhi initially felt it was inappropriate, but after being subjected to a certain someone’s "if you don't do it, it means you don't love me anymore" style of relentless brainwashing, he eventually resigned himself to the task.
And on the back of the tombstone was a hymn of praise written for them by the Federation’s Supreme Executive Officer himself.
It was very heartfelt, yet also very perfunctory.
Someone had suggested that Gu Suizhou carve the hymn in the most prominent position, but he refused, bluntly stating that putting it on the back was already giving them plenty of face. When this reached the Executive Officer’s ears, it nearly fumed him to death.
Yet there was nothing to be done about him; the man had no choice but to swallow his rage.
Shen Feizhi stood beside Gu Suizhou and bowed respectfully toward the two of them.

What little care he had received from elders in his life had come from these two; it was impossible not to be grateful.
It was Lin Zizhuo and Gu Lang who had taught him self-love and courage. Even at the very end, they had only asked him to help Gu Suizhou to the best of his ability. When he said he would protect the other man with his life, he had been criticized by Gu Lang.
Gu Lang told him that she wanted him to live a good life, too.
"My parents should be very happy." Gu Suizhou stood beside him, quietly hooking his pinky finger.
Shen Feizhi raised an eyebrow. "They might be angry, for all we know."
"How is that possible?" Gu Suizhou disagreed. "They liked you very much."
Shen Feizhi asked, "How do you know?"
“Is that even a question? No one could help but love you.” Gu Suizhou chuckled. “I have to thank my parents. If not for them, I’d have either died young or spent my final years in lonely misery.”
“You really should thank them,” Shen Feizhi said. “If it weren't for them, the two of us probably would have been gone a long time ago.”
