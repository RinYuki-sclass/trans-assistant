Gu Suizhou had the sense to stop talking. He walked over to tuck in the covers on the other side, brushed back Shen Feizhi's hair, and quietly cleaned up the mess before carefully climbing back into bed to hold him from behind as they slept.
Shen Feizhi was a light sleeper. Though his eyes were closed, he was perfectly aware of the other’s movements.
He’s actually quite attentive, he thought.
Fine. If he turned out this way, then so be it. He could bear it if he just made do.
Shen Feizhi woke up in the middle of the night, parched.
The excessive physical exertion had left him drenched in sweat, and his throat felt like sandpaper.
Wrapped around his waist was the same tie Gu Suizhou had worn to the press conference that day. He closed his eyes and tugged at the tight knot—it wouldn't budge.
Just how tightly had he tied this?
The tie was ruined; there was no way it could be used again.
With great effort, he managed to pull it loose enough to create a small gap, letting it slide down past his hips.
Shen Feizhi felt immensely grateful for his own lean build and lack of excess weight. Had he been even a little heavier, he would have had to go downstairs and find a pair of scissors to save himself.
He fumbled in the dark to get up, but the moment his feet hit the floor, he let out a silent gasp. Below the waist, he felt practically paralyzed.
Looking at Gu Suizhou’s face alone, one would never have imagined the kid was so…
He pressed his fingers to his temples, unwilling to revisit those thoughts.
It took him a long while to adjust before he grabbed Gu Suizhou’s long pajama shirt, threw it over his shoulders, and headed toward the living room.
In his own home, there weren't many constraints. The white shirt hung loosely on his frame, unbuttoned and gaping open in the front, while the back barely covered the tops of his thighs.
At short intervals around the cross-shaped scar on his front were several bite marks.
This was the sight that greeted Gu Suizhou as he walked out.
He had just been trying to pull the other man closer in his half-asleep daze, but when he unexpectedly embraced thin air, the fright jolted him upright. Without even stopping to put on clothes, he strode toward the door.
He accidentally kicked over the trash can, spilling out several used small plastic packets and crumpled tissues.
