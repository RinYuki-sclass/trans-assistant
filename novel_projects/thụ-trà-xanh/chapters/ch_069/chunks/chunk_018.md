When Shen Feizhi had first seen these things, he’d been quite surprised and asked when he had bought them.
Gu Suizhou had confessed to buying them along with the watermelon.
That day, thinking that since he was buying them anyway, he might as well get a box of every variety. Looking back now, he really did have great foresight.
Shen Feizhi couldn't stand his self-praise and criticized, "Vile intentions."
Gu Suizhou quibbled, "It's called yearning."
Both had their own reasoning, but in the end, it was Shen Feizhi who, with a cold gaze, gripped Gu Suizhou and refused to let him finish, forcing him to take the initiative to admit his wrongs before the dispute finally drew to a close.
Shen Feizhi had just finished some water and was leaning against the kitchen doorway smoking. When he saw the other man arrive, he lifted his eyelids and asked softly, "Did I wake you?"
"No, I woke up on my own." Gu Suizhou's Adam's apple bobbed as he stared unblinkingly at those two long, slender legs. "Brother Shen, you're lacking in 'male virtue.'"
Shen Feizhi gave a mocking laugh, his gaze sweeping him from head to toe. "At least I'm wearing a shirt. What about you? Not a single scrap of fabric on you, so how do you have the nerve to talk about me?"
"A glimpse here and there makes it more impactful, doesn't it?" Gu Suizhou walked over. "Smoking again?"
It was so dark at night, and they hadn't turned on the lights, so there was no such thing as being shy. Of course, even if it were broad daylight, the two of them wouldn't necessarily feel embarrassed.
"Haven't you heard? It's a pleasure like no other."
Shen Feizhi was in a good mood and cracked a joke with him.
Gu Suizhou clicked his tongue, his fingers climbing up the hem of his shirt. "Brother Shen, teach me how to smoke."
"Get lost." Shen Feizhi blew a beautiful smoke ring by his ear. "At your young age, you don't learn anything good, but you want to learn this?"
"Young age?" Gu Suizhou narrowed his eyes, his thumb pressing down hard on a certain spot.
That area already had a bit of broken skin; Shen Feizhi frowned in pain.
"You know very well, I'm not small anymore." Gu Suizhou took his free hand and led it downward, repeating in a hoarse voice, "Bro, teach me."
Shen Feizhi: "…"
He roughly shoved the cigarette filter against the other's lips.
