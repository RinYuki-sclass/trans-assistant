The evening’s freshman orientation gala transformed from a simple series of motivational speeches into a victory banquet.
Shen Feizhi had also successfully transformed from a mere bystander into one of the night's main protagonists.
He cast a chilly look at the Director.
The Director, who had successfully thrust him into the spotlight, shrunk his neck, too guilt-stricken to meet his gaze.
"Congratulations, President Shen." The balding leader of the Biotech Team came over to clink glasses with him, his voice tinged with envy. "It would be great if we could develop a transgenic surgery that prevents hair loss forever."
Hair loss followed the laws of nature. Even now, they could only improve the condition; they couldn't eliminate the phenomenon at its root.
"Just follow the natural course of your research," Shen Feizhi said, glancing at the man’s scalp and offering some well-meaning advice. "You should stop using anti-hair loss potions and start using reverse-growth ones."
With nothing left to lose, what was the point of anti-loss medication?
The Biotech Team leader: "…"
How rude of you.
Shen Feizhi had a low tolerance for alcohol. He declined those who came to toast him whenever possible, and when he couldn't, he only took a sip.
Even so, by the time nine o'clock approached, he had finished a small glass.
Shen Feizhi felt a bit lightheaded.
He had no choice but to call Gu Suizhou. "Is your meeting over?"
"The W.N. discussion just finished when you called," Gu Suizhou said. "But there's another meeting later for the internal quarterly review, and you need to attend. Where are you? I'll come pick you up."
