Gu Suizhou’s voice tightened. “Drinking? Are you drunk? Who dared to force drinks on you?! Should we postpone the meeting?”
“I’m fine. No one forced me, I only had a little. I’m still clear-headed; the breeze will do me good.” The alcohol Shen Feizhi had consumed was low-proof, and it wasn’t nearly as much as the glass he’d downed earlier to cover for Gu Suizhou. Right now, he was just slightly tipsy—nothing that would interfere with work.
Gu Suizhou sounded skeptical. “Really? Otherwise, how about we… skip out on work?”
Shen Feizhi: “…”
How could this kid be so unmotivated?
In ancient times, he definitely would have been a faineant emperor.
He said speechlessly, “Cut the crap.”
As soon as those words came out, Gu Suizhou felt at ease.
He could still scold people; it seemed he really wasn't drunk.
In less than twenty minutes, Shen Feizhi spotted the familiar license plate. He got into the car and took the honey water from Gu Suizhou’s hand. “Next time, add more honey. I like it sweet.”
Gu Suizhou didn’t know whether to laugh or cry. “Are you trying to sober up, or are you just drinking juice?”
“Worry about yourself.” Shen Feizhi closed his eyes and leaned against the car door. “Don’t think that just because you’re my boyfriend, you have the unbridled right to nitpick everything I do.”
Gu Suizhou nearly slammed his foot on the accelerator. "Say that again. I'm your what?"
