Shen Feizhi forced his voice to stay buried deep in his throat, raspy as he said, "Didn't I turn them down?"
"Then what about the seventh one? The one with the delicate features." Gu Suizhou gripped his chin. "Fuck, that pretty boy’s 'cutesy' voice made me want to gag just listening through the monitors, yet you actually managed to smile?"
Shen Feizhi closed his eyes, not even bothered to scold him for his vulgarity.
Truth be told.
He hadn't even noticed the guy was using a cutesy voice.
But clearly, that wasn't an explanation he could use right now.
He could only sigh. "My mistake. I'll be more careful next time."
Gu Suizhou: "And someone touched your hand! Bro, are an Omega's hands soft?"
Shen Feizhi replied, "That was indeed an oversight on my part. I washed my hands over a dozen times afterward. I was too busy feeling disgusted to notice any other sensation."
Only then did Gu Suizhou's expression soften. "Were there a lot of Omegas at the gala tonight? Did you smell any pheromones? Did they smell good?"
“I don’t know, I didn’t smell anything, and if I did, it’s nothing I’d be interested in.” Shen Feizhi answered every question, his voice listless. “Satisfied?”
The smile finally rushed back into Gu Suizhou’s eyes, and he planted a loud, smacking kiss on Shen Feizhi’s lips.
“Love you, Husband~”
Shen Feizhi could only respond with a cold sneer in his heart.
When children aren't guided properly through puberty, they grow up into this kind of bastardly shape. If he could turn back time, he would definitely educate the boy into a bright, cheerful young man, rather than this mallet full of wicked ideas.
Having had his fill, Gu Suizhou clung to him like a large dog, pushing his luck with reckless abandon. “Brother, how was it? Do wild flowers smell better, or the home-grown one?”
Shen Feizhi was the classic type to flip a switch and turn cold once finished; he kicked the man straight off the bed. “Get lost.”
Gu Suizhou pouted. “So cruel.”
Shen Feizhi faced him with the back of his head. “Mhm.”
He was so sleepy he could barely keep his eyes open, and his reply was a mumble.
