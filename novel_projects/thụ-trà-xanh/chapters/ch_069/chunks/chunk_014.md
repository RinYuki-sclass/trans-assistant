"That won't do! You've taught me so much; in my heart, you're already my teacher," the Omega insisted. "I'm truly grateful to you. I'd like to propose a toast!"
Everyone expected Shen Feizhi to refuse.
But he didn't.
He actually raised his arm, lightly clinked his glass against the other's, and took a small sip. "Keep working hard in the future."
Gu Suizhou: "…"
Next, a flurry of youngsters began approaching Shen Feizhi one after another.
Shen Feizhi treated them all with equal courtesy, clinking glasses with most of them.
Gu Suizhou’s sharp eyes caught a glimpse of a pretty young boy whose hand even brushed against Shen Feizhi's.
He shot to his feet.
That vixen! Absolutely! Definitely! Did that! On purpose!!
Shen Feizhi was currently taking a shower, wondering what they were supposed to do later without any supplies, when he suddenly heard the bathroom door fly open with a loud thud , kicked in from the outside.
With quick reflexes, he tied a towel around his waist and asked in surprise, "Is something wrong?"
Before he could finish his sentence, Gu Suizhou walked straight under the showerhead fully clothed, getting drenched instantly.
Shen Feizhi noticed his displeasure. "What's all this about?"
Gu Suizhou pursed his lips. Even the hot water pouring over him felt like it was being frozen into ice by his temperament. Without a word, he grabbed Shen Feizhi's left hand, picked up an exfoliating washcloth, and started scrubbing.
Shen Feizhi: "…"
He looked fierce, but his movements remained measured; he didn't dare use too much force, and it didn't hurt.
Shen Feizhi was speechless. "What did I do to upset you this time?"
Gu Suizhou didn't let go until the hand he was scrubbing turned a faint red. Then, a dense rain of kisses trailed from Shen Feizhi’s fingertips up to his wrist. His gaze fell heavily upon the older man's chest, his voice leaving no room for argument: "I can't wait any longer."
