Gu Suizhou smashed the lock on Shen Feizhi’s bedroom door.
Standing in the doorway, his gaze burned as he stared at the man inside.
The room was pitch black. Shen Feizhi leaned languidly against the headboard, his shirt collar hanging open with only a single button at the abdomen still fastened. Both his hands were pulled back and handcuffed behind him, and an inhibitor collar was locked around his neck. Drenched in sweat, his head was tilted back against the bedframe.
Hearing the commotion, he struggled to lift his eyelids and glanced toward the door.
Likely lacking the strength to lose his temper further, his voice was hoarse as he asked, "What exactly do you want to do?"
Gu Suizhou said nothing, walking toward him in silence.
He thoughtfully tightened Shen Feizhi’s handcuffs, wiped away his sweat, and then picked up a cup to feed him some water.
Shen Feizhi accepted it all, though he was somewhat puzzled by the other man's reaction.
