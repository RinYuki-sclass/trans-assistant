Yet, if he were to say he hated it, that wouldn't exactly be true.
Gu Suizhou undid the last button of his shirt, his hand circling restlessly around his waist.
Shen Feizhi soon found it impossible to hold back; his breathing became ragged and uneven.
Gu Suizhou watched with satisfaction as a flush began to creep across the other man’s pale skin.
“Brother Shen, you said you wouldn’t lie to me,” he murmured. “So, do you hate it?”
What could Shen Feizhi do? He had dug this hole himself; he had no choice but to jump in.
He sighed. “No…”
Before the word had fully left his lips, Gu Suizhou gave a triumphant smile. He wedged his thumb between Shen’s teeth and kissed him back, rough and urgent.
This was no longer a tentative graze; it was a full-scale invasion.
Shen Feizhi bit him hard in frustration. “You’ve learned how to play games with me now?”
“You taught me well—use any means necessary to get what you want. Your words.” Gu Suizhou didn't pull away as he spoke, his voice muffled against him. “I know you don’t hate it. If you did, you would’ve broken your own arms just to push me off and beat me to death. But it wouldn't matter; even if you did hate it, I still wouldn't let you go.”
From the moment he took the first step, he had already decided to throw caution to the wind.
His previous stunts had all been for show, but this time he was going for something big, all for the sake of seeing exactly how high Shen Feizhi’s tolerance for him truly was.
The results were pleasantly surprising.
Shen Feizhi closed his eyes; unable to escape, he could only be forced to accept the other’s transgression.
However, Gu Suizhou was right about one thing: he was indeed acquiescing to his actions.
If he weren’t willing, no one could force him to do such a thing.
Simply put, he was just bearing the consequences of the little brat he himself had spoiled.
Gu Suizhou’s hand suddenly crawled up from his knee, stopping at a delicate spot.
Perhaps because his courage hadn't quite reached that level yet, he didn't go any further, instead looking at him with a half-smile. "Bro?"
