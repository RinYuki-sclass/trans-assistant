Seeing that he couldn't be persuaded, the doctor took the hint and stopped speaking, picking up his medical bag and leaving.
Only after he had gone did Gu Suizhou approach Shen Feizhi’s bedroom door and knock: "Brother Shen?"
From inside came a single, "Get lost."
Gu Suizhou gave a low laugh: "I won't."
He crouched down, hands resting on his knees, and leaned quietly against the door. "I'm staying right here to watch over you."
This time, the person inside remained silent.
Gu Suizhou leaned his side against the door, toyed with his wooden pendant, and carefully strained to distinguish the sounds from within.

"Yeah, it's me."

Gu Suizhou stopped what he was doing and pressed himself even closer to the door.
The man inside sounded weak, his voice intermittent and difficult to hear clearly.
"I don't have an Omega."
