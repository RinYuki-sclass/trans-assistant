Gu Suizhou hesitated.
Shen Feizhi’s eyes were cold. "Absolute obedience, was it?"
Gu Suizhou hurriedly moved to unlock him.
Shen Feizhi didn't let him help; instead, he used the strength of his core to sit up on the edge of the bed. He languidly lifted his eyes toward the other man. "Kneel."
His long legs were bent casually to support him, and one side of the cold metal collar pulsed with a faint blue light. His hands were still cuffed behind his back and his shirt was wide open. By all rights, he should have looked like a wreck.
But as Shen Feizhi sat there lazily, he looked more like the one in control than the fully dressed Gu Suizhou.
"Whatever I say goes?"
Shen Feizhi looked at Gu Suizhou—who was now down on one knee—with a ghost of a smile. He lifted a foot and pressed it lightly against the knee Gu Suizhou had on the floor, his gaze sweeping darkly over his lips.
Gu Suizhou froze, looking at him in disbelief. "Are you serious?"
To be honest, he had planned on using his hands. He really hadn't thought…
Gu Suizhou froze for a long moment, staring at him in disbelief. "Are you serious?"
To be honest, he had planned on using his hands; he really hadn't expected…
Shen Feizhi said, "Then never mind."
Gu Suizhou: "…"
This man really knew how to hold back. Even in this state, he still looked completely unfazed.
He grit his teeth. "Fine."
The smartphone’s ringtone cut through the air abruptly. Shen Feizhi leaned back. "Answer it for me. Put it on speaker."
The phone rang for a good while longer before C2’s voice finally came through the speaker.
"Shen Feizhi, are you okay? Dammit, that little brat Gu Suizhou won't open the door for me! I don't dare barge in, so how are you doing? I’m still at the door."
The shadows of Shen Feizhi’s half-closed lashes fell over his lower eyelids. He steadied his breathing and said, "I’m fine. Go home. In a couple of days… hiss … I’ll come find you."
