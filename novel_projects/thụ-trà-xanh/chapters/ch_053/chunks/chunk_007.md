Belatedly, Gu Suizhou caught the faint scent of coffee wafting from the bedroom.
His eyes dimmed. "I've differentiated into an Alpha, haven't I?"
The doctor nodded, his voice tinged with admiration. "That's correct. Moreover, you are S-tier—the highest level. There are no more than ten in all of Blue Star."
Gu Suizhou showed no excitement; he only asked, "Then why did I react so strongly to the pheromones of one of my own kind?"
He remembered exactly what had just happened.
The moment Shen Feizhi’s pheromones turned violent, he hadn't felt a shred of the typical repulsion or aggression toward a fellow Alpha. Instead, he had felt a desperate, predatory hunger to seize and possess.
The doctor froze. "That… are you certain?"
Clearly, he was at a loss to explain such a reaction.
Gu Suizhou didn't press him, simply waving a hand. "I’m fine now. You can leave."
"But I believe you still need to be kept under observation," the doctor said, sounding uneasy. "And Mr. Shen as well—he won't let me in. I have no idea what his current condition is."
