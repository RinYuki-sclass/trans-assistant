Chapter 53
As the saying goes, misfortunes never come singly. Shen Feizhi never expected his own estrus period to arrive at a time like this.
The temporary channel was too unstable, and C2 had urged him to wait a few more days. But Shen Feizhi felt deep down that something was going to go wrong, so he risked his life and forced his way back.
The result of such recklessness was total bodily dysfunction, triggering a pheromone riot.
Sitting in the car on the way back, he didn't know whether to be relieved that he'd made it in time for Gu Suizhou's differentiation or to have a headache over his own estrus.
The residents of the flat had already been settled elsewhere by Xu Zhong. Carrying a bag of medicine, Shen Feizhi knocked on the door to alert the person inside that he was back, then unlocked it directly.
The moment the door opened, Shen Feizhi’s keen senses picked up on a hint of danger.
But he quickly recognized the source of that danger and stood still.
As expected, Gu Suizhou's hand reached out from behind and pressed against his waist, his voice low and hoarse: "You're back."
Even through the fabric of his clothes, Shen Feizhi could feel the scorching heat of the other's palm.
Something was wrong with his voice.
Shen Feizhi frowned. Without time to overthink, and after confirming that nothing catastrophic had happened yet, he turned around and patted the other's head. "Go lie down on the bed. I brought your medicine."
Gu Suizhou stood rooted to the spot, his entire body unnaturally hot, his fair face flushed deep crimson.
Yet, the look in his eyes was icy, a stark contrast to his body temperature.
