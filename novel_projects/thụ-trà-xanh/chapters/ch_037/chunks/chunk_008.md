His condition had flared up again, worse than ever before. His gland burned as if it were about to explode, and he couldn't release any pheromones.
He had endured it for half a month, nearly dying twice before finally pulling through.
His private doctor said he might be about to differentiate.
Given his condition, the uncertainty and risk of accidents during differentiation were extremely high. Without absolute certainty, they dared not let him differentiate recklessly. The only option was to administer medication first and wait for his physical condition to stabilize.
The two walked one after the other along the tree-lined path on campus. Shen Feizhi suddenly spoke, "Once your parents' affairs are settled, what are your plans?"
Gu Suizhou paused for a moment. "Probably… focus on my studies and come back to take over W.N."
The early inheritance procedures had recently been approved. By next week at the latest, the money and stock shares would be in his hands.
Gu Suizhou didn't care about the wealth; what he truly wanted were certain documents included in the inheritance. Xu Zhong would deliver them to his home tonight. He hoped Lin Sui hadn't discovered and destroyed them.
Shen Feizhi nodded, his lips parting. He was about to say that once everything was over, they could both return to their separate lives.
But a soft click interrupted the words poised on his tongue.
Somehow, the other man had raised a camera and was now beaming at him. "Brother Shen, give me a smile."
"…" Shen Feizhi turned, his expression blank. "It's your graduation ceremony. Why are you taking a picture of me ?"
"Isn't a graduation ceremony for commemorating meaningful things?" Gu Suizhou carefully focused the lens. "And isn't that exactly what I'm doing right now?"
Shen Feizhi froze for a moment.
At that very instant, Gu Suizhou pressed the shutter.
