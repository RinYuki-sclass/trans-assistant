【Scoundrel: Hehe~】

Shen Feizhi stared, speechless, at the vibrant orange-and-blue color-blocked cardigan. For once, he felt utterly out of place.
Though this body looked twenty, he himself had lived for countless years, enduring ages untold. To wear such youthful attire with his ancient mindset felt deeply unsettling.
Yet, perhaps because he hadn't fully woken up this morning, or perhaps because he was tormented by someone's plea for a "once-in-a-lifetime ritual," he'd had a lapse in judgment and reluctantly put it on.
Outside, the sun blazed fiercely. Most people would have worn a hat or carried an umbrella, but Shen Feizhi was indifferent to such things. He didn't even bother with sunscreen, simply grabbing his smartphone and striding out the door.
Lu Qian had been waiting downstairs for some time. When she looked up, this was the sight that greeted her:
The ascetic, devastatingly handsome man, whose skin was so fair it seemed almost translucent in the sunlight, was uncharacteristically dressed in a youthful, vibrant, casual style. His hair was shorter than the last time she'd seen him. Aside from his ice-cold expression, he looked every bit the energetic college student.
She swallowed hard, not even noticing when he stopped right in front of her.
Shen Feizhi looked at the woman holding a beautiful parasol. "Lu Qian? What are you doing here?"
Lu Qian snapped back to reality, blinking. "I needed to see you about something. I hadn't gotten around to messaging you yet. Are you… heading out?"
