The person was stunning, the scenery lovely, but for some reason, he couldn't shake the feeling that… the man in the picture might vanish in the next second.
No, no, no!
What was he thinking? Such nonsense.
He shook his head, forcefully clearing his mind of the illogical thought. Spotting a passing student, he handed them the camera. "Excuse me, could you help us take a picture?"
The student readily agreed.
Gu Suizhou hurried over and gently pressed his shoulder against Shen Feizhi's arm.
He whispered, "Brother Shen, Brother Shen, look at the camera."
Look at my ass.
Shen Feizhi hated having his picture taken, but since the student was already poised, he didn't want to spoil the moment. Reluctantly, he fixed his gaze on the lens.
The student clicked the shutter twice, then studied the photos with a troubled expression. "Could you two smile? Otherwise, it looks too stiff. The pictures won't turn out well."
What do you mean, 'turn out well'? Shen Feizhi thought. Isn't it enough that you can see us?
His patience completely evaporated. "That's enough—"
"Brother Shen." Gu Suizhou tugged at the corner of his shirt, looking at him with pitiful eyes. "This is the first time I've had my picture taken since I was six."
