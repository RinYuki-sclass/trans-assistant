"Yes. Gu Suizhou's graduation ceremony."
Shen Feizhi got out of the car. "Wait for us here, or head off on your own?"
Lu Qian considered it. "I'll go ahead. I need to let my mother know."
"Alright, I'll call a car for you."
After watching Lu Qian's car drive away, Shen Feizhi turned to leave.
The moment the security guard saw him, he blocked his path. "Show me your student ID."
Shen Feizhi: "…I'm his guardian."
The security guard's face twisted in disbelief. "Who are you trying to fool? Someone like you? A guardian?"
Just as Shen Feizhi was about to patiently explain, a soft chuckle drifted from ahead. Immediately after, the alluring scent of chocolate unreasonably invaded all his senses.
He turned his head to see Gu Suizhou jogging toward him, dressed in a small formal suit. Gu Suizhou pressed a handmade chocolate against his lips. "Uncle, he really is my guardian. My parents died when I was young, and he's the only brother I have."

With that, he handed over his student ID.
The guard took it, verified the information, and finally waved them through.
Shen Feizhi instinctively curled his tongue, drawing the chocolate into his mouth.
Seeing Gu Suizhou again after two months, he suddenly felt the boy had grown taller—now a full forehead taller than him.
But he was still just as thin, probably not eating properly.
Shen Feizhi pulled his wrist free from Gu Suizhou's grasp. Only after the chocolate had melted and he'd swallowed did he say, "You get to wear a nice little suit, but you make me wear casual clothes?"
Their current clothing styles were completely swapped. The white shirt that little rascal was wearing underneath was almost identical to the ones Shen Feizhi usually had hanging in his room. However, Shen Feizhi rarely wore suits, so their outerwear styles still differed.
Gu Suizhou's own wardrobe was a riot of colors and styles, boasting more shades than a rainbow. It was so extensive you could stage a full-blown fashion show with it.
This included his underwear.
