The bad habit of smoking had taken root long ago.
He couldn't recall the exact reason, only that the pressure had been immense—enough to drive a person to madness—and only the scent of nicotine could calm him.
Shen Feizhi took a deep drag, pressing his palm to his forehead.
The casino's rule was simple: as long as the dealer didn't catch you cheating, it wasn't cheating. He'd played before, but this time, he could clearly feel his fingers weren't as nimble as they used to be—likely due to his recent physical decline.
He had a hunch this change was connected to the Main Star.
Dragging this out wasn't an option. He needed to resolve things quickly and make a trip to the Energy Storm Eye.
He'd only taken one drag from the cigarette, leaving it untouched as he quietly waited for it to burn down, inhaling the strong scent of tobacco until he'd had his fill.
He crushed the cigarette butt with his bare hand, tossed it into a trash can, and then sent a message to a media contact he'd recently reconnected with.
Before long, the W.N scandal from ten years ago resurfaced in the public eye.
