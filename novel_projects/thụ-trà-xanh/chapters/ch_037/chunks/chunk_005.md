"It's about the Shen family," Lu Qian said. "Elder Master Shen is getting restless."
This was serious business.
Shen Feizhi clicked his tongue. "Let's talk in the car."
"Is that okay?"
"Yes. Get in first."
This time, Shen Feizhi didn't drive himself. He activated the car's autopilot and sat in the back with Lu Qian, reviewing documents.
"Elder Master Shen is only just now realizing Shen Wei's shareholding? That's much later than I expected. His age is really showing."
He flipped through the Shen family's recent ledgers, his gaze lingering on a transaction from just yesterday. "One million… Doesn't this recipient account number look familiar to you?"
Lu Qian followed his gaze, pondered for a moment, then gasped. "Lu Fan?!"
"Yes." Shen Feizhi's eyes narrowed. "He's busy destroying the basement. It seems he really can't sit still anymore."
Lu Qian rubbed her hands together, eager. "Does that mean the forged ledgers I prepared can finally be put to use?"
"Soon." A low chuckle escaped Shen Feizhi's throat. "You can mention our engagement ceremony to your mother now."
They had to strike the Shen family when they least expected it. Only then could they expose that basement to the public eye at the first opportunity.
The W.N incident from years ago was already common knowledge. By linking it to the basement, netizens with vivid imaginations would undoubtedly spin countless dramatic theories. The ensuing scandal would entangle Shen Wei, buying them precious time to secure the R&D rights.
