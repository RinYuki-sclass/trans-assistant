Chapter 37
Upon their return to Blue Star, their schedules instantly filled.
Shen Feizhi moved swiftly, finalizing most of the agreements with Shen Wei in short order.
The entire process was conducted openly, so Elder Master Shen quickly learned of the developments.
"What do you think you're doing?" Elder Shen's call came through while Shen Feizhi was at a casino, playing roulette.
Hearing the question, Shen Feizhi finished his final round, gestured to the others that he was leaving the table, and walked to a quiet lounge area.
"Nothing much," Shen Feizhi replied. "You asked me to support Shen Wei, and I've done exactly as you wished. Is there something you're still dissatisfied with?"
He had given away the shares and relinquished power—a perfect "right-hand man" in every sense.
Lin Sui knew Shen Feizhi worked at W.N, but he would never have guessed he was the head of W.N's R&D department.
And what Lin Sui didn't know, Elder Master Shen certainly didn't either. Though the old man might suspect his motives, his suspicions would never stray in that direction.
The noisy background on Shen Feizhi's end gave Elder Shen a headache. His tone was sharp with displeasure. "If you didn't want the company shares, you could have refused from the start. Why go through all this trouble?"
Shen Feizhi knew that painting himself as too noble might backfire and arouse the old man's suspicion. So he said, "But only by personally handing him the shares could I earn a bit of his trust."
As expected, the Old Master's tone softened, though he still wasn't pleased.
"We're all family. There's no need for so many petty schemes."
