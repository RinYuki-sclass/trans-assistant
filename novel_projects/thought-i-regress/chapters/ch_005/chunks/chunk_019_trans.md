Again, while Liam was adorable, the trouble he caused was a headache. Caden pressed his brow with his index finger, smoothing out the frown that had formed as he recalled today's incident – Liam causing trouble without a word to him, forcing Caden to clean it up with strained logic.

Liam's sudden massacre was probably connected to 'going back.' If not, he'd better prepare to be hit by Caden. After all, Caden hadn't given him the raid information so he could do *this*.

Did he even know that what he'd done was a clear obstruction of official duties? Recalling Liam's actions, Caden's head began to throb again. He lightly tapped his head with his wrist, then rose from his seat. It was time to dig for new clues from the sloppy wizard who must have arrived at the mansion by now.

The sloppy wizard who greeted Caden was, contrary to expectations, very dejected. Caden had expected him to greet him with a gentle, eye-crinkling smile, so he was slightly surprised by this reaction and asked,

"Liam. I haven't even opened my mouth yet, so why the long face?"

"Cadeeen..."

Liam, perched on the bed, looked up at Caden, drawing out his name with a gloomy expression.

Who would think this pitiful, boyish face belonged to a mass murderer who had just slaughtered dozens of criminals? Caden sighed inwardly and opened his arms. Liam clung to him as if he'd been waiting.

Caden lightly stroked Liam's soft, curly hair as he nestled into his arms, speaking in a light tone.

"Well, now. I can't even scold you."

"...Don't scold me."

"Anyone listening would think I'd actually managed to scold you properly even once."

The truth was, he hadn't even been able to get properly angry at him yet. Caden clicked his tongue in exasperation, yet tightened the arms wrapped around Liam. Feeling Liam burrow deeper into his embrace, rubbing his face against Caden's chest, Caden stroked the back of the boy who clung to him so helplessly.

"Why are you so down in the dumps?"

"I'm sorry... Caden must have prepared so hard."

"As I always say, you have an exceptional ability to escape difficult situations. And you always choose to do things that make me weak for you."

Hearing Caden's words, Liam tightened his grip on Caden without a reply. Caden smiled faintly, thinking how cute Liam's silent gesture was, simply hugging him even tighter.

Seeing him avoid the topic again, it seemed to be related to 'going back,' just as Caden had suspected. Caden considered whether to press this dejected lump of secrets further, to dig out the truth he was hiding, but then, pitying his slumped shoulders, decided to give in just one more time.

He silently pulled Liam deeper into his embrace. Though Liam was a formidable size to hold completely in his arms, Caden spread his arms as wide as possible, holding him without a gap.

As he quietly held the boy in his arms, a floral scent rose from him. Liam smelled of daffodils.
