Chapter 5. Autumn Daffodils

The man was what people commonly called a villain. He belonged to a notorious criminal organization in the back alleys, one that even the king, the ruler of this country, couldn't easily eradicate. He lived by gathering in groups, preying on others, and stealing their possessions.

As a child, he was lucky enough to connect with a large organization and join it, and from then on, he sustained his own life by taking the lives of others.

The man had no major complaints about his life. On the contrary, he felt a thrill when people pointed fingers at him and condemned him as a villain. He thought he had always lived that way, and would continue to live that way—forever in the position of a villain.

But now, for the first time, the man found himself not in the position of a villain, but of the weak, trembling and weeping as death choked him.

The silence was so profound that the distinct sound of footsteps echoed clearly. The man clapped a hand over his mouth, afraid his own breath might escape.

His body shook uncontrollably at the sight of blood through the gap in the metal crate where he had managed to squeeze himself. Just thirty minutes ago, the faces that had been laughing together were now all gone, burst like fragile balloons, scattering flesh and marrow everywhere.

"Aargh!"

Another scream came from not far away. But the scream was not long. The choking, gurgling sound quickly faded, adding to the already pooling blood on the floor.

Seeing the flowing blood, the man soiled himself in terror of a gruesome death, trying to control his body, which shook like an aspen leaf.

But his body, certain that death was near, did not heed its owner's will. Tears streamed down his face in fear, and the man took in every detail of the scene outside, unable to even close his eyes.

In the middle of the pooling blood, the protagonist of this entire massacre stood. The black-clad man, standing under the moonlight, was as beautiful as a sculpture, but his appearance, unstained by a single drop of blood despite committing such a slaughter, seemed to the man to be closer to a demon.

The man lightly turned, as if checking if anyone was left. When he saw no one else, he tilted his head. Watching him, the villain in the crate silently wept, praying that the man would simply leave.

However, as if to cruelly betray the man's hopes, the man opened his hand. A small blue magic circle instantly appeared above it. The sorcerer hummed a tuneless, arrhythmic melody and spun around in the opposite direction.

"Solomon Grundy,
Born on a Monday,
Christened on Tuesday,
Married on Wednesday,
Ill on Thursday,
Worse on Friday,"
