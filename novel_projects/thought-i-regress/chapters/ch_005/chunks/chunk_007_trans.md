He would habitually look for Liam in places where he might be, and a slight disappointment would stir when he realized Liam wasn't there.

It had started as an amusement, watching Liam's reactions as he constantly stared and followed him, but now, if Liam's gaze was directed elsewhere, Caden felt a slight irritation. The moment he discovered this feeling, Caden was shocked. Oh, damn it.

He'd heard of people falling into their own traps, and that was exactly his situation now. Sitting at his desk in the bright midday sun, Caden rubbed his throbbing temples with hollow eyes, deep in thought. Should he confess?

But the thought of confessing first made him feel unnecessarily petty. *He* liked me first. For *me* to fall for him and confess in return, it felt somehow displeasing.

The childish, petty adult thought, *Alright, let's wait a little longer. If he doesn't confess by the time this work is done, then I'll do it.* The cowardly adult, feigning prudence, thus arbitrarily shifted the burden onto the young man who had yet to shed his boyishness.

But this was only half simple pettiness. He hadn't expected this situation. Really.

Caden looked at the tear-streaked, youthful face with eyes full of bewilderment. When he faced Liam, who had not only suddenly shown up during work but was also buzzing with alcohol, holding his hand and confessing, Caden thought, *Finally.*

The cowardly adult, whose patience had been slowly wearing thin, couldn't hide his satisfaction. His eyes crinkled as he smiled, accepting Liam's confession.

Liam, as if he hadn't expected his confession to be accepted, stared at Caden with his mouth agape before smiling as if he had gained the world. He was oblivious to being played right into the wicked adult's hands.

Caden, the wicked adult who had teased the young man, smiled, finding the sight adorable. Up to this point, it had truly been a satisfying development.

But then, Liam Carter suddenly began to insist he wanted to sleep with Caden. Caden was truly flustered by the demand. Even if their feelings were mutual, at three in the afternoon?

Besides, Caden was currently working. If he took Liam to the bedroom now, wouldn't it blatantly show his subordinates that their superior had devoured a much younger man right after they started dating? It wasn't proper behavior for a superior, especially when he was already calling his subordinates in to work on their day off.

And right now, Liam was clearly confessing under the influence of alcohol. Wouldn't his thoughts change once he sobered up and came to his senses?

Though he had no intention of letting Liam go even if he tried to back out after sobering up, Caden still felt the need to uphold a minimum level of propriety. After much consideration, Caden swallowed his regret and politely cut off Liam's insistence.

Hearing the refusal, Liam nodded, saying he understood, and bowed his head deeply. Feeling something was off, Caden asked him to look up. The moment Liam hesitantly raised his head and met Caden's eyes, he couldn't hold back, and tears streamed from his trembling blue eyes.

Caden wished Liam would cry loudly, if only to ease his own mind, but Liam was weeping silently, as if something was deeply sorrowful. Watching his pale, handsome face become a mess of tears, Caden felt a little troubled. Liam's crying face, more than he expected, stirred a certain desire in him.

Feeling somewhat like a scoundrel, Caden swallowed a sigh and took Liam's hand.

In bed, Liam Carter was more aggressive than expected. In fact, Caden was a little dumbfounded when Liam naturally climbed on top of him. *Is this guy trying to top me?*

It wouldn't have been difficult to flip over a drunk man, so he briefly considered changing positions but decided against it. What was the point of overpowering a young man who was struggling to sleep with him?

But when Liam inserted himself inside him, Caden regretted a decision he'd made less than ten minutes ago. He had never regretted a decision so quickly in his life.
