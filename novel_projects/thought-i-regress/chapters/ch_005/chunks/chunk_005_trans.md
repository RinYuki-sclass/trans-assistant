And unfortunately, Liam's words like that were the kind Caden detested.

Caden, suppressing the irritation that welled up for a moment, slowly decided to retrace Liam's massacre. Could the fact that he went and came back without telling him, even though he clearly knew Caden would dislike it, be related to that 'going back' he always mentioned?

Caden sharpened his gaze, recalling his first meeting with Liam, which was still vivid in his memory.

When Caden first saw Liam, he thought Liam was crying. In reality, far from crying, Liam was smiling with his eyes at Caden, his face innocent like a boy's, but Caden detected an unconcealed sadness in that smiling face.

He was puzzled. Why was he making such an expression towards him, when they had clearly just met? And why was he making such an expression only towards him? That must have been why he started observing the fellow closely.

During the final stages of pursuing an organization he had been tracking for a long time, the fellow brought by Ravi Fleur, whom he had deemed quite useful and brought into the operation, was strange from their very first meeting.

The fellow, who was said to have rescued Ravi Fleur after he got into danger by venturing deep into the hideout of some peripheral members of the organization, had been staring blankly into space the whole time, but as soon as he saw Caden arrive at the scene, his eyes sparkled, and he extended his hand for a handshake, saying he was pleased to meet him.

Although a little eccentric, it was behavior that could easily be considered that of a cheerful and good-natured person. However, Caden grew suspicious when he saw Ravi Fleur, who had actually brought him, somehow hesitate and treat Liam with an awkward attitude.

Still, until then, Caden had only perceived Liam as merely a slightly strange fellow. But a moment later, when Caden saw the scene Liam Carter had created, he completely revised that thought. From a slightly strange fellow to a madman.

He had heard that Liam had, in turn, dealt with the fellows who had tried to kill Ravi Fleur. But he had truly not expected it to be done in such a manner.

Guided by Ravi Fleur, whose complexion had been pale the entire time, Caden arrived at a place where headless corpses were strewn about in abundance. Seeing the corpses, not a single one spared from that fate, Caden felt his stomach churn and asked Liam.

"Did you do this?"

"Yes."

"Even if they were criminals, the method of dealing with them is excessive."

"Hmm. But isn't it efficient?"

"Efficient?"

"What if they're ambiguously dealt with and survive?"

At Caden's words, Liam blinked his eyes with an innocent expression and tilted his head. Seeing that face, Caden was dumbfounded. To talk about efficiency in murder with that innocent face.

As Caden's expression involuntarily twisted at Liam's re-questioning, the fellow, in turn, became more flustered and began to ponder what he had said wrong. And as he did so, Caden saw that familiar sad look, which had gradually faded while they talked, return to his eyes.

Seeing those eyes, Caden felt his resolve weaken, and the thought of pressing the fellow further vanished. Faced with Liam's gentle face, still unaware of what he had done wrong, Caden sighed and withdrew from the argument. What was he even talking about with a mage he had just met today?
