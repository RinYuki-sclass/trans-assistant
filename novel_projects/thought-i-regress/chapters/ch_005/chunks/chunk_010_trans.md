The documents, which he would normally have reviewed quickly, didn't register in his mind at all. He pressed his throbbing temples and recalled the utterly miserable events of that morning.

He had woken up later than usual, having overexerted himself dealing with Liam Carter, who, despite their twelve-year age gap, clung to him relentlessly without tiring.

Waking to the sunlight filtering through the curtains, he fought off his fatigue and slowly opened his eyes, blinking at the empty space beside him. Then, the obscene scenes that had unfolded until just before he slept flashed before his eyes, and he shot upright.

Caden, who had paused at the ache in his body from rising too quickly, inadvertently looked down at himself and was horrified.

His thighs were messy with the previous night's fluids, no cleanup having been done, his body ached everywhere, and bite marks covered him as if he'd been savaged by a beast. But worst of all was the empty space beside him.

Caden's expression twisted mercilessly as he reached out to steady himself, only to feel the empty space beside him, devoid of any lingering warmth. He pulled the cord, summoning a servant.

The servant, entering at his master's summons, briefly widened his eyes at his master's disheveled appearance before quietly lowering his gaze. To the obedient servant, his master's voice, on the verge of exploding, soon descended.

"Larry. Bring me the mage who visited the estate yesterday."

"Now!"

The command, which had started calmly, grew increasingly agitated, the last part almost a shout. The servant replied that he understood, instinctively sensing that the estate's atmosphere would be unsettled for some time.

And now, Caden had learned that Liam Carter was nowhere to be found on the estate. At his master's command, which had turned the entire estate upside down, all the servants had to gather and report if they had seen a man named Liam Carter.

Then, a new maid, after hearing Liam Carter's description, reported that she had seen a black-haired mage leaving the estate that morning. She even added that the man had hailed a carriage and headed towards the residential district.

Jackson, who delivered the news that the mage, having just slept with his master, had then brazenly walked out the front gate, seemed a little too amused, further fueling Caden's rage.

Caden, recalling the morning's events, swept a hand over his face, rigid with anger, and put down the documents he couldn't focus on. His aide, with a look of disbelief, asked his superior, who was rising from his seat,

"Where are you going?"

"I'm going to Liam Carter's house."

"He might not be home."

"I'll deal with that when I get there, so prepare the carriage at once!"

Caden, who had risen and given the aide his orders, gritted his teeth and strode forward, feeling the discomfort below with every step. He placed his hand on the doorknob, about to open the door, then paused, turned his head, and commanded his aide.

"Also, select some knights to come with us. Liam Carter is a skilled mage, after all."
