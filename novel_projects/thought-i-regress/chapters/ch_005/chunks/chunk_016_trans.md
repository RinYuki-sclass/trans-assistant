If my acceptance of his confession was unexpected, it was worth considering that his plan to 'go back' had been derailed from that point.

But Liam seemed to consider his return definite even up until we slept together. Could it be that staying with him until morning after we slept together caused a change?

My head was spinning. Developing hypotheses when it wasn't even clear what he meant by 'going back' led to many dead ends.

If 'going back' meant returning to a place, or, though it defied common sense, going back in time, and my acceptance of his confession and our sleeping together had interfered with his 'going back'....

Does an unexpected event prevent him from going back? The deduction didn't quite fit, but it was worth considering.

As my mind was tangled with these thoughts, the carriage arrived at the mansion and stopped. Feeling the carriage stop, Liam rolled his large eyes, glanced at me, and quickly stepped out.

The rascal, who had gotten out first, reached out a hand as if to escort me, so I chuckled softly, amused, and took his hand to descend. If this was how he was going to act, why on earth did he run away this morning?

Pushing aside my many questions, I first entered the mansion. After ordering my subordinates to take Liam to the mistress's room, I headed to my office.

Giving him the mistress's room didn't hold much significance. Giving him a guest room felt inappropriate since we were lovers, and after the commotion this morning, giving him the mistress's room would ensure the household staff treated him with proper respect...

In truth, setting aside all those reasons, I just wanted to give it to him.

Since the subject of 'going back' might be a place, I strictly warned the knights not to let Liam leave the room, then entered my office.

Jackson, who had been waiting in the office, handed me the unfinished work from this morning, with a rather disrespectful look in his eyes. I considered saying something about his usual impertinent attitude but decided against it. I couldn't postpone work again because of my emotions, so I had to endure it.

While reviewing documents under the gaze of my aide, a gaze so disrespectful it was hard to believe it was directed at his superior, a boisterous sound came from outside. I couldn't concentrate today, so the outdoor noise bothered me, and I turned my eyes towards it, whereupon Jackson looked out the window and answered instead.

"It's the sound of the mansion staff's children running around and playing for the Harvest Festival."

Whenever there was a national festival, parts of the mansion would be decorated so that the staff and their children could also enjoy the festivities within the estate.

Jackson, who informed me of the sound, seemed unusually excited for the Harvest Festival, and with a slight lift of the corners of his usually stern mouth, he added.

"It's already the season for the Spring Goddess to return to the Underworld."

"Time certainly flies."

Caden, who had been idly agreeing with his aide, suddenly widened his eyes at a thought that struck him. The Spring Goddess?

The Spring Goddess, whose ankle was caught in the Underworld after eating its pomegranates. Liam, who was presumably unable to 'go back' because his ankle was caught, perhaps due to Caden himself. A clue of some sort was teasing him, lingering just out of reach.
