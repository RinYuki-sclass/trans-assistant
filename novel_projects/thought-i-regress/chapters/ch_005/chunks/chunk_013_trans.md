Even as he sniffled, Liam asked me to change my position, perhaps because his stomach hurt from bumping against my shoulder. I pondered what position would be comfortable for both of us to go down, then picked Liam up again.

I carried him, supporting his back and under his knees, and started down the stairs. I thought this position was fine, comfortable for both of us, but Liam, for some unknown reason, asked me to change my position again.

He was usually agreeable with anything I did, smiling and happy, but now he was being particular in a strange way. I paused and asked if he had a preferred position. Liam seemed to ponder, then squirmed in my arms, readjusting himself.

He wrapped his legs around my waist and his arms around my neck, then said in a satisfied voice, “This is good.”

It was absurd that he rejected a comfortable position for one like an animal clinging to a tree, but if he was happy with it, I didn’t care. I held him firmly and continued down the stairs.

As we descended, I felt his long legs dangling, not fully wrapped around my waist. I asked him once more if he was truly okay with this position, and he nodded firmly, saying he liked it. I couldn’t understand his criteria for "okay."

On our way to the carriage, I felt Liam subtly bury his face in my shoulder. My shoulder was getting noticeably damp from his tears, which I found exasperating. Dating a kid really brings all sorts of strange things.

But I didn’t say anything, fearing I might make him cry again. As I walked, he carefully asked me,

“You won’t scold me anymore, will you…?”

“What good would it do to threaten a twenty-year-old further?”

I was sincere, but Liam, for some reason, tightened his grip on my arms and legs. He was cute, anyway.

This morning, when I was looking for him, I was so furious I couldn’t think straight, but now I was simply happy. I found my own state bewildering.

I put Liam in the carriage, vaguely answered his words, and observed him. Before coming here, I had considered many possibilities for what "I don’t want to go back" might mean, but seeing Liam’s current state, I felt I had to discard all my hypotheses, which was a headache.

He had willingly left the house, so it didn’t seem to mean he had to return somewhere, and his condition was also the same as usual. What exactly did "I don’t want to go back" refer to?

I watched him quietly, looking down and fiddling with his fingers as if he knew his own sins, but no answer came to me. From what he said yesterday, he didn’t seem to intend to explain it himself. It was a difficult problem.

I wasn’t feeling well, and continuously pondering this problem gave me a headache. I turned my head to look out the window.

Outside, children and adults were smiling brightly, moving along the streets. It was a clear blue sky, a bountiful autumn landscape, and the ordinary scene of a harvest festival, no different from previous years.

I paused at a sudden thought.

‘Previous years? Wait a minute.’

I gasped at the thought that flashed through my mind. It was absurd, but what if…
