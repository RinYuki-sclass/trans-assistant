The price of turning his back, thinking that since nothing had happened last time, it would be the same this time, was too great. Caden had protected Liam from the organization member's blade, giving his own life instead.

Liam still didn't know why Caden had protected him back then. Instead of delving into the reason, he vowed every time the incident came to mind that he would not repeat the same nightmare.

Liam quietly lowered his head and looked at the blood beneath his feet. He had to clear out this place anyway, even if only because of the 'original' timeline.

Although things were already distorted beyond recognition, as long as everything remained uncertain, it would be best to follow the original as much as possible. However, he could probably handle this incident alone.

Liam gazed down at the blood-stained moon reflected in the pool of blood, then quietly closed his eyes.

“Your Grace. A report has come in that the Tribula hideout, which was scheduled for a raid this dawn, has already been attacked by someone.”

Caden lightly frowned at his subordinate's flustered words and took the report from him. As Caden read the report, his face grew increasingly serious.

[No survivors. All deceased found with their heads burst open. The perpetrator is presumed to be a mage, as no traces were left behind.]

“Shall I issue an order to first track down mages who hold a grudge against Tribula?”

“No, we will not track them.”

“Pardon?”

Caden explained again to his subordinate, whose eyes were wide with surprise at his superior's words.

“What we were pursuing was Tribula, was it not? We cannot waste time and manpower chasing an unknown, unrelated individual right now.”

At Caden's instruction to prioritize understanding the situation and investigating whether the organization had other hidden hideouts, the subordinate nodded, saluted, and left the office.

Watching his subordinate fully exit the office, Caden pressed a hand to his forehead and tapped his fingers on the desk. He had told his subordinate that the identity was unknown, but Caden had a strong suspicion as to who had annihilated one of Tribula's hideouts.

Contrary to his gentle demeanor, Liam had a cruel streak in his methods. The state of the deceased in the report was exactly the way Liam typically killed people.

But he didn't understand. Why, exactly? While Liam didn't value human lives, he also wasn't dedicated enough to massacre those against whom he held no clear grudge.

Furthermore, given that he had used his usual method, there was no sign that he was trying to hide his involvement.

If he were to ask Liam, Liam would undoubtedly spout nonsense, as he always did, with innocent eyes, saying something like, "You must be tired since your birthday wasn't long ago, so I just went and came back myself, lest you get hurt if you went."

Caden loved the innocent expression Liam made towards him, with his round blue eyes, but that didn't mean he wasn't annoyed by the nonsense.
