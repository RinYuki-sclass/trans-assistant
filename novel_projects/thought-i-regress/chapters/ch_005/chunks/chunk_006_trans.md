Contrary to Caden's thought that this was just a chance encounter, he continued to get entangled with Caden. Their paths crossed once, then twice, while pursuing the organization, and the third time they met, he boldly demanded of Caden.

"Let me join this work."

Caden wondered if it was right to let this madman, whose past and identity were unclear apart from his affiliation with the Magic Tower, join their work, but he soon agreed.

As a mage from the Magic Tower, he would surely be a helpful asset, and if he pulled any stunts, Caden could always exclude him then. And above all, Caden didn't want to see those anxious eyes, which grew bluer with uncertainty the longer he hesitated.

At the time, he had justified not wanting to see the fellow's anxious gaze with all sorts of logic, but looking back now, he simply hadn't wanted to see that youthful face clouded with sadness.

As he began to work seriously with Liam Carter, Caden learned many things about him. The fellow's actual behavior differed greatly from his documented records.

Even if his past was unclear, unless he had fallen from the sky, there would naturally be traces and witnesses. Thus, it wasn't long before Caden received documents about Liam Carter, and the personality discernible from Liam Carter's documented actions was coldness itself.

Before understanding the real Liam, Caden sensed a bleakness in the documented version of him. A mage who acted impulsively according to his mood and showed little interest in anything outside of magic.

In a sense, he could be seen as the archetype of a typical mage. Looking at the documented Liam Carter, his focus on efficiency in killing made sense.

However, understanding and reality were different. The real Liam Carter was a lovable fellow who knew how to smile brightly, to an extent that made their strange first encounter hard to believe.

The man who often spoke to Caden, who liked whatever Caden said, and who smiled, his ocean-like eyes crinkling, quickly captured Caden's gaze.

Whenever Caden stared at him, Liam would quickly notice Caden watching him, then smile brightly and approach. That sight reminded Caden of the large dog he had raised as a child.

The fellow was very much like the black dog that would wag its tail and run to Caden whenever Caden looked at it, no matter where it was, and that fact quite amused Caden. That day was no different.

It was early summer, beginning to grow warm. The fellow, whom Caden happened to encounter at a shop he visited for an appointment, immediately spotted Caden, widened his already large eyes, and hurried toward him.

That sight, especially that day, overlapped with the dog in his memory. It was amusing to associate a person with a dog, so Caden chuckled lightly, and as the fellow stared at Caden with blank eyes before his face instantly flushed, Caden suddenly realized.

"Ah, this fellow. He likes me."

He wasn't so foolish as to miss such blatant emotions. The moment he realized Liam Carter's feelings, Caden felt amused.

The thought, "How many times has he even seen me?" did cross his mind briefly, but since love wasn't always a feeling that arrived in due course, he simply accepted it. After all, hadn't his own parents felt an attraction and decided to get engaged after only three meetings?

After realizing Liam Carter's love, Caden developed a new hobby. A truly useless hobby: 'Observing Liam Carter.' His friends would surely condemn it as a bad habit if they knew, but what did it matter? It was so enjoyable.

Staring at him sometimes made Caden feel like he was teasing a child, but since the fellow's face would flush and he'd become flustered whenever Caden's gaze met his, Caden figured he probably didn't dislike this hobby too much either.

One day, as he observed Liam, drawing his own conclusions, Caden suddenly felt something strange. It was because he realized he was now staring at Liam obsessively, though it had clearly started as a joke.
