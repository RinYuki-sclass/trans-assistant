Caden sighed and continued to fill out the documents. Yes, as always, it would be better to follow his heart than to regret not trying. And, perhaps, marrying Liam Carter might turn out to be better than he thought, regardless of its impact on him.

Just as Caden finished drafting the documents and instructed his aide to prepare for a visit to the royal palace soon, Liam happened to enter the office. Caden set aside the intense thoughts he had just been grappling with, donned the mask of a seasoned adult, and gestured to Liam.

“You’ve come at a good time. I have some documents that need your signature.”

“My signature?”

Watching Liam Carter approach with a bewildered expression, Caden swallowed his inner tension. It was time to slowly confirm if his hypothesis was correct.

To Caden’s surprise, his tension was for naught. Liam took the documents and readily accepted his proposal. He accepted so enthusiastically that Caden even worried a little that he might get scammed somewhere.

And over the past few months of living with him, Caden had managed to gather several insights.

First, Liam’s phrase, ‘going back,’ seemed to be related to March. Liam was a boy with little interest in anything. His only interests were Caden and magic.

That was why, no matter what Caden proposed, the boy would usually respond with an indifferent, ‘Whatever you want,’ but the one thing he had rejected, almost throwing a fit, was a March wedding.

The proposal for a March wedding itself had no other intention than to hold the ceremony quickly. However, as soon as Liam heard Caden’s suggestion, he uncharacteristically blurted out a refusal and strongly insisted on having the wedding in April.

Caden realized at that moment that this was an important clue. So, he feigned ignorance and asked what difference it made between the two months, but Liam Carter, instead of answering, showed him the face from that night.

The moment he saw that face again, the one that had cried so sorrowfully, ‘I don’t want to go back,’ Caden felt a strong thrill. This was it!

At the same time, he hated seeing that expression on Liam’s face again. He didn’t want the boy to be sad about anything other than him.

Caden could have pressed him further to dig out his secret, but he ultimately couldn’t overcome the sadness on Liam’s face and ended up grumbling, “Alright, I get it, stop making that face.”

Over several months, Caden meticulously collected the clues carelessly dropped by the clumsy mage that was Liam.

Liam’s anxiety about ‘going back,’ which sometimes even led to nightmares; his aversion to March; his intense reaction to Caden’s friend Edgar, whom he had clearly never met before; his uncharacteristic proficiency in killing for his age, yet his sexual naivete. Besides these, there were other actions that were strangely familiar yet somehow clumsy.

As Caden organized the clues, he became certain that Liam’s ‘going back’ was not about a place. Rather, seeing the clues become more plausible when he applied the most improbable hypothesis—time—Caden felt a little disturbed. What on earth have I brought into my life?

Still, marrying Liam wasn’t bad. The boy was more of a troublemaker than he had imagined, and he often caused trouble that made Caden’s neck stiffen just thinking about it, but despite all that, he was lovable.

If he could go back to the moment he proposed marriage to Liam Carter again, Caden would gladly welcome him into his life once more.

“Of course, that’s one thing, and this is another.”
