The man was not given time to understand those words. The moment the speaker finished his sentence and clenched his fist, the villain's head burst like a balloon.

“Well. If there is a next time.”

Hopefully, there won't be. Liam Carter gathered the blood droplets that had stopped in mid-air before reaching him, tossed them onto the bottom of a box, and turned away.

Above the clearing, where blood flowed like rivulets everywhere, the moon, having witnessed everything, shone brightly. The moon at this time was always a full moon, brimming with light. Liam Carter checked if there was anyone left, recalling the nightmare of this place.

Just because one regresses doesn't mean everything happens exactly the same way. Even if the main storyline remains, parts that can be changed by one person's actions often alter in a chain reaction, reflecting those changes.

It was the same back then. Liam, who had prevented the attack on Ravi and Caden, who were trying to find Tribula's leadership, exactly as he remembered, had forgotten this obvious fact and let his guard down. And carelessness came at the cost of a fatal sacrifice.

“…Caden?”

He coughed.

Liam, unable to even think of dodging, stared blankly upwards, receiving the blood flowing from above. A sharp sword blade, thrust from behind, protruded from Caden's left chest, as Caden had turned his body as if to embrace Liam Carter.

Meeting Liam's blank gaze, Caden forced a smile and opened his mouth.

“Are you... okay?”

Each time he spoke, black blood gushed from his throat and spilled from his lips. Caden, who had smiled as if to comfort the dazed Liam Carter, then collapsed.

Held in Liam Carter's arms, the strength drained from Caden's limp arms. Liam Carter raised a trembling hand and clutched Caden's clothes, only to feel a cold body whose heart had already stopped. Clutching that stiff body, Liam screamed silently.

Ah.

This terrible life.

Why did this person die for me? How much misfortune must this life bring me to be satisfied? Liam, trembling as he held Caden's body in his arms with empty eyes, slowly lowered his hand and grasped Caden's hand, which was still firmly clutching the sword.

“Just wait a little longer.”

Because I will bring your life back.

Without hesitation, Liam raised that hand and pierced his own chest with the sword.

The memory of that time made his head throb. Liam lightly rubbed his temples and narrowed his eyes. Back then, because he had been careless, he had missed a hidden member of the organization.
