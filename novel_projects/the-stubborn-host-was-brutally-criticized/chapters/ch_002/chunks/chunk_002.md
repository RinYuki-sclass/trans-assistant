I’m advising you, the next time you see me, hide—
A-Qing. A small female voice interrupted Sheng Qing’s remaining words. What are you doing?
Sheng Qing looked up, squinting, and saw a girl who looked as well-behaved as could be. She wore a white cotton dress that fell past her knees, her long hair draped over her shoulders. Her hands gripped the strap of her handbag as she stood five meters away, gazing timidly at the scene, not daring to step forward.
Sheng Qing thought the girl looked familiar.
Dear, she is the heroine of this book, Xia Li. Don’t hit her too. The System’s voice suddenly rang out.
Sheng Qing clicked his tongue. Why would I hit her? Mind your own business.
Fine, I’m meddling. But let me give you one last reminder: don’t scold her and don’t hit her, or you’re finished.
How am I finished? With that small frame of hers, she’s going to beat me? Sheng Qing didn’t care.
The System gave a snorting laugh. Host, don’t forget her other guardians. One of them is a boxer with muscles as hard as steel. It wouldn’t be a problem for him to kill you with one punch.
Sheng Qing had forgotten about that. The heroine had too many licking dogs around her. If she felt even a bit wronged, he was destined to die.
For the sake of his life, he decided not to actively seek death.
A-Qing. The girl plucked up the courage to take two steps forward, her eyes brimming with tears as she asked with a trembling voice, Did you hit someone?
Yeah. Sheng Qing tilted his chin slightly, asking with a bit of a roguish air, What about it?
Why did you hit him? Xia Li pursed her lips, her tears on the verge of falling, looking pitiful. The security uncle is a very good person. He often saves bottles for us. We can’t return kindness with ingratitude.
Return kindness with ingratitude?
A laugh escaped Sheng Qing’s nose. He stood up and said, He slapped my butt. Shouldn’t I hit him?
Xia Li froze, her voice soft. Was the uncle just joking with you? Besides, you’re both men. It shouldn’t matter if he slaps you once.
What do you mean it shouldn’t matter?
Sheng Qing almost rolled his eyes. He suppressed his temper and said to her, That is harassment and molestation. Didn’t your teachers ever teach you that?
Xia Li blinked, looking dazed and clearly not understanding.
