Chapter 2: Whose Butt Are You Slapping?

The streetlights on both sides flickered to life, casting a dim yellow glow over the dilapidated street and stretching the boy’s shadow very, very long.

Sheng Qing walked for an hour before finally reaching the entrance of the residential compound. He glanced at the name of the complex and was dumbfounded.

This broken-down place that looked like a shantytown was called Shengshi Haoting?! Which developer came up with that name? Does this compound even deserve to be called that?! Who are they trying to insult!

The security guard seemed to recognize him. Seeing him return, the man poked his head out of the window and greeted, Xiao Qing is back. How much did you make selling bottles today?

Sheng Qing shifted his feet, finding it hard to speak.

The guard chuckled, grabbed some empty bottles from the table, ran out, and stuffed them into Sheng Qing’s arms. He exhaled near Sheng Qing’s ear, I saved these specifically for you. Don’t say your big brother doesn’t look out for you.

The hot breath brushed against his ear, making Sheng Qing shudder. He hurriedly took a step back to distance himself. Thanks, thank you, Uncle.

Hey, don’t call me Uncle. You used to call me Brother. Dissatisfied with the distance between them, the guard leaned in, staring at Sheng Qing’s lips and swallowing hard. Why are you acting so distant all of a sudden?

The person in front of him was far too lecherous. Sheng Qing swore that if the man took one more step toward him, he would definitely make him regret it.

Xiao Qing, if you want bottles in the future, just come to me. The guard licked his lips, his hand itching as he slapped Sheng Qing’s butt. It was soft and bouncy; the feel was incredible. I’ll keep them all for you.

Go to hell! Sheng Qing grabbed the guard by the collar and punched him square in the face. Whose butt are you slapping?! My butt is precious; is it something you can just touch?!

The guard’s face was knocked aside, blood seeping from the corner of his mouth. He gave a dry laugh. Xiao Qing, I’m just joking. What’s wrong with you today? What’s the big deal about a slap on the butt?

He had done it plenty of times before. Was he in a bad mood today because he didn’t find enough bottles?

Joke my ass. I’m telling you right now, nobody touches my butt except me.

Sheng Qing’s face went cold. He clenched his fist and delivered two more heavy punches to the guard, not holding back at all.

The guard was knocked to the ground, curling his body up and howling in pain, though he didn’t forget to keep cursing under his breath.

Sheng Qing looked at his reddened knuckles, knelt on one knee beside the guard, and said coldly, If you don’t have a mirror at home, go piss and look at your own reflection to see what a beast you look like. If you dare lay a hand on me again, I’ll beat you so hard even your own mother won’t recognize you.

Fuck, did you eat gunpowder today! The guard covered his face, his voice squeezing out through the gaps in his teeth. You actually… hit me! Do you not want… to live?

Looking at the short, lecherous man on the ground who looked like a proboscis monkey, Sheng Qing felt so disgusted he wanted to vomit.

I’m advising you, the next time you see me, hide—

A-Qing. A small female voice interrupted Sheng Qing’s remaining words. What are you doing?

Sheng Qing looked up, squinting, and saw a girl who looked as well-behaved as could be. She wore a white cotton dress that fell past her knees, her long hair draped over her shoulders. Her hands gripped the strap of her handbag as she stood five meters away, gazing timidly at the scene, not daring to step forward.

Sheng Qing thought the girl looked familiar.

Dear, she is the heroine of this book, Xia Li. Don’t hit her too. The System’s voice suddenly rang out.

Sheng Qing clicked his tongue. Why would I hit her? Mind your own business.

Fine, I’m meddling. But let me give you one last reminder: don’t scold her and don’t hit her, or you’re finished.

How am I finished? With that small frame of hers, she’s going to beat me? Sheng Qing didn’t care.

The System gave a snorting laugh. Host, don’t forget her other guardians. One of them is a boxer with muscles as hard as steel. It wouldn’t be a problem for him to kill you with one punch.

Sheng Qing had forgotten about that. The heroine had too many licking dogs around her. If she felt even a bit wronged, he was destined to die.

For the sake of his life, he decided not to actively seek death.

A-Qing. The girl plucked up the courage to take two steps forward, her eyes brimming with tears as she asked with a trembling voice, Did you hit someone?

Yeah. Sheng Qing tilted his chin slightly, asking with a bit of a roguish air, What about it?

Why did you hit him? Xia Li pursed her lips, her tears on the verge of falling, looking pitiful. The security uncle is a very good person. He often saves bottles for us. We can’t return kindness with ingratitude.

Return kindness with ingratitude?

A laugh escaped Sheng Qing’s nose. He stood up and said, He slapped my butt. Shouldn’t I hit him?

Xia Li froze, her voice soft. Was the uncle just joking with you? Besides, you’re both men. It shouldn’t matter if he slaps you once.

What do you mean it shouldn’t matter?

Sheng Qing almost rolled his eyes. He suppressed his temper and said to her, That is harassment and molestation. Didn’t your teachers ever teach you that?

Xia Li blinked, looking dazed and clearly not understanding.

Fine, I’m going home now. Sheng Qing didn’t want to waste any more words with her; his only thought was to get home and eat.

Hearing this, the guard on the ground breathed a sigh of relief, crawled up stealthily, and slipped back into the security booth. Looking at the boy outside the window, his face darkened as an evil plan began to take root in his heart.

Xia Li stood frozen on the spot. It wasn’t until Sheng Qing’s back was almost out of sight that she hurriedly chased after him, saying softly, A-Qing, can you do me a favor?

What favor?

Hearing the response, a hint of surprise flashed in Xia Li’s eyes. Usually, as soon as she spoke, Sheng Qing would agree immediately. Why was today different?

What exactly do you want me to help you with? Sheng Qing stopped and turned to look at her, his tone laced with impatience. Speak up.

Xia Li tilted her small face up. When she saw his indifferent eyes, the rims of her eyes turned red. A-Qing, are you in a bad mood today?

Sheng Qing was stunned. Why did it feel like the heroine was about to cry? He clearly hadn’t done anything.

Summer cicadas chirped in the night, stars hung high in the sky, and the air was thick with a lingering, stifling heat. From time to time, lazy cries came from the bushes, sounding like cats.

By the time Sheng Qing rang the doorbell of the villa, he was still in a daze.

How did he end up agreeing to help Xia Li deliver test papers to a classmate?

Sheng Qing rubbed his face, blaming himself for being too soft-hearted. He couldn’t stand seeing a girl’s eyes turn red, especially a pretty one.

After waiting for less than two minutes, the villa door opened. A plainly dressed middle-aged woman walked quickly to the iron gate and asked politely, May I help you?

Sheng Qing waved the stack of papers in his hand and said, I’m here to deliver homework to Student Fu.

You must be the young master’s classmate. The woman opened the iron gate with a faint smile. Follow me.

Sheng Qing gave an oh and followed the woman into the villa. After changing his shoes, his eyes swept over the entire first floor, and his eyes widened. This was truly inhumanly luxurious.

The woman took all of this in and gestured with a smile, This way, please.

Sheng Qing moved his feet, his eyes darting from one thing to another, looking like a typical person who had never seen the world.

On the second floor, the woman pointed to one of the rooms and said, This is the young master’s room. You may go in.

With that, she turned and left without looking back.

Sheng Qing thought it was strange. He walked to the door and knocked. Is anyone there?

The next second, the door opened. Standing inside was an extremely tall boy, about 188cm. He was wrapped only in a bath towel, revealing a bare upper body. He had likely just finished showering, as his hair was still wet. His eyes, as black as ink, made one subconsciously nervous, even intimidated.

Ding-dong—Hello Host, this is the male lead of this book—Fu Nanyi, and he is also your target! The System announced mechanically, as if a switch had been flipped.
