Chapter 3: Forcing the Issue

Sheng Qing cursed the System in his heart. It hadn’t said a word earlier, and now that he was face-to-face with the male lead, what was the point of telling him now!

Who are you? Fu Nanyi asked, his voice raspy.

Sheng Qing forced a smile and held up the test papers in his hand. I’m a student from the class next door helping Xia Li deliver these papers to you.

A student from the class next door? Fu Nanyi lowered his gaze, staring intently at Sheng Qing’s face. The corners of his mouth curled into a smirk. How come I didn’t know there was such a good-looking student in the class next door?

Sheng Qing was stunned, his brain short-circuiting. Why was this male lead acting so flamboyant?

Come in. Fu Nanyi stepped aside.

Sheng Qing stood rooted to the spot. This male lead didn’t seem quite normal; he really didn’t dare to be alone in a room with him.

Do I need to invite you? Fu Nanyi’s voice was flat, making it impossible to read his mood.

Sheng Qing gritted his teeth and walked in. He was a guy, so he wouldn’t lose out either way. Even if they got into a fight, his odds of winning were high—after all, he had studied Sanda for three years.

Fu Nanyi closed the door and went to the wardrobe, casually picking out a black T-shirt and shorts. Put the papers on the desk.

Sheng Qing did as he was told. Turning around, he saw Fu Nanyi pull off his bath towel, revealing his bare body. He immediately spun back around and covered his eyes, cursing inwardly at the male lead for changing in front of people like a total pervert.

After Fu Nanyi finished dressing and saw this scene, he let out a scoff. We’re both guys, what are you pretending for?

Sheng Qing peeked through a gap in his fingers. Seeing that the other man was clothed, he lowered his hands and said stiffly, Even between men, one should be mindful.

Fu Nanyi thought he was being pretentious. He walked over and sat on the soft, large bed, propping himself up with his hands behind him as he leaned back slightly. He lifted his eyelids and said, Start then.

Start what?

Sheng Qing took a step back, his backside hitting the desk. His eyes were filled with disbelief. Surely he wasn’t expected to provide some kind of special service?

Seeing his lack of movement, Fu Nanyi frowned slightly. Didn’t Xia Li tell you what to do?

W-what to do? Sheng Qing was nervous, instinctively swallowing hard.

What do you think? Fu Nanyi clicked his tongue, stood up, and walked over. Looming over him, he asked, Do I really need to teach you?

The person in front of him wasn’t old, but his presence was incredibly oppressive. Sheng Qing felt his legs weakening but forced himself to stay upright. I… I’m not prepared yet.

Not prepared?

Fu Nanyi leaned down slightly, his hot breath brushing against Sheng Qing. They were so close that Sheng Qing could even see the fine peach fuzz on his face. What… what are you doing? You’re not going to force yourself on me, are you…

Hearing this, Fu Nanyi’s expression froze. He reached out a long arm, grabbed the test papers from the desk, and then stood up straight to maintain a safe distance. He looked at Sheng Qing with disgust. What kind of filth is filling your head?

Sheng Qing was dazed again. What did that mean?

Someone like you… Fu Nanyi swept his gaze up and down Sheng Qing’s body, his voice dripping with disdain. Even if you were given to me for free, I wouldn’t want you.

What do you mean by that! Sheng Qing got angry, mostly out of wounded pride. How dare this guy look down on him?

Back in the day, he had used this face to harvest the hearts of countless fangirls. No one who had ever seen him said he wasn’t good-looking.

Exactly what I said. Fu Nanyi glanced at the papers and casually tossed them at him, saying coldly, You’re here to write the papers, not to be a gigolo. Do you understand?

Write the papers?

Sheng Qing frowned. Xia Li had clearly told him he just needed to deliver the papers to a student named Fu; she hadn’t mentioned providing a ghostwriting service.

Did Xia Li lie to him?!

Do you not know how to do them? Seeing him clutching the papers without speaking for a long time, Fu Nanyi began to suspect he was mentally challenged. His patience exhausted, he said, If you can’t do it, get lost and send Xia Li over.

Taking the money and then finding an idiot to stall him just to annoy him—Fu Nanyi sneered. It seemed Xia Li was tired of living a peaceful life.

I can do them. Sheng Qing snapped back to his senses and nodded quickly. It wasn’t that he was afraid of getting Xia Li into trouble, but Fu Nanyi looked genuinely angry, and Sheng Qing feared he might not make it out of this room alive.

Then hurry up. Fu Nanyi gave the order. Don’t dawdle.

Fine, fine, fine.

Sheng Qing pulled out the chair, spread the papers on the desk, grabbed a pen, and immediately dove into the sea of questions.

The room instantly fell silent, save for the sound of the pen scratching against the paper.

Fu Nanyi pulled back the covers. Ignoring the fact that his hair was still wet, he lay directly on the large bed, pulled up the quilt, and closed his eyes to sleep.

Likely finding the light too bright, he commanded, Turn off the light.

Sheng Qing turned his head, searching for a while before spotting him on the bed. Hiring someone to do his homework while he slept comfortably—what a typical young master.

If the light is off, how am I supposed to write?

Fu Nanyi didn’t even open his eyes as he said dismissively, What does that have to do with me?

Sheng Qing glared at him and muttered under his breath, My eyes are so beautiful; if I go blind, can you afford the compensation?

When the light failed to dim, Fu Nanyi grew annoyed. Turn it off.

Extremely reluctantly, Sheng Qing stood up and switched off the light. The room plunged into total darkness. He swung his fists through the air several times in a silent, pathetic vent of frustration.

After a short while, the sound of steady breathing reached Sheng Qing’s ears. He rolled his eyes. This guy actually fell asleep!

Fortunately, there was a desk lamp on one corner of the table. Although the light wasn’t very bright, it was at least enough to illuminate the questions on the papers. It was better than nothing.

The difficulty of the papers was significant. Sheng Qing suddenly felt lucky that his high school grades hadn’t been bad; he had even gotten into a prestigious domestic university. However, it was after starting university that he began obsessively taking commissions, which led to the tragedy of his death from overwork.

He was only eighteen that year—truly a life cut short in its prime.

His beloved mother had cried until she fainted while clutching his photograph.

Sheng Qing regretted it immensely. His soul had lingered in the air, his obsession too deep to let him move on to the afterlife.

It was then that the System found him, saying that as long as he completed missions to obtain Resurrection Shards, he could return to the human world.

He had agreed immediately without a second thought.

There were four papers in total, covering Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, and Biology. Sheng Qing’s brain began to ache, but luckily the problem-solving process was relatively smooth. It took him a total of four and a half hours. When he finished, he stretched his back. His stomach was growling with hunger, and his eyes were so tired he could barely keep them open.

Fu Nanyi was sleeping soundly, not even having tossed or turned once. Sheng Qing almost thought he had died on the bed.

Hey, I’m finished. Sheng Qing tiptoed to the side of the bed and whispered, Time to settle the bill.

The person on the bed didn’t react, his thin lips pressed together. Who knew what kind of beautiful dream he was having.

Sheng Qing crouched down and admired his sleeping face for a moment. It was good to be young; his skin was smooth and delicate, and his facial features were sharply defined, like a masterpiece carved by a sculptor. His eyelashes were thick and long like small fans, and his nose was straight and high like a mountain peak. This face was handsome, but his mouth was just too unpleasant when he spoke.

Sheng Qing didn’t like it.

Your name is Fu Nanyi, right? He smiled mischievously. You’ll never have a girlfriend in the future.

What he would have was a boyfriend.

Sheng Qing was still holding the pen. Staring at that clean face, he suddenly felt the urge to create some art on it.

Just as the tip of the pen was less than five centimeters from the forehead, Fu Nanyi’s eyes snapped open. In the darkness, his gaze was incredibly deep, as if it could swallow all shadows.

What are you doing? His voice was thick with the rasp of someone just waking up. Murdering me for my money?
