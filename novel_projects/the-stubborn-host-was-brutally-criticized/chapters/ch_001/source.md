Chapter 1: Strategy to Win Over the Male Lead

Author Warning: This story contains themes of confinement, tracking devices, forced love… if you cannot accept these, please exit now.

The main text begins!

Coquettish moans filled the entire room. Sheng Qing had been hiding in the wardrobe for nearly ten minutes; if he didn’t get out soon, he was truly going to die.

Sheng Qing’s face was flushed red as he whispered urgently, System, get the hell out here!

Coming, dear.

Where the hell did you send me?! Sheng Qing gritted his teeth. Who are these two? The male and female leads?!

The System’s soul drifted out and immediately shrieked, Ahhh! I’m going to get sties in my eyes! Ying ying ying~

Sheng Qing nearly rolled his eyes out of his head. Just tell me, who exactly are the two people on the bed?

The female lead’s mother and the female lead’s mother’s lover.

The System’s voice was sweet, sounding like a girl in her late teens—delicate and soft, like a handful of sugary cotton candy.

After hearing this, Sheng Qing said in disbelief, You’re not making me strategy the female lead’s mother, are you?!

No, no way! I’m only eighteen, I’ve never even been in a relationship. I’m begging you, System, don’t make me strategy an auntie twenty years older than me. I’ll go crazy!!

The System giggled happily. The laughter drifted into Sheng Qing’s ears, making his heart pound uncontrollably against his ribs in a mix of shame and indignation. His heart was in his throat.

It won’t work, System. You’re so beautiful and kind-hearted, you definitely won’t make me strategy the female lead’s mother, Sheng Qing said pitifully.

The System was still laughing, nearly to the point of tears.

Stop laughing! Sheng Qing growled, irritably covering his ears.

The System rubbed its face, stifled its laughter, and struck a dramatic pose. I won’t. I’m not that heartless. Quickly now, thank me for my imperial grace.

Thank you, thank you! Thank you, Retired Emperor!

Hearing this, Sheng Qing breathed a sigh of relief, his hands dropping as he slumped completely limp inside the wardrobe.

What’s the rush~ you’re so annoying~ The woman’s eyes were like silk as she playfully punched the man’s chest. We have plenty of time. My daughter went to tutoring; it’s enough time for us to play~

The man caught her hand and slowly rubbed it. How do you want to play today? How about I tie you up with lace?

You’re a pervert. The woman said the words, but her eyes were filled with intense anticipation. So many tricks, I’m going to be played to death by you…

The man laughed lowly, tore away the fabric on the woman’s body, and pressed against her impatiently, kissing her wantonly.

Ahhh! So scandalous!! The System covered its eyes and screamed.

Sheng Qing’s head ached from the noise. He adjusted his sitting position to avoid seeing the couple outside doing it.

To be honest, he had never even watched a porno in his life. The impact of this scene was far too great; he was truly going to be traumatized.

System, since I’m not strategizing the female lead’s mother, I don’t need to stay in the wardrobe anymore, right? Sheng Qing said, sounding weary of life. Can you transport me to a normal place? If I stay here any longer, I really won’t be able to stop myself from rushing out to pick a fight with those two.

The System’s voice sounded slightly apologetic. Sorry about that. I’ve only been a System for three months, so my transport skills aren’t very practiced. Hang on, I’ll take you away now.

Good, good. Sheng Qing was so moved he almost shed tears.

A flash of white light passed.

The scenery around him began to shift. The moans in his ears finally vanished. Sheng Qing took a deep breath, but instead of the fresh air he expected, he smelled the stench of rotting swill.

Sheng Qing nearly gagged. Looking at his surroundings, he fell into despair.

How is this a trash heap!!!

Ahhh, System, I’m going to kill you!

Don’t be like that, I really didn’t do it on purpose. The System poked its fingers together. I’m still learning, please be patient with me.

Sheng Qing couldn’t bring himself to complain further. Pinching his nose, he finally remembered the main business. What is my mission for crossing into this place?

Congratulations to the Host for triggering a Small World Mission. The System became much more serious, speaking formally. Your mission is to strategy the male lead of this book. Once his Favorability Value toward you reaches one hundred, you can obtain Resurrection Shards and return to your original world!

Sheng Qing gave an oh of disdain. Just that? Simple~

Before he could gloat for more than a few seconds, he suddenly jolted, his eyes widening like copper bells. The male lead? I’m strategizing the male lead?!

You didn’t get it wrong, did you?! Shouldn’t it be the female lead?!

Dear, there’s no mistake. It’s the male lead.

System, I’m a man. A man! The male lead is also a man. A man!

Dear, I know.

Sheng Qing: System, have you gone mad?

The System rubbed its small hands and gave a cheeky laugh. Dear, I haven’t.

Let me ask you, is this a BL novel?

Of course not! It’s a romance novel with a male and female lead, the System replied.

Sheng Qing sneered. You know it’s a romance novel, so what the hell am I supposed to strategy?

The System went silent.

Sheng Qing couldn’t help but lose it. The male lead likes women. I’m a man. How am I supposed to strategy him?!

The System’s voice carried a hint of a smile. You’re better looking than the female lead; you can definitely do it. Go seduce the male lead.

The main reason she chose Sheng Qing was because he was good-looking, especially those peach blossom eyes that could hook a person’s soul. His pupils were a light amber color; if he cried, he would surely look beautiful!

Sluuurp~ If she hadn’t become a System, she would have made a move on such top-quality goods herself. How could she let the male lead of this book have him so cheaply?

Sheng Qing closed his eyes, suddenly wanting to die again.

Wait, he was already dead.

He regretted it. He would never stay up late playing games again. Staying up late really can cause sudden death. He would never call TV shows liars again.

No, his mother was still waiting for him. He had to complete the mission so he could go back as soon as possible!

System, tell me, who am I in this book?

The System snapped back to attention and hurriedly said, This book is called The Tycoon’s Palm-Sized Pet, the hottest romance novel of the last two years. You are the second male lead of this book—the female lead’s Flower Guardian.

Hearing Flower Guardian, Sheng Qing let out a mocking laugh. Why put it so elegantly? It’s just a Licking Dog.

You can interpret it that way too, the System said.

As the sky grew dark, Sheng Qing walked while absorbing the memories in the original body’s mind. The more he knew, the darker his face became.

The original body was also named Sheng Qing. He came from a poor family and entered a top high school along with the female lead. Once, while he was picking up bottles, the female lead gave him a large empty cooking oil bottle. The original body felt grateful toward her, thinking she was a truly good person, and developed a secret crush on her from then on.

This crush lasted for twelve years. During that time, he witnessed all the famous scenes of the male and female lead’s love-hate relationship.

Of course, he wasn’t the only one with a crush on the female lead. All told, there were four of them—all loyal Licking Dogs who would go through fire and water at a single word from her.

And the original body was the most, most, most, most pathetic Licking Dog among the four. He took a knife for the female lead, jumped off a building for her, donated a kidney for her, and smashed his head against walls for her.

In any case, if the female lead told him to go east, he wouldn’t dare go west. If she told him to eat shit, he wouldn’t even dare wait for it to cool down.

In the end, the female lead and male lead lived happily ever after, while the original body died unfortunately during the final kidney transplant surgery.

The female lead cried her eyes out, holding his corpse and saying, A-Qing, let’s be friends again in our next life.

Damn it, what an idiot, Sheng Qing kicked a pebble and cursed. Who wants to be your friend? He clearly wanted to be your boyfriend.

Since that’s the case, don’t blame me.

Sheng Qing gave a wicked smile, his eyes shining bright. If I can’t be your boyfriend, then I can only be your partner’s boyfriend.
