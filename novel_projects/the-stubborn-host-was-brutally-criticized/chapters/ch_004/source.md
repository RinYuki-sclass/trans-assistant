Chapter 4: The Landlord’s Silly Son

Sheng Qing blinked. He didn’t pull the pen back, and his mouth moved faster than his brain. Give me money.

How much? The boy closed his eyes again, clearly not taking him seriously.

Sheng Qing calculated the market price and said, Four exam papers. Charging you four hundred yuan isn’t excessive, right?

Not excessive. After saying that, he looked like he was about to fall back asleep.

Sheng Qing waited for a moment. Seeing that Fu Nanyi had no intention of getting up to give him the money, he pushed the boy’s arm through the quilt and urged, Give me the money.

The boy’s lips thinned into a straight line as he sat up, his face full of irritability. Go ask Xia Li for it. I settled the payment with her all at once. For a summer’s worth of homework, I gave her twenty thousand.

Twenty thousand?!

That much!

The way Sheng Qing looked at Fu Nanyi changed instantly. This was nothing but the pure, wealthy, silly son of a landlord.

At the same time, Sheng Qing was furious with Xia Li. She had actually lied to him, trying to use him as free labor without giving him a single cent, making him come all this way to deliver and write the exams.

When he got back, he would definitely question her fiercely and demand an explanation!

Leave once you’re finished. Fu Nanyi issued an order to see the guest out.

Sheng Qing raised his hand to check his watch. It was twelve-thirty. How was he supposed to get back? The last bus was at ten-thirty, and they had stopped running long ago. This was a villa district where it was impossible to hail a taxi. Was he supposed to walk back?

It’s twelve-thirty. Sheng Qing tried to keep his tone as calm as possible. The buses have stopped.

What does that have to do with me? He didn’t have a shred of human touch as he mocked, It took you this long to finish the papers? You’re incredibly dim.

Sheng Qing suppressed the fire in his heart and accepted his bad luck.

Don’t even think about me putting you up for the night. Fu Nanyi narrowed his eyes and tilted his chin slightly. The door is over there. Don’t let it hit you on the way out.

Sheng Qing gritted his teeth. Fine. Fine!

He threw down the pen and left without looking back, slamming the door extra hard to vent his frustration.

In the middle of the night, there wasn’t a soul on the streets. He walked for nearly an hour until his legs were sore and aching.

System, get out here!

Dear, I’m here. What’s wrong? The System let out a yawn, its tone soft and sluggish.

You actually need to sleep? Sheng Qing was surprised. Aren’t you just a string of code?

Dear, I am code, that’s true, but I’m also considered a person. I need to sleep, eat, rest, and have entertainment. It’s just that you can’t see me; I’m a virtual image.

I see. Sheng Qing suddenly asked, What is Fu Nanyi’s favorability value toward me right now?

Favorability Value: -10.

You didn’t see that wrong? -10? Sheng Qing didn’t believe it. How can it be a negative number?

The System laughed. Host, with that string of behaviors you just pulled, it would be hard for it not to be negative.

0615, please watch your choice of words, Sheng Qing said huffily.

Don’t call me 0615. I’m a person too, I just happened to die. The System was unhappy. How can a string of code represent me?!

Sheng Qing thought that made sense. Got it.

Host, the male lead is not easy to win over. His sexual orientation is still female, so turning him toward men is a bit difficult. You need to work harder. While the male lead doesn’t have feelings for the female lead yet, hurry up and butt in!

When did Fu Nanyi fall in love with Xia Li? Sheng Qing asked thoughtfully.

The System checked. In the book, it was at a party after the Gaokao. The female lead confessed while drunk, the male lead slept with her, and then they were together.

They were together just because they slept together? Sheng Qing’s mouth hung wide open.

City people really know how to play.

The book didn’t specify when he fell in love. I’ve read this book no less than five times, and the male lead never once said the word ‘love’ to the female lead from beginning to end.

Scumbag! Sheng Qing spat in disdain. Together for so long and he wouldn’t even say he liked her? What a piece of work!

Exactly, exactly! The System expressed its agreement.

After cursing the male lead for a while, Sheng Qing finally remembered the main business. System, hurry up and send me home. I’m about to die of exhaustion and sleepiness.

The System remained silent for a long while.

Sheng Qing thought it had fallen asleep and hurriedly called out, System! System! Hurry! It’s the middle of the night; it’s too unsafe for a boy like me.

The System stopped playing deaf and mute. Sorry, Host. The teleportation function can only be used once the male lead’s favorability value reaches 70.

What?! 70! Sheng Qing’s voice suddenly spiked in pitch. You’re not joking, right?

Sheng Qing clutched his hair and shouted at the starry sky, Argh!!! Everyone just go die! Fu Nanyi, you big stupid dog! I hope you’re impotent for the rest of your life!!!

The System didn’t speak; more accurately, it didn’t dare to speak.

By the time he reached home, it was already two in the morning.

Relying on the original owner’s memories, Sheng Qing fumbled through the dark to his room. Without changing his clothes or washing up, he collapsed onto the bed and fell into a deep, dreamless sleep.

He woke up the next day at one-thirty in the afternoon.

If he hadn’t been woken up by the urge to pee, Sheng Qing would have wanted to sleep a bit longer.

With a head of messy hair, he finished his business and washed his face. Looking at himself in the mirror, he curled his lips into a smile. Still as handsome as ever!

After washing up, Sheng Qing shuffled to the kitchen in his slippers. He opened the refrigerator door and nearly dropped his jaw—it was empty, unnervingly clean.

Sheng Qing slammed the fridge shut.

Ten minutes later, Sheng Qing was sitting in a noodle shop downstairs. A bowl of steaming plain noodles sat before him. Perhaps because he hadn’t eaten in so long, he finished the bowl in less than five minutes, even drinking every drop of the soup.

The shop had air conditioning, so Sheng Qing lingered, enjoying the cool air for a while. If the lady boss’s gaze hadn’t been so unfriendly and full of disdain, he would have had the thick skin to stay a bit longer.

The sun was scorching, and the air seemed stagnant without a hint of a breeze. The oncoming heatwaves made it feel as if the very air was burning.

Sheng Qing raised a hand to shield his eyes, squinting as he cursed, Hellish weather.

A-Qing. A sweet female voice came from behind him.

Sheng Qing turned around. Xia Li.

The girl gave a sweet smile, her cheeks flushed red. Sweat competed to squeeze out from her forehead. She was carrying a burlap sack filled with plastic bottles. I collected so many bottles today. Let’s go sell them together.

Sheng Qing froze. Looking at her clear, bright black eyes, he agreed.

A full sack of bottles only sold for ten yuan in the end.

Xia Li clutched the ten yuan and said beamingly, A-Qing, let me buy you a popsicle.

Sheng Qing paused. No need.

No, I have to. Xia Li insisted. You helped me deliver the papers yesterday; I haven’t had the chance to thank you yet.

Mentioning the delivery made Sheng Qing a bit angry. Xia Li, yesterday you only said to help you deliver the papers. You didn’t say I had to help you write them? Four papers! Four whole papers!

Because of that, he missed the last bus last night and had to walk for a full hour and a half to get home.

I’m sorry. Xia Li’s voice was low. I forgot to write them. A-Qing, please forgive me this once, okay?

Sheng Qing didn’t press the matter further, only saying, Don’t let it happen again.

Xia Li nodded and tugged at the hem of his shirt. I’ll buy you a popsicle.

Sheng Qing raised an eyebrow. Since she was treating, he certainly wouldn’t be polite. He would definitely pick the most expensive one to give her wallet a real sting!
