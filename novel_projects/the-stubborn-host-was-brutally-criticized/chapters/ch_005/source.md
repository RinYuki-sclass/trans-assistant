Chapter 5: Pervert

Little Pudding?! Sheng Qing did not take it.

Xia Li held it out again, her eyes filled with confusion. A-Qing, dont you like this the most?

Sheng Qing endured it for a long while before finally asking, Xia Li, you did Fu Nanyis summer homework and he gave you twenty thousand. Have you spent all twenty thousand already?

Xia Lis expression changed slightly as she fell into silence.

Sheng Qing frowned. All of it?

Logically, it didnt make sense. Twenty thousand was not a small sum. Judging by how Xia Li was willing to collect and sell bottles in this heat, it was clear she wasnt someone who spent extravagantly or lacked thriftiness.

A-Qing, the money was taken by my father for gambling.

Xia Li lowered her eyes, her voice very soft.

Sheng Qing was stunned. He took all of it?

Xia Li nodded. Yes.

Sheng Qing rubbed his forehead speechlessly. Was the author of this book an idiot?! They just had to write the female lead to be this pitiful and miserable! What a combination: a gambling father, a cheating mother, a forced-love boyfriend, and a broken girl!

Ahhh!!! Sheng Qing scratched his hair, wanting to send razor blades to the author!

A-Qing, if you dont like Little Puddings anymore, Ill buy you something else. Xia Li lowered her hand and turned to walk toward the canteen.

Hey, hey, hey! Sheng Qing hurriedly stepped forward to stop her, snatched the Little Pudding from her hand, tore open the packaging, and took a large bite. He said indistinctly, I like eating Little Puddings.

Xia Lis eyelashes trembled slightly. She stared at Sheng Qing for a long time before finally revealing a faint smile.

At the end of August, No. 1 High School opened for the term.

Sheng Qing stood at the entrance of No. 1 High School, squinting at the luxury cars filling two lanes, unable to help but frown.

Had he taken the wrong turn and accidentally arrived at a noble school?

Perhaps because he had been standing there for too long, the System couldnt help but come out and ask, Host, why arent you going in?

Sheng Qings gaze remained on the luxury cars as he spoke the truth. I dont dare to go in.

System, do you see the luxury cars parked outside? Sheng Qing asked.

The System took a glance; they were all cars it didnt recognize. I see them.

System, tell me honestly, are the students who come to No. 1 High School all extremely rich?

The System understood and said with a smile, Yes, the students here are either wealthy or noble. Their families all have some background.

So that means I’m the only poor ghost in No. 1 High School? Sheng Qings tone rose significantly.

The System hurriedly comforted him, Xia Li is here with you. You arent alone, dont be sad.

Sheng Qing thanked it expressionlessly. My thanks to you.

Sheng Qing processed this for a moment. Just as he regained his senses and was about to enter the school gate, a cry of pain suddenly came from behind him.

He turned around to see a boy lying on the ground, unable to get up.

What was the situation? What happened?

Sheng Qing quickly moved to the side, terrified that the person on the ground would crawl over in some dark, twisted way to grab his leg and frame him for money!

Get up. Werent you cursing at me quite happily just now? A youth stepped out from the crowd. He curled his lips, looking down at the person on the ground, and said lazily, Keep cursing. Why did you stop?

Huang Xing remained silent. He stood up and glared fiercely at Ling Yu, his eyes full of resentment.

Ling Yu clicked his tongue. What? Still want to curse?

Seeing more and more people gathering around, Huang Xing felt he was losing face. He dropped a line—I’ll see how much longer you can stay arrogant—and then ran away without looking back.

Ling Yu picked up the schoolbag from the ground, patted off the dust, and watched Huang Xings retreating back while muttering, Coward.

Sheng Qing witnessed the whole process and secretly asked the System who this handsome guy was.

Dear, this is the male leads rival, Ling Yu. He is a total smiling tiger. Do not be deceived by his appearance; he has more than a few dirty tricks up his sleeve.

Sheng Qing gave an oh and looked up, only to lock eyes with Ling Yu by pure coincidence.

Hiss… he looked a bit familiar.

Ling Yu slung his bag over his shoulder and strode toward Sheng Qing. He hooked an arm around Sheng Qings shoulder and said with a smile, Long time no see, deskmate.

Deskmate?!

Host, I forgot to tell you, you and he are deskmates. Your relationship is actually quite good, the System said before disappearing.

Sheng Qing was stunned. He looked up into those smiling peach blossom eyes and forced himself to say, Long time no see…

It really has been a long time. Ling Yu lowered his gaze, the hand on Sheng Qings shoulder lightly tilting his chin up as he asked, Did you miss me?

Sheng Qing was shocked. He broke free from the hand, his head buzzing. What on earth? Is this guy gay?

Ling Yu laughed. He put his hands in his pockets and looked at Sheng Qings startled expression, feeling in a quite good mood.

Qingqing, why are you still so bad at taking a joke?

Qingqing? Sheng Qing felt goosebumps rising.

Judging by Ling Yus tone, he must frequently tease him like this. Ahhh, this is driving me crazy.

Why is no one in this world normal?

All morning, Sheng Qing was in a state of distraction. Ling Yu found it strange and, as a good deskmate, asked a few caring questions. But Sheng Qing acted as if he couldnt hear, staring blankly straight ahead without saying a word.

Seeing this, Ling Yu tactfully shut his mouth and went back to playing games on his phone.

The bell rang, signaling the end of the last class. The teacher did not delay the lesson. The students of Class 1 closed their books and unhurriedly grabbed their companions to go out for lunch.

Ling Yu had played games all morning, and his neck was incredibly sore. He rubbed it while asking the person beside him, Going to the cafeteria for lunch?

Sheng Qing finally snapped out of it and declined, Im not hungry. You go ahead.

Ling Yu gave an oh and patted his shoulder. Let me out.

Sheng Qing flinched and instinctively slapped his hand away. Seeing Ling Yus slightly displeased expression, he hurriedly moved aside and explained, Im not used to people touching me.

Ling Yu took two steps to stand in front of him, lowering his head slightly with a half-smile. Since when did you have this problem?

Sheng Qing said nothing. Ling Yu found it boring and left.

Seeing him leave, Sheng Qing breathed a sigh of relief and dragged his exhausted body to the restroom.

Sheng Qing turned on the faucet and splashed water on his face. Looking at his pale reflection in the mirror, he felt angry for no reason.

He slapped his cheeks hard, trying to wake himself up.

Being that ruthless to yourself? a male voice drifted over.

Sheng Qing stiffened. He turned his head and saw the strategy target, Fu Nanyi.

Fu Nanyi glanced at the palm prints on his face, raised an eyebrow slightly, and then turned on the faucet to leisurely wash his hands.

Sheng Qing blinked and asked, Why are you here?

Fu Nanyi paused, turned off the faucet, and looked at him like he was an idiot. What do you think?

Oh right, this was the restroom. Fu Nanyi was definitely here to pee. Thinking of this, Sheng Qings gaze drifted downward, his eyes staring straight at Fu Nanyis lower body.

Just like a pervert.

Fu Nanyi noticed his gaze but didnt look away. He let out a sneer. What are you looking at?

Sheng Qing retracted his gaze, the tips of his ears turning pink as he stammered, No… nothing…

Fu Nanyi pressed closer, a wicked smile hanging on his lips. If its nothing, then why are you acting guilty?

Whos acting guilty? Im not! Sheng Qing shook his head. The water droplets on the tip of his nose slowly dripped down with his movement, hitting the floor.

Fu Nanyi narrowed his eyes, scrutinizing his face, and said meaningfully, It’s really a pity you aren’t working as a gigolo.

What did that mean?

Was he praising him? Or insulting him?

Fu Nanyis gaze moved down, landing on his chest. Sheng Qing took a large step back and wrapped his arms around himself.

What are you doing? he blurted out in panic. Are you some kind of pervert!
