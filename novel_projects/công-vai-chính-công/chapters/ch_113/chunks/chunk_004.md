Bai Han, who worked under Chi Zhuo, was a talented individual. Ji Chenxi also didn’t forget his own labs—since he had free time anyway, he might as well study how to break forbidden drugs and extend the lifespan of modified semi-experimental subjects.
He scheduled the data for the research institute to be sent a month later. After ordering his troops to maintain the illusion that they were still attacking Cocoon, the commander of the army simply ran off with his alpha.
Rose Planet was just as Ji Chenxi had imagined—mild weather, fragrant flowers. They booked a small villa with a large rose garden and released their spirit forms to play on their own.
The snow leopard, having spent years rolling around in the spiritual domain crushing ice roses, naturally wasn’t afraid of ordinary roses and went off chasing butterflies with the black mamba.
Meanwhile, Ji Chenxi and Chi Zhuo enjoyed the local hot springs and rose-themed afternoon tea before finally starting their sightseeing.
Boating, picking flowers, making rose tea, cooking rose dishes—Ji Chenxi found it surprisingly relaxing to do slow, simple things under warm sunlight.
After half a month, it seemed like tourist season had arrived, and the rose garden launched a “Flower King” blind box game.
Though called a blind box game, the top three prizes were already fixed—various rose-themed ornaments and plushies. Not particularly expensive, but rare and highly sought after. This year’s theme featured feline beasts and roses, with cute cat-and-rose badges and ornaments that instantly attracted tourists. Almost everyone who came to Rose Planet wanted to try it.
Ji Chenxi found it interesting—not the prizes, but the game itself.
It looked like piloting a starship to compete for flowers, but in reality, the “starship” was a transparent ball over a meter tall. Two people had to get inside and control it together. These balls could bump others away or get bumped themselves. To put it bluntly—it was bumper ball racing for flowers.
And the space inside the ball was tight, meaning couples playing together would inevitably end up pressed close.
Damn, such scheming—he likes it!
But it was a bit childish. Definitely not really something a mature adult like him should play.
Ji Chenxi turned to look at Chi Zhuo, ready to move on with their plan for the day—only to see Chi Zhuo staring at the top prizes.
The prizes weren’t just ornaments and plushies, but medals shaped like snow leopards, tigers, lions, black panthers, and other big cats, all decorated with rose elements. The craftsmanship was exquisite. Chi Zhuo was looking at a snow leopard medal in particular.
It even had diamonds embedded in it—shiny and beautiful.
Ji Chenxi paused, then immediately dragged Chi Zhuo over to register.
“You want to play this?” Chi Zhuo asked, a little surprised.
“What? Brother Chi Zhuo thinks it’s childish? It looks really fun. Come on, play with me~”
With Ji Chenxi’s coaxing, they quickly signed up and entered the bumper ball.
