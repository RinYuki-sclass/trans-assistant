They hesitated, but didn’t dare disobey.
“Your Highness came in to find something.”
Ji Chenxi nodded. “I really can’t hide anything from you. I am here to find something.”
At this point, since they were already here, the fastest way would have been to eliminate everything left behind and seal the place off. The fact that Ji Chenxi insisted on going deeper meant he was after data.
“In fact, this data involves you too. I just want to confirm if it’s real.”
Ji Chenxi gave a soft “mm.” “Over thirty years ago, there was a lab that created a 3S alpha experimental subject. But the experiment ended in failure—the subject went out of control and killed everyone in the lab. So I was wondering… what happened to that runaway subject in the end? And where did the omega researcher who was pregnant with his child go? Did they all die, or did they escape?”
Chi Zhuo frowned. He wasn’t foolish, and many things were already connecting in his mind.
SSS and SS-level individuals weren’t that common. If they were, there’d be no need to hide them.
The head of the Chi family knew Chi Zhuo’s spiritual strength. After all, he had been tested when returning to the family. That complicated gaze at the time, and the way the matter was forcefully suppressed afterward, all told Chi Zhuo one thing—an SSS level appearing was abnormal, especially when his mother was only an inferior omega.
Chi Zhuo had long suspected that woman might not be his biological mother. But no matter how he investigated, nothing ever came up. And genetically, he wa* the Chi family head’s bloodline. A case with no flaws… eventually had to be dropped.
He didn’t expect it to come up again today.
“Arbriana. The youngest genius researcher mentioned in that log. If she wanted to alter someone’s genetic sequence, it wouldn’t be difficult. The real question is—how did she make sure you could withstand that power?”
“So Your Highness is curious.”
“Not really. I don’t have that much curiosity about everything. I just think you might be that child, so I paid a little more attention. But honestly, no matter what your real identity is… you’re still my alpha.”
After all, people created from drugs and experiments rarely lived long. What Ji Chenxi was really worried about was whether Chi Zhuo had hidden dangers in his body—especially since he had previously been exposed to forbidden drugs. And those drugs… were from Chen Xing. There was no way they didn’t have research records.
“Ji Chenxi.”
Hearing his name suddenly, Ji Chenxi paused slightly and looked at Chi Zhuo with mild confusion.
Then he was pulled into a kiss.
The cool touch on his lips made Ji Chenxi instinctively touch Chi Zhuo. When they finally separated, he smiled. “Alright, I get it now. You just wanted to kiss me.”
The rest of the work went quickly. Ji Chenxi had already broken the machine systems of Chen Xing earlier, and after retrieving all research data, he packed everything and handed it over to Chi Zhuo.
