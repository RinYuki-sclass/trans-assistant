Ji Chenxi stared at the two slots in silence for a long moment, then looked back at the travel map again. In the end, he chose a yellow-marked destination.
“We’ll go here.”
It was a planet famous for roses—also known as Rose Planet. When it was first developed, it was extremely popular with tourists. But over time, a whole planet full of red roses wasn’t so rare anymore. Plus, since everything on the planet was roses, food had to be imported, making the cost of living extremely high—comparable to a first-tier star. Gradually, it went from a hotspot to a place only couples went to when they couldn’t think of anywhere else.
Chi Zhuo looked slightly surprised. “Are you sure? I thought you’d prefer somewhere newer or more spectacular.”
Ji Chenxi had grown up in a rose manor himself. Even if he liked roses, he wouldn’t treat them as anything particularly romantic like others did. So… was he choosing this place because Chi Zhuo liked it?
Chi Zhuo said, “You can pick somewhere you like.”
Ji Chenxi opened Chi Zhuo’s guide for Rose Planet, searched a bit on his lightbrain, and quickly expanded the plan. He even booked their accommodation in advance.
While doing all that, he answered, “I like it. Since it’s a trip for the two of us, it should be somewhere we both like. It doesn’t need to be too exciting or dramatic. Just walking around together and basking in the sun is already relaxing enough.”
After finishing everything, Ji Chenxi finally looked up at Chi Zhuo with a smile. “If Brother Chi Zhuo has other ideas, you can tell me.”
“So this is you compromising for me.”
“Yeah, yeah. Rare chance, don’t miss it.”
Chi Zhuo couldn’t help but laugh, and in the end, he didn’t object.
The Cocoon Organization was currently leaderless. If not for the fact that most of its members were modified humans and machines, it would have already fallen apart. In truth, even leaving it alone wouldn’t have been a problem—but Ji Chenxi was worried there might still be hidden experts inside, or that some research genius could take control.
Cutting grass without removing the roots only lets it grow back in spring. Better to wipe it out completely from the start.
And things turned out exactly as he expected—the organization still had backup measures.
The defense system alone was full of traps. Breaking into the base felt like attacking an impregnable fortress. For a moment, Ji Chenxi even wondered if Chen Xing had already prepared for him not coming back.
The gate’s defense didn’t hold him for long. After precise calculations, he finally cracked the system that would trigger attack mode if the wrong password was entered.
As the doors opened, a massive laboratory-like base lit up with harsh white lights. The people who came with him immediately formed a protective circle around him, afraid that the moment the door opened, lasers or artillery would fire out.
Ji Chenxi didn’t rush in. Instead, he first ran a full scan of the interior. From the start, he’d already sensed this lab couldn’t just be shut down directly—indeed, several rooms contained toxic gas and unstable modified humans. With strong spiritual power and scanning equipment working together, he pushed aside his guards and entered first.
Chi Zhuo followed closely behind. The others were about to move too, but Ji Chenxi raised a hand, signaling them to wait outside.
