The starship flew at high speed through space. Ji Chenxi leaned back in his seat, idly fiddling with his lightbrain.
“How is it?” Chi Zhuo asked as he brought a small piece of cake to Ji Chenxi’s lips.
Ji Chenxi ate it without hesitation, his hands still moving slow and steady as before. “Pretty strong defense system. I couldn’t break through it from long range.”
“If it’s too troublesome, we can take it slow and attack it bit by bit.”
Ji Chenxi shook his head. “I want a quick fight, a quick finish. The hardest part is probably locating it and opening the defense system. I tried taking control of all the mechanical lifeforms in that base—there was a response, but I couldn’t truly break through.”
He frowned slightly as he spoke. “Is it because of the defense system… or did those machines actually change their internal structure?”
The reason Ji Chenxi chose to handle the Cocoon Organization himself this time was threefold: the organization itself was a hidden danger, second was letting those people take advantage of the time to set up the wedding arrangements, and third—using this as an excuse to go out traveling was his real goal.
So this trip really had to be finished quickly.
“The Prince deliberately left Wu Xiang and the others behind this time. Is it so he can use this absence to carry out another purge on the noble families and powerful officials? If we go back too early, those people might not even have started acting yet.”
Ji Chenxi had been thinking, but paused slightly when he heard that. Then he laughed. “So you think I’m the one who’s in a hurry to go back?”
Chi Zhuo wiped off a bit of cream from the corner of Ji Chenxi’s mouth. “Aren’t you?”
There really were people who could see things so clearly, yet still insist on asking out loud. Ji Chenxi raised a brow and smiled. “No. I am in a hurry to go back. Best case, I want everything wrapped up in three to five days.”
“So urgent—what are you rushing back for?”
“To feed my cat.”
Chi Zhuo chuckled and opened his lightbrain. A holographic screen instantly appeared in front of Ji Chenxi. It showed all the tourist spots suitable for travel around the Morning Star base expansion area. Each location was marked on the map, with one or two small pictures and a few lines of description. The markings were divided into four colors—red, orange, yellow, and green—with red being the rarest and green the most common.
Ji Chenxi casually clicked on one red and one green location. As expected, the red one was more to his taste, and even the guides and descriptions were much more detailed.
The man who had just said he was in a hurry to feed his cat turned back to Chi Zhuo with a pure, harmless expression full of anticipation. “How many places can we go to~?”
After showing Ji Chenxi that, Chi Zhuo pulled up another option.
This time, Ji Chenxi’s eyes lit up—it was the interstellar exploration team he had once fought hard to get into but never managed to join.
“This one lasts three months, it’s a bit long, but Your Highness can consider it.”
