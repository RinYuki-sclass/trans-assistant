Even though Ji Chenxi had already destroyed quite a few by now, before this it had all been forced by the situation. Every single recorder had been “accidentally” shattered only because those people dodged too fast.
“I’m about to leave. If you’ve got anything you want to say, say it now.”
Chi Zhuo thought it over for a moment, then asked, “Do you want me to hold back in the next assessment?”
Under normal circumstances, Chi Zhuo would never ask something like this. Not taking an opponent seriously was a form of disrespect. But now there were recorders flying all over the place, capturing every candidate’s performance. If Ji Chenxi showed too much of his real strength, it would be easy to draw the attention of his older brother and second sister again. So he was bound to hide his power—but even hiding had its limits. Standing out too much was bad, yet being too average wasn’t good either.
What Ji Chenxi needed to do was match that S+ candidate, or be just a bit better. And on top of that, he still had to keep some strength in reserve, not letting his true power slip. Something that should’ve been simple suddenly became tricky.
Chi Zhuo coming here was largely just for fun. How he performed didn’t really matter to him, which made him the perfect tool for Ji Chenxi to make a splash.
“Hold back?” Ji Chenxi repeated, then smiled and shook his head.
“We’re about to part ways, and that’s what you want to say to me? Compared to you holding back, I’d much rather have a proper fight with you.”
“Your Highness, to the outside world, we’re both S-rank,” Chi Zhuo reminded him.
“Isn’t that even better?” Ji Chenxi said lightly. “I’m actually really curious—if we’re both S-rank, who would come out on top?”
Truth be told, Ji Chenxi had been wondering about this for a long time. If they were both S-rank, who would really be stronger in a head-on fight?
For now, purely in terms of combat power, SSS-rank Chi Zhuo definitely had the edge. If it weren’t for the fact that Chi Zhuo’s pheromones had gone out of control back then because of the drug, Ji Chenxi would never have been able to plant his mark on Chi Zhuo’s gland. And after that, there wouldn’t have been things tying Chi Zhuo down every time, or situations where Chi Zhuo even ended up at a disadvantage.
Because that win had been, frankly, ‘not very honorable’, Ji Chenxi had always wanted to beat Chi Zhuo properly, fair and square, at least once.
“Your Highness really has a strong desire to win,” Chi Zhuo said, clearly understanding what he meant.
Ji Chenxi let out a soft “hm,” a casual sound to show he was listening.
“But I’m more than happy to play along.”
The flight recorder Chi Zhou had picked up with his spiritual sense was already getting closer. Ji Chenxi answered with a short sound of agreement, then his figure flashed like a hunting leopard as he slipped into the grass.
Watching Ji Chenxi’s retreating back, Chi Zhuo stood there for a long while, until he could no longer catch even a trace of his presence. Only then did he turn and leave.
The flight recorder lingered beside Chi Zhuo as well, hovering for some time. After he finally moved, it followed him from a distance.
In a time when most alphas were only C-rank, an A-rank alpha was already considered outstanding, let alone an S-rank alpha like Chi Zhuo, who had been out of school for years. Back when he was a student, his many feats could be called low-key—or not low-key at all, depending on how you looked at them. Because of his past performances in large-scale trial grounds, the school’s higher-ups had kept a close eye on him. Now, naturally, they were curious just how far his strength had grown after all these years.
