His brows tightened. He backed up fast to avoid the next blow; the pretty dagger in his hand flipped and managed to block a strangely shaped weapon. Ji Chenxi glanced down — that weapon was nasty. If it gouged you, you’d bleed out. He never expected someone with a lion spiritual beast to use such a sneaky, vicious tool.
The dagger rang with a boom from the sudden heavy impact; even Ji Chenxi’s palm went a little numb from the shock.
The first move failed, and the man with the creepy smile attacked again. Ji Chenxi calmly met the strike with his dagger while still retreating, and the lion next to him seized the moment to leap once more.
In front was the young man — grinning like a madman and lightning fast, hard to fight — behind was the charging lion. A trace of pleasure finally showed on the young man’s lips; even if they couldn’t kill Ji Chenxi this round, they could at least badly wound him.
But Ji Chenxi was like a cat — even under that tight, no-escape pincer, he slipped away as if he’d melted into water.
He pushed off lightly and sprang through the air, staring at the strand of hair the wind had scattered. His face darkened.
The young man chuckled, “Long hair looks great — and it’s fun to cut.”
Ji Chenxi gave a cold, short “hm.” “Growing it out’s troublesome,” he said.
“Third Prince, Your Highness — care to share your tips with me?” The young man, seeing how many times Ji Chenxi had dodged, still wanted to trade words.
Ji Chenxi toyed with his dagger with one hand and brushed his smooth, waterfall-like hair with the other. “You keep slicing like that and my hair’s going to look awful.”
“So?” the other pushed.
“You gotta pay a price, right? My hair isn’t something just anyone can touch.”
A faint amusement flickered in the young man’s eyes. Oh — so this was the empire’s little rose.
“Third Prince, don’t worry. I’ll make your death a pretty one.”
“Big ego.” Ji Chenxi shrugged it off, voice casual, almost haughty, like the man in front of him didn’t even matter.
Then, in the next instant, the slight smile at the young man’s lips vanished — Ji Chenxi, the one who had been dodging the whole time suddenly struck first. Fast. Too fast.
There was none of the previous stiffness in his movements. Ji Chenxi’s injuries, which the others had assumed would slow him down, were completely undetectable. In his original guess, Ji Chenxi should have been limping, or else he wouldn’t need to cooperate with the Mamba. But now, there was no hint of weakness in his steps.
His relentless assault left streaks of blood on his dagger. A flick of his fingers sent a silent message: that’s all you’ve got?
Ji Chenxi hadn’t even said a word, yet the young man’s anger flared. It was time to get serious.
The battlefield had devolved into chaos — there was no chance for one-on-one fights. Seeing that the young man still hadn’t captured Ji Chenxi, Scorpion muttered about incompetence, while sending a few men to search for Chi Zhuo. At the same time, he ordered the others to increase firepower and deploy heavy weapons.
