Heavy weapons drained energy and caused massive noise — which is why they hadn’t used them before. But now, time was short. His informants had already reported that the target had been found and was arriving soon.
The moment this message came through, all other communications with the outside instantly vanished.
Scorpion sneered. They were prepared: even if caught, no one could reveal the other side.
“Open fire,” he commanded.
The operator, aiming the heavy weapon, blinked sharply. “Captain… Vice-Captain Ge is over there too.”
Scorpion’s face darkened. The Red Scorpion Star Thieves had only just arrived, and they were already ignoring his orders.
“I said, open fire.”
Ji Chenxi, the young man, and his lion had been locked in fierce combat, paying little mind to the photon blasts aimed at them. But in the middle of one brutal exchange, Ji Chenxi suddenly broke off, eyes widening slightly.
Crazy — they were using heavy weapons in this environment.
They intended to blow both of them to bits.
The young man recognized the danger too, his face grim. He quickly pulled back alongside Ji Chenxi.
Ji Chenxi chose the route with the best advantage. The young man did the same. Amid the deafening roar of the heavy weapons, the two enemies collided on the narrow path.
Ji Chenxi ignored him and melted back into the shadows. Now wasn’t the time to keep tangling with the young man — first, he had to deal with those idiots.
At the same time, the young man was thinking along the same lines.
But while Ji Chenxi moved with his usual agility to reach Scorpion and successfully subdued the S-class old man, the young man already had the heavy weapon aimed at him.
Ji Chenxi frowned. With the weapon pointed at him, it was hard to press the dagger any further.
“Looks like I’m still one step ahead of His Highness, the Third Prince,” the young man said with a smirk.
“Not necessarily,” came a lazy, indifferent voice.
The tall, long-haired beauty stood casually, plunging a dagger in without hesitation. Blood sprayed, yet the one behind the action remained utterly relaxed, speaking lazily in the young man’s direction: “You’re late, Chi Zhuo.”
