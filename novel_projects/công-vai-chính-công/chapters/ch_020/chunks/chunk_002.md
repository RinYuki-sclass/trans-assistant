“Because of you, we’ve wasted a lot of time. If we fail to capture these two, you know what happens next,” a sinister threat hissed.
It seemed to have little effect. The young man remained smiling, “Captain, a bit of rest actually helps us track the snake and the little kitten better. Otherwise, with the cold up ahead, we’d probably freeze half our own men before even finding them.”
“Vice-Captain Ge, are you trying to teach me how to do my job?” Scorpion pressed a hidden button.
“Not at all,” the young man said, his neck emitting a faint smell of scorched skin. Yet he didn’t even frown. Instead, he calmly said, “Here we go.”
“Here we go?”
A grin finally tugged at Ji Chenxi’s lips. Indeed, it was here.
The prepared spiritual attack struck suddenly. Blood sprayed everywhere.
Many who hadn’t reacted in time were killed instantly. Only those with some skill managed to survive the sudden onslaught.
Everyone tensed, eyes darting around, cautious and alert.
Ji Chenxi moved again, his spiritual threads snaking through the air. Thin as hair, they cut down several heads in an instant. He quickly withdrew. Using spiritual threads to kill was smooth and efficient. Panic among the enemies could lead to even more unexpected gains—but it also risked revealing his location.
This ghostlike killing method had shocked the star thieves. They thought some unholy force was at work. But before they could panic and flail, the young man shouted sharply, “Don’t move!”
Scorpion’s face darkened. In less than a minute, they had lost nearly half their men.
“Spiritual threads,” someone muttered.
By the time Scorpion said this, the young man had already launched his attack. A few rounds of special photon strikes followed the blood trails. When he fired, the enemy would have already backed off, so the photon blasts weren’t meant to hit—they masked his next move.
Ji Chenxi, drifting and retreating casually, barely noticed the photon blasts—until a massive lion leapt at him.
The lion was enormous. Its claws and sharp teeth alone could kill. But Ji Chenxi, now fused with his spiritual body, had fundamentally changed. His body was agile, and with his ankle treated after a previous injury, he wasn’t in top form—but even so, a massive lion couldn’t easily bring him down.
Still, the lion’s speed wasn’t a joke. The attack was lightning-fast, powerfully forceful—and utterly ruthless in pursuit of its goal.
It seemed to have noticed Ji Chenxi’s injury — its attacks were pointed and deliberate, even trying to bite at his tail. That long, fluffy tail, over a meter of fur, was just asking to be grabbed.
Ji Chenxi clicked his tongue; being spotted like this was bad news. He slipped sideways to dodge the lion’s charge while also avoiding the barrage of photon blasts tearing the air. Using his spiritual power to twist the attack, he redirected a few photon shots back at the attackers — they deserved a taste of their own medicine.
Light on his feet and half-casual even while dodging, Ji Chenxi finally sensed deadly danger closing in. He twisted aside just in time — a slice of force cut a lock of his silver hair clean off as he leapt.
