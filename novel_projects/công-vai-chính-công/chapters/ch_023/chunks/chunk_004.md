In a situation that should have infuriated him, Ji Chenxi couldn’t help but laugh. He released the tight hold on Chi Zhuo’s neck and leaned casually against the cold wall behind him. Clear, beautiful eyes sparkled with an inappropriate smile. “Chi Zhuo… what do you want me to do to behave?”
Chi Zhuo said nothing.
But Ji Chenxi had already started giving pointers. “If you want pheromones, the place to attack is the glands, not the lips. There’s very little in saliva. Even blood has far more pheromones than saliva. So if you really want mine, the best way is to bite my glands.”
“Biting the glands isn’t a good idea. My pheromones would clash with yours… they’d mess up your glands until they’re just a stabbing ache.”
Chi Zhuo’s strong, resilient arms pressed Ji Chenxi closer. He inhaled the faint natural pheromone scent coming off him. “I kissed you because your mouth still smelled strongly of—”
“Of what?”
“Other alcohol.”
Ji Chenxi let out a small laugh. The spiritual spike he had just formed in his hand reversed subtly, not piercing, then dissipated as silently as it had appeared.
“So, Chi Zhuo, you’re controlling me from drinking? Want to try some? It’s really good.”
“I’ve already tried.”
Ji Chenxi truly couldn’t help laughing this time—he had, indeed, tasted it. From his own mouth.
The lingering alcohol buzz seemed to rise again. Ji Chenxi, having just scratched Chi Zhuo’s neck a few times, took the opportunity to hook his arm around Chi Zhuo’s neck, his voice low and teasing: “Kiss again?”
