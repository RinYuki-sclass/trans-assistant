At 7:55, Ji Chenxi pointed to the nearby Ferris wheel. “How about this one instead?”
Compared to the carousel, the Ferris wheel was more popular with couples. Su Yu wasn’t too interested, but since it was Ji Chenxi’s first suggestion for the evening, he agreed without complaint.
At 7:58, following Ji Chenxi’s request, he got into a private cabin of the Ferris wheel.
At 7:59:32, Chi Zhuo, silent for hours, finally sent a message:
【Look up at the sky】
Even though they had guessed it might be fireworks, the real thing still hit with awe.
After the ten-second countdown, the fireworks exploded.
The spectacular fireworks kept blooming in the night sky, one cluster after another, like roses opening in the darkness—brilliant and dazzling.
What kind of confession could truly be called grand?
At the very least, it had to be romantic and thoughtful. After the burst of flowers, it was like a huge book flipping through its pages, filled with small symbolic items representing moments since they first met.
Anyone could admire this confession from the outside, but only someone who truly understood would grasp its deeper meaning.
The massive Ferris wheel climbed slowly. With still ten or so meters to the top, Ji Chenxi finally replied to Chi Zhuo:
【I’m almost at the top of the Ferris wheel】
Ji Chenxi counted the seconds. Yet in the very first second after sending the message, Chi Zhuo’s reply came back, as if he had been watching Ji Chenxi’s messages the whole time.
Whether out of caution or some other reason, the reply came as a video with audio.
“Can I confess at the very top?”
Ji Chenxi paused deliberately, not agreeing immediately.
“This confession isn’t perfect. I’ve thought about how to make it grand but low-key at the same time, and I couldn’t figure it out. Grand can’t be low-key—this is probably the best compromise.”
Ji Chenxi dragged his tone a little. “I see…”
“So… will Your Highness agree?”
