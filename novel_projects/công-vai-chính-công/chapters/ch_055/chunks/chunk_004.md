“I’m almost at the top.”
Chi Zhuo was caught off guard by that and quickly said, “Be my boyfriend!”
As always, a statement rather than a question—more like a notice than a request.
Ji Chenxi chuckled lightly.
Finally, the cabin he was in reached the Ferris wheel’s highest point. Looking out the glass at the fireworks, countless bursts of light reflected on his face, painting it in different shades.
Amid that dazzling color, he said with pleasure, “If you don’t mind me disrupting your rhythm, I’ll reluctantly agree… alright?
Boyfriend.”
