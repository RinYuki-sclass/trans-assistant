Was this something he could even talk about?
Lu Tu blinked, feeling a little surprised.
For him, surprises were just a fleeting thing—barely leaving a trace on his face. Less than two seconds after hearing Ji Chenxi speak, a smile bloomed across his face. “Confession? Seriously? That’s surprising. The Third Prince is so young and already taken… what a shame.”
“I’m not exactly taken yet.” Ji Chenxi said this with precise seriousness.
The relationship between him and Chi Zhuo was, if anything, an unspoken sort of dating. To put it strictly, it hadn’t reached the level of being boyfriends.
Lu Tu found this fascinating, his smile flared even brighter than his red hair. “I wonder… is Your Highness the one confessing, or the one being confessed to?”
Ji Chenxi closed the one-way chat he had been staring at just moments ago, a small smile tugging at his lips. “Actually, I was just joking. The reason I kept looking at it was because it was a message from a friend.”
“So there was no confession at all?” Lu Tu asked, half believing, half doubtful, instinctively glancing at Gu Hechuan for confirmation.
Gu Hechuan’s face remained warm, smiling gently as he looked at the two of them. “Alright, don’t be so curious about everything.”
It was clear he was speaking to the red-haired youth. Lu Tu looked completely crushed, his brows furrowed into tiny mountains. “Sweetheart… you don’t love me anymore, and you just say that to me? I was only a little bit curious!”
“Then rein in that little bit of curiosity,” Gu Hechuan said, his smile growing even warmer.
His smile was like a spring breeze, so gentle it almost made Lu Tu’s goosebumps laugh. “Oh, don’t look at me like that… I can’t handle it.”
Gu Hechuan knew the other image he had left on Lu Tu ran deep, so he didn’t press further. Instead, he turned to Ji Chenxi and said, “I heard on the star network that you were getting close to the Miss of the Lu family, but she’s already back in the Twelfth Fairy Star. I was wondering who our little rose was planning to confess to… turns out it was just a misunderstanding.”
Whether Lu Tu believed this or not, Su Yu didn’t know—but he certainly didn’t.
With how ambiguous Ji Chenxi and the Chi family guy had been before, even if there was going to be a confession, Su Yu felt like it would have been Chi Zhuo who finally couldn’t hold back.
Su Yu tried to read something from Ji Chenxi’s eyes, but the truth was—he couldn’t see a thing.
Ji Chenxi’s expression was far too calm. If Chi Zhuo were going to confess, the other person shouldn’t be this composed. Just a few extra glances at the chat, nothing more—no big deal, no big deal.
Su Yu comforted himself with that thought and even teased Gu Hechuan a little, “Hechuan, what did your friend just call you again?”
Gu Hechuan’s face didn’t change. “He… he says things like that casually, tossing out ‘baby’ or ‘sweetheart’ without a second thought.”
Lu Tu protested, “That’s not true! Am I really that casual?”
