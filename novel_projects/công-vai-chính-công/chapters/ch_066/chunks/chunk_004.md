Su Yu rolled his eyes so hard they nearly flipped over. “Do I look like I was holding back? If you don’t believe it, try him yourself.”
The group of friends quickly waved their hands. They were just here for fun. Teasing a few freshmen was fine, but they were all from the same circle—who in their right mind would actually go pick a real fight with the Third Prince?
“How about you all come at me together? It’s fine. Otherwise I’d feel bad taking your points,” Ji Chenxi said with a grin.
“Ah, no need. We just got word—if any freshman’s points go higher than the top senior’s, then we seniors have to step out. After that, it’s your little game,” Su Yu said, casually pushing Ji Chenxi’s dagger away.
The white wolf, still pinned by the snow leopard’s bite at its neck, kicked at the snow leopard’s leg in protest. If it weren’t for the illusion cast by that phantom butterfly, it wouldn’t have been slower.
Seeing the spirit forms’ reaction, Ji Chenxi couldn’t help but laugh. “Looks like someone’s not convinced.”
“I’m convinced. It’s the wolf that’s not. But if we’re fighting, then fight—why drag that annoying little butterfly into it?”
“Annoying little butterfly? It worked pretty well, didn’t it? So what’s going on with the underground maze?”
“What else? Chi Zhuo and Lan Mu teamed up—thick as thieves. They’ve fully taken over the maze and set up tons of nasty traps. Whoever goes down there is in for a bad time. Plenty of people have already fallen for it. But since Chi Zhuo’s holding a gold coin worth ten thousand points, there are still a lot of people willing to take the risk.”
“Ten thousand points?” Ji Chenxi’s brows lifted slightly.
For a split second, he almost thought the thing he had his eye on had already been found by Chi Zhuo. But whether Chi Zhuo had found it or not, they still had to make a trip into the underground maze.
After parting ways with Su Yu and the others, Ji Chenxi led his group toward the maze.
He first signaled Lu Jianhan to set up his illusions, then quietly spread out his own spiritual power to sense what was inside.
Almost at once, he caught a familiar presence—along with another alpha aura, strong and heavy.
Deep inside the maze, countless tiny blue spiders were spinning webs. Among them, a man sitting at ease seemed to sense something. He slowly raised his eyes, interest flickering within them.
“Chi, someone else is here. Is it the cat you’ve been wanting to catch?”
