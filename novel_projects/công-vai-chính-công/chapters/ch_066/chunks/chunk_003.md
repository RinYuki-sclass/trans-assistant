“The hidden mission in the West District is his underground maze. They’ll definitely be waiting inside, just sitting there for prey. If we walk straight in, we’re at a disadvantage. So instead of going in… we make them come out.”
“And why would they come out?” Lu Jianhan didn’t believe for a second that the ones who set the trap would leave their ground on their own.
Ji Chenxi just smiled and said nothing. He walked over to Bai Anran. “Anran, let me see the map again.”
Without hesitation, Bai Anran handed it over. After thinking for a moment, he lowered his voice. “Right now, we don’t even know the full situation in the West District. Bringing us along will only drag you down. Your Highness… you might as well memorize the map and part ways with us.”
Ji Chenxi looked slightly surprised. “You’re giving me the map? Isn’t that… a bit much?”
“It’s not. If it weren’t for you, we wouldn’t have kept it in the first place. Now we not only have the map, we’ve got points too. I’ve talked it over with everyone. No one objects. Keeping the map with us would just waste it. It’s better to give it to you.”
At the start, Ji Chenxi really had planned to leave. The strongest in this team was Bai Anran. The rest were three A-ranks and two B-ranks. And among those A-ranks, one was even an A-. To be honest, it looked like a solid team, but to Ji Chenxi, it was just average. If it weren’t for Bai Anran personally inviting him earlier, he wouldn’t have joined at all.
But now that he had agreed, there was no way he’d abandon them halfway.
So this time, he didn’t even hesitate. “Enough. Don’t say that. We move together.”
“Your Highness…” Bai Anran wanted to say more but stopped.
“It’s fine. I don’t really care whether I take first place or not,” Ji Chenxi said lightly.
Since Ji Chenxi had put it that way, Bai Anran said nothing more.
When the group reached the West District, they headed straight for the underground maze. Along the way, they ran into quite a few people. Most just watched them from the side. A few came up to greet them, trying to get close and team up. Almost all of those were turned down by Ji Chenxi.
As for the seniors Ji Chenxi had robbed before, the way they looked at him was a lot more dangerous. One by one, their eyes were sharp as knives, like they wanted to snatch back every last point he’d taken. But the seniors had rules. First, there was a cap on how many people could team up—freshmen didn’t know the exact number, but if too many seniors surrounded freshmen at once, it counted as a violation. Second, if a senior had already fought a freshman, they couldn’t rob that same person twice.
Still, after arriving in the West District, Ji Chenxi eventually ran into Su Yu and his group.
The moment the two sides met, before a single word was exchanged, they attacked.
At the same time Ji Chenxi and Su Yu moved, so did their spirit forms. The powerful white wolf and the snow leopard lunged at each other with blinding speed, teeth flashing as they tore forward.
Ji Chenxi was already fast. Add in Lu Jianhan’s illusions, plus Ji Chenxi’s own deep understanding of Su Yu’s fighting style, and the outcome came quickly. After just over a hundred moves—barely a short clash to those who couldn’t follow the action—Ji Chenxi’s dagger was already pressed against Su Yu’s throat.
The others standing beside Su Yu didn’t even get the chance to step in. It was already over.
“Su Yu, you weren’t holding back, were you?” someone who knew both Su Yu and Ji Chenxi very well asked, clearly shocked at how fast he’d lost.
