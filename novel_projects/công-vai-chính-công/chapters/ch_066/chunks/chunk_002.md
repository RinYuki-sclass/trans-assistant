Lu Jianhan went oddly quiet for a moment. Then he almost swore out loud. So this was what “cooperation” meant? The ones with zero points team up to rob the ones with high scores?
“Doesn’t that give the seniors a clear edge?” he asked, brows pulling tight.
“Not exactly,” Ji Chenxi said lightly. “Sure, they’ve got more experience. But none of the seniors who came are Double S rank. You’re S+. That means you can still fight them. So relax.”
“And what about Bai Anran? He’s S rank too…”
Lu Jianhan tried to put it gently, but Bai Anran’s performance against Su Yu really hadn’t looked great. If Ji Chenxi hadn’t shown up in time, that map might’ve already been taken.
“Oh, that? Totally normal. Su Yu’s spirit form is a white wolf. It suppresses Bai Anran,” Ji Chenxi replied casually.
Lu Jianhan didn’t press further about what Bai Anran’s spirit form was. Since they’d decided to keep heading toward the West District, he shifted his focus back to the surroundings, gathering intel in advance. His scouting skills were solid. Before they even fully reached the West District, he had already figured out who was running it now.
Ji Chenxi hadn’t really cared who was in charge. To him, it was simple—either it was Chi Zhuo, or it wasn’t. So when he heard the actual answer, he was genuinely surprised.
“What did you say? Mamba and Blue Spider?”
“Yeah. It’s normal if you haven’t heard of Mamba. His real name is Chi Zhuo. He’s quite a bit older than us. Around eight or nine years ago, he was pretty well-known at the Imperial Royal Academy. He kept a low profile, though. After graduating, the only big thing he did was take over the Chi family. After that, there wasn’t much news about him. So most people only really know Blue Spider.”
“Who’s Blue Spider?” Ji Chenxi asked.
Lu Jianhan didn’t even consider the possibility that Ji Chenxi knew Chi Zhuo but not Blue Spider. He simply assumed His Third Highness hadn’t bothered to look into the top graduates from the two elite schools.
So he eagerly explained, “Blue Spider was another big name from the same era as Mamba. His fame actually far surpassed Chi Zhuo’s. You could say he was the top figure at the Imperial Royal Academy in those years. During the inter-school exchange competitions, the First Military Academy was always pushed down a notch because of him.”
“Why would those two work together?”
Ji Chenxi didn’t need to hear more about Blue Spider’s past achievements. At this point, he already knew who it was.
Imperial Royal Academy. Spirit form most likely a blue spider. About the same age as Chi Zhuo. S rank or so. Put that all together, and the answer came fast—Lan family’s eldest son, Lan Mu.
Compared to listening to Lan Mu’s old glory stories, Ji Chenxi was more curious about something else. Chi Zhuo had once said he didn’t have friends. So why was he working with this guy?
“Well… they were probably close in private. Back then, Mamba was really low-key. You could even call him withdrawn. The few times he truly made a splash were mostly in trial tournaments he joined with Blue Spider. And Blue Spider was always publicly challenging him, saying he wanted to compete with Chi Zhuo. From that alone, you can tell Mamba’s real strength might’ve actually been a bit higher than Blue Spider’s.”
Behind them, the recording drone was capturing every move the freshmen made. Ji Chenxi wasn’t about to slip up in front of the academy. So after hearing that, he simply nodded.
“If we run into those two, how are we supposed to fight them?”
