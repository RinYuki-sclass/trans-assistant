Ji Chenxi let out a soft laugh. “Aren’t you getting tired of this? At first, it was all just a game, and that would’ve been fine. But you had to take it seriously. And I can’t just marry an alpha, can I? You understand, right? An alpha always looks for a highly compatible omega.”
Chi Zhuo’s composure finally began to crack. Along with the sinking weight in his chest came a killing intent. His dark eyes glinted with a look that seemed to regard Ji Chenxi as good as dead.
Chi Zhuo sneered. “If you tell me you’re wrong now… maybe I could forgive you.”
“Even for that, you’d forgive me? Chi Zhuo, it seems your feelings aren’t just two points,” Ji Chenxi said with a light laugh, his tone teasing in a way that was hard to resist. “We’re adults—can’t you stop taking this so seriously? I’d feel guilty otherwise.”
Chi Zhuo let out a quiet “hmph,” his eyes, full of killing intent, locking with Ji Chenxi’s. “Then… have you been enjoying yourself all this time, Your Highness? Or should I ask… have I been thrilling to you?”
Ji Chenxi tilted his head, a playful smile on his face, boldly accepting the words. “Thrilling.”
The screen suddenly went black. Chi Zhuo’s video call ended just like that.
Before the call cut, there was even the sound of heavy objects crashing down. With the strength Chi Zhuo could exert in just three seconds, his room must have been destroyed in that instant.
After the virtual image disappeared, Ji Chenxi’s smile faltered. On his desk had been a book—the black leather one.
His fingers traced the name “Chi Zhuo” on the page again, but his feelings were even more complicated than the first time he saw it.
“Chi Zhuo… a belated blaze.” Those were the words Chi Zhuo wrote when he finally confessed his feelings to Bai Anran near the story’s end. Yet now, this “belated blaze” had come as an early confession.
No matter how Ji Chenxi looked at Chi Zhuo’s final gaze, it seemed like he wanted to kill him.
Ji Chenxi thought he might be sick. Somehow, being looked at that way only made him more excited.
In truth, Ji Chenxi didn’t need to be so extreme. If there was still a chance for them later… but he didn’t want the old man to know about his relationship with Chi Zhuo yet. Losing the chance to secure that position before he was fully ready would be far too embarrassing.
Ji Chenxi had originally thought Chi Zhuo would come looking for him a bit later, when his heart had already calmed down. This timing felt a little too soon.
Stuck at home, Ji Chenxi finally moved. The first thing he did was go to the palace to see his good father.
Since the assassination attempt, he hadn’t visited, and his father hadn’t asked him to come either. His sudden initiative made him look somewhat weak, and, as expected, the emperor once again put on a display of fatherly care—after all, he had just sent the royal guard to ruin Ji Chenxi’s previous plan.
“Xier, are you mad at me?”
Ji Chenxi’s face broke into a gentle, amused smile. “How could I be mad at you? Didn’t you still send people to protect me?”
He smiled with all the innocence in the world, as if he had no idea that the emperor’s gesture was also a way to secure another pair of his children.
