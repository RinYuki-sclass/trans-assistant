It wasn’t surprising that Chi Zhuo reacted so strongly—Ji Chenxi’s words were the kind that made anyone overthink.
So, who am I supposed to trust?
When the person you care about asks you that question, there’s really only one answer: “Me.”
But Chi Zhuo found it hard to actually say that to Ji Chenxi. He could tell that Ji Chenxi had at least a bit of that kind of thought himself; otherwise, why would the Third Prince even bother getting entangled with an alpha? Yet their first two meetings had been too coincidental, and even Chi Zhuo didn’t fully trust Ji Chenxi. How could he expect Ji Chenxi to trust him?
In just a few seconds, though, Chi Zhuo collected himself. He looked at Ji Chenxi and said, “Your Highness, in your position, there are very few people you can truly trust. If you can, you should only trust yourself. Everyone else is guided by their own interests. And where interests are involved, there are no absolute friends—so trust becomes impossible.”
After hearing this, Ji Chenxi paused for a long while and then, surprisingly, smiled.
“This answer… I honestly don’t know what to say. It’s not what I wanted to hear, but it really is the most suitable for me.”
Chi Zhuo knew full well how unromantic his answer sounded. Seeing Ji Chenxi clearly say he didn’t want to hear it, Chi Zhuo quickly added, “Your Highness, you could also try trusting me. As long as there’s no huge conflict of interest, I won’t go against you. I’m even willing to help you if you actively ask for it.”
“And what if there’s an unavoidable clash of interests between us?” Ji Chenxi teased, poking at him.
Chi Zhuo frowned slightly. Given their current positions, a real conflict of interest wasn’t possible—but since Ji Chenxi had asked, he gave an honest reply. “I’m willing to give up part of my own interests for Your Highness… but only part.”
Ji Chenxi met his gaze. There was no hint of lying in Chi Zhuo’s eyes—of course, if he were lying, he wouldn’t need to emphasize that it was only partial. Just thinking about Chi Zhuo yielding to him stirred a familiar thrill, a faint spark of interest that had cooled not long ago, now rushing back all over again.
Chi Zhuo’s behavior wasn’t what Ji Chenxi had expected at first—it seemed like he was actually being a bit serious.
Ji Chenxi was a top-tier alpha, with a face so handsome it could make anyone melt. Add in the fact that he was a prince of the empire, and his omega and beta admirers were endless. He’d also had alphas showing him private attention before, and he had turned down too many of them to count, even crafting different reasons to refuse each one. The most persistent suitors would eventually give up, knowing better than to push.
With so many admirers, Ji Chenxi had even reached the point where he could easily tell why someone liked him. Most were drawn just to his looks. At first, Chi Zhuo had been the same—attracted to that simple charm. But now… was Chi Zhuo’s liking really that simple anymore?
Ji Chenxi thought Chi Zhuo’s approach was a little off. Showing your feelings first made it easy for the other person to take control.
“You’re not afraid that if I only take that little part of your interests each time, I’ll eventually numb you… and eat you completely, leaving nothing behind?”
Chi Zhuo’s lips curled slightly into a relaxed smile, as if he didn’t feel threatened at all. “Your Highness, you can try.”
That same alpha confidence, proud and unshakable, was fully on display in Chi Zhuo.
Some would be drawn to it… and naturally, some would want to break it.
Ji Chenxi rested his head on one hand, frowning slightly. “Chi Zhuo, doing this really makes me feel like you do have some thoughts about me.”
