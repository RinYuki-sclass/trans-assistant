“And if I really did… what would Your Highness do?”
Chi Zhuo had never answered Ji Chenxi’s teasing directly before, yet this time he did. Ji Chenxi hadn’t expected that just ten days without contact would make Chi Zhuo so upfront. Next time they met, would he tear down that thin veil of ambiguity entirely?
Ji Chenxi was curious, but he quickly killed that possibility in its cradle.
“Of course, I’d refuse. Alpha and alpha are never a good match.”
Chi Zhuo’s breath hitched for a moment, but he quickly regained control. “Is that what Your Highness means?”
A smile still lingered on Ji Chenxi’s lips. “Of course, that’s what I mean. If I did anything before that gave you the wrong idea, I apologize.”
At first, Ji Chenxi had guessed that Chi Zhuo might get annoyed by such a blunt rejection and hang up right away. But Chi Zhuo didn’t. He listened—and after a brief frown, he thought for a moment. Then, in a calm, almost emotionless voice, he said, “I don’t think I’ve hidden anything on purpose. If I’ve been unclear before, I can say it now: I do like Your Highness… just a little, two points, let’s say.”
Ji Chenxi didn’t understand. He had already refused him—there was no need for Chi Zhuo to humiliate himself again.
“You…” Ji Chenxi started but stopped mid-sentence.
“Before being refused, a confession should at least be heard once. A refusal without a confession feels… too unclear,” Chi Zhuo said, as if pointing at the already murky nature of their relationship.
“Do I need to refuse you again?”
“Would Your Highness refuse me a second time?” Chi Zhuo’s warm gaze, which he usually gave Ji Chenxi, slowly changed tone.
“Of course I would. Refusing someone isn’t hard.”
“Then why did you string me along before? If it was for some benefit… you haven’t gained a single thing from me.”
Ji Chenxi had already won this little game of who fell first, but somehow, he didn’t feel much joy.
Some things really weren’t meant to be played with—secretly teasing didn’t feel right either. He frowned, already annoyed at the words he was about to speak.
When he finally spoke, his face carried that elegant, teasing smile. “Actually, it wasn’t hard to guess. I wanted to leave a bit of dignity between us. How could an alpha be interested in another alpha? The first time I marked you, your scent nearly drove me crazy for a while.”
Chi Zhuo let out a cold “hmph,” probably thinking something like ‘serves you right’.
Ji Chenxi, unfazed, continued, “But… being tangled up with an alpha isn’t bad. Especially after you kissed me—I admit, I got a little mean. The alpha in me even wanted to take you to bed.”
“Now Your Highness doesn’t want that?” Chi Zhuo interrupted.
