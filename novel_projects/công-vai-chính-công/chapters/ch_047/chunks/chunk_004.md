“Xier,” the emperor’s voice grew slightly stern.
Ji Chenxi’s smile softened but didn’t disappear. “Your Majesty, if you wanted to play the game, you shouldn’t have let me end up passive again.”
“So… you admit it was your doing before?”
Ji Chenxi, of course, didn’t admit it directly. He only said, “I wouldn’t send someone to kill myself.”
“Then why were you angry?”
“Naturally, it’s because of your favoritism, Father. Why wouldn’t I want to play the game? Because if I step in, I’ll have no way out. I haven’t even joined the struggle yet, and you’ve already shown favoritism to my older brother twice. If I really join, I dare not imagine what will happen.”
Today, Ji Chenxi had already done two bold, reckless things, yet his mood felt surprisingly light.
He still didn’t know why the old man had interfered with that matter, but his words today had already achieved the effect he wanted.
As expected, the emperor, upon hearing this, didn’t get angry. Instead, he seemed to pity his youngest son a little. “Wu Xiang is a good blade. Use him well.”
This was essentially a promise that Wu Xiang would obey his orders.
Ji Chenxi smiled and took his leave.
Chi Zhuo, probably much calmer now, sent him one last message:
[Don’t let me catch you!]
Ji Chenxi’s smile, which had been a little fake before, was now completely real. What could he do? He wasn’t scared at all.
