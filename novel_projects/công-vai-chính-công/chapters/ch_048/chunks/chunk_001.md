Most of the objects in the quiet room had been smashed to pieces under Chi Zhuo’s spiritual force, yet a few items made of sturdier materials remained intact. The loud crash that echoed just now came from one of those unbroken objects hitting the floor.
Even in a soundproofed room, such a massive commotion could be noticed from outside.
Dust swirled through the air, and amidst the shattered fragments on the floor, the door creaked open lightly. After three polite knocks, a voice came from outside—Bai Han’s, asking carefully,
Chi Zhuo gave a single “Hmm,” signaling her to enter.
Bai Han stepped inside and, facing the chaotic mess, didn’t flinch. Calmly, she continued briefing the issues they needed to handle this time.
“Leave the Y Route and the XM-52 star business to Yin Man. Let Hua Yao handle the current problem.”
Following Chi Zhuo’s orders, Bai Han lowered her head and laid out the tasks. “So, we’re not going to Uzzi Star?”
“Then… Master, are you going to check out the new project our gaming company just launched? It’s not too far from headquarters.”
“That dating game?”
“Yes. Virtual games centered on romance are really popular lately. We just released a few closed beta spots and already got a lot of praise. But for long-term profits, we can’t rush the initial investment. My suggestion is to enhance the sensory effects a bit more—make it feel fully immersive so people are willing to spend money—but also include anti-addiction measures so players don’t get hooked. Master could also take a look at our other games we’ve launched…”
Before Bai Han could finish, Chi Zhuo cut him off: “Return to Imperial Star. We’re heading back now.”
Bai Han, who over the years had grown used to spending half a year at a time away with Chi Zhuo without returning to Imperial Star, paused. She set down the electronic recording pen and asked, “Is something wrong with the industries on Imperial Star? There are still a lot of high-ranking people over there.”
Bai Han’s words were meant to reassure her master—not to worry. Even if someone were bold enough to provoke the Chi family, there were people who could handle it. But as if recalling something, Bai Han added, “Actually, there is one matter on Imperial Star. At the time, I thought it was minor, so I didn’t report it to you immediately.”
“At that time, Master, you had kept all the ores used to make the ‘Mineral Rose’ and had them crafted into the rose that was sent to your home. There were some leftover scraps, and your jewelry company turned them into pieces intended for market release.”
Chi Zhuo was silent for a long moment before saying, “Suppress it. Don’t sell them.”
“Master, is your return to Imperial Star for personal matters?”
Chi Zhuo’s face, already sharp, darkened even further. “Bai Han, are you questioning my business?”
“Of course not.”
“Return to course.”
