Ji Chenxi laughed. If Chi Zhuo truly wanted to kill him, why give a warning in advance? Was he supposed to feel psychological pressure from such words?
Setting the holocomputer aside, Ji Chenxi endured a moment before finally activating voice input. He spoke firmly: “Do I need to hide? At the Rose Manor, I await you anytime. Come if you dare.”
After delivering his sharp retort, Ji Chenxi curled up completely under the covers, even positioning the fairly large snow leopard in a protective embrace around him. He lay beside it, enveloped by its tail.
Still, the restlessness clung tightly to him.
Looking back at the message he’d just sent, he realized—was he provoking? No. He was calling his alpha over. Since he had marked him, he belonged entirely to Ji Chenxi and, naturally, should stay by his side through this sensitive period.
Two hours had passed, and Ji Chenxi could feel sharp pangs twisting through his stomach. His palms were covered in self-inflicted bloodstains from trying to suppress his sensitive period.
Each breath grew heavier, his body being pulled between extreme desire and a deep, world-weary frustration.
He kept pressing at the wounds in his hands, using pain to reclaim some semblance of reason. One thought kept circling in his mind—he hadn’t come. The alpha he had marked hadn’t come.
On the other side, Chi Zhuo sneered at Ji Chenxi’s display of defiance and quickly began planning an infiltration of Rose Manor. But today, the manor was guarded even more strictly than the last time he had come.
Chi Zhuo, however, was among the top-tier in skill. With Bai Han opening the path covertly, after a bit of effort, Chi Zhuo finally slipped inside the manor.
The first thing that hit him upon arrival was the thick, sweet scent of roses.
A small alpha in a sensitive period was fragile and insecure. Even as his killer instincts sharpened, Chi Zhuo restrained himself a little, his desire to see Ji Chenxi in person softening him.
Moving carefully, he climbed through a window and reached the spot where the pheromones were strongest.
When he opened the window, he saw the silver-haired alpha curled up, hugging his knees, and beside him the snow leopard, also curled up, tail wrapped protectively around the alpha. Both looked utterly pitiful, vulnerable.
The blade-ready, murderous Chi Zhuo felt a twinge of softness. Bathed in the heavy rose scent, his own breathing faltered for a moment.
Ji Chenxi, sensing the movement, lifted his face.
Chi Zhuo saw him immediately.
His eyes were red-rimmed, lashes wet with tears.
Chi Zhuo’s dark gray eyes flickered, and even his own breathing hitched.
Alphas in a sensitive period were so fragile.
