Back in the cave, Ji Chenxi measured the cave’s entrance and cut the stone to fit. By now, he could feel Chi Zhuo’s heartbeat racing faster than usual, and his muscles trembled slightly.
Waiting any longer could cause some frostbite.
Once the stone was cut carefully but not rushed, Ji Chenxi quickly sealed the entrance. After a moment of thought, he left a small gap to let some air flow.
With most of the cold blocked out, the remaining chill became far more bearable.
Ji Chenxi draped the protective jacket he had been wearing over Chi Zhuo again.
The jacket, warmed by his body, settled over Chi Zhuo, who let out a quiet “Mm?”—a sound of slight confusion.
“Your pheromones are too strong. I can’t get comfortable.”
Ji Chenxi had now arranged the dry branches into a thick pile suitable for a fire, but when he tried to light it, it wouldn’t catch. He gathered some smaller, easy-to-burn twigs, and this time the fire took easily.
He placed the lit sticks onto the larger pile and held out his cold hands to warm them. The snow leopard, sensing its master’s chill, pressed closely against Ji Chenxi and tried to wrap its long tail around his hand—but Ji Chenxi pushed it away.
“Careful, you’ll burn your fur.”
The warm jacket carried little of the rose scent, but a faint, lingering fragrance remained. Chi Zhuo, already able to make out a soft glow and human figure in the dim light, said, “Third Prince.”
“Hm?” Ji Chenxi replied casually, generously letting Chi Zhuo share a portion of the snow leopard’s warmth.
Feeling the furry warmth close, Chi Zhuo continued the sentence he hadn’t finished earlier. “I just realized… you’re actually pretty gentle.”
