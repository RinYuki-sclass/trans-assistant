The young man walked a long stretch before suddenly remembering something. He called back to the middle-aged man behind him: “Captain, if you please.”
Scorpion’s heart sank further, though his face remained calm and friendly. “Just by looking at the positions where they died, you could trace Chi Zhuo’s movements. Vice-Captain Ge, you’re quite the talent.”
“That’s only because the Captain has an eye for talent. Picking me out from a bunch of drifters… Captain, you’ve always been my patron. Without you, I wouldn’t be where I am today. Every moment of these past two years, I’ve been grateful for you.”
The young man smiled so widely that his eyebrows nearly disappeared.
Meanwhile, under the setting sun, Chi Zhuo and Ji Chenxi were quickly searching for a relatively hidden place to spend the night.
With Chi Zhuo’s pheromones drifting like they were, even finding a safe spot wouldn’t keep them hidden for long. But they did notice one thing: as the sun dipped, the surrounding air suddenly grew much colder.
It wasn’t just normal evening chill. The temperature plunged from a comfortable twenty-something degrees straight down into the teens—and still seemed to be falling.
Dangerous night conditions were fast approaching.
By the time the last sliver of the sun disappeared, the outdoor temperature had dropped to just a few degrees. A simple jacket was no longer enough to keep out the cold.
Even a light breeze made Ji Chenxi shrink his neck. He quietly tucked his head behind Chi Zhuo and his long hair for warmth.
Noticing the small movement, Chi Zhuo coldly reminded him, “Watch the path.”
“I am,” Ji Chenxi replied quickly. “Keep going, nine o’clock direction. My spiritual scan picked up a spot that looks good for shelter—about five, maybe six kilometers ahead.”
The sudden chill was unbearable for Ji Chenxi. He didn’t have a snow leopard’s thick, warm fur. The protective suit offered some defense against harsh environments, but the earlier descent had already worn it down. As someone who got cold or hot easily, Ji Chenxi buried his face into Chi Zhuo’s shoulder again, planning to alert him only when they reached an obstacle.
“Your spiritual form is a snow leopard,” Chi Zhuo reminded him.
Even with a beast king from a freezing land as a spiritual body, this guy was still colder than him.
“The snow leopard gets cold too,” Ji Chenxi said. “Besides, I don’t have its fur or thick fat to block the cold. So I’m cold.”
Chi Zhuo stopped and gently set Ji Chenxi down from his back.
Ji Chenxi’s eyes widened slightly. He hadn’t even complained about the other’s pheromones leaking out, yet somehow that person was already struggling with him first.
Before Ji Chenxi could react further, Chi Zhuo had already taken off his protective jacket and draped it over Ji Chenxi’s head. Only his eyes were visible now, and they widened even more. His long, slender eyes—normally sparkling when he smiled—almost looked like cat eyes from the surprise.
The familiar scent of tequila hit him instantly, wrapping Ji Chenxi in it completely.
