It was the smell of another alpha, too strong and intoxicating. Ji Chenxi nearly felt his head spinning from the alcohol scent alone.
“Alright, shouldn’t be as cold now.”
Chi Zhuo, having fully endured the biting wind, seemed completely unaffected by the cold. He crouched down again, signaling for Ji Chenxi to climb up.
Ji Chenxi tightened the protective jacket around him. “Hey, why are you suddenly so considerate? I’m flattered, you know.”
“I just suddenly remembered that Your Highness is a delicate little rose.”
Ji Chenxi laughed. “You know, if I get mad, I could just run off with your jacket all by myself.”
“Don’t believe it.”
The smile on Ji Chenxi’s lips widened. He knew Chi Zhuo believed it—he was just daring to gamble.
Those who accomplish great things are often gamblers.
“Well then, thank you for your kindness, Brother Chi Zhuo,” came a voice as sweet as honey.
“Mm, come on, climb up. If we find a place out of the wind, it’ll feel much better.”
Ji Chenxi stopped trying to read Chi Zhuo’s thoughts and didn’t refuse the obviously warmer jacket.
Once the oversized jacket was over his head, all he had to do was close the hem, and most of the cold was kept at bay.
His eyes swept over Chi Zhuo with a mix of amusement and something more complex. Without thinking too much, Ji Chenxi climbed onto his back again, leaving only his eyes visible to guide the way.
The cold wind cut across them like a sharp blade, stabbing the skin with every gust. Bone-chilling and merciless, nature made humans feel small and helpless.
Chi Zhuo’s body, in that extreme cold, turned icy. At that moment, he seemed more like a cold-blooded creature than even his own snake. Ji Chenxi silently spread himself out as much as he could to shield them both and calmly gave directions, his voice steady despite the cutting wind.
He now felt like he was being carried around on the back of a giant block of ice.
After some time, the two of them finally reached the spot Ji Chenxi had spotted. It truly was a perfect place to shelter from the cold.
It was a cave—not particularly large, but enough for the two of them to hide inside and block the biting wind.
Ji Chenxi looked around the cave, then directed Chi Zhuo to another spot. Once there, he had Chi Zhuo move a huge stone while he gathered a bunch of dry branches, letting the snow leopard guide him back.
