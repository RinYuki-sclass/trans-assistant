This was like when you have some little secret—when you openly share it with your best friend, and it becomes a symbol of your friendship. But one day, that secret is known by someone else. Would you suspect that someone overheard you by accident, or would you start to doubt your friend?
The balance in Ji Chenxi’s heart had already begun to tilt. Wu Yang, the one he had marked, and Chen Xing, who had already caused a bit of unpleasantness.
His rational side kept analyzing how likely it was that Chen Xing was involved, and reminded him that when Chen Xing first arrived, hadn’t he himself also been unable to fully trust him? But emotion always countered—’we’ve known each other for so many years; during the hardest time, we were each other’s support.’
Ji Chenxi let out a slow breath, the flame in his hand flickering on and off again.
Let’s just treat it as an accident.
That thought had barely formed when he couldn’t help but laugh, mocking his own self-deception.
【It’s a bit cold tonight, Your Highness. Please remember to put on more clothes.】
While Ji Chenxi was feeling irritated because of someone, that someone’s concern still arrived as usual.
Chen Xing.
Next to that name was a small star symbol, one Ji Chenxi had personally added years ago—it meant that to him, the other person was like the morning star.
Ji Chenxi stared at the message for quite a while before replying.
【Chen Xing】
【If I find that something I once thought was a treasure has quietly turned yellow behind my back, how should I deal with it?】
The other side seemed a bit surprised; even the message carried a hint of emotion.
【I didn’t expect Your Highness to share everyday matters with me. If it has already turned yellow, you can just replace it.】
【But I’ve cared about it for many years. If I throw it away just because it’s yellowed, it feels a bit hard to let go.】
【Then Your Highness can keep it stored away. Take it out and look at it when you miss it.】
【I thought you’d tell me to throw it away quickly and then help me find something better. After all, you don’t seem to tolerate imperfection very well.】
It sounded like a joke, but if Chen Xing thought about it a little more, he would definitely sense the deeper meaning behind Ji Chenxi’s words.
Ji Chenxi didn’t know whether Chen Xing picked up on it. This reply took longer than the previous ones, yet it gave the feeling that the person on the other side had typed each word carefully.
