“Why did you mark me back then?”
Ji Chenxi’s brow twitched slightly. Even now, about to die, he wanted to bring up old grievances?
“Why else? Just a bit of anger,” Ji Chenxi replied. His temper wasn’t the best.
“Because of that bead?”
Ji Chenxi answered casually, already losing some motivation to swap the “hot towel” again. The other really did seem to be in a faint state of recovery, a momentary glow before collapse.
“That bead… does it have… special meaning?” Chi Zhuo asked. He still remembered it as a yin-yang tassel earring. Earlier, when he saw Ji Chenxi, the earring had changed to a koi-glass bead.
For a moment, Ji Chenxi thought he might be just using it as an excuse to talk.
Normally, if Chi Zhuo asked like this in a different state, Ji Chenxi wouldn’t answer. But in this cold, lonely night, with the two of them huddled for warmth, and seeing Chi Zhuo barely alive, Ji Chenxi glanced at him and simply said, “It’s something given to me by someone important.”
“An omega?”
Ordinary friends didn’t need to give something so treasured.
Hearing “omega,” Ji Chenxi didn’t hesitate. “Mm.”
“So… a… beautiful omega, then?”
Ji Chenxi liked hearing that. “Not just beautiful. But in terms of sheer beauty, even all the omegas combined don’t compare to her.”
Chi Zhuo’s vision was still blurry, but he could just make out the smile at Ji Chenxi’s lips. The haze made that smile seem softer, warmer, more tender.
The strong scent of tequila filled the cave. Even in his breathing, Ji Chenxi carried Chi Zhuo’s pheromones, yet he showed no irritation. He asked as if they were simply having a casual conversation, “Want to hear more about that omega?”
Chi Zhuo declined. “No need.”
His refusal was firm, but Chi Zhuo felt that by speaking at all, he had already softened the refusal.
Ji Chenxi felt a flicker of regret. Opportunities to talk about her were rare, and if Chi Zhuo hadn’t looked like he was dying, he wouldn’t have shared at all. Now that Chi Zhuo didn’t want to hear it, Ji Chenxi naturally retracted that little urge to share.
The tequila smell was thick, overwhelming. A single breath brought its aroma. Previously, it had hints of woodiness; now, the smoky, spicy notes were far stronger.
Ji Chenxi’s Adam’s apple moved slightly. His throat burned with a sharp, uncomfortable heat.
