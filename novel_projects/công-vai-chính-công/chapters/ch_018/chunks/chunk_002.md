The hot silk bow pressed tightly against Chi Zhuo’s head like a steaming towel.
The heat was intense. Chi Zhuo, whose eyelids had been half-closed, opened his eyes. The dim light of the cave flickered across his vision. The previously blurry figure in front of him seemed a little clearer, yet he still couldn’t make out Ji Chenxi’s hand.
“You’re using this as a hot compress?” His low voice was soft, almost hesitant.
Ji Chenxi nodded. He wasn’t entirely sure if this was the right thing to do, but fever, warm water, and a hot towel… it seemed reasonable.
“It’s very hot.”
Chi Zhuo’s voice had slowed, sounding drowsy, as if he could fall asleep at any moment.
Ji Chenxi, mistaking it for annoyance, furrowed his brows in irritation. As someone caring for another for the first time, he almost gave up.
Then Chi Zhuo added, “Is your hand okay?”
Ji Chenxi let out a quiet chuckle and looked down at his own hand, reddened from the hot water, the fingertips especially sensitive. He rotated it casually and answered, “It’s fine.”
The water was hot but not enough to scald.
After a short while, the silk “towel” stopped giving off steam. Ji Chenxi touched it—it was just warm now. He removed it, planning to reheat it.
His stored water was limited and couldn’t keep up with his quick towel-swapping pace.
He glanced at the remaining water and asked, “Do you want to drink more?”
Before Chi Zhuo could answer, Ji Chenxi gave the answer himself. “Drink some. Later, you won’t be able to.”
He poured a full lid of water for Chi Zhuo, setting it aside to cool. The silk strip he tossed into a container to briefly heat—just a second or two. But as he reached for it, Chi Zhuo’s hand accurately grabbed Ji Chenxi’s arm.
“Your Highness, you don’t need to trouble yourself for me. I won’t die.”
Ji Chenxi gave him a skeptical look. “You don’t look like you won’t die. You look like you’re about to faint any second.”
“Maybe I’ll be fine by tomorrow.”
“Unlikely.” Ji Chenxi was certain. Alphas were stronger by nature, but in this freezing night, if Chi Zhuo’s fever made him pass out, he might not wake up at all.
Chi Zhuo’s expression was listless, his eyelids drooping, looking completely drained. Even the hand resting on Ji Chenxi’s arm was about to fall.
