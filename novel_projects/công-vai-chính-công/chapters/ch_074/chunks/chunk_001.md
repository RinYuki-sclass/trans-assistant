Ji Chenxi didn’t care whether Lu Tu believed him or not. Anyway, at that time, he really was in a fight.
After wrapping up that “friendly negotiation” with Lu Tu, Ji Chenxi still sent Chen Xing a message: ‘Looking forward to your arrival.’
【I’m also looking forward to meeting Your Highness (smile)】
Chen Xing replied almost instantly. Ji Chenxi’s brows lifted slightly, and he sent back a smile emoji as well.
He had known Chen Xing for many years. For a time, he had even treated him as a close friend. On the road of growing up, Chen Xing had helped him more than once. No matter whether their beliefs would ever truly align in the end, that line about looking forward to his arrival—it came from the heart.
After briefly contacting the others on the darknet, Ji Chenxi was about to log off. But before he could actually exit, Manye Zhe’s messages started popping up like crazy.
【Why did you just disappear mid-chat?】
【You only told Chen Xing you’re looking forward to meeting him, not me?】
【So after meeting me once, I’m not your favorite anymore?】
【Bai Wei, you’ve hurt me.】
Ji Chenxi skimmed through the flood of messages and typed back one line.
【You’re coming?】
【Yes. I’m coming. And I’m coming with Chen Xing. After that, the two of us will probably stay on the Imperial Star for quite a long time.】
Manye Zhe—the number one assassin on the killer rankings. His whereabouts were always hard to track, and he loved keeping that air of mystery. Yet now he was typing in such an easy, casual tone, saying something that didn’t fit his usual style at all.
Ji Chenxi didn’t point that out. He simply replied in the same light tone.
【Wow, I’m so honored.】
That line clearly pleased Manye Zhe. He happily continued typing.
Manye Zhe: 【Hahaha, you’re already that honored? I even helped you by giving Chen Xing some “mental prep.” Not much, but at least when Chen Xing sees our White Rose being chased by an alpha, he’ll be able to keep a straight face.】
Ji Chenxi couldn’t help but laugh. He chatted a few more lines with him before finally logging off.
Only after going offline did he notice that Su Yu and several other familiar friends had already sent a pile of messages. Even Chi Zhuo, who was usually quiet and didn’t talk much, had sent him one.
