Ji Chenxi: “…”
For some reason, it suddenly felt like he was reviewing memorials from court officials.
He had only been offline for a month—how had the messages piled up this much?
Ji Chenxi didn’t have the patience to reply to them one by one. After giving them a quick glance, he chose a few to respond to and left the rest alone.
That included Chi Zhuo, who had sent only a single message.
Two days passed in the blink of an eye. Ji Chenxi had barely gone back to rest for a bit when it was already time for Chen Xing to arrive at the Imperial Star.
After confirming the exact arrival time, Ji Chenxi changed his clothes and headed out. Even with a little “tail” following behind him, he didn’t mind. Instead, he shot Wu Xiang a half-smile. “Aren’t you going to ask who I’m picking up, Major?”
“You’re not really picking anyone up, Your Highness. Why would I ask who you’re picking up?”
The corner of Ji Chenxi’s lips lifted slightly. That faint, teasing look finally turned into a real smile. “Now that’s not very honest. I clearly did something, yet you’re saying I did nothing. Wouldn’t that just draw more attention?”
“You did go to pick someone up, Your Highness. It’s just that I didn’t see who it was.” Wu Xiang smoothly changed his answer.
“And what if my dear father doubts your ability because of that?” Ji Chenxi continued, smiling as he pressed on.
“His Majesty knows my capabilities. I won’t deliberately go easy on you either. So he’ll believe my report and assume that Your Highness is far more capable than you appear. On the surface, that message may seem unfavorable to you, but in truth, it’s greatly to your advantage. As the Empire’s rightful heir, it’s only fitting that you outshine the other two.”
Ji Chenxi gave a small nod. “What should be said and what shouldn’t—I trust you understand. I won’t say more.”
After that, he didn’t pay Wu Xiang much attention. Ever since he had left a mark on Wu Xiang’s gland during his susceptible period, winning him over had only been a matter of time. No matter how much favor the Emperor had shown Wu Xiang, his lifeline was now in Ji Chenxi’s hands—unless Wu Xiang was truly loyal enough to give up his own life for the Emperor.
Private docking platform.
Ji Chenxi didn’t wait long before a gust of wind swept up his coat and long hair.
Before the starship had even fully stabilized, the hatch opened. Manye Zhe—whom Ji Chenxi had met once before—jumped straight down from dozens of meters in the air.
The tall man landed with smooth, clean precision, the move sharp and cool. He gave Ji Chenxi a nod.
His face looked as cold as ever. After that brief nod, he lowered his head and started tapping on his light brain.
Wu Xiang’s brows drew together slightly. For someone the Third Prince had personally come to receive, this man seemed far too disrespectful.
