The touch along the side of his neck sent a shiver through Ji Chenxi, making his scalp tingle a little. He had seen Chi Zhuo’s hand twist necks in an instant many times before, so having it so close now naturally couldn’t be ignored. But he didn’t pull away—he watched with quiet curiosity, wondering exactly what Chi Zhuo would do next.
His fingers lifted the silver strands, gathering all the hair that covered the back of Ji Chenxi’s neck into his palm. Then he leaned in closer.
The gesture, like a prelude to marking, made Ji Chenxi’s eyes cool slightly.
With the hair lifted, Chi Zhuo’s aim was clear—he moved toward Ji Chenxi’s glands. Ji Chenxi could even feel his breath brushing against them.
He nudged the marking teeth with his tongue. Honestly, he didn’t mind marking Chi Zhuo a third time.
Casually, he hooked a hand around Chi Zhuo’s neck.
What looked like an intimate gesture was naturally laced with danger, and when Chi Zhuo’s lips finally landed on Ji Chenxi’s glands, it was just a gentle, shallow kiss.
Afterwards, Chi Zhuo let out a muffled laugh. “Just now… it felt like I was kissing a rose.”
“Did your heartbeat speed up just now, Your Highness?” Chi Zhuo asked, his voice low and husky.
Among alphas, a kiss on the back of the neck was like dancing on the edge of a blade. The risk of being bitten at any moment made it far more thrilling than kissing lips, sending the heart into overdrive.
Ji Chenxi smiled. He should have known—while he was teasing Chi Zhuo, Chi Zhuo was teasing him in return.
So after a brief moment of surprise, Ji Chenxi laughed and asked, “And what about you, Chi Zhuo? Did your heart race for me at that moment?”
When speaking to Chi Zhuo, Ji Chenxi’s words always carried a smile, but this time his voice was unusually soft and lingering, as if whispering words of affection.
He didn’t know if Chi Zhuo’s heartbeat had quickened when kissing his glands, because he’d been entirely focused on not getting bitten. But now, a single short question sent a clear flutter through him.
Chi Zhuo prided himself on being a good hunter. After being rejected in that little game, he quickly shifted tactics. A snake hidden in the shadows hunting a cat wasn’t hard, especially when the cat was curious about the snake on the ground, pawing at it.
Everything should have gone smoothly. Even small setbacks weren’t a problem. He was confident—confident that his fangs could paralyze the little cat. Yet in reality, as soon as his tongue flicked across the kitten’s cheek, it was already alert and wary.
Before Chi Zhuo could answer, Ji Chenxi had already reached out to touch the black mamba that had wandered near Chi Zhuo’s neck. Then, with a smooth, effortless motion, he slipped out of Chi Zhuo’s grasp.
He turned and walked away, not forgetting to lift the mineral rose in his hand—the color already cooled—“I’ll take my reward for now.”
Chi Zhuo’s eyes flickered. The mineral rose that had been in his hand was so easily taken with a clever touch.
“Then… thank you for your hospitality, Your Highness.”
