A cold night breeze swirled gently, sending a chill down his back. Ji Chenxi, dressed lightly as usual, rubbed his hands together in response. The chill made his steps quicken slightly without him noticing, and once he realized it, he forced himself to slow down, regaining control over the reflex.
It was a brief moment, and just as Ji Chenxi resumed his previous pace, a presence approached brazenly.
He was slightly surprised; he had thought Chi Zhuo would keep his distance.
Chi Zhuo, who hadn’t persisted in keeping his distance, brought over Ji Chenxi’s coat and draped it over him. “It’s pretty chilly tonight. Your Highness should have brought a coat,” he said.
“I didn’t feel cold when I left. By the way, Chi Zhuo, why are you here? Not continuing your silent flower-guarding act?” Ji Chenxi teased with a smile.
Chi Zhuo remained expressionless, tightening the coat around him. “A flower guard should step in when the delicate flower is in trouble, not just trail behind it.”
Another gust of wind blew, scattering some sand along the path. Ji Chenxi blinked, giving Chi Zhuo a small piece of bad news.
“Some sand got into my eyes.”
Blinking rapidly from the discomfort, his vision quickly blurred with a thin mist of tears. He instinctively reached up to rub his eyes, and the corners quickly turned slightly red.
Before Ji Chenxi could touch his eyes again, Chi Zhuo promptly grabbed his hand, stopping the movement. “There are bacteria on your hands.”
Half-closing one eye, Ji Chenxi didn’t pull his hand away but frowned instead. “My eyes hurt.”
“I’ll check them for you.”
Warm breath brushed against his cheek. Ji Chenxi wasn’t sure if Chi Zhuo had noticed the sand in his eyes; all he could focus on was feeling the hand gently holding his eyelids open, the careful, almost fearful movements that seemed worried about hurting him.
Finally, Ji Chenxi’s eyes shed a single tear from the discomfort. His lids were half-closed as it slid down his cheek.
As the tear fell, a kiss landed softly on his face.
“Taking advantage of me, are you?”
Chi Zhuo, using his thumb to wipe away the tear, this time pressed his lips to Ji Chenxi’s. His voice was muffled in the kiss: “Or… maybe you’re just irresistible.”
