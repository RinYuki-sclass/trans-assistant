She hated him. She wanted to escape him—or maybe escape the man who trapped her life.
But she was just a delicate flower in a greenhouse, a bird trapped in a gilded cage. No matter how big the space looked, she was still inside a prison.
Now she was dead. She was free.
If the last person she saw before dying hadn’t been Ji Chenxi, maybe she would’ve been happier.
Time passed. The half-grown child grew into the version Chi Zhuo was familiar with again—but his mind remained stuck at that moment.
At this point, Chi Zhuo broke through the barrier of the little snake, taking human form inside Ji Chenxi’s spiritual domain. The moment he gained a human body, he grabbed Ji Chenxi’s hand tightly, trying to pull him back—but it didn’t work.
No matter what he said, Ji Chenxi couldn’t hear him.
He was trembling, whispering to himself: it’s fine, it’s fine—even if the person he cared about didn’t love him, it was fine. He was calm. He didn’t care. He had already guessed it, hadn’t he?
But the more he forced himself not to care, the more the woman’s broken screams echoed in his ears.
“I knew it… I knew it—you’re just like him. Disgusting.”
“You’re his child. You really are the same. I actually thought you might be different… haha… how ridiculous!”
“Stop laughing. Stop laughing already.”
Why did it sound more like crying than laughter?
Why did someone so gentle and elegant become like this?
Ji Chenxi’s heart pounded painfully. Wave after wave of stabbing pain blurred the line between illusion and reality.
“Don’t cry, Mother. I’ll become what you want me to be,” he murmured.
His smile was soft—but stiff, forced, unnatural. Like someone who had never smiled before trying to shape their lips into something human. Strange. Off. Almost terrifying.
His face was pale, silver hair falling messily around him. The darkest color on him was the blood-red bite on his lip and those deep blue eyes—too bright, too haunting.
Blood stained his lips, adding a strange beauty to him. His gaze was gentle and careful on the surface, but underneath it was dead silence… and fragility that could shatter at any second.
And once he did shatter, his entire spiritual domain would collapse with him.
