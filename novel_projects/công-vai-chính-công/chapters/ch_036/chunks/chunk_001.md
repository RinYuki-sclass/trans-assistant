Two weeks flew by in the blink of an eye. On the eve of their meeting, the little temporary group the three of them had set up unexpectedly buzzed with activity.
Manye Zhe: [Oh man, we’re finally going to see our mysterious Bai Wei! I’m so excited I couldn’t sleep on the starship. Bai Wei, do you want me to secretly tell you Que Qi’s real identity? You’d never guess.]
Bai Wei: [I’m looking forward to meeting both of you too.]
Que Qi’s reply carried a sharp hint of annoyance: [Manye, you’re acting all hyped up now. Let’s see if you can keep that up when you actually meet Bai Wei.]
Manye Zhe: [Huh? Wasn’t I excited when I met you back then?!]
Que Qi: […Are you seriously saying that without feeling guilty?]
Manye Zhe: [What guilt? I have no guilt at all!]
What followed was a flood of messages from Manye Zhe, a long string of texts all trying to explain that he had been too nervous the first time he met Que Qi. Now, with experience on his side, he was absolutely sure he’d do better this time.
Que Qi ignored him entirely this round and sent their own message instead: [Lu Churan, from the Twelfth Fairy Star, female omega. Given your control over StarNet, you must already know my real identity.]
Bai Wei: [I’ve long heard of Miss Lu’s reputation.]
Coincidentally, this was exactly the wealthy omega with the freesia pheromones that Su Yu had mentioned earlier.
Ji Chenxi and Que Qi had known each other for years. During the division of their families’ power, he and his online friends had quietly lent support more than once. Even though he was one of the people who helped her seize authority back then, he couldn’t help but admire this female omega’s skill and cunning.
Just as Ji Chenxi was about to reveal his own identity in advance, so they wouldn’t be caught off guard, Manye Zhe’s messages had already popped up at lightning speed.
Manye Zhe: [Bai Wei, don’t tell them who you are! If you reveal your identity too soon, half the fun of meeting in person is gone! At least leave me a little room to imagine.]
And in the next moment, Que Qi’s private message also landed in Ji Chenxi’s inbox.
[He’s seen a blurry image of you and, misled by Morning Star, thought you were an omega. Don’t tell him—let me see if his eternally expressionless face changes when he realizes Bai Wei isn’t an omega.]
Ji Chenxi chuckled. He hadn’t expected Que Qi to be this mischievous.
Since both of them were asking for it, he didn’t want to spoil the fun either. He just said he’d bring a single white rose as a symbol of his identity when the time came.
Earlier, Su Yu had been reminding him that Gu Hechuan was about to return. Ji Chenxi thought it would be soon, but in reality, the day before the auction, Gu Hechuan still hadn’t come back.
Ji Chenxi first tried contacting him through normal channels. Less than two seconds after the call went through, Gu Hechuan picked up, his voice low and hoarse: “Chenxi, is something wrong?”
