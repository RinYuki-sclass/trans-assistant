‘Bai Anran spoke coldly to the dark-haired man in front of him. The man carried an invisible alpha pressure, one that made most people feel uneasy or even afraid in his presence. But Bai Anran had noticed long ago that when Chi Zhuo listened to him, he slightly lowered his head and leaned in closer—a gesture of genuine attention.’
Having just read that book, even this small scene immediately triggered corresponding words in Ji Chenxi’s mind.
His mood soured instantly.
Even if he had stopped their first meeting at the banquet, would they still end up meeting? It seemed as though, even without his guidance, Bai Anran would have come to this auction anyway.
Su Yu had come with Ji Chenxi and noticed Chi Zhuo with Bai Anran as well. Raising an eyebrow slightly, he first looked at his good friend—only to see Ji Chenxi’s face entirely calm, with not a trace of negative emotion, just a faint smile at the corner of his lips.
“You… I can barely figure you out anymore,” Su Yu said, vaguely. But Ji Chenxi understood perfectly.
The smile on Ji Chenxi’s face deepened. He even greeted Bai Anran from afar, “Anran.”
A soft, affectionate tone. To the person beside Bai Anran, Ji Chenxi only gave a polite nod.
Ji Chenxi had always called Bai Anran this way since they were kids. No one else, like Su Yu, thought it strange. Only Chi Zhuo’s eyes darkened slightly. He whispered something to Bai Anran, then left with another man following behind him.
Before Ji Chenxi could speak, Su Yu eagerly asked, “Little Anran, so you know Chi Zhuo?”
Bai Anran felt a little awkward. “Not really. I was supposed to accompany my cousin to the auction, but she couldn’t make it last minute. By chance, we ran into Mr. Chi, and she asked him to escort me.”
Ji Chenxi nodded slightly. So this was the convergence of fate?
Even if he tried to interfere, what was meant to happen would still happen.
And that was fine—everything was about to become far more interesting.
Once inside the venue, Ji Chenxi briefly parted ways with Su Yu and made his way to the secret spot he had arranged beforehand with Manye Zhe and Que Qi, ready for their meeting.
Leaning lazily against the back of a sofa, Ji Chenxi lowered his head and gently inhaled the scent of the pristine white rose in his hand, still glistening with dew.
“The white rose in the Third Prince’s hand is really beautiful.”
A soft voice came from behind.
Ji Chenxi couldn’t help but laugh.
He hadn’t met his online friends yet—but he had already run into Chi Zhuo, returning unexpectedly.
