Ji Chenxi honestly had no idea where that kind of confidence came from. He was speechless for a moment.
Chi Zhuo seemed to truly believe he was being pretty low-key. He didn’t quite understand Ji Chenxi’s concern, but he respected it and still gave an answer. “If you want, I can be even more discreet.”
Ji Chenxi finally asked the most critical question between them. “You want to sleep with me, and I want to sleep with you. I’m not going to back off just because there are more layers to our relationship.”
What he really meant was—maybe they should just forget it.
Chi Zhuo hummed in response, took one of Ji Chenxi’s hands, and gently toyed with it. “We can take this slow.”
Taking it slow was nothing more than testing the other person’s limits.
Ji Chenxi understood that perfectly, which was why he tightened his grip around Chi Zhuo’s hand instead. “Then let’s take it slow.”
Predators were always like this—conquering, chasing, forcing the other person to yield beneath them.
Winners become kings; losers become nothing. That’s how it’s supposed to be.
