A thin slice of sunlight slipped through the heavy curtains outside the window.
On the bed sat a handsome young man with silver hair, brows knit as he mulled something over, glancing now and then at the person beside him.
Under that steady gaze, the sleeping man finally opened his eyes.
“Forget it. Nothing happened yesterday.” Ji Chenxi frowned, wearing a look that clearly said he planned to deny everything.
Chi Zhuo woke up fast. In just the short moment it took to hear those words, he’d already figured out the situation. The rush and impulse from the susceptible period had faded completely. It was obvious—the little prince regretted it.
Chi Zhuo pulled the blanket up a bit and stayed right there, wrapped in bedding filled with Ji Chenxi’s scent, acting like he hadn’t heard a thing.
“Staying quiet won’t solve anything,” Ji Chenxi said, unhappy.
“Since Your Highness slept with me, I naturally have to take responsibility,” Chi Zhuo replied calmly. “Eat and run is scummy behavior. Your Highness shouldn’t learn bad habits like that.”
Ji Chenxi had already prepared himself to play the role of a scumbag, but Chi Zhuo’s words still made a vein twitch at his temple. “First of all, who exactly did I sleep with? What did I eat? We’re just chatting under the blanket—do I still have to take responsibility for that?”
Chi Zhuo sat up from the warm covers. As he moved, the blanket slid down, revealing his bare chest—marked all over with clear, intimate traces, like solid proof laid out in the open.
If this were a piece of candy, even if Ji Chenxi hadn’t swallowed it whole, he’d definitely licked it more than a few times. Trying to claim he’d had no improper thoughts about it was basically impossible.
Susceptible periods really mess people up!
Just as Ji Chenxi was about to force out an explanation, Chi Zhuo hooked a finger under his chin and leaned in, pressing a warm kiss to his face.
The moment the kiss landed, Chi Zhuo added softly, “How does this not count as eating it, Your Highness?”
In the silence, Ji Chenxi’s face slowly, unmistakably turned red.
Before he could finish, Chi Zhuo had already lifted a long, well-shaped finger and pressed it lightly to Ji Chenxi’s lips.
“Even if Your Highness plans to deny it, that won’t work anymore.”
Ji Chenxi had a slight headache now. “Can’t we really just let this go?”
“…Then can you at least keep it low-key?”
Ji Chenxi was fully prepared—if Chi Zhuo dared say “no” again, he’d just lean into the scumbag role completely. But instead, Chi Zhuo looked genuinely confused. “Am I not low-key enough already?”
