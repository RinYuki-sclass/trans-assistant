The sounds coming from inside weren’t loud, but they were more than enough to feel suggestive, the kind that easily sent the mind drifting in the wrong direction.
When Chi Zhuo walked over, he didn’t bother hiding his footsteps. As long as the person in the bathroom was still awake, there was no way he wouldn’t notice Chi Zhuo approaching. Yet the soft, faintly intimate breathing inside continued without pause.
For a moment, Chi Zhuo found himself stuck—leaving felt wrong, but going in didn’t feel right either.
He pressed his lips together, about to pretend none of this was happening, when the sound from inside grew a little heavier than before. There was a hint of pleasure to it, mixed with a lazy ease, the drawn-out ending making it far too easy to imagine things.
Chi Zhuo froze mid-step.
He could clearly hear the faint sound of water spilling over, along with that light panting that, wrapped in steam, carried an unmistakable sense of desire.
Letting out a slow breath, Chi Zhuo turned back and placed his hand on the door handle. The person inside seemed completely unaware—or maybe just didn’t care—not making the slightest move to stop him.
With a gentle twist, the unlocked bathroom door opened.
Chi Zhuo pushed the door open without hesitation, opening it wide. And Ji Chenxi, soaking in the tub to ease himself, showed himself just as openly.
The corners of Ji Chenxi’s eyes were tinged red with desire. When his half-lidded gaze lifted to meet Chi Zhuo’s, there was a hazy, unfocused look in his eyes. But the movements of his hand were slow and careless, more like going through the motions than truly into it.
Outside was the pitch-black night. Inside, a dim yellow night light glowed softly. In the milky-white bathtub, Ji Chenxi’s skin was flushed red from the heat, warmed further by the gentle light. Just one look was enough to make someone’s heart stir and their throat tighten.
“What are you doing?” Chi Zhuo asked, his voice low.
Ji Chenxi kept lazily moving his hand, his voice carrying a teasing, almost hooked kind of intimacy.
“I think it’s pretty obvious what I’m doing.”
That relaxed, casual tone carried a trace of irritation, like someone who just couldn’t quite get what they wanted.
“Then hurry up and finish,” Chi Zhuo said. “Come back and sleep.”
The Chi Zhuo who had acted on impulse lowered his gaze, looking toward that spot blurred by rippling water and soft light, then quickly pulled his eyes away, clearly uneasy.
Ji Chenxi let out a soft laugh, as if mocking Chi Zhuo’s half-hearted attempt to hide it, or maybe laughing at himself.
“But I can’t,” he said lightly. “I can’t get there.”
Chi Zhuo: “…Is this really something you should be saying to a guest who’s just crashing here?”
