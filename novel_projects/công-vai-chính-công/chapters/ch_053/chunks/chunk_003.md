In the end, they still took another shower before heading back to bed. Ji Chenxi was the one rummaging through the closet, trying to find clothes for Chi Zhuo, who was wrapped in a bathrobe.
That earlier outfit had already used up all of Ji Chenxi’s luck. A couple of days ago, he’d cleared out all the clothes he’d never worn. New ones were supposed to arrive soon, but for some unknown reason, that shipment never came. What was left was either the wrong size or clothes Ji Chenxi had already worn.
After looking around, he turned to Chi Zhuo and let him pick for himself.
Chi Zhuo didn’t really care about things like this. He casually grabbed a set that looked comfortable enough for sleeping—only to notice a small snow leopard embroidered on it. Not just that, even the details on it were kind of cute.
And Ji Chenxi had definitely worn this before.
Seeing Chi Zhuo choose it, Ji Chenxi kept his face straight, though there was a slight hint of awkwardness.
By the time they both lay down on the bed, it was already late.
Having another alpha’s scent show up in one’s private space usually made most alphas restless and uneasy, but Ji Chenxi took it surprisingly well. After all, there were still fresh bite marks on Chi Zhuo’s neck and shoulder, left by him not long ago.
And Chi Zhuo’s body carried a strong, clean rose scent.
Just as Ji Chenxi was drifting toward sleep, he suddenly heard the man beside him speak in a low voice.
“Your Highness, if you really want that position, I can help you get it.”
“Oh?” Ji Chenxi replied with his eyes closed. “So Brother Chi Zhuo is planning to keep me? Push me up the ranks?”
“Keep you?” Chi Zhuo repeated the word, his eyes darkening as clear disapproval showed through. “You’re so good with words—can’t you use something nicer to describe what’s between us?”
Ji Chenxi slowly opened his eyes, rolled over, and faced him.
“Between us? What kind of relationship?” he asked. “Bed buddies?”
“If that’s how Your Highness wants to put it, that’s fine,” Chi Zhuo said. “Since we’re bed buddies, doesn’t that mean I can do a few things that fit that kind of relationship?”
His teasing was thrown right back at him, yet Ji Chenxi didn’t look flustered at all. His fingers lightly traced over Chi Zhuo’s chest before he leaned in, pressed a kiss to the spot near his neck and collarbone, and applied just a bit of force, leaving behind a clear mark.
“Your Highness… what exactly are you doing?” Chi Zhuo slid an arm around Ji Chenxi’s waist, his voice sinking lower and lower.
Ji Chenxi didn’t even lift his head, replying vaguely, “Fulfilling my duty.”
Chi Zhuo let out a low chuckle, curious to see just how far this so-called duty would go. In the end, though, Ji Chenxi was at most like a pup that hadn’t quite been weaned yet—kissing here, nibbling there, but nothing beyond that.
