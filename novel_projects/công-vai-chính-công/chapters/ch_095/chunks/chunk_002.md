Darkness fell, and sleep rushed back over him.
“Your Highness, go to sleep. Do you want me to wake you up at noon for lunch?”
“Then I’ll only get a bit over an hour of sleep.”
“Should I let you wake up naturally?”
“I still need to go to the meeting,” Ji Chenxi continued to nitpick.
“Then I’ll pick a suitable time to wake you. Maybe you’ll wake up on your own right on time anyway.” Chi Zhuo’s voice grew softer and softer, gradually carrying a gentle tone. It was the kind of voice perfect for sleep—paired with a bedtime story, it would be even better.
Sure enough, Ji Chenxi, who had just been struggling to keep talking, slowly closed his eyes. Just as Chi Zhuo was about to pull away, his hand was suddenly grabbed.
“Hm? Your Highness, what is it? Don’t tell me you want a bedtime story?”
“I don’t need a bedtime story, but a snake that doesn’t hibernate can still nap with me.” As he spoke, Ji Chenxi had already pulled Chi Zhuo onto the bed.
Chi Zhuo practically fell right onto him. Shaking his head helplessly, he said, “Your Highness really isn’t worried about whether I have things to do.”
“Didn’t you stay up with me last night? Whatever needed to be handled should already be done. If it’s not, then it must not be that important. Compared to those things, I’m obviously more important. So, sleep with me.”
“Your Highness is really domineering.”
“You could also say I’m willful, spoiled, and unreasonable. But I’m not like this with just anyone. Doesn’t being treated specially make you a little happy?”
Chi Zhuo smiled.
He hadn’t even changed his clothes yet, so naturally he couldn’t just sleep like that beside Ji Chenxi. After successfully persuading him, Chi Zhuo went to take a shower first, dried his hair, and kept all his movements light. When he came back, Ji Chenxi had indeed already fallen asleep.
Not wanting to wake him, Chi Zhuo picked up that special book and sat beside him. He had only just opened it when a warm presence moved closer and wrapped around his waist.
“Did I wake you?” Chi Zhuo asked in a whisper.
Ji Chenxi wasn’t fully awake. He mumbled a couple of words and said, “Don’t read that book.”
“Hm? Is there something special about it? Not allowed to read?”
“It’s just a book full of nonsense. What’s there to look at?”
