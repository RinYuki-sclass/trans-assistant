It wasn’t strange at all that Chen Xing wanted to meet Ji Chenxi. If the other party hadn’t come looking for him, that would’ve been the weird part. After all, Ji Chenxi had first gone into a sensitive period triggered by an omega’s heat, and then he disappeared for several days. Both of them were on the Imperial Star, so Chen Xing should’ve met him long ago. Yet all their communication during this time had only been a few short exchanges online.
At first, it could be excused as him having just woken up and not being in a good state to meet, so as to avoid unnecessary trouble. But now, it was actually the perfect time to meet.
Chen Xing’s identity was that of a young professor who had just been hired by the Imperial Royal Academy with a high salary. The most suitable place for them to meet was naturally the Imperial Royal Academy. It just so happened that although there were three days of balls after Ji Zhiqing’s birthday, those events were all held at night, so they wouldn’t interfere with their meeting.
The Imperial Royal Academy—no matter how much it had been infiltrated—was still a place where the Empire trained its talents. It wasn’t like Ji Chenxi would be afraid to show up at a meeting there.
After learning that this “friend” was Chen Xing, Chi Zhuo didn’t ask Ji Chenxi anything more, but he already had a clear idea in his mind. When he saw the invitation now, he frowned first, then said, “I’ll go with you.”
“There’s no need to make it that troublesome. Inside the academy, Chen Xing can’t really do anything.”
“Your Highness, you still need to be cautious,” Chi Zhuo reminded.
Inside the academy, it wasn’t that nothing could be done.
“At this timing…” Ji Chenxi said, then suddenly laughed. “I just tested Chen Xing not long ago. He’s a very smart person. That little test was enough to let him know that I’ve already figured out something. With his personality, he’ll either clear my suspicion, or choose to lay everything out and talk about it openly.”
Back then, Ji Chenxi thought Chen Xing was trying to brush the matter aside, but looking at this invitation now, it felt more like he wanted to dispel that bit of suspicion. Still, Ji Chenxi had a strange feeling—it wasn’t just a simple test and back-and-forth.
The timing was too perfect. It was right before the day of action. Rather than a meeting to talk things out, it felt more like a direct and open showdown.
After finishing breakfast, Ji Chenxi didn’t rush to report to the academy. Instead, he took a leave and buried himself in his blanket.
“Not going to class?” Chi Zhuo teased.
“I can just flip through the books later and catch up. Instead of going to class, I’d rather get some proper rest.” Ji Chenxi half-closed his eyes, looking like he might fall asleep any second.
“You should’ve rested properly yesterday and dealt with things after waking up.”
Lying under the blanket, Ji Chenxi slightly raised his eyes. “Brother Chi Zhuo, time doesn’t wait for anyone. It might not look that different, but sometimes just a few hours’ delay can make a huge difference, right?”
Chi Zhuo nodded in agreement. Opportunities were fleeting; people really had to seize them themselves.
“Little snake, come hibernate with me.” Sleepy, Ji Chenxi patted the spot beside him.
“First of all, I’m a snake that doesn’t hibernate. Second, it’s still a bit early for hibernation.”
Ji Chenxi laughed. He forced himself to stay awake, wanting to joke around a bit more, but then a hand covered his eyes.
