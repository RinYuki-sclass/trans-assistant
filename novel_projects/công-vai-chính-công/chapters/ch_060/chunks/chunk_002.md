They chatted loosely, back and forth, until the estate came closer into view. Ji Chenxi then casually said, “I heard the Royal Academy is planning a month-long trial starting in August, inviting top graduates to come and really put us freshmen through the wringer.”
“Huh? Why are you telling me this all of a sudden?”
Ji Chenxi raised an eyebrow, confident Chi Zhuo had picked up on the hint. “Just thought I’d mention it, since soon we’ll be out of touch for a month.”
This time, Chi Zhuo couldn’t pretend he didn’t understand. He nodded and said, “Alright, got it.”
Ji Chenxi liked to drop hints and leave them hanging. Mentioning it once was already a signal; whether Chi Zhuo would actually go or not was entirely up to him.
The two walked in easy silence all the way to the estate gates.
By now, they were very close to the vast Rose Estate. Normally, Chi Zhuo should have stopped and let Ji Chenxi walk the rest alone—after all, the closer they were, the greater the risk of being seen—but he accompanied him right up to the door.
“You’ve got me confused—are you trying to be low-key or high-profile?” Ji Chenxi shook his head with a sigh.
“It just shows I’d rather get you home than stay low-key,” Chi Zhuo said.
If anyone else had said that, Ji Chenxi would’ve thought it was some cheesy line. But coming from Chi Zhuo, it was almost certainly what he genuinely meant. After all, this was a man who rarely wasted words on lies.
A flicker of emotion passed through him, and he smiled. “Just sending me home?”
“Yes, just sending you home.”
Ji Chenxi tilted his head slightly, pretending to ponder. “Oh, so all you want is to send me home, huh, Brother Chi Zhuo?”
Chi Zhuo felt a subtle weight behind those words again.
Ji Chenxi could be blunt at times. He asked directly, “Want to stay with me? It’s not the first time you’ve spent the night anyway.”
Chi Zhuo was a careful, deliberate man. Today he had successfully confessed; the joy was piled high, and sending someone home was already one of the things a boyfriend was supposed to do. Staying over was clearly not on the list. Yet, in that moment, he wavered—more than wanting to go home, he wanted to stay with Ji Chenxi.
Just as Chi Zhuo almost opened his mouth to agree, Ji Chenxi suddenly shifted tone, waving goodbye with a smile. “Just kidding. I’m off, Brother Chi Zhuo. Thanks for taking the trouble to walk me home.”
Chi Zhuo, nearly caught off guard, felt no irritation at being teased—just a mixture of helplessness and affection. “Alright, see you next time.”
He watched Ji Chenxi walk away, but as the other turned to leave, Ji Chenxi suddenly ran back, caught Chi Zhuo’s chin, and pressed a brief, gentle kiss on his lips.
After the kiss, he laughed. “Almost forgot—the parting kiss.”
