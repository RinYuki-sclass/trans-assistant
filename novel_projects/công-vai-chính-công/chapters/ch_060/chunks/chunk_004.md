[Got it, understood.]
Ji Chenxi wanted to reply further. He didn’t want to reveal too much; otherwise, he could have taken them all out in a few swift moves. Unfortunately, the spiritual energy he had sent out before was minimal, and after just a few exchanges, their connection was cut off.
Ji Chenxi clicked his tongue lightly and struck with lightning speed.
He didn’t go all-out; instead, he pulled and took them out in small groups, handling three at a time. It didn’t look abrupt at all—it actually seemed natural.
By the time Chi Zhuo arrived, he caught Ji Chenxi finishing off the last of them. The silver-haired alpha looked insanely handsome and wild after defeating everyone. His gaze toward Chi Zhuo was pure danger. “You’re here for the points too?”
Before Chi Zhuo could answer, Ji Chenxi lunged straight at him. Chi Zhuo dodged with a twisting, unpredictable motion, but Ji Chenxi managed to land a perfect kick, shattering the recorder drone behind him.
The fierce, untamed expression on Ji Chenxi’s face melted into a smile almost instantly. “How did you know I wanted to go after the recorder behind you?”
“Because there are already a bunch of recorders scattered on the ground, it wasn’t hard to guess. But the commotion’s a bit loud—probably more will come soon.”
Noticing the fine sweat on Chi Zhuo’s forehead, Ji Chenxi asked, “Running over here?”
Chi Zhuo nodded.
Ji Chenxi laughed again. “I really—love you to death, Brother Chi Zhuo—”
Chi Zhuo’s calm expression faltered. In the summer sunset, the silver-haired youth was all smiles, his body bathed in a warm, golden glow that made hearts skip a beat. Chi Zhuo’s own heartbeat went haywire, completely out of control.
He took a deep breath, trying to calm himself, but the tips of his ears betrayed him with a faint flush. Every ounce of self-control melted against a single confession from the boy. He wanted to sound authoritative, to speak like an older, composed man—but all that came out was a low, muffled “Mm,” unsure if it was a response or an acknowledgment.
“…That’s the worried look you give me, huh?” Ji Chenxi added between breaths, finally finishing his teasing with a laugh.
Chi Zhuo: “…”
Feeling completely baited, his brain practically staging a revolt, Chi Zhuo turned on his heel and walked away.
