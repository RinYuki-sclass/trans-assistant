“Little Rose, you’re such a gentle child. What will you do if someone hurts you in the future?”
The fragments of this dream passed quickly, but he no longer wanted to watch. Little Ji Chenxi wasn’t as innocent as the woman imagined—every action he took was calculated, every move designed to weave a beautiful, unwithering dream for her.
But that dream inevitably shattered. She saw through him, saw his true self.
Like a rose at its peak, the woman lost control for the first time. She shoved him away and repeated over and over, “You’re just like him.”
Confused and helpless, he watched as that true rose slowly withered before his eyes.
Gentle, huh? Hah!
The other person was the truest definition of gentle—but the gentler someone was, the deeper they could wound.
The night dragged on, bone-chilling and endless. Even with the snow leopard curled beside him and the fire flickering in front, Ji Chenxi still felt cold.
The biting chill made him restless, and he caught the slightest movement from Chi Zhuo immediately.
The snow leopard’s ears twitched, and it opened its eyes along with its master.
Ji Chenxi tilted his head to look and saw Chi Zhuo leaning against the cave wall. By most standards, Chi Zhuo was a very alpha presence—just one look made him feel dominant and strong. Ji Chenxi hadn’t paid much attention before, but now, seeing Chi Zhuo flushed unnaturally, there was an unexpected hunger in his expression.
Ji Chenxi frowned and moved closer, intending to touch Chi Zhuo’s forehead—but his hand was immediately caught by the very eyes-closed person.
A hand, unnaturally hot, gripped his wrist tightly. Chi Zhuo’s eyes opened, his lowered gaze tracing Ji Chenxi’s hand upward as he called out a name.
“Ji Chenxi.”
Ji Chenxi responded casually.
By now, he was certain—this wasn’t a fever caused by pheromones. Chi Zhuo was genuinely burning up.
His black hair was damp, and his normally sharp, cold gaze was hazy. That haze, combined with the fever, made him almost intoxicating, the heat turning into raw, unrestrained desire.
Ji Chenxi’s frown deepened. He lightly shook the wrist he was being held by and said coldly, “Let go.”
The hand didn’t loosen—instead, it gripped tighter, drawing the slight coolness from Ji Chenxi’s hand.
Looking down at him, Ji Chenxi repeated, “You’re making me a little sore here. If you don’t—”
