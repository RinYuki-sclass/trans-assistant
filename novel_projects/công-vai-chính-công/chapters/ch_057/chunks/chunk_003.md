“No.” Chi Zhuo shook his head. “I just thought giving a small planet with a not-so-great location might feel a bit cheap. If Your Highness has something you like, you can tell me. If not, then I’ll just figure it out myself.”
“Once a gift is given, there’s no taking it back.” Ji Chenxi said calmly. “I like shattered-star roses.”
Chi Zhuo’s expression stayed steady. “As long as Your Highness likes it.”
They said gifts should go both ways. Just taking things without giving anything back didn’t sit right with Ji Chenxi. He started thinking about what to give Chi Zhuo, running through everything, even digging through their time together for clues. But no matter how he looked, he couldn’t find anything Chi Zhuo clearly liked. Even that black-covered book about Chi Zhuo dating Bai Anran hadn’t given him a single hint.
It was like Chi Zhuo didn’t need anything at all—he only needed to give.
Ji Chenxi wasn’t the type to overthink on his own, so he went straight to the point. “Brother Chi Zhuo, is there anything you like?”
“There is.”
Ji Chenxi raised his brow again, signaling he was listening.
“I think I’ve made it pretty clear that I like Your Highness.” Chi Zhuo said. “Did you really not notice?”
“You mean you want me?” Ji Chenxi broke into a grin. “Brother Chi Zhuo, don’t say things like that. I won’t be able to stop myself from wanting to—”
“Wanting to what?”
Ji Chenxi dragged out the last word, his tone lifting playfully. “Kiss you to death~”
Chi Zhuo froze for a moment, then lifted a hand and rubbed Ji Chenxi’s head.
Ji Chenxi caught that tiny flicker of surprise and leaned close to his ear, whispering, “Disappointed?”
Chi Zhuo’s lips parted, just about to answer, when Su Yu’s voice suddenly rang out from a distance—loud and dramatic, so loud it clearly didn’t care about anyone else’s well-being at all.
Ji Chenxi lowered his gaze and glanced at the light-brain on his wrist. Sure enough, it was already flooded with messages from Su Yu, plus several missed calls. Su Yu was always the type to spam messages, so Ji Chenxi had turned on Do Not Disturb. If it were just Su Yu, that’d be fine—but the problem was that Gu Hechuan had also messaged him to ask what was going on.
Ji Chenxi sighed. He’d lost track of time. Even if this was some hidden easter egg, he’d been gone way too long.
He really hadn’t expected that one day he’d end up getting intimate with someone inside a creepy, ghost-filled haunted house. They’d already delayed things enough, and now that Su Yu was looking for him, Ji Chenxi simply waved at Chi Zhuo. “Alright, I’m heading out. See you next time.”
“Wait. There’s something for you.”
Ji Chenxi turned back and lowered his eyes to Chi Zhuo’s hand. Lying there was a small red-and-black key. “So this is the key item that lured me over?”
