“Yeah. Just say you spent a ton of effort and brainpower to get it.”
Ji Chenxi nodded seriously. “Absolutely. I even used a beauty trap.”
Before Chi Zhuo could reply, Ji Chenxi had already started walking away. His figure quickly disappeared into the darkness.
Su Yu’s shouting—so loud it clearly didn’t care about the lives of other visitors—only lasted a few more calls before stopping. Obviously, the haunted house staff had stepped in and shut him down.
By the time Ji Chenxi returned to the group, Su Yu was negotiating with one of the staff members dressed as a ghost. “No, bro, listen—my friend got dragged away by your coworkers. You seriously don’t know where he went? Don’t tell me this isn’t some hidden event your haunted house set up?”
The ghost Su Yu had grabbed was already beyond annoyed and could only answer, “We do have hidden events. He’ll probably regroup with you later. And also, sir, the way you chase ghosts around and interrogate them really hurts a ghost’s feelings. Do I really look that fake?”
“I can smell your alpha pheromones leaking.”
The poor ghost gave up on talking altogether. Was that even a reason? What alpha didn’t have a scent?
While Su Yu was busy harassing ghosts, Gu Hechuan and Lu Tu had already spotted Ji Chenxi.
Gu Hechuan gave Ji Chenxi a brief nod. Lu Tu, on the other hand, was much more enthusiastic. The red-haired alpha wore a careless grin, slung an arm over Ji Chenxi’s shoulder the moment he came over, acting like they were close buddies.
“So, Third Highness,” Lu Tu said, “what did you wander off to do?”
Ji Chenxi casually slapped the hand off his shoulder, then opened his palm to show the key resting there. “Went to find this. Took a bit of time.”
“Oh, so you were hunting for the key item.” Lu Tu’s tone shifted slightly. “But… doesn’t Your Highness’s lips look a little red?”
Ji Chenxi didn’t take it to heart and replied with a smile, “My lips have always been red.”
While talking, he waved over in the other direction. Su Yu finally stopped tormenting the poor “ghost” staff member and came running over.
“Little Rose, I thought you got kidnapped by some bad guy.” Su Yu went straight in for a bear hug, but Ji Chenxi dodged it just in time.
“Cut it out. It’s not that dramatic. I just went to get the key item—and triggered a small easter egg on the way.”
Sure enough, Su Yu’s attention was quickly pulled elsewhere. But the look coming from behind, from Lu Tu, didn’t ease up much at all.
With the key item secured, and none of them being the type to scare easily, the rest of the haunted house went smoothly. During a brief pause right before they were about to exit, Lu Tu once again drifted over to Ji Chenxi’s side.
“Something you need?” Ji Chenxi asked quietly.
