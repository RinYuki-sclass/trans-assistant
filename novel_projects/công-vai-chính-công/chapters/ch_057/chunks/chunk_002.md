Who would’ve thought that after all this time, Ji Chenxi still hadn’t forgotten that old script of his.
“Who’s the boyfriend?” Chi Zhuo already knew the answer, but he still asked.
“Chi Zhuo,” Ji Chenxi replied without the slightest shame. “Fresh off the line.”
“Oh, him? Don’t worry about him. Just stick with me.”
Having the other play along like that, Ji Chenxi couldn’t hold back his laugh. “That won’t do. I still have to care a little.”
Green himself or whatever—Chi Zhuo took a deep breath, pulled him in tighter, and the kisses turned firm, sharp, and full of push, completely sealing Ji Chenxi’s lips before he could say anything else.
Ji Chenxi blinked, then pushed at Chi Zhuo’s chest with a bit more effort this time. After forcing him back, before he could speak, Chi Zhuo had already pressed his thumb against his lips. “Shh, Your Highness. I’m not into playing some sneaky affair game with you.”
“What are you even thinking?” Ji Chenxi laughed out loud this time. His hand looked lazy, but it didn’t allow refusal as he pushed that thumb away. “Stop kissing me. If you keep going, there really will be marks. I was just trying to remind you we’re still in the haunted house. Didn’t expect Brother Chi Zhuo’s head to already be full of all this stuff. Wow, really couldn’t tell.”
For a moment, Chi Zhuo wasn’t sure if he felt more amused or more annoyed. “Your Highness really knows how to take all the credit.”
“Easy, easy. Brother Chi Zhuo praising me like that—I’ll get embarrassed.” Ji Chenxi replied with a grin, acting like he hadn’t heard the sarcasm at all.
“I don’t see even a hint of embarrassment.” Chi Zhuo said so, but after that, he only held Ji Chenxi and didn’t kiss him again. “Actually, when I confessed, there was another gift I planned to give Your Highness.”
“What is it?”
“A small planet.”
Ji Chenxi was a little surprised. “Why would you suddenly think of giving me a planet?”
As far as he knew, he’d never shown any special love for planets.
“It just felt right.” Chi Zhuo said. “The scenery’s nice, though the location’s average, so it’s hard to turn into a tourist spot. The only real highlight is that the whole planet is planted with shattered-star roses. That kind of rose feels like it suits Your Highness perfectly.”
“Shattered-star roses? Brother Chi Zhuo, that’s a pretty big move.”
Ji Chenxi’s voice carried a smile, like he had no clue what that planet really meant to Chi Zhuo. But the truth was, he knew very well. That black-covered book had mentioned it in passing—Chi Zhuo’s very first planet was a small one perfect for growing shattered-star roses. It was that planet that helped him rake in his first huge pile of money.
“So,” Chi Zhuo asked, “do you want it?”
“If I say no, are you just not giving it to me?” Ji Chenxi raised a brow.
