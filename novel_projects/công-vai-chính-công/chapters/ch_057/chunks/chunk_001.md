“A little innocent?”
Ji Chenxi nodded with a straight face. “Yeah. What, do I not look like it?”
“You do.” Chi Zhuo laughed, the sound low and pressed into his throat, vibrating through his whole chest. “Then let this little innocent one give me a kiss.”
As he spoke, Chi Zhuo leaned in.
Ji Chenxi tilted his head slightly. The kiss that should’ve landed on his lips brushed past his cheek instead.
Chi Zhuo froze for a split second and was about to move again, but before his lips could reach him, Ji Chenxi had already looped an arm around his neck and gave him a quick peck. “Must’ve been hard for our Brother Chi Zhuo to lie through his teeth. Here, a reward kiss for you.”
“So His Highness doesn’t see himself that way, huh?”
“Whose ‘pure and innocent’ acts like me?” Ji Chenxi leaned back against the wall and shot back.
Chi Zhuo pretended to think, stepping a little closer. The distance between them had already been small, but now it shrank to the point where they could clearly feel each other’s breath on their faces. In that overly intimate space, Chi Zhuo lifted a finger and lightly tapped Ji Chenxi’s chest, then said something that sounded almost like a love line, “Mine.”
If it were just a sweet line, Ji Chenxi might’ve brushed it off. But Chi Zhuo said it so seriously, like he truly meant it.
Ji Chenxi clicked his tongue, acting like he didn’t care at all—yet the faint red creeping up his ears gave him away.
Chi Zhuo raised a hand and touched his ear. “Blushing?”
“No. It’s just hot.” Ji Chenxi said stubbornly.
“Just hot, huh?” Chi Zhuo helped straighten his clothes. “Want me to help you take them off, Your Highness?”
Ji Chenxi sucked in a sharp breath and accused him, “Shouldn’t you have let that slide and changed the topic instead?”
Even so, he hooked an arm around Chi Zhuo’s neck again and said casually, “But whatever. So what if I’m shy?”
Faced with that shameless honesty, Chi Zhuo couldn’t help himself. He lowered his head and lightly pecked Ji Chenxi’s lips, then pressed in harder. With the faint sting, Ji Chenxi felt a spark of excitement. He pushed at Chi Zhuo’s chest, looking like he meant to resist, but he didn’t use any strength at all—playing hard to get to the fullest—while still murmuring softly, “Be gentle…”
Chi Zhuo’s eyes darkened, caught by Ji Chenxi’s kitten-like way of refusing and by that line that was flirtatious to the extreme—“Be gentle.” Just as he was about to devour that pretty little rose in one bite, Ji Chenxi spoke again, “Don’t leave any marks.”
Chi Zhuo was willing to meet that small request from his boyfriend. But just when he really did slow down like Ji Chenxi asked, Ji Chenxi’s eyes shifted slightly, and he smiled as he added, “After all, I really like my boyfriend. I don’t want him seeing marks someone else left.”
Chi Zhuo froze hard.
