While everyone was engaged in elegant conversation, Ji Chenxi found an opportunity to quietly slip out of the banquet hall.
With so many hidden threats present, it was hard to say whether Chen Xing and his second sister planned to act ahead of schedule. Ji Chenxi needed to reach the palace’s control center and take control of the system first. As long as he held the central system, he could turn the situation around and increase his chances of winning.
The only problem was that this trip would definitely not go smoothly.
Sure enough, as soon as Ji Chenxi reached the only path leading to the control center, he sensed that he was being watched.
He clicked his tongue. Since the other party hadn’t acted yet, he pretended not to notice.
After walking a bit further, the people lurking in the shadows finally couldn’t hold back. First came a silenced explosive, followed by several smoke bombs. As the smoke spread, many agile figures in black rushed in. Ji Chenxi dodged a weapon aimed at his neck, then swiftly evaded dozens of photon bullets.
He moved incredibly fast—running, dodging, sliding his dagger downward, flipping upward, launching spiritual attacks.
When the smoke cleared, all the black-clad attackers who had surrounded him lay defeated.
Blood dripped from his dagger, but Ji Chenxi had no time to wipe it. He sped toward the control center. Chen Xing also had experts skilled in hacking the star network. If control of the system was seized first, the situation could easily be reversed.
Ji Chenxi moved quickly. Even though many people tried to stop him along the way, he dealt with them swiftly. The cost was that his spiritual energy was being drained rapidly.
When he was only a few hundred meters away from the control center, Ji Chenxi stopped.
A massive mechanical figure stood guard at the entrance. Sitting atop it was a young man. Compared to the others around the machine, this young man felt more alive. Ji Chenxi could even see indifference and an indescribable loneliness in his eyes. As for the others, they had human shapes but didn’t feel human at all. Some had empty, emotionless eyes, while others were fused with strange creatures. The most active among them was a male alpha merged with an octopus.
Ji Chenxi tilted his head at the overwhelming scene before him, then smiled.
“What are you laughing at?” the young man on the giant machine asked.
“Feels like you guys really think highly of me,” Ji Chenxi replied honestly.
The young man didn’t speak this time. He raised his hand slightly and brought it down. Instantly, all those things seemed to receive an order and rushed toward Ji Chenxi.
Everything felt bizarre. Monsters and beauty. Slaughter and justice.
So who was justice? In the end, justice would always be written by the victors.
During the fight, Ji Chenxi displayed strength far beyond what an S-rank should possess. The young man had stayed here precisely to verify this. After getting his answer, he didn’t linger and leapt upward, preparing to leave.
But the moment he moved, a dangerous aura closed in. He reacted quickly, twisting midair, yet still ended up with a deep cut across his face.
