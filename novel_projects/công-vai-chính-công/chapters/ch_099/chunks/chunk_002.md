The young man fell silent. He knew that the one who used to call him “Little Three,” the one who treated him like a real person, was already gone. Even if repaired, it would only be another entity with the same data as Ge You. He just wanted to try. With the same data and the same setup, who could say that wouldn’t still be Ge You?
But at this moment, there should be no rebuttal. The young man lowered his eyes, sincerely apologized, and prepared to leave to continue his work.
“Little Three.”
The familiar nickname made the young man stop in his tracks. He turned back to look at Chen Xing, waiting for what came next.
“There are no banquets in this world that never end. We always grow through partings.” Chen Xing placed the most beautiful rose back among the cluster.
“Boss, I understand.”
Chen Xing smiled faintly and flicked a flame in his hand, setting the entire pile of roses on fire.
“Move the plan forward. Let’s launch a surprise attack tonight.”
Tonight was the second day of the ball. Compared to yesterday, the young men and women had loosened up a lot. Some continued chatting with people they had enjoyed talking to the day before, hoping for deeper interaction. Others, who still hadn’t found someone they liked, began a new round of searching among the crowd.
By the time Ji Chenxi arrived, there were already many people present. The first person he made eye contact with was Ji Zhiqing. The beautiful female alpha raised her glass to him from afar. Ji Chenxi returned a smile. Anyone watching would say the royal family had a harmonious relationship.
Ji Zhiqing was wearing an extremely beautiful red evening gown today. It was the first time Ji Chenxi had seen her wear such a bold and striking color. And today, she seemed to be in a particularly good mood.
Ji Chenxi appeared cold and indifferent, barely interacting with the people around him, but in reality, he was quietly observing the situation.
There were too many people. The scent of perfume mixed with faint pheromones, along with the smell of flowers and alcohol, creating a chaotic atmosphere. Ordinary people might not notice anything wrong, but for Ji Chenxi, whose spiritual strength was exceptional, it wasn’t difficult. The distribution of the palace guards had several suspicious points, and there were also people hidden in the shadows. Was this just scouting in advance, or…
Ji Chenxi, deep in thought, suddenly felt someone slap his shoulder. He wasn’t startled. While quietly scanning with his spiritual power, he had already noticed Su Yu’s arrival. He turned and smiled. “Looks like your attempt to scare me failed.”
“I didn’t expect to scare you anyway.”
Su Yu had arrived early at the ball just to catch Ji Chenxi. He lowered his gaze and looked at Ji Chenxi’s hand, which was wrapped in multiple layers of bandages, then shook his head and sighed.
After putting on an exaggerated performance, he leaned closer and whispered, “What’s going on with you?”
“What do you mean?”
“Stop playing dumb. You and Chi Zhuo. Yesterday’s explosion was covered up quickly, but plenty of people still saw it.”
“Your information network is impressive.” Ji Chenxi sincerely praised him.
