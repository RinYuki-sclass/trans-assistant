Night was about to fall. The young man, who had been rapidly inputting all kinds of data, suddenly paused his fingers. It was as if he had discovered something, and his expression changed sharply.
His fingers jumped quickly across the massive control screen, as though he was repeatedly confirming something.
The assistant beside him looked at the young man at the center in confusion when the data stream suddenly stopped.
The young man ignored the question and stubbornly pulled up information unrelated to their work.
Again and again, amid countless flashing red “operation failed” alerts, the young man closed his eyes. When he opened them again, his face was already calm. After giving a brief explanation to the others, he quickly left the control room and hurried toward another place.
He opened the heavy electronic door, and a faint scent of roses drifted out from inside.
Only after the person inside said “Come in” did the young man step into the large room.
The handsome man was playing with a blooming, vivid rose. On the long black table in front of him were many more roses, just as fiery and beautiful. The young man’s sudden intrusion was almost rude. Even if he hadn’t walked in immediately after opening the door, it was still crossing a line.
After entering the room, the young man knelt on one knee and reported to the man:
“Ge You’s chip has been completely damaged. His existence can no longer be detected.”
The man didn’t react much. “In a revolution, whether it succeeds or fails, there are always sacrifices.”
“But he is the second-in-command of our organization, your most capable assistant.”
“What are you trying to say?”
“Boss, can I repair his data?”
“No need. He had too many ideas of his own. I need more obedient subordinates.”
“But…” The young man hesitated.
“You can speak directly.”
“He may not have been obedient enough, but he was completely loyal to you, Boss, without any second thoughts. If you think he’s not obedient enough, I can personally train him.”
The young man had never once opposed any of Chen Xing’s decisions. He believed in every decision made by his creator. But this time, for the first time, he spoke against Chen Xing, trying to fight for a sliver of survival for a companion who had been created alongside them.
The way Chen Xing looked at the young man changed in that instant. He clenched the rose in one hand and placed the other on the young man’s head. His voice sounded like a sigh, yet also confused. “No matter how human I make you, at your core, you are still machines without emotions. You cannot possess human feelings. Ge You is an exception, since I gave him permission to simulate human emotions. But what about you? D3-857, don’t tell me you secretly enabled that permission for yourself.”
