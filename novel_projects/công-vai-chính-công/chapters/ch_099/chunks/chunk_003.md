“Cut it out. How could an explosion happen inside the Royal Academy? Did you run into a terrorist attack? And His Majesty didn’t even ask about it? This happened right under his nose. That’s just blatantly disrespecting the royal family.”
“Yeah, so bold. Why didn’t His Majesty ask about it?” Ji Chenxi said with a smile.
Su Yu frowned, about to say something, but Ji Chenxi raised a finger lightly to his lips, signaling him to stop.
Su Yu was full of questions, but now he could only swallow them down.
Fortunately, Ji Chenxi knew Su Yu wasn’t the type to hold things in. He quickly typed a line on his terminal. Su Yu understood and glanced at it, seeing the words: “That person is probably too busy dealing with their own problems right now.”
The seemingly simple sentence shocked Su Yu.
“That’s the…”
He didn’t finish, but the meaning was clear. That was the emperor. Who could possibly infiltrate the palace and threaten him? The emperor had countless guards, but there were still moments when his defenses were weaker—whether handling affairs or sleeping, he would still be alone in a sealed space.
Humans were both incredibly strong and incredibly fragile. Death was never that far away.
After recovering from his shock, Su Yu quickly typed on his own terminal.
[Then what’s going on between you and Chi Zhuo? People are saying you two were hugging after the explosion. Very ambiguous.]
Ji Chenxi typed even faster. Almost as soon as Su Yu finished, his reply appeared within three seconds.
[Not a big deal. If it can’t be suppressed, just admit it.]
Admit it? Admit what?
Su Yu almost wanted to close his eyes. This guy really had guts.
“What are you two doing? Secretly typing to each other?” Someone familiar to both of them walked over.
Ji Chenxi said calmly, “We were secretly discussing who would be the most eye-catching person tonight.”
“Oh? Who is it?”
Su Yu reacted quickly and naturally took over. “The Third Prince and I were each sharing our opinions. It’s fine to talk about it quietly, but we can’t say it out loud. Otherwise, we’d hurt the feelings of all the beautiful omegas here.”
The ball continued as usual. Not many people were aware of the undercurrents, so most of the young men and women chatted with smiles. Whether they found a partner or not, being able to interact with so many nobles was beneficial.
