“If Your Highness were by my side,” Chi Zhuo asked softly, “would I be able to kiss you then?”
“Probably not,” Ji Chenxi said with a sigh. “Major General Wu Xiang is glued to my side, doing his job far too well. If I kissed you today, I’d wake up tomorrow with a perfectly proper fiancee.”
“A fiancee?” Chi Zhuo pressed his lips together, his dark eyes steady and cold. “I hope Your Highness never has such a thing.”
“Then what about a fiance?”
“Even if there is one,” he said calmly, “it can only be me.”
Ji Chenxi shook his head and sighed. “Brother Chi Zhuo, you’re way too domineering. That won’t do.”
Firelight rippled across the night as the shy moon slipped out from behind the clouds, answering the fireworks in silence.
Chi Zhuo let out a quiet chuckle. “Here it comes. Look.”
Clusters of blue fireworks shot into the sky, blooming into blue roses one after another. Before the deep blue could fully fade, black light burst forth, weaving together into the shape of a small black snake. With subtle shifts and changes, it finally formed the scene of a snake playing among roses.
“This was supposed to be done with aircraft carrying different colored lights,” Chi Zhuo explained. “But half a day was too rushed, and the result wouldn’t have been good enough. Luckily, I had a plan B.”
“So this hurried confession of yours is full of flaws everywhere,” Ji Chenxi teased.
“Not satisfied?”
“Not really. Life is full of imperfections anyway. Too much perfection isn’t a good thing either. Like this confession you planned in half a day—I find it pretty unforgettable.”
“I didn’t want to rush it like this,” Chi Zhuo said quietly. “But today is special. It’s a day I’ve always hated. I wanted to wash that bad feeling away with something happy.”
“So—” Ji Chenxi dragged out the word, smiling, “I agreed without hesitation.”
“Does Your Highness know fortune-telling?”
“Or maybe,” Ji Chenxi said lightly, “we’re just on the same wavelength.”
Chi Zhuo paused at the playful tone. “Your Highness, I can feel how happy you are.”
“Happy? Some things are better left understood and not said out loud,” Ji Chenxi replied lazily. “Brother Chi Zhuo, you’re making it sound like I was really looking forward to this. Honestly, I’m just so-so about it. I mainly went along with you.”
“Then thank you, Your Highness, for being so generous.”
