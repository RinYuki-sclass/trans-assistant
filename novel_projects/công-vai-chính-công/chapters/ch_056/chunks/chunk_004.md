He kept walking at an unhurried pace.
As he turned a corner, the ambushing “ghost” lunged at him. Ji Chenxi had already lifted his hand, ready to knock the guy out in one clean chop—but his movement paused mid-air as the “ghost” slammed him against the wall. Ji Chenxi’s lips curved into a slight smile, unbothered.
“Hmm? Something wrong?” he asked, his tone light and teasing.
“You’ve been caught.”
“Oh~ so there’s that kind of scene too, huh? You guys really go all out for this haunted house. So, how does this brother plan to let me go?”
“Caught visitors are usually teased by the ghosts—like, for example, with a kiss.” Chi Zhuo’s lips curved slightly, his voice carrying an unusual softness that didn’t often show.
“Is that so? But I already have a boyfriend, so you can’t just kiss me. If you insist, I’ll scream for help.”
Chi Zhuo raised an eyebrow. “Go ahead. Scream your head off—no one’s coming to save you.”
Ji Chenxi’s smile widened, more playful than before. His lips held a teasing curve, his face pretending to protest. “That’s not right. My boyfriend would get angry if he knew.”
“Then let him get angry,” Chi Zhuo murmured, his voice rough and low. Without warning, he pressed a lingering, tender kiss onto the lips he had long wanted to touch, adding teasingly, “You’re no longer ‘pure’ now. Might as well stay with me.”
A muffled laugh escaped Ji Chenxi as he pulled Chi Zhuo’s collar closer and playfully bit his lips. “You really go all out,” he murmured with feigned anger. “A little innocent like me can’t handle this.”
