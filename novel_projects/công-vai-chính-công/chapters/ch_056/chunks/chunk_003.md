Ji Chenxi laughed. “No need to thank me. Respecting elders and caring for the young is a fine virtue.”
The two of them chatted back and forth like this. None of it really meant anything, and in the past Ji Chenxi would’ve coldly labeled it a waste of time. But with their relationship changed, even this kind of idle talk felt oddly fun.
As the Ferris wheel was about to reach the bottom, Ji Chenxi said, “My ride’s over now. So, Brother Chi Zhuo, see you around~”
“See you, little rose.”
After getting off the Ferris wheel, Ji Chenxi was clearly in a much better mood. Su Yu even teased him a few times.
“Anyone who didn’t know better would think that confession just now was meant for you. Why are you so cheerful?”
“Because of the roses!” Ji Chenxi said brightly. “I like roses, and there were so many roses in those fireworks. I wasn’t the one being confessed to, but I was still happy just because those roses were there.”
With such a simple answer, Su Yu didn’t think too much of it. The only thing that made him hesitate was that firework image of a snake sniffing roses. Just that image alone—wasn’t it clearly Chi Zhuo confessing?
But looking at Ji Chenxi’s expression, it really didn’t seem like it. Besides, who confesses when the two people aren’t even in the same place?
After that, the group played a few more rides as the night grew deeper. Then Su Yu suggested going to the haunted house.
At first, none of the four of them had any interest in haunted houses, so they skipped it. But now that they’d almost finished all the other attractions, going at this hour actually sounded pretty good.
Ji Chenxi bought himself an ice cream and bit off Snowball Bunny’s little ear in one mouthful. Hearing the suggestion, he laughed and asked, “Why the sudden urge to go to a haunted house?”
“I heard some passersby talking about it,” Su Yu said excitedly. “They said tonight’s haunted house is super intense. I’ve heard three different groups say the same thing. It’s gotta be fun. Come on, let’s go.”
Ji Chenxi was in a good mood, so he was up for anything and went along with them. Gu Hechuan never objected to whatever they wanted to do, so he followed naturally. As for Lu Tu, he scoffed at the so-called ‘must-try’ haunted house the tourists kept praising—but still tagged along with the group anyway.
They bought their tickets and stepped into the haunted house, swallowed up by near-total darkness lit only by eerie, flickering mood lights.
The place was pretty big—three floors above ground and three more underground. At first, Ji Chenxi stayed with Su Yu and the others, but before long he noticed something odd: the actors playing ghosts kept trying to lure him away from the group.
Ji Chenxi raised a brow, assuming this was some hidden in-game Easter egg, and decided to play along.
The “ghost” guiding them—supposedly leading them to find some kind of potion—was being blatantly obvious, pointing the way the whole time. Ji Chenxi went with the flow, waiting for a chance to corner the guy instead. Since it was just a game, he wasn’t taking it too seriously.
While tracking him, Ji Chenxi sensed another presence nearby.
An accomplice?
