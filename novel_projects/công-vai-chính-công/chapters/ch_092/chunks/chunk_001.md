The last time the two of them talked, it had just been fake concern, polite words they didn’t mean. That kind of concern didn’t need to happen all the time, so what could the other party possibly want by contacting him at this hour?
Ji Chenxi frowned slightly and slid his finger to open the message.
【Find some time, let’s meet】
【For what?】 Ji Chenxi asked, though he had no intention of meeting at all.
【To talk about cooperation. The night after tomorrow, Ji Zhiqing will make her move. You can choose to meet me tonight or tomorrow morning. If you miss it, that’s it. I’ll be leaving the Imperial Star tomorrow night.】
Ji Chenxi tilted his head, a hint of confusion showing on his face.
If it was just fake cooperation to probe him, there was no need to directly say when Ji Zhiqing would act. And that line about leaving the Imperial Star tomorrow night…
He glanced at Chi Zhuo.
Sensing his gaze, Chi Zhuo gave a soft “Mm,” and asked, “What’s wrong?”
Ji Chenxi openly showed him the message in his hand. “Yesterday, he even deliberately mentioned in front of that person that I should find an omega. I guessed he might already suspect something about us. But today, he suddenly wants to talk about cooperation.”
And that wasn’t all.
Ji Chenxi operated his system, hacking into the main network just enough to check. He found that the First Prince’s forces had quietly started moving. They had originally been heading toward the palace, which matched the forces possibly cooperating with Ji Zhiqing, him, and Chen Xing. But now, under the cover of night, they were retreating.
Chi Zhuo fell into thought. At the same time, he also received a report from Bai Han.
Recently, Chi Zhuo had asked Bai Han to keep an eye on all major forces, so he naturally caught this abnormal movement immediately and reported it.
In the short time Ji Chenxi hadn’t replied, Ji Anze sent another message. This time, it included a picture—a broad alpha hand holding a beautiful ruby necklace.
Right after the image came another message.
【You can message Chi Zhuo and let him see this necklace. See what he says.】
Ji Chenxi tilted his head toward Chi Zhuo. It was clearly a necklace meant for a woman.
Chi Zhuo’s originally calm expression shifted slightly. It was obvious he recognized the necklace.
