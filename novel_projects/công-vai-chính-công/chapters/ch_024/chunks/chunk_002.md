Unlike Chi Zhuo, who always wanted to rush straight to the point, Ji Chenxi had endless patience. He kissed gently at the edge of Chi Zhuo’s lips, feeling the warmth between them. He only moved deeper when Chi Zhuo couldn’t resist, exploring awkwardly but carefully.
Whenever Chi Zhuo tried to force his way in again, Ji Chenxi would pull him back, repeating until Chi Zhuo followed his pace. Only then did he ease his hold.
Since his hands weren’t moving as smoothly as he liked, Ji Chenxi placed the other hand under Chi Zhuo’s jaw, holding it lightly, while his first hand moved from the back of the neck to the hair at the nape, fingers threading through. This gave him more control over their movements.
The sharp lines of Chi Zhuo’s lips were far hotter than Ji Chenxi had expected, even more so than his own. Heat always had a way of clouding the mind, and even Ji Chenxi, who had thought he was calm, couldn’t help pressing a couple more kisses.
Their lips and teeth tangled together, and Ji Chenxi’s soft mouth was cut by Chi Zhuo’s sharp teeth. The taste of blood lingered between them, and Chi Zhuo’s suppressed desire surged again. But this time, he didn’t go wild or try to devour him. Instead, he lightly licked away the blood, swallowing every drop.
Ji Chenxi lowered his eyes, a smile pressing quietly in his throat. Between kisses, he asked suddenly, “Chi Zhuo… who do you think is being good now? You, or me?”
The question caught Chi Zhuo off guard. He didn’t respond immediately, continuing instead to chase that faint scent of roses.
Ji Chenxi laughed softly again, staring into those deep, crimson eyes. “Are you still in your right mind?”
When there was no answer, he simply decided to release a little of his pheromones.
As expected, the scent he released was quickly picked up by the tequila in the air, merging and transforming into a faintly rose-scented sweet liquor.
“Let me guess… it’s not that you have no control, you just don’t really want to pay attention to me, right?”
Chi Zhuo’s passion seemed to be forcibly stopped in that instant. The heat faded, leaving only a coldness behind. Pressed together, their strong chests tight against each other, he could even hear Ji Chenxi’s heartbeat. That’s when he realized that even while kissing, Ji Chenxi’s heart hadn’t changed its pace one bit.
“…Looks like Your Highness really dislikes me,” Chi Zhuo sighed.
“Not exactly. I wouldn’t kiss someone I dislike.” Ji Chenxi loosened the hand holding Chi Zhuo’s jaw, tracing gently over the lips he had just kissed. His fingers slid down along the lips to the chin, then the neck, finally resting at the Adam’s apple, the spot he had been looking at for so long.
The night air was heavy with tequila, mingled with the faintest trace of his own rose scent—so faint it was almost imperceptible. But that wasn’t surprising; its owner was stingy with pheromones.
“So, I guess it’s because you weren’t happy with my tone earlier?” Chi Zhuo asked.
“You think I wasn’t happy about something?”
Ji Chenxi’s cool fingers against his skin felt like a slab of chilled jade, slowly moving across Chi Zhuo’s neck, evoking strange sensations completely different from those of kissing.
Yet, the pheromones Ji Chenxi had released earlier had calmed Chi Zhuo considerably. Even though the heat within him remained just as unbearable, he now had a hint of ability to think clearly.
“One… for suddenly taking Your Highness’s first kiss.”
