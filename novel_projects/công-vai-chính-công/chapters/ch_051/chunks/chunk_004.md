Ji Chenxi let out a soft snort. “Praising my looks, huh? Then thanks. Guess that means you’ve got good taste.”
“Didn’t you say before you weren’t playing any feelings games?”
“That was before. This is now. What, I’m not allowed to suddenly get interested?” Ji Chenxi half-narrowed his eyes. “Though honestly, I’m not even sure if this counts as a feelings game.”
“Were you scared before?”
“Scared of people finding out you like alphas.”
Ji Chenxi finally sat up in bed. His summer clothes were thin to begin with, now worn loose and messy, and he couldn’t be bothered to fix them. He just stared at Chi Zhuo without blinking. “Do you think you like alphas?”
Chi Zhuo shook his head. If he really liked alphas, he wouldn’t have mistaken Ji Chenxi for an omega in the first place—and paid for it badly.
“Then if you don’t think you like alphas,” Ji Chenxi asked, “why do you think I must like alphas?”
“Your desire to conquer.”
Ji Chenxi smiled, but not quite. “That logic doesn’t really hold up. All alphas have that—including you.”
Chi Zhuo didn’t insist on his answer. He simply hummed in agreement, as if accepting Ji Chenxi’s words. “Treat the wound first.”
Ji Chenxi didn’t continue digging into the topic. He really did go and heal himself.
The night was deep. Moonlight slanted in through the window, leaving behind a hazy glow. It was as if even the moon favored the silver-haired young man, laying a thin veil of light over Ji Chenxi’s face as he lowered his eyes to tend to his injury.
Chi Zhuo’s gaze seemed to drift with the night. Watching Ji Chenxi, he suddenly said, “The night looks beautiful tonight.”
“Huh? Does it?” After finishing the treatment, Ji Chenxi lay back down on the bed, taking up only half of it.
Once he buried himself fully under the covers, he patted the empty space beside him and asked, “Coming?”
Before Chi Zhuo could answer, he casually offered other options. “If you don’t want to, you can sleep on the floor or the couch. Or if you’re not scared of being found out, you can go find a guest room now.”
Chi Zhuo didn’t answer directly. Instead, he asked, “And what about you, Your Highness? Where do you want me to sleep?”
Bathed in moonlight and starlight, the silver-haired alpha dropped all his restraint and pride, as if Chi Zhuo’s question was ridiculous. “Where else would you sleep? Of course you’re sleeping with me.”
