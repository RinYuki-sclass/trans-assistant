Ji Chenxi blinked, clearly not expecting Chi Zhuo to joke with him so seriously. He couldn’t hold it in and burst out laughing again, his whole body starting to shake. “You’re asking me about the price? Hahaha—this one’s sky-high. I don’t think Brother Chi Zhuo can afford it.”
Rich and powerful as Chi Zhuo was, he wasn’t scared off by Ji Chenxi’s so-called sky-high price. Instead, he asked, “Then say it. If you don’t tell me, how do you know I can’t afford it?”
Ji Chenxi lifted his hand and hooked it around Chi Zhuo’s neck. There were still bloody marks on his hand from earlier, but he didn’t care at all. He only dragged out his words, speaking in that familiar, spoiled tone—light, cheerful, and clearly pleased. “You really can’t afford it. Some things just can’t be bought with money. But even if Brother Chi Zhuo can’t pay, I’ll still reluctantly let you stay the night. Can’t help it—I’m just a soft-hearted good person.”
Chi Zhuo took Ji Chenxi’s hand down from his neck, spread his palm open, and gently pressed a kiss onto the place where skin and blood met, the wound looking almost scary at first glance.
“Then maybe this soft-hearted good person should treat his injury first.”
“Oh?” Ji Chenxi raised his brows. “Feeling sorry for me?”
“No,” Chi Zhuo said calmly. “The smell of blood is just too strong.”
His heart had already settled completely. Outside things wouldn’t shake him so easily anymore.
Ji Chenxi let his eyes droop and put on a pitiful look, fully showing off that dramatic, attention-seeking alpha-in-rut behavior.
Chi Zhuo didn’t budge. “No one feels sorry for someone who doesn’t even care about himself.”
Ji Chenxi lowered his gaze and looked at his own hand. “I don’t like pain. I ended up like this because you didn’t come, and I was anxious and scared. Do you get it? Only physical pain can make me forget the pain in my head for a while.”
“That’s not what you said a few days ago.”
“Yeah, that’s not what I said back then. We’re both adults—don’t get too carried away. That was for you to hear, and for me too.”
Chi Zhuo’s fingers, still lightly holding Ji Chenxi’s hand, stiffened for a moment. “Use the healing device first.”
Ji Chenxi didn’t care much about the small wound. After injecting the inhibitor, his sense of pain had gone dull. Taking advantage of that leftover sensitivity and honesty from after his rut, he said to Chi Zhuo, “You were a little angry back then. I know. I also know why you didn’t contact me for so long—you were trying to cool this relationship down.”
Chi Zhuo recalled that video call. What should’ve been clear anger now seemed mixed with something else.
Being so easily affected by someone else’s emotions was terrifying.
The two of them were both moving carefully, trying to trap the other, yet at the same time, they were also the ones being caught. It wasn’t a pleasant feeling at all.
“In a hunt between two hunters, one of them always ends up as food on the table.” Even without a reply, Ji Chenxi kept talking to himself. He pulled Chi Zhuo’s hand closer and asked, “Brother Chi Zhuo, who do you think will win this hunt?”
“Your spirit form shouldn’t be a snow leopard. A fox suits you better.”
