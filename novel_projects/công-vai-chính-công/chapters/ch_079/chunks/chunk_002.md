“Did I bite you too hard?”
Chi Zhuo hadn’t even used much force just now. There wasn’t any broken skin—barely even a mark, and that would fade soon enough.
Ji Chenxi shook his head. “No.”
He said he was unhappy, but the smile on his lips was even more alluring than when he claimed to be in a good mood. Like a whisper between lovers, he asked softly, “Do you like my pheromones? Do you want to mark me?”
Want to mark him? Of course he did. There wasn’t an alpha alive who didn’t want to mark their partner. Chi Zhuo was just forcing himself to hold back that instinct, suppressing the urge rooted deep in his body. When it got too hard to bear, he would only press close to Ji Chenxi, breathing heavily against him.
By now, after spending so much time together, Chi Zhuo understood one thing clearly—Ji Chenxi might look gentle and easygoing, but in truth, the little prince had quite the temper.
Faced with such a sudden question, Chi Zhuo thought it over—both on the surface and beneath it—before frowning slightly and asking in a low voice, “Do you want to mark me?”
Ji Chenxi blinked lightly, then smiled again. His fingers moved with a kind of tender care as they brushed over Chi Zhuo’s head, sliding through his hair. Around his wrist, the small black snake coiled and tightened faintly.
At that moment, Ji Chenxi looked like someone toying with a cold-blooded venomous snake. The snake twisted in his grasp, craving something, as if everything was under control—but the moment he slipped, it could just as easily bite back.
Some people were addicted to that feeling—dancing on the edge of danger, wrapped in something sharp and deadly. Ji Chenxi was the same. That was why he had approached a snake… and was now trying to tame it.
His fingers slid down, half teasing, half gentle, brushing over the gland as Ji Chenxi said, “Marking and possession are an alpha’s instinct.” And rejecting another alpha’s pheromones was an instinct too.
Chi Zhuo’s current state wasn’t normal.
So how long could this “not normal” last?
Chi Zhuo caught a hint of something deeper hidden in those words.
He thought it over, then lifted his hand, catching Ji Chenxi’s wandering fingers and firmly lacing their fingers together. Following his line of thought, he asked, “So you want to mark me?”
“Mm-hmm.” Ji Chenxi’s tone lifted slightly at the end—neither confirming nor denying.
Chi Zhuo lowered his head, resting it against Ji Chenxi’s shoulder, deliberately exposing his long, clean neck.
He didn’t say a word, but Ji Chenxi, clearly in a mischievous mood, leaned in and blew lightly against that spot. “An invitation?”
“Or maybe… an invitation into a trap.”
This time, Ji Chenxi really laughed. The doubt and unease buried deep in his heart faded in that instant, unable to hold on any longer. Leaning close to Chi Zhuo’s ear, he murmured, “Is that really how you use ‘walking into a trap’? If I bite you, do you think you can bite me back? Why not say you’re bowing down to me instead?”
