Ji Chenxi covered one eye with his hand, not looking at the person at all. When he heard the words, he only said in a hoarse voice, “I know!”
A light, quick laugh followed.
Chi Zhuo lifted a strand of Ji Chenxi’s hair that was spread across the bed and brought it to his lips, kissing it gently. “It hurts a little.”
This time, Ji Chenxi really didn’t feel like looking at Chi Zhuo. He moved his hand away just a bit and glanced through his fingers at the person, saying, “So you actually know what pain is.”
“Your Highness, of course I know pain.”
Ji Chenxi didn’t believe that at all. If he really knew pain, he wouldn’t still be moving when they were knotting.
“Your Highness, you look really beautiful right now.”
Chi Zhuo’s voice was very low, and when it reached Ji Chenxi’s ears, it carried a faint breathy tone. Ji Chenxi’s eyelashes trembled slightly, feeling a bit uncomfortable from that voice. His already red ears turned even brighter.
Teasing a pretty little rose was honestly a very pleasing thing. Even that bit of discomfort was ignored by Chi Zhuo. He kissed the earlobe hidden under the messy hair, going a bit further in. In that movement, Chi Zhuo let out a muffled groan, pausing for a moment before speaking to Ji Chenxi, “Little rose, open your spiritual domain to me.”
Ji Chenxi already knew it would be like this. This guy just wanted to enter that place where an alpha’s regression happens. In that space, knotting during marking could reach a certain level of spiritual unity. Of course, all of this was written in books. Ji Chenxi himself wasn’t interested in this kind of education at all. If it wasn’t a required course, he probably wouldn’t even bother learning the basics.
He reached out and grabbed Chi Zhuo’s hair, clearly unhappy. “You’re so annoying.”
“If it was when we just met, Your Highness wouldn’t only say ‘annoying’.”
“Don’t make me sound like I’m scary.”
“When we first met, Your Highness really was a very aggressive little wild cat.”
“Heh, little wild cat.” Ji Chenxi showed his dissatisfaction. He sounded like he didn’t want to cooperate, but he had already heard the unbearable pain hidden in Chi Zhuo’s casual tone. Right now, they were like a mismatched blade and scabbard forced together—the scabbard only restricted the blade, but the blade, in order to gain more space, kept forcing the scabbard open, cutting and tearing it apart.
What kind of mess was this.
This kind of thing was supposed to be something very enjoyable, but Ji Chenxi really couldn’t feel happy right now. He slowly loosened his grip on Chi Zhuo’s hair. The black cat pendant hanging from a black cord swayed gently, flashing a strange light through the soft black hair.
“Chi Zhuo, I’m in pain,” Ji Chenxi said softly.
At first, Chi Zhuo thought Ji Chenxi’s spiritual domain had gone out of control again, causing the pain. Only a second later did he realize—this place was too tight. The pain he was talking about was the pressure of being forcibly squeezed.
He kissed Ji Chenxi’s earlobe in a soothing way. “I’ll loosen it a bit more.”
