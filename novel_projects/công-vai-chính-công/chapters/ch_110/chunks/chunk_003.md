Second genders aren’t usually determined at birth. Many are formed later. It was clear this little prince hadn’t fully differentiated yet. Or rather, he already had a spiritual domain, but hadn’t yet received his spirit animal.
Unfortunately, Chi Zhuo was not his spirit animal.
He didn’t know what went wrong, but in this spiritual domain, he was treated as Ji Chenxi’s spirit beast.
The child even carried him out of the spiritual domain, eyes shining, and showed him to a woman who looked like an adult version of Ji Chenxi.
The woman was elegant and refined, so beautiful it made everything else pale in comparison. But from the first glance, Chi Zhuo didn’t like her. There was a hidden disgust in her gentle eyes.
She hated her own child.
Little Ji Chenxi was just a silly kid, hugging the black snake everywhere—playing, sleeping with it, doing things completely unlike his real personality. Clearly a little wild cat with a bad temper, yet he forced himself to look well-behaved.
Chi Zhuo stayed with him as he grew up, and whenever the kid overacted, he would gently tap his hand with his tail, reminding him to keep it in check.
Chi Zhuo didn’t even really want to remind him. He already saw it—Ji Chenxi was forcing himself to become someone else, someone who didn’t really exist, someone that woman would like.
Gentle, kind, soft like a little cream bun that anyone could take a bite of.
Looks like an angel, but actually a little devil underneath.
After pretending all day, he would complain to his little snake.
Older Ji Chenxi hugged the snake and said, “That person today was so annoying. But I dug a big pit for him anyway—he’s definitely going to suffer later. Also, little snake, you were too fierce today. You scared my mother. What if she thinks I’m like you?”
Chi Zhuo: “…”
Who cares about this brat.
Just as he was about to slip away to be alone, the pretty kid suddenly kissed his head hard.
“You’re so fierce… but I like you. You actually protect me.”
Chi Zhuo decided to forgive him again.
The little snake stuck out its tongue and licked Ji Chenxi’s face in response.
Ji Chenxi laughed happily.
