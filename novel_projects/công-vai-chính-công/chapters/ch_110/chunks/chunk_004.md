“Do you love me the most?”
The snake hissed twice, answering in its own way—anyway, the kid couldn’t understand.
At night, Ji Chenxi lifted the snake higher, studying it under the dim light. The silver-haired child held the black snake carefully in his arms like it was a treasure.
Then he said lightly, “You love me this much… so why doesn’t my mother seem to like me the way I thought she would?”
Not just “not as much”—Chi Zhuo could clearly feel that sometimes, that woman’s gaze could even be described as disgust.
The little snake gently licked his finger.
Poor little rose… you actually understand everything, don’t you?
