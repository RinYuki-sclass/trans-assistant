At this moment, Ji Chenxi suddenly understood, in a strange way, what Chi Zhuo felt every time he got injured.
Was this something that could be solved just by “loosening” it?
He buried his head against Chi Zhuo’s chest and said nothing.
Chi Zhuo didn’t rush him to open his spiritual domain. A spiritual domain was something very private. Even the closest person couldn’t just be allowed in easily. If he wasn’t worried that things wouldn’t be properly stabilized otherwise, Chi Zhuo wouldn’t have made such an unreasonable request.
He absentmindedly brushed through the silver-white hair. The cool strands felt slightly itchy against his fingers, but it didn’t distract him much—the presence down there was still too strong.
He extended his spiritual power to check Ji Chenxi’s spiritual domain again, only to realize his consciousness slipped in smoothly into another space.
Ji Chenxi had already opened his spiritual domain for him.
Chi Zhuo was moved.
This stupid little cat wasn’t afraid it might be a bad person’s trick at all, just like that, he exposed his softest, most vulnerable place.
He fully immersed his consciousness into that world.
The first thing he saw was a fairytale-like world—colorful candy houses, dreamy trees wrapped in pink and purple like cotton candy, and cloud puffs shaped like white cat heads. How could it be… so cute?
Chi Zhuo was genuinely shocked.
His own spiritual domain was a wild jungle where black mambas grew freely, so he had naturally assumed Ji Chenxi’s would be a snowfield, since his spirit animal was a snow leopard. But why was the first thing he saw something like this?
After entering Ji Chenxi’s spiritual domain, Chi Zhuo’s consciousness took the form of a small black snake. Even though his form had changed, his perception remained human-like. He quickly scanned the surroundings, trying to find the snow mountain suitable for a snow leopard. What he found instead was a field of pink and purple flowers everywhere. There weren’t even roses planted in this “little rose’s” spiritual domain, which felt oddly wrong.
The black snake moved quickly through the area, but every time, it ended up circling back to the candy house.
After a moment of thought, Chi Zhuo decided to enter the candy house. Before he could even squeeze through the gaps in the candy structure, the candy door opened.
A very small, soft little bundle stood there, looking curiously at the snake on the ground. That delicate face—Chi Zhuo didn’t even need to look twice to know who it was.
The pretty little child reached out with soft, pink hands and picked him up, spinning in place happily. “Snake snake! Mine!!”
Chi Zhuo: “…?”
He quickly understood the misunderstanding.
