Ji Chenxi almost laughed. The reason Manye Zhe had gone to all that trouble was just to show how “excited” he was.
“Yeah, I think it’s pretty cool,” Ji Chenxi said simply.
Manye Zhe lowered his head, picked an emoji, and tapped it three times in a row to show how happy he was.
The corner of Ji Chenxi’s lips lifted without him even noticing. “Cool, yeah—but it’s still missing something. Next time, try a black trench coat. When you land, you might look even cooler.”
“Okay. I’ll try it next time.” Manye Zhe finally spoke his first sentence since they met today.
When Wu Xiang heard that, his face almost twitched. It was hard to connect the steady, serious—almost reliable—man in front of him with the one who had typed those playful messages.
Ji Chenxi, on the other hand, took it in stride. Not a hint of discomfort showed on his face. After a brief exchange with Manye Zhe, he turned his gaze back to the starship. Manye Zhe had arrived with Chen Xing, so of course Chen Xing was still inside. And from Ji Chenxi’s senses, there was more than one person on that ship.
Sure enough, once the starship settled, Chen Xing stepped out—tall, well-dressed, refined and handsome. Behind him was another person in loose clothing, a large black hood covering most of his face. Ji Chenxi first gave Chen Xing a gentle smile and nod, showing he had been expecting him. Only then did he spare a glance at the person following behind Chen Xing and Manye Zhe.
A beta. Light footsteps. The clothes were too baggy to show any muscle shape. If he hadn’t come with Chen Xing and the others, Ji Chenxi probably wouldn’t have looked twice. Ji Chenxi had a mild obsession with cleanliness—he liked things neat and tidy—but there were clear oil stains on that person’s clothes.
His glance lasted only a second. If you didn’t catch the tiny shift in his expression, you wouldn’t even know he had looked.
“Third Prince, after knowing each other for so many years, we finally meet in person. How about a hug?”
Even if they weren’t that close, it wasn’t an unreasonable request. And after years of knowing each other, it should have been natural. But Ji Chenxi just smiled and shook his head. “You know me. I’m not big on physical contact.”
Chen Xing had already expected the refusal, so he didn’t even open his arms. It was clearly their first meeting, yet they talked like old friends. Chen Xing teased Ji Chenxi about being fussy, then introduced the person behind him. “This is my mecha engineer.”
The man, almost swallowed by his clothes, his face hidden, greeted Ji Chenxi in a hoarse voice. Ji Chenxi gave a light response, enough to count as a greeting.
Once Chen Xing, Manye Zhe, and the others boarded the aircraft, Ji Chenxi immediately sealed off the cockpit from the seats in the back. Even though he had Wu Xiang’s lifeline in his grip through pheromones, he didn’t dare fully trust him. For now, it was better to block him out completely.
With no outsiders around, Chen Xing didn’t relax. Instead, he frowned slightly.
“How could you bring Major General Wu Xiang with you?”
Chen Xing carried a strong scholar’s air. It often made his words sound like a lecture. But there was also a strange kind of gentleness about him, which softened that edge. So this question leaned more toward worry than blame.
“Just testing him,” Ji Chenxi said lightly. “I wanted to see if my pheromones really have him under control. If he was sent by that man, then he’s someone that man trusts a lot.”
“You should still be careful,” Chen Xing warned in a low voice.
