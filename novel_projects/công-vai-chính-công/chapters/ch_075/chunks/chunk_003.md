“Of course I hope you’ll stay my partner,” Ji Chenxi replied calmly. “And it’s not like I have nothing to rely on. As things stand, I’m the legitimate heir. In many ways, I fit your idea of perfection better. If I can’t have your support, then I guess we’re just not meant to work together.”
Chen Xing didn’t comment right away. He fell silent for a moment before finally saying, “Your Highness, I hope you won’t regret today’s decision.”
“So,” Ji Chenxi asked evenly, “what’s your answer?”
“What you told me before… to me, it’s a stain I can’t accept,” Chen Xing said slowly. “But before I was your follower, we were probably friends first. And as a friend, I should be able to tolerate this small flaw.”
Ji Chenxi looked like he wanted to say something, but the words didn’t come out.
“If you have something to say, just say it,” Chen Xing urged.
“Then I will,” Ji Chenxi said. “First, it’s not a flaw. Second, I won’t regret it. Once I make a decision, regret stops being an option.”
His tone was so firm that even someone as expressionless as Manye Zhe almost showed a reaction.
Quietly, Manye Zhe typed a line and projected a light screen behind Chen Xing.
