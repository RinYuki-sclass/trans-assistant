By the end of it all, Ji Chenxi took first place with barely any effort. Lu Jianhan came in second. Bai Anran took third.
The ranking was so flat and predictable that Lu Jianhan felt no sense of achievement at all. But then he thought about it—if he hadn’t chosen to cooperate with Ji Chenxi at the start, he might not even have made third place. Thinking that way, he felt a lot more balanced.
When the exchange event between the two schools wrapped up, the Imperial Royal Academy produced the first-place winner—and that winner was the Third Prince himself. Second and third both went to the First Military Academy. Sure, Ji Chenxi’s presence played a part, but the results still showed real strength. Both schools’ leadership were extremely satisfied.
By the later stage of the trial, Ji Chenxi had already lost most of his interest. The moment it ended, he boarded the aircraft and started fiddling with his light brain.
The old butler piloted the aircraft back while briefing him on the current situation, including matters related to the Lan family.
“Lan Mu showed up at this freshman trial. On its own, that’s nothing. It could be explained as him trying to avoid the eldest prince and second princess. But with Your Highness present at this trial, every move he makes will be overanalyzed. Not to mention the senior officials watching the event. Who knows if any of them are aligned with your elder brother or second sister.”
Ji Chenxi tapped his fingers lightly. “Exactly. This game can’t stay under control for long. The fight for power could surface at any moment. Call Wu Xiang over. I need to make sure our camp has no traitors.”
The butler acknowledged quietly.
Ji Chenxi’s fingers flew across the interface. The virtual screen flickered rapidly before his eyes as he filled in the gaps in recent information, weighing the current situation and considering whether there were any hidden risks.
He even logged into the dark web once.
The most eye-catching messages were from Lu Tu.
There were only a few, sent at different times. At first, Lu Tu had messaged him casually, like normal small talk. But then, as if something had happened, the tone suddenly turned urgent—three urgent messages in a row. After that, days later, there were just two scattered follow-ups.
Ji Chenxi frowned slightly as he read through them. Part of him felt Lu Tu couldn’t possibly have run into real trouble. But another part worried that Lu Tu might have already left Gu Hechuan’s side and encountered something during the return trip.
After a moment of thought, he sent a single question mark.
The reply came almost instantly.
【Sweetheart, where have you been this past month? Why haven’t you replied to any of my messages?】
【Nothing much. Haven’t logged into the dark web lately. Did you handle your matter?】 Ji Chenxi already sensed something was off, but he played along.
【All handled. But Bai Wei, isn’t it a bit too much of a coincidence that you reply right after the Royal Academy and First Military Academy freshman trial ended? Don’t tell me, sweetheart, you just finished participating in the trial?】
Lu Tu said it half-jokingly. Ji Chenxi had already expected this, so he wasn’t surprised at all. He admitted it openly.
【Yeah. I’m one of the Royal Academy’s freshmen this year.】
