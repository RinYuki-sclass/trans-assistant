“This place is a lab for human modification experiments, Your Highness. What do you think our goal is?”
“Stealing research data? Or maybe taking some of their test samples?”
His words weren’t exactly graceful—steal, take—but Chi Zhuo still gave a small nod. “Pretty much. I’ve been keeping an eye on this lab since I came back. Didn’t expect to bump into Your Highness while checking it out.”
Chi Zhuo’s voice deepened slightly at the end. It wasn’t just that they happened to meet by chance—he suspected Ji Chenxi had orchestrated it deliberately.
Catching the subtle implication, Ji Chenxi offered a small defense. “First, it really was a coincidence. I’m not some all-powerful god who can track your every move. Second, since you were about to blast the door open anyway, why not let me give it a shot first?”
Doors like this, layered and permission-locked, were notoriously hard to open—but it just so happened that Ji Chenxi had just recently done deep research on this exact kind of system.
He activated his light-brain, and part of the device unfolded into a small mechanical spider. It crawled onto the narrow slot where clearance was scanned. As the two systems connected, Ji Chenxi began typing rapidly on the holographic screen projected from the spider’s back. A whirlwind of green code flickered across the display.
Chi Zhuo was already bracing himself to yank Ji Chenxi back the moment the vault blew up—but after only a few minutes of typing, there was a soft “ding.”
The faint blue light over the door vanished.
After the door opened, Ji Chenxi didn’t rush inside. The mechanical spider that had just crawled onto the glowing door dropped down, and as soon as it landed, it began crawling into the room. Sure enough, the inside wasn’t anywhere near as peaceful as they had imagined.
The little spider was immediately attacked by countless photon beams. Luckily, it was incredibly fast, sliding and dodging as the beams followed its every move. It spent nearly a full minute weaving through the attacks before the beams finally vanished.
The mechanical spider continued to move randomly inside, opening some weapon cabinets here and there, but no further attacks followed.
Ji Chenxi glanced at the person next to him. “You going in?”
Chi Zhuo didn’t answer—he just stepped forward.
This guy had some serious guts.
Ji Chenxi strolled in behind him, casually browsing for weapons that caught his eye.
There were plenty of high-damage weapons, but most were too bulky to carry easily. For convenience, weapon experts had designed a few that were powerful yet portable. The only downside? Expensive.
As Ji Chenxi expected, this lab, flush with wealth, was far more lavish than an ordinary one. Several weapons that caught his attention were laid out neatly, glinting under the light.
While picking their gear, the two of them also found a dust-covered mech.
It was gorgeous. Ji Chenxi couldn’t resist running a hand over it.
