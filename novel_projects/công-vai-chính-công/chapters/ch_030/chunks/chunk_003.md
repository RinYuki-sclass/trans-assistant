“I’ll drive,” he said, taking the initiative for once.
“Suit yourself.”
One took the main cockpit, the other the co-pilot seat, and together they maneuvered the mech, using its firepower to clear the way.
At first, the lab staff had intended to rely entirely on their skilled technician to handle them. But with Bai Han outside creating distractions, and Ji Chenxi inside, the technician was caught between two fronts, getting frustrated. They hadn’t used heavy weapons at first, mostly to avoid drawing attention from the outside arena.
But now, their lab was crawling with infiltrators, and if they didn’t deal with it quickly, things were going to get serious.
So from this point on, Ji Chenxi and Chi Zhuo weren’t just facing traps and combat-type mini-robots.
If they’d been in narrow corridors earlier, with countless enemies chasing them, Ji Chenxi might have panicked a bit. But now? They had firepower in spades.
All the fear before had come from being underpowered. With enough firepower, none of these things were a problem.
Ji Chenxi piloted the mech, sweeping everything in their path.
At first, Chi Zhuo offered some backup, but seeing that Ji Chenxi handled it effortlessly, he stopped assisting altogether. He bent over the light-brain device and, after a while, projected a map for Ji Chenxi.
He pointed at a red dot. “We go this way.”
Ji Chenxi acknowledged with a nod and steered the mech in that direction, keeping an eye out while probing a bit. “Chi Zhuo, are you here to take a look at human research too, so you came specifically to steal someone else’s findings?”
Ji Chenxi waited for that denial, already guessing it would come. “Since it’s not to snoop on human experiments, I assume you didn’t just happen to stumble in here like I did and discover some big secret.”
It was obvious this wasn’t a coincidence. Unlike Ji Chenxi’s on-the-spot improvisation, Chi Zhuo had clearly scouted the place ahead of time.
“Since Your Highness already knows they’re doing human experiments, why don’t you try to guess what kind of human experiments they’re running?”
Those creatures were part human, part animal, with strange, bizarre appearances. Ji Chenxi had originally summed them up simply as monsters. Now, a little calmer, he could make some educated guesses. It was obvious these creatures were being modified in that direction.
“Almost all of those creatures are Alpha and Omega,” Ji Chenxi pointed out first.
“Mm,” Chi Zhuo acknowledged.
“It’s not too hard to guess. Mixing humans with animals isn’t because they had nothing better to do—ignoring robots to experiment on real people—it’s because they’re trying to boost their levels. A and O are the same in this regard. Almost only S-level or above can project their spiritual body for extended periods and even achieve fusion, while anyone below S-level might never see their own spiritual body in a lifetime.”
Chi Zhuo nodded in agreement, approving Ji Chenxi’s reasoning.
