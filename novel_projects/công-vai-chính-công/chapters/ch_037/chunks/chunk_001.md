Whether the rose was beautiful or not was another matter, but the way this person spoke carried a sharp, almost mocking edge.
Ji Chenxi casually moved the flower away from his nose and glanced back over his shoulder.
This time, the black-haired Alpha wasn’t accompanied by anyone. He leaned alone against the wall not far from Ji Chenxi, his cold, stern face perfectly matching the Rakshasa painting slanted above him.
The Rakshasa in the painting held a sharp blade, its terrifying, monstrous features formed in a shocking mix of black, red, and blue—striking, eerie, almost dazzling. A painting like this could be called art, and it was rare to find someone standing beside it without being overshadowed by its colors. Chi Zhuo counted as one of the few.
Ji Chenxi looked at him for a moment, and somehow, he felt the urge to smile. “This painting’s really something.”
The man beneath it was just as impressive.
Chi Zhuo, his face dark, raised his eyes and glanced at the painting Ji Chenxi had mentioned.
He didn’t really understand art, but even just based on the impression it left, this piece could fit perfectly in a museum.
“Came back just to see me?” Ji Chenxi chuckled lightly.
One second he’s talking about a painting, the next he’s already onto personal matters. That was the sort of presence Ji Chenxi had—you might even think there’s a hint of interest in him one moment, and the next, he could push you away without hesitation and drift off as gracefully as ever.
Chi Zhuo’s eyes darkened. “No. I just thought the flower was nice… and wanted to take it.”
Ji Chenxi dragged out his words. “What flower?”
“Could it be that Your Highness has another flower in mind?”
At this, Ji Chenxi’s smile widened, his tone bright and teasing. “Of course not. The one in my hand is a rose. In my spiritual realm, it’s a snow lotus. And my pheromones? Roses, too. So tell me, which flower do you want?”
Whatever flower he wanted—even the snow lotus from Ji Chenxi’s spiritual realm—would only be a projection forced into reality; it would vanish unless Ji Chenxi was willing to spend spiritual energy to sustain it. The rose in his hand was pretty, yes, but it was just an ordinary rose. What Chi Zhuo truly wanted had always been clear.
Knowing exactly what you want is one thing; actually saying it out loud is another.
So when faced with Ji Chenxi’s teasing words, Chi Zhuo’s face barely moved. He just said flatly, “A rose.”
Ji Chenxi laughed, genuinely amused. “So it turns out Brother Chi Zhuo only wants this little rose. What a shame—I actually thought you came on purpose to borrow a flower as a metaphor.”
“Then, will Your Highness give it?”
At this point, the flower Chi Zhuo meant was clearly no longer just an ordinary one. But Ji Chenxi pretended not to understand. “I’m afraid not. I promised to meet an online friend, and this rose is the proof. If I give it to Brother Chi Zhuo, I might be impossible to find later.”
