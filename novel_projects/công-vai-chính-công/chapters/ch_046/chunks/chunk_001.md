After Chen Xing’s words, Ji Chenxi stayed silent for a long while. In the quiet so deep he could almost hear the other person’s breathing, he finally spoke, “What if I don’t want it?”
“Alright, I’ve got it… Your Highness is saying no.”
“Hope this is just a joke,” Ji Chenxi said calmly.
“Of course it’s a joke. I hope I didn’t scare Your Highness.”
Ji Chenxi responded with a cold, brief hum.
Just as his finger was about to hit the button to hang up, Chen Xing spoke again, “Your Highness… are you rejecting me because you don’t like me, or because you like that alpha?”
“Does it make a difference?” Ji Chenxi asked with a faint, playful smile.
“Of course it does. Not liking me is normal—after all, I’m twenty years older than Your Highness. But if it’s because you like that alpha, that’s a whole different story. If Your Highness isn’t happy with Queqi, I can try to find someone else suitable… maybe you just don’t like women.”
“Women, men, alpha, or omega… what I like seems none of Chen Xing’s business, right?”
After hanging up, a faint smile tugged at the corner of Ji Chenxi’s lips, though a slight annoyance crept in. There was a bit of a teacher-student bond between him and Chen Xing.
Having lost his mother at a young age, surrounded by rival packs, with a father whose attitude was unclear, he had gone through a period of isolation. It was during that time he met Chen Xing and later some online friends. Chen Xing had taught him a lot—he had even considered Chen Xing half a teacher. Back then, Chen Xing had been a truly good teacher. But now, all it took was a single ambiguous remark about an alpha to provoke probing words.
If he had known it would be this troublesome, he should have denied having any ambiguous connection with an alpha from the start.
But even if he could do it all over again, Ji Chenxi probably still wouldn’t deny it. Whatever he and Chi Zhuo had between them was their business, not for outsiders to poke their noses into.
Already irritated by the thought of having an old man looming around, Ji Chenxi’s mood sank even further.
Maybe getting involved with Chen Xing from the start wasn’t such a smart move.
Just as he was pondering whether to switch to a different ally, a message from Chen Xing popped up.
[Your Highness, you know my loyalty to you. I misspoke just now; I hope you won’t take it to heart.]
Ji Chenxi smiled faintly at the message. He typed his reply deliberately, each movement calm and measured, though the smile on his face was cold and sharp despite the words.
[How could I ever doubt your loyalty, Chen Xing? You guided me when I was lost.]
The help he received in his youth was priceless, so rationally, Ji Chenxi wanted to believe Chen Xing hadn’t spoken carelessly. But he had already investigated Chen Xing thoroughly a long time ago. If his memory served him right, Chen Xing was a perfectionist. And how could a perfectionist accept that the ruler he supported was caught up in an AA romance?
