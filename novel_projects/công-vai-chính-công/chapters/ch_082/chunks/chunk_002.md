His eyes narrowed slightly. “So, just a rat.”
The hidden observer felt a flicker of annoyance, but in that brief moment, Ji Chenxi had already yanked the needle out of the suppressant in his hand. In the next instant, he flung it sharply. The angle it shot out at was the exact opposite direction of where the rat had appeared.
And just like that—one strike, straight to the throat.
Ji Chenxi casually tossed aside the sweet-smelling omega and started walking toward where the person was hiding. Without the needle, the suppressant began dripping down slowly, drop by drop, releasing a faint, refreshing scent. But relying on smell alone, its effect was almost nothing.
On the other side of the surveillance feed, a man watching from multiple angles cursed under his breath, tapping irritably at the control panel. But as Ji Chenxi got closer step by step, his fingers began to curl slightly, and his breathing grew quieter.
A clear sign of fear.
Ji Chenxi’s expression was just too cold. Even though what he held now was nothing more than a nearly harmless suppressant, the pressure he gave off was overwhelming.
The handsome silver-haired young man suddenly smiled toward the camera.
Then—’buzz’—the screen went black.
The man watching felt cold sweat break out across his back in that instant.
Ji Chenxi’s spiritual power surged out without restraint, sweeping through the area to make sure that the “rat,” so good at hiding, was completely dead. Only after confirming that even the surveillance and recording devices had been destroyed did he slowly move in a certain direction.
That was where the scent of alcohol was coming from.
But Ji Chenxi didn’t think it was Chi Zhuo. If Chi Zhuo had really come, he wouldn’t still be hiding.
A blade formed from his spiritual power appeared in his hand. With a single swing, he cut down all the reeds blocking his view.
For a brief moment, he thought he saw Chi Zhuo.
But when he blinked again, all that remained on the ground was a small, extremely well-hidden device. It was quietly releasing a faint scent into the air—something like a cat toying with its prey just before the catch… and at the same time, another kind of catalyst.
A tequila-scented catalyst—there was no such thing as that many coincidences in this world. The moment Ji Chenxi confirmed that the smell worked just like a lure, he knew it—someone had already noticed what was going on between him and Chi Zhuo.
Without hesitation, he drove his spiritual power blade straight down into the small device on the ground. The moment it broke, a burst of white smoke sprayed out.
Ji Chenxi covered his nose and mouth and quickly pulled back. Even so, he still ended up breathing in a bit.
The heat surged up in waves. This wasn’t just a normal sensitive period anymore — his sensitive period colliding with a highly compatible omega, and on top of that, being hit with an aphrodisiac.
