The heart that had just been hanging in his chest finally settled back down.
“Don’t be so curious. Either way, it has nothing to do with you,” Chi Zhuo said coldly.
“If you hadn’t mentioned it, I would’ve forgotten—once a freshman’s points pass the highest score among the seniors, we’re supposed to step off the stage.”
In other words, from the moment Ji Chenxi snatched that coin from Chi Zhuo’s hand, this stage no longer belonged to Lan Mu and the others. As someone the school paid close attention to, there was no way Lan Mu hadn’t received the notice. He had clearly done all of this on purpose—he had even pierced his own recorder drone with spider silk ahead of time.
“Then, Your Third Highness, I wish you good luck in advance,” Lan Mu said lazily. “Let’s see if you can hold up against all the freshmen from both schools.”
Ji Chenxi put on his polite smile mask and gave a slight nod in response.
“Chi, leaving together?”
Lan Mu didn’t care much about Ji Chenxi’s attitude. Compared to that, he was far more curious about what exactly was going on between Chi Zhuo and Ji Chenxi. Was Chi Zhuo being a little too indulgent with the young prince? That discovery was even more interesting than crushing a bunch of freshmen.
After stirring the waters just enough, he left with a grin, open and unbothered. The dense swarm of blue spiders vanished with him.
Once Lan Mu was gone, Chi Zhuo looked at Ji Chenxi and said, “Your Highness, may I have a word with you?”
“And what if I say no?”
Chi Zhuo fell silent for a moment, pressing his thin lips together. “Of course, we’ll go with Your Highness’s wishes.”
Ji Chenxi laughed. “Take care, senior. I won’t see you out.”
