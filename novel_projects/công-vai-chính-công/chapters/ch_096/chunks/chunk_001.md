That was really blunt.
Ji Chenxi walked into the room naturally and sat across from Chen Xing. He lowered his gaze and looked at the cards in Chen Xing’s hand. “There seem to be quite a few reversed.”
“Yes, Your Highness, what meaning do you think this spread reveals?”
“I don’t really study this kind of thing. Being able to tell upright from reversed is already pretty good.”
Ji Chenxi lowered his eyes and looked at the cards a bit longer. His long lashes hid most of the emotion in his eyes, making it impossible to see any deeper meaning.
Chen Xing let out a light laugh. “That’s really a pity. Actually, from Your Highness’s perspective, this spread is mostly upright. It’s a very good spread. But unfortunately, there are two cards that are reversed, ruining the overall fortune of the spread.”
Ji Chenxi wasn’t so clueless that he couldn’t hear the implication in Chen Xing’s words, but throughout the whole process, he didn’t make any further comments. From his perspective, the king wore a crown and faced him upright, yet the guard symbolizing protection and the scepter symbolizing power were reversed. Even as an outsider, he could read a hint of something ominous from this spread.
Whether the other party had just happened to draw such a spread, or had done it deliberately, was already quite obvious.
He casually tapped the two reversed cards with his finger. “Can’t you just switch them around? After all, things depend on people. If these two cards being reversed is bad, then they should be corrected in time.”
“But that’s how it looks from Your Highness’s perspective. From mine, these are actually the only two upright cards. Even those reversed cards weren’t too bad to begin with, but unfortunately, the king card—which plays a key role—is reversed. Instead of switching two cards, it would be better to just switch this one.”
As he spoke, Chen Xing flipped the king card.
The movement was so quick. Almost the moment he finished speaking, the card had already been deftly turned in his hand.
Ji Chenxi stared at the cards for a few seconds, then leaned back casually, his shoulders completely relaxed.
He smiled, his tone light and natural. “Speaking through the cards?”
“Not entirely.”
At this moment, Ji Chenxi’s smile floated too much on the surface. With just a slight drop of the curve of his lips, there would be no trace of a smile left, and his eyes were filled with a cold stillness. “Looks like there’s no room left to turn things around.”
Unlike his earlier light tone, his voice suddenly carried no emotional fluctuation, yet it held many unclear meanings.
Throughout the whole process, Chen Xing remained elegant and gentle, as if he hadn’t noticed Ji Chenxi’s change at all. His fingers brushed over the king card as he smiled. “Regretting it now would be the most meaningless thing. Since it’s already been done, it’s better to admit it openly. Actually, it’s not like I never gave Your Highness a chance.”
Ji Chenxi didn’t try to figure out when that “chance” had been. He stayed silent, tapping lightly on the king card that had just been flipped. “Not even the slightest bit of regret?”
When Chen Xing heard this, the smile on his face instantly faded. He stared at Ji Chenxi without blinking.
