“Your Highness…”
At this moment, the look Chen Xing gave Ji Chenxi was complicated, but Ji Chenxi still caught a trace of disappointment in it.
Only at this moment did Ji Chenxi finally confirm that his earlier feeling had been right.
The other party had been looking forward to meeting him, looking forward to the tension between them, looking forward to his anger and hatred. Even though Chen Xing had appeared calm before, there had been a strange sense of excitement about him. Ji Chenxi had originally thought that excitement came from whatever action Chen Xing planned for tomorrow night, but now it seemed to carry many more unclear meanings.
Following that point which might have caused Chen Xing’s disappointment, Ji Chenxi spoke again. “Right now, I only want to know this.”
At this moment, he really looked like a stubborn child fixated on getting an answer. Only then did Chen Xing realize once more that the person in front of him—someone he had known for many years, someone he had treated as an adult long ago—was actually just a child who had only recently turned eighteen. Wasn’t it normal for a child to be weak? Yet at this moment, Chen Xing felt an unexpected surge of anger.
This was something Chen Xing would never normally say, but perhaps something he once thought was perfect had changed in some unnoticed corner. For a perfectionist like him, that alone was enough to make him uncomfortable.
After getting the answer he wanted, Ji Chenxi didn’t feel any relief. Instead, he felt a bit uncomfortable.
There was indeed anger in the process, but more than that, he had hoped this was just a misunderstanding. He had gone through many things and seen too much. He thought he had already seen through the ugliness of human nature, but when this happened to him, he realized he wasn’t as indifferent as he believed. He even wished things could be simpler, like a fairy tale—no matter how twisted the process, it would still end in a perfect conclusion. But life was not a fairy tale.
Ji Chenxi applied a bit of force with his hand and pulled out the card representing the king.
“Maybe I just don’t want to admit that I misjudged someone.”
Chen Xing’s tightly furrowed brows didn’t deepen at Ji Chenxi’s slightly offensive words. Instead, they relaxed, and a faint smile appeared on his face. “Very arrogant. But compared to weakly seeking an answer, I’d rather accept this result.”
“Arrogant? Not as much as you.”
Chen Xing raised a brow slightly. “What does Your Highness mean?”
“Coming to see me alone like this—how are you so sure I wouldn’t take this chance to attack you directly? After all, instead of letting our forces fight each other to the death, this is a perfect… opportunity to kill you.”
As Ji Chenxi said, this was indeed a perfect opportunity to strike. Hesitation was not his style. In an instant, he showed no mercy as he clasped his hand around Chen Xing’s neck.
Ji Chenxi had a pair of strikingly beautiful eyes. When those eyes narrowed slightly, the darkened color made them seem extremely dangerous.
“Your Highness might as well try.”
This time, the smile on Chen Xing’s face already carried a visible hint of excitement.
