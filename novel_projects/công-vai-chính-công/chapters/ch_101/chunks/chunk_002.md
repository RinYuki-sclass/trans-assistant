“The commotion you caused has already alarmed those guests.”
“It only counts as good if tonight gets a little chaotic.” Now that the man had already arrived in front of him, the young man stopped struggling and instead looked at Ji Chenxi. “I have very strong calculation abilities, and my brain stores countless pieces of knowledge that I can use at any time. But even so, why can’t I take control of this place from you?”
Was this how their conversation was supposed to go?
At first, Ji Chenxi hadn’t even planned to talk to him, but since he asked, he casually gave him an answer. “Why don’t you guess who taught me my hacking skills?”
The young man froze for a moment, seeming surprised, yet also as if it made perfect sense. “The main system here—the Core.”
Ji Chenxi gave a light acknowledgment. The reason he could take control so easily was, of course, because the main system here was his accomplice.
“Just now, were you trying to trigger a self-destruct? Or planning to alert whatever garbage you’ve buried here? With it around, that kind of thing is very hard to pull off. It’s far more terrifying than we imagine—not just a simple artificial creation.”
After saying that, Ji Chenxi frowned slightly, feeling like his words carried some unintended meaning. Only recently had he learned that the second-in-command of Cocoon wasn’t a real lifeform, but a robot centered around a chip. That made the young man in front of him more likely to be a machine than a real human.
“In the past, I would have scoffed, thinking the main system was nothing special. But today, it’s clear that it’s not something I can challenge.”
While trying to stop Ji Chenxi, he had deployed countless viruses that could disable systems, yet every one of them disappeared the moment they touched it, as if they had never existed.
“It’s a pity—you won’t get another chance to challenge it.”
Black-red thorns extended from the shadows, surrounding him tightly. Sharp spikes pierced into his flesh, but the young man had no time to care about them. Instead, he looked in surprise at the roses blooming on the vines, their appearance strikingly delicate. He reached out and gently touched one, realizing it wasn’t a spiritual illusion but something real.
A physical manifestation of spiritual power. And at this level of manifestation.
“You really aren’t just S-rank. You’re the super S-rank he’s been trying to find. If you had told him from the start that you were far beyond S-rank, he might not have attacked you.”
Ji Chenxi showed no reaction, neither regretful nor as angry as before when Chen Xing was mentioned.
The young man’s face was pale. Even though he was about to die, his expression was relaxed, waiting for death in a posture completely unlike his usual style.
“You seem to be looking forward to it.”
The young man, who had already closed his eyes, slowly opened them again, looking toward the overly bright white light above. “Sometimes, death is also a kind of relief.”
“Is that so? Don’t you want to return to Chen Xing’s side?”
“My mission failed. Since it failed, I’m something useless to him. Rather than getting in his way, I think death is better.”
