People always have their own preferences, things they like and things they don’t.
As if trying to express his feelings, Ge You said “I like Xiao San the most” to him more than anything else, but he himself couldn’t really be considered kind to Ge You.
He was a highly calculated artificial human, and he hadn’t been created for battle in the first place, but to serve as Chen Xing’s assistant. Ge You counted as his senior, yet this senior didn’t act like one at all. Many times, he even had to clean up the mess for him, and that was how the two of them gradually became familiar.
At the very beginning, he didn’t like Ge You at all, thinking the other only relied on his combat system and various data advantages. Later on, he began to feel a bit sorry for this senior, since the other had to keep dying over and over for the master’s strange ideas. Ge You, however, took it all lightly, saying with a grin, “As long as the chip isn’t destroyed, I won’t die.” But what if the chip got damaged? That was what he thought, so he asked. For once, Ge You showed a rare complicated expression, “Well, that depends on whether the master still needs me. If he does, no matter what, I’ll protect my chip. But… we’re consumables. Who can really say how long we’ll live?”
They were just consumables. From the moment they were given consciousness, they already understood that. Maybe in their master’s eyes, they weren’t even conscious beings at all, just lifeless objects. Even their personalities were designed and planned by the master. Then why, when the master was young, did he design someone like Ge You?
The other was too special. Because of that uniqueness, he knew Ge You was different, someone who could live for a long time. But now, for the greater plan, Ge You had been abandoned by the master.
Ji Chenxi’s words of dislike only made the young man pause for a brief moment. He quickly snapped out of it and pressed the button that issued the command.
The massive high-tech cannon aimed at Ji Chenxi, and once it locked onto the target, it fired.
Ji Chenxi activated his spiritual power, and a massive blade formed from it sliced the cannon apart with perfect precision. At the same time, thorny vines pierced into a certain spot, damaging the circuitry there. With that line destroyed, the cannon was completely scrapped, and the huge energy it had gathered dissipated before it could even take shape.
Right now, the elevator had become the main target of attacks. Ji Chenxi obviously wouldn’t still take the elevator in this situation.
He tapped his light brain several times, and a virtual screen appeared. Earlier, he had already experienced what it felt like when his equipment level wasn’t high enough to seize control, so how could he let himself make the same mistake again?
His device scanned the area, and data kept popping up on the virtual screen. Ji Chenxi first connected his screen to the system here, then used that connection to begin fighting for control.
At first, the massive light screen in front of the young man showed no reaction at all, but it was precisely this lack of reaction that made him realize something was wrong.
He had clearly seen through surveillance that Ji Chenxi was doing something, so the system having no response now was the truly terrifying part.
He didn’t just sit there waiting to die. His fingers flew rapidly across the controls, the sound of tapping echoing nonstop. The false calm finally showed its cracks—Ji Chenxi was seizing control of the elevator. He tried his best to take it back, but every attempt was rejected. Failure after failure made him realize just how terrifying the other party was. He simply couldn’t stop him.
First the elevator’s control, then the control over every attack and every defense.
He had clearly left no backup plans, issuing commands directly from the control center that held authority. Even with such an advantage, his control over this place was still taken away.
This was completely a difference in strength, especially with how powerful the other party’s decryption system was.
The last line of defense was broken.
The silver-haired alpha had gone through countless obstacles to reach him, yet his expression was calm. He even had the mood to glance at other scenes on the surveillance.
