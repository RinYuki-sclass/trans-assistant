Ji Chenxi fell silent for a moment. He didn’t understand, but he respected it. He decisively gave the other a relatively easier death—if destroying the chip directly could be considered easy.
Jumping out of the window, the troublesome giant robot had already been firmly restrained, unable to do anything under the tight net. Those strangely modified humans were either dead or injured. Flames had already ignited outside, with several places deliberately set on fire. Faint screams and explosions could be heard from afar.
The night, which should have been peaceful, had become anything but pleasant. The earlier laughter and joy had only been the calm before the storm.
A communication request came in. Ji Chenxi answered without even looking. It was Gu Hechuan’s voice.
“We’ve taken advantage of the chaos to get in and have already subdued some of the rebels. It’s better to activate the palace defenses. Otherwise, quite a few forces will want to join in the fun. Even if the noble families hold great power, they’re still under others. Who knows if they also want to take this position?”
“This position belongs to whoever is capable. If they have the guts, let them all come.”
There was silence on the other end for a while, then a voice that sounded both helpless and indulgent said, “You really want a complete reshuffle, huh? The risks are huge. But as long as you want it, the Gu family will stand with you.”
Chaos was something Chen Xing both liked and disliked.
Countless sounds of fighting had already erupted within the palace. This was true chaos.
He looked from afar at the white-gold tower. At this moment, there was no fire there, which meant the other party had failed.
Ji Zhiqing’s expression was no longer as confident as before. After all, what she was doing was treason. Even she found it hard to stay completely calm. She was a bit excited, but also a little nervous. Seeing Chen Xing looking in that direction, she asked, “What are you looking at, Your Excellency?”
“At that tower.”
“That’s the control center of the palace.”
“I planted a powerful bomb there. I thought I’d get to enjoy a nice firework show, but it seems that won’t happen.”
Ji Zhiqing caught the underlying meaning and frowned. “So Xiao Di’s side failed.”
“It’s fine. If it had succeeded on the first try, that would be the real problem. The First Legion and the Second Legion should have already entered by now, right?”
Ji Zhiqing nodded. The commander of the First Legion was her maternal grandfather, and her mother was his only daughter—there was no way he wouldn’t act. As for the Second Legion, as long as it was bound by marriage interests, that commander couldn’t possibly side with Ji Chenxi. The only downside was that they acted too early and too suddenly. Even if they caught others off guard, they lacked a bit of timing. That old fox had definitely held something back, after all, if they failed this time, it would involve the interests of the entire legion and family.
“I’m a bit worried about the Second Legion.”
“Your Highness needn’t worry. Once the bow is drawn, there’s no turning back. The commander of the Second Legion understands that much. He has no chance to regret it.”
Ji Zhiqing frowned. The closer it got to the critical moment, the more a faint unease lingered in her heart.
