The first sentence might have been a tease, but the words that followed were a clear signal to leave.
Chi Zhuo didn’t respond right away. Instead, he used the momentum to pin Ji Chenxi against the chair back. At that moment, he was like a starving beast, drawn by the scent of blood, and after finally tasting that first bit of flesh, he couldn’t stop himself from wanting more.
They had kissed before, but this time, as their lips met, Chi Zhuo felt his very soul tremble.
Ji Chenxi tilted his head, silver strands falling over his forehead, half-covering his eyes. The light caught his pale-colored irises, making them look strikingly beautiful. As Chi Zhuo’s strong, possessive kiss paused, he gently brushed his lips against the corner of Ji Chenxi’s eye. Unlike the kiss before, this one was soft, delicate, like kissing something fragile.
Ji Chenxi shivered from the light touch, his lashes fluttering, but he was laughing happily.
It was, without a doubt, the most genuinely joyful laugh Ji Chenxi had shown in recent times.
“So, Chi Zhuo, just one little kiss is enough to get you this worked up?”
His voice was laced with teasing, the kind that made every word playful.
Chi Zhuo could only helplessly hold onto Ji Chenxi, who was laughing so hard he could barely sit up straight. No matter the mood, it was completely ruined by this laughter. Just as Chi Zhuo tried to stand, Ji Chenxi, whose hands were already around his neck, applied just enough pressure to stop him. Leaning close to his ear, he murmured:
“But didn’t I tell you before, Chi Zhuo? Next time you kiss me, you should know what gentleness feels like. Or is your gentleness only a thing of last night?”
His voice darkened slightly at the end, a silent threat spreading through the air. Chi Zhuo tensed every muscle, forcing his instincts under control, and said calmly, “Your Highness can choose to kiss me back.”
Ji Chenxi raised an eyebrow. So confident, so fearless…
Of course, there was a fundamental difference between a beast with claws and a golden canary. No matter how Ji Chenxi tried to guide him earlier, Chi Zhuo’s instincts would always take over eventually.
Ji Chenxi wasn’t exactly known for patience, but this time he simply leaned in, brushing his lips slightly against Chi Zhuo’s, and then, smiling, said, “Go home, little snake.”
Facing Ji Chenxi’s eyes—shimmering and full of warmth, as if every glance carried endless affection—Chi Zhuo quickly looked away. “What kind of name is that?”
Ji Chenxi let out a playful “Ah—” and pretended to be serious while teasing, “Not happy with it? How about ‘small snake’? Or… baby…”
Chi Zhuo’s throat rolled as he stopped the words just before Ji Chenxi could spill out anything more embarrassing, pinching both of Ji Chenxi’s cheeks with two fingers. “Your Highness, I’ll leave right away. I won’t stay the night or cause trouble.”
Even with his face being pinched, Ji Chenxi still looked unbearably cute. He pointed to his cheeks, silently signaling for him to let go.
“No more nonsense,” Chi Zhuo warned.
Ji Chenxi nodded, indicating he would behave.
