Chi Zhuo hesitated for a moment, then chose to release him. After letting go, Ji Chenxi obediently didn’t say any more humiliating names, instead pointing toward the door.
When Chi Zhuo stared at him with a dark expression, Ji Chenxi blinked innocently, again signaling that he could leave.
Chi Zhuo pursed his lips and turned away.
Ji Chenxi rested his chin on his hand, watching his retreating figure, guessing at his mood. Maybe he was just embarrassed and a little annoyed—that would pass in a few days. Or maybe he was genuinely angry, thinking this budding affection wasn’t worth continuing and wanting to cut it off entirely.
Ji Chenxi picked up the crystal dice they had thrown during their earlier game. The pinkish-purple dice were dreamy in appearance. He idly tossed them in his hands, and this time, without trying to catch them, they rolled onto the floor. Both landed perfectly on six.
The old butler came over and reported, “Young master, Mr. Chi has taken his hovercar and is heading out of the estate.”
Ji Chenxi nodded, acknowledging it, still playing with the dice they had spent over three hours with.
“Is young master planning to use the Chi family’s influence?” The butler finally asked, after waiting quietly since Mr. Chi arrived at the estate, bringing up the question that had been quietly brewing all along.
“I thought about that before… but maybe not anymore,” Ji Chenxi replied.
Recently, Ji Chenxi had suddenly realized a problem.
Chi Zhuo’s possessiveness was too strong. On the surface, he seemed quiet and restrained, but when he liked someone, he could be surprisingly bold and blatant. Just yesterday, Lu Churan and Manye Zhe had already assumed that Ji Chenxi and Chi Zhuo were in some sort of ambiguous relationship. And today, watching how the two interacted with Su Yu only made Ji Chenxi even more aware of the issue.
If he and Chi Zhuo actually got together, could they really be secret lovers? With Chi Zhuo’s attitude, there was no way they’d stay hidden—everyone would know in minutes.
And if Chi Zhuo was just playing around, using affection as leverage, that would be one thing. But the scary part? What if he was serious too?
If both of them were serious, and neither of them was exactly the type to stay low-profile… Ji Chenxi could already imagine the chaos. The throne would practically be off-limits—his old man would be the first to cancel any possibility of his succession. The royal family would never allow someone in an AA relationship to become the crown heir.
Playing with the two crystal dice, Ji Chenxi grew restless. Finally, with a single decisive toss, the dice shattered into powder.
People often pursued power to gain more freedom, but sometimes, power itself became another kind of cage.
This month had been particularly turbulent. First, the Third Prince had been chased by star pirates. Then, the Imperial Star had discovered a human experimentation lab. And now, the attempted assassination of the Third Prince while he was out with the Lu family heiress. Understandably, the Emperor was furious, and in response, he handed over a sharply trained unit—the Royal Guard—to the Third Prince.
This guard was led by a highly accomplished young major general. Originally, this team was supposed to serve the Crown Prince, and the young general was supposed to become the crown prince’s confidant. But now, the emperor had handed the unit to the Third Prince instead. The only thing missing was the official title of heir apparent.
Receiving this news, Ji Chenxi, who had been in a perfect position to reap the benefits, frowned. Things weren’t looking good.
Morning Star was the first to contact Ji Chenxi after hearing the news. The moment the connection was established, Morning Star let out a long sigh.
