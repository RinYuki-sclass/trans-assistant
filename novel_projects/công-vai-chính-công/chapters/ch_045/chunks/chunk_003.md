Ji Chenxi’s strategy had been going perfectly: manipulating public opinion to trap the eldest prince and the second princess in a situation where they would have to prove their innocence while suspecting each other of sabotage. This should have led to a bloody showdown between them. But now, before this silent war had even begun, the Emperor had already intervened, giving the Royal Guard to the Third Prince.
“Third Prince, I honestly can’t tell whether that person likes you or dislikes you.”
“Probably both,” Ji Chenxi said. “He doesn’t want me to get that position too easily. If those two had been fighting, I could’ve stolen a couple more years to build my influence at the Royal Academy. But now that the Royal Guard has been assigned, those two won’t want to waste their strength. If it weren’t for this new friction, they might even team up against me.”
“Your Highness… have you considered teaming up with either the eldest prince or the second princess?” Morning Star’s voice came over, along with the soft scratching of a pen across paper.
“I did think about it,” Ji Chenxi replied. “I tried bringing up old matters with my eldest brother. It stirred something in him, but he listens too much to his mother—and she happens to see me as a thorn in her side, a threat she’d love to get rid of immediately. So the chances of cooperation there are almost zero. My second sister, on the other hand… she’s spent these years keeping a low profile. Her reputation and strategy are flawless. But precisely because of that, she’s not someone I should ally with either.”
Working with someone clever means always being ready for betrayal. If he teamed up with his second sister, suspicion would run both ways. Success would be difficult.
Morning Star had already analyzed both of them, and asking Ji Chenxi now was just to help plan the next move.
“Your Highness… now that you’ve been pushed to the edge, you must tread carefully.”
Ji Chenxi chuckled lightly. “What exactly are you trying to say?”
“The Night Wanderer mentioned that you have an ambiguous relationship with an alpha.”
“He didn’t tell you which alpha it is?”
“No,” Morning Star frowned slightly. “No matter how I asked, he wouldn’t reveal who the alpha is. Even when he mentioned it to me, he emphasized not to force Que Qi to be with you or push you toward anyone you’re interested in.”
Ji Chenxi’s expression softened. Just as he expected, even if he didn’t say anything, the Night Wanderer wouldn’t give away Chi Zhuo’s identity.
“So… how do you know it’s an alpha?”
Ji Chenxi wasn’t all that surprised. Even in their group chat, there were clear circles within circles—people who were closer to some than others. Like Traveler and Crow, the Night Wanderer and him, and then Morning Star and Que Qi.
They said that Que Qi had known Morning Star since she was a teenager. And even though she was surrounded by talented alphas, she never showed the slightest interest in any of them. The Night Wanderer had even come to Ji Chenxi once, half-jokingly trying to figure out whether Que Qi might actually like Morning Star.
Compared to them, Wang Tu was the only one who truly treated everyone equally. He was friendly with everyone in the group, always speaking openly and never singling anyone out for private chats.
Morning Star had already prepared himself for Ji Chenxi’s anger. After all, not only had he carried out their earlier plans without even informing Ji Chenxi first, but now he’d also tried to dig into his personal life.
But, unexpectedly, Ji Chenxi’s tone was calm.
“It’s true,” Ji Chenxi said lightly. “I do have an ambiguous relationship with an alpha.”
