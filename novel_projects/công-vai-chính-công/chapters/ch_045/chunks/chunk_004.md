“Your Highness,” Morning Star replied, “if you want to succeed, you can’t leave behind any weakness for others to use. The Royal Guard will be arriving soon—they’re there to protect you, yes, but also to watch you. You—”
Before he could finish, Ji Chenxi cut him off. “Chen Xing, the reason I chose to work with you in the beginning was because I saw your potential. You’re not like the others. Your background and knowledge make you the perfect candidate to be a nation’s teacher. But maybe that’s exactly the problem. You’re too driven by what you think benefits me—you make decisions without even asking. I hope you still remember who is the master and who is the servant between us.”
For the first time, Morning Star felt the sharp edge beneath Ji Chenxi’s usually mild demeanor. But instead of being alarmed, he laughed softly.
“Your Highness, three years ago, I knelt before you and pledged myself to your cause. Three years later, I still would. It’s just… during these years, you’ve made me wonder if there’s still any point in following you.”
“Is that so?” Ji Chenxi’s voice turned dangerously low.
“Of course,” Morning Star said with an almost teasing sincerity. “Even if the one you wanted… was me, I’d offer myself to you without a second thought.”
