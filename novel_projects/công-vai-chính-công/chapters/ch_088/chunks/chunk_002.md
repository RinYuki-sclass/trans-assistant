Once everyone was seated, servants began serving the meal.
Ji Chenxi cut into the piece of exotic beast meat on his plate, still streaked with blood. As his silver knife sliced down, the blood slowly seeped out.
The dish preserved the original taste as much as possible, but the strong smell of blood couldn’t be hidden. Ji Chenxi had hated this dish since he was a child. The other three at the table probably didn’t truly like it either, yet it was always the main dish at every family banquet.
Because it was an old tradition. As if eating bloody meat could show an alpha’s strength. As if a throne won through endless struggle and bloodshed could truly be called legitimate.
Outdated. Boring.
Ji Anze, pale-faced and having barely exchanged even polite words earlier, raised his wine glass first. “To our little princess, another year older.”
Ji Zhiqing didn’t look like any little princess. In terms of presence alone, she even outmatched Ji Anze. Still, she smiled and accepted it. “Thank you, big brother. I was lucky this time—not dying out there. Otherwise, I wouldn’t even have had the chance to celebrate this birthday.”
As she spoke, she smiled and took a sip of her wine.
Ji Chenxi kept working on his beast meat, acting like he didn’t hear the sarcasm in her words.
The entire banquet kept up a surface-level peace while tension simmered underneath. Ji Chenxi originally thought the fire wouldn’t reach him anymore. He had even messaged the butler to prepare some late-night snacks. As for the food on the table, he only took a few casual bites.
But just as he was waiting for the Emperor’s final words to wrap up the banquet nicely, his dear elder brother spoke again.
“I’ve been recovering in the hospital these past days. I heard Chenxi was triggered into a rut earlier by the Wen family’s youngest daughter.”
Ji Zhiqing followed up, “That did happen.”
Ji Anze looked at Ji Chenxi and smiled. “That incident was unpleasant indeed. The Lan and Wen families both crossed the line. But Chenxi… it might be time for you to find an omega.”
Ji Anze’s smile was gentle, and with the soft piano music in the background, he looked every bit like a caring older brother. Ji Chenxi suddenly felt the late-night snacks he ordered were pointless—his appetite was already gone.
The Emperor seemed to agree that Ji Chenxi should find an omega and get engaged first. Political marriages might lack love, but they were useful ties of interest.
Seated at the head, the Emperor asked, “Chenxi, do you have any omega in mind?”
Ji Chenxi feigned surprise, putting down his utensils. He looked a bit helpless yet amused. “I’m only 18, aren’t I? The way you’re talking, I’d think I was 28. Even big brother only got engaged recently. I’m not in a rush. I’ll look around first.”
“How long do you plan to ‘look around’?” Ji Anze asked.
Ji Chenxi smiled flawlessly. “I don’t know. I just think rushing won’t help. Maybe my future partner is still a kid right now.”
