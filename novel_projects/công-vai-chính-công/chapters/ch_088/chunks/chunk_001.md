Ji Chenxi’s expression didn’t change, and he even smiled as he shot back, “I heard Second Sister ran into trouble not long ago. Getting out of it so fast… I wonder who helped you?”
“Oh? So in your eyes, little brother, I can’t get myself out?” Ji Zhiqing raised a brow. “Just some interstellar drifters and a space storm. That’s nowhere near enough to take my life.”
Ji Chenxi gave a half-smile. “Second Sister makes a good point. I shouldn’t underestimate you. But… does that mean you think I can’t get through my rut on my own?”
“So you’re saying this is my fault?” Ji Zhiqing narrowed her eyes. Her sharp phoenix eyes looked especially aggressive when she did that. As she stared at Ji Chenxi’s face—one that didn’t lose to any omega—she couldn’t help but think of that woman. A face like this, whether omega or alpha, brought nothing but trouble.
The smile on Ji Chenxi’s face didn’t change. “Second Sister, I never said that.”
Ji Anze arrived a few steps later, perfectly missing their clash.
No one knew if it was to let them tear into each other more, or to make the three of them “bond,” but His Majesty the Emperor didn’t show up early this time. He only appeared right as the banquet was about to begin.
Ji Chenxi didn’t really care. He felt strangely detached, like he was both part of it and watching from the outside.
And from an outsider’s view, the whole banquet felt dull and unnecessary.
At seven in the evening, the Emperor arrived on time, and by then, the three had already stopped clashing and put away their hostility.
Among the three, Ji Anze had suffered the most losses, so he barely joined in their sarcastic exchanges. But behind his calm look, his eyes were dark and cold. It was obvious he believed this heavy loss was caused by either Ji Chenxi or Ji Zhiqing.
Ji Chenxi felt a bit regretful. Ji Anze had taken too big of a hit this time. The blame he had originally planned to shift onto his eldest brother was now hard to pass off. Not only that, Ji Zhiqing might have already seen through things and realized that her recent misfortune was his doing. Otherwise, she wouldn’t be targeting him like this now.
With everything going wrong, it was hard to feel happy.
Last time was one thing, but this time, the plan had been airtight, yet something still went wrong.
The one who had ordered Lan Ruoxi, and the one who nearly blew his eldest brother to death—if they were two different groups, fine. But if it was the same person, then that person was the real winner of this whole fight.
If Ji Zhiqing were a bit bolder, she might even think Ji Chenxi was playing the victim, that both her danger and Ji Anze’s were his doing. After all, running into rebels and running into space storms weren’t that different in nature.
Once the Emperor arrived, the tense mood eased a lot.
As the true focus of this family banquet, Ji Zhiqing smiled brightly and stepped forward to chat with the Emperor for quite a while.
In front of Ji Chenxi, she was sharp and aggressive, but in front of her father, she showed a bit of a daughter’s charm—playful but not clingy. Combined with the steady, commanding presence of an alpha she naturally gave off, she was far better at presenting herself than Ji Chenxi or his eldest brother.
Seeing his daughter act spoiled like this, the Emperor played the role of a loving father perfectly. Of course, he didn’t forget to show equal care, asking his two sons a couple of questions as well.
