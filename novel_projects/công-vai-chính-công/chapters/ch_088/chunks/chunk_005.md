“Bias. When the one in power already has a preferred answer, we’re just clowns dancing on stage. But who says clowns can’t become the main character?”
Ji Anze reached out and tucked a loose strand of her hair behind her ear. “Very confident.”
“Or maybe it’s just a clown trying to save herself.”
“No, Ji Zhiqing. I saw ambition in your eyes when you were very young. No need to dress it up.”
After she left, Ji Chenxi’s smile disappeared.
He had only walked a few steps when Wu Xiang appeared beside him. A luxurious hover car stopped in front of him, and the door opened automatically.
Ji Chenxi and Wu Xiang got in. Another alpha from the royal guard sat in the driver’s seat, and once they boarded, the car slowly began to move.
Silence filled the small space. Ji Chenxi played with an old, exquisite lighter in his hand. The flame flickered as his fingers moved, and each soft flare made the space feel even quieter.
“You know I have something to ask you,” Ji Chenxi said with a smile, breaking the silence.
Ji Chenxi smiled lightly. “What do you think of my second sister?”
“The Second Princess is highly intelligent.”
“And my big brother?”
“The First Prince is both capable and virtuous.”
“You really don’t want to offend anyone. Then what do you think of this game of ours? Who will be the final winner?”
Wu Xiang, who had been answering smoothly, suddenly fell silent. This was no longer something he could casually answer.
But after just two seconds, Wu Xiang dropped to one knee in front of Ji Chenxi. “Your Highness, it’s not about who I think will win. The final winner can only be you.”
With a soft click, the flame dancing in Ji Chenxi’s hand went out as he closed the lighter.
In the darkness, his smile turned cold. “But right now… I find it incredibly boring.”
