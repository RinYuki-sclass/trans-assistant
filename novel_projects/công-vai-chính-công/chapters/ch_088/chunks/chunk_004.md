Running into each other was normal—they came from the same place—but the timing was clearly intentional.
Ji Chenxi looked at her following behind and smiled. “Second Sister, need something?”
“My incident—it was your doing, wasn’t it?”
Ji Chenxi’s smile faded slightly. “I already said before, it wasn’t. If you don’t believe me, there’s nothing I can do.”
Ji Zhiqing laughed as well. After laughing, the bright and beautiful female alpha brushed her hair back. “Honestly, I already know the answer. I won’t tease you about it.”
Ji Chenxi knew she didn’t come just to say that, so he didn’t rush her and quietly waited.
“I don’t quite understand big brother’s move this time. What about you, little brother? Do you know what he’s trying to do?”
Ah, so she was testing him.
Ji Chenxi didn’t know if she already knew anything, but it didn’t affect his calm expression. “How would I know what big brother is planning?”
“I figured you wouldn’t. Little brother, in the current situation, who do you think has the upper hand?”
Ji Chenxi fell silent. Right now, the one with the upper hand… was the person standing in front of him.
“We’re fighting each other to the death for that position. But that position isn’t just power—it’s also a cage. Once we get it, we’ll be trapped in this palace forever, no longer free like we are now. If you really want that position, I can help you. But if you don’t, then help me instead. I can promise that once I take it, I’ll protect you and give you power no less than what you have now.”
Ji Zhiqing spoke with complete sincerity, nothing like the person who had just been digging at him earlier. But right now, only an idiot would believe her.
“I know you don’t trust me. But I really am your best ally. Big brother’s mother never got along with yours, but my mother and yours can at least speak to each other in this palace. You can think about it.”
Ji Chenxi smiled and agreed.
Ji Zhiqing nodded. When she returned to her hover car, she wasn’t surprised to see someone who shouldn’t be there.
