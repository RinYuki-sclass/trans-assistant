He explained simply: “The Red Scorpions were built from scratch by their captain, Poison Scorpion. The Red Scorpions are basically his kingdom, and Poison Scorpion… he’s really something.”
Not many people could be called “really something” by Chi Zhuo—and now even Ji Chenxi felt a little pressure.
The worst part? They still couldn’t jump. The local magnetic field had been completely disrupted, and multiple jump attempts had already failed. Such a large-scale manipulation of the field was noticeable to anyone investigating. Yet even after all this, the enemy was still trying to take Ji Chenxi out.
Ji Chenxi’s hands left afterimages as he tried to ignore the chaotic magnetic field entirely to make a jump, but once again, it failed. Meanwhile, the ship’s display flashed warnings under repeated attacks.
Chi Zhuo coldly muttered, “Maybe I should just hand you over.”
Ji Chenxi checked quickly to see if he was serious or joking, then pouted pitifully. “Don’t! How can our partnership be so fragile?”
“And if I did, what could you do?”
Ji Chenxi smiled, a little innocent in the curve of his lips. “What could I do? At most, I’d just drag Brother Chi Zhuo down with me.”
