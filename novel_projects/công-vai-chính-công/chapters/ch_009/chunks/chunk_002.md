Ji Chenxi had already guessed the answer earlier. But royal feuds weren’t exactly the kind of thing you shared with outsiders. So he just put on a smile and played dumb. “Isn’t it obvious? Right now, isn’t it the Red Scorpions?”
“Of course it’s them. But what I’m asking is—how did His Highness manage to piss off the Red Scorpions in the first place?”
“I wouldn’t know about that,” Ji Chenxi said with a faint smile on his lips, though his eyes carried a chill.
Chi Zhuo didn’t press further about who was backing the Red Scorpions. Instead, he stepped up to the console, pushed the ship’s speed even higher with one hand—while still holding his drink in the other.
Not only that, he casually fired off two photon blasts behind them. Each one landed perfectly. The first hit the pirate ship that was pressing the hardest, and the one right behind it smashed straight into the wreck. In just a heartbeat, the collision left both ships wobbling and out of control. The second blast finished them off, blowing them to pieces—and the explosion even slowed down the other two ships chasing after them.
The smoothness of that whole move lit up Ji Chenxi’s eyes.
‘Single-handed, and pulling that off? Now that’s interesting.’
The book hadn’t mentioned that Chi Zhuo was good at piloting too. After all, bastard son or not, he was still the only heir of the Chi family. Normally, he had people doing everything for him.
Chi Zhuo didn’t push for a head-on fight though. Sure, their ship’s equipment was solid, but against an entire pirate crew it was still risky. So after those two beautiful shots, he let go of the controls and left the flying back to the middle-aged man.
“Can we call in help from the nearest fleet?” the younger man asked while tapping on his wrist console.
Chi Zhuo brushed that aside. “Contact my men nearby first.”
But not far from them, more and more light dots lit up the radar—ships closing in fast. Whoever wanted Ji Chenxi dead wasn’t about to let this go.
Chi Zhuo, having just given one order, quickly changed his mind. “Forget that. Reach out to the nearby systems, all of them. Make it quick.”
“Yes!” the young man answered, sending out messages at once.
Meanwhile, Bai Han was assisting the middle-aged pilot. Compared to Chi Zhuo’s sharp, flashy moves, her support seemed steady but plain.
Ji Chenxi walked over and pointed at the man on the floor who was still twitching. “Hey, shouldn’t you take a look at him?”
Bai Han glanced back at Chi Zhuo, waiting for her master’s call.
Chi Zhuo swirled the glowing glass in his hand, voice cold. “Give him the antidote.”
Bai Han didn’t hesitate after that. She crouched down beside Shadow and injected him with another dose.
The young man who had finished sending out messages joined Nether in watching the screen. Neither of them relaxed just because the two groups were working together for now. On the contrary, they kept their guard up, watching each other closely for any slip.
