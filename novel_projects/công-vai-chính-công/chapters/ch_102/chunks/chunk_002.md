Between the two of them, who had ever truly trusted the other? Chen Xing had simply struck first.
“Hah? Hahahahaha…” After a mocking chuckle, Ji Zhiqing burst into loud laughter. Thick streams of dark red blood poured from her mouth. “The winner takes all… I was just not skilled enough. But do you really think… if I die, you’ll… walk away unscathed?”
“That won’t be something Your Highness needs to worry about.”
After Ji Zhiqing collapsed, Chen Xing wiped his hands with visible disgust, rubbing them over and over again. A small flying device hovered beside him, reporting in a flat mechanical voice that the Third Leader had been found on the underground floor of the control center.
With both his right-hand and left-hand men dead one after another, there wasn’t a trace of sorrow in Chen Xing’s eyes. After carefully cleaning his hands, he spoke softly toward the distance:
“Now that the irrelevant people have mostly been cleared out, there’s no need for Your Highness to keep hiding.”
“Irrelevant people? Second Sister would be heartbroken to hear that.” Ji Chenxi hadn’t expected to stay hidden forever. Now that he’d been pointed out, he simply stepped out openly.
Chen Xing let out a light laugh. “Are you satisfied with the gift I prepared for you?”
“You killed her now, which means everything is no longer justified. Or is it that you want to take that position yourself, Chen Xing?”
“Maybe I’m paving the way for you.”
Ji Chenxi shook his head, rejecting the idea. “If you were paving the way for me, you wouldn’t have tried to kill me again and again. If you were paving the way for me, you wouldn’t be standing against me right now. Just admit it—what you want is a puppet that listens to you. You want that puppet to be ‘perfect’ as you define it, yet you don’t want it to show even the slightest defiance. That just proves how arrogant you are. From the very start, what you wanted wasn’t a perfect successor, but a perfect marionette—one that could spread through the entire empire and let you become the true power behind it.”
Chen Xing’s gaze at Ji Chenxi first showed a hint of surprise, then he smiled. “If you were just a little more obedient, this would’ve been a perfect plan. Haven’t we gotten along quite well?”
Gotten along well?
Ji Chenxi let out a cold laugh, but he had something more important to figure out.
“The banned drug used at Chi Zhuo’s banquet—you had a hand in that too, didn’t you?”
“Pretending not to know is pointless. After all, besides being the one behind Cocoon, you’re also a famous researcher. As a researcher, how could you not know about ‘Miracle’? Back then, you reacted so strongly—was it because you thought me being with an alpha was a stain, or because you thought I had defiled your ‘Miracle’?”
Just knowing Chi Zhuo’s level and reading the logs labeled “Miracle” had already made Ji Chenxi suspect that Chi Zhuo was a descendant of the experimental subject “Miracle.” So would the people who had actually handled the experiment really never find that descendant?
At some point, Chen Xing must have learned Chi Zhuo’s true identity. And since Chi Zhuo had always claimed to be S-rank, what happened at the banquet had definitely been a test—a probe, and also a way to push Bai Anran forward. Ji Chenxi didn’t know all the details, but it wasn’t hard to guess. What else could two high-level AOs do when brought together? The answer was obvious—reproduction. The offspring of two high-level, well-matched AOs had a chance of surpassing their parents.
The drug at the banquet, the strange man encountered on that small planet, that human experiment lab, Chen Xing’s bait at the auction—all those blurry threads suddenly connected in that moment.
“Ah, ‘Miracle’!” Chen Xing chuckled softly. “That was actually information I left behind on purpose.”
