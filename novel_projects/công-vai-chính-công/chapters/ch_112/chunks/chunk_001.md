The space that was originally still collapsing felt like someone had hit a pause button.
At the very center of the space, Ji Chenxi didn’t say much. He only gave a very light smile while looking at Chi Zhuo, as if he was trying to confirm something, or maybe trying to see through his very nature with just his eyes.
“People always wrap their lies up so nicely, so sweetly. How am I supposed to know yours isn’t just another lie?”
“Whether it’s a lie or not, Your Highness should at least stabilize your spiritual domain first before slowly verifying it, don’t you think?”
“But right now, it’s about to collapse.” Ji Chenxi threw out the problem.
Chi Zhuo let out a soft laugh. “Your Highness, this is your domain. As long as you want it, you can restore everything back to normal in an instant.”
Ji Chenxi also smiled at that. It was true—if he pulled himself out of that unstable state and stopped spiraling further, then this place was entirely under his control. As long as he wished, he could restore his spiritual domain to its original state.
With a shift of thought, the entire space began to change and reorganize. In the end, it formed into a towering ice plain. On top of the ice fields, countless fiery red roses wrapped in frost were planted.
A graceful snow leopard walked over to Chi Zhuo’s side and curled the tip of its tail around his leg.
In this familiar scene, Ji Chenxi no longer kept up that fake cold distance. He smiled brightly. “Welcome to my spiritual domain. Want to go skiing?”
“Compared to skiing, I’d rather…”
Chi Zhuo walked closer. Ji Chenxi waited for the rest of his words, but the man directly leaned in and kissed him on the lips. Between kisses, he murmured out the unfinished sentence.
“…kiss Your Highness.”
Men really were good at sweet talk. Still, Ji Chenxi quite liked it—just like how Chi Zhuo still hadn’t changed how he addressed him back to “Your Highness.”
Things done inside a spiritual domain were different from reality. After all, this was his space. He could do whatever he wanted. And Chi Zhuo was only a consciousness that had entered—what could he possibly use to resist him?
The moment snow brushed through their senses, Chi Zhuo let out a helpless sigh. Even as his body trembled slightly, he deepened the kiss instead.
Ji Chenxi even “helped” him by pinning a vivid rose onto him. When his fingers brushed past it and removed the thorns, the rose trembled—its petals shaking like they were about to fall from a passing breeze.
After a long while, the rose finally couldn’t hold on anymore. Petals scattered everywhere, staining the white snow with bright red color.
Ji Chenxi woke up at night.
Chi Zhuo was lying quietly beside him, eyes closed, exhaustion written all over his face. He still hadn’t woken up.
