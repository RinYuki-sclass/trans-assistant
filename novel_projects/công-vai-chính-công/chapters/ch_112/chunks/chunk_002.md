Looks like he played a bit too far.
His pale fingers touched the man’s cheek, stopping just near his lips.
Repairing a spiritual domain was dangerous and extremely delicate work. Aside from the special compatibility between an Alpha and Omega that could stabilize parts of it, there were basically no proper treatments—and even medical drugs for it were extremely rare. That was also why Chi Zhuo had previously gone into spiritual disorder just from a small exposure to illegal drugs.
And yet now, this man had actually sent his own spiritual consciousness straight into Ji Chenxi’s spiritual domain. He didn’t even seem afraid of dying inside it.
His nails pressed lightly, leaving a faint half-moon mark on the man’s face.
Was this… favoritism?
People always had bias in their hearts. There would always be someone they favored more.
Ji Chenxi’s growth path was complicated. He lacked love, yet he had also received quite a bit of it—but someone directly choosing to favor him like this? That was a first.
Birds of a feather flock together, and people always drew boundaries with lines like “we’re not the same kind of people.” Ji Chenxi and Chi Zhuo, to outsiders, were clearly from different worlds.
Two people from different circles, like parallel lines—yet somehow they kept intersecting and entangling.
Fate was a strange thing. But maybe… it wasn’t entirely fate.
From their first meeting, there had clearly been a chance for a fatal strike. So why did it end with hesitation instead? Was it really just bad timing? No opportunity?
Ji Chenxi quietly smiled into the night. He didn’t want to admit that from the very beginning, he had his own selfish intent.
So… it was still fate’s intersection after all.
After staring at Chi Zhuo for a long while, Ji Chenxi walked to the window, intending to pull the curtains shut for a better sleeping environment—only to unexpectedly find a firefly outside.
The sight made him pause slightly, and he didn’t rush to close the curtains.
Ji Chenxi had made some noise when he woke up, but after moving away from Chi Zhuo, he was as light as a cat—silent as he landed. Even so, Chi Zhuo still woke up.
Seeing a firefly floating uncertainly above Ji Chenxi’s fingertips, Chi Zhuo stepped closer and called out hoarsely, “Your Highness.”
Ji Chenxi released the firefly—still gently held in place by his spiritual power—and turned back to him. “This isn’t a natural habitat for fireflies. I barely ever see them in the palace. The last time I saw one was when my mother took me to a forest park on a nearby planet.”
Chi Zhuo followed his gaze and said, “If you want to see them, I can accompany you every year from now on.”
