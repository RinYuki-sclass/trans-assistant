“So now you’re planning to give up that position?”
“We’ll see.”
“Chenxi, if you really want to fight for that spot, there are things you shouldn’t touch. Even if you do, it should be after everything’s settled. Are you really not afraid Chi Zhuo might stab you in the back?”
“I don’t know,” Ji Chenxi said lightly. “I don’t think he will. And I did want to keep a low profile, you know. It’s just… I really can’t manage it.”
He was still smiling when he said it.
“I think I shouldn’t keep trying to talk you out of this.”
“Hm? So you’re saying you’re not going to anymore?”
“Yeah. Feels like there’s no way to stop you.” Su Yu sighed. “You’ve got your own plans. You know exactly what you want and what you don’t. If you think this is right, then go for it. But there’s one thing I have to say—do you really call this keeping a low profile? This is straight-up showing off in public. Those fireworks were his doing, right?”
“You’re pretty sharp.”
“Obviously.”
“I’ll think of an excuse then. Hmm—” Ji Chenxi pretended to ponder. “Love just can’t be hidden?”
“Tch.” Su Yu couldn’t stand it anymore. “Stop, stop. This is way too sickly sweet.”
Ji Chenxi parted ways with his friends in a noticeably good mood.
Before the hovercar even reached the estate, Ji Chenxi told them to stop. He asked Major General Wu Xiang to take the car back first. Wu Xiang naturally didn’t like the idea of leaving Ji Chenxi alone, but in the end, he still agreed under Ji Chenxi’s smiling insistence.
Under the silver glow of the moon, Ji Chenxi walked on at an easy pace.
As he moved forward, the alcohol-scented pheromones around him—so faint they’d barely been noticeable—slowly grew richer.
The shift was controlled just right. Silent and subtle, yet enough for anyone with sharp senses to pick up on it.
Ji Chenxi laughed to himself. He’d noticed long ago that someone was escorting him home, yet he pretended not to notice at all, letting those pheromones quietly wrap around him.
Halfway through the walk, just as Ji Chenxi was starting to think Chi Zhuo planned to play the role of an anonymous “knight in shining armor,” his wrist terminal gave a soft vibration.
【Your Highness, I’ve noticed your sense of caution seems a bit lacking.】
