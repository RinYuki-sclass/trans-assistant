For a fleeting moment, the usually lazy and indifferent Ji Chenxi thought he had misheard.
Was this guy teasing him?
A long, elegant finger casually lifted a strand of silver hair, rubbing it lightly. “Not sure if I should say this.”
Ji Chenxi sometimes had a strange, subtle politeness, like he was afraid his words might hurt someone.
Chi Zhuo’s dark eyes locked onto Ji Chenxi, watching his every tiny movement under the harsh white light. A faint, almost imperceptible curve appeared on his lips. “Your Highness, just say whatever you mean.”
Ji Chenxi had been waiting for exactly this line. He tucked the silver strand behind his ear and smiled brightly, letting a truth slip out: “But even if you’re an alpha, haven’t I already marked you twice? If you were an omega…”
After a brief pause, Ji Chenxi continued with a tone almost like a sigh, “That’s not something you can solve just by marking without hesitation.”
No matter how weak an alpha was, these words alone were sharp enough to wound. And Chi Zhuo, a high-ranking alpha who had held power for years, was certainly not someone who could be dismissed lightly.
He should have been angry, but Chi Zhuo, who could read Ji Chenxi’s every expression clearly, showed not a trace of fury. Instead, his chest involuntarily skipped a beat.
At this moment, Ji Chenxi’s flippant words didn’t feel like a declaration of war—they felt more like… flirting.
Chi Zhuo forced down the unusual thump of his heart and followed Ji Chenxi’s lead. “Oh? And what else does Your Highness want to do?”
“Huh?” Ji Chenxi couldn’t hold back his laugh this time.
“You asked me what I want to do. What can an alpha do to an omega? Of course, I’d f— into your… reproductive cavity.” Ji Chenxi’s words were loud and bold, crude yet direct, hitting desires in a way only such blatant talk could. When he reached the last word, he softened it just slightly—a subtle drop in tone that turned what should have been an audacious, arrogant line into something low, sultry, and intimate, like a lover whispering in secret.
Chi Zhuo was momentarily stunned by the audacity of the words.
He quickly rejected the idea. No way. Even if he really were an omega, Ji Chenxi wouldn’t mark him directly. And as for something like a reproductive cavity… that would never be spoken aloud.
These words were meant for Chi Zhuo as an alpha, and no one else.
“Even if I’m not an omega, Your Highness could still give it a try. Just… not sure who’s going to end up inside whose reproductive cavity.” By the end, Chi Zhuo’s voice had dropped low, and even his eyes seemed impossibly deep, unreadable.
“That’s really hard to say.”
Ji Chenxi laughed, narrowing his eyes at Chi Zhuo. His blue gaze radiated a predatory danger, like a beast about to hunt.
A single squint revealed all the sharp disdain in his eyes, arrogance dripping off him as if he were staring at some reckless fool speaking nonsense.
