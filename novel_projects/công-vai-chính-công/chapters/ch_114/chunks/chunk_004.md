He found the scenes he had forgotten. He also saw everyone’s ending.
It was a book mainly from Bai Anran’s perspective. Many things were only briefly mentioned, but one thing was clear and impossible to hide—Ji Chenxi died by Chi Zhuo’s hand in the end.
Chi Zhuo only read it once, then put it back and left Rose Manor.
Coincidentally—or maybe not—he ran into Bai Anran on his way there.
Bai Anran was a bit surprised to see Chi Zhuo, but quickly returned to polite calm and greeted him.
Chi Zhuo originally didn’t plan to respond. But when he was about one meter away from passing him, he stopped.
“You and Ji Chenxi…”
“Hmm?” Bai Anran quickly turned back.
Chi Zhuo then walked forward again, leaving only one sentence behind.
“Nothing.”
Chi Zhuo felt a little ridiculous.
He was uneasy. Afraid Ji Chenxi would, like in the book, fall for Bai Anran again.
He wanted to ask, “You and Ji Chenxi have known each other for many years?” But there was no need. If he wanted to know, he could already find out everything.
The people he sent earlier finally brought back the most accurate news—the palace was preparing a wedding.
When the subordinate said this, he even looked at Chi Zhuo carefully. Everyone knew their master was in a relationship with the young emperor.
“Not bad,” Chi Zhuo only said two words.
Ji Chenxi getting married meant the other person could only be him. Even if this secretly prepared wedding made him feel like an outsider.
After returning and being busy for half a month, Ji Chenxi finally came to find Chi Zhuo.
As soon as Chi Zhuo entered the palace, he could tell everything inside had been quickly decorated. The luxurious palace now carried a faint sense of joy.
He walked slowly and arrived at Ji Chenxi’s palace.
