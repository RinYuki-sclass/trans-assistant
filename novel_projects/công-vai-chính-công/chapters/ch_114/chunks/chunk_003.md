In the book, Ji Chenxi liked Bai Anran and even did many things for him. Chi Zhuo had only flipped through it quickly back then, not paying attention to details. He had just been curious why Ji Chenxi even had such a strange book.
But once he connected Ji Chenxi’s past with his own first meeting… it was obvious. Ji Chenxi probably read that book and went to that banquet because of it.
Their first meeting started from that book. So what exactly was that story supposed to mean?
Chi Zhuo didn’t want to care at first, but the more he tried not to think about it, the more it bothered him.
That night, Chi Zhuo even had a dream.
In the dream, Bai Anran walked step by step through a rainy night. His clothes were half transparent from the rain, his hair stuck together in wet strands. The Omega looked fragile and exhausted, but still carried a strange toughness like grass bending in the wind.
Not far behind him, there was a beautiful Alpha wearing fancy clothes, a silver half-cape, and ice-like decorations on his body. His beauty was almost blinding, but the sharpness in his eyes made it clear he wasn’t an Omega.
The Alpha didn’t speak. He just held an umbrella and followed behind the Omega. Cold, distant. He didn’t look like a lover—more like a stranger passing by who just didn’t want to see someone get hurt.
But Chi Zhuo knew Ji Chenxi too well.
That outfit alone already showed he had dressed carefully. Even his usual glass bead earrings were replaced with ice-like ones matching his clothes. And the fact that he kept following like that… it already meant something.
In the dream, Ji Chenxi even tried to hand the umbrella forward, but hesitated. In the end, he let himself get half soaked by the rain too.
Ji Chenxi hated getting wet the most, but in the dream he didn’t seem to care at all.
Finally, he held the black umbrella over Bai Anran and said coldly, “The umbrella is too heavy. Help me hold it.”
“Your Highness, you don’t have to care about me,” Bai Anran said.
“Who said I care about you? I just said it’s heavy. And since you look so pitiful, I’ll share it with you,” Ji Chenxi replied.
“Your Highness, if you act like this, you won’t be able to find an Omega in the future,” Bai Anran said.
“Then I won’t find one,” Ji Chenxi answered.
Bai Anran didn’t react much. He just sighed and took the umbrella, pulling himself out of his pain for a moment.
Chi Zhuo knew Ji Chenxi too well. This was clearly his awkward way of comforting someone.
The next morning, Chi Zhuo took out that black-covered book again. This time he didn’t flip through it casually. He read it carefully from beginning to end.
