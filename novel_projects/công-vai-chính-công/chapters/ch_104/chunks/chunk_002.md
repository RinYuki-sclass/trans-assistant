“We’ve known each other for years, after all. I think I should try to persuade you. Right now, it’s just my spirit form stopping you. Next, it’ll be the mamba. You’ve studied the human body for so many years—you should already know how many people that mamba’s spirit form has eaten, right?”
Was the seven-meter black mamba really Chi Zhuo’s full strength? Of course not. If that were the case, it wouldn’t live up to Ares being 3S-class. Their earlier clash had been brief, but Chen Xing had already made a judgment. At the very least, the other party was double S, and very likely Chi Zhuo was 3S.
A 3S-class alpha—when his spirit form revealed its true shape, what kind of scene would that be? Chen Xing thought of Ares. That man had lost control even with absolute defense and overwhelming force, wiping out an entire laboratory. The higher the level, the harder it was to control one’s power, and the easier it was to lose control. Chen Xing did not want to face an out-of-control giant snake, but he also knew there was no turning back once the bowstring had been pulled.
“Lu Tu, it’s a bit late for you to try to persuade me now.”
“If I tried earlier, you might not have listened anyway!”
“You’re going to stop me?”
“You’re putting me in a tough spot like this. But I’ve decided to help you once.”
Chen Xing was slightly surprised, and then heard Lu Tu continue, “Next time we meet, I won’t go easy on you. After all, I’m quite curious whether, after getting past me, you can escape from that person.”
The massive red bird suddenly flapped its wings. Even the sturdy starship was blown away. The giant bird spat flames, setting the ship on fire. When the ship’s emergency system activated, its speed shot up much faster than before.
Lu Tu faintly heard someone on the other side cursing. That voice clearly didn’t belong to Chen Xing. Lu Tu smiled in delight. “My friend, good luck.”
