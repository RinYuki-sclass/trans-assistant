After Ji Chenxi met up with Chi Zhuo, he first hacked into Chen Xing’s robots. Then he stayed in constant contact with Gu Hechuan, even using Gu Hechuan’s starship to check on Chen Xing’s situation.
He couldn’t hack into the artificial humans at all. Their structure was very different from robots, and the way they took orders wasn’t the same either. Ji Chenxi had originally been ready to give up—until he noticed the modified human beside Chen Xing. That guy was clearly someone whose body had been almost completely altered except for his brain.
So Ji Chenxi took a near reckless gamble and used his spiritual power remotely to sway that person.
The odds of success were extremely low. The reason it worked was probably because Chen Xing hadn’t fully trusted that modified human from the start. The guy clearly still had a human brain, but inside his body there was also a system similar to that of a robot.
Chen Xing’s own caution ended up becoming the final push.
At this moment, Ji Chenxi’s spiritual domain was injured, but the result was good. Still, facing Chi Zhuo, he couldn’t help feeling a little guilty.
Just one simple “Does it still hurt?” made that guilt even worse.
Without saying much, he pulled Chi Zhuo closer and pressed a kiss onto his lips. The kiss carried a faint smell of blood and was far from pleasant, yet it was unexpectedly gentle.
“You know this is the best outcome,” Ji Chenxi said softly after pulling away, trying to defend himself a little.
“I also know I don’t want to see you get hurt,” Chi Zhuo said, brushing his fingers over Ji Chenxi’s lips that had only recently been stained with blood. His tone left no room for refusal. “Last time.”
No one knew whether that “last time” was meant for Ji Chenxi—or for himself.
Ji Chenxi could only stay silent. It was hard for anyone to guarantee they’d never get hurt again. He could only say he’d try his best not to end up in a situation like today again.
He didn’t give himself much time to recover his aching spiritual domain. Instead, he followed Chi Zhuo in reorganizing the internal structure of the palace, purging everyone with hidden intentions in one sweep. As for the accusations tied to him because of his alliance with Chi Zhuo, Ji Chenxi ignored them completely.
Ji Zhiqing’s death had been a trap set by Chen Xing for Ji Chenxi, meant to use the First and Second Legions to restrain him. Now, one legion was being held down by their own people, and the other had its commander killed by Ji Chenxi on the spot—no longer a threat.
Snakes swarming everywhere, all vying for the throne. Tonight was destined to be a night soaked in blood.
Ji Chenxi had never been like this before—killing, intimidating, everything done in one smooth flow, leaving no way back for anyone.
By the time everything was finally handled, it was already very late.
The entire palace was brightly lit, glowing like daytime. Ji Chenxi almost didn’t realize it was about to dawn.
Darkness faded. Dawn was approaching.
With iron-blooded methods, Ji Chenxi had already shaken many people. He pressed on through a splitting headache and exhaustion, dealing with the rebel forces, and then handling the deaths of his father and sister.
