In reality, making his eldest brother and second sister clash was only one small part of the plan. The old man wanted to draw Ji Chenxi into the game, so he deliberately didn’t assign him a bodyguard, forcing him to be pushed into the situation by the two of them.
No matter how complicated his feelings for him had become, he had once been someone he truly cared for. So when he got hurt right under his eyes, could he really stay indifferent?
A power struggle only begins once the waters are fully stirred, and he was the most innocent player in this game.
After replying to the messages, Ji Chenxi let out a soft sigh. His fingers swiped, and somehow he was back at the very first message from Chi Zhuo.
It felt like quite a long time had passed.
He tapped his fingers lightly and, clearly in a good mood, typed out a few words:
[So concerned about my recent situation? By the way, Brother Chi Zhuo, are you flirting with me?]
Chi Zhuo was busy handling some family businesses, reviewing profits and planning future directions. He gave a few suggestions for adjustments, and it wasn’t until the third time he looked at the photo of Ji Chenxi protecting Lu Churan with his own injury that he finally received Ji Chenxi’s reply.
Asking such a direct question when the two had no relationship — Chi Zhuo had expected him to be annoyed, but instead, the response was so candid.
Chi Zhuo’s finger brushed over the word ‘flirting’, pretending not to see it as he asked:
[I heard His Highness was injured protecting the beautiful omega lady.]
Ji Chenxi raised an eyebrow slightly, his mood lifting, and his handsome features relaxed with a faint smile.
[Yes, I got this wound on my hand for that beautiful omega lady. It really hurt.]
Chi Zhuo’s normally dark expression deepened even further, as black as water. He clicked his tongue in displeasure but didn’t say more about the omega. He glanced over the woman’s profile—she wasn’t just some simple girl. All those online rumors about a wealthy heiress instantly falling for the Third Prince were nonsense. If Ji Chenxi wasn’t careful, he could even end up getting caught off guard.
When it came to disobedient children, the usual approach was to give them a harsh lesson. But Chi Zhuo was willing to be a bit gentler, so he typed:
[Third Prince, that Lu family lady runs deep waters.]
The reminder made Ji Chenxi’s expression flicker strangely. At first, he had assumed Chi Zhuo, like himself, was merely curious about her. Now, it seemed Chi Zhuo might actually be serious.
For a moment, Ji Chenxi didn’t even know how to respond.
Then another message arrived, replying to his very first one:
[And if I am flirting with Your Highness, what would you do?]
