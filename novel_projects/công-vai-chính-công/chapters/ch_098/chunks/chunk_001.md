Chi Zhuo lowered his gaze, letting his eyes rest for a long time on that hand that was still dripping blood.
The fingers trembled slightly from the pain—an instinctive reaction of the body—but Ji Chenxi himself still wore a calm, indifferent expression.
For a powerful alpha, not fearing pain seemed perfectly normal, but in the end, everyone was made of flesh and blood, not someone with a broken sense of pain—how could they not feel it?
“Hm?” Chi Zhuo’s voice was so low and soft that Ji Chenxi almost thought he had misheard.
Chi Zhuo lifted the injured hand, carefully wiping away the blood and dirt clinging to it. He examined the wound that was still oozing blood, and a heavy, blocked feeling spread from his chest to his limbs. After gently wiping away the blood again, he asked, “Doesn’t it hurt?”
“A little, I guess. It’s not that painful.”
Chi Zhuo didn’t say anything else. He went to a vending robot not far away, bought a bottle of medical spray, and carefully sprayed it over Ji Chenxi’s hand.
During the process, Ji Chenxi let out a soft hiss. It hurt a bit. That faint sound of pain had barely escaped before he quickly pulled it back in. It was so fast that if Chi Zhuo hadn’t been standing right beside him, he probably wouldn’t have heard it.
“Didn’t Your Highness just say it didn’t hurt much?”
Ji Chenxi lowered his eyes to look at the wound on his hand, then hissed again softly. His eyelids drooped, and he looked a little listless. “I think it actually hurts quite a lot. I’m already in so much pain—Brother Chi Zhuo wouldn’t still scold me, right?”
Under the spray and cotton swabs, the blood on his hand was quickly cleaned away, and even the small shards of glass embedded in the flesh were carefully removed by Chi Zhuo.
The spray had some hemostatic effect. After the wound was treated carefully, what remained were just a few cuts from smashing the glass too hard.
Looking at that red and swollen hand covered in small wounds, the look in Chi Zhuo’s lowered eyes grew darker and harder to read. His fingers lightly brushed over the back of Ji Chenxi’s uninjured hand, and he said in a low voice, “I’m not scolding you. I’m just worried about you.”
Ji Chenxi fell silent for a moment, suddenly feeling a bit at a loss. Chi Zhuo’s attitude was too serious. Because of that seriousness, all those pretty, teasing words were hard to say. In the end, everything turned into a simple sentence: “I’m fine.”
Chi Zhuo brought the injured hand to his lips and gently kissed a spot that wasn’t hurt. The bitter taste of medicine passed through his lips and spread through his body. He calmly responded with a soft “Mm.”
Ji Chenxi was indeed fine. But in a completely sealed room, with an explosion like that—what if? What if Ji Chenxi had been even a few seconds slower getting out?
He had always made the best choice for himself in every danger, but it all seemed too painful. Being pierced in the gland by a sharp needle hurt. Smashing tough glass with bare hands hurt just as much. None of this was unavoidable. As long as he protected Ji Chenxi well enough… The sight of that bright red blood made him want to lock him away in his own fortress, raise high walls, and keep the noble little prince all to himself. But reason kept reminding him again and again—this was an alpha, not something he could keep as a possession.
“Chi Zhuo?” Ji Chenxi frowned as he looked at him. He could sense something was off about Chi Zhuo, but couldn’t pinpoint what. So he simply pulled his hand back and openly pulled the man into a hug. “I already said I’m fine.”
“You clearly aren’t. I didn’t protect my little alpha well.”
