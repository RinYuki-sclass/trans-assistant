Ji Chenxi couldn’t help but laugh. “Whose little alpha doesn’t face things alone and slowly grow up? It’s not like a parent can stay by their side for everything. A little alpha will grow up someday too.”
This was still the academy, and there had just been an explosion. The two of them hugging and being this close was way too eye-catching. Even if Chi Zhuo’s subordinates were trying to control the scene, there were bound to be a few eyes that saw them.
Ji Chenxi wasn’t unaware of what consequences this might bring, but he didn’t really care. It wasn’t anything he couldn’t deal with.
Queqi only heard about the explosion half an hour later. At that time, she was sitting in her room drinking black tea. When she heard, her brows knit tightly, and then she said, half helpless and half regretful, “He made a move.”
“This is earlier than I expected. It doesn’t match Chen Xing’s usual habits,” another male voice said.
“You know him well?”
“Not exactly. But since Chen Xing has chosen to act openly, hasn’t he contacted you?”
Queqi set down her cup, her eyes narrowing slightly. “Wangtu, don’t assume that just because I once pursued Chen Xing, I’ll definitely stand on his side.”
“But you really liked him back then. You even gave up benefits and paved the way for him.”
“He was the one who gave way for me first. At most, I just returned the favor. But I’ll admit, I did like him. After all, it’s hard for a confused teenage girl not to be moved when she meets someone who helps her so much and even changes her life.”
“Plus, he’s pretty good-looking?” The man laughed.
Queqi showed a hint of displeasure toward the person on the other side of the screen. “You’re being too blunt.”
After Wangtu toned down his laughter, Queqi continued, “Bai Wei isn’t planning to come to us.”
“It’s right that he doesn’t. You and Chen Xing are closer. Not contacting you is to avoid putting you in a difficult position. Besides, your power base isn’t even on the Imperial Star. As for me, same reason. No matter which side I take, I’m far away from the Imperial Star, and my influence isn’t there either. Even if I rush over nonstop, I won’t make it in time for their clash. And we’ve known each other for years—no matter how well we get along, it’s still not the same as his relationship with Chen Xing. Now that he can’t even trust Chen Xing, why would he disturb me? Not informing anyone and acting alone is the right move.”
“You and Chen Xing are the same. Neither of you value the bond we’ve built over the years.”
Wangtu could talk about everything lightly before because he didn’t really care—everything was just for fun, just for his own amusement. But hearing the omega’s seemingly calm yet lonely words, he still chose to comfort her. “Maybe it’s because when Chen Xing and I met you, we were already mature adults, while you two were still growing teenagers.”
“Then who do you think will win?”
“In the past, I’d say Chen Xing had a higher chance. The Cocoon’s influence is huge. Compared to a cocoon, I think they’re more like spiders—silent, and by the time you notice, their web has already spread everywhere. And his research is pretty interesting too. But now, I think Bai Wei shouldn’t be underestimated either.”
“Don’t you know? The one by Bai Wei’s side is the Mamba—the uncrowned king of the underground world.”
“Crazy. Completely crazy.”
