Switching to another device, Chi Zhuo spoke gently, “Your Highness, I’ll need a bit of your blood for testing.”
Ji Chenxi lowered his eyes to look at the sharp silver needle, then pulled his hand back and tucked it against his stomach. “It hurts.”
Chi Zhuo: “…I’ll be gentle.”
Ji Chenxi still didn’t move.
“I’ll be very gentle. Like a feather barely touching you.”
Only then did Ji Chenxi reluctantly stretch out a single finger. Chi Zhuo quickly disinfected it, drew the blood, and placed the sample into the device for analysis.
Ji Chenxi stared at his fingertip, where a tiny bead of blood was still forming, not moving at all. A small cotton ball was already pressed lightly against it, and a compact healing device began treating both his finger and wrist.
“So now Your Highness knows it hurts,” Chi Zhuo said, voice low. “Why didn’t it hurt when you were hurting yourself earlier?”
“It did hurt… just not as much as wanting to mark someone and not being able to.”
Chi Zhuo paused, something clicking in his mind. The heavy smell of blood hadn’t faded even after they left Sun-Moon Lake… was it really just from the cuts on his wrist?
He reached out, intending to remove the suppressor around Ji Chenxi’s neck. This time, Ji Chenxi immediately leaned back, his expression turning cold.
“What are you trying to do?”
“Your Highness,” Chi Zhuo said softly, “you can release your pheromones now.”
“Ha… trying to trick me.”
This time, Chi Zhuo didn’t hold back—he moved straight in. Ji Chenxi, who had seemed gentle and harmless just a moment ago, immediately raised his hand to block.
In just a few breaths, they had already exchanged dozens of moves. One was trying to rip off the suppressor, the other forcing himself to protect it. Even their spiritual strength came into play as they clashed.
In the end, Chi Zhuo—pinned down against the bed—still managed to shatter the suppressor into pieces.
The moment it broke, a bitter rose scent flooded the entire room.
Chi Zhuo’s raised hand trembled slightly. There were dark red bloodstains on the broken suppressor… Ji Chenxi’s gland was clearly injured. Chi Zhuo reached out, wanting to touch it. He had obviously noticed something—his fingers trembled, hesitating, almost afraid to make contact… yet he still reached forward.
Just before he could touch Ji Chenxi, his hand was stopped.
