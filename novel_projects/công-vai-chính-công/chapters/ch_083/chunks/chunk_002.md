Ji Chenxi hadn’t forgotten what this man had just said. “Of course it’s natural for an alpha to be drawn to an omega—but I held back. I even stayed here waiting for you for quite a while.”
“And how long did Your Highness wait for me?”
Without even thinking, Ji Chenxi replied, “Sixteen minutes and twenty-seven seconds.”
“You remember it that clearly.”
Ji Chenxi let out a cold laugh. Of course he remembered—every extra second he waited, he came up with another way to kill Chi Zhuo.
Before Ji Chenxi could say anything more, Chi Zhuo gave his wrist one last slow lick and kiss, then pulled him into an embrace. Leaning close, he pressed a light kiss to his earlobe and murmured, “I swear on my family’s name… I won’t make you wait again.”
His voice was low, and at such close distance, it made Ji Chenxi feel like even his eardrum trembled slightly.
From being expressionless at first, Ji Chenxi slowly curved his lips into a faint smile. “A promise? An alpha’s promises aren’t exactly reliable.”
“I don’t like making promises lightly. But once I do, I’ll do everything I can to keep it.”
Ji Chenxi’s brows relaxed a little, the sharp tension in his face easing, though doubt still lingered in his voice. “You’ve already made me wait twice.”
Chi Zhuo let out a quiet “Mm.” The man who was usually so cold now looked at him with unexpected softness, like he was coaxing a sulking little cat. His fingers gently pinched the soft flesh at the back of Ji Chenxi’s neck. “There won’t be a next time.”
They were about the same height, yet Ji Chenxi still looked down at him with a faint air of superiority, his gaze almost condescending. “Sounds like a sweet lie.”
Chi Zhuo was just considering whether he could forcefully take Ji Chenxi away when the man in front of him suddenly reached out, lightly brushing his cheek in a teasing way. “But… I can believe you this once.”
“Then, thank you for Your Highness’s favor.”
Ji Chenxi’s eyes lifted slightly at the corners. “You’re welcome.”
Chi Zhuo let out a soft laugh. “Let’s go, Your Highness. I’ll take you home. Someone will handle everything and give you a satisfying answer.”
Chi Zhuo managed, more or less smoothly, to coax Ji Chenxi back with him. And this “home” obviously didn’t mean Ji Chenxi’s estate—it was Chi Zhuo’s place, the one he’d lived in for years.
The room was all black, white, and gray, cold and empty with no hint of warmth. Above the bed hung a massive painting, looking like someone had just spilled black and white paint and let it mix into chaos. A piece like that could easily look ugly if you weren’t careful—but Ji Chenxi stared at it for a long time. Next to it hung another, smaller and more normal-looking painting. It felt familiar somehow, but he just couldn’t remember where he’d seen it before.
He felt awful, but still obediently curled up on the small sofa in the bedroom. Just looking at him like this, anyone might even call him well-behaved.
While Ji Chenxi sat there quietly, Chi Zhuo was busy using several small devices to check his condition. After running multiple tests, all the results showed the same thing—Ji Chenxi was in his sensitive period. But this time, he was a little ‘too’ well-behaved. And the constant high fever that wouldn’t go down made it all feel even more off.
