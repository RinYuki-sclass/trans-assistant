Chi Zhou’s eyes shifted, landing back on Ji Chenxi. The first thing he noticed was the fresh blood smeared across Ji Chenxi’s skin.
A beauty stained with blood — now that was a sight to behold. The crimson streaks added something wild and dangerous to that already pale, handsome face, turning elegance into something almost wicked. But Chi Zhou didn’t seem to appreciate the view. His brows furrowed slightly at the smear of red, and he asked quietly, “You okay?”
Ji Chenxi chuckled. “Worried about me?”
Chi Zhou’s tone was so flat it was impossible to tell if he actually meant it.
Ji Chenxi was busy wiping the blood off his face, silently regretting his earlier move. Of all places to stab, ‘why’ did he have to go for the guy’s carotid artery? The blood had shot out like a fountain—sure, it looked cool for a second, but the cleanup was a nightmare.
Luckily, he’d let go fast enough, so only a few drops had splattered on him. Still, those few drops were more than enough to ruin his mood.
“I’m fine,” he said at last, scrubbing away the last of the sticky, metallic stains.
Only then did he finally answer Chi Zhou’s earlier question.
The black mamba had grown huge—more than seven meters long now—and had entered full-on hunting mode. With the enemy so close, Chi Zhou barely needed to lift a finger. Just releasing a touch of his top-tier Alpha pheromones was enough to suppress everyone nearby.
After that, the rest of the fight might as well have been the black mamba’s solo show.
Ji Chenxi didn’t bother interfering much. Seeing that Chi Zhou had literally closed his eyes to rest mid-battle, he figured he might as well relax too. The only time he stepped in was when someone tried firing a photon cannon at the giant snake—he casually deflected it right back.
To him, the whole fight was mostly dull. The only opponent worth watching had been that young man he’d fought earlier. Without their heavy weapons, the Red Scorpion pirates were nothing but a scattered mess.
When Ji Chenxi finished wiping his face, he even took the time to clean his hands. Behind him, his long tail swayed lazily—a sure sign he was in a good mood.
Chi Zhou’s gaze lifted, following the motion of that tail before settling back on Ji Chenxi. His voice dropped low. “Your Highness, are you sure you’re not hurt?”
“I said I’m fine, didn’t I? But you—” Ji Chenxi’s smile deepened, eyes gleaming as his gaze drifted toward the back of Chi Zhou’s neck. “—are ‘you’ really okay?”
His eyes landed squarely where Chi Zhou’s gland was.
Back then, Chi Zhou’s condition wasn’t something as simple as a fever. His pheromones had been a complete mess—on the verge of collapse. And when pheromones break down like that, the spiritual realm tied to them usually shatters too. No one could’ve imagined he’d actually walk out of it alive.
“Your Highness,” Chi Zhou said, his tone calm but pointed, “didn’t you already guess I’d make it out okay?”
After all, when Ji Chenxi had said ‘You’re late’, it hadn’t just been a complaint. Hidden underneath was the assumption that Chi Zhou would come.
“I just saw your fever had gone down,” Ji Chenxi said lightly. “And besides, didn’t big brother Chi Zhou say he’d protect me?”
