‘Chi Zhou?’
What a joke.
That guy should be writhing in torment, crushed by his own pheromones going haywire.
The young man just assumed Ji Chenxi was trying to throw him off and was about to sneer at the “little cat” for running out of tricks—when a sudden rush of air behind him made his heart lurch.
His instincts kicked in. He twisted to the side, dodging fast. And standing right behind him was exactly who Ji Chenxi had mentioned—‘Chi Zhou’.
Under the moonlight, a black mamba coiled around Chi Zhou’s shoulders. Its dark, glossy scales shimmered with a deadly kind of beauty, a light that was both mesmerizing and terrifying.
The next second, that beautiful creature lunged, fangs bared, straight for the young man’s throat.
That shift of air he’d felt before? It was from the snake’s strike.
Chi Zhou’s black eyes were cold, unreadable. He appeared out of nowhere—silent, ghostlike—and just as quietly, he moved. The instant the young man tried to back away, Chi Zhou’s hand shot out like lightning, his fingers clamping tight around the man’s throat.
He was fast. So fast it made the young man’s scalp tingle. In barely a blink, his throat tightened, and pain shot through him, sharp and suffocating.
It wasn’t that he couldn’t see the attack. He saw it. He even knew how to dodge. But under the crushing weight of a high-level alpha’s pheromones, his body wouldn’t respond. Just being close to Chi Zhou felt like standing under a mountain—he couldn’t even breathe.
The power of an ‘SSS-rank alpha’ was back.
Shock flickered across the young man’s face. His throat strained as he forced out a few broken words, “How… how is that possible?”
With that much suppressant and poison mixed together, Chi Zhou should’ve been done for. How could he possibly have recovered?
Chi Zhou tilted his head slightly, his voice low and even.
“You’re strange. You’re only an S-rank alpha. How are you still thinking straight under a pheromone strike?”
The young man’s expression twisted, but he said nothing.
Chi Zhou didn’t bother pressing him. He simply let go, tossing the man aside like he weighed nothing.
Before the young man could hit the ground, the black mamba—now grown massive, thick as a man’s thigh—whipped through the air and wrapped around him like a bolt of black lightning.
Its coils tightened, inch by inch, until he couldn’t even twitch. The snake bit deep into his neck, then squeezed harder, crushing the breath out of him, forcing him to feel every second of the pain.
