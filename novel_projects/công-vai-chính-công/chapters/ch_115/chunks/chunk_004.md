Ji Chenxi hung up impatiently, but the calls kept coming one after another.
When he finally picked up, Chi Zhou’s voice was already rough and hoarse with something like restraint: “Little snow leopard… emergency… Bai Xinke banquet… drugged.”
Ji Chenxi: “Then just die.”
After saying that, he frowned in annoyance. “…Location.”
Annoying mamba. Even such a low-level trick as being drugged could succeed on him.
Though he complained inwardly, he still went to find the man first thing.
Chi Zhou had spent the first twenty years of his life wanting power more than anything. But after truly holding absolute authority, he felt an indescribable emptiness and loneliness.
It was a trivial matter that didn’t even require him to show up, yet it led him to discover someone very interesting.
The beautiful little prince didn’t obediently attend his coming-of-age ceremony. Instead, he was secretly squatting on a hidden rooftop, watching his subordinates deal with an unruly minor force.
He was truly beautiful. Pure silver-white long hair, clear blue eyes, holding a pink-and-white cake, eating bite by bite with puffed cheeks. Everything about him was cute—except for the emotion hidden in those eyes.
Loneliness.
Could a prince born at the peak of power, with countless friends and followers, still feel lonely?
Chi Zhou found it curious, so he stayed a little longer. And sure enough, the little prince spoke to him.
He had long heard that the third prince was elegant and gentle. But after actually talking to him, he realized the other wasn’t as gentle as the rumors said. Rather than gentle, he was more like a large feline baring its claws, his dangerous mental power locking onto him, ready to tighten and crush at any moment. Chi Zhuo deliberately wished him a happy birthday, and then he saw something—
The one about to hunt showed a very faint, very faint smile. Even the mental attack dispersed.
A soft-hearted kid. A foolish decision.
But Chi Zhou’s heart still skipped at that smile, lost and leaving him momentarily entranced.
From that moment on, a real hunting plan began in his mind.
