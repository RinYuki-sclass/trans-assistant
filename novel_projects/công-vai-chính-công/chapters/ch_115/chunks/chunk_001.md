Today could barely count as a special day..
Ji Chenxi had turned eighteen.
The Emperor naturally made proper arrangements for his youngest son, but for Ji Chenxi, it didn’t feel any different from any other birthday.
Everyone sat together at the table, pretending to be harmonious, polite smiles hanging on their lips, but underneath it all, undercurrents were raging. Even every seemingly casual remark was actually a trap being dug for someone else.
Ji Chenxi honestly had no interest in any of it.
Calling it a birthday, but today Ji Chenxi didn’t even get a gift or a slice of cake.
After the birthday, there would be a three-day banquet. At that time, many people would give gifts, and fancy cakes would be a must-have decoration. But what meaning did a cake have if it wasn’t eaten on the actual birthday?
Everyone kept their polite smiles locked on their faces. Back and forth they went, acting out the long performance of “brotherly harmony,” and finally, the birthday meal came to an end.
After leaving the palace, it wasn’t too late yet. Ji Chenxi drove his hover car into the city, wandering around aimlessly, looking for something.
Maybe he should give himself an adult gift today, or maybe just buy himself a cake.
For him, whether it was cake or gifts, none of it was really a luxury. Whether he went home or gathered with friends, he could always receive endless blessings and a cake that every birthday must have. The gifts would be beautiful and well-made, the cake would be sweet and delicious, and even every blessing would be sincere.
But today was a little different from before, because it was his eighteenth birthday.
He kept wandering, not even knowing what exactly he was looking for.
After drifting around for quite a while, Ji Chenxi found a cake shop hidden deep inside an alley. He picked a small cake that was already made, then left again with it in hand.
His wrist computer kept buzzing nonstop. Gu Hechuan had been extremely busy lately, and the birthday wishes had already been sent at exactly midnight last night. Ji Chenxi didn’t even need to look to know it was Su Yu.
He opened the wrist computer and rejected Su Yu’s invitation, then kept walking.
This alley had already drifted away from the brightly lit city center. The hidden darkness slowly started to show itself.
Ji Chenxi held a fork in one hand and cake in the other, watching a gang fight happening in a hidden corner.
The cake was unexpectedly good, and the fight was unexpectedly interesting too.
He had long heard that the underground forces had been completely brought under control, but unfortunately, this particular faction had hidden itself too well. Even after multiple investigations, he hadn’t found anything. He didn’t expect that today he would be lucky enough to run into it live.
