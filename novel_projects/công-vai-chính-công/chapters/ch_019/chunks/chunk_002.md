The companion quickly realized that this wasn’t the aimless wandering from before. This time, the young man was moving with purpose.
The observer whispered, “Deputy Ge, did you sense the pheromones?”
“No! I’m taking you to a place suitable to rest.”
The man’s eyes widened in shock.
“Relax. On nights this freezing, even the mamba and the little kitten will find a place to rest. If we wandered around blindly, we might freeze to death before even finding them. It’s better to wait until dawn, when Chi Zhuo’s pheromones can be tracked properly, before making a move.”
The night in the icy land was unbearably cold. Even with the fire going, Ji Chenxi could feel the chill gnawing at him.
The snow leopard, bundled up almost like a giant plush toy in Chi Zhuo’s arms, twitched, trying to climb out and warm Ji Chenxi, who was clearly more sensitive to the cold.
Ji Chenxi, noticing the movement, reached toward Chi Zhuo’s head to check that it was still warm and alive. Satisfied, he pulled back his hand and signaled the snow leopard to stay put.
A soft “mew” slipped from the snow leopard’s mouth, so gentle it stole the last bit of majesty and dominance from the beast king.
“I’m actually okay,” Ji Chenxi said.
Of course, that wasn’t the answer the snow leopard wanted. It tightened its hold on Ji Chenxi’s hand.
Ji Chenxi stayed silent for a moment, then shifted Chi Zhuo’s head off his shoulder, added a few more logs to the fire, and stacked the remaining dry branches next to himself.
As gravity pulled downward, Chi Zhuo’s head leaned slantwise against the icy cave wall.
Ji Chenxi wrapped his arms around Chi Zhuo’s waist from behind, letting most of Chi Zhuo’s head rest on him, turning the nearly frozen man into a human heater. Ji Chenxi hugged him tightly—after all, the wall was ice, and he himself was cold—so that Chi Zhuo could stay warm in his final moments, radiating a bit of leftover heat.
Once he had him held, Ji Chenxi rested his own head on Chi Zhuo’s shoulder. He glanced at the snow leopard, still staring at him, and, with a little shared understanding, said, “Okay, I have a hug now too. He’s warm.”
The large gray-white snow leopard wagged its fluffy tail, clearly satisfied with the outcome.
Ji Chenxi then pulled his head back from the sick man’s shoulder. Holding someone even larger than himself wasn’t particularly comfortable, and he could clearly see Chi Zhuo’s glands.
Chi Zhuo hated wearing the restraint ring, and had never worn one since they met. From Ji Chenxi’s angle, he could watch the swollen red gland, constantly releasing pheromones so thick it made him feel like he was soaking in wine.
At first, Ji Chenxi just looked, but he couldn’t resist reaching out to gently poke the warm, delicate spot.
It was hot, soft, and fragile.
