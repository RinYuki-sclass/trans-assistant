“Settle down. Instead of worrying about this, why don’t we drink a couple more bottles?” Ji Chenxi teased, clinking his glass against Su Yu’s.
The crisp sound made Su Yu chuckle despite himself. “I’m just worried about you.”
“I know. No need to worry.”
The barely-eighteen-year-old kid saying that to someone two years older… Su Yu wanted to make a sarcastic remark, but in the end, all he could manage was: “As a friend, I just want to help you avoid things that could harm you. At the same time, as a friend, I respect all your choices.”
Ji Chenxi’s smile widened. “Thanks!”
“By the way, when you meet any good-looking omegas in the future, introduce a couple to me, okay?”
“You still need omegas around you?”
“Of course!”
By the time Ji Chenxi returned to his family estate, night had fallen. Even so, he went straight for a bath.
After washing up, with only two or three hours before dawn, sleep wasn’t coming, so he switched on a small lamp and started fiddling with his lightpad.
The interface once again led him to the log titled ‘Miracle’.
This time, he opened it without hesitation.
[**** Year ** Month ** Day]
‘Our miracle has been born! Unbelievable!! But he’s so weak… maybe tomorrow, no, maybe even today, he could die.’
Ji Chenxi raised an eyebrow in surprise. This log read more like a researcher’s personal diary than anything else.
[**** Year ** Month ** Day]
‘No… he is the miracle, an almost perfect miracle. He didn’t die. He survived. Thank the heavens, we will achieve our lifelong goal. I’ve named him Ares.’
The moment Ji Chenxi saw the name, his casual flipping froze. Ares… the god of war.
The entries that followed detailed the log owner’s experiences raising this “creature.” At first, he tried nurturing him like a human. Frustratingly, his miracle began to actually behave like a human; his initial wildness slowly faded. Then, the researcher switched to feeding him raw meat and blood. He taught him human knowledge while guiding this naturally strong and incredibly spiritually powerful being toward slaughter.
As the log went on, it became more and more unhinged. The researcher even used himself and others who spent time with Ares as subjects to see how strong Ares’ empathy really was.
