“Releasing spiritual power does take a bit of feeling. Once you find that sense of extension and practice more, you’ll be able to control it freely. When we enroll, there will be teachers guiding us.”
The guy shot Bai Anran a grateful look, thinking he was incredibly considerate.
Bai Anran was, in fact, very attentive. The reason he had let the alpha sit in awkward silence for a few seconds was because he had noticed something else—Lu Jianhan had been quiet ever since they entered the underground maze, and his face looked a little too pale.
It was a small detail, but enough for Bai Anran to glance at him a few more times. After helping the alpha out, Bai Anran looked for a chance to speak, intending to ask Lu Jianhan if he felt unwell—
Then he suddenly sensed a dangerous presence spreading through the air.
The feeling didn’t come out of nowhere.
The instant that sense of danger hit him, a massive explosion erupted.
A deafening blast and a burst of fire filled the space. Bai Anran’s eyes widened.
At such close range, with that kind of power, the explosion could tear them into pieces.
This was insane!
How could the school allow the seniors to set up a trap like this?
The moment the explosion went off, Bai Anran reacted fast and threw up his spiritual shield. A soft milky-white barrier with a faint glow spread out and wrapped around everyone. At the same time, he felt Ji Chenxi’s spiritual power surge forward. That powerful force didn’t shield them—instead, it rushed toward the hidden weapons that had triggered the blast, wrapping them up and compressing them again and again.
Even with Bai Anran’s shield and Ji Chenxi’s counter move, the heat from the explosion still hit them in waves. The light curtain around the blast kept shrinking and swelling, struggling to contain it. Ji Chenxi glanced at it once and hissed under his breath. “Run. It’s about to blow.”
The second he said it, he took off first, heading straight toward the direction where he sensed Chi Zhuo’s presence.
Seeing him move, the others didn’t dare hesitate and quickly retreated. Two of them, sharp enough to know this place was way beyond them, didn’t even follow—they ran straight for the exit.
Ji Chenxi respected everyone’s choice. He looked like the first to leave, but in truth, he held on until the very last person had run a good distance away before finally releasing the compressed heat wave trapped in his spiritual power.
Every single hidden weapon had been a bomb. Containing that level of destructive force, wave after wave, had drained quite a bit of his energy.
Originally, Ji Chenxi had planned to stick with Lu Jianhan and Bai Anran. Who would’ve thought that after dodging the explosion, Lu Jianhan—who had been only a few meters away—was suddenly nowhere to be seen. Looks like this maze could shift and change. Ji Chenxi didn’t insist on searching for them. Instead, he followed the faint trace of Chi Zhuo’s aura.
As Ji Chenxi drew closer, Chi Zhuo stood up at the same time and started walking out.
“Chi,” Lan Mu called, stopping him. “Why not just wait here for them? I even prepared a few little traps especially for them.”
