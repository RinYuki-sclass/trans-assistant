They had fought several times before, but most of those matches had ended early or been interrupted. This might’ve been the first time neither of them was injured or restricted. Ji Chenxi’s spirit power leaned toward pure offense. His movements were sharp and fast, like a flash of light. As for Chi Zhuo—his spirit form was a black mamba snake. Speed was something he never lacked.
Both of them kept their strength capped at S-rank, which only made it clearer—they had finally met someone on their level.
Chi Zhuo was surprised by Ji Chenxi’s speed. At the same time, Ji Chenxi quietly admitted he had underestimated Chi Zhuo’s power and skill. The alpha’s extra ten years of life gave him experience and technique far beyond Ji Chenxi’s. Still, Ji Chenxi was fast enough to dodge most of Chi Zhuo’s attacks.
But the moment he failed to dodge—even once—he would fall into a weak spot. And turning the tide after that wouldn’t be easy.
They traded blows again and again.
Finally, in one split second, Chi Zhuo seized Ji Chenxi’s wrist—the one holding the dagger—and with his other hand, he caught Ji Chenxi at a fatal point.
Ji Chenxi’s reaction had been slower by just a fraction of a second.
“Your Highness,” Chi Zhuo said calmly, “looks like your points are about to become mine.”
Ji Chenxi didn’t sigh or look annoyed at being pinned down. Instead, he laughed. The sound was bright and cheerful, but when he spoke, his voice dropped low.
“Don’t you think this win would be a bit unfair, Senior? After all, I already burned through quite a bit of spiritual power dealing with those traps earlier.”
“That’s strategy too,” Chi Zhuo replied calmly.
Ji Chenxi nodded in agreement. “I think so too. But did you notice something? Ever since we entered the underground maze, Lu Jianhan hasn’t made a single move.”
Both of them were S-rank. And yet, even during the explosion earlier, Lu Jianhan hadn’t acted at all. That alone was strange.
Chi Zhuo suddenly realized something.
Lu Jianhan. Spirit form—phantom butterfly. Specializes in illusions.
For a brief second, Chi Zhuo’s mind blurred.
And in that tiny gap of time, Ji Chenxi flipped the situation completely, pressing him down onto the ground.
Ji Chenxi dropped to one knee, one arm locking around Chi Zhuo’s back to stop him from fighting back, the other holding that razor-sharp dagger against Chi Zhuo’s heart.
Having fully subdued him, Ji Chenxi leaned in close. Very close.
His smile was light, almost playful. Near Chi Zhuo’s ear, he whispered softly—his tone sounding almost intimate, yet completely casual.
