After breaking through the spiderweb trap, Ji Chenxi strode forward without hesitation. He was so bold it made the people behind him nervous, worried there might still be invisible strands stretched across the path.
Luckily, they walked quite a long way without running into any danger.
Just when everyone was about to relax, something changed.
From above and at an angle, countless hidden weapons suddenly shot toward them. Sharp metal projectiles rained down, thick and packed so tight they almost sealed off their retreat. One freshman with weak nerves nearly burst into tears. With that many blades flying at them—if they got hit, would they even survive?
Bai Anran had never believed their lives were truly at risk. This was a joint freshman trial between two schools. No matter how serious it sounded, it was still just a student exercise. The school would never let anyone die. But now, in a trial meant to test their level, they were actually using a trap like this.
The moment the weapons came down, Ji Chenxi narrowed his eyes. A strange force rippled out from his body, and just like that, the projectiles that were about to strike froze midair, held firmly in place.
“Spiritual power control!!” someone shouted in shock.
Countless metal blades floated in the air, pointed straight at them—yet restrained by some invisible force. Ji Chenxi stood at the very front and walked forward at an easy pace, as if none of this even mattered.
Sharp blades. A silver-haired young man with delicate, striking features.
The scene was both dangerous and beautiful.
Facing the danger head-on, Ji Chenxi let out a soft “Mm.”
“Classmate Ji, you can already use spiritual power so smoothly? That’s insane. You haven’t even enrolled yet and you’ve already mastered it.” The overly friendly alpha from earlier hurried up beside him, chatting nonstop.
“It’s not just me,” Ji Chenxi said calmly. “Lu Jianhan and Anran can do it too. I just acted first.”
“So almost half our team can do this?!” The guy looked like he was about to lose it.
Ji Chenxi was about to nod, but then seemed to remember something. He softened his tone. “You’ll learn soon too.”
“Not necessarily. I heard even the seniors who enrolled a year before us—only a handful of them can release their spiritual power.”
This time Ji Chenxi didn’t answer right away. He frowned slightly.
Too simple.
Just this kind of hidden weapon wasn’t enough to be impressive. As long as someone could use spiritual power control, these things were basically toys. So what was he missing? What else was hidden among them?
His silence made the friendly alpha awkward. Just as he was about to laugh it off and change the topic, Bai Anran spoke up at the perfect time.
