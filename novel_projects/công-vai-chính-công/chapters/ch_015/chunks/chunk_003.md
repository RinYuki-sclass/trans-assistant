Ji Chenxi’s presence had made what was supposed to be a smooth hunt for Chi Zhuo suddenly much more complicated.
Zuo Xian glanced at Ji Chenxi and gently patted his subordinate. “Amis, go take care of him.”
“Just me?” Amis asked. She had heard that the prince ahead was S-class.
“Don’t worry. He won’t dare kill directly. The two people who ran into him before didn’t die,” Zuo Xian said confidently.
It wasn’t hard to guess what was happening. Chi Zhuo’s pheromones were seriously out of control, and to deal with the two previous attacks, he had clearly relied heavily on Ji Chenxi’s help. Ji Chenxi, a young prince raised in an ivory tower and spoiled by countless people, could carry out a kill even in the dark—but if there was even a moment of hesitation in a direct fight, Amis could easily turn the tables and kill him.
This was Zuo Xian giving Amis a chance to climb higher.
Amis, an S-class in physical ability and A-class in spiritual power, was more than capable of taking down the young prince.
Ji Chenxi was still focused on giving Chi Zhuo directions, even as more and more people fell. The smell of blood was strong enough to reach his nose. He wrinkled it slightly—this scent was unpleasant, to say the least.
Then he noticed a tiny figure moving toward him at incredible speed, staying perfectly hidden. A female alpha. If not for her terrifying physical strength, Ji Chenxi might have thought she was an omega. Unlike most alphas, who tend to be physically strong but spiritually weaker, omegas are usually the opposite—high spiritual power, low physical ability.
Even with part of his attention on the approaching woman, Ji Chenxi could still guide Chi Zhuo, almost as if he hadn’t noticed her at all. When a photon bullet shot straight at him from her hand, he simply sidestepped it.
Amis knew he had noticed her, yet she still closed the distance. She hadn’t earned Deputy Zuo Xian’s favor because she was obedient or a good starship pilot—it was her terrifying close-combat skills that made her stand out.
Once the tiny woman was close enough to attack, Ji Chenxi realized she was skilled. He continued giving Chi Zhuo directions at a steady pace while simultaneously engaging Amis in combat.
He casually swung his dagger in a flourish, and Amis felt slightly underestimated. Of course—this young master clearly hadn’t been through much.
She drew a double-edged blade from her lower back and rushed forward for a kill.
The first strike was caught by Ji Chenxi’s dagger, but don’t forget—Amis was using a double-edged weapon.
Ji Chenxi gracefully stepped back. A twisted ankle limited him somewhat, but it was still manageable.
Compared to a long weapon, his dagger with a sapphire inlay was tiny—more like a delicate piece of art than a real weapon.
Yet in the face of absolute strength, even a dagger no bigger than a palm was enough to kill a person.
When the dagger slid into the neck, Amis was momentarily in disbelief. ‘Wasn’t he supposed to not dare to kill?’ And the very person who had just plunged the dagger into someone’s throat was still giving directions to Chi Zhuo, who was finishing up the last of his own tasks.
In an instant, Amis understood—this person might respect life to a degree, but when it came to killing, he was just as decisive and efficient.
