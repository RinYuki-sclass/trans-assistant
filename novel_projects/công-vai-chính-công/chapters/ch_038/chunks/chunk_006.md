“One million nine hundred ninety thousand, anyone willing to raise the bid?”
“One million nine hundred ninety thousand, twice. We have one last chance! Interested bidders can raise the price again. This rare mineral was only found in a small piece, most of it used to craft this mineral rose. Miss it now, and who knows when another will appear?”
Her voice rose and fell with deliberate rhythm, her gestures guiding the crowd. As expected, the previously quiet auction began to stir again.
Nobles loved two words most: ‘rare’ and ‘unique’.
If something was merely rare, giving it to someone else wasn’t a big deal. But if a second rose like this might never appear… would those seeking to win an omega’s favor, or those who simply had more than enough money, really give up?
As the price climbed wildly past three million, Ji Chenxi already felt it wasn’t worth it.
A rare mineral sounded fancy, but it was still just a rock. How much had even been mined? No one knew. Maybe this rose was unique today, but tomorrow there could be orchids or peonies in its place.
Since someone had started bidding earlier, there was a brief peak in competition. By the time the price reached five million, bids had thinned out again. Each price had to be called once or twice, with the auctioneer coaxing the crowd about the rose’s rarity before the next bid appeared.
Seeing the price stabilizing, Ji Chenxi had no intention of bidding further. Manye Zhe asked, “Not interested anymore?”
“No, I still like it. But I feel the price isn’t worth it—the stone isn’t that big.”
Ji Chenxi laughed. “No need. I bid one million nine hundred ninety thousand because I think that’s all it’s really worth. Any higher, and it’s just not worth it.”
Lu Churan added, “Although I’d like to say liking it is more important, a newly mined rare mineral is only rare when it first appears. Once there’s a little more, its value drops like crazy.”
Manye Zhe was genuinely silent this time. Why did these two—both delicate and beautiful in appearance—see things even more clearly than he did when it came to value?
Just as the system voice was about to announce a final bid of six million four hundred thousand, a male bidder’s system voice cut in:
[Bidder No. 51, bid: 9,990,000]
Nine million nine hundred ninety thousand? That’s a number worthy of this rose, Ji Chenxi thought silently, wondering who this mad spender could be.
A small decorative item selling for that much! The beautiful auctioneer’s smile brightened further, her voice tinged with excitement. “Nine million nine hundred ninety thousand, anyone willing to raise?”
Ji Chenxi guessed that the person determined to win this mineral rose must have been embarrassed and furious. But upon hearing this bid, he still didn’t give up and called out ten million.
Ji Chenxi adjusted his posture, expecting the two to continue a slow bidding battle, buying some time. But the calm male system voice rang out again:
[Bidder No. 51, bid: 99,990,000]
