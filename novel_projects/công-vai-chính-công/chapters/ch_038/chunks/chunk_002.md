By the time Ji Chenxi approached with the white rose in hand, Manye Zhe had already shifted his gaze back to the newcomer.
A killer’s instincts told him instantly: this was no ordinary alpha.
The seemingly slender, tall figure could probably unleash a terrifying strength that didn’t match his frame at all. If he were an opponent… he would be a formidable one.
At roughly two meters’ distance, Manye Zhe gave a slight nod.
Third Prince Ji Chenxi—though no public images of the newly adult prince existed—was instantly recognizable to a professional killer. Just a glance was enough to confirm his identity.
Ji Chenxi held up the white rose in his hand and said the single, almost pointless word: “Bai Wei.”
It was the classic way to verify identity.
Manye Zhe responded with his signal: “The night is nice. Perfect for a walk.”
Ji Chenxi smiled politely. “Shall we walk together?”
Manye Zhe nodded, stepped aside to make room, and only began to follow once Ji Chenxi moved close to his side.
Ji Chenxi didn’t mind. Most killers didn’t like exposing their backs.
Moving slightly ahead, Ji Chenxi set up a layer of spiritual barrier and then transmitted his voice directly into the other’s mind: “You haven’t reunited with Que Qi yet?”
“I was supposed to come with her, but on the way here to find you, someone spilled wine on her clothes. She’s probably still changing.”
Ji Chenxi frowned. “Just an accident?”
“No. In a place like this, any servant who can’t even carry wine properly should be fired. This time, someone deliberately distracted Que Qi. I originally planned to wait for her to change before meeting you, but she probably wanted to see what they were up to… so she played along.”
“She’s a little vulnerable on her own.”
Ji Chenxi didn’t fully approve of Que Qi acting alone—this auction was a mix of all kinds of people.
“I took her situation into account,” the other replied. “Her spiritual body can block most dangers, and she has two hidden guards following her. She’ll be fine. Compared to that, I was more worried about you, Bai Wei—your spiritual body is just a big cat.”
Ji Chenxi: “…That’s a snow leopard.”
If his spiritual body counted as a big cat, then Que Qi’s would probably be… a little blue fish?
