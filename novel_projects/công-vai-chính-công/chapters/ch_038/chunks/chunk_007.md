Nearly one hundred million just for a small, slightly unique, pretty decoration. The entire hall fell silent. This person was insane.
As expected, the item ended up being won by Bidder No. 51.
Manye Zhe, probably realizing words couldn’t express his excitement, typed rapidly and showed Ji Chenxi a string of characters: [Whoa, is everyone on the Imperial Star just dripping with money?! This tiny thing nearly sold for a nine-digit sum in star currency!!!”]
From the barrage of exclamation marks, Ji Chenxi fully understood Manye Zhe’s excitement. After a moment of thought, he said, “Maybe that person really does love that mineral rose.”
Though he said it aloud, Ji Chenxi couldn’t help thinking that the person was just a foolish, rich dupe.
The rest of the auction went by quickly, with few items seeing the kind of crazy overbidding that the mineral rose had. When the auction reached the items they were actually fishing for, Ji Chenxi quickly hacked into the network again and began their true mission for the night—‘fishing’.
At first, Ji Chenxi didn’t track who was bidding. The experimental report had a lot of hype, and unrelated interested parties were also placing bids. But once the price started climbing steadily and the bids came mainly from those few numbers, Ji Chenxi activated tracking mode.
The auction numbers were randomized. Except for the first three, the rest had no special meaning, and there was even an extra layer of protection to safeguard clients’ privacy. But for Ji Chenxi, equipped with his precision tools, it was nothing.
Just as they were checking the bidders’ identities and digging deeper, someone knocked on the door.
Ji Chenxi’s eyes darkened as his fingers continued pounding in frantic commands, while Manye Zhe drew a thin, wing-like short knife from his waist.
Ji Chenxi exchanged a glance with the others. They ignored the knock at first, finishing copying all the data, then linked with Manye Zhe’s AI and transmitted it to him.
Then came the second knock. After three polite taps, Ji Chenxi had already compressed his equipment and opened the door.
His face held a trace of displeasure, though he asked politely, “What is it?”
He was clearly annoyed at being interrupted.
The servant knocking looked nervous and lowered his head. “Your Highness, the client who won this item specified that it should be sent to you. The manager feared you might leave early, so we brought it ahead of time.”
Ji Chenxi had been ready to be ambushed when opening the door. Who could have expected that the item he had been curious about not long ago would be delivered straight to him?
At that moment, the mineral rose—its color shifting with temperature—was glowing fully in the fiery red hue of warmth.
It was like a silent declaration of love.
