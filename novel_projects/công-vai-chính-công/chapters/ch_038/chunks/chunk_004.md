“Why do you want it to look like that?” Manye Zhe asked, confused.
Before Ji Chenxi could respond, a soft, slightly sweet young female voice came from behind: “What’s so funny?”
Just hearing the voice, Ji Chenxi immediately knew who it was. He glanced back and met a gentle, beautiful face—one that stood out even among the many beauties of the Imperial Star In the Twelfth Fairy Star, almost no one would fail to recognize her.
Ji Chenxi nodded politely, a warm smile still on his lips. “Que Qi.”
Que Qi returned the gesture with a polite nod, and after a few steps forward she said, “Your Highness, Third Prince, hello. I apologize for being late on our first meeting.”
“Miss Lu, how could you say that? You, such a beautiful omega, came all this way to see me. The one being rude here is me.”
Manye Zhe couldn’t help but interject: “You two are really polite with each other.”
Ji Chenxi chuckled lightly and once again used his spiritual power to transmit his voice: “Miss Lu, did you encounter anything just now?”
The faint scent of the sea clung to Lu Churan, but Ji Chenxi’s senses were sharp. Even from a distance, he could tell she was using her spiritual body.
Connecting her spiritual power with his, Lu Churan transmitted her voice: “I ran into a few insignificant pests trying to extract information. Didn’t go too smoothly.”
Knowing Manye Zhe couldn’t hear their private conversation, she added directly to him: “I’ve already taken care of it.”
Manye Zhe nodded. Even without a direct explanation, he understood her meaning.
The three of them chatted casually, like ordinary online friends meeting in person, as they made their way toward their destination.
Being a member of the royal family, events held on the Imperial Star always had reserved spaces for them. Whether they showed up was one matter; whether the space was reserved was another. So, with their purpose clear, the three of them headed straight for the room where Ji Chenxi was supposed to be.
After arriving at the room, Ji Chenxi first used some tools to hack into the local network. Once he confirmed that no surveillance or listening devices had been planted in their private room, he still wasn’t fully at ease. He activated the outward jamming device and laid down a layer of spiritual power, then finally got to the point: “Que Qi, do you know who the people who lured you earlier were?”
“No idea. Those few had some skill, though. They probably discovered that the materials came from me for auction and might have even thought about kidnapping me directly. But I think it was more of a probe than anything else.”
Ji Chenxi hummed softly. “It’s probably just those few factions. We’ll be patient and play the long game—fish with a long line for the big catch.”
Lu Churan smiled. “But who would’ve guessed they were so impatient? The auction hasn’t even started and they already wanted to make a move on me.”
Ji Chenxi didn’t ask where those people had gone. Instead, he said, “The material you auctioned… was it about human research?”
“No, about miracle,” Lu Churan replied with a smile.
