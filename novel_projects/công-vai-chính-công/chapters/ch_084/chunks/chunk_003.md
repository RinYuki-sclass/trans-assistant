Chi Zhuo leaned in close to his ear again, his voice soft and low. “Not sure if I’ve told you before… but your lips are really nice. Perfect for kissing me.”
“And why exactly are they ‘perfect’ for kissing you?”
“Because they’re only meant to kiss me, of course. After all… even you belong to me, Your Highness.”
Chi Zhuo teased him without restraint. Maybe to him, this wasn’t even teasing—just saying something intimate to his own little prince. But to Ji Chenxi, each word chipped away at what little reason he had left.
The heat kept rising, wave after wave, stronger each time. He tried to find some way to ease it, but this wasn’t something an alpha was built to handle. The dryness made everything worse—there was nothing pleasurable about it, only pain.
And it wasn’t just Chi Zhuo who felt it. Even Ji Chenxi flinched from the pain, his mind snapping back to clarity for a brief moment.
He suddenly bit down on Chi Zhuo’s gland. The strong, overbearing alpha pheromones rushed into his senses, leaving his head dizzy and layering even more pain on top of everything else.
After the bite, Ji Chenxi’s eyes were red as he looked at him. “You really aren’t willing… it hurts so much.”
Chi Zhuo, who was also hissing from the pain, forced himself to relax, speaking in a tone as steady as usual. “Feels more like Your Highness is getting back at me.”
Enduring the pain, Chi Zhuo quickly checked something on his terminal. Then he shut his eyes, a trace of despair slipping through. “Suddenly… I’m kind of glad I gave in to you.”
He pulled Ji Chenxi into his arms, biting down on his shoulder to bear the pain.
Ji Chenxi was already in a lot of pain himself, and now he was being bitten on the shoulder on top of that. In his sensitive state, right in the middle of his susceptible period, he almost couldn’t hold it in—his eyes stung, on the verge of spilling over.
After that intense wave passed, Chi Zhuo leaned down and kissed Ji Chenxi’s shoulder, still calm enough to soothe him. “It’ll be fine in a bit,” he murmured. “I don’t think it’s a big problem.”
Reality proved otherwise—there was still quite a problem. But luckily, alphas had strong bodies. Even so, they managed to push through it, stumbling along the way.
With the added effect of the susceptible period, the room was filled with Ji Chenxi’s rose scent. At first, Chi Zhuo had thought the smell was a little too bitter—that was from the damage to the gland earlier. But after the needle was removed and the gland began to heal itself, that rose fragrance slowly picked up a faint sweetness.
The sharp scent of tequila and the soft sweetness of roses clashed, resisted, and blended—until, in the end, they mixed into a rich, heavy fragrance of their own.
