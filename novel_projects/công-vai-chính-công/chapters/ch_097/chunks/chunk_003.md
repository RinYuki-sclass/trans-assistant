Chen Xing had expected to see at least a trace of fear on Ji Chenxi’s face, but he didn’t catch even a hint of it.
Ji Chenxi ignored the beeping and went to the lab equipment, starting to tinker with it.
One minute. Even if the other could move freely through the star network, there was no way to stop the explosion. So Chen Xing watched calmly—until he saw Ji Chenxi forcibly use nearly unusable equipment to move the cover blocking the skylight.
Forty-three seconds left.
Although the skylight was now the weakest point in the lab, it was still made of glass that could withstand photon guns. It wouldn’t be easy to break.
The skylight was locked, which didn’t surprise Ji Chenxi. From the moment the beeping started, he could feel his spiritual energy being suppressed, meaning there was also a device here that suppressed spiritual power. Everything had been meticulously arranged. The other party clearly intended to kill him in this meeting. In the entire lab, there were barely any sharp objects he could use as heavy weapons.
When Ji Chenxi found a foothold and leaped up to the skylight, the only dagger he had was already embedded into the metal wall to support him.
Time was tight. He had no time to dismantle metal to strike the glass. He clenched his fist and smashed it directly against the glass.
The dull sound of flesh hitting a hard surface echoed, but Ji Chenxi didn’t stop.
“Your Highness, knowing there are tigers in the mountain yet still going in—can I ask why?”
Seven seconds remained before the final explosion.
This time, Ji Chenxi didn’t answer.
Whether it was genuine curiosity or an attempt to stall didn’t matter. A layer of pale blue energy gathered in Ji Chenxi’s hand. Then he struck hard. In the final two seconds, the glass—already showing cracks—finally shattered like a spiderweb, completely breaking apart. Amid the rolling heat waves, he leaped out of the lab.
A massive explosion followed right after. Flames lunged greedily, trying to swallow him whole. And just before leaving, Ji Chenxi seemed to see that fake Chen Xing smiling again.
Chi Zhuo had been very uneasy about Ji Chenxi going to meet Chen Xing. He had no personal ties with Chen Xing—strictly speaking, they hadn’t even met—but from scattered bits of information, it was clear that Chen Xing was someone capable and ambitious. Would a person like that show mercy? No matter how you looked at it, the invitation was a trap.
Using his identity as a graduate of the Imperial Royal Academy, Chi Zhuo entered the academy, but he didn’t go with Ji Chenxi. Whether Chen Xing had tricks prepared or not, the two needed to meet alone, to have what might be their final proper conversation.
But even standing only a few hundred meters away, Chi Zhuo felt a strong sense of unease.
A huge explosion suddenly rang out, and the laboratory Ji Chenxi had entered not long ago burst into flames.
Seeing this, Chi Zhuo’s heart trembled violently, his breathing almost stopping. Ignoring the people with him, he rushed toward the explosion.
Many people were startled by the blast. It wasn’t a wide-range explosion, but happening on campus was enough to cause a commotion.
