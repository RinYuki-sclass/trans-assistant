The suffocating feeling from having his neck tightly squeezed would make almost anyone instinctively try to grab the hand choking them, but Chen Xing didn’t. Not only did he not move, he even looked at Ji Chenxi with a bright smile, as if he was expecting something.
Expecting.
Expecting what?
A strange feeling rose in Ji Chenxi’s heart, yet the hand gripping Chen Xing’s neck tightened even more.
The skin under his fingers was warm and firm, and he could feel the faint movement caused by the other’s breathing. With just a bit more force, his strength as an alpha would be enough to easily snap the man’s neck. But this complete lack of resistance stirred an odd unease in Ji Chenxi.
Too calm. There wasn’t even a hint of pain from suffocation on the other’s face.
“You think I can’t kill you?”
Ji Chenxi tilted his head slightly, asking directly without beating around the bush.
Chen Xing looked a bit surprised, then laughed. “Your Highness can try.”
Ji Chenxi did try. The strength in his hand neither increased nor decreased, but he released the suppressive pheromones that symbolized an alpha.
The pheromones surged wildly, reaching a terrifying concentration in mere moments. Yet even under such intensity, Chen Xing showed no reaction.
At this moment, Ji Chenxi also smiled. He exerted force and casually threw the man aside. “Being unaffected by high-level alpha pheromones is an advantage of artificial humans, but it’s also a good way to tell whether someone is real or not. So now, should I suspect that you yourself are an artificial creation, or that you deliberately left an artificial human here to lure me over?”
“Your Highness is indeed smart,” Chen Xing praised, but didn’t say which guess was correct.
Ji Chenxi had told Chi Zhuo he was walking into a trap. Now it really did look like a banquet set for him.
“You told me so directly that you’ve betrayed me, leaving no room at all. That’s not your usual style. You like to control people’s emotions and plan out every step they take. Should I take it that everything before was just you provoking me, trying to make me attack you? Then after I attack you, or even kill you, what happens here?”
Ji Chenxi quietly took a few steps back.
The “Chen Xing” in front of him let his smile fade a little. “This place was specially prepared for Your Highness. Why can’t you just behave?”
The sense of unease grew stronger. Ji Chenxi lightly tapped his wrist terminal without making a sound—no response.
The signal was blocked.
“You figured it out even faster than I expected.”
