Students nearby quickly ran away, but Chi Zhuo stood out, moving in the opposite direction. Someone even tried to stop him.
“Wait! There’s been an explosion! There might be another one—run!”
Chi Zhuo ignored it and headed straight toward the burning area. Numerous metal fragments had been blown out, many still burning and igniting nearby objects. Just as Chi Zhuo was about to rush into the center of the explosion, a figure walked out from the flames.
He looked a mess. Several strands of his silver hair were singed, his clothes stained with dirt and blood. The hand he held was dripping blood. But the moment Chi Zhuo saw him, his nearly stopped heartbeat roared back like thunder.
“You should’ve let me go with you.” Chi Zhuo’s tone had never been worse.
Despite looking disheveled, Ji Chenxi’s eyes curved slightly, and he gave a soft smile. “Right now, I’m extremely glad I didn’t let you come.”
