The moment Ji Chenxi realized the signal was cut, countless thoughts had already run through his mind. He coldly said, “You planted a bomb here. Or rather, you yourself are the trigger for that bomb right now.”
“Correct.” A crisp snap of fingers rang out, filled with praise for how quickly he had figured it out. “But even if you realized it from the start, it’s too late. From the moment you stepped into this room, everything is under my control.”
To capture thieves, capture the leader first. Chen Xing naturally understood that principle.
The card in Chen Xing’s hand was suddenly raised and slashed toward his own neck.
Even without knowing what kind of bomb it was, an explosion in such an enclosed space would be extremely terrifying. Ji Chenxi condensed a spiritual-energy blade and knocked the card away. At the same time, he rushed forward to close the distance, stopping any chance of Chen Xing triggering the explosion.
The power of a double S-level was terrifying, and the speed far surpassed S-rank. In just a few seconds, the two had already exchanged countless moves. During that time, Ji Chenxi showed no mercy—he twisted off one of Chen Xing’s arms and bound him with spiritual-energy vines, restraining his movements.
Chen Xing lay on the ground in a miserable state. One arm had been broken by Ji Chenxi, and the other had its palm pierced by the vines. Yet it was as if he felt no pain at all. He looked at Ji Chenxi with a smile. “Actually, being able to die together with you while conscious isn’t so bad.”
That faint unease from when he first learned there was a bomb finally settled into certainty.
“The one here is from the A97 series—Thunder.”
This time, Ji Chenxi didn’t phrase it as a question, but as a firm statement.
It was impossible to plant a large bomb inside the academy, and smaller bombs usually lacked enough power. The A97 series broke that limitation, and among them, Thunder was the most complex to manufacture and the most destructive. Once activated, it would explode after a set time—unstoppable and impossible to dismantle. Destroying the trigger directly would only cause an immediate massive explosion.
“Congratulations, Your Highness, that’s correct.” This artificial Chen Xing was unusually straightforward.
Ji Chenxi felt no joy at getting it right. If this really was Thunder, a bomb requiring countless precious materials, was there any way out?
He ignored Chen Xing lying on the ground and went to the door he had entered from. As expected, it wouldn’t open at all. Even the system had completely crashed. Checking his wrist terminal, the earlier input was fine—the place truly had no signal. Beneath this laboratory was a massive signal jammer.
This kind of signal block wasn’t impossible to remove, but it would take a lot of time. And even if he managed to reconnect, he still wouldn’t be able to stop an already activated Thunder.
Ji Chenxi first tried to forcibly break the central system with his spiritual energy, then blast open the door sealed by a defensive barrier.
The result was a series of beeping sounds echoing through the room.
The constant beeping sounded like a warning.
Chen Xing, still lying on the ground, smiled. “Your Highness, your spiritual energy is too strong. The lab’s secondary self-destruction will activate in one minute, and Thunder’s explosion timer has also been moved forward.”
One minute. Even for a double S alpha, still made of flesh and blood, escaping this lab in one minute was nothing but a fantasy.
