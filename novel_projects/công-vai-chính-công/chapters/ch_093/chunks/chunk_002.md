As for Gu Hechuan, Ji Chenxi chose to lay everything out directly—he was actually Bai Wei, and he knew that Gu Hechuan was Crow. The shock on Gu Hechuan’s side was probably quite big; he stayed silent for a long time before finally saying, “Watching me make a fool of myself?”
“No, I didn’t even dare recognize you at first. Crow is just too different from how you usually are. Later on, I just thought I’d give you a surprise when we met.”
“More like a scare.”
Gu Hechuan didn’t lash out at Chen Xing’s actions. Instead, he analyzed it calmly. “I already felt before that he had strong leadership ability. Under that gentle exterior is ambition that’s hard to hide. I originally thought he’d make a good commander and strategist, and I even wanted to recommend him to you. Now it seems his ambition is even bigger than I imagined.”
“Maybe from his point of view, he didn’t do anything wrong. After all, he’s a thorough perfectionist.”
“A perfectionist? Maybe. But I think besides chasing perfection, he was also driven by your loss of control. For someone with a strong need for control, your actions are basically resistance and unpredictability.”
Ji Chenxi only smiled at that. Could he really not guess? Was Chen Xing really only doing all this because of his flaws?
He already had an answer in his heart. He just felt that admitting it so directly would make his trust from those earlier years seem ridiculous.
“When you’re out in the world, it’s normal to misjudge people. Finding out earlier is a good thing. Not just you—even we were fooled by him, weren’t we?” Gu Hechuan sharply noticed that slight shift in emotion and used a gentle tone to soothe him, though his eyes were cold.
After ending the call, Gu Hechuan looked at the red-haired young man beside him. “Chen Xing is no longer hiding his ambition. He’s about to make a move.”
The red-haired young man was admiring and playing with the pile of weapons in Gu Hechuan’s collection. Hearing that, a sweet, sticky smile appeared on his lips. “I’ve disliked him for a long time. Perfect chance to have a proper fight.”
“You really don’t care about old ties at all.”
“Chen Xing never cared about old ties with us. Between him and me, it’s just a relationship where we help each other when needed. You might not know this, but he has another force. All these years, his people have been secretly snatching things I had my eye on in my territory. I’ve wanted to shoot him for a long time.”
“There are seven people in our group. Do you think Queqi is likely to take our side?”
“Ha, my god, that’s really putting our precious omega in a tough spot. Queqi, huh—there’s a seventy percent chance she’ll cooperate with Chen Xing, and a thirty percent chance she’ll stay neutral and not get involved. The Fairy Twelve Stars’ power depends on which side they stand. Compared to Queqi, we might as well look forward to Wantu’s choice.”
That was the biggest arms dealer of them all.
