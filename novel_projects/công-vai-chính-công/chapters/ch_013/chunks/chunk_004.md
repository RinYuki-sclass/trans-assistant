The snow leopard had swept the area clean with its massive tail, but even so, sitting there made Ji Chenxi sigh—everything still felt dirty.
When life and death are on the line, that doesn’t matter. But in downtime, he couldn’t sit still.
He finally took off his gloves and washed his hands properly, taking the chance to rinse the snow leopard’s tail too.
His spiritual beast’s blue eyes widened slightly, staring at him, probably wondering why he suddenly grabbed its tail and dunked it in water.
“Maybe I should wash the whole of you, huh, Bao Bao? What do you think?”
The snow leopard: “…”
Just moments ago, it had been clinging to Ji Chenxi. Now it leapt lightly to the side, nimbly avoiding his hand.
Finding someone on a huge planet wasn’t easy, even for Zuo Xian himself.
Luckily, someone in their scouting team soon picked up the scent of pheromones. The hint of tequila—there was no mistaking it—belonged to the person from the Chi family. So the news about his unstable pheromones recently was true.
Especially when that single exclamation mark popped up, Zuo Xian’s eyes darkened slightly. He not only notified the people already on the planet but also reported to their team leader, requesting reinforcements.
Beside him, Amis quickly said, “Deputy Leader, he said in the team channel that it was sent by mistake.”
“Sent by mistake?”
“Yeah… he also said he only smelled the pheromones, didn’t actually see anyone.”
Zuo Xian thought briefly, but he still issued the order to continue the pursuit.
“Deputy Leader, what if there’s really no one there?” Amis showed Zuo Xian his chat log with the hooked-nose man.
Even Zuo Xian hesitated for a moment, but he quickly gave the command again: “Go. Keep sending him messages. Let’s see if he replies.”
Once they reached the coordinates, Zuo Xian didn’t care whether the two were alive or not. He used the pheromone detector to trace Chi Zhuo’s movements.
“Deputy Leader, isn’t our main target the Third Prince? Why are we making such a fuss over someone named Chi Zhuo?”
Zuo Xian seemed somewhat satisfied with his subordinate and elaborated: “The client is paying to have the Third Prince killed. Chi Zhuo is the one our team leader wants dead.”
Even mentioning Chi Zhuo made Zuo Xian wary. The mess with his unstable pheromones had some involvement from the Red Scorpion—they’d sold some banned drugs to that side. Though they were only peripheral in the whole affair, he had already heard rumors that everyone involved in that plan had paid a heavy price. Who knew when it would be Red Scorpion’s turn?
