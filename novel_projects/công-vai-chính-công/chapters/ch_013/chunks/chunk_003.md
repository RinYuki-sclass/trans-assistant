Sliding the mech’s controls with the man’s hand, Ji Chenxi planned to get down from the mech. Then he noticed a private message from someone labeled with a pet name:
[Really just an accident?]
[Ignore those idiots’ filthy words.]
Ji Chenxi paused, then mimicked the man’s tone and sent a reply:
[Just had bad luck—got bitten by a snake, that’s why I sent it wrong. Don’t know if it’s poisonous. Bring me some antidote, okay?]
After sending it, Ji Chenxi didn’t look for a reply. He searched the mech, found a healing spray, and then climbed down.
“We need to go. They’ll probably notice something’s wrong soon and come with a big group.”
“Your Highness… you want to come with me?”
Good question. Right now, the fact that the two of them hadn’t started fighting again was already a small miracle. Going together would feel completely unsafe—like a sword hanging over your head.
Ji Chenxi barely hesitated. “Of course not.”
The lame beauty mounted his snow leopard and disappeared into the depths of the forest, while Chi Zhuo stayed behind for a moment, frowning slightly. He probably hadn’t expected someone who had once wanted to cooperate with him to now refuse so easily.
After pausing, he finally moved off in a direction completely different from Ji Chenxi’s, guided by the black mamba snake.
Parting ways—may each survive as best they can.
No two people can have that many coincidences without intention.
Ji Chenxi leaned forward on the snow leopard, one arm hooked around its neck to avoid falling while it ran, the other hand occasionally patting its head.
The terrain looked a bit like a primeval jungle, but unlike a normal jungle, it was eerily quiet. Too much quiet made the heart race, and along the way, there were no signs of any life forms inhabiting the planet.
After putting enough distance between himself and the danger, the snow leopard slowed.
Ji Chenxi straightened, releasing some spiritual energy to sense the surroundings, looking for a place with water that could also offer cover.
Once he found a spot, the snow leopard started moving again, taking him exactly where he had sensed.
He used his light-brain to check the water source. Once he confirmed it was safe, he found a corner to sit down, lightly shaking the spray in his hand before misting it over his twisted ankle and the few bruises from earlier.
