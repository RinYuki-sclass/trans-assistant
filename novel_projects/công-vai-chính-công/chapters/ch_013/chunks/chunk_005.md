Two hours of a death march order left Zuo Xian no time to waste. Following the pheromone trail, he pressed on.
The scent of blood spread fast. Once Ji Chenxi caught the faint trace in the air, he grabbed the filtered water he’d prepared, waved at the snow leopard beside him, and prepared to leave.
Back on the starship, Ji Chenxi had already sent a distress signal. No matter the delay, someone would reach him before morning.
To conserve some energy, Ji Chenxi planned to ride the snow leopard.
But just as he started moving, the snow leopard sensed something and slipped sideways into a dense bush. Dust-covered branches jutted right in front of Ji Chenxi, making his brow twitch.
After a few rounds of jungle exploration like this, he was pretty sure his little germophobia could be cured for good!
By the small forest stream, the dark-haired, cold-faced man quietly reached the water’s edge, washed his wounds, and only withdrew his hand once the blood had stopped flowing.
Ji Chenxi held his breath, shrinking his presence as much as possible, waiting for the man to leave.
Unfortunately, while he had hidden himself, he hadn’t tucked away his snow leopard in time.
The snow leopard leapt down from the tree, shook its massive head, and curiously stared at the black mamba. The snake, too weak to resist, hooked its tail around the snow leopard’s paw, looking rather pitiful. And the big cat got even closer, stretching out a paw to bat at the snake.
Chi Zhuo’s expression barely changed at the sight of the snow leopard. He used the snake’s sensing ability to locate the cat, then approached it and gently patted its head with his uninjured hand.
“Where’s your master?”
Hidden in the bushes, a certain master waved at his snow leopard.
“Bao Bao, come here.”
Chi Zhuo followed the voice and looked, slightly surprised, at the silver-haired beauty.
There was no spark in those eyes—but Ji Chenxi managed to read a few other meanings into them.
Bao Bao—sounds just like “baby.”
Who names a powerful alpha’s spiritual beast something like that?
Hiss… why does it feel a little embarrassing?
