One pair—a man with a bushy beard and another with a hooked nose—happened to be scouting nearby. At first, they hadn’t expected to run into anyone. But then, a faint whiff of pheromones in the air gave them pause.
The two exchanged a glance. They didn’t dare move forward recklessly. Once they were safely in their mechs, they cautiously headed in that direction, sharing the group’s location with the others at the same time.
The commotion they made was hard to miss. They were confident they could fire a photon cannon before being discovered. Yet the reality was that near a massive tree thick with pheromones, there were broken branches and signs someone had moved through—but no person in sight.
Had they run?
The hooked-nose man was extremely sensitive to smells. Speaking through comms to the bearded man, he said, “Cheng, they probably didn’t get far. The scent’s still strong.”
As he spoke, he moved toward where he felt the scent was thickest. Sure enough, a patch of grass rustled—not like it had been stirred by wind.
Found them.
He didn’t fire the photon cannon carelessly, not wanting to alert whoever was hiding in the grass. But the cautious hooked-nose man completely ignored the fact that the man on the other end of the comms hadn’t responded for a while.
As an A-level alpha, he felt a cold gust on his back and instinctively tried to pull back to fight—but there was no one behind him.
Confused, he realized too late: his mech’s arm had been ripped off from behind with brute force.
“Here you are!” A young man’s cheerful voice rang from the rear of the mech.
He’d been exposed.
Before the hooked-nose man could send a distress call over the public channel, a black mamba, thin as a strand of hair, suddenly struck, sinking its fangs into his hand.
That one exclamation mark was all the distress message ever became. His arm stiff and throbbing with pain, the hooked-nose man barely managed to send that single symbol before the mech’s door inexplicably swung open.
From a distance, a tall alpha in black stared blankly, while another, more familiar and strikingly handsome youth climbed effortlessly onto the mech.
His gaze lingered for just a second on the man struggling under the neurotoxin, then Ji Chenxi shifted his attention to the mech’s holo-screen.
That single exclamation mark had already caught the attention of the Red Scorpion Star Pirate gang. People were asking what had happened.
Ji Chenxi didn’t type a reply himself. Instead, he moved the man’s hand across the holo-screen and efficiently sent a message:
[Nothing. Accidentally sent it. Just smelled some pheromones, didn’t see anyone, probably ran off.]
On the public channel, some people cursed the man for scaring them, while others warned caution.
