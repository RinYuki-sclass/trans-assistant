Chi Zhuo didn’t flinch. He simply asked, “Still going to fight?”
Ji Chenxi said nothing. He pulled back the dagger that had been aimed at Chi Zhuo’s neck, its cold, deadly gleam now harmless.
His action alone had already made his stance clear. Like a coiled snake that had almost strangled him, Chi Zhuo released his hold and casually fixed the broken hand.
Breaking a hand and setting it back in place was a painfully slow process, but the person in front of him didn’t even flinch.
“Your Highness’s foot doesn’t look injured at all.”
Ji Chenxi replied with a faint smile, “And your eyes don’t look blind either.”
Chi Zhuo sneered.
Ji Chenxi didn’t respond with a sneer this time. Instead, he rubbed his neck. Fighting was fun, sure—but the pain was real. And long hair got in the way during close combat.
After sharpening his senses to scan the surroundings, Ji Chenxi was certain that the noises were made by humans. Whoever it was probably had already detected the pheromones Chi Zhuo was leaving behind.
This guy was like a walking, talking GPS.
Chi Zhuo’s expression shifted slightly. Their spiritual forms were engaged in battle too, but now, the fierce snow leopard in front of them was… licking the black mamba. That made things difficult for Chi Zhuo, who relied on his connection with the black mamba to sense the area.
The intimate sensation of the snow leopard’s tongue made him immediately sever his link with the mamba, but the tingling, almost numbing experience hit him before he cut it off. His face flushed a strange shade of red, and even his fingers trembled slightly.
Ji Chenxi was busy observing the distance and hadn’t noticed Chi Zhuo’s odd reaction. He nodded at the snow leopard, knowing the human couldn’t see it, and added casually, “Detox the snow leopard for me.”
He sounded confident, but inside, he was still worried Chi Zhuo wouldn’t comply. Earlier, the snow leopard had been bitten while hugging the man, and repairing the spiritual beast’s damage took a lot of energy.
No response came. Ji Chenxi glanced at Chi Zhuo and noticed his strange expression. He quietly stepped back and asked, “What’s wrong? Feeling unwell?”
Chi Zhuo didn’t answer him. He simply detoxed the snow leopard with precise efficiency.
“You’re so weird sometimes,” Ji Chenxi complained.
Technically, he was the last person who should say that. Chi Zhuo shot back, “And you’re not?”
Ji Chenxi wasn’t exactly acting without reason. He was unpredictable—warm and tender when he needed you, calling you ‘brother’ at every turn, but once you crossed his boundaries, he struck first without hesitation.
This little planet was huge, and only a few people had come here. They explored in pairs, tracking the group’s movements.
