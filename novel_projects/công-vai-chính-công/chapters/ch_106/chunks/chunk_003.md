As expected, along the way he ran into two more near-fatal attacks. Their starship was already barely holding together.
The pilot beside him was getting furious at his driving. “Can you at least try to dodge a bit? This ship can only take two more heavy blasts at most!”
Chen Xing gave a flat “mm,” cold and indifferent.
“If this keeps up, I’ll take over,” the pilot said, unable to stand his driving style anymore.
“We were supposed to have backup. But now, there’s no sign of them,” Chen Xing said suddenly.
“Huh? Maybe they’re just delayed.”
Chen Xing smiled faintly. “You and I both know—it’s not that they’re late.”
“It’s that they can’t come at all.”
