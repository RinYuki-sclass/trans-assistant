But in the end, their story turned into a tragedy. No matter what the truth actually was, deep down, Ji Chenxi still carried a trace of distrust toward marriage and love.
“You really do care about me,” he said.
But how long would that “care” last?
This time Ji Chenxi didn’t smile, nor did he show much expression. They had gone through a lot together, but sometimes he still wondered—once the heat and impulse faded, would they grow tired of each other?
They were both Alphas. Neither soft nor delicate. Their beginning had only been because of conquest.
And anything born from conquest… often ended the same way. The strongest thing that stirred an Alpha’s desire for conquest, after all, was still an Omega.
“We’re in a relationship. Of course I care about you,” Chi Zhuo said.
“If we weren’t in a relationship, you wouldn’t care?”
“Then I’ll take back the first part,” Chi Zhuo said smoothly.
“I care about you. That’s the part that matters.”
Ji Chenxi laughed. “Brother Chi Zhuo, that’s not really a good habit. If you give too much from the start, even the slightest withdrawal will be noticed.”
“Mm.” Chi Zhuo accepted it without hesitation. “So I won’t pull back.”
Ji Chenxi lifted his chin, forcing him slightly closer, then kissed him—without any gentleness. It was a kiss full of claiming and taking, breath stolen and mixed between them, hot and urgent.
This was a kiss that should have happened the moment they met.
When Ji Chenxi was young, he was mostly raised by his mother, and even his preferences leaned a bit toward Omega traits—he liked gentleness, liked calm restraint that stopped at just the right point. Even when things went out of control, he preferred the other person to lose their mind for him.
So this rare, fierce, dominant kiss from him was almost uncharacteristic.
When they finally separated, both of them were slightly out of breath. After a moment, Ji Chenxi shifted back into serious mode.
“As for Chen Xing, the robots and artificial humans he left behind—I handled them all myself. At first I didn’t notice anything strange, but I’ve known him for years. From the traces he left behind, I realized all his machines use the same system. And as long as it’s a system, it can be hacked.”
And Ji Chenxi was best at hacking.
On the other side, Chen Xing had escaped the palace, but he knew even if Chi Zhuo didn’t personally come after him, countless others would.
