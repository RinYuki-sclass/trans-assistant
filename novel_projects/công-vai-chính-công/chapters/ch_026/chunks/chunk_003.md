But Ji Chenxi just smiled at Chi Zhuo’s silence. His narrow phoenix-like eyes seemed to hide tiny hooks, and that light glance carried endless teasing and provocation.
“If you dare… go ahead and try.”
He even deliberately exposed his neck. Long, fair, flawless—radiating irresistible allure.
Chi Zhuo’s instincts screamed that this wouldn’t be simple. Poison hidden in honey. Yet he couldn’t suppress the craving inside him.
He wanted it—whether marking, possessing, or just closeness—he wanted to be near.
As Chi Zhuo leaned in, Ji Chenxi let out a muffled chuckle and turned his head, dodging just enough to keep him from reaching the vulnerable glands at the back of his neck.
Chi Zhuo frowned slightly, pressing his body down to try and stop Ji Chenxi’s wriggling, when a voice as sweet as honey sounded in his ear.
“Chi Zhuo… seems like it hasn’t been long, yet you’ve already forgotten our meeting on the starship.”
Soft words, lightly laced with a smile.
Ji Chenxi’s lips were beautiful—rose-colored, full, the kind that just begged to be kissed—but the words spilling from them were cold and cutting.
“Scared? You could try saying something nice, flatter me a little. Maybe then I’ll let you die in one piece.”
Chi Zhuo’s sharply defined face only flickered at the mention of the starship, then returned to its usual calm, composed mask.
“Something nice? How about this—Your Highness would look absolutely beautiful when you are marked.”
Ji Chenxi couldn’t help but laugh again. “That counts. Of course it counts.”
Chi Zhuo wasn’t sure whether to believe him or not. But when he stubbornly tried to mark Ji Chenxi again, even though he’d been careful, a sudden, violent pain shot through the glands at the back of his neck.
The sting was sharp enough to make his whole body go limp, and he collapsed right on top of Ji Chenxi.
Ji Chenxi silently thanked his luck that their distance wasn’t too great; even with Chi Zhuo falling on him, the weight was still bearable.
He easily freed his hand from Chi Zhuo’s grip and asked lightly, “Do you still think I would look beautiful when marked? You probably won’t get to see that version of me. Honestly, compared to that, I think you look surprisingly perfect right now, Chi Zhuo.”
Chi Zhuo wanted to scoff, but the pain in his glands made speech almost impossible. Any sound he made now wouldn’t be sarcasm—it would be agony.
Sweating coldly, Chi Zhuo finally succumbed to the relentless, stinging pain, completely collapsing onto Ji Chenxi.
