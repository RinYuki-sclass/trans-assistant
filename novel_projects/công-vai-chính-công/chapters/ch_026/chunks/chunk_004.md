“So you see,” Ji Chenxi said, radiating lazy satisfaction even though he knew Chi Zhuo couldn’t hear him, “I still trust you a little. Otherwise, how could you have managed to bite my neck in this state?”
After flipping Chi Zhuo off him, Ji Chenxi got to his feet. The first thing he did was put away his dagger and brush the dust off his clothes.
No matter how much he brushed, he still felt filthy, and in the end, he had no choice but to accept the fact with a resigned shrug.
Squatting down to look at Chi Zhuo, Ji Chenxi considered whether to give him one last jab.
His neck was still aching; even without seeing it, he could guess the deep bite marks left behind.
Holding the dagger, Ji Chenxi made a small gesture in the air, comparing it to where he’d been bitten—then pressed the blade gently to Chi Zhuo’s neck, carving a rose-shaped cut matching the size of his own bite.
Blood welled up, and the rich scent of tequila once again made its presence known.
Ji Chenxi, who never let a grudge go unanswered, used up the last of the scent suppressor, wiping out every trace of Chi Zhuo’s pheromones before they could spread again.
Once everything was done, he didn’t bother to look back.
This time, he left without hesitation—no turning back.
After Ji Chenxi’s departure, Chi Zhuo’s fingers twitched. Gritting through pain that would be unbearable to most, he activated his personal light-brain.
At that same time, Su Yu, whose evening at the banquet was winding down, went to find his friend.
He ended up spotting him sitting alone on a set of steps.
The meticulous, cleanliness-obsessed prince was… sitting on the steps.
“What’s our Third Prince doing hiding out here all alone?”
“What else?” Ji Chenxi replied casually. “Sober up a bit, I guess.”
Su Yu laughed and flopped down beside him. “The moon looks pretty good tonight.”
“The implication being… our prince isn’t here secretly meeting some little omega, right? I saw Bai Anran heading this way earlier, so I didn’t want to come look for you—didn’t want to ruin your little rendezvous. But just now, Nuan Nuan told me that Bai Anran’s mother wasn’t feeling well, and they’d both gone back.”
“I already said there’s nothing going on between me and Bai Anran,” Ji Chenxi said, his tone slightly more serious. “You can joke around in front of me all you want, but don’t say that outside.”
“Alright, alright, I get it—you’re not interested. Won’t say it again. But what’s that scent on you?”
