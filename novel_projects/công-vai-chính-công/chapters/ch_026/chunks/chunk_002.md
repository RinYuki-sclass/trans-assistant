Earlier, when he had spoken, Chi Zhuo had been flustered with anger. Now, with no response, Chi Zhuo wasn’t exactly feeling better.
“You looking like this… makes it feel like I’m the one bullying you,” Chi Zhuo sneered. “Maybe I should ask differently—Your Highness, you came back despite leaving once. Does that mean the humiliation you gave me wasn’t enough?”
He admitted that forcing a kiss on Ji Chenxi had been wrong. Being marked by Ji Chenxi was his own fault, a consequence of being tempted by beauty. But returning after leaving… and forcing another round of humiliation? That was pushing it.
“Humiliation?” Ji Chenxi’s face wasn’t any prettier, especially seeing that Chi Zhuo seemed even angrier than himself.
If he hadn’t come back this time, he’d already be on his second round of drinks.
Yet now, having gone out of his way to return, he was expected to take the blame for humiliating the other person?
“Your Highness… didn’t erasing the pheromone around me already count as humiliation?”
Ji Chenxi, who had been too lazy to even respond before, lifted his eyelids and spoke bluntly. “What nonsense are you talking about? How could anyone calm down if their pheromones weren’t suppressed during the susceptibility period? And didn’t I even bring you a suppression ring and inhibitors? You’re not seriously planning to spend your sensitive period here, are you? If that’s what you’re thinking, just forget I said anything.”
Now, with the scent of tequila covering him again, Ji Chenxi no longer reacted to it at all.
Chi Zhuo’s eyes narrowed slightly, as if trying to figure out whether this was the truth—or just another lie from the beautiful alpha.
Ji Chenxi clicked his tongue, growing impatient.
Chi Zhuo’s usually cold gaze softened a little. “I’m not in the right state right now. Completely leaving the pheromone behind makes me anxious.”
Ji Chenxi let out a light hum. Well, trying to do something nice and messing it up—typical.
“Let go,” Ji Chenxi ordered.
“Your Highness seems to have forgotten this is our second grudge. I’m already carrying your mark from before. Coming close to me now… it’s like a lamb walking straight into a tiger’s mouth.”
The ice in Ji Chenxi’s expression melted a bit at Chi Zhuo’s blunt words. He stared at him for a moment, then lifted the corner of his mouth into a faint smile. “So, Chi Zhuo… what exactly are you trying to do?”
“Of course… tit for tat.”
“Oh? You want to mark me.”
Chi Zhuo didn’t answer directly, but at that moment he truly wanted to mark this prickly, guarded person—to let his own pheromones completely saturate the other’s scent.
Usually, when an alpha hears another alpha wants to mark them, it sparks fury. Alpha pride isn’t to be trampled—being marked by another alpha is the ultimate humiliation, especially for someone like Ji Chenxi, barely eighteen.
