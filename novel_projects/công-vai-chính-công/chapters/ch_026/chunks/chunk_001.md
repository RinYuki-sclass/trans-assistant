Caught off guard, Ji Chenxi, who had been bitten, grabbed the other’s neck and hissed coldly, “Let go.”
Chi Zhuo didn’t loosen his bite; if anything, he sank his teeth in deeper.
What had been just a trickle of blood at first now started flowing freely down Ji Chenxi’s neck.
Ji Chenxi drew a sharp breath. He knew just squeezing the man’s neck wouldn’t make him release. So he reached for Chi Zhuo’s jaw, fingers pressing hard, ready to force it open—or even dislocate it if he had to.
Before Ji Chenxi could get vicious, Chi Zhuo shifted all his weight against him. The sudden press of force sent Ji Chenxi, already on the leaner side, stumbling backward as his balance faltered.
A small bottle of inhibitor rolled away with a clatter as Chi Zhuo slammed Ji Chenxi to the ground.
He inhaled deeply, breathing in the scent of Ji Chenxi’s blood—laced with pheromones—and for a moment, it calmed him.
But the calm didn’t last. The alpha beneath him started to struggle, and that brief peace shattered. A rush of anxiety hit again, sharp and greedy, driving him to bite deeper into the tender flesh of Ji Chenxi’s neck, craving more.
The sting from the sucking bite made Ji Chenxi hiss again.
Tonight, the sky was just right—dim moonlight, a dark, windy night perfect for killing.
Ji Chenxi fumbled for his dagger, the one he always carried. It was beautiful, almost decorative, but in his hand it became deadly. With a flick of his wrist, the thin, wing-like blade glinted coldly in the moonlight. Planning to strike at a safer spot to avoid splattering himself with blood, he froze when he suddenly felt someone licking his wound.
The warm, soft touch left Ji Chenxi momentarily dazed.
That brief hesitation was all Chi Zhuo needed. With his tongue, he cleaned up every drop of blood on Ji Chenxi’s neck. Then, freed from the torment of the heat, Chi Zhuo made his move. In a split second, he pinned down Ji Chenxi’s hand that had been holding the dagger.
Ji Chenxi still had his other hand free—the one that had been ready to grab Chi Zhuo’s jaw earlier—and this time, he didn’t hesitate. In an instant, a razor-thin blade of spiritual energy formed at his fingertips, shimmering like glass. More spikes of energy gathered in the air around them, ready to strike.
His intent was clear—he was ready to kill.
Everything he did was just too fast, but Chi Zhuo wasn’t slow either. Using clever leverage, he pinned Ji Chenxi’s other hand to the ground as well, while spiritual energy formed a protective barrier around him.
Before Chi Zhuo could even speak, a thin, sharp spike pierced through that barrier, aiming straight for his forehead.
Chi Zhuo tilted his head just in time to dodge it. But in that same moment, another spike broke through the barrier, heading for where he was now. Once again, Chi Zhuo maneuvered in a way so precise it was almost impossible, narrowly avoiding it, yet even in this dangerous position, the grip on Ji Chenxi didn’t loosen an inch.
Chi Zhuo’s eyes, cold and sharp as stars, locked onto Ji Chenxi’s. He asked softly, “Your Highness… how does it feel to be bitten?”
Ji Chenxi didn’t want to answer. He stayed silent.
