The murderous aura gathering between Chi Zhuo’s brows didn’t soften at all in response. Instead, he let out a low chuckle, his voice hoarse. “Your Highness is so… kind?”
Ji Chenxi feigned innocence. “I’d like to think I look like a good guy. How about taking a shot of the inhibitor to calm down a little?”
Chi Zhuo, with his alpha senses, could clearly detect that the pheromones on Ji Chenxi had disappeared. And even though new pheromones were clinging to him, they weren’t actually leaving a lasting scent.
This discovery only made Chi Zhuo, already in the throes of the susceptibility period, even more agitated. Overwhelmed by emotion, he rejected the offer outright.
Ji Chenxi assumed he just didn’t trust what he’d brought. “Then at least wear the suppression ring—it’ll help keep the scent in check.”
This time, he didn’t ask for permission. He simply fastened it around his neck.
The sleek black leather ring sat snugly against that long, slender neck—looked good on him, actually.
Ji Chenxi briefly admired it, then set the inhibitor beside him and sprayed him with the pheromone-neutralizing mist. The omega-grade spray worked fast—what had been a dense, overwhelming scent instantly thinned. The bottle in Ji Chenxi’s hand was now empty.
For Ji Chenxi, the fading scent was a relief, helping him stay clear-headed. For Chi Zhuo, though, it was like the final straw crushing a camel’s back.
Being marked by an alpha was unpleasant enough, but the scent had been what Chi Zhuo wanted. Now, the pheromones that had already entwined in the air vanished, making Chi Zhuo’s already restless gaze even more dangerous.
Once he was done, Ji Chenxi was ready to pull his hand free and make his exit. But the grip on his wrist didn’t ease. Instead, in that moment when Ji Chenxi relaxed just slightly, Chi Zhuo suddenly tightened his hold and pulled hard.
Half crouched, Ji Chenxi lost his balance and fell toward him—
—and Chi Zhuo didn’t hesitate. He sank his teeth into Ji Chenxi’s neck.
A dull pain shot through him, blood rising to the surface.
The rose scent that had completely faded from the air bloomed again.
Ji Chenxi’s eyes widened.
That’s what he got for being softhearted—like the farmer and the snake. Ji Chenxi and Chi Zhuo, same story, same ending.
