Bai Anran had grown up alongside Ji Chenxi, partly because their mothers were close friends, but also because Bai Anran’s grandfather was a war god and now the commander of the Third Legion. Bai Anran himself was a suitably aged S-rank omega.
Now, with Ji Anze choosing a noble girl from the Four Great Families, it seemed he had hit a dead end with Bai Anran.
Ji Chenxi’s mind turned quickly, piecing together a rough picture. His expression was hard to read. “You used me as a shield?”
Bai Anran nodded. He and Ji Chenxi had a verbal engagement. They weren’t officially betrothed, but many already treated them as fiances. That, in turn, allowed Bai Anran to successfully use Ji Chenxi as a buffer against this arranged engagement.
“I’m truly sorry about this. Back then, time was tight, and my grandfather was busy with military affairs. I couldn’t contact him immediately. As for my father—you know how he feels about me being an omega—he’s never been satisfied. When the empire’s crown prince extended the engagement invitation, he was tempted, and I had no choice but to take drastic measures.”
Ji Chenxi’s earlier light mood faded considerably. “You used me as a shield. If we end up together in the future, that’s one thing. But if we both find other partners, do you think my brother would let you off easily? Deceiving the royal family—that’s a serious matter.”
“That’s why I originally came to discuss the engagement with Your Highness,” Bai Anran explained. “We’ve only just come of age. There’s time to negotiate for years. If Your Highness ever finds someone you truly care for, I would willingly bear the consequences of ending the engagement. As for the reason to break it off, that’s even easier—lack of emotional compatibility, low pheromone match—there’s always a reason. My previous refusal doesn’t count as deceiving the royal family.”
Such boldness bordered on reckless audacity.
If he hadn’t read that book, Ji Chenxi might have felt as though he were seeing Bai Anran in a whole new light—perhaps even intrigued by this unusually clever omega, just as the book described. But having learned some of Bai Anran’s nature from the book, Ji Chenxi’s mood remained steady, without much surprise.
“If you had come to me two months ago, I might have agreed,” Ji Chenxi said, letting the words hang.
After all, they shared a childhood bond. Had Bai Anran come sooner, his chances would have been much higher.
Bai Anran smiled, showing no trace of disappointment at being refused.
“Honestly, the moment I got close to Your Highness, I already suspected I’d be rejected.”
“There’s a sweet wine scent on Your Highness, blended with your pheromones. That’s not a smell that comes from ordinary interaction. Looks like you already have your own omega.”
Ji Chenxi, who had been lazily leaning back in the chair, couldn’t help but laugh at that remark. His omega… Chi Zhuo?
If Chi Zhuo really was an omega… that would be wild.
Ji Chenxi had been in a good mood, but thinking it over made him hesitate, and he even felt a little uneasy.
“You think the scent on me is from an omega?” Ji Chenxi asked with a laugh.
Bai Anran was a little doubtful himself. Omegas with a wine-like scent were extremely rare, and this scent was vaguely familiar. Bai Anran had once caught a similar scent on Ji Chenxi, but back then he could clearly tell it was alpha pheromones.
In just a moment of hesitation, Bai Anran’s mind raced through several possibilities before he finally said, “Your Highness, let’s just pretend I didn’t run into you tonight.”
