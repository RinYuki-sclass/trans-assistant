An alpha carrying another alpha’s scent wasn’t a good sign, especially when the two scents clashed and intertwined, showing obvious signs of blending.
“It’s fine. I know you wouldn’t say anything random. I just got into a scuffle with another alpha because we didn’t get along, and our scents mixed,” Ji Chenxi said lightly.
“Mm,” Bai Anran replied softly, then pulled out a small bottle of scent remover and handed it to Ji Chenxi.
Ji Chenxi’s eyes narrowed slightly, and he smiled. “Thanks.”
He took the bottle, sprayed himself lightly, and the lingering scent that had clung stubbornly to him finally faded significantly.
“You used the verbal engagement we have as a reason to refuse the marriage—it’s not a big deal. We really do have that agreement. If your brother tries to bring it up later, just contact me.”
“Thank you, Your Highness.”
“No problem. And hey, since I borrowed your scent remover, I suppose I should give a little something back.”
Bai Anran knew well Ji Chenxi’s good intentions and could only nod seriously, expressing thanks again.
Just as Bai Anran was about to leave, Ji Chenxi, unusually proactive, called him back. “I just remembered something from when we were very young—you told me you wanted to follow in your grandfather’s footsteps and join the military.”
It really had been a long time. Bai Anran paused for a moment, trying to recall the memory.
He answered carefully, “I did say that back then.”
“If you still feel that way now, go for it boldly. Back then, when I said omegas weren’t fit for the military… my apologies.”
Bai Anran was momentarily stunned, then laughed. “Your Highness, not at all. It’s true that an omega in the military carries risks. When the mating season comes, it could be irresponsible and affect military operations.”
“But omegas have become generals before, haven’t they? The reason people don’t encourage omegas to serve is just that training is intense, and they worry about the physical toll. If none of that’s a problem, then there’s nothing stopping you. And for the mating season—aren’t there inhibitors?”
Bai Anran was about to say more, but Ji Chenxi waved him off. “Go on back. I see your aunt came with you—she’ll be worried after waiting so long.”
“Thank you for your thoughtfulness, Your Highness.”
Bai Anran cast Ji Chenxi a deep, lingering glance before taking his leave.
Ji Chenxi sprayed a little of the scent remover onto his hair. It wasn’t really surprising that Bai Anran hadn’t chosen him in the book—when he had said those words, he probably hadn’t meant anything by them. Most of his attention at the time was on his mother; Bai Anran had just been a playmate. But his careless remark had stuck with Bai Anran.
After spraying himself until the lingering scent was mostly gone, Ji Chenxi got ready to return.
