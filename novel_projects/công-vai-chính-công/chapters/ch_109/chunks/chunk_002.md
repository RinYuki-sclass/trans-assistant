“Then sleep well. I’ll take care of anything that comes up,” Chi Zhou said.
“Then I really will sleep, okay?”
Chi Zhou hummed in response, watching as Ji Chenxi yawned. A bit of moisture gathered at the corner of his eyes, and Chi Zhou lifted a finger to gently wipe it away.
“The prince really is carefree. Not afraid I might sideline you while you’re asleep?”
Ji Chenxi half-opened his eyes, glanced at him once, then closed them again without hesitation. “Go ahead and sideline me. I don’t want to deal with that pile of annoying stuff anyway.”
Chi Zhou gave a low laugh and said nothing more.
He stayed by Ji Chenxi’s side. Only when Ji Chenxi’s breathing gradually steadied, drifting toward sleep, did he silently get up. The current situation was turbulent—forces were shifting, and many factions would be reshuffled. It was the perfect time for underground powers to expand again. Some families were about to fall, making space for others to rise.
But before Chi Zhou could even leave the bed, his hand was grabbed.
“Did I wake you?” he asked.
Ji Chenxi’s eyes were still closed, but his grip was firm and steady—nothing like someone asleep.
“Or… is Your Highness reluctant to let me go?” Chi Zhou teased the “sleeping beauty.”
“I’m just afraid you’ll try to sideline me while I’m asleep,” Ji Chenxi threw back the exact words Chi Zhou had said earlier.
“So you’re staying here with me,” Ji Chenxi said simply.
“Just that?”
Ji Chenxi finally lifted his eyelids slightly. His deep blue eyes were completely clear now, staring straight at Chi Zhou.
“Or is there something else? Like Your Highness being reluctant to part with me.”
“You’re overthinking it,” Ji Chenxi said, lowering his gaze slightly. The half-lidded look made him seem distant and condescending, cold and untouchable—but his face softened it into something almost feline, proud yet strangely cute.
Even while saying “overthinking it,” Ji Chenxi still dragged Chi Zhou back into bed with him.
Chi Zhou lay down beside him without hesitation, immediately sinking into the warmth. The only trace of rationality left was that he quickly distributed his unfinished tasks through his terminal before fully giving in.
Once he put his work aside, he became even more aware of Ji Chenxi’s presence.
