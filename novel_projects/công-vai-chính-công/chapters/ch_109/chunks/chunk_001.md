After using forceful means to temporarily deal with a pile of messy problems, Ji Chenxi returned to the palace chamber. His expression looked slightly worn out as he closed his eyes, seeking a brief moment of peace.
But the moment he closed them, his hand was already taken. A vast surge of spiritual power flowed along his wrist, probing into him and starting to sort out the chaos in his spiritual domain. That enormous force didn’t feel uncomfortable at all—instead, it felt like being soaked in warm water, soft and calming.
Ji Chenxi, relaxed by the comfort, grew a little lazy. He half-lifted his eyelids, like a big cat lazily licking its fur, and looked faintly at the person approaching him.
“You seem to be enjoying this quite a bit,” Chi Zhou pointed it out.
“My head hurts,” Ji Chenxi said, hooking his fingers around Chi Zhou’s hand and smoothly dodging the topic.
Chi Zhou let out a light laugh. Ji Chenxi’s headache was really self-inflicted—he had used his spiritual power to remotely control someone who still had human consciousness, so of course he would suffer backlash. Normally, someone like that deserved a few sarcastic remarks.
But right now, Ji Chenxi’s eyes were half-closed, dark circles visible under them, even his brows carrying a faint trace of exhaustion. Looking at him like this, Chi Zhou couldn’t bring himself to say anything harsh. One moment he thought the other was being far too reckless, the next he felt the kid was still young—being a little willful wasn’t such a big deal.
In fact, even Chi Zhou himself was starting to get used to Ji Chenxi’s habit of not taking care of himself.
With his free hand, he reached up and massaged Ji Chenxi’s temples, lowering his voice as he responded, “Two more sessions of this and you’ll be fine.”
“You being this gentle all of a sudden… I’m not really used to it,” Ji Chenxi said.
“Oh? So in your eyes, I’m usually scary?”
“Can’t blame me. After all, Brother Chi Zhuo doesn’t show much mercy to me, this delicate flower.” Ji Chenxi himself was amused by the words “delicate flower” he had just said. No matter how you looked at it, even a weak Alpha had nothing to do with being a flower.
“If you didn’t say it, I almost forgot you’re still a delicate flower. Hmm? Little rose.”
The last words were rolled slowly on Chi Zhou’s tongue, carrying a faint, lingering intimacy.
Ji Chenxi chuckled at the “little rose.” “I really don’t like that nickname. When I was a kid, fine, but now? It doesn’t suit an adult Alpha.”
“But it’s cute.”
“Then I’ll allow you to call it in private.”
“Su Yu calls you that all the time.”
Ji Chenxi’s eyes were already closed again, like he could fall asleep any second. “He’s been calling me that since we were kids. I can’t exactly force him to stop.”
Right after saying that, Ji Chenxi hooked an arm around Chi Zhou’s neck and muttered that he was tired.
