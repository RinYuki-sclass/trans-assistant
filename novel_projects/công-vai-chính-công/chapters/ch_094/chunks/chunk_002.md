“Maybe it’s exactly that mystery that drew me in?”
“Although it’s a bit late, Your Highness can still eat something to fill your stomach.” Chi Zhuo raised his hand, his fingers moving slightly as he closed the book again. The page Ji Chenxi had just flipped to happened to be a somewhat intimate marking scene between Chi Zhuo and Bai Anran.
The book was closed a little too quickly and cleanly.
Ji Chenxi lowered his eyes and didn’t continue talking about the book. He casually tossed it aside and went to eat breakfast with the other man.
Ji Chenxi had a preference for seafood porridge, and today’s breakfast just happened to be his favorite. That bit of restlessness in him was soothed nicely by the food.
He used a spoon, taking small sips of the porridge, and while eating, he continued planning in his mind. Thinking from Chen Xing’s perspective—if Chen Xing wasn’t satisfied even with someone like him, whom he had known for years, then Ji Zhiqing would be even less likely to satisfy him. Unless Ji Zhiqing’s mother became Empress, making Ji Zhiqing the absolute rightful heir, otherwise, with Chen Xing’s personality, he would never serve Ji Zhiqing.
Since it wasn’t about paving the way for someone else, then what was the reason behind Chen Xing’s bold actions? It couldn’t be that he really wanted to rebel. Rebellion wasn’t impossible, but it didn’t fit his usual sense of aesthetics at all.
Just as Ji Chenxi frowned in thought, his communicator vibrated lightly again.
From the vibration pattern, Ji Chenxi was certain it was the darknet account he had kept logged in since yesterday.
As if sensing something, when he opened it, he wasn’t surprised to see Chen Xing’s invitation.
The other party wanted to continue planning with him.
This was normal—if he hadn’t already known that Chen Xing was no longer an ally.
“What is it?” Chi Zhuo asked.
Ji Chenxi smiled. The smile looked gentle, but it carried a sharp edge. “Looks like I’m about to attend a Hongmen Banquet.”
