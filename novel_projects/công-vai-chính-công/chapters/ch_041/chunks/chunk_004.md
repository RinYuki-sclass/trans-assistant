Ji Chenxi had listened quietly the whole time. Only when she finished did he say, “So that’s why you invited me to this forest park — even making a point to say you wanted to mingle with the crowd.”
“Morning Star said it’d be best if we got some pictures and attention out of it,” Lu Churan admitted with a faint laugh. “You’ve been keeping too low a profile lately, and public support matters. A ‘hero-saves-the-damsel’ moment would boost your image in no time. But honestly, with Your Highness’s looks, I think you’d win fans even without playing hero.”
Ji Chenxi smiled too, but this time there was a trace of frost behind his eyes. “Since Morning Star planned all this so carefully, why didn’t he tell me himself?”
She hesitated, then said, “He figured you’d refuse outright if he did. But if the request came from me — an Omega — you’d be less likely to turn me down.”
Ji Chenxi had suspected as much from the moment he noticed her little signals with Manye Zhe. Even so, it still annoyed him.
But standing before an Omega, he kept his temper in check. His voice stayed warm as he asked, “So, Miss Lu — do you actually plan to go through with this?”
Lu Churan tilted her head slightly. “If I fall into the lake, will Your Highness save me?”
“Of course,” Ji Chenxi said, his voice steady.
“Even if it means letting their plan lead you by the nose?”
“Compared to your safety, that doesn’t matter,” Ji Chenxi replied.
After all, the two of them had known each other for years.
Lu Churan smiled, this time not bothering to hide behind her fan. The gentle curve of her lips softened her small, delicate frame, giving her the quiet warmth of an older sister.
“But I’ve changed my mind,” she said softly. “I don’t want to go through with the plan anymore. Manye Zhe asked what you did after last night — he even wanted to stay behind for you. He just wanted to respect your choice. And now, I want to do the same. Even if we were to carry this out, it should only be with Bai Wei’s approval. Before you’re the one we’re destined to follow, you’re still our little rose first.”
That faint irritation from earlier melted away at the sound of her calm, gentle voice.
Ji Chenxi sighed, half helpless, half amused. “The lake water’s still cold this time of year — not even June yet. Let’s skip the part where you ‘accidentally’ fall in.”
Lu Churan laughed. “Looks like Your Highness just turned me down. Still, I am curious — who exactly is that mysterious someone you’re seeing? You really don’t plan to tell me?”
Ji Chenxi only shook his head. “Even if I didn’t have anyone, I’d still turn this proposal down.”
As he spoke, his comm bracelet gave a soft buzz. He lowered his gaze to check the screen — and there it was, a new message flashing at the top:
[Your Highness is doing great — you take my flower, and the next thing I hear, you’re getting cozy with some little girl.]
