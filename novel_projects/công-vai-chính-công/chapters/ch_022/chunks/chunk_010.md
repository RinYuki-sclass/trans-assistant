He had expected Chi Zhuo to at least question him about it, but… nothing.
Ji Chenxi’s lips curved in a faint smile, though he didn’t dwell on it.
As long as he didn’t actively interact with Bai Anran or Chi Zhuo, the book that supposedly chronicled his future was just that — a book.
A few leisurely days passed, and Ji Chenxi soon felt restless again. He had been thinking about whether to invite Su Yu to see the beautiful nebulae of Yunmu Star, when his butler handed him an invitation to a banquet.
Ordinarily, the butler wouldn’t deliver such invitations directly to Ji Chenxi — everyone knew he disliked formal gatherings, let alone his long-serving butler.
So this invitation, placed directly in his hands, was clearly hard to refuse.
Ji Chenxi opened it, and a smile spread across his face.
It was an invitation to his elder brother’s engagement three days from now — the engagement to a noblewoman.
Ji Chenxi had initially made such a scene on purpose. He wanted his brother’s actions to leave traces — a move so reckless it would expose his intent to kill. His extremely capable second sister would never miss such an opportunity.
While his brother was busy handling the fallout, and his sister exploited these cracks to target him, Ji Chenxi could simply enjoy his own life. The only unexpected twist had been his chance encounter with Chi Zhuo.
Now, it seemed his brother planned to lean on the noblewoman’s influence to solve a pressing problem.
Ji Chenxi shook his head lightly — that was a poor move.
Holding an engagement under these circumstances could easily make the noblewoman feel slighted. Her father, a man of considerable status, might also look down on Ji Chenxi’s elder brother.
The butler, respectful as ever, waited for Ji Chenxi’s reply.
“Master, will you attend?”
“I’ll go. After all, it’s my brother’s engagement banquet. If I don’t show, people will start gossiping.”
Three days later, Ji Chenxi arrived at the banquet.
Since it had nothing directly to do with him, he leisurely found a quiet corner and sampled the fine wines.
The engagement hall was stunning, lavishly decorated. Ji Chenxi, taking advantage of the elegant atmosphere, indulged in the wine enough to feel slightly tipsy.
Su Yu, ever mindful of his close friend, didn’t disturb him when he saw Ji Chenxi focused on tasting the wine.
