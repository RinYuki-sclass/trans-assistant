The irritation in Ji Chenxi’s eyes eased a little, though a flicker of surprise hid between his brows.
If it had just been a regular message, fine — Chi Zhuo could’ve easily gotten his contact through someone else. But this one came as a private message. Seeing the new name pop up on his light-brain’s friend list, Ji Chenxi didn’t even need to guess who pulled the trick — that quiet, refined-looking female beta.
When Chi Zhuo’s squad regrouped, that beta had practically taken charge of cleaning up the battlefield. She’d only passed by Ji Chenxi once, barely a glance, yet somehow that single brush-by was enough for her to sneak herself onto his friend list.
That little silent move alone told Ji Chenxi she wasn’t just capable — she was sharp. No wonder she could stick by Chi Zhuo’s side like a right-hand aide.
He looked twice at the newly added contact, then finally tapped open the message.
【Thanks for your help at the cave earlier, Your Highness.】
Ji Chenxi arched a brow, lazily tapping a few times before sending a reply.
【Chenxi: Oh? Just a thank-you and that’s it?】
【Then what kind of thank-you gift does Your Highness want?】
Ji Chenxi had only meant to tease, but when the question of a “gift” actually came up, the fun sort of faded.
He hadn’t helped expecting any reward. As a prince of the Empire, whatever he wanted would be handed to him without him lifting a finger — no need to earn favors through a rescue.
His fingers tapped twice on the words ‘thank-you gift’, but instead of brushing him off completely, he typed back a cool:
[Haven’t thought about it.]
Once that message was sent, Ji Chenxi set the light-brain aside. His elder brother had already walked up to him.
The Empire’s eldest prince was named ‘Ji Anze’ — a name that sounded gentle and refined.
But no matter how you looked at him, “refined” was the last word that came to mind. If anything, Ji Chenxi thought the man had picked up quite a few of the classic ‘alpha flaws’.
Top of the list? Arrogance.
Then again, that wasn’t exactly rare among alphas. Even Ji Chenxi himself wasn’t completely free of it.
Still, that didn’t mean he had to like the man standing in front of him, putting on this whole “affectionate elder brother” act. Ji Chenxi had long since seen through Ji Anze’s wolfish ambition, yet the man insisted on keeping up that fake harmony between them.
As expected, the moment Ji Anze arrived, he began his usual round of concern and comfort.
