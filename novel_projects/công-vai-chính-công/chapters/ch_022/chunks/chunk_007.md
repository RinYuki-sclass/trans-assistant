“Call Mr. Hua. I have urgent matters to discuss with him. You won’t need to come in later.”
When Mr. Hua, still wearing his bionic mask, entered, Bai Han immediately set up multiple layers of protection, ensuring no one could see what was happening inside.
Mr. Hua’s expression turned grave.
“Xiao Bai, what is it?”
“The master’s pheromones are destabilizing.”
“How is that possible?!”
“I don’t even know what’s really happening right now,” Bai Han said, tension in her voice. “By all logic, it should be slowly stabilizing, but I suspect the master forced his spiritual energy ‘before’ his pheromones had fully settled.”
“Do you have a solution?” Mr. Hua asked.
“Two options. First, remove the glands entirely.”
“That’s definitely not an option,” Mr. Hua said immediately, just hearing it.
“Then there’s the second,” Bai Han continued. “We can re-inject the pheromones extracted from the master’s glands earlier. Those pheromones aren’t highly antagonistic with his own, so they could neutralize the destabilizing ones. There’s about a seventy percent chance it could stabilize the breakdown. But if we force another pheromone in while his own is still weak and unstable, it could create dependency between the two pheromones — plus some side effects I can’t fully predict.”
Mr. Hua’s forehead was already slick with sweat. He wondered why Bai Han, who could make the decision herself, had to drag him in. Was this because of the master’s punishment? If things went wrong, the two of them would die together.
Seeing the look on Mr. Hua’s face, Bai Han shook her head.
“I didn’t call you in to drag you down. It’s just that something this important shouldn’t be decided by me alone. If the master were conscious, I’d ask him directly. But since he’s unconscious, the only person I can consult is you.”
“Neither option is good.”
“True. But they’re the only options I can think of.”
“What if we simply never touch that pheromone again?”
“Then naturally, there’d be no effect.”
Mr. Hua exhaled softly.
“Then option two it is.”
