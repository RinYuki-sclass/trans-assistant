So Chi Zhuo really had been fooling him.
“Must’ve been hard for you, keeping such a straight face.”
Chi Zhuo chuckled again. “Even if that’s not what actually happened, am I not allowed to imagine it?”
Ji Chenxi was a little curious about what really happened, but if he asked now, wouldn’t that make his earlier confidence look fake? He had only just gotten over the fever, his body still weak, his mind a bit sluggish. He was about to skip the topic when Chi Zhuo suddenly added—
“Though, when Your Highness was in your susceptible period, those fierce, bossy orders of yours… they were kind of cute in their own way.”
“Cute? I’ll give you one chance to pick a different word.”
Chi Zhuo paused, briefly at a loss, before offering, “Another kind of… enjoyable—”
He didn’t even get to finish before Ji Chenxi cut in, putting on a fake shy look. “Enjoyable fun?”
“I believe what I meant to say was situation.”
Of course, Ji Chenxi knew exactly what he had been trying to say.
Now that the susceptible period had passed, everything felt laid out in the open. He could clearly feel Chi Zhuo’s feelings for him—and his own feelings, too, were far deeper than he had ever expected. Neither of them planned to say it out loud. It was enough that they both understood. That was also why, after Ji Chenxi woke up, neither of them brought up that “inappropriate” thing he had said before. Given how things stood now, even if it had been inappropriate before… it no longer mattered. It had to fit, whether it should or not.
After a brief moment of tidying up and lingering warmth, Ji Chenxi got straight to dealing with the matter of his forced susceptible period.
At first, he thought Chi Zhuo had already handled everything cleanly, leaving nothing for him to do. But it turned out Chi Zhuo had only sorted through the entire situation from start to finish, completed all the groundwork, and deliberately left the final step—the closing move—unfinished.
“Your people getting less efficient?” Ji Chenxi teased, even though he knew full well Chi Zhuo had done it on purpose, leaving him a chance to handle things personally.
“Mm,” Chi Zhuo agreed without hesitation. “They’ve been on break lately, so efficiency hasn’t been great. Your Highness want to step in and help?”
Ji Chenxi took the investigation report Chi Zhuo handed over. Normally, a reluctant “fine, I’ll do it” would’ve fit his usual style. But this time, after taking the documents, he spoke with rare sincerity.
Chi Zhuo could’ve stayed out of this completely. Putting in extra manpower and resources like this was nothing more than helping him catch the enemy by the tail.
“No need to thank me. Why not take a look and see who’s behind it?”
“Actually, I don’t need to. I already had a guess.” As Ji Chenxi quickly scanned through the files, the answer in his mind was confirmed.
