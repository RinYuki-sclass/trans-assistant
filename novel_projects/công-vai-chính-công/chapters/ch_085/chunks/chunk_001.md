An alpha’s susceptible period was always a long process. By the time he fully came back to his senses, several days had already passed.
When Ji Chenxi first woke up, he was still a little dazed, staring blankly at the ceiling for quite a while.
The mix of rose scent and alcohol in the air wasn’t as strong as it had been at the start. Instead, it had settled into a deep, lingering fragrance, like the soft aftertaste of something rich. His gaze slowly drifted away from the ceiling and landed on Chi Zhuo not far away.
Chi Zhuo was fiddling with his light brain, probably replying to messages. When he noticed Ji Chenxi staring at him, he seemed to think Ji Chenxi wanted a kiss. Without hesitation, he closed the virtual screen, pulled Ji Chenxi into his arms, and lightly kissed his lips.
That smooth, almost practiced way of coaxing with a kiss made Ji Chenxi’s expression turn a little strange. He even asked, a bit puzzled, “That way you kiss…?”
Chi Zhuo, who had just scooped him up and kissed him like it was second nature, lifted his eyes and studied Ji Chenxi’s expression. “You’re better now?”
“Mm.” Ji Chenxi gave a low, muffled reply.
“Do you remember what happened these past few days?” Chi Zhuo asked, gently patting Ji Chenxi’s head, as if checking his temperature.
Ji Chenxi nodded at first, then after thinking for a moment, shook his head instead. Only the tips of his ears quietly turned red.
To be honest, he did remember quite a bit. But as for why Chi Zhuo was kissing him with such ease, like he’d done it many times before… that part, he really couldn’t recall.
“I see,” Chi Zhuo murmured, thinking for a moment before explaining, “After the first couple of days of your susceptible period, Your Highness started running a high fever that just wouldn’t go down.”
Ji Chenxi nodded. When he woke up earlier, he’d already felt how dry his throat was, burning with pain. It matched the rare times he’d had a cold before. As for why no treatment device had been used, it was probably because this susceptible period had been triggered by an omega’s heat, and later there had been an inhibitor injection along with some unknown catalyst.
“Anything else?”
Chi Zhuo nodded. “There’s more.” He continued, “These past few days, your fever never went down, and you never fully came out of your susceptible state. Every day, you were crying, acting spoiled, begging me to hold you.”
By the time he heard that, Ji Chenxi’s brows were already tightly furrowed. He didn’t think he would act like that at all. But Chi Zhuo’s earlier, completely natural movements seemed to back up his words, like there wasn’t a single lie in them.
Ji Chenxi found himself half-convinced. An alpha in a susceptible period was naturally more sensitive and prone to tears. Even though the idea of himself crying and acting clingy felt unbearably embarrassing… maybe it really had happened.
But none of that showed on his face. If anything, his expression turned even stranger. “I was in a susceptible period, not drunk and blacked out.”
The meaning was obvious—’stop messing with me.’
Chi Zhuo let out a soft laugh. “So Your Highness really does remember.”
Ji Chenxi: “…”
