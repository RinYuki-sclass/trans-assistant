Because by the last day, the security checks wouldn’t be as strict as the first two days.
Ji Chenxi nodded in agreement. “But how would they act on the last day? It’s not like they can just bring in large-scale bombs.”
“No matter how relaxed the checks are, they won’t be able to bring in large weapons. But the real concern is if they seize control of the palace’s internal heavy weapons.”
Ji Chenxi sucked in a light breath, feeling a bit troubled.
“Your Highness doesn’t need to worry too much. I’ll be attending the next two days. Just stay within my sight as much as possible. Even if you move alone, be careful.”
Ji Chenxi casually brushed his hair back, pushing the loose strands off his forehead again. “We’ll cross that bridge when we get there. Let me see what kind of game they want to play.”
Ji Chenxi’s departure was noticed first by Ji Zhiqing, aside from Lu Churan who was by his side.
She took a light sip of the champagne in her glass, staring thoughtfully in the direction Ji Chenxi had left.
Beside her stood a male omega with delicate features. This omega wasn’t the traditionally handsome type. He was too thin, to the point that his cheekbones stood out a bit. There were even a few faint freckles on his face, but those features, which should have been somewhat cute, didn’t quite match his overall look.
“You’re looking at Ji Chenxi?”
The male omega’s voice pulled Ji Zhiqing back to her senses. She gave a soft “Mm.” “I can’t not pay attention to him.”
Ji Zhiqing was ambitious, and also arrogant. She didn’t think much of Ji Anze. He was just smart, and even his current power mostly came from his maternal family. But Ji Chenxi was different. Even though he lost his mother at a young age, he still held an absolute advantage among the three of them.
“Honestly, compared to you, I like Ji Chenxi’s face more. He’s very good-looking. No omega would want to refuse him.”
Facing such words from the omega she was about to get engaged to, Ji Zhiqing only smiled. “But in the end, you still chose me.”
“As expected. No matter how good his face is, there’s no real possibility between him and me. And we’ve at least grown up together.”
“Then thank you for your favor.”
“To be honest, I’m a bit curious why my mother chose you. Compared to the First Prince and the Third Prince, your chances aren’t that great.”
“Probably because I’m a bit smarter than my elder brother, and just happen to be older than my younger brother.”
“But His Majesty favors the Third Prince!”
This time, Ji Zhiqing only smiled without saying anything.
