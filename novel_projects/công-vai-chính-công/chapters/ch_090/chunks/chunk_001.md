Ji Chenxi blinked lightly, but in the end couldn’t hold back a laugh. He shook his head as he laughed and said with a sigh, “That’s really straightforward.”
Chi Zhuo didn’t speak, which basically counted as admitting it.
No matter whether there were people around or not, they were still inside the imperial palace. Naturally, they didn’t do anything inappropriate. But sometimes, just meeting each other was already enough to make them happy, even if they did nothing at all during that time.
“Actually, you didn’t have to come find me right now. It’s easy to give people something to use against you.”
“But I really wanted to see you.”
That explanation from Chi Zhuo sounded like no explanation at all, but Ji Chenxi still caught the deeper meaning. Of course, the other man knew that coming directly to pick him up wasn’t appropriate, yet he still came—not out of arrogance in his own strength, but simply because he wanted to see him.
“Actually, even if you didn’t come find me today, I would’ve come to find you.”
“Looks like we were thinking the same thing.”
Ji Chenxi let out another soft laugh. “Yeah, we were thinking the same thing.”
The two of them walked together through the quiet night, keeping a distance that was neither too close nor too far, chatting casually. The atmosphere was relaxed and pleasant.
“Something happened recently, something that made me a bit unhappy.”
“What happened?”
Ji Chenxi didn’t really want to tell Chi Zhuo about possibly being stabbed in the back by someone he trusted. But there were too few people he could talk to about this. Maybe the night was just too gentle, because for once, Ji Chenxi took the initiative to show a bit of his vulnerability.
“I have a friend. Of course, this friend isn’t me—I really do have such a friend. We haven’t actually met much in real life. We’ve known each other online for many years, and we mostly keep in touch through the internet. He’s been with me through a lot of important moments in my life, and he’s guided me when I felt lost. I once thought we would be friends forever. From an adult’s point of view, ‘forever friends’ might sound a bit naive and laughable, but at least until half a year ago, I still believed that.”
Chi Zhuo hadn’t interrupted before, just listened quietly. But when he heard the words “naive and laughable,” he still frowned slightly. “That’s not naive and laughable. Who says bonds from youth can’t last forever? Even if they really can’t, that’s his problem.”
Because of talking about this, Ji Chenxi’s mood dipped a little. He shook his head and sighed again. “Brother Chi Zhuo, I think the filter you have on me right now is a bit too strong. Maybe you should tone it down a little.”
“Alright, alright. Now I feel like Su Yu should come learn from you about how to please people.”
“Then he probably wouldn’t learn much.”
“Don’t say that,” Ji Chenxi adjusted his mood slightly, making his tone sound like he didn’t care much. “You probably guessed some of it already. I trusted him completely, but he seems to have betrayed that trust. From the beginning, I was indeed in the wrong. I did something he thought was wrong and needed to be corrected immediately. I refused his suggestion and insisted on doing things my own way.”
“Then does this ‘wrong thing’ you did have anything to do with that friend’s own interests?”
