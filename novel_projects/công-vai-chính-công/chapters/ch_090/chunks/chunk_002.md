“I personally think it doesn’t,” Ji Chenxi said. “After all, it’s my own business. It’s just that he’s a bit of a perfectionist. Maybe I shouldn’t have told him in the first place.”
Chi Zhuo fell silent for a moment after hearing that. It wasn’t clear if he had guessed something. He raised his hand and gently rubbed Ji Chenxi’s head, like a caring elder patting a child, full of endless tolerance and warmth. “Our Highness wouldn’t regret something he already decided on, right? Since it has nothing to do with him, what right does he have to betray you over it? So it’s all his fault. Your Highness doesn’t need to feel unhappy over someone else’s mistake.”
“You…” Ji Chenxi hesitated, then stopped. “Why do I feel like even if this whole thing was completely my fault, you’d still say he just didn’t know what was good for him?”
“Oh, Your Highness is really smart.”
Ji Chenxi laughed. Suddenly, he felt like it was actually pretty good that they were both alphas. No kids. Otherwise, with the way Chi Zhuo was, he’d definitely spoil a kid into a little troublemaker.
“The ‘wrong thing’ Your Highness mentioned… does it refer to being with me?”
Chi Zhuo hadn’t wanted to ask, but after only a second of hesitation, he still decided to say it out loud.
“I already said, it’s something he thinks is wrong,” Ji Chenxi emphasized.
Chi Zhuo let out a low laugh. His hand had just left Ji Chenxi’s head, and now he already wanted to put it back again. “Your Highness, there’s something I originally planned to tell you after everything settled, but telling you now isn’t bad either.”
“Hm?” Ji Chenxi made a small sound of confusion.
“Before, I mentioned that the person behind Lan Ruoxi had some connection to the people behind the earlier human experiments. Now it’s basically confirmed that they come from the same force. That force has infiltrated quite a few places. Judging from their recent actions, they’re probably planning to interfere in your succession struggle.”
Back when it came to the human experiments, Ji Chenxi had focused all his attention on the observation records, and didn’t pay much attention to the power behind the lab afterward. He hadn’t expected their reach to be this wide.
“This force has been around for a long time?”
“I’m not sure when they first appeared, but when my people dealt with them seven years ago, they already showed signs of being a well-established force. They have three leaders in total, and right now, all three should be on the Imperial Star. Bai Han has already started investigating. Based on what we’ve found, they might be planning another big move soon.”
From their previous two moves—using Lan Ruoxi as a pawn and bombing Ji Anze—the next move would definitely also be related to their fight for the throne.
So if they wanted to interfere in the succession struggle, when would be the best time to make a move?
Ji Chenxi’s eyes turned slightly cold.
The three-day ball after the princess’s birthday was the time when the palace was most crowded and chaotic. If they were going to act, it would definitely be during those days.
“If they choose these three days of the ball, when do you think is the best time?”
“The last day.”
