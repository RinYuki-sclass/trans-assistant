Fortunately, it was a wound that would heal in a few days, but hearing the news made Song Tae-won dizzy. What if it had been a worse injury? What if something went wrong and Sung Hyun-jae left his side?

That could not happen.

Song Tae-won had made up his mind. If Sung Hyun-jae had found someone new, then that was something Song Tae-won could not help. There was nothing left for Song Tae-won to do for him. His role ended there.

Someone else would take his place and do everything he had done until now. Song Tae-won had to step away from Sung Hyun-jae’s side. If that was what Sung Hyun-jae wanted, then it was only natural.

However, once he heard the news that Sung Hyun-jae was hurt, he couldn't hear anything else. His mind, his body, and even the blood flowing through his veins were all on edge for Sung Hyun-jae.

Would he be alright? He must be. Why would a man who never tolerated even a single scratch become so fragile?

"Ugh, haa."

"Just hold on a little longer."

"...Yes. Ugh."

Song Tae-won wanted to see Sung Hyun-jae. He wanted to rush home right now, grab him, and see for himself. How badly he was hurt, how much pain he was in.

He wanted to strip away all the clothes he was wearing and examine every corner of that pale, beautiful body. He wanted to check for any other injuries, and once he found them, he wanted to lick them until they were healed.

"The treatment is finished. Do you have any discomfort moving?"

"...Yes, thank you."

"As you requested, I won't report this to Seseong. Honestly, I never expected someone who rarely gives up on overtime to come back covered in blood less than an hour after leaving early."

"I apologize."

"Don't apologize. At this rate, it looks like it will take more than a week to heal. Are you really planning on not going home for a week?"

It was obvious. There was no way Song Tae-won could stand before Sung Hyun-jae in this state.

"...Yes."

"Haa, I don't even know what I'm going to tell Seseong this time."

"I'm counting on you."
