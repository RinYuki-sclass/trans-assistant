"Good heavens, where on earth is your mind!"

Song Tae-won was a man who, by all accounts, was as stoic as a block of wood, the type who wouldn't bleed even if you pricked him with a needle. He was that firm and rational a person. While he was respected by others for his precise work ability that never allowed for a single mistake, he was simultaneously so chillingly perfect that it left people clicking their tongues in awe. Yet, such a man had been making frequent mistakes lately.

Fortunately, there was no lapse in his work. Whether it was instinct or something else, he remained excellent in his professional duties. However, everything else was hazardous.

For instance, he would pour boiling water over his hand while preparing coffee, or draw blood with a utility knife while opening a pack of copy paper. Seeing these mistakes, one after another—things that Song Tae-won would never normally do—his colleagues realized that something was going on with him.

"You're making mistakes that even the new recruits wouldn't make, Chief."

"…I suppose so."

"Goodness. I've never seen someone slice themselves up like this with a utility knife. You'll need to be careful for a while."

"Yes."

They didn't pry. That was a relief for Song Tae-won. He clenched and unclenched his hand, which had become heavy from the bandages, and let out a soft sigh. There was no need to go to the hospital. He would just have to suffer for a few days. His wounds healed faster than most people's, after all.

The problem was that he had already been hurt. The news of the incident where Song Tae-won got injured would have already reached him.

"I heard you were hurt."

"Yes."

Sung Hyun-jae approached Song Tae-won as he arrived home and asked with concern. His pale face was filled with worry. He lifted the bandaged hand to examine it closely, asking to see the wound. Even though the injury was hidden beneath the bandages, the way he furrowed his brows and contorted his face made it seem as though he were truly worried.

Song Tae-won looked at that handsome, masculine face. This beautiful man was his lover. For a long time, misunderstandings and misinterpretations had ruined them and left them both wounded.

It seemed that fate had intervened after all, granting them another chance and allowing them to understand each other's feelings. It was a result they had obtained with great difficulty. That was why he wanted to be with him for as long as possible.

"You should have been more careful."

"…Were you worried?"

"What an obvious thing to ask."

"It seems you are worried, after all."

"…What?"
