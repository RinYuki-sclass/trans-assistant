"You're back."

"Yes, I've returned."

The awkward air brushed against his skin. It wasn't Song Tae-won’s imagination. The lover he knew would never treat him this way. Even if it weren't Song Tae-won, it would be the same.

No one could ever imagine Sung Hyun-jae reacting with such awkwardness toward anyone.

"You're late. Go wash up quickly. Let's have a simple meal."

"...Yes."

He watched the back of Sung Hyun-jae as he walked into the kitchen. Then, it suddenly struck him that Sung Hyun-jae’s attire was not for indoors. Sung Hyun-jae, who should have returned home earlier than Song Tae-won, was still dressed for going out. He was still wearing the three-piece suit that one would immediately associate with him.

Why was he still in his outdoor clothes? When did he get home? Why hadn't he changed his clothes from the moment he arrived until now? Had he been on the phone the whole time? If he was on the phone, what was the conversation about? Who was the person on the other end?

Who could it be?

Something had begun to go wrong.

Even if he told himself not to care, he couldn't help it. That was true even for Song Tae-won, who was indifferent to others. Moreover, the person in question was not just anyone. He was Song Tae-won’s lover.

It was only natural for Song Tae-won to be concerned about his lover’s abnormal behavior, and in a way, it was only natural that Song Tae-won would leave work early every evening instead of staying late.

The whispers of a secret meeting in the dark bedroom without turning on the lights had ended on the first day. Claiming that it was a lie, Sung Hyun-jae had not engaged in any such abnormal behavior since. That was precisely why Song Tae-won could not help but be even more suspicious.

If it were the usual Sung Hyun-jae, he would have told Song Tae-won everything without hiding a single thing. But this time was different. Sung Hyun-jae was unusually intent on keeping secrets, even if it meant giving Song Tae-won reason to be suspicious.

Song Tae-won grew anxious. As the days passed, Sung Hyun-jae withdrew further into himself. He had created a secret. He intended to hold it tightly in his hand and never show it. Song Tae-won had never asked him to show it, yet he wanted him to.

That was why he had sent countless signals. In one way or another. Sung Hyun-jae should have known. If it were Sung Hyun-jae, no matter how late it was, he should have confessed to Song Tae-won long ago.

However, Sung Hyun-jae did not. Sung Hyun-jae ignored Song Tae-won’s signals. Why? What had made Sung Hyun-jae change? What on earth was Sung Hyun-jae planning?

"Chief!"
