Sung Hyun-jae let out a weak laugh. A lovers' quarrel. Yes, it certainly felt like they were fighting, but he had no way of knowing why they were fighting in the first place. Perhaps he himself was the one who wanted to die the most.

"You do realize that the report on your recent injury was transferred to the Awakening Management Office, right?"

"Hmm."

"They pried into everything—how you got hurt, how badly you were injured, how much blood you lost, whether you received treatment, and even how many days it would take to heal. They called back thirty minutes later just to ask if there would be any lasting side effects."

It was said that when a superior suffered, their subordinates suffered too. The subordinates, who had vaguely sensed the abnormal behavior between Song Tae-won and Sung Hyun-jae, had initially dismissed it as a simple change of heart.

However, a week passed, and as the long period stretched toward a month, the cold war between the two showed no signs of ending. The anxiety fell to the subordinates.

"You should go home early and get some rest today. Please, just make up already."

Sung Hyun-jae agreed with the suggestion that he should rest. His relationship with Song Tae-won was not improving; it was only getting worse by the day. Because of this, his personal emotions were running wild, unable to distinguish between public and private matters.

He needed some rest to dull his overly sharpened senses. Besides, he felt he needed to have a proper conversation with Song Tae-won.

1:00 AM. Sung Hyun-jae caught his breath, unable to help himself. He wondered what this was. Sitting on the sofa, Sung Hyun-jae buried his face in his hands.

Song Tae-won was not coming home.

Song Tae-won never stayed out overnight. Yet, even though the date had changed, there had been no word from him, and he had not returned home. For Sung Hyun-jae, who had been waiting to regain his usual, peaceful life, it was a moment that felt like betrayal.

How had it come to this? Why, exactly? What was the reason? They had both suffered and strived so hard just to have each other. They had protected one another without giving up, despite the interference and threats from others. Was the magnitude of their feelings really only this much?

Sung Hyun-jae did not believe it. No. That couldn't be it. Sung Hyun-jae knew better than anyone the depth of Song Tae-won’s feelings for him.

Song Tae-won would never let him go like this.

But.

But, what if it were true? What if Song Tae-won no longer wanted him? If that were the case, what was Sung Hyun-jae supposed to do?

While Sung Hyun-jae was shuddering in agony, Song Tae-won was also suffering in a different way.

Upon receiving the report that Sung Hyun-jae had been injured, Song Tae-won felt as if all the blood had been drained from his body. How badly was he hurt? Was he in a dangerous condition? Would there be any lasting side effects?
