"Birthday, ah."

It was Song Tae-won’s birthday, a fact he had completely forgotten.

"So, today... it was my birthday. Thank you."

Song Tae-won expressed his gratitude in a low voice. Sung Hyun-jae remained silent. Lost in his own thoughts, Song Tae-won paid no further attention to him.

The conversation died out. Watching him, Sung Hyun-jae walked into the bedroom. He returned carrying a black paper bag.

"This is..."

"Open it."

Song Tae-won recognized the brand. It was one that Sung Hyun-jae dealt with personally. It was surely crafted from the most expensive, high-quality fabric available. Song Tae-won opened the box. Yes, just as he had expected, it was a black suit of exquisite material.

"It’s a suit."

"Yes. Do you like it?"

"...Yes."

"I spent every evening glued to my phone in my room, trying to commission a gift for Chief Song."

Ah.

"They don't usually take private orders, so it was quite a struggle. If you like the gift, I would appreciate a compliment in return."

How could he be so narrow-minded and foolish? As if he understood exactly why he had acted that way, Song Tae-won pulled his lover, who was smiling faintly, into an embrace.

The suit, which had been carefully folded and wrapped, tumbled to the floor and became crumpled, but neither of them cared.

Sung Hyun-jae, trapped in Song Tae-won’s arms, squirmed. He seemed to be laughing. Song Tae-won, holding Sung Hyun-jae, also flinched. He wondered if he might be crying.

"Song Tae-won."

"I am... sorry."

His cracked voice betrayed his true state. Breaking free from Song Tae-won’s embrace, Sung Hyun-jae looked up at him.
