Song Tae-won realized his mistake and swallowed a groan inwardly. He had become overly sensitive. It was a state born from a mixture of Sung Hyun-jae’s inexplicable changes, Song Tae-won’s own anxiety, and an invisible future.

"Song Tae-won, what on earth—"

"…I’m tired. I’m going to sleep first."

It was Song Tae-won who severed the flow. He avoided Sung Hyun-jae.

The warmth that had been touching the hem of his suit sleeve fell away. The bedroom door opened and closed. The sound of Song Tae-won’s presence could be heard from within, muffled and distant.

"What is this…"

Sung Hyun-jae witnessed an inexplicable misalignment. A cold ache spread through a corner of his chest.

Perhaps it was from that day on. The emotional tether between Song Tae-won and Sung Hyun-jae began to fray.

Their precarious tightrope walk was not limited to their home life. It spilled over into their respective professional lives as well.

To Sung Hyun-jae, Song Tae-won’s inscrutable behavior eventually turned into poison. For days, Sung Hyun-jae had approached him with gentleness, only to be met with Song Tae-won’s cold indifference.

Eventually, Sung Hyun-jae let go as well. Not knowing the reason for Song Tae-won’s behavior, Sung Hyun-jae grew sensitive, plagued by an inexplicable irritation and the anxiety of their changing relationship.

"Chief Song is said to have been injured during overtime yesterday."

"Again? Why?"

"I’m not sure. They say it was another issue of focus."

"Ha, for heaven's sake."

His head throbbed from a headache. It was a new ailment that had appeared only in the last few days.

His hand pressed heavily against his furrowed brow. Sung Hyun-jae, making no effort to hide his foul mood, had a bandage wrapped around his left hand, stained here and there with blood.

"Setting Chief Song aside, you’re a problem too, Guild Leader. Why do you keep coming back injured lately? You’re an S-rank."

"…I have a lot on my mind."

"Right, right. Please keep the domestic squabbles to a minimum. It’s only us subordinates who are dying here. Have some mercy, please."
