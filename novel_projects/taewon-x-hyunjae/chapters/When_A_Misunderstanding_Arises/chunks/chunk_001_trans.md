The front door opened and Song Tae-won stepped inside.

"I'm home."

His greeting, gentle yet weary, echoed softly in the foyer before fading away. As he took off his shoes and looked ahead, Song Tae-won paused, scanning the interior of the house.

Except for the orange glow of the entryway light, the house was submerged in thick darkness. It was still early evening, yet the home was as silent as the dead of night.

Filled with bewilderment, Song Tae-won slipped into the indoor slippers neatly placed by the door and walked inside. He tugged at his necktie and unbuttoned the top button of his shirt.

Unlike Song Tae-won, the second master of the house who often volunteered for overtime, the first master always left work right on time. He had always been there to welcome Song Tae-won back from his late shifts, so why was there no greeting today?

Unable to shake off his lingering confusion, Song Tae-won drifted toward the dressing room as if spellbound. Across from the dressing room was the bedroom. It was a secret space where the two, who had become lovers late last spring, would entwine their bodies and whisper words of love.

Through a gap in the door that hadn't been pulled shut—a space barely a hand's breadth wide—a familiar voice leaked out from the lightless room.

"Yes, that’s perfect. It seems there is no one else quite like you."

The nuance was such that anyone listening might mistake it for a conversation with a very affectionate lover. Song Tae-won swallowed a hollow laugh and shook his head.

That couldn't be. The owner of that charming, pleasant voice was his lover. His lover was in the same house right now, so who—

"Yes. It was even better than I had imagined. It’s beautiful."

Song Tae-won now stood at the crossroads between the dressing room and the bedroom.

Whatever that conversation was about, Song Tae-won should have just gone into the dressing room, changed his clothes, and come back out to receive his lover’s greeting. He shouldn't be eavesdropping on a phone call like an uninvited guest in the dead of night.

He wasn't a thief, and listening with bated breath was far from his nature. But he felt uneasy.

The way his lover—the most beautiful man in the world—was whispering as if having a secret tryst made Song Tae-won’s heart beat with a heavy, thudding rhythm.

"I'll be waiting."

Waiting for what? His hand, which had been loosening the end of his tie, tightened into a fist. Song Tae-won tried to reason. It couldn't be what he was thinking.

He comforted himself, telling himself this was merely a misunderstanding born from the short-sightedness of a man exhausted by overtime. He tried to soothe his own mind.

There were footsteps, lighter than Song Tae-won’s own. The sound of rustling fabric brushed against the floor. He knew his lover inside the bedroom was approaching. He also knew that standing in this spot was a bad idea. Yet, his feet remained rooted to the spot, frozen.

The gray bedroom door opened. The light from the living room intruded into the darkness. A figure appeared—undoubtedly the lover he had missed throughout his entire shift. Song Tae-won and Sung Hyun-jae stood face to face.

"......."
