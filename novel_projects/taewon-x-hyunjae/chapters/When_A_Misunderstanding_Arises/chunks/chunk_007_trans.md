The moonlight spilling through the window was exceptionally pale. It was whiter than the winter snow, resembling Song Tae-won’s lover. He missed him. He wanted to run to him. He wanted to hold him. He wanted it to be forever.

Song Tae-won looked at his phone screen, his face damp with sweat. He wanted to reach out, to hear his voice at the very least. But what would he say if he did? What words would those red lips utter?

There was a time when Sung Hyun-jae did not exist in Song Tae-won’s life. But now that he had to spend his days without Sung Hyun-jae, why did it feel so heavy and painful? If he ran home right now, his lover would be there.

He didn't know.

He had thought he should let him go. But that was impossible. Song Tae-won’s desire for Sung Hyun-jae was too great. It had grown to a size that Song Tae-won could no longer control. So please, he prayed, he just wanted Sung Hyun-jae to come back to him.

Song Tae-won closed his eyes. Behind his eyelids, the man who shone brighter than anyone else appeared. He wished that for as long as he was apart from him, Sung Hyun-jae would appear in his dreams.

It had been a week. A week since Song Tae-won had stepped foot in the house. Sung Hyun-jae couldn't even bring himself to sigh, watching him stay away not just on weekdays, but through the weekend as well. Or rather, he swallowed the sigh that threatened to escape.

Their peaceful relationship had twisted and collapsed like a lie. It had turned Sung Hyun-jae’s world upside down. The existence of Song Tae-won had ruined everything for him. He had made him look only at him, to the point where he couldn't do anything without him. He had made him a fool.

Where had the collapse begun? What about Sung Hyun-jae had displeased Song Tae-won? Why had Song Tae-won acted that way? What had Sung Hyun-jae done to make Song Tae-won treat him with such coldness?

Ah.

Ah?

He had originally planned to stay in the duty room for a few more days. But at Sung Hyun-jae’s summons, Song Tae-won returned home for the first time in a week.

His wounds had not yet healed, but as long as Sung Hyun-jae didn't find out, it would be fine. That was why he had returned.
